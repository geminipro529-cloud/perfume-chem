## HOUSE DNA

Serge Lutens' Paris exclusives are **architectural, dark, powder-driven, and unapologetically French**. Iris Silver Mist is the reference — a cold, abstract, rooty-butter orris with no floral sweetness. Féminité du Bois is cedar-rose-violet, sharp as architecture. Lutens doesn't make "pretty" — he makes **compelling**.

**Jasmin d'Orris à la Lutens:**
- **Maximum ionones:** Beta Ionone DOMINATES (not balanced) — pure violet-iris perfume
- **Coumarin-Vanillin eutectic:** Vintage powder compact effect (hacks §17 — pre-mix forms slow-release micro-crystals)
- **Ethyl Maltol micro-dose:** Perception distortion softens angular ionones without sweetness (hacks §19)
- **Myristic Acid:** Authentic orris butter — Iris Silver Mist territory (hacks §1)
- **Rose Oxide at trace:** Lychee-metallic coldness (hacks §23 — the 1% dilution for lychee-fruit register)
- **No jasmine sambac:** Lutens would use cis-Jasmone as the SOLE jasmine signal — green-ketone cold jasmine, not indolic warmth
- **Musk Ketone powder-echo:** Central to Lutens' powder aesthetic — slow bloom at 120 min
- **Dark, dry, cold:** No gourmand sweetness (beyond eutectic), no warm ambers, no tropical florals
