# Immortelle Fougère Corsica — 30mL EdP

## Character
A wild, aromatic fougère where Immortelle Absolute's curry-honey-maple warmth replaces the traditional tonka bean sweetness in the lavender-coumarin-oakmoss frame, creating a distinctly Corsican interpretation of the classic masculine structure. The composition opens with a bright lavender-bergamot-rosemary aromatic blast, transitions through an immortelle-labdanum-germanium heart (where immortelle's herbal-curry facet merges with lavender's camphor and geranium's minty-rose), and settles into the classic fougère base: coumarin, oakmoss, patchouli, and cedar. Unlike the soapy-clean commercial fougères (Brut, Paco Rabanne PH), this is an untamed, botanical fougère — the lavender of wild Corsican hillsides rather than Provençal fields, with immortelle as the "wild card" that adds Mediterranean warmth and complexity. Think *Fougère Royale* reimagined through a Corsican lens — aromatic, herbal, slightly animalic, with immortelle as the golden thread that runs through every layer.

**Family**: Aromatic Fougère — lavender-geranium-coumarin-oakmoss structure, immortelle as golden heart modulator, bergamot-rosemary aromatic top

## Material Justification

- **Immortelle Absolute (10%)**: The golden thread through the fougère — 180 µL of 10% = 18 µL active (0.3% of concentrate). At the core modifier dose, it replaces the traditional tonka-vanilla sweetness that defines classical fougères with something more herbal, more complex, and distinctly Mediterranean. Its curry facets merge with lavender's camphor, its honey facets merge with geranium's rose-mint, and its maple facets merge with coumarin's tonka. The result: a fougère that smells like Corsica, not a barbershop.
- **Lavender EO BONTAUX (150 µL) + Spike Lavender EO (60 µL)**: The dual-lavender heart of the fougère. BONTAUX provides honeyed-floral lavender sweetness — the softer, more refined lavender character that pairs with immortelle's honey facet. Spike Lavender adds the sharper, camphoraceous-aromatic edge that gives the fougère its masculine architecture. Together they create a wild, uncultivated lavender character — the lavender of Corsican hillsides. 210 µL total lavender is substantial for a fougère and justified by the dual lavender strategy.
- **Geranium EO (120 µL)**: The essential fougère floral heart at elevated dose. Geranium's fresh rosy-green-minty character is THE heart of the classical fougère — more important than the lavender in many formulations. At 120 µL neat, it provides the minty-rosy lift that prevents the lavender-coumarin axis from becoming medicinal. Period-correct for a historical fougère reference — geranium EO was used at significant doses in late-19th-century fougères.
- **Rosemary EO (50 µL) + Clary Sage EO (50 µL)**: Aromatic-herbal reinforcement. Rosemary's camphoraceous-herbal character amplifies the lavender's herbal facet — the "wild maquis" dimension. Clary sage adds the dry, tea-like complexity that bridges the aromatic top to the coumarin-oakmoss base. Both at moderate doses — supporting players in the lavender structure.
- **Coumarin (20%)**: 150 µL of 20% = 30 µL active. The ESSENTIAL fougère material — coumarin IS the fougère base. At elevated dose (30 µL active vs typical 20-24 in other formulas), it anchors the lavender-oakmoss structure with its hay-sweet tonka character. The higher dose is justified because this IS a fougère — coumarin is the defining material, not an accent. Paired with immortelle (proven synergy) to create a coumarin-immortelle accord that replaces the traditional coumarin-vanilla axis.
- **Oakmoss Absolute (10%) + Evernyl (50%)**: The fougère's mossy foundation. Oakmoss at 20 µL active provides the bitter-lichen character that pairs with coumarin — the classic coumarin-oakmoss axis that DEFINES the fougère genre. Evernyl at 75 µL active adds synthetic mossy body. Substantial doses because the fougère demands moss presence equal to the lavender-coumarin frame.
- **Labdanum Resinoid (10%)**: 150 µL of 10% = 15 µL active. The resinous-amber bridge between the herbal lavender-geranium heart and the mossy-coumarin base. Labdanum adds warm complexity to the fougère's structure — the "amber glow" that prevents the fougère from reading as purely herbal-medicinal. Pairs with immortelle for the golden-resinous thread.
- **Bergamot FCF (350 µL) + Cedrat FCF Sicilian (100 µL)**: The fougère's canonical citrus top. Bergamot provides the classic bright opening — the essential contrast to the dark mossy base. Cedrat adds the bitter citron edge that cuts through the lavender's camphor. Together they create the "fresh start" that begins every fougère.
- **Terpinyl Acetate (50 µL)**: Pine-citrus-herbal lift — adds a subtle coniferous dimension that aligns with the Corsican-maquis character. Pairs naturally with rosemary and lavender. Chosen specifically to add geographic specificity — Corsica's pine forests are integral to the island's olfactory identity.
- **Hedione HC (500 µL) + Hedione (300 µL)**: Radiance amplifier pair. Hedione's synergy with immortelle expands the golden-herbal heart. Essential in a fougère because the lavender-coumarin-oakmoss structure can feel dense and grounded — Hedione provides the lift and radiance that modernizes the classical structure. 800 µL total is appropriate.
- **Cedarwood oil Virginia (250 µL)**: Natural cedar backbone at elevated dose — the "wood structure" of the fougère. Virginia cedarwood provides the warm, slightly pencil-shaving character that anchors the aromatic top notes. Higher dose than typical to provide the woody architecture that supports the substantial lavender-geranium heart.
- **Iso E Super (500 µL) + Vertofix (180 µL)**: Woody backbone. Iso E Super provides the molecular cocoon — volume and the anosmic skin-sit. Vertofix bridges the wood to the musk layers — essential in fougères where the wood-musk transition must be seamless.
- **Azarbre (80 µL) + Timberol (70 µL) + Cedramber (80 µL) + Koavone (60 µL)**: Multi-axis woody-amber layer. Azarbre provides cedar-amber warmth — warmer than Ambrox, complementing the golden-immortelle character. Timberol adds dry-architectural cedar precision — the rigid structure that gives the fougère definition. Cedramber bridges cedar and amber. Koavone supports with balsamic warmth. Together they create a complex woody-amber bed beneath the aromatic crown.
- **Ambrofix (30% w/v) + Amberwood F (60 µL)**: Clean amber accents. Ambrofix at 24 µL active provides crystalline sparkle that cuts through the aromatic density. Amberwood F adds invisible transparent warmth. Both at moderate doses — the fougère's character comes from lavender-geranium-coumarin, not amber.
- **Romandolide (160 µL)**: Projection musk — diffusive woody-musk that extends the fougère's aromatic-herbal architecture outward. The slightly woody facet aligns with the cedar backbone. Chosen over Habanolide (too warm-intimate for a fougère that should project clean and masculine) and over Galaxolide (too laundry-detergent — would clash with the botanical character).
- **Ethylene Brassylate (200 µL)**: Depth musk — creamy lactonic skin-sit that anchors the highly volatile lavender-rosemary-geranium top notes. The lactonic quality softens the oakmoss's bitterness and coumarin's sharpness, creating a more refined, modern fougère texture.
- **Ambrettolide (10% in DPG)**: Character-echo musk — 80 µL of 10% = 8 µL active. Naturalistic fruity-musky warmth that bridges immortelle's honeyed-herbal character to the fougère's botanical structure at the skin level. The "wild Corsican hillside after rain" impression at the drydown — botanical, natural, warm.
- **Hexyl Salicylate (150 µL) + Benzyl Salicylate (100 µL)**: Asymmetric fixative gradient. Hexyl Salicylate (light, green-floral) at 150 µL is the primary fixative — creates adhesion for the aromatic top and aligns with the fougère's herbal-green character. Benzyl Salicylate (heavy, cosmetic) at RESTRAINED 100 µL — half the typical dose. Chosen deliberately because the fougère should project clean and botanical, not cosmetic-warm. The small Benzyl Salicylate dose provides structural diffusion volume without the "perfume counter" character.
