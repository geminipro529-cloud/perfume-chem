# Tuberose Radiance — 30 mL Extrait

**Character:** A radiant white floral with Hedione-jasmonate brilliance. Tuberose Absolute leads with its methyl benzoate narcotic bloom. Linalool provides fresh-floral lift. Cassis Base adds blackcurrant brightness. A creamy sandalwood-musk base with subtle gourmand warmth underneath. Cocoa and patchouli provide skin-level depth only — this is a floral perfume, not a gourmand.
