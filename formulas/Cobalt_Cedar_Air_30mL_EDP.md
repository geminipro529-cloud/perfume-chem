# Cobalt Cedar Air — 30 mL EDP Recipe

**Batch:** 30.00 mL · ~20 % EdP · 35 materials  
**Profile:** Bleu de Chanel / Sauvage / Luna Rossa Carbon / ADG Profumo — blue-family aromatic woody. Citrus-zest top, creamy sandalwood heart, incense-amber depth, Ambroxan-driven projection.  
**Maceration:** 4 weeks minimum  
**No IFRA adjustment needed** (all doses within Cat 4 limits)

---

## What You Need

- 1 × 30 mL glass spray bottle (amber preferred)
- 1 mL graduated glass pipette (for 200–600 µL pours)
- Micro-pipette or 1 mL syringe with 0.01 mL marks (for 5–100 µL pours)
- Ethanol 96 % (perfumer's grade)

---

## Mixing Steps

Pipette each directly into the 30 mL bottle. Wipe or switch pipettes between materials.

### C — Add the top layer (most volatile — add last)

| Order | Material | Dilution | Amount | Notes |

| 29 | Linalyl Acetate | neat | **50 µL** | floral-citrus bergamot DNA |
| 30 | Methyl Pamplemousse | 10% | **30 µL** | grapefruit-rhubarb sparkle (replaces Grapefruit FCF) |
| 31 | Aldehyde C11 undecylenic | 1% | 40 µL | aldehydic-citrus effervescence |
| 32 | Dihydromyrcenol | neat | 100 µL | clean-blue ozonic lift |
| 33 | Beta-Pinene | neat | **15 µL** | pine-green terpene spark — subtle forest-breeze distinction |
| 34 | Scentenal | **1%** | **10 µL** | marine "air" at sub-ODT — modern feel, no calone |
| 35 | Floralozone | 10% | **80 µL** | sheer ozonic lift — transparent mineral air |

**Running total: ~5,920 µL**

---

### D — Top up with ethanol

1. Pour ethanol 96 % into the bottle until it reaches the **30 mL fill line**.
2. The concentrate is ~5,920 µL, so you need roughly **~24.1 mL ethanol** (adjust visually to the 30 mL mark).
3. Cap tightly. Invert **50 times** — do NOT shake violently, just smooth inversions.
4. Label the bottle: "Cobalt Cedar Air — 2026-05-03 — macerate 4 weeks — open after 2026-05-31."

---

## Maceration

Store in a cool, dark place. Invert gently once per week. **Minimum 4 weeks** — the Ambroxan-heavy base, three-sandalwood stack, and new incense/moss additions need time to integrate. The citrus top will soften and the woody-amber heart will bloom after 3–4 weeks.
