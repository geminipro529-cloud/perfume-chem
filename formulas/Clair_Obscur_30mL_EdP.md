# Clair-Obscur — 30mL EdP

## Character
A chiaroscuro immortelle soliflore — immortelle from the first breath, haloed by light, anchored by layered darkness. Immortelle Absolute at 70 µL active (700 µL of 10% in DPG, OAV ~1.23M) dominates completely. The opening is a golden halo — Bergamot and Frankincense provide brightness without competing, while Phenyl Ethyl Acetate bridges the honey register. As the halo fades, two layers of darkness emerge: Vetival's suede-vetiver dryness in the heart, Vetiver EO India's black root in the base, with Cassia's liquid fire and Safranate's dark spice between them. Eucalyptus sits in the heart as a cineole blade — brightness that survived the opening. Rose de Mai Absolute at ghost level (25 µL of 10%) whispers in the drydown. The musk chord (Musk Ketone character-echo, Ethylene Brassylate depth, Ambrettolide intimacy) extends the golden field to 8+ hours. Coumarin and Benzoin provide the proven hay and honey parallels.

Named after Caravaggio's painting technique — light and shadow define each other. Neither wins. Both burn together. The flower holds the space between.

**Family**: Immortelle Soliflore — niche chiaroscuro architecture, immortelle-first opening, layered darkness drydown. ~16.7% concentration (EdP).

**Key literature references**: Corticchiato (Immortelle Corse, saffron-apricot-citrus prelude), Lie (Immortelle Solaire, extreme sparseness with novel ultrasonic extract), Piot/Charabot (tobacco-leather immortelle for masculine structures), Delacourte/Guerlain (immortelle as untamable Mediterranean soul), Andreani et al. 2019 (GC-O: italidiones, 1,8-cineole, nerol as key odorants)

## Rationale — Material by Material

### HALO (0-30 min — the light around the flower)

**Bergamot FCF (60 µL)**: A window, not a room. Reduced from 150 to 60 — the opening is immortelle-first. Bergamot provides a gentle brightness halo, not a dominant citrus blast. Chosen over Cedrat (too bitter-mineral) and Blood Orange (too fruity).

**Frankincense EO (100 µL)**: Cathedral ceiling. Boswellia carterii, ethereal pine-citrus-incense lift. VP 0.5 Pa — persists 1-2 hours, bridging the halo into the heart. The only contrast spoke that survives past the opening. Creates olfactory "space" around the immortelle without adding a recognizable incense note.

**Phenyl Ethyl Acetate (100 µL)**: Honeyed-rose lift. Shares immortelle's sweet register but in floral, not spicy. Doubles the honey while adding a rose dimension immortelle lacks naturally. VP 3.0 Pa — burns off in 2-4 hours. At OAV ~550K, it's clearly perceptible but subservient to immortelle's 1.23M.

**Hedione HC (800 µL)**: Radiance halo. The cis-isomer concentration provides rapid lift. Creates the "space" around the immortelle — the olfactory equivalent of sunlight through stained glass. At this dose, it doesn't add a note — it adds dimension.

### THE GOLD (5 min — 6 hr)

**Immortelle Absolute 10% in DPG (700 µL = 70 active)**: THE FIELD. OAV ~1.23M. At this dose — matching Les Indémodables' Immortelle Solaire at 3.3% ultrasonic extract — the curry-maple-honey-tobacco character dominates completely. The 10% dilution in DPG is a feature, not a limitation: the DPG solvent smooths the harsh/dry facets of standard hexane-extracted absolute (Lie's warning), while keeping OAV above 1M for soliflore-level dominance. No other material competes at equal register — they exist beneath it. At 70 µL active (vs. the formula series' 15-20 µL), this is 3.5-4.7× the modulator dose — firmly in soliflore territory while managing the curry-food risk that Piot and Delacourte warn about at extreme doses.

**Eucalyptus Essential Oil (30 µL)**: The cineole blade. Moved from top to heart — it now persists 30+ minutes alongside Frankincense instead of burning in 10. VP 210 Pa is still high enough to provide lift, but its cineole sharpness now extends into the early heart as a "brightness survivor." TRPM8 cooling adds a physical sensation of light against immortelle's golden density.

**Coumarin 20% (150 µL = 30 active)**: Hay parallel. Proven synergy from all 10 Immortelle formula series. Doubles immortelle's hay-herbal facet without introducing a new register. The coumarinic-tonka sweetness bridges immortelle's maple to the benzoin-vanilla base.

**Gamma Decalactone (60 µL)**: Lactonic cream bridge. Converts immortelle's maple from "syrup" to "cream." Texture modifier — smooths the standard absolute's harsh/dry edges. At this dose, it reads as body, not as peach-lactone.

**Heliotropal (10 µL)**: Trace almond-heliotrope powder. Adds a shimmer to immortelle's honey facet — like light through amber glass. Not identifiable as "heliotrope" at this dose — just a powdery golden glow.

**Hedione (600 µL)**: Sustained radiance through the heart. Total Hedione load (HC + standard) = 1,400 µL. Justified because immortelle sits close to skin (γ=0.6 in ethanol matrix) and needs expansion to project. The literature confirms Hedione as an immortelle synergy material.

**Siam Benzoin 50% (450 µL = 225 active)**: The honey amplifier. Largest active dose after EB and Musk Ketone. Natural vanilla-balsamic warmth that merges with immortelle's honey register and extends it 6+ hours. The base IS the honey-maple persistence.

**Labdanum Resinoid 10% (200 µL = 20 active)**: Resinous amber bridge. Connects immortelle's heart to the benzoin-vanilla base. Matched in active dose to immortelle's modifier materials — co-heart, not competitor.

**Rose de Mai Absolute 10% (25 µL = 2.5 active)**: The ghost. OAV ~6,700 — just above conscious threshold. VP 0.001 fixes it to the base. It whispers "rose" in the drydown, 4+ hours in, when immortelle's honey begins to fade. Someone who knows perfume will lean in. Everyone else will just think the immortelle is unusually beautiful.

### DARKNESS — TWO LAYERS

**Upper Darkness — Suede-Heart (30 min — 4 hr)**

**Vetival (30 µL)**: Suede-vetiver dryness. Synthetic vetiver material with ODT air of 0.1 ppb — 50× more potent than Vetiver EO. At 30 µL (OAV ~90K), it provides a suede-leather-vetiver texture in the heart without dominating. VP 6.53 Pa means it burns off in 3-4 hours — exactly where the lower darkness takes over. Synergizes with both Vetiver EO and Iso E Super (literature-confirmed).

**Lower Darkness — Root-Floor (30 min — 8+ hr)**

**Vetiver EO India (80 µL)**: Black root. Indian ruh khus — deeper and more complex than standard Vetiver EO. Maximum olfactive contrast to immortelle's golden sweetness. At this dose, it creates the "floor" — the dark earth beneath the golden field. Reduced from 100 to 80 to make room for Vetival's suede layer above.

**Cassia EO (40 µL)**: Liquid fire. 80% cinnamaldehyde. A smoldering ember in the drydown — the warmth that persists after brightness is gone. At 40 µL in 4,995 µL concentrate (~0.80%), and ~16.7% concentration in EdP: cinnamaldehyde equivalent ~0.13% — under the 0.25% IFRA Cat4 limit with margin. The danger is deliberate. The complexity of natural Cassia EO (cinnamyl acetate, coumarin traces, linalool) provides more nuance than pure Cinnamaldehyde.

**Patchouli EO (70 µL)**: Wet soil. Proven immortelle synergy from all 10 Immortelle formulas. Earthy-camphoraceous grounding that prevents the honey from floating into cloying territory. The roots in the soil.

**Evernyl 50% (80 µL = 40 active)**: Moss crypt. Oakmoss replacement — provides chypre skeleton without making this a chypre. Adds dark-green structure beneath the immortelle, not a competing moss note. Used in Immortelle Corse (Corticchiato).

**Ethyl Safranate (60 µL)**: Dark spice. Immortelle's proven literature partner — Corticchiato, Piot, and Fraterworks all name saffron as immortelle's natural companion. At this dose, it reads as "immortelle's spicy facet amplified" rather than "saffron + immortelle."

**Vanillin 10% (80 µL = 8 active)**: Classic vanilla whisper. Supports benzoin's vanilla facet without reading as "vanilla."

### BASE (1 — 8+ hr)

**Musk Ketone 10% (350 µL = 35 active)**: THE character-echo musk. Boosted from 250 to 350 — the only musk in inventory that reinforces immortelle's honey-powder through the drydown. At this dose, it echoes the flower long after the absolute itself has faded. This follows immortelle's natural direction: dry, warm, golden — not clean, not fresh, not aquatic. Crystalline solid — ensure fully dissolved before adding ethanol.

**Ethylene Brassylate (350 µL)**: Depth musk. Lactonic-creamy body fills out immortelle's frame. Reduced from 400 to 350 to let Musk Ketone's character-echo dominate the drydown. Still substantial enough to carry the honey-musk ghost to 8+ hours.

**Ambrettolide 10% (120 µL = 12 active)**: Intimacy musk. Naturalistic warmth bridges immortelle to living skin. "The flower on warm skin." Warmer and more animal-adjacent than Habanolide — follows immortelle's natural direction toward warm, not clean.

**Cedarwood EO (100 µL)**: Warm wood bed. Natural pencil-cedar beneath the flower — not a woody note, just a warm-textural foundation. Chosen over Iso E Super (too abstract-molecular), Timberol (too architectural), and Sandalwood materials (would add competing creamy register).

**Hexyl Salicylate (150 µL)**: Transparent adhesion film. Green-floral fixative, lighter than Benzyl Salicylate (too cosmetic-heavy for a soliflore). Creates diffusion without adding character.

**Benzyl Benzoate (200 µL)**: Invisible mass. Near-odorless balsamic fixative — adds molecular weight and tenacity without contributing to the scent profile.

## Musk Chord Direction

All three musks follow immortelle's natural gravity — dry, warm, golden — rather than pulling toward clean, fresh, or aquatic:

| Axis | Musk | Active µL | Direction alignment |
|------|------|-----------|---------------------|
| **Character-echo** | Musk Ketone 10% | 35 | Honey-powder. Same golden register as immortelle. |
| **Depth/body** | Ethylene Brassylate | 350 | Lactonic-creamy. Adds body without changing direction. |
| **Intimacy** | Ambrettolide 10% | 12 | Animal-warm. "Flower on living skin." |

Deliberately excluded: Habanolide (too clean), Romandolide (pulls outward, not intimate), Galaxolide/Tonalide/Zenolide (synthetic-laundry, fights immortelle's warmth).

## Known Risks

1. **Cassia EO IFRA edge**: At 40 µL neat, ~0.80% of concentrate. At ~16.7% concentration in EdP, cinnamaldehyde equivalent ~0.13% — under the 0.25% IFRA Cat4 limit with slim margin. Any formula adjustment that increases Cassia or reduces concentrate volume risks non-compliance.

2. **Standard absolute harshness**: Unlike Les Indémodables' ultrasonic full-spectrum extract, standard hexane-extracted immortelle absolute has harsh/dry facets at high dose (Lie's warning). The 10% dilution in DPG mitigates this naturally — DPG smooths the edges. Gamma Decalactone and Heliotropal provide additional smoothing.

3. **Immortelle OAV dominance**: At ~1.23M OAV, immortelle dominates perceptually for 4+ hours. Supporting materials exist beneath it, not beside it. This is intentional for a soliflore — 70 µL active is 3.5-4.7× the modulator dose from the 10-formula series.

4. **Rose de Mai audibility**: OAV ~6,700 — just above conscious threshold. May not register as a distinct note to all wearers. This is intentional.

5. **Musk Ketone solubility**: Crystalline solid at room temperature. Must be fully dissolved in concentrate before adding ethanol. Warm gently (not above 40°C).

6. **Vetival ODT potency**: At 0.1 ppb air, ODT is 50× lower than Vetiver EO. OAV ~90K at 30 µL. Clearly perceptible but not dominant. At higher doses, suede-vetiver would overwhelm.
