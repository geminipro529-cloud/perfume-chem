## R2 Concept Lock

The darkest classical amber in the set: labdanum, benzoin, olibanum, opoponax, myrrh, and Peru balsam beneath a dry tobacco-suede-saffron identity. **Amber means the resin body and its frankincense return.** Ambrox Super is retained only as a subordinate crystalline-mineral drydown and performance support; it does not own the amber identity. Amber Xtreme remains excluded because its extreme superamber register is unnecessary. Azarbre, Cashmeran, Kephalis, and Suederal remain differentiated wood, textile, tobacco, and suede transitions—not substitutes for resin amber.

The R1 Amber Xtreme dose was not numerically excessive: 100 µL of the 10% stock supplied a 10 µL active-volume proxy and modeled opening OAV 1.5. It remains omitted. The 200 µL Ambrox Super dose supplied a 50 µL active-volume proxy and modeled opening OAV 38.7, so it is retained as a moderate supporting return voice under the darker resin, tobacco, suede, and saffron structure.

R2 strengthens the classical resins, retains subordinate Ambrox support, and preserves the exact 6,000 µL raw concentrate total by removing/reducing two modeled sub-threshold structural materials:

- Amber Xtreme 10% w/w in DEP: 100 → 0 µL.
- Ambrox Super 25% w/w in DPG/IPM/ethanol: retained at 200 µL.
- Labdanum Resinoid 10% in DPG: 250 → 475 µL.
- Olibanum Resinoid 50% w/w in DPG: 400 → 475 µL.
- Bacdanol: 450 → 350 µL.
- Vertofix: 100 → 0 µL.

This is an intentional target rebalance, not active-dose equivalence. The full resin uplift remains the amber identity; the retained Ambrox supplies a cool mineral return and persistence beneath it. Bacdanol remains a substantial 350 µL sandalwood body, while Vertofix is omitted because its modeled OAV was about 1 and its bridge role is redundant with Ambrox, Azarbre, Kephalis, and Cashmeran.

Recursive Deep Plane Perfumery remains intact: deep residue → dry woody body → guaiac-resin rear contour → luminous classical resin amber → tobacco-suede identity → saffron-irone veil → peppered mandarin glint → transformed saffron-frankincense aura above the residue. ## Basket Compounding Procedure

1. Use one clean amber vial and add rows 1–32 in exact basket-first order, completing each basket before moving to the next.
2. Inside each basket, keep repeated-volume settings together and turn the dispenser knob only down. Reset it only when starting the next basket. Cap and gently roll periodically until homogeneous. The DPP planes describe functional relationships, not physical stratification.
3. After the concentrate reaches 6,000 µL, mix until uniform.
4. Add approximately 22 mL ethanol 96%, mix, then add ethanol q.s. to the 30.00 mL final-volume mark.
5. Inspect clarity and color immediately, at 24 hours, 7 days, and 14 days. The saffron and resin materials are expected to make this the darkest concentrate, but haze or sediment must still be recorded.
6. Evaluate on blotters at 0, 5, and 30 minutes; 2, 6, 24, and 48 hours. The decisive R2 question is whether the rear residue regenerates a saffron-olibanum-labdanum aura rather than ending as flat generic amberwood.

## Material Selection and Rejection Rationale

- **Classical resin amber:** Labdanum supplies the leathery seed, Siam Benzoin the warm balsamic volume, and olibanum the luminous frankincense return. Opoponax, myrrh, and Peru balsam extend that structure into the dark rear contour.
- **Rejected Amber Xtreme:** its modeled R1 OAV was only 1.5, at threshold. Its superamber pressure was dispensable and contradicted the corrected identity.
- **Supporting Ambrox Super:** the user explicitly permits ambrox character for distinction and performance when it remains subordinate. The 200 µL current stock dose provides a 50 µL active-volume proxy and historically modeled OAV 38.7. Its crystalline-mineral return sits beneath the labdanum-benzoin-olibanum, tobacco, suede, and saffron identity. Ambrofix is rejected because its owned liquid stock remains heterogeneous and non-executable; Ambermax would add a warmer synthetic amber body that competes more directly with the resins; Amberwood F is too transparent to justify another row.
- **Tobacco identity:** Tonkarome supplies coumarinic body, Kephalis dry tobacco-wood structure, and Suederal worn texture. Heliotropal and the ionones connect tobacco to the saffron veil.
- **Saffron recursion:** Alpha Irone forms a violet-suede seed, Safraleine supplies the dry leathery edge, and Red Saffron Infusion supplies the colored as-supplied identity. Hedione and linalool raise it into the upper aura.
- **Single musk:** Ethylene Brassylate supplies a soft, creamy residue. A clean projection musk would weaken the aged tobacco-resin image.
- **Fixative gradient:** Benzyl Benzoate anchors the residue while Benzyl Salicylate carries it toward saffron and frankincense.
