# Osmanthus Vert Printanier — 30mL EdP

## Character
A vibrant green-spring composition where osmanthus absolute provides an unexpected warm-floral anchor beneath a canopy of crushed leaves, galbanum, and violet-green freshness. The osmanthus's apricot sweetness prevents the aggressive green character from reading as bitter or harsh, acting as the "spring sunlight" that warms the chlorophyll. Think Balmain Vent Vert's galbanum-green architecture with an osmanthus heart replacing hyacinth, or Sisley Eau de Campagne with more floral warmth.

**Family**: Green — galbanum-cut grass-violet leaf, osmanthus floral anchor, sheer musks

## Material Justification

- **Galbanum EO (20 µL neat)**: The defining green material — steam-distilled, greener/sharper than the resinoid. At 20 µL neat, it's the primary green character note. The EO form is chosen over the resinoid because green compositions need the sharp, almost violent chlorophyll character, not the softer resinous galbanum.

- **Dynascone (10%, 30 µL = 3 µL active) + Parmavert (50 µL) + Leafovert (40 µL)**: The green triad — Dynascone provides the powerful green-galbanum bomb at trace, Parmavert adds green violet-leaf (pairs with Beta Ionone), Leafovert contributes sharp cut-grass. Together they create a multidimensional green accord that no single material achieves. Dynascone is the most potent and must be kept at trace to avoid harshness.

- **Triplal (10 µL neat)**: Ultra-powerful fresh-watery-aldehydic lift — at 10 µL (the ceiling for this material), it adds the "spring rain on leaves" character. Kept at exactly 10 µL to avoid the harsh-metallic overdose threshold. Distinct from Cyclamen Aldehyde, which provides the metallic-green floral heart.

- **Zenolide (190 µL)**: The coldest musk in the inventory — citrusy-fresh character that echoes the green opening. Chosen as the primary character-echo musk at a substantial dose because green compositions need cold, clean projection. Rejects: Galaxolide (too detergent), Habanolide (too warm-skin), Musk Ketone (powder clashes with green).

- **Beta Ionone (1% in TEC, 120 µL = 1.2 µL active)**: Green violet-leaf at precise trace dose — the 1% dilution allows exact control. At 1.2 µL active, it provides the violet-leaf character that bridges galbanum's sharp green and the floral heart, without the woody-ionone body that higher doses would introduce.

- **Alpha Ionone (60 µL neat)**: Rich orris-woody depth — provides the earthy-woody facet that complements the galbanum, creating the "forest floor" dimension. At modest dose, it's a supporting character, not a dominant ionone.

- **Undecavertol (1%, 60 µL = 0.6 µL active)**: Green-violet-leaf alcohol at trace — adds the "wet leaf" freshness. The 1% dilution enables precision dosing. Pairs with Parmavert for the violet-leaf dimension and with Triplal for the aqueous-green effect.

- **Evernyl (50% in DPG, 30 µL = 15 µL active)**: Oakmoss replacement — provides the bitter-green lichen character that defines the green family's chypre heritage. At modest dose, it adds earthy depth without the heavy mossiness of a full chypre.

- **Clearwood (80 µL)**: Modern patchouli replacement — clean-earthy-woody at modest dose provides the soil dimension without patchouli's heavy camphoraceous character. The "forest earth" beneath the green leaves.

- **No Benzyl Salicylate as primary fixative**: Deliberately minimized — green compositions need transparency, not cosmetic weight. Hexyl Salicylate at 300 µL carries the primary fixation role with its transparent green-floral film. The small Benzyl Salicylate dose (150 µL) provides just enough cushion to carry the osmanthus through the drydown.
