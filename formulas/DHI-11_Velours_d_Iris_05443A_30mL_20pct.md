## Target identity before materials

The reference target is not an undifferentiated “Dior Homme” impression. It is
the Dior Homme Intense re-edition sold in the 2011–2014 period and commonly
associated by collector photo chronology with formula code **05443/A**. A
reference sample must therefore be a documented 05443/A bottle with recorded
batch, acquisition, authenticity assessment, storage history, and opening date.
A bottle merely described as “2011” is not a sufficient target lock.

The target architecture supported across Dior and contemporaneous launch
material is:

- a short lavender veil;
- a dominant powdery iris/orris object;
- ambrette-mediated musk, pear-liqueur nuance, and silk-talc texture;
- restrained amber and vanilla warmth inside the iris fabric;
- a dry Virginia-cedar and vetiver return;
- an intimate musky-textile landing that retains iris rather than turning into
  laundry musk.

Velours d'Iris is an original architecture study using that silhouette. It is
not Dior's formula, a clone claim, a measured reconstruction, or evidence
of sensory similarity.

### Positive recognizers

1. Lavender is recognizable on fresh application but is no longer the subject
   by 30 minutes.
2. Iris/orris is the dominant named object at 5 minutes, 30 minutes, and 2
   hours, with powder body, violet movement, root coolness, and woody anatomy.
3. Pear is a small fermented or liqueur-like gleam *within* an ambrette/talc
   membrane, not a freestanding juicy-fruit note.
4. Warmth is perceived beneath the iris but cocoa, vanilla, tonka, and pastry
   are not named before iris.
5. Dry cedar and vetiver become clearer after the heart maximum while leaving a
   residual iris trace.
6. The 4-hour landing reads as dry wood, silk talc, warm fabric, and iris skin.

### Negative recognizers / immediate drift failures

- flat lipstick or chalky methyl-ionone wall;
- chocolate, brownie, praline, caramel, or pastry gourmand;
- leather, oud, or creamy sandalwood architecture associated with Dior Homme
  Parfum rather than DHI 05443/A;
- bright neroli-muguet soap or pressed-shirt Prada drift;
- cold blue Ambrox/amberwood pressure;
- syrupy pear candy;
- pencil shavings or hamster-bedding cedar;
- lavender-coumarin fougère;
- detergent white musk or anonymous musky persistence.

Complexity here means target-linked anatomy, perspective, texture, recurrence,
and temporal relation. Ingredient count earns no complexity credit.

raw-stock concentration, substrate, application mass, maturation,
room, and evaluation schedule. Use opaque codes, balanced presentation order,
at least one hidden repeated control, and fresh applications for each temporal

## OAV, natural-mixture, and psychophysical policy

1. Lavender EO High Altitude, Vetiver EO (India), and Cedarwood oil Virginia
   use the repository's constituent-composite natural OAV. Any future Cocoa
   Absolute arm must do the same, never a monomolecular whole-material shortcut.
2. Every active dose above is convertible to active ppm under the declared
   repository volume convention. W/w stocks remain nominal active-volume
   proxies until density and basis permit exact mass conversion.
3. OAV = concentration ppm / ODT ppm for the declared phase and threshold
   basis. Headspace uses mole fraction, vapor pressure, and non-unit activity
   coefficient; an air ODT is not silently exchanged with an ethanol ODT.
4. OAV below 1 is acceptable for Benzyl Benzoate because its selected role is
   purely structural. A sub-threshold Ambrettolide, Alpha Irone, Orivone,
   lavender, cedar, vetiver, Tonkarome, or future Cocoa test row is a failure
   because its declared role requires perceptibility or causal testing.
5. Modeled OAV-per-time is a screening alarm, never percent perceived
   contribution, beauty, liking, or sensory-similarity evidence.
6. Separate formula materials can suppress, mask, or blend nonlinearly. The
   controlled probes, not ingredient arithmetic, decide whether a relation is
   present.

### Perfumer analysis of the modeled screen

1. **Character** — The intended family is a dense iris/orris amber-wood with a
   short aromatic entry, a musk-fruit-talc membrane, restrained coumarinic
   warmth, and a cedar-vetiver return. It evokes the documented DHI 2011
   architecture without claiming Dior's formula. The computational structure
   is heart-dominant with a deliberately narrow top and a distinct dry counterform.

2. **Opening (0–5 min)** — Total modeled vapor is 1.570157672 ppm. Iso E is
   massive (>1000 OAV); Hedione, Lavender, Alpha Ionone, Orivone, and AIMI are
   very strong (100–1000); Linalyl Acetate is strong (50–100); Cashmeran and
   cedar are moderate (10–50); vetiver is perceptible (5–10); Alpha Irone,
   Tonkarome, and Ambrettolide are at threshold (1–5). This supports a highly
   legible aromatic/iris launch in the model, but the huge generic Iso E and
   Hedione OAVs are warnings against assuming the model predicts note naming.

3. **Heart (30 min–2 h)** — The screen keeps the iris anatomy, Iso E, Hedione,
   and lavender numerically active. The desired sensory event is not “all of
   these notes remain equally loud”; it is lavender adaptation and blending
   while ionone/orris and the ambrette membrane become the named object. Since
   the simulator does not model human adaptation or mixture masking, the
   lavender handoff is a required blind-test endpoint, not a computational pass.

4. **Drydown (2–4 h+)** — Formula-state base allocation rises only from 25.1%
   to 26.0%, while the engine still classifies Iso E as heart. At 4 h the
   cedar, vetiver, Tonkarome, Ambrettolide, and Alpha Irone rows all remain above
   OAV 1; therefore no declared base character is computationally dormant.
   The intended landing is dry cedar-vetiver, silk talc, and residual iris, but
   that character remains unobserved.

5. **Sillage & Diffusion** — Modeled opening diffusion is carried mainly by
   Lavender, Linalyl Acetate, Alpha Ionone, Iso E, and Hedione vapor. At 4 h,
   the highest modeled OAVs remain Iso E, Hedione, and the ionone system. The
   OAV-intelligence diffusion-layer check reports all layers present, but OAV
   is not a measured sillage radius and cannot establish projection.

6. **Longevity** — The simulator retains 5,754.094940 µL raw-equivalent at 4 h,
   a heuristic 4.098418% loss. That number is not calibrated to blotter or skin
   mass loss and cannot be converted into expected hours of wear. Longevity is

   `sigma_log(OAV) = 1.07`, inside the generic 0.8–1.2 target. The same
