## What you'll need

## Step 2 — Mature

1. Cap the build vial tightly. Label `F2-50mL concentrate · DD-MM-YYYY`.
2. Rest **dark, 18–22 °C, minimum 4 weeks** before dilution. Evernyl + Coumarin + macrocyclic musks integrate slowly.

---

## Step 3 — Dilute into the 50 mL bottle

**Procedure:**
1. Transfer all **10.00 mL (10 000 µL) matured concentrate** into the 50 mL atomizer bottle.
2. Add **40.00 mL (40 000 µL) perfumer's ethanol ≥ 96 %** (weigh ~31.6 g on a 0.01 g scale; ρ ≈ 0.79 → 40 mL).
3. Cap, invert 10×.
4. Macerate **2 more weeks** dark before evaluation on skin.

The finished EDP totals **50 000 µL (50.00 mL)** at **20 % concentrate**.
