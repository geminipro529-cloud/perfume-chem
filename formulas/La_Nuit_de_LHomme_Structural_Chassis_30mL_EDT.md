## Concept Lock

This is the reusable night chassis with cardamom removed and replaced by a neutral 50 uL DPG slot. It preserves the dark woody-amber field, lavender/cedar heart, coumarinic warmth, and vetiver drydown so the signature spice can be added, omitted, or exchanged without rebuilding the base.

## Flanker Interface

Replace the 50 uL DPG slot one-for-one.

| Direction | 50 uL replacement module | Expected shift |
|---|---|---|
| Realistic signature study | Cardamom EO 50 | Restores the cool cardamom recognizer |
| Cardamom-light electric | Cardamom 20 + Lavender 15 + Linalyl Acetate 15 | Cooler, brighter aromatic flanker |
| Spice-free dark woods | Keep DPG 50 | Isolates the woody/lavender/coumarin parent |
| Drier vetiver night | Vetiver EO 20 + Cedarwood Virginia 20 + Bergamot FCF 10 | Drier and less sweet; must be rechecked for OAV and allergens |

Caraway is absent from live inventory. Anise EO was rejected because licorice is not a faithful caraway replacement.

## Selection and Rejection Ledger

- The three-musks chord is Galaxolide for diffusion, Exaltolide for soft skin, and Zenolide for clean character.
- Ethylene Brassylate appears in the secondary public list but is omitted to keep the musk chord to three complementary materials.
- Lavender EO, linalool, and Ethyl Linalool create a tiered aromatic bridge; Florol prevents a thin heart.
- Amberwood F, Vertofix, Iso E Super, Cashmeran, and Ambrofix form the dark structural field.
- Heavy vanilla, leather, incense, balsams, and gourmand materials were rejected as optional directions rather than core chassis materials.

## Mixing and Evaluation

1. Preblend the woods, musks, coumarin/tonka materials, and Ambrofix.
2. Add the floral-aromatic heart and bergamot.
3. Add DPG last for this structural control.
4. Add 4.5 mL concentrate to 25.5 mL ethanol 96%; invert gently.
5. Mature at least 14 days. Compare against any module variant at 0, 5, 30 minutes, 2 hours, and 4 hours.
