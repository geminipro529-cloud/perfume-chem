## Selection and Rejection Ledger

- Cardamom EO is chemically suited to the brief because its cineole/terpinyl-acetate system bridges bergamot, lavender, and warm woods.
- The three-musks chord covers diffusion, soft skin, and clean character with Galaxolide, Exaltolide, and Zenolide.
- Ethylene Brassylate was omitted to prevent a four-musk pileup in this compressed formula.
- Caraway is unavailable. Anise, cumin, and heavy pepper substitutions were rejected as misleading.
- Pink Peppercorn appears only at trace rank in the secondary list and is not in live inventory; Black Pepper was not forced into La Nuit.
- Vanillin and patchouli are in the secondary list but omitted from this structural compression to avoid gourmand or earthy drift.

## Mixing and Evaluation

1. Preblend woods, musks, coumarin/tonka materials, and Ambrofix.
2. Add floral-aromatic materials and bergamot.
3. Add Cardamom EO last to the concentrate and mix gently.
4. Add 4.5 mL concentrate to 25.5 mL ethanol 96%; invert gently.
5. Mature at least 14 days. Compare directly with the cardamom-free chassis at 0, 5, 30 minutes, 2 hours, and 4 hours.
