## WHY PATCHOULI ISN'T IN THE AIR — AND WHY THAT'S OK

Arrhenius note tiers from Clausius-Clapeyron (AGENTS.md):

| Tier | VP range | Materials in this formula |
|------|----------|--------------------------|
| TOP | > 50 Pa | (none) |
| HEART | 0.5–50 Pa | Dihydrojasmone (7.67), Lavender (~3.5), Geraniol (2.67), Bergamot (~2.5), Grapefruit (~1.8), Petitgrain (~6), Black Pepper (0.3) |
| **BASE** | **< 0.5 Pa** | **Patchouli (0.001), Clearwood (0.002), Vetiver (0.003), Vanillin (0.0003), Iso E (0.23), Galaxolide (0.07), Cedramber (0.35), Hedione (0.089), Evernyl (0.13), Ambrox (0.05), Romandolide (0.10), Benzyl Salicylate (0.01), Coumarin (0.13)** |

**Every material with a "dark" character sits in BASE tier.** Nothing dark can fly. The waste ratio for Patchouli is **5,061:1** — five thousand times more in the bottle than in the air. This is true for Terre d'Hermès, Coromandel, TF Patchouli Absolu, and every patchouli chypre ever made.

**The gestalt bundle:** Vanillin (0.90 hedonic, 98:1 waste) sits at the edge of perception and bundles subliminally with the skin patchouli. The brain stitches "warm" + "earthy skin feel" + "herbal-woody halo" into a single unified "this is a patchouli chypre" experience.
