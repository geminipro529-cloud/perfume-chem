## Design Lock

This is not L'Homme EDT and not La Nuit. The Y signature is cleaner, bluer, more mineral, and more extroverted. The formula keeps the "white T-shirt and black jacket" contrast: bright aldehydic freshness over a dark polished woody base.

| Axis | Kept In Formula | Reason |
|---|---:|---|
| Aldehydic clean flash | Aldehyde C10, C11, C12 MNA | Original Y EDT-style white aldehydes; crisp shirt-collar brightness |
| Citrus and ginger lift | Bergamot FCF oil Sicilian, Ginger EO, Linalyl Acetate | Y-family fresh opening without copying L'Homme too closely |
| Blue/mineral freshness | Dihydromyrcenol, Helional, Calone trace | Modern Y EDT cleanliness; mineral-water sparkle without aquatic overdose |
| Aromatic heart | Lavender EO High Altitude, Clary Sage EO | Current official Y EDT emphasizes lavender and clary sage |
| Crisp geranium | Geranium EO | Green-rosy, slightly minty Y heart; not a rose direction |
| Violet leaf effect | Alpha Isomethyl Ionone, Parmavert | Replaces Alpha Irone; gives violet-green cosmetic texture without orris butter |
| Radiant air | Hedione HC, Hedione | Designer diffusion and lift through the aromatic heart |
| Fougere memory | Coumarin trace, Evernyl trace | Keeps masculine aromatic architecture without making the perfume mossy or sweet |
| Woods / ambergris / incense | Iso E Super, Cedarwood oil Virginia, Ambrofix, Frankincense EO, Vetiver EO | Y drydown: mineral ambergris, cedar, incense, and dry masculine woods |
| Clean skin trail | Habanolide, Romandolide, Cashmeran | Long EDT trail without cheap laundry musk dominance |

Alpha Irone is deliberately absent. It would push the formula toward Prada/Dior Homme iris grooming powder, which is not the Y EDT target.

## Why This Should Track Y EDT

The first impression should be clean and kinetic: aldehydes, bergamot, ginger, DHM, and a tiny mineral-blue shimmer. The heart should turn aromatic and crisp, led by clary sage, lavender, and geranium rather than the ginger-tonka warmth of L'Homme. The drydown should be Iso E cedar, ambergris-mineral, incense, vetiver, and clean musks.

This intentionally avoids Alpha Irone because Alpha Irone would make the perfume smell more expensive in the wrong direction: orris butter, powder, and grooming cream. Y needs violet-leaf polish, not iris luxury. Alpha Isomethyl Ionone plus Parmavert gives that green-violet facet while staying closer to the Y allergen/listed-material world.

## Material Rejections

| Rejected | Why |
|---|---|
| Alpha Irone | User constraint; also too iris/orris for Y EDT |
| Heavy ionone/orris accord | Would drift toward Prada L'Homme or Dior Homme instead of Y |
| Cardamom EO | Pulls toward La Nuit and L'Homme Luxe rather than Y |
| Tonkarome / Coumarin pillar | Makes it closer to Y EDP or L'Homme; only a trace coumarin support is used |
| Rose EO / Rose absolute | Geranium should read crisp and masculine, not floral rose |
| Galaxolide overdose | Too laundry-clean; Habanolide/Romandolide gives a better skin trail |
| Heavy balsams / labdanum / benzoin | Too ambered, too dark, wrong for fresh EDT |
| Smoke/leather phenolics | Would destroy the clean Y shirt-and-jacket effect |

## Mixing Order

1. Add 25.5 mL ethanol to a 30 mL bottle.
2. Add base materials from largest to smallest. Swirl after the Iso E/Cedar/Ambrofix block, then after musks.
3. Add aromatic heart materials from largest to smallest. Swirl gently.
4. Add top/aldehydic materials last, from largest to smallest. Cap and invert gently.
5. Rest 2 weeks minimum, 4 weeks preferred. Evaluate strip at 0 min, 15 min, 2 hr, and 6 hr.
