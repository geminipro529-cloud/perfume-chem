## Design Lock

This is not L'Homme EDT and not La Nuit. The Y signature is cleaner, bluer, more mineral, and more extroverted. The formula keeps the "white T-shirt and black jacket" contrast: bright aldehydic freshness over a dark polished woody base.

## Why This Should Track Y EDT

The first impression should be clean and kinetic: aldehydes, bergamot, ginger, DHM, and a tiny mineral-blue shimmer. The heart should turn aromatic and crisp, led by clary sage, lavender, and geranium rather than the ginger-tonka warmth of L'Homme. The drydown should be Iso E cedar, ambergris-mineral, incense, vetiver, and clean musks.

This intentionally avoids Alpha Irone because Alpha Irone would make the perfume smell more expensive in the wrong direction: orris butter, powder, and grooming cream. Y needs violet-leaf polish, not iris luxury. Alpha Isomethyl Ionone plus Parmavert gives that green-violet facet while staying closer to the Y allergen/listed-material world.

## Mixing Order

1. Add 25.5 mL ethanol to a 30 mL bottle.
2. Add base materials from largest to smallest. Swirl after the Iso E/Cedar/Ambrofix block, then after musks.
3. Add aromatic heart materials from largest to smallest. Swirl gently.
4. Add top/aldehydic materials last, from largest to smallest. Cap and invert gently.
5. Rest 2 weeks minimum, 4 weeks preferred. Evaluate strip at 0 min, 15 min, 2 hr, and 6 hr.
