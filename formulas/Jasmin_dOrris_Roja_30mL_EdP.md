## HOUSE DNA

Roja Dove builds at Gulf luxury level — real oud, real ambergris, real orris butter at **working concentrations**. The architecture is **opulent layering**: multiple resins, multiple musks, incense, balsams, and real florals stacked into symphonic complexity. Nothing is subtle. Everything is rich.

**Jasmin d'Orris à la Roja:**
- **Oud substitute:** Nagarmortha Oil + Guaiacol 10% + Birch Tar Rectified — smoky-animalic-mineral "oud" from inventory
- **Ambergris substitute:** Ambrofix at high dose + Labdanum Absolute — marine-amber-resin warmth
- **Real orris butter:** Alpha Irone (highest dose) + Myristic Acid 2:1 (hacks §1)
- **Salicylate diffusion:** Hexyl Sal (close aura) + Benzyl Sal (trail) — true spatial depth (hacks §16)
- **Musk bloom timeline:** Romandolide → EB → Musk Ketone (hacks §14)
- **Ionone receptor balance:** Beta Ionone reduced — iris-powder character (hacks §25)
- **Wax co-dissolution:** Benzoin Resinoid 50% provides resin-tackiness to slow vapor pressure of volatiles (hacks §2 — benzoin as wax substitute)
