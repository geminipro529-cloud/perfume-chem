# Iris Intense — 30 mL EdP

**Inspired by Dior Homme Intense (François Demachy, 2011).**  
**Family:** Woody Floral Musk · Powdery Oriental  
**Concentration:** ~18% EdP · ~5,400 µL concentrate in 30 mL  
**Character:** Lipstick-powder iris, pear-ambrette sweetness, lavender opening, vanilla-cocoa-cedar drydown.

## What the Allergen Label Reveals

Dior's official label (formula #10950/A) lists these allergens in descending concentration:

> LIMONENE · LINALOOL · COUMARIN · **ALPHA-ISOMETHYL IONONE** · CITRAL · FARNESOL · GERANIOL · BENZYL CINNAMATE · CINNAMAL · CITRONELLOL · BENZYL BENZOATE

**Key confirmations**: Alpha-Isomethyl Ionone is the dominant iris material. Coumarin appears before the ionone — the tonka-coumarin loading is massive. Benzyl Benzoate is the fixative (not benzyl salicylate — this is Demachy, not Polge). Benyl Cinnamate + Cinnamal = cinnamon warmth in the base.

## The Iris Accord (Reconstructed)

From published analysis (I Make Scents, Jicky 2.0, Olfactorian), DHI's lipstick iris is built from:

| Material | Evidence |
|---|---|
| **Alpha Isomethyl Ionone** — dominant | On allergen label; "methyl ionone" identified by Jicky 2.0 |
| **Orivone** — woody-earthy foundation | "Orivone entered in force in the 2011 reformulation supporting both iris and pear" (Jicky 2.0) |
| **Alpha Ionone** — woody-violet backbone | Standard in all published orris reconstructions |
| **Beta Ionone** — sweeter-fruitier violet | Provides the "peachy glow" alongside damascone |
| **Alpha Irone** — true iris butter | Orris absolute at 0.25% in the original (I Make Scents GC data) |
| **Carrot Seed EO** — green-earthy carrot | The "carrot facet" of iris (I Make Scents) |
| **δ-Damascone** — peachy warmth | 0.11% in original DH (I Make Scents); bridges iris to pear |
| **Coumarin + Vanillin** — powdery-sweet | On allergen label; "coumarins (vanillinate)" per Jicky 2.0 |

## Dosing Notes

- **Alpha Isomethyl Ionone at 350 µL**: This IS the fragrance. Do not reduce. Below 250 µL the lipstick effect collapses into generic powder.
- **Orivone at 60 µL**: 1.1% of concentrate. The sweet spot. Below 0.5% the iris lacks earthy foundation. Above 2% it becomes camphoraceous and dominates.
- **Ethyl 2-Methylbutyrate 0.1%**: Ultra-trace. If you don't have the 0.1% dilution prepared, skip it — the Hexyl Acetate + Damascone Beta + Ambrettolide will approximate the pear effect. DO NOT dose neat — it will obliterate everything.
- **Patchouli at 30 µL**: Now structural, not cocoa-primary. With Cocoa Absolute carrying the true cocoa note, patchouli's role is earthy-woody support + residual cocoa synergy. Reduced from 60 µL original.
- **Cocoa Absolute 10% at 60 µL**: 6 µL active = 0.14% of concentrate. The real pyrazine source that enables the iris-cocoa cross-receptor enhancement the reverse engineering doc mapped. Patchouli + vanillin + coumarin was a ~60% approximation; this is the real thing.
- **Musk Ketone 10%**: Pre-diluted from powder. If using pure powder, weigh ~6 mg and dissolve in DPG first.
- **Maturation**: 4 weeks minimum. The ionones + coumarin + musks require integration. Testing before 2 weeks: the iris will dominate and the cocoa-vanilla won't have bloomed.

## Material Rejections

- **No Galaxolide** — laundry-detergent clean kills the sensuality. Romandolide + EB + Ambrettolide + Exaltolide = luxury musk chord.
- **No Calone / marine** — DHI is NOT an aquatic. Zero marine materials.
- **No citrus beyond bergamot** — the limonene on the label comes from bergamot and lavender, not from orange oil. DHI has no noticeable citrus opening.
- **No Iso E Super overdose** — present at 350 µL for the molecular scaffold, not at 800+ µL. The ionones are the abstraction, not the Iso E.
- **No rose absolute** — the rose register is PEA-only, for radiance, not character. Rose de Mai would shift the iris toward floral-soliflore.
