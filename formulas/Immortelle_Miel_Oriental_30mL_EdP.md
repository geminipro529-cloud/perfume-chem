# Immortelle Miel Oriental — 30mL EdP

## Character
A honeyed floriental where Immortelle Absolute's maple-curry warmth is amplified by a rich oriental base (Siam Benzoin, Labdanum, Vanillin, Coumarin) and softened by a luxurious floral heart (Rose de Mai, Jasmine Sambac, Ylang). Gamma Decalactone creates a lactonic peach-skin bridge between the florals and the immortelle, while Ethyl Maltol at trace adds a caramelized-sugar shimmer. Musk Ketone powder-echo reinforces the immortelle's honeyed character without introducing synthetic-clean laundry tones. Think a less indolic *L'Instant de Guerlain* with immortelle replacing the magnolia — warm, enveloping, golden-hour floriental.

**Family**: Floriental — floral heart (rose-jasmine-ylang) over an oriental base (benzoin-labdanum-vanilla), with immortelle as the honey-maple character anchor

## Material Justification

- **Immortelle Absolute (10%)**: The honey-maple character anchor — 180 µL of 10% = 18 µL active (0.3% of concentrate). At modifier level, it defines the scent's identity without overwhelming the floral heart. Its natural synergies (Coumarin, Labdanum, Hedione, Patchouli) are all present, creating a cohesive immortelle accord rather than an isolated note. Kept at modifier dose — floral roses and jasmines would be buried at higher immortelle levels since immortelle's ODT is extremely low (1.0 ppb air / 0.01 ppm ethanol), making it perceptually dominant even at trace.
- **Rose de Mai Absolute (10%) + Jasmine Sambac (10%) + Ylang Comoros Complete EO**: The opulent floral heart. Rose de Mai at 30 µL active provides the honeyed-rose character that mirrors immortelle's own honey facet — they amplify each other rather than competing. Jasmine Sambac at 20 µL active adds indolic warmth and narcotic depth. Ylang at 40 µL neat brings tropical banana-custard opulence — the classic floriental floral triad. Rejected: Tuberose Absolute (too creamy-camphoraceous, would fight immortelle's herbal edge), Champaca (too tea-like, would pull toward chypre).
- **Hedione HC (600 µL) + Hedione (300 µL)**: Radiance amplifier pair at high dose. Hedione's known synergy with immortelle expands the curry-honey character outward while simultaneously lifting the rose-jasmine heart. High-cis HC variant for faster floral lift; standard Hedione for sustained projection. 900 µL total Hedione load is appropriate for a floriental — the genre demands projection, and Hedione is the most volume-efficient material for it.
- **Gamma Decalactone (60 µL)**: Lactonic peach-skin bridge. Creates creamy-fruity adhesion between the rose-jasmine florals and the honey-maple immortelle. Without it, the floral heart and immortelle would read as adjacent layers rather than an integrated accord. Chosen at 60 µL — enough to be perceptible as a bridge without reading as a "peach" note. Rejected: Gamma Undecalactone (too coconutty, would exoticize the floriental toward tropical).
- **Damascone Beta (10%) + Damascenone (1%)**: Rose-ketone reinforcement. Damascone Beta at 8 µL active adds metallic-rose depth that enriches the Rose de Mai. Damascenone at 0.3 µL active provides the dried-fruit-plum undertone that bridges the rose to the oriental base. Both at trace — rose ketones are powerful and would dominate if overdosed.
- **Siam Benzoin (50% in DPG) + Labdanum Resinoid (10%)**: The oriental foundation. Benzoin at 175 µL active provides the classic sweet-balsamic-vanillic warmth — the "golden glow" of orientals. Labdanum at 18 µL active adds resinous-amber depth with its leather-amber complexity. Together they create the warm, enveloping base that defines the floriental genre. Benzoin chosen at 50% over Sumatra 10% for more body at lower volume.
- **Vanillin (10%) + Coumarin (20%) + Ethyl Maltol (10%)**: Sweetness gradient. Vanillin at 20 µL active provides the classic vanilla warmth. Coumarin at 20 µL active adds hay-sweet tonka character that pairs with immortelle (a known synergy). Ethyl Maltol at 4 µL active adds a trace caramelized-sugar sparkle that makes the immortelle's maple facet glow — kept at trace because Ethyl Maltol at perceptible levels turns florientals into gourmands. The gradient is vanilla (deep) → coumarin (mid) → ethyl maltol (sparkle).
- **Patchouli EO (120 µL) + Evernyl (60 µL)**: Earthy-structural anchors. Patchouli provides the dark-earth register that prevents the floriental from floating away into candy territory. Evernyl adds the mossy-bright dimension of a chypre — not enough to make this a chypre-floriental, but enough to give the base lift and prevent benzoin-vanilla from becoming cloying.
- **Sandalwood FO (200 µL) + Sandalore (60 µL)**: Sandalwood bed. Sandalwood FO provides warm-opaque sandalwood volume — chosen over Javanol because this formula needs warmth and creaminess, not dry-mineral precision. Sandalore adds the lighter, citrusy-woody facet that keeps the sandalwood from becoming heavy. Together they create a rounded sandalwood bed that supports the floral-oriental weight above it.
- **Iso E Super (600 µL) + Cashmeran (80 µL) + Cedarwood EO (100 µL)**: Woody backbone triad. Iso E Super provides the molecular cocoon — volume and skin-adhesion without identifiable wood character. Cashmeran adds textile-warmth that aligns with the oriental character. Cedarwood EO provides a natural-wood anchor so the backbone has identity beyond abstract molecules.
- **Vertofix (150 µL)**: Woody-musky bridge fixative — glues the wood layer to the musk layer. Essential in florientals where the transition from floral to wood to musk can feel segmented without a bridging material.
- **Ethylene Brassylate (250 µL)**: Depth musk — creamy lactonic skin-sit. The primary musk anchor. Its lactonic quality deepens the benzoin-vanilla warmth and pairs naturally with the Gamma Decalactone lactonic bridge. Higher dose than usual (250 vs typical 150-200) because florientals need musk muscle to hold the floral volume.
- **Habanolide (100 µL)**: Skin-warmth musk — warm, intimate, macrocyclic. Chosen specifically over Romandolide (too outward-projective for an intimate floriental) and over Ambrettolide (too naturalistic-fruity, would compete with the Gamma Decalactone peach bridge). Habanolide's warm-skin intimacy is the right register for a floriental where the base should embrace, not project. Combined with EB for a two-axis depth-warmth musk.
- **Musk Ketone (10% in DPG)**: Character-echo musk — 120 µL of 10% = 12 µL active. The ONLY musk that directly reinforces immortelle's honeyed-powder character. Nitro-musk powder resonates with immortelle's maple-sweetness, creating a character echo that persists into the drydown long after the immortelle heart note has faded. This is the strategic musk choice that makes the formula "about" immortelle rather than just "containing" it.
- **Ambrofix (30% w/v) + Amberwood F (80 µL)**: Clean amber layer. Ambrofix at 24 µL active provides crystalline-mineral sparkle — prevents the oriental base from becoming muddy. Amberwood F adds invisible transparent warmth — the most "undetectable" amber, useful here because this formula already has plenty of identifiable character from immortelle, benzoin, and the florals. Chosen over Azarbre (too cedar-forward) and over Ambrox Super 30% (depleted).
- **Orivone (50 µL)**: Buttery orris warmth. Adds a subtle iris-butter undertone that enriches the oriental base without introducing violet-powder (which would clash with Musk Ketone's powder). Functions as a "texture enhancer" — makes the benzoin-vanilla base feel more luxurious and expensive.
- **Bergamot FCF (400 µL)**: Classic floriental top. Bergamot's hesperidic-lavender freshness creates the bright opening that contrasts with the warm base — the essential tension of florientals. Chosen over Cedrat (too sharp for this warm composition), Blood Orange (too juicy, would tilt fruity-floral), and Red Mandarin (too gourmand-adjacent).
- **Cardamom EO (30 µL) + Cinnamaldehyde (5 µL)**: Aromatic-spice trace top. Cardamom provides green-spicy lift that brightens the bergamot opening and foreshadows the oriental warmth. Cinnamaldehyde at 5 µL neat is a whisper of cinnamon — not enough to read as "spice," just enough to add warmth to the top note. Both at trace doses to avoid dominating the floral heart.
- **Rhodinol ex Citronella (80 µL) + Geraniol (50 µL) + Phenethyl Alcohol (100 µL)**: Rose accord reinforcement. Rhodinol provides the natural geranium-rose heart richness that amplifies the Rose de Mai. Geraniol adds the fresh-rose alcohol character. PEA provides the watery-rose freshness that prevents the rose from becoming jammy. Together they create a multi-faceted rose presence that extends beyond the Rose de Mai absolute alone.
- **Alpha Ionone (60 µL) + Dihydrojasmone (40 µL)**: Heart connectors. Alpha Ionone adds violet-berry sparkle that lifts the floral heart and bridges to the orris-butter of Orivone in the base. Dihydrojasmone provides fresh-jasmine warmth that connects Hedione to the Jasmine Sambac — fills the gap between radiance amplifier and natural absolute.
