# Opus V Iris Crystal - 2400uL Prep

**Purpose:** exact prep sheet for the `2.40 mL` `Opus V Iris Crystal` needed in `Aperture Iris No Jasmine FO`.

This is a straight `2.4x` scale-up of the `1000uL` crystal module.

---

| # | Material | Dilution | Amount (uL) |
|---|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Alpha Irone | 10% | 696 |

---

## Mixing Order

1. Add the iris base materials first: `Orivone`, `Methyl Ionone Pure`, `Orris F-TEC`, `I-IRIS F-TEC`, `Allyl Ionone`, `Dihydro Beta Ionone`, `Irotyl`, `Ultralia`.
2. Add `Alpha Ionone`, `Beta Ionone`, then `Alpha Irone (10%)`.
3. Add `Bacdanol`.
4. Add `Hedione`.
5. Add `Carrot Seed EO`.
6. Add `Rose Oxide (1%)` last.
7. Rest `7 days` minimum, `14 days` preferred.

---

## Why This Prep Matters

This is the exact crystal center used in the Aperture formula. Making it as its own batch keeps the iris core reusable across other formulas without forcing you to remake the full 30mL bottle each time.
