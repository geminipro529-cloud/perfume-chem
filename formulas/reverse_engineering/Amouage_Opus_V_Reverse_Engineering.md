## 1. Olfactive Profile Analysis

Opus V is **not** a Dior Homme–style powdery-sharp iris. It's the other school of iris:
**round, lipid, buttery, almost edible** — closer to smelling actual orris butter dissolved
in warm amber. The key differences vs. other iris benchmarks:

| Aspect | Dior Homme Style | **Opus V Style** |
|--------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Iris character | Powdery, mineral, cold | **Buttery, creamy, warm** |
| Dominant ionone | Methyl Ionone (sharp) | **Alpha Irone (lipid)** |
| Texture | Chalky, dry powder | **Suede, soft leather** |
| Base support | Vetiver, labdanum | **Amber, sandalwood, musk** |
| Violet role | Structural | **Prominent, warm** |
| Sweetness | Low | **Medium — balsamic/vanillic** |

### What makes Opus V unique:
1. **Alpha Irone dominance** — gives the authentic orris butter character (carrot-y, waxy, lipid)
2. **Suede envelope** — a soft, non-animalic leather quality wrapping the iris
3. **Amber bed** — warm, resinous base that makes the iris feel expensive and deep
4. **Violet bloom** — Beta Ionone and Alpha Ionone add a true violet dimension beyond iris
5. **Low powder** — Methyl Ionone and AIMI are supporting, not leading
6. **Musk transparency** — clean macrocyclics letting the iris speak

## 4. Theory Notes

### Why this works (Carles Method):
- **Top:Heart:Base = 11:45:44** — heart-dominant pyramid, biased toward base.
  This is correct for iris: iris needs a substantial base to anchor it, since ionones
  are notoriously volatile on their own. The amber-suede-musk bed gives longevity.

### Why this works (Roudnitska Roles):
- **Noblesse:** Alpha Irone — the expensive, noble note providing character
- **Éclat:** Hedione + Alpha Ionone — radiance and sparkle in the opening
- **Fond:** Ambrox + Galaxolide + Iso E Super — diffusion and persistence

### Why this works (Jellinek Map):
- Opus V sits in **lower-left quadrant** (erogenic/narcotic) — warm, skin-close,
  intimate. The amber and musk push it there. The iris prevents it from being purely
  oriental by adding the cool/powder counter-tension.

### Suede Effect (Critical):
Suederal at 0.4% active is specifically calibrated:
- Too much → overwhelms iris with leather
- Too little → loses the Opus V signature suede envelope
- At 0.4% → barely perceived consciously, but your brain reads "suede gloves" subliminally
- Cashmeran reinforces this with its fuzzy warmth

### The Violet Bridge:
Alpha Ionone (top) → Beta Ionone (heart) → Dihydro Beta Ionone (base)  
This creates a **violet cascade** down the pyramid — the violet character persists
from first spray to drydown, evolving from fruity to warm to woody.

## 5. Mixing Instructions

### Step 1 — Mix the Iris Accord (Separately)
Combine the 12 materials from Section 2 into a concentrate. Label it "Opus V Iris Accord."
Let it sit 48 hours minimum before using.

### Step 2 — Build the Full Formula
Either use the full formula from Section 3 directly, or:
- Mix 45% Iris Accord + 55% supporting materials
- This way you can adjust iris intensity by varying the accord dose

### Step 3 — Dilution
- For EDP: 20% concentrate in ethanol 96%
- For intense EDP: 25% concentrate
- For parfum: 30% concentrate

### Step 4 — Maceration
**Minimum 2 weeks.** Ionones are notorious for needing time — they start harsh
and chemical, then bloom into rich violet-iris. Alpha Irone in particular transforms
dramatically with maceration. Judge no earlier than day 14.

### Adjustment Guide:

| If it smells... | Adjust |
|----------------|--------|
| Too powdery | Reduce AIMI to 3%, add 2% more Beta Ionone |
| Not buttery enough | Add 2% more Alpha Irone (10%), reduce Methyl Ionone by 2% |
| Too sweet | Reduce Vanillin to 1%, Heliotropin to 1.5% |
| Suede too strong | Reduce Suederal to 2.5% |
| No suede at all | Increase Suederal to 5.5% |
| Weak projection | Add 1% more Hedione, 1% more Romandolide |
| Too sharp/metallic | Reduce Orivone to 0.5% |
| Lacks depth | Add 1% more Ambrox Super, 0.5% more Labdanum |
| Opening too thin | Add 0.5% Aldehyde C10 (1%) — waxy aldehyde lift |

### To Complete the Full Opus V Experience, You'd Need:
1. **Oud synthetic** — Georgywood, Oud Synth, or similar (this is the biggest gap)
2. **Rose materials** — Citronellol, Geraniol, Phenylethyl Alcohol, Rose Oxide
3. **Civet/animalic** — Civetone, Indole, or Castoreum substitute
4. **Rum accord** — Ethyl Maltol, Benzyl Alcohol, Rum Ether

### Current Formula Validity:
The iris accord (Section 2) and iris-focused full formula (Section 3) are **valid as
an iris interpretation** — capturing what Cavallier does in the first 10 minutes.
But the actual Opus V experience is iris → oud → leather → wood, a full journey
that our current inventory can only partially replicate.
