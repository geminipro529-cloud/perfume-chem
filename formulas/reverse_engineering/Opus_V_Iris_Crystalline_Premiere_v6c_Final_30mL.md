## Design Philosophy

A **clear crystal EDP** that frames the Opus V iris luxury block. The composition reads as transparent architecture — refractive, mineral, luminous — with the six-ionone iris stack at its core radiating through a Hedione HC radiance amplifier and salicylate diffusion film.

**v6c Optimization:** Single surgical swap from v5 — Violet Fleuressence (30 µL) → Paradisamide (30 µL). This adds one RICH material (5 strong character dimensions) with a unique dominant character ("tropical") not duplicated elsewhere in the formula. At 0.4% of concentrate, Paradisamide reads as subliminal cassis-rhubarb crystalline tartness, not as tropical fruit. This technique bridges the iris-green mineral facets and adds perceived complexity without shifting the olfactive identity.

**Hedione HC Rebalance:** The original v6c had Hedione HC at 1400 µL (18.7% of concentrate). Hedione HC is the high-cis variant (~75% cis-isomer) — 2–3× more potent than standard Hedione. At 1400 µL it drowns the iris in jasmine radiance, defeating the purpose of an iris showcase. Cut to 350 µL (4.7%), equivalent to ~10–14% standard Hedione dosing, and poured the freed 1050 µL back into the iris ionone core. Alpha Irone goes from 255 → 450 µL active (6.0% of concentrate). The iris is now the undeniable star.

**Result:** Geometric total identical (80.7), longevity +2.9, sillage +1.4, skin performance +1.0. The iris has breathing room.

**Dual-Hedione Addition:** Standard Hedione (racemic, ~35% cis) added at 400 µL alongside the 350 µL Hedione HC. This is standard fine fragrance practice — HC provides concentrated radiance punch via the high-cis isomer; standard Hedione fills the "transparent air" around the iris crystal with broad-spectrum jasmine-clean lift. Combined Hedione equivalent: ~1275 µL standard dose (17% of concentrate) — firmly in Ellena-territory transparency dosing. Volume sourced from opacity-adding materials: Iso E Super −200 µL (still 13.3%), Ethylene Brassylate −100 µL (reduces lactonic cream), Orivone −100 µL (less buttery warmth). Net effect: Luxury +2.6, Synergy +0.6, Texture +0.5 — at the cost of Longevity −3.8 (85.3 → 81.5). The longevity trade is a feature: a genuinely transparent crystalline iris SHOULD be lighter than a dense powdery one.

## Accord Architecture

### Layer 1 — Crystal Aperture (Top, 600 µL = 8.0%)
Bitter-mineral citrus lift via Cedrat FCF (NOT Bergamot — the bitter citron edge reads more crystalline than cologne-fresh). Linalool provides the faceted transparent bridge to the iris heart. Neroli EO adds natural floral-citrus complexity. Aldehyde C12 MNA and Rose Oxide at 1% dilution create the fizzy, champagne-sparkle metallic surface. Scentenal adds a thin mineral-ozone veil. Petitgrain EO contributes a green-aromatic facet with premium richness (3+ strong character dimensions).

### Layer 2 — Iris Luxury Block (Heart, 3570 µL = 47.6%)
The six-ionone iris stack spans the full iris-violet spectrum:
- **Alpha Irone (1500 µL at 30%)** — the irreplaceable iris root butter, orris absolute in a molecule. 450 µL active. The single largest component in the formula — this IS an iris perfume.
- **Alpha Isomethyl Ionone (400 µL neat)** — powdery-floral iris smoothness, the bridge between root and petal.
- **Orivone (200 µL neat)** — warm buttery iris base, reinforces the Irone's rootlike quality. Trimmed from 300 µL to increase transparency.
- **Alpha Ionone (200 µL neat)** — violet-floral facet, pairs with Beta Ionone for full violet-iris chord.
- **Dihydro Beta Ionone (200 µL neat)** — woody-powdery iris without the violet twist, adds iris depth.
- **Irotyl (120 µL neat)** — synthetic iris-woodiness, the most architectural ionone.
- **Beta Ionone (100 µL neat)** — true violet character, the purple within the iris.
- **Ultralia (40 µL neat)** — ghost iris/orris at trace level, powdery-transparent halo.
- **Allyl Ionone (30 µL neat)** — warm iris bloom, ketone V powdery roundness.

**Dual-Hedione radiance system:**
- **Hedione HC (350 µL neat)** — high-cis variant (~75% cis-isomer), 2–3× more potent than standard. The concentrated radiance punch — makes the iris facets project. At 4.7% of concentrate.
- **Hedione (400 µL neat)** — standard racemic methyl dihydrojasmonate (~35% cis). The broad-spectrum "transparent air" material. Fills the composition with clean, luminous volume that the iris radiates through. At 5.3% of concentrate.
- Combined: 350 HC + 400 std ≈ 1275 µL standard equivalent (17% of concentrate). This dual-source approach is standard fine-fragrance practice: HC for quality, standard for quantity. The iris sits inside a Hedione atmosphere that makes it glow without drowning it.

**Crystalline bridge:** Paradisamide (30 µL neat) — subliminal cassis-rhubarb tartness at trace level. 150-hour tenacity means this thin cassis thread persists through the entire evolution, adding a subliminal blackcurrant-grapefruit facet that reads as crystalline mineral complexity, not as fruit. Bridges the mineral top (Cedrat/Scentenal) to the powdery iris core. This is the v6c luxury-boosting addition: 5 strong character dimensions (tropical:8, fruity:7, citrus:6, green:5, freshness:5) + unique "tropical" dominant = +3.8 luxury score over v5.

### Layer 3 — Crystal Base Architecture (Base, 3330 µL = 44.4%)
**Molecular cocoon:** Iso E Super (1000 µL neat) — the abstract-cedar molecular skin effect. Creates the "invisible expensive" quality where the iris seems to resonate directly on skin. At 13.3% of concentrate, still a generous dose. Trimmed from 1200 µL to source volume for standard Hedione — transparency > opacity.

**Musk chord (3-axis architecture):**
- **Romandolide (450 µL, projection axis)** — the most diffusive clean musk, extends sillage architecture outward. Chosen over Habanolide as primary musk because the brief demands PROJECTION, not intimacy.
- **Ethylene Brassylate (300 µL, depth axis)** — lactonic skin-adhesion musk, provides the creamy bed the iris sits on. Trimmed from 400 µL to reduce lactonic opacity, improving crystal transparency.
- **Habanolide (100 µL, warmth fill)** — warm macrocyclic musk as a bridge between Romandolide's projection and EB's depth.
- **Musk Ketone (50 µL at 10%, character-echo axis)** — powdery nitro musk directly reinforces the iris powder register. The ONLY musk that reads as "talcum" — it echoes Alpha Irone's powderiness.

**Transparent fixation gradient:**
- **Hexyl Salicylate (400 µL neat)** — light, green-floral transparent salicylate film. Chosen over Benzyl Salicylate because the brief demands SHEER crystal framing, not cosmetic-heavy diffusion.
- **Benzyl Benzoate (80 µL neat)** — near-odorless molecular weight anchor beneath the Hexyl Sal.

**Amber register:** Ambrox Super (200 µL at 30% = 60 µL active) — crystalline-mineral amber. Chosen as mineral-transparent, not warm-rounded (that would be Ambermax). Reinforces the mineral crystal concept. Amberwood F (100 µL neat) — the most "invisible" amber. Adds warmth without adding a note. Clean amber-wood transparency.

**Wood structure:**
- **Clearwood (270 µL neat)** — clean-earthy-woody, modern patchouli replacement. Adds earth without darkness.
- **Norlimbanol Dextro (80 µL neat)** — extreme-tenacity structural pillar. At this level, it's felt, not smelled — adds woody-amber backbone that outlasts everything else.
- **Cashmeran (100 µL at 20% = 20 µL active)** — textile warmth, the "cashmere wrap" around the crystal structure. Keeps the composition from reading clinical.

**Sandalwood chord:**
- **Ebanol (90 µL neat, creamy-soft register)** — creamy, milky-soft silk sandalwood. Provides the yielding softness beneath the crystal surface.
- **Javanol (80 µL neat, dry-intimate register)** — precise, mineral-intimate sandalwood. Provides skin-scent intimacy. Together with Ebanol: dry+soft = full sandalwood spectrum.

**Patchouli EO (30 µL neat)** — earthy-woody natural anchor. At trace level, adds natural complexity and fixative depth.

## Key Material Justifications

### Why Cedrat FCF and NOT Bergamot FCF?
Cedrat's bitter-citron mineral edge reads crystalline. Bergamot's cologne-fresh character would soften the crystal concept toward a more classical opening. The 8 alternatives were evaluated; Cedrat is the most architectural citrus in the inventory.

### Why Hexyl Salicylate and NOT Benzyl Salicylate?
The brief demands SHEER crystal framing. Benzyl Sal adds cosmetic-heavy diffusion volume — thick, waxy — that would obscure the iris. Hexyl Sal provides invisible transparent adhesion: fixation without character, exactly what a crystal frame needs.

### Why Romandolide as primary musk (not Habanolide)?
**Projection axis:** Romandolide is the most diffusive clean musk in the inventory. The brief demands good sillage — Romandolide extends the iris radiance outward. Habanolide at 100 µL provides warm fill, not the structural projection. Ethylene Brassylate provides the lactonic depth layer.

### Why Iso E Super and NOT Timberol or Cedarwood EO?
**Molecular-skin register:** Iso E is abstract-cedar that makes the fragrance resonate ON skin. The "what are you wearing?" effect. Timberol is too architectural/rigid for a transparent iris. Cedarwood EO adds identifiable pencil-shaving cedar that would compete with the iris. Iso E is invisible luxury.

### Why Ambrox Super and NOT Ambermax or Amberwood F?
**Crystalline-mineral register:** Ambrox at 30% creates crystalline mineral depth — the coldest amber. Ambermax would add warm, cozy roundness that works against crystal clarity. Amberwood F is also used but in a different register (transparent invisible warmth vs Ambrox's mineral architecture).

### Why Paradisamide at trace (30 µL)?
At 0.4% of concentrate, Paradisamide is below its typical 0.1–1% dosing sweet spot. At this level:
- It does NOT read as tropical or fruity
- It adds a subliminal cassis-rhubarb tartness that bridges the bitter Cedrat top → the powdery iris heart
- Its 150-hour tenacity means this thin thread persists through the entire dry-down
- It is the ONLY material in the inventory that is both RICH (5 strong dimensions: tropical:8, fruity:7, citrus:6, green:5, freshness:5) AND has a unique dominant character not duplicated by any other formula material
- Luxury impact: +2.2 (richness) + 1.6 (distinctiveness) = +3.8 points
