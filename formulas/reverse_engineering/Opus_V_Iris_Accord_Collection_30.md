## Opus V Accord Positioning

- `Star` formulas use roughly `470-560 µL` of `Opus V Iris Accord` per 10 mL bottle.
- `Sub-Star` formulas use roughly `380-430 µL`.
- `Layering` formulas use roughly `280-320 µL` so they can still function as standalones without crushing other scents.
