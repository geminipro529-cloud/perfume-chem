### Short Practical Version

1. Add the heavy iris body, steps `1-8`.
2. Add the ionone/irone section, steps `9-11`.
3. Add `Bacdanol`, then `Hedione`.
4. Add `Carrot Seed EO`.
5. Add `Rose Oxide (1%)` last.
6. Cap and shake or stir thoroughly.
7. Rest `7 days` minimum. Better at `14 days`.

### What You Should Smell During Build

- after step `8`: dense violet-orris body
- after step `11`: the real iris identity should become much clearer
- after step `13`: more air and diffusion
- after step `15`: a cold metallic edge on top of the iris body

## Use

Use the full `2400 uL` batch directly in the `Aperture Iris` formula.

This is the right size if you only want to make:
- one `30 mL` bottle
- no leftover crystal stock
- no overproduction of the iris module

If you want to scale later, multiply every line proportionally.
