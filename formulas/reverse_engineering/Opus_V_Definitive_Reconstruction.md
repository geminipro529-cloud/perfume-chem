## 1. Architectural Improvements Over v2

| Issue in v2 | Correction in v3 | Chemical Rationale |
|-------------|-------------------|--------------------|
| **No musk bed** | Added 4-musk foundation (175 µL, 7.0%) | v1 analysis identified 15% musks in real Opus V. Musks provide the "skin cocoon" that makes Amouage drydowns legendary. Zero musks = skeletal drydown. |
| **No Methyl Ionone** in iris core | Added Methyl Ionone Pure (100 µL, 4.0%) | Methyl Ionone is the BODY of synthetic orris — powdery-woody ionone that fills the gap between Alpha Irone's sparkle and Orivone's butter. Essential for orris realism. |
| **No Clearwood** | Added modern transparent woody base (50 µL, 2.0%) | Clearwood (Patchoulol-rich, low patchouli odor) provides modern transparent woody structure that Iso E Super alone cannot. |
| **No Suederal** | Added suede leather dimension (20 µL, 0.8%) | Opus V's "dirty leather" drydown requires a suede material alongside IBQ's dirty-quinoline character. Suederal provides the clean-leather substrate. |
| **No Styrax** | Added Styrax FTEC for smoky balsamic (not selected — moved to adjustment knob) | Opted to keep the smokiness from Guaiacol alone to prevent the base from skewing too smoky. Styrax available as "darkening knob." |
| **Minimal Rose Oxide** | Added Rose Oxide (1%) at 12 µL | Metallic-green-rosy lift that real orris butter contains. Creates the "platinum shavings" effect Angela/NST described. |
| **No Heliotropin** | Added Heliotropin Fleuressence (15 µL, 0.6%) | Powdery-warm almond-vanilla modifier that bridges iris heart into sweet base — a Cavallier technique material. |
| **No AIMI** | Added Alpha-Isomethyl Ionone (15 µL, 0.6%) | Extends the ionone field dimensionality. AIMI provides a floral-powdery violet that Alpha Ionone alone lacks. |
| **No Bacdanol/Vetival** in base | Added both | Creamy sandalwood (Bacdanol 25 µL) + suede-vetiver dryness (Vetival 20 µL) give the base textural depth. |

Based on exhaustive review analysis (NST Angela, CaFleureBon, Reddit communities):

## 6. Temporal Architecture — How This Formula Evolves

### Act I: Lush Iris (0–10 min)
**Dominant headspace:** Alpha Ionone, citronellol, Eugenol, Rose Oxide  
**Character:** Metallic, rosy-violet iris PLUS warm boozy rhum  
**What you smell:** Lush green-violet sparkle with boozy dark-sugar warmth  
**Why:** Alpha Ionone and Rose Oxide have the highest VP among iris materials → project immediately. Eugenol and Ethyl Vanillin flash bright. Hedione amplifies everything into a radiant cloud.

### Act II: Oud Takeover (10 min–2 hr)  
**Dominant headspace:** Alpha Irone, Orivone, Methyl Ionone, Guaiacol fading, Jasmine FO  
**Character:** Buttery-powdery iris with smoke and jasmine dimensionality  
**What you smell:** The "real" orris emerges — warm, buttery, powdery, with dark wood smoke seeping through  
**Why:** Low-VP iris materials (Alpha Irone, Orivone, Methyl Ionone) reach perceptual dominance as top notes evaporate. Guaiacol still present as smoky whisper. Jasmine and rose provide floral contrast.

### Act III: Dirty Leather (2–5 hr)
**Dominant headspace:** Iso E Super, Patchouli, IBQ, Vertofix, Evernyl  
**Character:** Abstract cedar wrapping dark leather and iris ghost  
**What you smell:** "Delicious dirty leather, old wood of the barn" (Angela/NST)  
**Why:** Iso E Super's molecular cocoon takes over. IBQ's animalic leather emerges. Evernyl's dry oakmoss joins. The iris becomes a ghost echo within the woody frame.

### Act IV: Woods Symphony (5+ hr)
**Dominant headspace:** Ambrox Super, musks (Galaxolide, Habanolide, EB), Benzyl Salicylate, Clearwood  
**Character:** Crystalline amber-musk-wood skin scent  
**What you smell:** Intimate woody-amber cocoon with iris memory, worn very close to skin  
**Why:** Ultra-low VP/ultra-low ODT materials dominate. Ambrox at 0.003 ppb threshold remains perceptible when everything else has faded. Musks provide the velvet cushion. The iris is a memory carried in the amber-musk matrix.
