### AIMI Position Validated

**AIMI listed LAST among 11 allergens.** This proves our entire design philosophy was correct:
- Opus V is **irone-forward**, not AIMI-forward
- AIMI is a supporting powder, not the star
- The buttery-lipid iris character comes from irone + orivone + myristic acid context
- The Dior Homme school (AIMI-forward) is the WRONG approach for Opus V

### Hydroxycitronellal — The Missing Link

This was our biggest blind spot. Every reviewer who described Opus V's iris as "juicy" or "lush" was partly sensing the **hydroxycitronellal transparency modifier**. It adds:
- Fresh, dewy, slightly watery transparency
- Cosmetic-clean muguet facet (NOT a lily note at this dose — a texture)
- Bridges the heavy orris into breathable, 3D space
- Similar role to Hedione but on a different axis: Hedione = jasmine radiance; Hydroxycitronellal = muguet dewiness

### Benzyl Salicylate — The Skeleton

Listed FIRST = highest concentration allergen. This means Benzyl Salicylate is not just a "diffusion cushion" — it's a **major structural component** at probably 4–8% of the concentrate. It provides:
- Diffusive cosmetic volume (the "invisible wind" beneath the iris)
- Fixative properties extending the iris heart
- The smooth, cosmetic, slightly sweet floral body that makes Amouage compositions feel expansive
- **When you restock Benzyl Salicylate, replace the Hexyl Salicylate with 150 µL Benzyl Salicylate**
