## D. ODT Ratio Validation & Scale Independence

### D1. The Iris DNA Ratios Are Batch-Size Independent

The internal ratios (8 : 4 : 3 : 2.5 : 2.5 : 3 : 2) define **percentage composition**, not absolute amounts. When you change batch size:

| Batch Size | Concentrate (20%) | Iris DNA (50% conc.) | µL per part (÷25) | Internal Ratios | ODT on skin |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| 5 mL | 1,000 µL | 500 µL | 20 µL | 8:4:3:2.5:2.5:3:2 | Identical |
| 10 mL | 2,000 µL | 1,000 µL | 40 µL | 8:4:3:2.5:2.5:3:2 | Identical |
| 30 mL | 6,000 µL | 3,000 µL | 120 µL | 8:4:3:2.5:2.5:3:2 | Identical |
| 50 mL | 10,000 µL | 5,000 µL | 200 µL | 8:4:3:2.5:2.5:3:2 | Identical |
| 100 mL | 20,000 µL | 10,000 µL | 400 µL | 8:4:3:2.5:2.5:3:2 | Identical |

**Why identical?** Because ODT on skin depends on:
1. **EDP concentration** (20%, fixed across all sizes)  
2. **Internal ratios** (8:4:3:..., fixed by design)
3. **Spray volume** (≈0.1 mL per spray, independent of bottle size)

Changing from 10mL to 100mL changes the *number of sprays available*, not the *iris perception per spray*.

### D2. Cross-Receptor Additivity Model

The xlsx data reveals that at accord level (25 parts in ~45 total), each individual iris molecule sits at approximately **63% of its individual ODT** (VP/ODT ratio ≈ 0.63). This means:

- **No single molecule is above its perception threshold alone**
- **The 7-molecule stack creates supra-threshold iris through cross-receptor additivity**
- **This is "phantom iris"** — you smell iris but no single molecule is responsible

The additivity works because the 7 molecules span 3 distinct olfactory receptors:
- **OR5A1** — activated by Alpha-ISO, Alpha Ionone, Beta Ionone, DHBI (4/7 molecules)
- **OR5A2** — activated by Alpha Ionone, Orivone, Ultralia (3/7 molecules, with Alpha Ionone bridging both)
- **OR7A5** — activated by Koavone alone (anti-anosmia, prevents receptor fatigue)

This 3-receptor architecture means that even if OR5A1 adapts (fatigues), iris perception continues through OR5A2 and OR7A5. The perfume maintains iris identity for 8+ hours because receptor fatigue never hits all 3 simultaneously.

## Appendix: Mixing Protocol

### Order of Addition

1. **Myristic Acid first.** Weigh 0.12g into a clean glass beaker. Add ~5mL warm ethanol (40°C) and stir until dissolved. Set aside.
2. **Iris DNA Core.** In the main mixing vessel (30mL atomizer or graduated cylinder), add materials #1–7 in order. These are all neat materials; pipette accuracy is critical at 240–960µL.
3. **Crystalline Frame.** Add Helional, Cyclamen Aldehyde, DHM (and any formula-specific character materials like Scentenal, Parmavert, etc.)
4. **Alpha Irone 30%.** Add with precision — this is a 30% dilution; the carrot-like raw smell will seem wrong until maceration integrates it.
5. **Support materials.** Add Hedione (bulk), then Hedione HC (top-up), Hexyl Salicylate, musks, Javanol, Ambrox Super, and any remaining support materials.
6. **Norlimbanol Dextro** (if present). Add last among concentrates — it's very powerful. 30µL only.
7. **Add dissolved Myristic Acid** ethanol solution from step 1.
8. **Top up with remaining ethanol** to reach 30.00 mL total.

### Storage

- **Dark glass.** UV degrades ionones (especially Alpha Ionone and Beta Ionone). Amber or cobalt blue glass atomizers. Never clear glass for storage.
- **Cool, dark place.** 15–20°C optimal. Avoid temperature swings.
- **Shake gently** once daily for first week to distribute Myristic Acid depot molecules.
- **Do not refrigerate.** Myristic Acid (mp 54°C) will crystallize out of solution at low temperatures. If this happens, warm to 40°C and shake to re-dissolve.
