## Design Intent

This is the **Opus V iris/orris DNA** captured in a clean, reusable concentrate. Not a stripped-down generic iris — the actual Opus V double-orris signature, crystallized.

**What "crystallized" means here:**
- Clean, transparent, polished — like a faceted crystal
- No dark ballast (no Vetival, Ambrox Super, Coumarin, Heliotropin) — those belong in the destination perfume
- Every material serves the iris identity or makes the accord breathe
- Modular at 8–25% of concentrate in destination formulas

**What stays from real Opus V:**
- Buttery irone-led orris with metallic-cool concrete edge
- 8-ionone dimensional field (not just Alpha Irone + generic ionones)
- Waxy-earthy root character from Orris F-TEC + Carrot Seed carotol
- Rose Oxide "platinum shavings" metallic sparkle
- The specific Opus V ratio architecture between irone, Orivone, and Methyl Ionone

## v1 → v2 Optimizations (Engine-Validated)

| Parameter | v1 Crystal | v2 Crystal | Rationale |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| **Orivone** | 50 µL (5.0%) | 100 µL (10.0%) | **Doubled.** v3 analysis proved Orivone is critical Opus V identity material — 5% was too low. Buttery-metallic orris body must be prominent, not subordinate. |
| **Beta Ionone** | 45 µL (4.5%) | 35 µL (3.5%) | **Reduced.** Temporal engine returned OAV 4390 at 1.8% of total concentrate. At 4.5% in a standalone accord, Beta Ionone overwhelms the irone character. 3.5% preserves dark violet depth without ionic bulldozing. |
| **AIMI** | absent | 15 µL (1.5%) | **Added.** v3 research identified AIMI as essential for ionone field dimensionality. Creates a floral-powdery violet facet neither Alpha Ionone nor Beta Ionone can provide. |
| **Hedione** | 170 µL (17.0%) | 120 µL (12.0%) | **Reduced ~29%.** When this accord enters a destination formula at 15% of concentrate, Hedione contributes ~1.8% — the formula adds its own radiance. 17% made the accord too Hedione-forward. |
| **Orris F-TEC** | 85 µL (8.5%) | 95 µL (9.5%) | **Increased.** More waxy-earthy orris concrete mass. This is what makes the accord read as "orris concrete" rather than "violet powder." |
| **Methyl Ionone** | 120 µL (12.0%) | 110 µL (11.0%) | **Slight reduction.** Improves Alpha Irone : Methyl Ionone active ratio from 1:4.1 → 1:3.7. Closer to v3 reconstruction's 1:2.7 target. Still the structural backbone. |
| **DHBI** | 55 µL (5.5%) | 40 µL (4.0%) | **Reduced.** Still the strongest persistence bridge. The saved volume goes to Orivone increase. |
| **Allyl Ionone** | 15 µL (1.5%) | 20 µL (2.0%) | **Increased.** Slightly more powdery projection lift — Ketone V projects the iris outward. |
| **Carrot Seed EO** | 5 µL (0.5%) | 8 µL (0.8%) | **Increased.** More carotol earthy-root realism without muddiness. |

## What Materials Are NOT Here (And Why)

These materials exist in the Signature Rich version and Shadow Booster but are deliberately excluded from the crystal accord:

| Material | Why Excluded | Where It Belongs |
|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| Ambrox Super | Biases toward amber structure — the destination formula controls its own amber envelope | Woods/amber layer of destination formula, or Shadow Booster module |
| Vetival | Introduces suede-vetiver darkness that narrows the accord's usability | Shadow Booster, or destination formula's dark-woods layer |
| Heliotropin | Biases toward sweet/oriental powderiness — subliminal at OAV 0.49 even in full formula | Add to destination formula's sweet/balsamic layer at 10–20 µL |
| Coumarin | Pushes warm-tonka-hay direction — not universal | Destination formula's structural layer at 25–50 µL (20% dilution) |
| Iso E Super | Woody molecular cocoon belongs in the destination formula — not inside the iris module | Destination formula at 3–8% of concentrate |
| Vertofix | Woody-amber fixative character — competes with destination formula's own woody architecture | Destination formula's woody base |

## Usage Guide

### Dosing in Destination Formulas

| Destination Family | Crystal Accord % of Concentrate | Notes |
|-------------------|-------------------------------|-------|
| **Woody/amber niche** | 15–25% | The accord carries the iris identity. Build woody base (Iso E, Ambrox, Vertofix) around it. Optional: add Shadow Booster at 3–5% for darkness. |
| **Floral/musky modern** | 10–18% | The accord provides iris sophistication. Frame with clean musks, Hedione, and transparent woods. |
| **Oriental/incense** | 12–20% | The accord provides violet-powder contrast against heavy base. Add Labdanum, Benzoin, and woods separately. |
| **Transparent/mineral** | 8–14% | Lower doses keep iris as a transparent thread. Pair with Cedrat FCF, Clearwood, and light musks. |
| **Sport/bright daily** | 6–10% | Iris as a quality signal rather than a theme. Use Light version instead if this still reads too heavy. |
| **Full Opus V reconstruction** | 25–30% | Use this as the core and build all 6 remaining accord layers around it (rhum, jasmine·rose, oud·civet·leather, woods symphony, musk bed, structural). |

### Hedione Interaction

This accord already contains 12.0% Hedione. When adding Hedione in the destination formula, account for the accord's contribution:

**Example:** Building a 10mL formula at 25% concentrate (2500 µL), using 15% Crystal Accord:
- Accord contribution: 375 µL × 12.0% = 45 µL Hedione from accord
- If the destination formula targets 7.4% Hedione total: 2500 × 7.4% = 185 µL total Hedione needed
- Remaining Hedione to add: 185 − 45 = 140 µL additional Hedione

## Mixing Order

1. **Heavy iris body first:**
   `Orivone`, `Methyl Ionone Pure`, `Orris F-TEC`, `I-IRIS F-TEC`, `Dihydro Beta Ionone`, `Irotyl`, `Allyl Ionone (Ketone V)`, `Alpha-Isomethyl Ionone`, `Ultralia`.

2. **Irone-violet section:**
   `Alpha Ionone`, `Beta Ionone`, `Alpha Irone (10%)`.

3. **Root and metallic edge:**
   `Carrot Seed EO`, `Bacdanol`.

4. **Radiance amplifier:**
   `Hedione`.

5. **Metallic sparkle last:**
   `Rose Oxide (1%)`.

6. **Rest 7 days minimum. 14 days optimal.**

The accord will read sharp and disjointed for the first 3 days. By day 7 the ionone field integrates. By day 14 the Orivone buttery character fully blooms.
