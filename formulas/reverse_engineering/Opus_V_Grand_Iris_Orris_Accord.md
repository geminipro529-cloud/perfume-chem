## Design Philosophy

This accord is built from one premise: **the difference between a good iris and a great iris is the difference between AIMI-forward powder and irone-forward butter.**

Most iris accords in the hobbyist space lean on Alpha-Isomethyl Ionone (AIMI) and Methyl Ionone for body. That gives the Dior Homme school: sharp, powdery, mineral, cosmetic. Opus V does the opposite. Jacques Cavallier built the iris around the lipid-waxy character of real orris concrete — where **cis-α-irone** creates a buttery, fatty, almost edible iris that smells like warm root wax rather than cold face powder.

What makes this reconstruction compete at industry level:

1. **Irone dominance over methyl ionones** — the irone:powder ratio is inverted from Dior Homme
2. **Orivone as buttery-metallic orris body** — provides the cyclopentanol character alongside actual myristic acid for the lipid matrix
3. **Seven ionone voices creating constructive interference** — multiple OR5A1 receptor activation patterns integrate as "orris" in the brain, like RGB pixels creating white
4. **Carrot Seed EO for carotol** — the same terpene alcohol found in Iris pallida rhizome, providing the earthy-root dimension that pure ionones lack
5. **Rose Oxide at extreme trace for the cold metallic concrete edge** — what Angela at Now Smell This describes as "platinum shavings"
6. **Myristic acid (10% in warm DPG) for authentic orris butter wax** — the actual lipid matrix of real orris butter
7. **Indole at sub-threshold for narcotic root-depth** — the difference between "synthetic" and "alive"
8. **Suederal subliminal envelope** — the soft suede texture that wraps Opus V's iris
9. **Hexyl Salicylate as sheer diffusion cushion** — lighter, greener, more transparent than Benzyl Salicylate

## Why Each Material Earns Its Place

### The 5 materials that separate "industry-grade" from "hobbyist":

**1. Carrot Seed EO (20 µL)** — Most iris accords skip this. Real orris concrete is distilled from Iris pallida rhizome (aged 3+ years underground). Carrot Seed EO contains **carotol**, the same sesquiterpene alcohol found in iris root. Without it, the accord smells like "ionones assembled into iris shape." With it, the accord smells like actual root material. The earth dimension.

**2. Rose Oxide at 1% (12 µL = 0.12 µL active)** — This is borderline homeopathic dosing. At this level, Rose Oxide does NOT smell like rose and does NOT smell metallic in isolation. What it does is create a **cold metallic edge** underneath the warm iris — the "orris concrete chill" that distinguishes real orris concrete from warm, round, synthetic ionone constructions. This is the "platinum shavings" in Angela's review. Ceiling: 15 µL. At 20+ µL it becomes a distracting metallic-floral artifact.

**3. Myristic Acid at 10% in DPG (30 µL = 3 µL active)** — The single most impactful addition you can make to any iris accord. Real orris butter is 60–80% myristic acid (tetradecanoic acid, C14:0) by weight. It's a waxy saturated fatty acid with mp 54°C — nearly odorless, but it dramatically shapes the TEXTURE of everything around it. Without myristic acid, ionone stacks smell like chemicals arranged in iris formation. With it, the lipid context makes them smell like actual orris butter extract. Dissolve in warm DPG (heat to 60°C, stir until clear, cap immediately). The Aldehyde C11 undecylenic is kept alongside it — myristic acid provides the textural matrix, Aldehyde C11 provides the olfactory waxy-clean character. Together they reconstruct both the feel and the smell of real orris butter's lipid environment.

**4. Indole at 10% (3 µL = 0.3 µL active)** — Master perfumers from Roudnitska to Demachy add trace indole to iris compositions. At 0.3 µL in 1000 µL, indole is below its fecal threshold by a factor of 30×. What it provides is a **narcotic, living quality** — the difference between iris that smells like it came from a factory and iris that smells like it came from the earth. One reviewer called Opus V's iris "juicy, floral earth" — the "earth" part is Carrot Seed + this indole trace.

**5. Suederal at 10% (15 µL = 1.5 µL active)** — At 0.15% active concentration, Suederal is fully subliminal. Nobody will consciously perceive "suede." But the brain registers a **soft leather texture** surrounding the iris. This is the Opus V signature — iris wrapped in suede gloves. It's what separates Opus V from austere iris compositions like Prada Infusion d'Iris. The suede makes it warm, intimate, slightly dirty without being dirty.

## Mixing Instructions

### Step 1 — Build the structural cushion first
Add in order:
1. `Hexyl Salicylate` — 42 µL
2. `Bacdanol` — 25 µL
3. `Cashmeran (20%)` — 20 µL
4. `Ambrox Super (30%)` — 25 µL
5. `Vetival` — 15 µL
6. `Suederal (10%)` — 15 µL

Let sit 5 minutes. This creates the bed everything sits on.

### Step 2 — Build the cream-powder bridge
7. `Heliotropin Fleuressence` — 15 µL
8. `Coumarin (20%)` — 12 µL
9. `Hedione` — 28 µL

### Step 3 — Build the ionone field (from least volatile to most)
10. `Dihydro Beta Ionone` — 35 µL
11. `Irotyl` — 25 µL
12. `Allyl Ionone (Cetone V)` — 30 µL
13. `Alpha-Isomethyl Ionone` — 35 µL
14. `Beta Ionone` — 45 µL
15. `Alpha Ionone` — 45 µL

### Step 4 — Build the irone core
16. `Methyl Ionone Gamma` — 90 µL
17. `Orivone` — 120 µL
18. `Alpha Irone (10%)` — 320 µL *(add when restocked)*

### Step 5 — Add the orris modifiers LAST
19. `Carrot Seed EO` — 20 µL
20. `Myristic Acid (10% in DPG)` — 30 µL *(prepare 10% solution: heat DPG to 60°C, dissolve myristic acid, stir until clear)*
21. `Aldehyde C11 undecylenic` — 5 µL
22. `Ultralia` — 18 µL
23. `Indole (10%)` — 3 µL
24. `Rose Oxide (1%)` — 12 µL ← **last material added**

### Step 6 — Rest
- **Minimum rest:** 7 days before evaluating
- **Optimal rest:** 14–21 days
- **Best read:** Smear test on back of hand, check at 0, 15 min, 1 hr, 4 hr, 8 hr

Alpha Irone is notoriously slow to integrate. It starts harsh and "chemical" in the first 48 hours, then blooms into the lipid-waxy character over 1–2 weeks. Evaluate the full accord no earlier than day 14 after adding the Alpha Irone.
