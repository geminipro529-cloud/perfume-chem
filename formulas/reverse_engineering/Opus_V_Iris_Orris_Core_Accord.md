## Accord Architecture

### Three Textural Planes

1. **Irone-waxy root** — Alpha Irone + Carrot Seed EO  
   The authentic orris concrete character. Without this plane, the accord is a violet ionone stack. With it, it reads as genuine orris.

2. **Powdery violet cloud** — Methyl Ionone Pure + Alpha Ionone + Alpha Isomethyl Ionone + Ultralia + Violet Fleuressence  
   The diffusive, space-filling violet that projects. Methyl Ionone Pure is the structural backbone; Alpha Ionone adds brightness; Ultralia and Violet Fleuressence add ghost-transparency.

3. **Warm buttery depth** — Orivone + Irotyl  
   The bottom of the iris — the creamy, warm, almost amber-adjacent quality that makes orris concrete feel expensive on skin. Without this, the accord reads thin and synthetic.

### Woody-Violet Bridge

Dihydro Beta Ionone + Allyl Ionone (Ketone V) span between the violet heart and Opus V's base (Iso E Super / Cedarwood). The cedarwood-dry facet prevents the accord from reading as sweet violet candy and ensures seamless register transition into the woody-amber base.
