## Why This Version Is Better

### 1. It uses Orivone for the job Orivone is actually good at

Once `Orivone` is genuinely available, there is no reason to keep pretending it is absent.

Here it does the exact job the previous version had to reconstruct indirectly:
- buttery body
- cold metallic iris tone
- fatty orris-concrete suggestion

That lets the accord feel more naturally Opus V-like and less assembled from compensating supports.

### 2. The iris stays irone-led

Even with `Orivone` back in, the accord still has to stay **irone-first**.

So the backbone remains:
- `Alpha Irone (10%)` as the core orris identity
- `Orivone` as buttery-metallic body
- `Orris F-TEC` as waxy-rooty concrete mass
- `Methyl Ionone Pure` as bulk and diffusion

The other ionones are there to shape the field, not to replace the irone/orivone center.

### 3. The substitute stack is reduced, not removed

`Orivone` solves a lot, but not everything.

The small support materials still matter:
- `Carrot Seed EO` keeps the root realism
- `Bacdanol` gives creamy wood cushioning
- `Heliotropin Fleuressence` and `Coumarin (20%)` soften and warm the powder
- `Vetival` keeps the accord dark enough
- `Ambrox Super (30% w/v)` gives only a trace drydown bridge

The key difference is that these now support `Orivone`, instead of trying to impersonate it.

### 4. The metallic-cold concrete edge stays controlled

`Rose Oxide (1%)` remains at trace because it is still doing a very specific job:
- not rose character
- not floral sweetness
- just the **cold metallic concrete edge** that makes the accord feel more like real orris material and less like violet powder

## Why Not Mix Ambrox Super With Ambrofix Here

In this version, I do **not** run `Ambrox Super` and `Ambrofix` together.

Reason:
- once `Orivone` is present, the accord already has a better buttery-metallic body
- the ambergris bridge only needs to be a **small shadow**
- stacking `Ambrox Super` and `Ambrofix` inside the iris accord starts pushing attention toward amber structure instead of orris texture

So this file keeps:
- `Ambrox Super` only, at a low trace-support level

If you want the driest possible version, you could swap that `15 uL` of `Ambrox Super (30% w/v)` for `15 uL` of `Ambrofix (30% w/v)`, but I would not use both together inside this accord.

1. Add the small structural cushion:

## How To Use It

For an Opus V style full concentrate, start this accord at:
- `30-38%` of the perfume concentrate if you want the iris to dominate
- `22-28%` if you want the woods/base to speak earlier

Compared with the previous no-Orivone version, this one can usually be used a little lower because the iris body is more convincing by itself.

It still needs, outside this accord:
- a warm `rhum` opening
- a restrained `jasmine/rose` support layer
- a dark `oud/civet/dry woods` frame

Without that shell, it will smell like a dark luxury iris accord with clear Opus V tendencies, but not fully like Opus V.

## Adjustment Knobs

If it reads too pretty or too violet:
- reduce `Alpha Ionone` by `10 uL`
- reduce `Beta Ionone` by `5 uL`
- raise `Vetival` by `5 uL`
- raise `Dihydro Beta Ionone` by `10 uL`

If it is not buttery enough:
- raise `Orivone` by `15 uL`
- raise `Alpha Irone (10%)` by `20 uL`
- raise `Bacdanol` by `5 uL`
- reduce `I-IRIS F-TEC` by `10 uL`

If it is too cosmetic or too powdered:
- reduce `Heliotropin Fleuressence` by `5 uL`
- reduce `Coumarin (20%)` by `5 uL`
- reduce `Methyl Ionone Pure` by `10 uL`
- raise `Carrot Seed EO` by `5 uL`

If it lacks the cold concrete edge:
- raise `Rose Oxide (1%)` from `10 uL` to `15 uL`

That is the ceiling. Beyond that it starts reading as metallic floral instead of orris concrete.

If it feels too dark or woody:
- reduce `Vetival` by `5 uL`
- reduce `Ambrox Super (30% w/v)` by `5 uL`
- raise `Methyl Ionone Pure` by `10 uL`

If the Orivone is too obvious or too "constructed":
- reduce `Orivone` by `10-15 uL`
- raise `Orris F-TEC` by `10 uL`
- raise `Alpha Irone (10%)` by `10 uL`
