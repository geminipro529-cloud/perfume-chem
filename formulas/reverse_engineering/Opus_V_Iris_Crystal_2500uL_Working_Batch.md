## What To Keep In Mind

- The final bottle only needs `2400 uL` of this batch.
- The extra `100 uL` is the handling cushion.
- If you are dispensing with a syringe or micropipette, keep the batch sealed between pulls so the irones do not flash off.
- Mix the heavier iris materials first, then add `Hedione`, then the trace materials.
- Rest the batch at least `7` days before using it in the main perfume.

## Practical Notes

- If you want the batch to be easier to handle, pre-rinse the pipette with a small amount of the batch solvent before final transfers.
- Do not scale this batch down below `2400 uL` unless you are comfortable losing some material to dead volume.
- If you are filling more than one bottle later, make a larger batch from the same ratio rather than repeatedly remaking tiny portions.
