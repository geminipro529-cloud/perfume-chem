# Osmanthus Rose Oriental — 30mL EdP

## Character
A rich floral-amber composition where osmanthus absolute and rose materials create a dual-floral heart over a warm oriental base of labdanum, benzoin, and amber. The osmanthus's apricot-leather facet pairs with rose's honey-spice character, creating a jammy rose-osmanthus accord that reads as luxurious and slightly vintage. The oriental base provides warmth without heaviness — think a more floral Shalimar, or Portrait of a Lady's rose-patchouli-incense architecture softened by osmanthus.

**Family**: Floral Amber (Oriental) — rose-osmanthus heart, labdanum-benzoin-amber base

## Material Justification

- **Rose de Mai Absolute (10%, 220 µL = 22 µL active)**: The rose heart — natural rose absolute provides the complex jammy-honey-spice character that synthetics alone cannot achieve. At 22 µL active, it's the primary rose carrier, supported by the synthetic rose accord.

- **Rose synthetic chord (PEA 150 + Rhodinol 100 + Geraniol 80 + Citronellol 60 + PEDMC 50)**: The full rose accord — PEA for rose volume, Rhodinol for natural geranium-rose richness (chosen over Geraniol alone because Rhodinol's natural complexity pairs better with the Rose Absolute), Geraniol for fresh-rose lift, Citronellol for clean-rose sweetness, PEDMC for rose-magnolia softness (chosen over more PEA because PEDMC is cleaner, softer).

- **Damascenone (1%, 60 µL = 0.6 µL active) + Damascone Beta (10%, 60 µL = 6 µL active) + Damascol (10%, 50 µL = 5 µL active)**: The rose-ketone triad — Damascenone provides the ultra-powerful apple-rose-plum character at trace (the most important rose material per µL), Damascone Beta adds fruity-rose body, Damascol provides softer rose-plum depth. Together they create the multidimensional rose that defines luxury orientals.

- **Osmanthus Absolute (500 µL of 10% = 50 µL active)**: The co-star — osmanthus's apricot-leather-tea character provides the fruity-leather depth that makes this a rose-OSMANTHUS oriental, not just a rose oriental. The natural leather facet pairs with labdanum's animalic warmth.

- **Indole (10%, 30 µL = 3 µL active)**: Animalic-floral depth at trace — adds the "living flower" dimension to both the rose and jasmine facets. At 3 µL active, below the threshold where it reads fecal; above the threshold where it adds no character.

- **Labdanum Resinoid (10%, 150 µL = 15 µL active)**: The warm-amber undercurrent of every great oriental. At moderate dose, it provides the resinous-animalic depth without shifting to full amber-dominant.

- **Sandalwood FO (100 µL)**: Pre-blended warm sandalwood fill — chosen over individual sandalwood materials because orientals need volume rather than precision in the sandalwood register. The FO provides a consistent warm-woody bed.

- **Ambermax (50%, 100 µL = 50 µL active)**: Warm, rounded, full-bodied amber — the "cozy" amber register that defines the oriental base. Chosen over Ambrofix because orientals need cozy warmth, not crystalline mineral.

- **Coumarin (20%, 60 µL = 12 µL active)**: Warm-hay sweetness at modest dose — adds the coumarinic undertone without shifting from floral-amber to tobacco. Within family-shifting ceiling.
