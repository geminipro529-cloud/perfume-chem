# Immortelle Ambre Doré — 30mL EdP

## Character
A golden, honeyed amber where Immortelle Absolute's maple-curry warmth merges with a lush labdanum-benzoin-vanilla oriental base. Unlike dark, smoky ambers (Ambre Sultan) or powdery-clean ambers (L'Eau d'Ambre), this is a luminous, sun-drenched amber — the olfactory equivalent of late-afternoon golden light filtering through amber glass. Immortelle's honeyed-maple facet is amplified by Siam Benzoin's vanilla-balsamic warmth and Ethylene Brassylate's lactonic creaminess, while Ambermax provides the rounded, full-bodied amber core. Coumarin bridges the immortelle to the coumarinic-tonka axis, and a trace of Cinnamaldehyde adds subtle spice warmth. Think *Annick Goutal Ambre Fétiche* reimagined through a Corsican lens — warm, enveloping, and radiant without being cloying.

**Family**: Amber Oriental — labdanum-benzoin-vanilla golden base, immortelle honeyed heart, bergamot-citrus top

## Material Justification

- **Immortelle Absolute (10%)**: The golden heart — 200 µL of 10% = 20 µL active (0.34% of concentrate). At the upper end of modifier range because this concept is BUILT around immortelle's honey-maple character as the defining identity — not just an accent. Paired with Siam Benzoin (vanilla-balsamic amplification of the honey facet), Labdanum (resinous counterpoint), and Coumarin (tonka-sweet bridge). The immortelle-benzoin-vanilla axis is the formula's olfactory spine.
- **Siam Benzoin (50% in DPG)**: 400 µL of 50% = 200 µL active. The warm, vanilla-balsamic heart of the amber — the largest single dose because ambers NEED benzoin mass. Siam chosen at 50% over Sumatra 10% for maximum body at manageable volume. The benzoin's natural vanilla-sweetness merges with immortelle's honey and Vanillin into a seamless golden warmth.
- **Labdanum Resinoid (10%)**: 200 µL of 10% = 20 µL active. The resinous-amber anchor — labdanum IS amber in classical perfumery. Its complex leather-amber-animalic warmth provides the structural backbone that benzoin alone would lack. At 20 µL active, it's present but not dominant — the golden sweet character leads, labdanum supports.
- **Ambermax (50%)**: 100 µL of 50% = 50 µL active. Warm, rounded, full-bodied synthetic amber — the "cozy" amber that fills the space between the natural labdanum-benzoin and the transparent Ambrofix. Chosen specifically because this formula needs WARM amber (cozy, enveloping), not crystalline-mineral (Ambrofix) — Ambermax is the most "cashmere blanket" amber in inventory.
- **Amberwood F (80 µL) + Ambrofix (30%, 70 µL)**: Transparent amber layer. Amberwood F adds invisible clean-amber warmth without identity — the "background amber glow." Ambrofix at 21 µL active provides a subtle crystalline sparkle that prevents the dense benzoin-ambermax from becoming muddy. Together they add lift and clarity to the golden mass.
- **Vanillin (10%) + Coumarin (20%)**: Sweetness gradient. Vanillin at 18 µL active provides the classic vanilla warmth — the essential sweet anchor of any amber. Coumarin at 24 µL active adds hay-sweet tonka that pairs with immortelle (a proven synergy) and bridges the vanilla to the immortelle's maple character. The combination is sweet but not gourmand — this is amber, not dessert.
- **Gamma Decalactone (50 µL)**: Lactonic peach-skin bridge — adds creamy-fruity adhesion between the vanilla-benzoin base and the immortelle heart. The lactonic quality converts the immortelle's maple from "syrup" to "cream" — smoother, more sophisticated. Pairs with Ethylene Brassylate's lactonic character for a full creamy chord.
- **Ethylene Brassylate (300 µL)**: Depth musk at high dose (300 vs typical 200). The lactonic-creamy skin-sit is the primary musk anchor in this amber — its buttery character deepens the benzoin-vanilla warmth and marries with Gamma Decalactone for a seamless lactonic foundation. Higher dose because ambers need musk muscle to prevent the benzoin from becoming thin.
- **Musk Ketone (10% in DPG)**: Character-echo musk — 150 µL of 10% = 15 µL active. The ONLY musk that reinforces immortelle's powdery-honey character. As the immortelle heart fades, Musk Ketone's talcum-powder sweetness echoes at the skin — the olfactory equivalent of "remembering" the golden opening.
- **Habanolide (120 µL)**: Intimate-warmth musk. Chosen over Romandolide (too outward-projective — ambers should embrace, not project) and over Ambrettolide (too fruity-wine-like, competes with immortelle's maple character). Habanolide's warm-skin intimacy is the perfect register for a golden amber.
- **Bergamot FCF (350 µL) + Red Mandarin EO (80 µL)**: Dual-citrus golden opening. Bergamot provides the classic bright top — essential tension against the warm base. Red Mandarin adds sweet tangerine warmth that foreshadows the golden amber — the citrus equivalent of immortelle's honey. Chosen over Blood Orange (too juicy-red), Cedrat (too sharp-mineral), and Grapefruit (too bitter — this is golden, not pink).
- **Cardamom EO (20 µL) + Cinnamaldehyde (6 µL)**: Spice trace at the top. Cardamom adds green-spicy lift that brightens the bergamot and adds aromatic complexity. Cinnamaldehyde at micro-dose provides a whisper of cinnamon warmth — just enough to add depth to the top without reading as "spicy." Both kept at trace to avoid shifting the family toward spice-oriental.
- **Hedione HC (500 µL) + Hedione (250 µL)**: Radiance amplifier pair. Hedione's known synergy with immortelle expands the honey-maple character outward. The high-cis HC variant provides faster floral lift at the opening; standard Hedione sustains projection through the heart. 750 µL total is appropriate for an amber that needs volume without identifiable florals.
- **Cashmeran (100 µL)**: Textile-warmth — the "cashmere blanket" effect that makes the amber feel tactile and enveloping. Aligns with the golden-amber warmth without introducing powdery-vanilla. Used moderately — amber already has warmth from benzoin and EB.
- **Ebanol (50 µL) + Orivone (60 µL)**: Creamy-buttery texture layer. Ebanol adds creamy sandalwood softness — the "silk" in the golden amber. Orivone provides buttery orris warmth — enriches the base texture without introducing violet powder. Together they make the amber feel luxurious and expensive.
- **Benzyl Salicylate (200 µL)**: Cosmetic-heavy fixative — creates the diffusion cushion beneath the amber base. The white-floral cosmetic character adds a subtle "perfume-like" quality that distinguishes this from a simple vanilla-benzoin blend. Chosen over Benzyl Benzoate because an amber NEEDS the character contribution of a salicylate.
- **Patchouli EO (100 µL)**: Earthy-dark anchor — prevents the amber from becoming cloyingly sweet. The dark camphoraceous register provides the contrast that makes the golden sweetness feel balanced rather than monolithic. Essential in any amber composition.
- **Rhodinol ex Citronella (50 µL) + Phenethyl Alcohol (80 µL) + Dihydrojasmone (30 µL)**: Subtle floral heart trace. Not enough to read as "floral" — just enough rose-geranium-jasmine complexity to prevent the amber from being two-dimensional (sweet + warm = boring). These act as olfactory seasonings, not ingredients.
- **Damascenone (1%)**: 25 µL of 1% = 0.25 µL active. Trace rose-ketone — adds the barest whisper of dried-fruit-plum depth that enriches the Benzoin-Vanillin axis. At this dose, it's subliminal — you don't smell rose, but you miss something when it's gone.
