## Concept

`Ember Vow` is a market-facing alternative in the same broad territory as
Emporio Armani Stronger With You Intensely: pink pepper, aromatic lavender and
sage, a roasted chestnut/toffee impression, and a vanilla-suede amberwood
drydown. It is deliberately not a clone. The signature is a clearer natural
pink-pepper flare over warm red mandarin, followed by a drier roasted-tonka
heart and a more transparent amberwood trail. Sugar is restrained so that the
pepper and aromatic structure remain legible in warm evening weather.

The Armani product description is used only to identify marketed sensory
architecture. Commercial composition, proportions, sensory equivalence,
population liking, and market performance of this candidate are unknown until
tested.

## Revision rationale

Alpha Irone is the closest owned replacement for the removed Orris Liquid in
this formula. Its warm powdery violet character preserves the bridge from
lavender into Cashmeran and Suederal without introducing Violet Leaf's wet
cucumber-green character, Orivone's drier camphoraceous wood, the fruitier
diffusion of Alpha/Beta Ionone, or the unresolved product identity of the
current Givaudan AIMI stock.

The replacement keeps the physical stock transfer at 120 uL and retains DEP as
the declared carrier. Changing from 9% to 10% w/w is a small intentional
increase in nominal material fraction because Alpha Irone is a narrower
single-material effect than the broader Orris Liquid product. It is not claimed
as exact active-mass equivalence: exact comparison would require the densities
of both prepared stocks.

## Expected olfactive sequence

- **Opening:** vivid rosy-citrus pink pepper, warm mandarin, juniper, and cool cardamom. Pink pepper is the recognizable hook, not a decorative trace.
- **Heart:** Bontoux lavender and clary sage remain clearly aromatic. Alpha Irone and geranium smooth the pepper into a restrained violet-powdered, cinnamon-toasted transition.
- **Drydown:** roasted chestnut is an accord rather than a claimed ingredient: trace 2-acetyl pyrazine supplies crust, Tonkarome/coumarin supplies nutty hay, heliotropal supplies almond powder, and vanillin/Isobutavan plus benzoin supply glaze. Azarbre, Amberwood F, Bacdanol, Suederal, and Cashmeran form the warm woody-suede frame.

## Why this is differentiated rather than cloned

1. Pink Pepper EO is treated as the perfume's named signature, while red mandarin gives it a warmer and more rounded launch.
2. The chestnut effect relies more on roasted, coumarinic, and almond facets and less on ethyl-maltol sweetness.
3. Alpha Irone provides a precise violet-powder seam rather than a broad natural-style orris mixture.
4. Azarbre supplies smooth warm cedar-amber body while Amberwood F supplies transparent persistence; no crystalline Ambrox material is needed.
5. One diffusive woody musk is used instead of a laundry-style musk chord, making the trail clearer and easier to adjust.
6. Patchouli, clary sage, and Iso E Super keep the finish drier than a syrup-heavy vanilla gourmand.

## First physical evaluation and adjustment logic

This design is intended for one controlled pilot before any market claim. Smell
on the same blotter type at 5 minutes, 30 minutes, 2 hours, and 4 hours. Compare
blind against the exact Stronger With You Intensely EdP edition if a reference
sample is available.

- If Alpha Irone reads too cosmetic or powdery: reduce its 10% stock from 120 to 90 uL in the next revision and replace the removed volume with Hedione.
- If roast reads as popcorn: reduce `2-Acetyl Pyrazine 1%` from 90 to 60 uL in the next revision.
- If sweetness obscures pepper after 30 minutes: reduce `Vanillin 10%` from 350 to 300 uL before reducing Tonkarome or coumarin.
- If pink pepper disappears too early: test a +15 uL bottle delta, then re-evaluate at 30 minutes; do not make several simultaneous corrections.
- If the base feels too dry: test +20 uL Bacdanol before increasing vanilla.
- If the base feels too dense in heat: reduce Azarbre by 30 uL and replace that volume with Romandolide in the next formula revision.
