# Cassis Iris Smoke — 30 mL EdP

**Date:** 2026-07-05
**Design:** Transparent dark chypre. Cassis-forward fruit, iris signature heart (Alpha Irone + Ultralia), cade-guaiac-nagarmotha smoke triad, three-cedar wood, oakmoss-labdanum base. Musk-amber scaffold. No pineapple, no birch tar — differentiated from Aventus.

**Target Concentration:** 20% EdP

**Ethanol Volume:** 24.0 mL (96% EtOH)

live stock contract.

---

## Design Philosophy

A dark, transparent chypre playing on the same field as Aventus but clearly its own creature. Cassis 345B leads the fruit instead of pineapple. Alpha Irone + Ultralia iris replaces the Lyral-muguet heart. Cade Oil provides dry ashy smoke instead of birch tar's oily leather. Pear softens the citrus→dark fruit transition. Osmanthus Absolute bridges fruit, iris, and leather across all three layers. Damascenone + Geosmin bridge across heart layers.

**Differentiation from Aventus:**
- Cassis + Pear instead of Pineapple
- Iris (Alpha Irone + Ultralia) instead of Lyral-muguet
- Cade + Nagarmotha smoke instead of Birch Tar
- Three cedars instead of one
- Damascenone + Geosmin bridges
- Spice axis (Cardamom + Black Pepper)
