## Named Identity Contract

**Name:** Eclipse VII — Event Horizon / Gravity Skin.  
**One-sentence identity:** bitter pepper-citron light is pulled through a smoky black-suede boundary and reappears as warm mineral skin inside a dry gravitational wood contour.  
**Recognizable subject:** one black suede surface crossing from cold exterior to warm worn interior.  
**Emotional effect:** dangerous attraction followed by bodily reassurance; the wearer wants to move closer, not escape a harsh wood wall.  
**Wear context:** unisex evening fine fragrance; intimate but with a defined rear contour.  
**Allowed DHP2025 axis:** one subject in opposed states, five planes, recurrence, pressure/release, identity-bearing base, and disciplined omission.  
**Forbidden donor transfer:** no Dior iris-leather formula, no oud signature, no reference reconstruction, and no claim of similarity to DHP2025.  
**Takeover exclusions:** laundry musk, generic ambrox bomb, lipstick iris, gourmand vanilla, campfire, clove, cosmetic salicylate wax, or bright citrus cologne.  
**Minimum nucleus:** Cedrat + black/pink pepper function + Suederal/IBQ + Evernyl/vetiver + Ambrox/wood contour.
