## Design Decision

This is a bounded additive Lavender Current successor. The frozen V1.3 lavender/DHM/mineral-wood topology remains the design control. The new object is not “orange blossom perfume.” It is **lavender athletic fabric seen through two genetically connected bitter-orange-tree states**:

1. **Petitgrain EO Paraguay** supplies a bitter green leaf/twig pressure that shares linalool/linalyl-acetate chemistry with lavender and points into Terpinyl Acetate, cedar, and vetiver.
2. **Neroli EO 10% DPG** supplies a restrained luminous flower state that opens the lavender fabric toward current standard Hedione and Mayol without creating a white-floral bouquet; the TARGET keeps the high-cis jasmonate ideal separately.
3. **Givaudan Alpha Isomethyl Ionone** is rebased from 70 to 90 uL so its current translation reaches the pre-existing 15,000-ppm TARGET seam. It supplies the slower powdery violet-suede counterplane against the green-bitter leaf and luminous flower.

The candidate fails if orange flower, orange tree, eau de cologne, cosmetic iris, or floral soap becomes a more accurate name than Lavender Current. The relationship is a **testable counterpoint**, not a sensory result.

## Literature and Model Boundary
