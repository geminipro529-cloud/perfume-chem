## Corrected Market Question

The primary question is not “Which fragrance performs best in Bangkok weather?” and not “What do foreigners buy while in Thailand?” It is:

> Among adult local Thai men in the intended buyer segment who actually use or buy fragrance, which lead architecture produces the strongest blinded combination of liking, desire to re-smell, clean self-care, daily wearability, recognizable sport motion, distinct identity, social-distance comfort, and desire to wear in Thai heat?

The design uses these Thailand-specific signals only as hypotheses:

- Thai consumers commonly link smelling good with cleanliness and prefer subtle, adaptable scents that complement hygiene routines: [Mintel, 2025](https://www.mintel.com/press-centre/thais-rely-on-fabric-softeners-as-daily-scents-but-gen-z-drives-a-shift-to-fragrances/).
- Thai men's fragrance use is associated with self-care and individual character; frequency and longevity priorities differ by age: [Mintel, 2023](https://www.mintel.com/press-centre/sniffing-out-the-opportunity-the-influence-of-scent-trends-in-thai-mens-beauty-market/).
- A mixed-gender Bangkok/metropolitan survey offers only weak support for fresh/softened floral character and cannot select a men's formula: [Pala-ard et al.](https://so17.tci-thaijo.org/index.php/BASSBJ/article/view/774).

These sources do **not** prove orange, lavender, ambrox, sandalwood, vetiver, strong projection, or any individual material is preferred. The formula comparison exists because the correct lead remains unknown.

## Phase 1 — Internal Three-Direction Screen

### Samples

If separately authorized, prepare O, L, and M independently at their exact frozen 6,000-uL raw totals. Do not rescale individual formula rows. For each 6-mL evaluation sample, use a homogeneous aliquot corresponding to 1,200 uL raw concentrate plus 4,800 uL ethanol 96%, preserving 20% raw-concentrate fraction.

The formulas have different internal carrier and nominal-active fractions. Phase 1 therefore compares **finished design directions at equal raw-concentrate fraction**, not equal active mass. It cannot identify the cause of a winner. A close result requires a follow-up matrix/active-fraction control before attribution.

### Blinding and order

- Generate unrelated random three-character codes; never use O/L/M in participant-facing labels.
- Serve three candidates plus one coded duplicate, giving four samples maximum in a session.
- Balance order with a Williams or other pre-registered sequence.
- Keep the key inaccessible until every ballot for the block is locked.
- Use opaque identical bottles or a screened blotter presentation because natural-color differences can reveal the orange control.
- Complete two independently prepared blocks. A repour from Block 1 is not Block 2.
- Exclude an evaluator's direction ranking if duplicate reliability is outside the pre-registered tolerance; retain and report the failure rather than silently deleting it.

### Timepoints

Record free description first at 0, 5, 30, and 120 minutes, then 6 and 24 hours. Intensity, liking, identity, and persistence are separate fields. Stop for irritation, headache, or inability to discriminate the coded duplicate/reference reliably.

### Primary ballot

| Domain | Required record |
|---|---|
| first object | unprompted first odor object before any vocabulary is shown |
| overall liking | anchored score plus free reason |
| desire to re-smell | separate from strength and novelty |
| clean self-care | clean/groomed versus detergent, deodorant, or sterile |
| daily adaptability | office, commute, active leisure, and social use without burden |
| sport motion | athletic ease, velocity, moving air, gym-to-social fit |
| personal identity | distinctive enough to represent the wearer without being strange or loud |
| social-distance comfort | comfort at personal and conversational distance |
| warm-weather desire | desire to wear under a described/logged Thai hot-humid condition; not a performance claim |
| subject continuity | one object across opening, heart, and base versus disconnected top/heart/base |
| spatiality | front, near-body, living middle, rear contour, and skin/fabric echo |
| pressure/release | tension, pleasurable yielding, re-smell event, and seam visibility |
| late recurrence | whether 2-24 hours recalls the opening subject rather than generic musk/wood |
| failure burden | harshness, fatigue, headache/irritation, muddiness, stickiness, sweetness, blankness |
| takeover terms | orange soda/cleaner, grapefruit, lavender soap/barbershop, shower gel, deodorant, laundry, muguet/aquatic, iris makeup, ambrox wall, sandalwood cream, harsh lumber |

Ask purchase intent, expected occasion, and acceptable price **only after** all blind odor records are locked. Purchase intent is secondary and cannot be converted into actual buying behavior.

### Phase-1 decision rule

There is no score-total winner. Advance the Pareto candidate that, in both independent blocks:

- improves or preserves overall liking and desire to re-smell;
- is unmistakably sporty without higher generic-blue, deodorant, or fatigue burden;
- improves or preserves clean self-care, daily adaptability, social comfort, and warm-weather desire;
- preserves one clear subject and a recognizable late echo;
- does not merely win by being stronger;
- does not increase headache/irritation, harshness, muddiness, sweetness, stickiness, laundry, or ambrox-wall flags.

If different candidates win different domains, do not average away the tradeoff. Preserve both and run the smallest pre-registered resolving comparison. If O ties on liking but orange is not desired as a primary identity, O does not receive an incumbency advantage. If L or M ties with fewer takeover failures, the lower-failure direction advances.

## Phase 2 — Winner-Specific Causal Learning

Run only after Phase 1 selects a direction. Use no more than four arms per session and prepare a fresh independent block for replication.

### Common cross-candidate tests

1. **Dihydromyrcenol ladder:** select the lowest dose that gives clear sport motion. Use the exact ladder frozen in the winning candidate's formula packet. Reject shower gel, deodorant, harsh coolness, aromatic flattening, or fatigue.
2. **Red Mandarin/fruit-light test:** compare zero, half-center, and center dose with DEP compensation. In O, use its existing whole-orange-tree factorial rather than pretending Blood Orange and Red Mandarin are equivalent. In L/M, Red Mandarin survives only as a subordinate liking-enhancing glint.
3. **Pressure x release 2 x 2:** cross the winner's cold/mineral pressure module with its luminous/warm release module. The combined arm must exceed the additive expectation on transition, liking, and desire to re-smell without higher takeover burden.
4. **One-musk selection:** no musk versus the center's one musk versus one equal-volume alternative. Never combine musks. A modeled sub-threshold musk cannot be called projection; if zero musk ties, zero wins.
5. **Complete versus compressed:** compare the high-count center with a controlled compression based on the frozen survival audit. Hide count from evaluators. A tie favors compression; only a replicated complete-formula win authorizes individual add-backs.
6. **Matrix control:** repeat any close carrier-compensated contrast with a pre-registered matched-carrier design. DEP, DPG, and TEC are not assumed thermodynamically or sensorially neutral.

### Direction-specific tests

- **O — Solar Velocity:** use its existing whole-orange-tree 2 x 2, Ginger omission, Paradisamide omission, Tonkarome comfort test, and orange recurrence ballot.
- **L — Lavender Current:** literal lavender-surface x dry lavender-recurrence 2 x 2; AIMI seam omission; clary takeover check; Zenolide structural-matrix versus zero/Romandolide; barbershop and laundry vetoes.
- **M — Mineral Grain:** wet mineral shell x warm grain 2 x 2; three-plane sandalwood collapse; AIMI seam omission; rear-pressure balance; ambrox wall, generic wood, and harsh-lumber vetoes.

## Phase 3 — Environmental Crossover

After Phase 2 freezes one successor, compare it with the nearest informative control in separately randomized sessions:

- air-conditioned condition: target 22-24 C, relative humidity logged;
- hot-humid condition: target 30-34 C, relative humidity logged, with no bottle heating or uncontrolled sunlight.

Use identical storage, maturation, loading, distance, and timepoints. Treat temperature/humidity as an interaction. Neither room-temperature OAV nor one crossover establishes all-day Thai wear performance.

## Phase 4 — Intended Local Thai Buyer Screen

Run only after structural selection and applicable safety clearance. The primary analysis must use the intended **local Thai consumer segment**, not tourists or short-term visitors.

### Directional-panel eligibility

- adult Thai men resident in Thailand and within the pre-declared age/use segment;
- purchased or personally selected a fragrance for their own use within the prior 12 months;
- record normal purchase channel, spend band, purchase frequency, fragrance-use frequency, and current owned styles before testing;
- exclude perfume-industry professionals from the consumer-primary analysis or pre-register a separate expert stratum;
- exclude short-term visitors and tourists from the primary analysis; any expatriate or foreign-resident data must be separately labeled and never merged into the local-Thai estimate.

A practical first screen is at least 24 completed local participants with balanced order. It is a directional product-development panel, not a statistically representative account of Thai buying habits. Actual buying-habit claims require appropriate sales or larger representative behavioral data.

### Advancement rule

Advance only if both independent preparations show a coherent advantage or non-inferior result on liking, desire to re-smell, daily wear, clean self-care, sport identity, warm-weather desire, and social comfort, with no higher failure burden. Report distributions, missingness, duplicate reliability, age/use strata, and conflicting signals. Do not announce “Thai-market winner” from a mean score alone.

## Inference Rules

- A whole-formula winner identifies a direction, not a causal material.
- One block is preliminary. Two independent blocks establish only replicated learning under the recorded protocol.
- A detected 2 x 2 interaction supports the named modules, not every row inside them.
- A null or unnoticed omission is **HOLD / NON-INFORMATIVE**, not proof of invisible luxury or universal dormancy.
- Strength, OAV, longevity, complexity, and liking are different variables.
- Retail assortment, review counts, climate data, and foreign-buyer behavior cannot establish local Thai preference.
- A local directional panel cannot establish national market share, repeat purchase, sales velocity, safety, stability, or release readiness.
- Any code leak, unequal finished fraction, unlogged carrier difference, sub-floor direct measurement, maturation mismatch, visible cue, or environmental mismatch makes the affected result HOLD.
