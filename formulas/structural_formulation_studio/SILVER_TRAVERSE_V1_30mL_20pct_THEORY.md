## Named Identity Contract

**Name:** Silver Traverse.  
**Subject:** charged technical sailcloth.  
**Main note:** luminous pink pepper — rosy, effervescent, and tensile rather than culinary, hot, or dry.  
**Cold state:** pink-pepper sparks and citrus peel move across wet, taut, silver-mineral aromatic cloth.  
**Warm state:** the same peppered cloth dries, flexes, and becomes sun-warmed from within by woody amber.  
**Invariant cue:** rosy pepper pressure braided through lavender-linalyl fabric cleanliness and recalled by Cashmeran, patchouli, vetiver, and ambrox warmth.  
**Emotional effect:** alert forward motion followed by controlled bodily warmth; sporty without shower-gel cheerfulness, luxurious without powder or sweetness.

The formula sits inside three broad official reference axes:

- [Prada Luna Rossa Eau de Toilette](https://www.prada-beauty.com/fragrance/luna-rossa/luna-rossa-eau-de-toilette/MPL01360.html): fresh lavender, spearmint, and lasting amber in a clean athletic aromatic frame.
- [Montblanc Explorer Eau de Parfum](https://www.montblanc.com/en-us/explorer-eau-de-parfum-100-ml-MB124069VG.html): bergamot, pink pepper, clary sage, patchouli, ambrofix, vetiver, leather, and cedar; only the pink-pepper/bergamot/aromatic-to-amber trajectory is used as an axis.
- [Montblanc Explorer Extreme Parfum](https://www.montblanc.com/en-ph/montblanc-explorer-extreme-parfum-60-ml-MB134815VG.html): bergamot opening, patchouli heart, and amber base.

“Montblanc Explorer Parfum” is interpreted as the current Explorer Extreme Parfum for depth and concentration, while the original Explorer supplies the explicit pink-pepper reference axis. These axes define a design interval, not a reconstruction target.

### Takeover exclusions

- reject iris, orris, lipstick powder, oud, rum, or a copied DHP/Opus identity;
- reject calone/melon or literal marine identity;
- reject generic blue shower gel;
- reject hot, dry, culinary black-pepper takeover that fails the rosy-effervescent Pink Pepper subject;
- reject vanilla/tonka sweetness takeover;
- reject creamy sandalwood or leather/smoke takeover;
- reject laundry-musk bloom;
- reject a base that loses the peppered aromatic textile identity and becomes anonymous ambroxan wood.
