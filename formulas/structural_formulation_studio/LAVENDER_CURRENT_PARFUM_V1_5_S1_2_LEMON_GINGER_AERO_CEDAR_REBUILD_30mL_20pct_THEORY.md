## Design Correction

S1.1 was a conservative stock correction and dose recut, not the requested rebuild. S1.2 therefore does not inherit every old structural row by default.

Three approaches were considered:

1. **Minimal recut:** keep the old Iso E / sandal / woody-musk chassis and retune it. Rejected because it repeats S1.1's failure.
2. **Maximal material substitution:** replace nearly every row. Rejected because it would no longer preserve the liked lemon–ginger lavender identity.
3. **Selected — identity nucleus plus new chassis:** retain the recognizable subject and sport current, remove the old rear architecture, simplify the middle, and rebuild recurrence, warmth, mineral pressure, and fabric diffusion with a different material set.

This is an authorized successor after the user's explicit rejection of S1.1. It is not claimed to be better until a blinded physical comparison says so.

## ppm, ODT, OAV, and Stock Boundaries

Exact dose conversion is defined by:

`active_mass_i = raw_volume_i × stock_density_i × active_mass_fraction_i`

`ppm_w/w_i = 1,000,000 × active_mass_i / total_concentrate_mass`

`OAV_i = modeled_headspace_concentration_i / ODT_i`

Exact ppm w/w is **HOLD** where density or stock basis is absent. The release pipeline may provide model ppm/OAV from its standardized property spine; those values are computational diagnostics, not bottle-specific measurement.

- Ambrox Super: 450 uL of the 5 g / 15 mL stock contains a nominal **150 mg Ambrox Super** by the stated w/v preparation. Whole-concentrate ppm w/w remains density-dependent.
- Lemonile: 30 uL of labeled 1% DPG stock gives 0.3 uL nominal active-volume equivalent only if the label is interpreted on a compatible volume basis; exact active mass and ppm w/w remain HOLD.
- Evernyl: 25 uL of 20% w/w DPG stock; active mass requires stock density.
- Coumarin: 25 uL of approximately 25–30% DPG-saturated stock; exact fraction basis and active ppm remain HOLD.
- Pink Pepper Base: raw volume is known; active mass, ppm, ODT, OAV, composite composition, density, and carrier are HOLD.
- Givaudan AIMI: raw neat volume is known under user authority; exact product identity and authoritative physics remain HOLD.
- Naturals: Lavender and Vetiver have local composite-natural models. Lemon FCF, Ginger, Red Mandarin, and Cedarwood Virginia lack exact-name composite coverage; parent-material OAV must not be presented as whole-oil authority.

## Hedonic Hypothesis

The intended pleasure mechanism is not “more materials.” It is a repeated three-beat event:

1. a tart lemon–ginger snap invites immediate attention;
2. Pink Pepper Base blooms inside a large standard-Hedione lavender field, producing a softer and more social release;
3. warm Sandalore/Amberwood F/Zenolide skin is held in tension by cedar, vetiver, Helional, Koavone, and reduced Ambrox pressure.

The new chassis should be clearer, warmer, less scratchy, less laundry-like, and more immediately legible than S1.1. Every one of those statements remains a **theory hypothesis** until blinded smelling. No numerical model proves hedonism.

### Perfumer analysis

#### 1. Character

The intended character is a tart aromatic fougere/sport perfume: lemon–ginger current at the front, premium lavender in a large moving heart, a restrained pepper-lit suede/mineral hinge, and a warm cedar-vetiver/ambrox skin tail. Structurally this is no longer S1.1's Iso E–Ebanol–Cashmeran–Timberol–Azarbre–Romandolide chassis. The new rear grammar is Koavone–Sandalore–Amberwood F–Vertofix–Ambrox–Zenolide around literal cedar and vetiver.

The engine predicts 182.651 ppm total opening vapor. The largest modeled channels are Lemon FCF sensitivity 18,014 (**HOLD** as whole-oil authority), DHM 7,956, Hedione 6,815, BONTAUX lavender 6,461, Linalyl Acetate 3,140, and Linalool 1,886. Because the internal models disagree, actual longevity is **NOT TESTED**.
