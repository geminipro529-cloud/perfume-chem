## 0. Resource Correction and Hard Ceiling

The superseded plan treated every possible causal arm as a separate 6,000-uL concentrate. If every listed ladder, decomposition, omission, and pressure/release arm had been made, it could have required 36 full arms, or **216 mL of concentrate**. Its blanket two-preparation clause could have doubled that to **432 mL**. That is not an acceptable hobbyist validation budget.

This revision supersedes that batch schedule:

1. No validation arm is 6,000 uL. Every arm is **600 uL**, the smallest exact scale at which the formula's existing 120-uL Neroli stock row becomes a 12-uL aliquot and therefore remains above the 10-uL direct-stock floor.
2. Prepare, if separately authorized, one **5,550-uL fixed master base**. It is aliquoted at 555 uL per arm and supports ten micro-arms without remeasuring the full formula.
3. The first decision uses only **two arms / 1.2 mL total arm concentrate**.
4. No factorial completion, component decomposition, dose adjustment, omission, replication, climate test, or consumer screen occurs unless the preceding cheaper comparison clearly earns it.
5. An ambiguous result triggers re-smelling from the same coded vials, not automatic preparation of more material.
6. A tie goes to the simpler arm. No stock is spent merely to fill a design matrix.
7. The fixed master and unused working-stock reserve are retained, labeled, and never discarded merely because a stage stops.

The full 6,000-uL theory formula remains the composition definition. This protocol changes only how little material is needed to interrogate it.

If the user is the only available evaluator, conceal the assignment as far as practical and label the result self-blinded/exploratory rather than replicated panel evidence.
