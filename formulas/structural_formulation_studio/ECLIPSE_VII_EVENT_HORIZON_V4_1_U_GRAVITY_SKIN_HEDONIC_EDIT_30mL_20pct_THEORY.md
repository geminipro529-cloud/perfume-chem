## Hedonism-First Identity

The intended smell is a bright, expensive-feeling bergamot/citron invitation pulled immediately toward a black suede center. Pepper is a glint, not punishment. The center should feel soft and mobile rather than built from a list of iris, fruit, smoke, and wood effects. The drydown should become warm, clean, touchable suede skin: mineral enough to retain the Event Horizon name, but not a hard amberwood wall.

Four perceptual modules own the perfume:

| Module | Owners | Hedonic job | Rejection signal |
|---|---|---|---|
| bright invitation | Bergamot, Cedrat, Black Pepper, Pink Pepper, Safraleine | creates alertness and intrigue without sourness or culinary heat | cleaner, hot pepper, harsh citron, tobacco |
| tactile black-suede subject | Suederal, IBQ, trace Guaiacol, Evernyl, vetiver | gives the perfume a memorable object rather than generic woody ambience | rubber, tar, smoke, bitter moss |
| mobile soft interior | Hedione, Dihydrojasmone, AIMI, Hexyl Salicylate | makes the suede breathe, flex, and invite repeated smelling | blank jasmine, lipstick, green floral blur |
| yielding skin trail | Iso E, cedar, Ambrox, patchouli, Cashmeran, Ebanol, Habanolide, Benzyl Benzoate, benzoin/vanillin | converts darkness into warm tactile persistence instead of dry pressure | anonymous haze, laundry musk, sandal cream, sweetness |

This is deliberately not scored by ingredient count. It happens to use **24 aromatic materials plus DEP**, because those are the rows that survived function ownership and omission review.

### Why the large cuts are hedonic, not minimalist

- The former rear plane had Iso E, cedar, Timberol, Clearwood, Vertofix, patchouli, Kephalis, Norlimbanol, Ambrox, Amberwood, Cashmeran, Ebanol, and Javanol competing for one woody impression. The successor gives space to Iso E, keeps literal grain with cedar, keeps one mineral recurrence with Ambrox, keeps organic darkness with patchouli, and lets Cashmeran/Ebanol provide the yielding skin.
- Orris, Osmanthus, and Alpha Damascone were attractive but made Gravity Skin narrate several cosmetic/fruit-floral ideas. AIMI alone owns the powder-suede bend.
- Elemi and Eugenol were plausible recurrence details but did not own enough pleasure to justify resin/incense and clove risks.
- Labdanum, Coumarin, and the larger Vanillin/Benzoin block could turn smoothness into amber-gourmand identity. A narrow benzoin/vanillin finish is retained.
- Recovered volume is spent mainly on Hedione, Iso E, Hexyl Salicylate, Benzyl Benzoate, and Ebanol: air, space, tactile film, quiet mass, and yielding warmth rather than more named effects.
