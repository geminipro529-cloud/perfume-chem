# Identity-First Structural Formulation Method

**Version:** SFSM-1.3  
**Status:** THEORY METHOD / NOT A COMPOUNDING AUTHORIZATION

## 1. Named Identity Contract

The perfume name is the optimization target. Scores, material count, ingredient prestige, and reference similarity are subordinate.

Fill before selecting materials:

```text
Name:
One-sentence identity:
Recognizable subject:
Emotional effect:
Wear context:
Allowed reference axes:
Forbidden reference ingredient identities:
Takeover exclusions:
Minimum recognizable nucleus:
```

**Pass question:** If the name were hidden, could a blinded evaluator recover the intended subject and distinguish it from a generic family example?

### 1A. Lead-Note Ownership Gate

When the brief names a main note, “present” is insufficient. Before dosing, assign that subject three different ownership jobs and budget its likely competitors explicitly.

```text
Lead note / subject:
Opening owner or exact material:
Heart-state transformation owner:
Base/echo recurrence owner:
Materials most likely to displace it:
Maximum role permitted for each competitor:
Lead-note free-description criterion:
Lead-note rank at 0, 5, 30, and 120 minutes:
No-lead and half-lead constant-total controls:
Exact-material comparison needed:
```

- The TARGET / IDEAL dose must make the named lead structurally more important than its supporting character materials; large projection or matrix materials may exceed it only when they are explicitly non-identity functions.
- Citrus, lavender, fresh-air molecules, florals, woods, ambers, and musks are supports unless the name assigns them leadership. Their dose is reduced or isolated when they become the first named object in the governed diagnostic.
- For an unavailable natural or proprietary mixture, a reconstruction may separate surface, body, and echo functions, but must remain labeled non-equivalent until an exact-material blinded comparison exists.
- Modeled OAV can identify displacement risk and audibility, but cannot prove perceptual leadership. Leadership requires blind free description and time-specific lead-note ranking.

**Pass question:** Does the lead own the first recognizable object, a transformed heart state, and a late identity echo without a support material becoming the perfume's more accurate name?

## 2. One Subject / Multiple States

Choose one subject and express it in at least two causally connected states. A state changes temperature, moisture, tension, texture, illumination, distance, or time; it does not introduce an unrelated second subject.

```text

```

**Pass question:** Do both states still read as the same named subject when each module is tested alone?

## 3. Three-Dimensional Spatial Planes

Assign every material one primary plane. Secondary interactions may be recorded, but cannot excuse missing primary ownership.

| Plane | Perceptual job | Failure mode |
|---|---|---|
| Front glint/edge | Immediate directional cue, edge definition, motion | glare, volatility wall, generic freshness |
| Near-body identity | Closest recognizable subject material | flat theme, cosmetic or functional-product drift |
| Living middle | Air, volume, transition, negative space | hollow heart, disconnected top and base |
| Rear contour/mass | Perspective, shadow, pressure, distal shape | generic woody-amber block, heaviness |
| Skin/fabric echo | Memory of the subject after projection recedes | anonymous musk/fixative drydown |

```text
Front owners:
Near-body owners:
Middle owners:
Rear owners:
Echo owners:
Front-to-middle bridge:
Middle-to-rear bridge:
How the rear changes the opening before drydown:
```

**Pass question:** At a fixed timepoint, are at least three planes perceptually separable without becoming three unrelated accords?

### 7A. Hedonic Successor Rebase

A request for the “most hedonic” version does not authorize ingredient accumulation. Build a versioned successor and preserve its predecessor unchanged. Begin with the named identity nucleus, then subtract likely glare, rigidity, fatigue, and generic-family competition before adding any new character material.

```text
Protected identity nucleus and invariant dose:
Opening glare candidates:
Heart masking candidates:
Rigid or fatiguing rear-pressure candidates:
Comfort/release owners already present:
Raw-volume reduction budget:
Release/cushion/negative-space reallocation:
Single-musk candidates, one per arm:
Predicted pleasure gain:
Predicted identity cost:
Constant-total predecessor control:
```

Apply these gates in order:

1. Keep the lead-note dose and its three ownership jobs fixed unless the experiment is explicitly a lead-dose test.
2. Reduce the smallest set of support doses predicted to cause glare, masking, rigidity, or fatigue. Record every predecessor-row disposition.
3. Reallocate the released volume only to an existing release owner, an existing tactile cushion, or a declared low-character negative-space compensator. A compensator is never presumed sensorially neutral.
4. If musk character may change pleasure, compare no musk and exactly one musk per arm at equal raw volume. Do not infer a chord from separate winners and do not add the candidate musks together.
5. Compare the complete rebase against its predecessor before optimizing individual deltas. Only then localize the effect with one-factor constant-total arms.
6. Judge identity, first-object leadership, liking, desire to re-smell, comfort, harshness, fatigue, muddiness, and takeover flags separately at every governed timepoint.

**Pass question:** Does the successor improve repeated liking and desire to re-smell while preserving the named lead, recurrence, spatial planes, and base identity—and can the gain be localized to subtraction, release/cushion, or one exact musk rather than to uncontrolled formula change?

### 7B. Reference-Axis Replacement and Hedonic-Sport Escalation

A request to replace one reference perfume with another changes the allowed structural comparison axes; it does not authorize a donor-note transplant or a hidden reconstruction. Freeze the exact predecessor first, keep the perfume's name and lead-note contract unchanged, and translate each new reference into an abstract motion, texture, contrast, or temporal relationship.

```text
Exact frozen predecessor and SHA-256:
Protected lead and invariant dose:
Reference being retired:
Replacement reference A — allowed structural axis:
Replacement reference B — allowed structural axis:
Official source basis:
Donor-signature notes or accords explicitly excluded:
New sport-motion module:
New hedonic contrast module:
Base-depth change, if any:
Rows added, rebased, transferred, or removed:
Constant-total localization arms:
Named-identity and donor-takeover stop rules:
```

Apply these gates in order:

1. Preserve the perfume name, lead-note ownership jobs, one-subject/multiple-state anatomy, recurrence cue, and base identity. A replacement reference cannot become the new subject.
2. Use official note descriptions only to establish broad reference axes and explicit anti-copy exclusions. Do not infer a reference formula, imitate a signature accord, or use a donor note merely because it appears in the reference.
3. Define “sport” as observable motion—acceleration, air shear, directional projection, cooling-to-warm transition, or bodily ease—not as more Dihydromyrcenol, citrus, ambrox, or generic blue freshness by default.
4. Define “go hard on hedonism” as stronger tension/comfort/release contrast and a repeatable re-smell moment. It is not permission to maximize modeled hedonic scores, sweetness, diffusion, or material count.
5. Add or rebase only function-owned rows. Every new row needs a donor-takeover risk, an omission prediction, and a constant-total owner; missing ODT, composite-natural coverage, stock basis, or safety remains HOLD.
6. Separate fruit-lit propulsion, projection texture, and base-depth changes into independently reversible modules. The complete successor must face its predecessor in fresh blinded blocks after module localization.
7. Keep zero or one musk per arm. A musk-role transfer requires equal-volume one-musk and no-musk controls; do not infer a chord from separate winners.
8. Test the full-count successor against a compressed control. High count survives only when the complete version repeatedly preserves the name and earns a target-linked hedonic or structural advantage without extra takeover failures.

**Pass question:** Does the new reference interval intensify named sport motion, luxury, and re-smell desire while the original lead remains the first object and all recognizable donor signatures remain absent?

## 11. Material Admission Gate

A row is admitted only when every answer is recorded:

1. Is the material physically authorized by current inventory and overrides?
2. Is the stock basis exact: material identity, supplier/lot when material-specific, solvent, concentration basis, and preparation status?
3. Is every direct aliquot at least 10 uL?
4. Does repository name normalization resolve the material?
5. Are MW, vapor pressure, activity coefficient, note/profile, and ODT present and nonconflicting?
6. For a natural mixture, is composite OAV coverage present and applicable to this material identity?
7. What single primary plane does it own?
8. What named temporal transition does it cause?
9. What nonredundant part of the perfume name disappears on omission?
10. What constant-total omission or protected-nucleus test owns the claim?
11. Does it create a takeover risk?
12. If it is a musk, why can the existing musk or no musk not do the job?

Any unresolved answer is **HOLD**, not an invitation to use a default.

## 12. ppm, ODT, OAV, and Stock Basis

For a conventional volume-fraction stock:

```text
nominal_active_uL = raw_uL x stock_fraction
concentrate_ppm = nominal_active_uL / total_concentrate_uL x 1,000,000
```

For a 6,000 uL concentrate, 60 uL neat is 10,000 nominal active ppm. A 60 uL dose from a 10% stock is 1,000 nominal active ppm.

For a w/w or w/v stock, do not claim exact volume-active ppm unless density and conversion basis are known. Record the pipeline's required numerical mapping as nominal and place exact w/w active ppm on HOLD.

The repository headspace diagnostic follows the material model, including mole fraction, vapor pressure, activity coefficient, and ODT. OAV remains a model:

```text
OAV = modeled_headspace_concentration / applicable_ODT
```

- OAV greater than 1 supports modeled perceptibility only.
- OAV below 1 is a diagnostic flag when the row requires perceptibility.
- OAV below 0.1 is not evidence of a useful sensory effect.
- Missing physics, ODT, activity coefficient, or alias resolution is HOLD.
- Duplicate ODT entries must be reconciled because the repository's last entry may win.
- Do not substitute monomolecular OAV for an EO, absolute, resinoid, or other natural mixture.
- If composite decomposition is missing or identity-mismatched, report composite OAV as HOLD rather than zero.

## 13. One-Musk Default

Start with zero musk. Admit one only if it owns one exact role that the woods, salicylates, balsams, or solvents cannot supply. Allowed roles include outward fabric halo, inward skin warmth, or a character echo—but one musk cannot be credited with all three without isolated evidence.

A second musk requires a constant-total A/B test showing a distinct, target-linked function and no masking, laundry drift, or redundancy. Inventory presence is irrelevant.

## 14. Controlled Omission Ledger

Every row receives one status:

- **NUCLEUS:** removal would destroy the named identity; validate through a whole-nucleus control before individual omission.
- **MODULE:** tested within a named constant-total factorial module; module evidence does not prove each row.
- **ISOLATED:** tested as an equal-volume omission/add-back.
- **FINISHING CANDIDATE:** remove first in high-count-versus-compressed testing.
- **HOLD:** function, stock, physics, safety, or identity evidence is unresolved.

Compensators must be low-character within the relevant structure and cannot be described as sensorially neutral until tested.

## 15. Blinded Validation Ladder

### Stage 1 — Protected nucleus

Confirm that the formula's minimum nucleus is recognizable against a family-generic control at constant total.

### Stage 2 — Constant-total 2x2

Test two causal modules as 00, 10, 01, and 11. Use equal total concentrate, coded module masters, two independently prepared blocks, and balanced presentation.

### Stage 3 — High-count versus compressed

Remove low-evidence finishing candidates together, reallocate their raw volume to the declared compensator, and test the complete and compressed arms blind.

### Stage 4 — Isolated add-backs

Only if the complete arm wins, add each removed material back separately at constant total. Retain a row only when its observed change matches its predicted nonredundant function.

### Stage 5 — Single-musk necessity

Compare the selected formula with and without its musk at constant total. The musk remains only if its exact function is observed without laundry drift or masking.

## 16. Evaluation Schedule and Endpoints

Evaluate blotter and skin in separate sessions at:

- 0 minutes;
- 5 minutes;
- 30 minutes;
- 120 minutes;
- 6 hours;
- 24 hours.

Lock results before decoding. Record identity match, state legibility, transition continuity, spatial separation, temporal recurrence, base identity, liking, desire to re-smell, harshness, muddiness, fatigue, and each takeover exclusion separately.

Protocol balance is not an observed sensory result. One block is preliminary. Missing cells, broken codes, unequal totals, visible differences, or provenance conflicts force reconciliation or HOLD.

## 17. Disciplined Finishing Stop Rule

Stop adding materials when every named function has one owner and all remaining deficits require physical evidence to distinguish. Do not add a row to improve ingredient count, a global score, note-list richness, or imagined luxury.

The formula is structurally finished only after:

1. the identity nucleus is recognizable;
2. both states are legible and connected;
3. at least three spatial planes are observed;
4. the opening motif recurs in the base;
5. pressure and release are preferred to the corresponding single-module controls;
6. the base remains identity-bearing;
7. the high-count version defeats or equals the compressed version on identity and liking without extra takeover failures;
8. every retained secondary row survives its controlled omission test.

Until then, completion, luxury, depth, hedonism, diffusion, longevity, and safety are **NOT TESTED** or **HOLD** as applicable.
