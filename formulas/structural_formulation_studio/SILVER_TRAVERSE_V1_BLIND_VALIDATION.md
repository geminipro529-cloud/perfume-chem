**Status:** PROTOCOL DESIGN ONLY / NOT RUN / NO COMPOUNDING AUTHORIZED  
**Claim ceiling:** The design can establish experimental control. It cannot establish Pink Pepper identity or equivalence, luxury, depth, completion, hedonism, preference, safety, stability, diffusion, or longevity until the corresponding blinded physical observations exist.

## Purpose

First test whether the CURRENT-INVENTORY Black Pepper chord contributes a recognizable rosy-effervescent Pink Pepper subject rather than dry culinary heat, without claiming equivalence to Pink Pepper EO. Then test whether Silver Traverse's cold charged surface and warm woody-amber interior make separate and interacting contributions to the named identity while holding total concentrate volume constant. Isolate the added Bergamot citrus-depth and trace Labdanum base-depth hypotheses before comparing the surviving 34-material construction with a compressed formula and testing remaining finishing rows.

This protocol does not authorize preparation of the Peppermint working stock, common core, module masters, finished articles, or skin applications.

## Experiment 1 — Surface × Interior 2×2

### Causal questions

1. Does the cold surface extension make the sailcloth feel charged, taut, wet-cool, and fast rather than generically fresh?
2. Does the warm interior extension make the same cloth feel flexed, sun-warmed, and three-dimensional rather than generically woody-amber?
3. Does their interaction create pressure/release and temporal recurrence without harshness, muddiness, or a hard seam?

### Replication and coding

- Prepare two independent blocks on different dates or from independently rebuilt core/module masters.
- Assign each block four fresh, nonsemantic codes generated after the logical arms are fixed.
- Keep the arm-to-code key outside the evaluator's accessible folder or in a sealed physical envelope until data lock.
- Do not reuse cap color, bottle position, label length, or fill-height cues.
- Present each logical arm once in each serial position across the two blocks using balanced Latin/Williams order generated by a preparer who does not evaluate.
- Randomize blotter position separately from bottle code.
- Do not discuss impressions between blocks.

If one person must prepare and evaluate, use delayed self-blinding: prepare and code, store the key inaccessible, wait until code memory is no longer reliable, randomize presentation independently, and document that blinding is weaker than independent preparation.

Run blotter evaluation first. Skin evaluation is a separate stage and remains HOLD until safety review and separate authorization.

For each coded article, record at 0, 5, 30, and 120 minutes, then 6 and 24 hours:

### 2×2 decision rule

1. Apply exclusion stop rules first. An arm that repeatedly becomes generic blue, marine, sweet, leathery/smoky, powdery, creamy-sandalwood, or laundry-musk is ineligible even if liked.
2. Require recognizable Silver Traverse identity in both blocks.
3. Compare 10 versus 00 for cold-surface contribution and 01 versus 00 for warm-interior contribution.
4. Compare 11 with both single-module arms for interaction: pressure/release, continuity, spatial separation, recurrence, and desire to re-smell.
5. Select 11 only if both states remain the same subject and the interaction provides repeatable structural or hedonic value without more takeover failures.
6. If one extension has no repeatable value, select the corresponding simpler arm and revise the formula before compression testing.
7. Discordant blocks, missing time cells, revealed codes, unequal totals, or compensator detection produce **HOLD**, not averaging.

Run only after Experiment 0 selects an eligible pepper-proxy dose and Experiment 1 selects an eligible surface/interior architecture. Keep that selected background fixed.

| C1B0 | 100 uL | 0 uL | 200 uL | 6,000 uL | Bergamot without resin shadow |
| C0B1 | 0 uL | 100 uL | 200 uL | 6,000 uL | resin shadow without Bergamot |
| C0B0 | 0 uL | 0 uL | 300 uL | 6,000 uL | neither intervention |

Benzyl Benzoate remains a declared compensator limitation. The hypotheses are deliberately narrow:

1. **Citrus depth:** Bergamot should create dimensional peel and a floral-citrus bridge around the pepper/lavender subject, not merely louder citrus.
2. **Base depth:** trace active Labdanum should create a far-rear low-vapor shadow from 2 to 24 hours without sweetness, smoke, leather, or chypre takeover.
3. **Coupling:** the opening's peppered peel should recur as warm resin-vetiver/patchouli dryness without a top/base seam.

Retain Bergamot only if C1 arms repeatedly improve dimensional peel, pepper integration, or recurrence over matched C0 arms. Retain Labdanum only if B1 arms repeatedly improve far-rear plane or identity-bearing base over matched B0 arms without takeover or fatigue. If neither main effect nor useful interaction repeats, select C0B0. A stronger sample is not automatically deeper.

- “Richer,” “more complex,” or “more expensive-smelling” without a localized structural observation does not rescue the high-count arm.

## Stop / HOLD Rules

Stop the affected stage and preserve all observations if:

- stock provenance, dilution, identity, or safety authority conflicts;
- any direct raw or working-stock aliquot is below 10 uL;
- the formula or an arm does not total exactly 6,000 uL concentrate;
- codes are revealed or inferable before data lock;
- preparation blocks are not independent;
- a material visibly precipitates, separates, or changes the blind;
- irritation occurs;
- both blocks repeat a takeover exclusion;
- canonical cells are duplicated or missing and cannot be reconciled;
- the compensator is itself detected as the causal change.

Do not average through a HOLD and do not replace a failed or missing arm after decoding.
