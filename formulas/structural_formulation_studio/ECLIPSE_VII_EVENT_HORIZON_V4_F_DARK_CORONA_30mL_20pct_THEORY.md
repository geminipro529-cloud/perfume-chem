## Named Identity Contract

**Recognizable subject:** one black suede surface observed first in radiant floral light and later as warm worn skin.  
**Emotional effect:** glamorous attraction with danger still visible at the edge; tenderness does not erase gravity.  
**Wear context:** feminine-leaning evening fine fragrance, still wearable as unisex by preference rather than gender claim.
