## Why This Is a Separate V1.5 Portfolio

The V1.4 quartet remains the clean citrus screen. Rewriting those files with spices would destroy the controls and confound citrus effects with spice effects. V1.5 therefore creates one additive successor per citrus chassis. The first causal question is not “which finished perfume wins?” It is “does this exact spice improve this exact citrus-to-lavender transition?”

No wood is changed. The current base already has separate owners for rear perspective, mineral pressure, yielding face, radiant vein, dense fiber, literal grain, root, tactile flex, distal edge, warm countercurve, outward fabric, and hay/moss recurrence. Without a repeated named wood failure, adding wood would answer a different question.

## Stage 1 — Four Independent Paired Screens

Run P1, P2, P3, and P4 as four separate experiments. Each experiment uses its own exact V1.4 control and V1.5 successor.

- two independently prepared control/successor sets;
- anonymous random codes;
- balanced AB/BA order;
- identical age, dilution, bottle/headspace geometry, blotters, and environment;
- free description before prompted attributes;
- no ingredient list, formula name, OAV, or expected effect shown.

Evaluate at 0, 5, and 30 minutes, 2 hours, 6 hours, and 24-hour fabric residual:

1. first object named;
2. whether Lavender Current remains the best whole-perfume name;
3. exact spice/citrus takeover terms;
4. athletic bodily motion versus shower-gel/deodorant motion;
5. citrus-to-lavender transition continuity;
6. moving fabric and living-middle volume;
7. pressure/release contrast;
8. yielding versus rigid wood;
9. late lavender-stem-hay-moss-grain recurrence;
10. comfort, harshness, fatigue, and muddiness;
11. spontaneous desire to re-smell;
12. overall liking, recorded independently of depth or strength.

A successor advances only if it improves its predicted transition and repeated re-smell desire in both independent preparations without losing lavender leadership or triggering a veto. A tie retains the simpler V1.4 control.

## Stage 3 — Citrus x Spice Interaction, One Candidate at a Time

For each surviving candidate, construct a constant-total 2 x 2 around the immutable V1.3 background:

| Arm | Added 60-uL citrus | Candidate spice center | Question |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| 00 | absent | absent | V1.3 background |
| 10 | present | absent | citrus main effect; exact V1.4 control |
| 01 | absent | present | spice main effect without candidate citrus |
| 11 | present | present | complete V1.5 successor |

DEP is the declared compensator in every arm. The interaction is:

`interaction = score(11) - score(10) - score(01) + score(00)`

A positive liking score is insufficient. The interaction must match the named transition theory in free description and improve lavender continuity/re-smell desire without increased takeover, harshness, fatigue, or muddiness.
