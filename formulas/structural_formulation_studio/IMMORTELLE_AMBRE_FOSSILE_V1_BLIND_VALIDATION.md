## 1. Questions Fixed Before Any Physical Work

1. Is immortelle recovered without prompting as the first or co-first object, rather than only after the formula name is shown?
2. Does the same subject transform from bitter pollen/hay into cured leaf and then amber-resin grain?
3. Do incense-balsamic and woody/mineral amber modules deepen that subject together, or merely add unrelated darkness and power?
4. Does the 42-row build outperform a 31-row compressed control on identity, spatial depth, transition, liking, and desire to re-smell without extra takeover failures?
5. Is Ethylene Brassylate necessary, and is it better for this exact skin-echo role than no musk, Habanolide, or Romandolide?
6. Where is the highest sweet-warm dose that preserves amber hedonism without pancake, curry-food, vanilla, tonka, or gourmand drift?

Depth, complexity, strength, longevity, and liking are scored separately. No proxy or modeled score adjudicates another endpoint.

### Structural-evidence hierarchy

The word `structure` is reserved for a passed evidence chain, not for ingredient count, module labels, an OAV ranking, or a persuasive narrative:

```text
actual-bottle identity and carrier validity
    -> one coherent immortelle-amber object
    -> one subject recognizable in two distinguishable states
    -> the states occur in the intended temporal order and recur late
    -> controlled modules causally own the predicted transitions
    -> FULL-42 earns a blinded advantage over COMP-31
```

The eight formulation modules are experimental causes, not eight objects that the assessor is expected to smell separately. The intended percept is one golden immortelle subject preserved in amber. A report of many detached recognizable objects is a fragmentation failure even when the description sounds complex or luxurious.

Published human mixture work supports both configural and elemental processing, but it does not guarantee either mode for this formula. Component recognition declines rapidly in mixtures, while prior component exposure and an analytical task can make a blending mixture seem less like a unitary target object. A 2026 preprint also found that trained-panel descriptor profiles for many two-to-ten-component mixtures were approximated well by intensity-weighted component averages. Therefore this protocol measures object unity before teaching any state, plane, material, or donor language. See the literature and transfer limits in Section 13.

## 3. Scale and Independence

Use **3,600 uL concentrate per experimental arm** (one-half of the linked formula). Every 20 uL formula row becomes a 10 uL direct transfer, preserving the raw-stock floor. Each arm can later be diluted at 24% only after authorization; for example, 720 uL of a completed arm plus 2,280 uL ethanol gives one 3.00 mL coded bottle.

For each decisive stage:

- prepare two independently compounded arm sets on different days or from independently prepared cores and variable modules;
- repeated strips or bottles from one concentrate are technical repeats, not independent preparations;
- use fresh anonymous three-character codes for every block;
- seal the key before serving;
- balance order with a Williams/Latin-square sequence or separated sessions;
- do not pool discordant blocks before auditing weights/transfers, labels, order, temperature, and evaluator notes;
- record free description before showing scales or discussing the brief.

Separate perceptual tasks into locked sessions:

1. **Synthetic/object session:** no component exposure, formula name, material list, module labels, reference perfumes, or architecture words. Collect free description, object count/unity, first object, and confidence.
2. **Non-verbal relationship session:** sort anonymous samples by similarity and collect pairwise similarity without state or plane labels.
3. **Analytical/diagnostic session:** only after the first two datasets are locked, show the pre-registered identity, state, texture, transition, takeover, and liking scales.

Do not reuse an assessor's analytical-session result as an independent synthetic-session result. Prior component exposure and task framing can alter whether a mixture is processed as a unitary object.

Development work with fewer than 12 eligible assessors is **EXPLORATORY**. A scoped panel claim requires a pre-registered sample size justified from pilot variance/effect estimates, at least 12 eligible assessors, two independent preparations, balanced order, and assessor-level results. This floor does not replace power analysis and does not authorize population-wide claims.

The 3,600 uL scale is a minimum practical design, not a mandate. A smaller method is acceptable only if every raw transfer remains at least 10 uL through pre-registered module masters and the same carrier/active comparison is preserved.

## 10. Evaluation Schedule and Ballot

Evaluate blotter first at 0, 5, 30, and 120 minutes, then 6 and 24 hours. Skin work is a separate later session after formula-specific safety approval. Lock each timepoint before continuing. Pre-register either a same-strip longitudinal design with strict sniff limits or a matched-strip cross-sectional design; do not silently mix those evidence types within one block.

### 10.1 Synthetic object record — always first

Record up to five objects or materials without showing the formula name. Then answer:

- first recognizable object;
- strongest object;
- `one coherent odor`, `one main odor with integrated facets`, or `several detached odors`;
- count and names of detached objects, if any;
- whether the same object appears to return later in changed form;
- whether the base recalls a flower, hay/maple leaf, amber resin, wood, incense, vanilla, musk, or something else;
- confidence from 0–5.

The prompted base-recall choices are shown only after the unprompted object record is locked. The synthetic record is primary for object unity and named identity.

### 10.2 Opening microsequence — TDS-style exploratory record

In a separate opening-focused analytical block, follow 0–15 minutes with a controlled sniff schedule. At each observation, select the **most striking/dominant** quality, not automatically the strongest or most pleasant. Use at most eight trained terms, fixed before decoding, selected from:

- golden dry flower;
- pollen/hay/maple flesh;
- citrus/aromatic/incense glint;
- cured leaf/root;
- balsamic amber;
- mineral dry wood;
- waxed warm skin;
- pre-registered takeover/other.

TDS-style dominance can establish a sequence but cannot establish simultaneity. Repeated sniffing can also accelerate adaptation; sniff duration, interval, nostril procedure, room conditions, and any assessor split must be pre-registered. This method transfer from food/flavor research to fine fragrance is **EXPLORATORY** until locally validated.

### 10.3 Long-window co-presence — repeated RATA/TCATA-inspired record

At 0, 5, 30, and 120 minutes, then 6 and 24 hours, collect applicability for all pre-registered qualities and allow several to coexist. Record intensity separately from applicability. This is the primary record for opposed textures and airy/dense simultaneity; a forced single dominant term cannot prove co-presence.

Pre-register the intended trajectory:

| Window | Required state evidence | Required continuity evidence | Failure modes |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–5 min | bitter golden flower/resin glint | unprompted target identity exceeds N0 | citrus, cardamom, Hedione/jasmine, or incense is the first detached object |
| 30–120 min | pollen/hay gives way to cured leaf and balsamic cereal warmth | target identity remains connected while its state changes | syrup, tobacco, vanilla, or generic floral air replaces the subject |
| 6 h | amber-resin and mineral wood become dominant | a transformed hay/leaf/immortelle cue remains more applicable than in N0 | generic woody amber, sandalwood, smoke, or musk erases the subject |
| 24 h | warm amber/skin trace | target-linked late echo remains detectable without prompting in at least part of the panel | laundry musk, blank sweetness, or anonymous fixative residue only |

**Temporal-recurrence rule:** continuous strength is not recurrence. A passing result requires an early target cue, a distinguishable middle transformation, and a late target-linked cue that exceeds N0 while the dominant state has become amber/fossil. The direction must agree across independent preparations. A static profile, a prompted-only late cue, or generic amber persistence is **NOT ESTABLISHED**.

## 11. Decision and Evidence Rules

- Decode only after all scheduled observations for a block are locked.
- Preserve raw assessor-level free descriptions, object-unity choices, similarity records, applicability selections, intensities, time stamps, and serving order.
- Treat assessor as a repeated measure and independent preparation as a block. Report distributions and uncertainty intervals; do not promote a mean score that hides preparation or assessor reversal.
- Pre-register primary endpoints and direction before decoding. Exploratory adjective mining may generate a later test but cannot retrospectively pass the current arm.
- The object gate precedes state, spatial, temporal, and hedonic interpretation. A formula that is not target-recognizable cannot pass because its labeled modules receive high architecture scores.
- The state-continuity gate precedes literal plane language. Plane labels are interpretations of a stable relationship pattern, not observations by themselves.
- One block is preliminary; two independent, directionally consistent blocks are required for scoped learning.
- An unrecognized intervention is **HOLD / NON-INFORMATIVE**, not proof of universal dormancy.
- A null result means no detected effect at this dose, matrix, assessor set, and protocol.
- If identity rises while liking falls, record the tradeoff; do not average it away.
- If depth rises while immortelle leadership falls, reject the intervention for this named perfume.
- If the formula is stronger but not more dimensional, do not call it deeper.
- If the full build and compressed control tie, prefer the compressed build.
- If a compensator or visible color difference reveals the arm, invalidate or relabel the evidence as unblinded.
- Missing codes, duplicated canonical cells, unequal totals, discordant independent preparations, or unresolved stock provenance force reconciliation/HOLD.
- Omission and compressed arms are diagnostic controls, not authorization to alter the preserved V1 master formula. A versioned formula change requires a separately accepted evidence decision.
- No physical outcome authorizes procurement, scaling, skin release, or sale without separate decisions.

## 12. Optional Instrumental Mechanism Tier — Dynamic Headspace

Measured headspace is not required to establish that assessors perceive a temporal structure. It is required before claiming that the proposed vapor-release mechanism, material-specific evaporation order, or substrate behavior has been physically demonstrated.

If separately authorized and analytically available:

1. pre-register marker compounds only after the actual Immortelle bottle and analytical standards are characterized;
2. measure coded FULL-42, COMP-31, N0, and the winning factorial arms on blotter at fixed deposited mass and controlled temperature/humidity;
3. collect HS-SPME/GC or validated dynamic-headspace measurements at matched timepoints;
4. analyze blotter and skin as separate substrates; skin follows formula-specific safety approval only;
5. compare measured marker trajectories with sensory dominance/applicability without treating correlation as proof of causal pleasantness or identity;
6. retain non-detects as detection-limit statements, not sensory absence.

Simplified published fragrance studies show that vehicle, mixture composition, musk/fixative, surface, and skin can alter release profiles. They do not validate this formula's current heuristic five-window simulation. Without measured data, the physical mechanism remains **HOLD** even if the sensory sequence passes.
