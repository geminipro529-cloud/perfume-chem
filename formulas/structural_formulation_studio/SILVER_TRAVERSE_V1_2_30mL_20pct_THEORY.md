### Pink Pepper literature boundary

Published GC-MS studies show that *Schinus* fruit oils can be monoterpene-rich but vary markedly with species, origin, maturity, and method. One ripe-fruit *S. terebinthifolius* study reported delta-3-carene, limonene, alpha-phellandrene, and alpha-pinene as major components; other fruit datasets were pinene-rich, and *S. molle* samples were rich in limonene/beta-phellandrene, alpha-phellandrene, and myrcene. These sources support a volatile pepper-citrus-spice architecture and a material-identity caution; they do not establish the composition, odor, safety, or OAV of a future supplier bottle or proprietary Pink Pepper Base.

- [Ripe-fruit Schinus terebinthifolius GC-MS study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4204964/)
- [Fruit/leaf/twig Schinus terebinthifolius GC-MS comparison](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10819699/)
- [Schinus molle fruit-oil composition study](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/j.1745-4514.2010.00391.x)

## Named Identity Contract

**Name:** Silver Traverse.  
**Subject:** charged technical sailcloth carrying luminous Pink Pepper.  
**Main note:** Pink Pepper — rosy, effervescent, berry-bright, tensile, and gently woody-spicy; never hot culinary black pepper or a literal rose accord.  
**Cold state:** Pink Pepper sparks move over bitter peel, wet silver-mineral air, and taut lavender-linalyl cloth.  
**Warm state:** the same peppered cloth dries and flexes while ambrox, transparent woods, vetiver, patchouli, and Cashmeran expose its woody-spice interior.  
**Invariant cue:** a rosy-pepper pressure signature remains classifiable through the aromatic heart and is recalled texturally by the base.  
**Emotional effect:** alert forward motion followed by controlled bodily warmth; sporty without shower-gel cheerfulness and luxurious without powder, leather, or sweetness.

The broad reference interval remains:

- [Prada Luna Rossa Eau de Toilette](https://www.prada-beauty.com/fragrance/luna-rossa/luna-rossa-eau-de-toilette/MPL01360.html): athletic lavender/mint/amber cleanliness.
- [Montblanc Explorer Eau de Parfum](https://www.montblanc.com/en-us/explorer-eau-de-parfum-100-ml-MB124069VG.html): Pink Pepper/bergamot/clary-sage movement toward patchouli, ambrox-like woods, and vetiver.
- [Montblanc Explorer Extreme Parfum](https://www.montblanc.com/en-ph/montblanc-explorer-extreme-parfum-60-ml-MB134815VG.html): a denser bergamot-patchouli-amber trajectory.

These are directional axes, not reconstruction targets.

### Takeover exclusions

- reject lavender, Dihydromyrcenol, citrus, Hedione, Iso E Super, or ambrox becoming the perfume's named lead;
- reject literal rose, lychee, plum, lipstick, iris, or cosmetic powder from the rosy support materials;
- reject hot, dry, culinary Black Pepper or trigeminal aggression;
- reject calone/melon, generic blue shower gel, mint candy, or marine identity;
- reject oud, rum, leather, smoke, creamy sandalwood, vanilla/tonka sweetness, or laundry musk;
- reject a detachable generic amberwood base that forgets the Pink-Pepper aromatic textile.
