## Research Synthesis and Its Limits

Plugin Management was used to inspect installed research capabilities and dependencies. It is installed and enabled but exposes no chemistry-literature connector; the appropriate route was therefore current repository authorities plus direct primary public sources.

The research supports four structural hypotheses:

| Candidate | Literature-informed odor-system hypothesis | Structural assignment | Main veto |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 Lemon FCF | lemon peel aroma depends on a changing terpene/aldehyde/oxygenated system, not limonene alone | hard tart voltage from front into linalyl fabric | cleaner, polish, sour beverage |
| C2 Grapefruit FCF | very-low-threshold thiols and multiple additional odorants create grapefruit identity | pithy vertical arm from front into vetiver/cedar/Iso E | sulfur, sweat, punishing bitterness |
| C3 Cedrat FCF | Citrus medica oils are rich in limonene/gamma-terpinene with lower-level aldehydic and other character contributors | dry green rind transformed into mineral grain | turpentine, citronella, rigid cedar |
| C4 Bergamot FCF | linalyl acetate, linalool, limonene, and lower-level odorants act together; vapor differs from liquid composition | seamless transition from citrus to lavender/linalyl fabric | cologne, barbershop, excessive softness |

Primary sources consulted:

- Sawamura et al., Characteristic odour components of bergamot essential oil, DOI 10.1002/ffj.1604.
- Rombola et al., equilibrium headspace composition of bergamot essential oil, PMCID PMC5668225.
- Buettner and Schieberle, most odor-active volatiles in grapefruit, PMID 10606593, DOI 10.1021/jf990071l.
- Schubert et al., structure-odor work on 1-p-menthene-8-thiol, PMID 27121638, DOI 10.1021/acs.jafc.6b01645.
- Dugo et al., Citrus medica cv. Diamante composition by extraction method, PMID 19051190, DOI 10.1002/jssc.200800404.
- Yang et al., GC-MS/GC-O characterization of finger citron oil, DOI 10.7506/spkx1002-6630-201520037.
- Cano-Lamadrid et al., lemon peel-oil volatile variation by rootstock, PMID 32102291.
- IFRA 51st Amendment standards and 2026 notice that 52nd Amendment formal notification is expected later in 2026. Formula-specific safety remains HOLD.

These sources do not establish the composition of the user's bottles, the sensory effect of 60 uL in this matrix, Thai-local liking, Cat-4 clearance, phototoxicity clearance, or release readiness.

## Why the Wood Chassis Is Fixed

The base already assigns distinct nonredundant ownership:

| Base axis | Existing owner |
|---|---|
| rear perspective | Iso E Super |
| smooth mineral pressure | Ambrofix |
| fresh yielding face | Sandalore |
| narrow dry-radiant vein | Javanol 10% future stock |
| dense inner fiber | Ebanol |
| literal grain | Cedarwood oil Virginia |
| root contour | Vetiver EO India |
| tactile flex | Cashmeran |
| distal edge | Timberol |
| warm countercurve | Azarbre |
| outward woody fabric | Romandolide, sole musk |
| hay/moss recurrence | Coumarin plus Evernyl |

No missing wood function was demonstrated. Changing wood would create a second causal variable and could make a citrus look successful merely because its base changed. A later winner-specific wood study is allowed only as a new version with an unchanged-citrus control and an explicit nonredundant function.

## Stage 1 — Five-Arm Citrus Identity Screen

Prepare nothing under this document. If preparation is later authorized, use two independent preparations per arm, anonymous random codes, identical concentrate age, identical dilution, identical bottle/headspace geometry, and carrier-matched totals.

Use a balanced 5 x 5 Williams or Latin-square order across evaluators/blocks. Run free description before any attribute list. Do not show candidate names, ingredient lists, OAVs, pipeline warnings, or expected winners.

### Identity-first ballot

At 0-5 min, 30 min, 2 h, 6 h, and 24 h fabric residual, record:

1. first object named;
2. whether Lavender Current remains the best whole-perfume name;
3. whether sport movement is bodily/kinetic versus detergent/deodorant;
4. front-to-heart continuity;
5. living middle volume;
6. yielding versus rigid wood;
7. late lavender-hay-moss-cedar-vetiver recurrence;
8. comfort and fatigue;
9. spontaneous desire to re-smell;
10. explicit veto vocabulary.

### Advancement rule

A candidate advances only if it beats CTRL in both independent blocks on:

- athletic movement without higher perceived DHM takeover;
- lavender identity and continuity;
- middle and base completion;
- comfort/fatigue;
- desire to re-smell;

and triggers none of its identity vetoes. If no candidate beats CTRL, retain V1.3. If two candidates tie, choose neither from preference scores alone; move both to Stage 2. Pipeline/OAV rank is never a tie-breaker.

For each surviving citrus, hold every aromatic row fixed except that citrus; move only the candidate citrus against DEP:

## Stage 3 — Architecture Necessity, Not Ingredient Count

Only after a citrus and dose survive:

1. compare complete pressure/release architecture with pressure absent and release absent, at equal total;
2. run independent omissions of the candidate citrus, Hedione HC, Helional/Cyclamen mineral line, Sandalore, Ebanol, trace Javanol, Azarbre, Terpinyl Acetate, Evernyl, and Romandolide;
3. compare the complete 34-aromatic-row candidate against a count-hidden compressed control;
4. a row survives only when its predicted perceptual consequence is reproduced in both preparations;
5. a tie removes the tested row, except that historical V1.3 rows remain unchanged until their own successor study is authorized.

## Stage 4 — Wood Change Only If a Failure Names the Missing Function

A new wood version is allowed only if the surviving citrus repeatedly fails on a named function such as missing root continuity, absent yielding interior, or insufficient rear perspective. The new arm must add one exact-function wood candidate against DEP while a same-citrus unchanged-wood control is retained. “More depth,” a higher score, or a desire for more ingredients is insufficient.

## Stage 5 — Climate and Intended Local Wearer

Only after technical blind survival, run a crossover between controlled temperate-indoor and Bangkok-relevant warm/humid wear conditions. Climate behavior cannot establish Thai-local preference. Any local-market claim requires separately authorized evaluation by intended local Thai men, recruited and analyzed as that population rather than tourists, expatriates, or foreign buyers.
