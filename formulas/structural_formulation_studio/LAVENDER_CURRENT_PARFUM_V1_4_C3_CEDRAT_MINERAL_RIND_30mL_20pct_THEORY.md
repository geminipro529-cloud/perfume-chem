## Candidate Theory

**Name:** Cedrat Mineral Rind.  
**One sentence:** the accepted lavender current receives a green, dry citron-rind plane that touches rosemary in front and reappears as mineral cedar/vetiver grain behind the heart.  
**Why this is not merely “more citrus”:** Cedrat is assigned as a material bridge between citrus peel and wood, not as juice. Red Mandarin remains the warm reflection; Cedrat supplies green rind, gamma-terpinene lift, and a faint woody implication that anticipates the unchanged base.  
**Hedonic hypothesis:** the rind-to-mineral continuity should make the formula feel more architectural and less generically blue while retaining bodily ease from DHM and tactile release from Sandalore. This remains NOT TESTED.  
**Failure veto:** turpentine, citronella, medicinal rind, aggressive green bitterness, furniture polish, or a mineral/cedar line that becomes rigid and joyless.

Primary Citrus medica studies report high limonene and gamma-terpinene with aldehydic and other lower-level contributors, while GC-O work shows that abundant constituents are not automatically the only characteristic odorants. The repository has a Cedrat FCF constituent model, but species, extraction, FCF processing, user-bottle composition, and lot-specific thresholds are not established; composite output is a hypothesis only.
