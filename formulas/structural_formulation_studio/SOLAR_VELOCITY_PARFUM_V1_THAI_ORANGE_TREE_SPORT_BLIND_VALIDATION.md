## Purpose and Pre-Registered Questions

This protocol prevents one pleasant complete bottle from being used as evidence for every row. It asks ten separable questions:

1. Does the clean-sheet orange-tree direction beat the rejected pepper direction at equal finished raw-concentrate fraction?
2. Do Blood Orange/Grapefruit peel and Petitgrain/Neroli/Nerolin leaf-flower continuity create one whole-orange-tree object rather than independent citrus, green, and floral notes?
3. What is the lowest Dihydromyrcenol level that makes the fragrance unmistakably sport without generic-blue, shower-gel, deodorant, or fatigue takeover?
4. Does Ginger add desirable kinetic cut without becoming tonic, kitchen spice, or heat?
5. Do the bitter/mineral pressure and radiant/soft-wood release modules create a more desirable transition together than either module alone?
6. Does the 33-aromatic-row complete topology beat its controlled 23-row compression?
7. Does Paradisamide preserve citrus light after the volatile opening without becoming a tropical-fruit object?
8. Does restrained Tonkarome create attractive dry comfort without sweetness or hot-weather burden?
9. Does Romandolide own a necessary outward woody-fabric role, and is it preferable to no musk or one equal-volume Zenolide alternative?
10. Does the selected architecture remain pleasurable and legibly sporty across documented air-conditioned and hot-humid conditions?

“Maximally hedonic” is operationalized as a replicated Pareto choice: the selected arm must improve or preserve liking and desire to re-smell while preserving sport identity, orange-tree coherence, hot-weather comfort, and structural transition, with no increase in takeover, fatigue, or muddiness. It is not the arm with the largest modeled OAV, raw dose, projection score, or ingredient count.

## Common Master and Sample Logic

For each stage and independent block:

1. Build the stated common-core and variable-module masters at no less than one full formula-equivalent scale. Direct raw-stock measurements in those masters must be at least 10 uL.
2. Homogenize and rest every compared master for the same recorded interval. The repository's predicted seven-day maturation is not treated as proof that seven days is sufficient; a maturation ladder is required if the odor continues to change.
3. For a center 6-mL evaluation sample, use 20% of the 6,000-uL formula-equivalent master: 1,200 uL concentrate plus 4,800 uL ethanol 96%.
4. For any diagnostic master whose raw total differs from 6,000 uL, take the exact proportional aliquot that produces the same stated finished raw-concentrate fraction. Record that the diagnostic is not the frozen center formula.
5. A dispensed aliquot from a homogeneous master may be below 10 uL only if it is not a direct raw-stock measurement and the pipette is validated for that aliquot. Otherwise enlarge every master and sample by the same factor.
6. Prepare a new independent common core and new modules for Block 2. Repouring Block 1 material is a coded duplicate, not independent preparation.

DEP is only a declared constant-total compensator hypothesis. It is not assumed sensorially, physically, or thermodynamically neutral; a matrix-control comparison is required if a result could plausibly be caused by its changed level.

## Stage 1 — Clean Sheet Versus Rejected Pepper Direction

Prepare the frozen Solar Velocity 6,000-uL master and the frozen Pepper Current V1.1 6,600-uL master independently. Dilute an aliquot of each to the same 20% finished raw-concentrate fraction in identical 6-mL coded samples. This is a direction-selection screen only. The formulas differ in subject, concentration history, carrier distribution, and many material doses. A winner cannot identify a causal row.

## Stage 10 — Climate-Robustness and Target-Wearer Hedonic Confirmation

Run only after Stages 2-9 select a successor and formula arithmetic is re-frozen.

### Environmental crossover

Repeat the selected successor and the most informative nearby control in separate sessions under:

- **air-conditioned condition:** target 22-24 C, relative humidity recorded;
- **hot-humid condition:** target 30-34 C, relative humidity recorded, without heating bottles or exposing materials to uncontrolled sunlight.

Use the same independent blocks, blotter loading, read distances, and balanced code order. Do not compare samples that experienced different storage or maturation. Temperature/humidity effects are interactions, not proof of Bangkok-wide wear performance.

### Target-wearer screen if separately authorized

After internal structural screening and safety clearance, use a pre-registered blinded target-wearer panel of adult Thai-resident men from the intended age and fragrance-usage range. A practical first screen is at least 24 completed participants, balanced across code order; it is a directional product-development panel, not a population-representative market study. Preserve individual ballots and report missingness, duplicate reliability, and distributions rather than only means.

Advance only if the selected perfume improves or preserves:

- overall liking;
- desire to re-smell;
- desire to wear in Thai heat;
- recognizable sport/active identity;
- modern masculine fit;
- orange-tree coherence;
- comfort at personal and social distance;

without increasing cleaner, soda, body wash, detergent, deodorant, floral soap, generic blue, ambrox wall, sweetness, fatigue, headache/irritation reports, or old-fashioned cologne recognition. Purchase intent is secondary and must be asked only after the blind odor ballot.

## Coding, Presentation, and Blinding

- Generate non-semantic random three-character codes before pouring.
- Keep the decoding key inaccessible until all ballots in a block are locked.
- Balance order with a Williams or other appropriate sequence; never present dose ladders in ascending order.
- Use identical visual presentation. Any color or opacity difference that reveals an EO arm must be masked safely or the comparison marked unblinded/HOLD.
- Use no more than four arms per session and separate stages to limit adaptation and cognitive overload.
- Include one coded duplicate pour per session to estimate reading consistency. A duplicate is not an independent preparation.
- Use a new common core, variable modules, codes, and order key for Block 2.
- Record anosmia/adaptation checks and stop if the evaluator cannot rank a reference odor consistently or reports irritation.

## Inference and Decision Rules

- One blinded block is preliminary. Two independently prepared blocks with the same directional result support only replicated learning within this protocol.
- A whole-formula winner cannot identify its causal material.
- A 2 x 2 interaction supports the named module relationship, not every row in the modules.
- A retained finishing row needs a complete-versus-compressed win followed by a matching leave-one-out or add-back effect.
- An undetected omission is **HOLD / NON-INFORMATIVE**, not proof that a material supplies invisible luxury or depth.
- An equal hedonic result with fewer materials or fewer takeover failures wins.
- Missing cells, code leakage, unequal totals, different maturation, direct sub-floor dosing, stock drift, visible unmasked differences, or unlogged climate differences make the affected contrast HOLD.
- Modeled OAV, pipeline PASS/WARN, current retail assortment, and a directional panel cannot establish safety, stability, commercial performance, Thai population preference, or release readiness.
