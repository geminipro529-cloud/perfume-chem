## Named Identity Contract

**Name:** Mineral Grain Parfum.  
**One-sentence identity:** a cool wet-mineral wood surface accelerates through clean aromatic air, then yields into sun-warmed sandalwood grain, cedar fiber, and dry vetiver skin.  
**One subject:** a piece of modern mineral wood shown wet and cold at the surface, flexible and warm inside, then dry-grained on skin.  
**Emotional effect:** athletic composure, tactile polish, restrained sensuality, and a compelling warm return after the clean opening.  
**Wear context:** daily work, commuting, active leisure, gym-to-social transition, and warm evenings; projected intensity must remain socially comfortable.  
**Minimum recognizable nucleus:** Dihydromyrcenol + Helional/mineral points + Iso E/Ambrofix + Sandalore/Polysantol/Ebanol + cedar/vetiver recurrence.  
**Red Mandarin role:** short warm glint around the wet surface; if citrus remains the perfume's public identity, the dose fails.  
**Anti-copy boundary:** named sport and blue-parfum references define only broad fresh aromatic, mineral amber, and polished wood expectations. No donor formula or signature accord is reconstructed.

5. **Rear-pressure test:** Ambrofix/Cedramber/Timberol/vetiver pressure crossed with Sandalore/Hedione/Cashmeran/Azarbre release.
