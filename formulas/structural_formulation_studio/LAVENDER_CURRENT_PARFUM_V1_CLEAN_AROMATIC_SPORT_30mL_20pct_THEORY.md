## Named Identity Contract

**Name:** Lavender Current Parfum.  
**One-sentence identity:** cool crushed lavender and clean moving air open into luminous aromatic fabric, then settle as warm lavender-tonka grain over mineral sandalwood and dry vetiver.  
**One subject:** a lavender current moving through three physical states — crushed cool herb, clean luminous fabric, and warm dry aromatic wood.  
**Emotional effect:** freshly groomed, energetic, composed, and attractive at conversational distance rather than aggressively projecting.  
**Wear context:** repeated daily use, commuting, office, active leisure, and air-conditioned-to-hot-humid transitions.  
**Minimum recognizable nucleus:** Lavender EO High Altitude + Linalyl Acetate + Linalool/Ethyl Linalool + Dihydromyrcenol + Hedione HC + Tonkarome/Evernyl/cedar recurrence.  
**Red Mandarin role:** front glint and momentary warmth only; if orange or mandarin is the first persistent object, leadership has failed.  
**Anti-copy boundary:** the requested Prada/blue-parfum references supply broad sporty, aromatic, mineral, and polished-wood expectations only. No named perfume's formula, signature accord, or ingredient identity is reconstructed.
