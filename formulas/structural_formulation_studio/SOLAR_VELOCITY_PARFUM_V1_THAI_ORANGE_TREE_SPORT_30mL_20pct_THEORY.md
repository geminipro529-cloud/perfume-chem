These official descriptions support abstract fresh-aromatic-woody and orange-tree axes only; this formula does not copy either perfume: [YSL Y Eau de Parfum](https://www.yslbeautyus.com/fragrance/mens-fragrance/y/y-eau-de-parfum/727YSL.html), [Versace Pour Homme](https://www.versace.com/us/en/pour-homme-edt-100-ml/R720010-R100MLS.html).

## Named Identity Contract

**Name:** Solar Velocity Parfum.  
**One-sentence identity:** an iced, juicy orange-tree current accelerates through aromatic air, then warms into clean citrus wood, mineral amber, and dry skin.  
**Recognizable subject:** the whole orange tree in motion — Blood Orange peel, Petitgrain leaf/twig, Neroli flower, and a persistent orange-blossom/citrus memory — not a generic blue accord.  
**Emotional effect:** instant uplift and bodily energy followed by smooth, attractive, close-wearing confidence.  
**Wear context:** Thai daytime, commuting, active leisure, gym-to-social transition, and hot-weather evening; it must survive air-conditioning-to-outdoor transitions without becoming cloying or skeletal.  
**Minimum recognizable nucleus:** Blood Orange + Petitgrain Paraguay + Neroli stock + Dihydromyrcenol + Hedione HC + Sandalore/Ambrofix/Vetiver echo.  
**Market positioning hypothesis:** designer-prestige sport parfum with immediate mainstream legibility and more internal architecture than a simple fresh-ambrox formula.  
**Explicit anti-copy boundary:** current retail products establish only broad market axes. No donor formula, signature accord, or named-perfume reconstruction is inferred.

| laundry musk | clean fabric or musk is named instead of orange-tree wood | reject Romandolide level; do not add another musk |
| old-fashioned cologne | lavender, neroli soap, or barbershop becomes the public identity | reduce aromatic/floral support even if technically coherent |
