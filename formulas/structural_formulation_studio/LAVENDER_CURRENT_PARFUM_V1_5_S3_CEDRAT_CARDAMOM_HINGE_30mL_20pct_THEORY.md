## Candidate Theory

**Name:** Cedrat-Cardamom Hinge.  
**One sentence:** the cedrat-rind control receives a minimum-measurable green cardamom hinge whose cineolic face cools the rind while its terpinyl-acetate face joins the linalyl and Terpinyl Acetate recurrence system.  
**New spice function:** a green cineolic/terpinyl hinge that bends cedrat rind into lavender esters and then into the existing mineral-air seam.  
**Hedonic hypothesis:** The cool-to-warm cardamom hinge may make the angular cedrat architecture feel alive and expensive, with release occurring when the medicinal edge dissolves into soft lavender fabric. This is a design hypothesis, not a sensory result.  
**Failure veto:** toothpaste, eucalyptus, medicinal chest rub, chai, curry, La Nuit de L'Homme drift, classic cologne, or cardamom leadership.

### Alternative gate

Ginger would push cedrat toward beverage/citral; Black Pepper would duplicate dry angularity; Juniper would make the rind coniferous and potentially gin-like; Clary Sage would make the result more conventionally fougere rather than supplying a discrete hinge.

### Literature and local-data boundary

[Primary source](https://doi.org/10.1080/14786419.2018.1561687): Primary GC-MS work on Elettaria cardamomum varieties reports 1,8-cineole around 29-35% and alpha-terpinyl acetate as a major ester, supporting a two-faced cool-cineolic/soft-terpinyl hinge rather than generic warmth.

Cardamom EO is current neat inventory and has an exact-name composite model. The model assumes generic fractions and is not a bottle assay. The active monomolecular ODT registry says 3 ppb while C.yaml says 25 ppb; composite output is still only a sensitivity diagnostic and bottle-specific OAV remains HOLD.

Literature composition is not silently assigned to the user's bottle. Species/chemotype, extraction, supplier lot, oxidation state, bottle headspace, current safety documentation, and behavior in this formula remain lot-specific or HOLD.
