# PRE-DILUTION: Musk Ketone 10% in DPG
## Required for Jasmin d'Orris formulas

> **⚠️ Inventory Check:** Read [`inventory.txt`](/inventory.txt) before use.

**Reason:** Musk Ketone is pure crystalline powder. Pre-dilution to 10% in DPG enables precise dosing. Per synergy_reference.md: *"Musk Ketone is the ONLY musk that echoes iris-powder character."*

### MAKE:

| Step | Material | Amount |
|------|----------|--------|
| 1 | Musk Ketone powder | 100 mg |
| 2 | DPG (Dipropylene Glycol) | 900 µL |
| 3 | **Shake well, warm gently if needed, label "Musk Ketone 10% in DPG"** | |

**Storage:** Amber vial, dark cupboard. If crystals reform, warm to 30°C.

## PRE-DILUTION: Myristic Acid 15% in Ethanol
### Required for Jasmin d'Orris Collaboration Edition (1.5 g / 10 mL)

> **⚠️ Inventory Check:** Read [`inventory.txt`](/inventory.txt) before use.

**Reason:** Per `perfumery_hacks_reference.md` §1: Myristic Acid –COOH forms H-bonds with ionone carbonyls, softening the synthetic violet edge. Natural orris butter is ~80% myristic acid (40:1 ratio). Functional perfumery minimum: 2:1 myristic:irone. Collaboration Edition targets 12:1 for near-natural orris butter chemistry. ODT = 5,000 ppb / 1,000 ppm (near-odorless — functions chemically, not aromatically).

### MAKE:

| Step | Material | Amount |
|------|----------|--------|
| 1 | Myristic Acid powder | **1,500 mg** (1.5 g — weigh on mg scale) |
| 2 | Ethanol 96% | add to **10 mL total** (~8.5 mL ethanol) |
| 3 | Warm to 30–40°C (water bath) until fully dissolved | |
| 4 | **Label "Myristic Acid 15% in EtOH — 1.5 g/10 mL"** | |

**Storage:** Amber glass vial with PTFE-lined cap. Dark cupboard at 15–30°C. Shake before each use — fatty acids may settle. Shelf life ~12 months.

### Usage (Jasmin d'Orris Collaboration)

Pipette **600 µL** from this stock into the mixing beaker during the orris block (Step 2, material #25). This delivers **90 mg active** Myristic Acid at **12:1** myristic:irone ratio.

**Thailand notes:** At Thai ambient (28–33°C), no warming needed. The acid dissolves readily at room temp. Crystallization risk is zero. Shake before each use anyway.
