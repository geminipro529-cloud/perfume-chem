# Osmanthus Marine Brise — 30mL EdP

## Character
A marine-aquatic composition where osmanthus absolute provides an unexpected warm-floral current beneath the cool ocean surface. Instead of the typical calone-melon aquatic (Calone is depleted), this uses Helional, Floralozone, and Dihydromyrcenol for a more mineral-sea-spray effect. The osmanthus acts as the "warm current" — a hidden apricot-floral undertow that prevents the aquatic structure from reading as sterile or synthetic. Think Hermès Un Jardin en Méditerranée meets Heeley Sel Marin, with osmanthus replacing fig.

**Family**: Aquatic / Marine — mineral-sea-spray, osmanthus warm current, clean-musk drydown

## Material Justification

- **Dihydromyrcenol (400 µL)**: The marine-fresh workhorse — provides the lime-metallic-clean backbone that defines the aquatic family. The largest single dose because marine compositions need a volatile-fresh anchor. At 400 µL, it's the primary projection carrier in the opening.

- **Helional (80 µL) + Floralozone (10%, 100 µL = 10 µL active)**: The sea-mist + ozonic duo. Helional provides the mineral-sea-spray character (distinct from Calone's melon-marine); Floralozone adds violet-like ozonic freshness. Chosen together because no single material replaces Calone — two materials at moderate doses create a more nuanced marine effect than overdosing either.

- **Scentenal (1%, 50 µL = 0.5 µL active) + Triplal (10 µL)**: Ultra-trace marine-green effects. Scentenal at sub-1 µL active provides metallic-green ozone; Triplal at 10 µL (near the ceiling for its potency) adds watery-aldehydic freshness. Both kept at trace to avoid the harsh-metallic overdose.

- **Zenolide (180 µL)**: The coldest musk in the inventory — citrusy-fresh character that echoes the grapefruit opening. Chosen as the primary character-echo musk because aquatic compositions need cold, clean projection. Rejects: Galaxolide (too detergent-synthetic), Habanolide (too warm-skin), Musk Ketone (powder clashes with marine).

- **Macrolide (10%, 60 µL = 6 µL active) + Exaltolide (10%, 70 µL = 7 µL active)**: Barely-there musk depth pair — Macrolide provides clean-soft whisper, Exaltolide adds skin-fatty oceanic intimacy. Together they create the "your skin, but at the beach" effect without reading as perfumed.

- **Benzyl Salicylate (180 µL)**: Used at modest dose as a light cosmetic cushion — marine compositions need some diffusion weight to carry the osmanthus through the drydown. Kept lower than in heavier formulas to maintain transparency. The salicylate cushion also helps the osmanthus read as a warm current rather than a disconnected floral.

- **Cedramber (120 µL) + Clearwood (80 µL)**: Clean-woody pair — Cedramber bridges cedar-amber registers providing warmth without density; Clearwood provides patchouli-free earthiness. Both chosen because traditional heavy woods (Patchouli EO, Vetiver) would sink the marine transparency.

- **No Calone**: Depleted — and the Helional/Floralozone/Triplal/Scentenal combination creates a more sophisticated mineral-marine effect than Calone's single-note melon-aquatic.
