## DESIGN RATIONALE

**Family:** Chypre — Mineral-gin opening → juniper-cardamom heart → chypre-amber drydown  
**Concept:** Rain-on-limestone petrichor meets gin-botanical clarity. Cool, transparent, zero florals.  
**Pivot from v1:** Jasmine heart removed — fought the cold-stone register with indolic warmth. Replaced with Juniper Berry EO + Cardamom EO gin accord: cool, peppery, cineolic, mineral-compatible.  
**Gin accord:** Juniper (coniferous-gin backbone) + Cardamom (spicy-cineolic sparkle) — the classic gin pairing, sitting against Geosmin petrichor and Calone marine.  
**Jasmine removed entirely:** cis-Jasmone, Jasmine Sambac, Dihydrojasmone, Benzyl Acetate, Indole, γ-Undecalactone, Hedione HC — all cut.  
**Orris removed:** Alpha Irone + Orivone + AIMI + Beta Ionone — stripped. Iris was the #3 OAV signal competing with the gin accord. Pure mineral-gin, no floral warmth of any kind.  
**Mr. Sandman aldehydic note eliminated:** Aldehyde C12 MNA dropped from 100 µL to 30 µL. Bergamot slightly reduced. Hedione kept at 1,200 µL for radiance but without cis-Jasmone it reads transparent, not jasminic.  
**Iso E Super added:** Universal volume/transparency builder — missing from v1.  
**Chypre backbone retained:** Evernyl + Cashmeran + Ebanol + Ambrettolide + EB.

## PROCEDURE

1. **Dose Base** (1→6). Swirl after each. Iso E Super first — it's the canvas.
2. **Dose Heart — Gin** (7→9). Juniper + Cardamom should read as gin-and-tonic clarity. Swirl.
3. **Dose Top** (10→19) quickly — ≤60 seconds. Cap immediately.
4. **Add ethanol** in 3 portions. Swirl 15s between.
5. **Macerate** 4 weeks minimum, dark, 18-22°C.
