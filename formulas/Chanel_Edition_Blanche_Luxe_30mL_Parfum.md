## 1. What Édition Blanche Smells Like

**The short version:** "Lemon meringue pie" — creamy lemon-vanilla-sandalwood with a crystalline, "hot and cold" tension.

**The detailed version:**

| Phase | Character |
|-------|-----------|
| **Opening (0-30 min)** | Bright lemon zest + bergamot — sharp, invigorating, high-quality. "Pure, refined citrus energy." |
| **Heart (1-2 hrs)** | Creamy sandalwood emerges — lemon softens into "lemon meringue pie" accord. Smooth, sophisticated, slightly gourmand without being sweet. |
| **Drydown (4+ hrs)** | Warm vanilla-vetiver — soft, woody, slightly sweet. Lemon creaminess lingers. "Professional, comforting, undeniably chic." |

**Jacques Polge's concept:** "Diamond fragrance" — crystalline quality, tension between hot (lemon) and cold (sandalwood-vanilla).

**Key character from reviews:**
- "The most expensive lemon scent"
- "White linen shirt elegance"
- "Lemon meringue pie but sophisticated"
- "Like Xerjoff Uden but cleaner"

## 6. The "Lemon Meringue Pie" Triangle

The signature accord of Édition Blanche:

| Material | Role in Triangle |
|----------|-----------------|
| **Lemon FCF** (500 µL) | The lemon zest — sharp, bright, persistent |
| **Vanillin + Ethyl Vanillin** (40 µL active) | The meringue — sweet, creamy, not gourmand |
| **Javanol + Ebanol** (600 µL) | The pie crust — warm, buttery sandalwood |

This triangle is what makes Édition Blanche unique. The lemon stays present even in the drydown (reviewers confirm), the vanilla is creamy not sweet, and the sandalwood provides warmth without heaviness.

## 8. Temporal Architecture

### Act I: Lemon Zest (0–30 min)
**Dominant headspace:** Lemon, Bergamot, Cardamom, Hedione  
Sharp, bright, invigorating. "Pure, refined citrus energy." The cardamom adds spicy-fresh lift. Hedione makes it volumetric.

### Act II: Lemon Meringue (30 min–2 hrs)
**Dominant headspace:** Lemon, Sandalwood, Vanilla, Iso E Super  
The famous "lemon meringue pie" accord emerges. Lemon softens into the creamy sandalwood. Vanilla adds sweetness without gourmand quality. Iso E Super creates molecular cocoon.

### Act III: Warm Skin (2–6 hrs)
**Dominant headspace:** Sandalwood, Vanilla, Musk, Vetiver  
Warm, elegant skin scent. Vanilla-vetiver provide soft, woody, slightly sweet base. Musk bed creates clean, intimate drydown. Lemon creaminess lingers.

### Act IV: Clean Memory (6+ hrs)
**Dominant headspace:** Musk, Sandalwood, Vetiver  
White musk + sandalwood + vetiver skin scent. Clean, elegant, masculine.

## 9. Dosing & Mixing Notes

1. **Order of addition:** Iso E Super → Javanol → Habanolol → Lemon FCF → Bergamot → Ebanol → Cedarwood → Romandolide → Vetiver → Sandalwood FO → Coumarin → Vanillin → Ethyl Vanillin → Benzyl Salicylate → Hedione → Galaxolide → Ambrox Super → Amberwood F → Linalool → AIMI → Vertofix → Azarbre → Kephalis → Cashmeran → Tonkarome → Patchouli → Black Pepper → Cardamom. Ethanol last.
2. **Lemon FCF at 500 µL** is the star — do not reduce. Reviewers say lemon persists throughout the scent's life.
3. **Vanillin + Ethyl Vanillin** — keep doses subtle. The vanilla should be "creamy" not "sweet." If it reads too gourmand, reduce Ethyl Vanillin to 100 µL.
4. **Coumarin at 20%** — 50 µL active. This is the Allure drydown DNA. Do not increase beyond 75 µL active or it shifts toward fougère.
5. **Maceration:** 2 weeks minimum. The lemon-sandalwood-vanilla triangle needs time to integrate. First 3 days: lemon will read sharp. Week 1: sandalwood emerges. Week 2: "lemon meringue pie" accord fully develops.
6. **Cardamom as ginger substitute** — if you acquire Ginger EO, replace Cardamom with Ginger at 40 µL for more authentic spicy-fresh lift.
