# Rose Tuberose Bouquet — 30mL EdP

## Character
A lush white floral bouquet where Rose de Mai Absolute and Tuberose Absolute form a dual-floral heart, supported by a jasmine-muguet-ylang chorus. This is the most floral, most feminine composition in the Rose collection — not a rose soliflore but a rose-centric bouquet where tuberose adds its creamy, narcotic-indolic richness alongside the rose. Tuberose Absolute (10%) at 200 µL and Tuberalia base at 150 µL create a natural-synthetic tuberose accord that provides the white-floral depth without the full cost of natural tuberose absolute. Jasmine Sambac (10%) adds green-indolic jasmine complexity, while Ylang Comoros Complete EO bridges the rose to the white florals with its creamy-banana floral warmth. Dihydrojasmone and Hedione HC provide the jasmine-hedione radiance that makes the entire bouquet project. The rose core is the most complex (5 materials plus all 3 modifiers) because the rose must stand as an equal partner to the tuberose. The base uses Benzyl Salicylate as the primary fixative — its cosmetic-white-floral character is the perfect diffusion base for a white floral bouquet. Think a more transparent Fracas, or Frederic Malle Carnal Flower with rose as co-star instead of soloist.

**Family**: White Floral (rose-tuberose) — citrus top, rose-tuberose-jasmine heart, cosmetic-musk base

## Material Justification

- **Rose de Mai Absolute (10%)**: The natural rose anchor at 400 µL (40 µL active, 0.69% of concentrate). Paired with Tuberose Absolute at 200 µL (20 µL active) in a 2:1 rose-to-tuberose ratio — the rose leads the bouquet, the tuberose provides the creamy-indolic depth. The absolute's honeyed-spicy warmth is the natural partner to tuberose's narcotic richness.

- **Tuberose Absolute (10%) + Tuberalia base**: The tuberose accord at 200 + 150 µL. Tuberose Absolute (20 µL active) provides the authentic natural tuberose character — creamy, narcotic, slightly rubbery-indolic. Tuberalia base (150 neat) extends the tuberose accord with synthetic tuberose-lactonic depth — it adds the creamy-white-floral body that natural tuberose absolute at 20 µL active cannot provide alone. This 2-material accord creates a convincing tuberose presence at a fraction of what pure natural tuberose absolute would cost.

- **Citronellol + Geraniol + PEA + Rhodinol + Nerol**: The complete 5-material rose accord at constrained doses. Citronellol (280) provides fresh-citrus-rose lift — the brightness that prevents the white florals from becoming heavy. Geraniol (60) at maximum — sweet-geranium body. PEA (360) fills honeyed-rose volume — essential because the tuberose competes for the same olfactory bandwidth. Rhodinol (30) at maximum — the natural geranium-rose richness adds the organic complexity that prevents the rose from reading as synthetic against the natural tuberose absolute. Nerol (100) adds sweet citrusy-rose lift that connects the rose to the bergamot-petitgrain top.

- **Damascone Beta (10%) + Damascenone (1%) + Rose Oxide (10%)**: The full rose modifier triad — all three because the rose must hold its own against tuberose's powerful character. Damascone Beta at 80 µL (8 µL active) provides dark fruity-rose ketone — the plum-apple facet that bridges rose to the fruity-lactonic facets of tuberose. Damascenone at 60 µL (0.6 µL active) provides honeyed-woody rose depth. Rose Oxide at 50 µL (5 µL active) adds metallic-green freshness — the green counterpoint to tuberose's creamy opacity.

- **Jasmine Sambac (10%) + Dihydrojasmone + Hedione HC**: The jasmine-hedione radiance system. Jasmine Sambac at 180 µL of 10% (18 µL active) provides green-indolic jasmine complexity — the natural jasmine that bridges rose to tuberose (jasmine, rose, and tuberose are the classical white-floral trio). Dihydrojasmone at 40 µL adds the warm-fatty jasmine body — the jasmine depth that Hedione alone cannot provide. Hedione HC at 540 µL — the highest dose of any Rose formula — provides maximum jasmine-hedione radiance, lifting the entire bouquet with the luminous floral projection that white florals demand.

- **Ylang Comoros Complete EO + Bourgeonal**: The creamy-muguet bridge at 120 + 30 µL. Ylang Complete EO provides the banana-custard creamy floral warmth that connects the rose-tuberose heart to the cosmetic base — ylang's benzoate esters (benzyl benzoate, benzyl salicylate) are the same molecules used as fixatives, making ylang a natural bridge between the floral heart and the fixative base. Bourgeonal at 30 µL adds the green-muguet lily-of-the-valley facet — the clean white-floral brightness that prevents the bouquet from becoming indolic-heavy.

- **Benzyl Salicylate**: The primary fixative at 440 µL — the highest Benzyl Salicylate dose of any Rose formula because WHITE FLORALS ARE WHERE BENZYL SALICYLATE BELONGS. Its cosmetic-warm salicylate character is not just acceptable in a white floral — it is the defining textural element. Benzyl Salicylate's natural affinity for tuberose, jasmine, and ylang (all three naturally contain or benefit from salicylate diffusion) makes it the perfect fixative for this composition. The 440 µL dose creates maximum white-floral diffusion volume.

- **Benzyl Benzoate**: Inert mass fixative at 240 µL — adds molecular weight without competing with the florals. Paired with Benzyl Salicylate to create a two-layer fixative system (heavy cosmetic + inert mass).

- **Galaxolide (50% in DEP)**: Character-echo + projection musk at 300 µL of 50% (150 µL active). In a white floral bouquet, Galaxolide's synthetic-clean-fruity character is the ideal projection musk — it extends the floral sillage outward with the clarity that white florals need to avoid becoming heavy. The fruity-floral facet of Galaxolide harmonizes naturally with the jasmine-tuberose accord. Chosen over Romandolide (too woody — would fight the floral character) and Zenolide (too cold-citrus — would clash with the creamy tuberose).

- **Ethylene Brassylate**: Creamy depth musk at 280 µL — its lactonic-milky quality directly echoes the creamy-lactonic character of tuberose. EB is the only musk that can harmonize with tuberose's natural creaminess without competing. Chosen over all other depth musks because the EB-tuberose synergy (lactonic-milky + creamy-tuberose) is a natural pairing.

- **Macrolide (10%)**: Gentle skin-sit musk at 180 µL of 10% (18 µL active). The "barely-there" musk — chosen because the florals should dominate. Macrolide provides just enough musky skin-adhesion to make the fragrance feel personal without the musk asserting itself. Chosen over Habanolide (too warm-skin — would add an intimate note that distracts from the floral bouquet), Ambrettolide (too fruity-wine — would blur into the ylang), and Exaltolide (too fatty — would fight the clean floral projection).

- **Iso E Super + Sandalore + Ambrofix + Vertofix + Cashmeran**: The transparent base structure. Iso E Super (380 µL) creates the molecular skin-cocoon. Sandalore (160 µL) provides the lightest sandalwood — its citrus-woody freshness keeps the base from becoming heavy. Ambrofix (120 µL, 36 active) adds crystalline depth. Vertofix (160 µL) bridges wood and musk. Cashmeran (100 µL) adds textile-warmth — the "cashmere wrap" that softens the entire bouquet.

- **Bergamot FCF + Petitgrain EO Paraguay + Linalool + Linalyl Acetate**: The clean citrus top at 360 + 140 + 200 + 100 µL. Bergamot FCF provides classical freshness. Petitgrain EO Paraguay adds green-woody citrus lift — the petitgrain character bridges the bergamot to the indolic-green facets of jasmine and tuberose. Chosen over standard Petitgrain EO (depleted). Linalool + Linalyl Acetate extend the citrus opening. No other citrus materials because the bouquet needs a clean frame, not a citrus character — the flowers are the stars.
