# Rose Oud Oriental — 30mL EdP

## Character
An oriental rose where Rose de Mai Absolute's honeyed-spicy character meets a constructed oud accord built from IBQ leather, Nagarmortha cypriol smoke, and amber-labdanum warmth. Rather than relying on real oud oil (not in inventory), this composition assembles an oud impression from the leathery-animalic facet of Isobutyl Quinoline, the dry-earthy-smoky character of Nagarmortha Oil, and the balsamic-saffron warmth of Ethyl Safranate and Siam Benzoin. The rose core is deliberately restrained (Geraniol at 60 µL, Rhodinol at 30 µL) to prevent the rose alcohols from competing with the dark, complex oriental base. Damascenone and Rose Oxide provide rose character through trace impact rather than alcohol mass. The musk chord (Ethylene Brassylate depth, Habanolide warm-skin intimacy, Romandolide projection) wraps the composition in a rich, persistent trail. Think a cleaner, more floral Oud Ispahan or a rose-forward Oud Wood.

**Family**: Oriental Floral (oud-rose) — saffron-cardamom top, rose-oud heart, amber-balsamic-musk base

## Material Justification

- **Rose de Mai Absolute (10%)**: The natural rose anchor at 420 µL (42 µL active, 0.71% of concentrate). The absolute's honeyed-spicy warmth provides the organic rose character that stands up to the dark oriental base — without the natural absolute, the molecular rose would be swallowed by the oud accord and balsamic base.

- **Citronellol + Geraniol + PEA + Rhodinol + Nerol**: The molecular rose accord — constrained per OAV overdose prevention. Citronellol (300, boosted from 250 to compensate for Geraniol reduction) provides fresh-citrus-rose lift. Geraniol (60, reduced from 180) at the new maximum — provides sweet-geranium body without OAV explosion. PEA (400, boosted from 350) fills honeyed-rose volume. Rhodinol (30, reduced from 150) at the new maximum — just enough natural geranium-rose richness. Nerol (90) adds sweet citrusy-rose lift. The leaner rose alcohol profile is intentional — in an oriental, the rose should be the floral counterpoint to darkness, not an overpowering rose soliflore.

- **Damascenone (1%) + Rose Oxide (10%)**: The rose modifier pair. Damascenone at 80 µL (0.8 µL active) — the most powerful rose-impact molecule — provides the honeyed-woody depth that bridges rose to the oud-amber base. Rose Oxide at 60 µL (6 µL active) adds metallic-green geranium leaf. Only two modifiers (vs three in the Patchouli Moderne) because the oriental base already provides complexity — Damascone Beta and Damascol would add fruity facets that compete with the saffron-balsamic direction.

- **Hedione HC**: Radiance amplifier at 580 µL — lifts the entire rose heart into the saffron-cardamom top, creating luminous projection that prevents the dark base from pulling the fragrance inward. High-cis for maximum jasmine-lift radiance. The highest Hedione dose of any Rose formula because the dense oriental base requires maximum lift to maintain projection.

- **Isobutyl Quinoline (10%)**: The leathery-animalic facet of the oud accord at 140 µL (14 µL active). IBQ provides the "dirty" dimension that real oud possesses — that phenolic, slightly barnyard complexity. At this dose it creates a sophisticated leather undertone rather than outright animalic shock. Synergizes with Nagarmortha's smoky dryness to form the core of the oud impression.

- **Nagarmortha Oil**: The cypriol-oud bridge at 80 µL neat. Provides the dry, earthy, smoky-woody character that connects the IBQ leather to the woody base. Functions as the "budget oud" material — Nagarmortha's cypriol profile (Cyperus scariosus) shares olfactive territory with oud's dry-woody-smoky facets. Chosen over using only IBQ because Nagarmortha adds the woody dimension that IBQ alone cannot provide.

- **Ethyl Safranate**: The saffron effect at 60 µL — provides the leathery-spicy-iodine facet characteristic of high-quality oud oils and the classical rose-oud pairing (saffron is the traditional bridge between rose and oud in Middle Eastern perfumery). Dosed in the top alongside Cardamom EO so the saffron impression hits immediately and persists into the heart.

- **Cardamom EO**: The green-spicy top at 80 µL — cardamom's cineolic-aromatic character provides the fresh counterpoint to the dark base. The cardamom-saffron pairing is classical in both perfumery and cuisine, and it creates the spicy opening that announces "oriental" before the rose even emerges.

- **Labdanum Resinoid (10%)**: The warm amber-resin bridge at 200 µL (20 µL active). Labdanum's leather-amber warmth connects the IBQ leather to the balsamic base. Essential for the leathery-amber facet that makes the oud accord read as "aged" rather than "raw." Without labdanum, the oud accord would sound one-dimensional — labdanum adds the oxidized, time-matured complexity.

- **Siam Benzoin (50% in DPG)**: The balsamic sweetness at 240 µL of 50% (120 µL active). Benzoin's vanilla-cinnamon warmth provides the sweet counterbalance to the dry-smoky oud accord. Chosen over Benzoin Sumatra Resinoid (10%) because the 50% dilution allows sufficient balsamic mass without excessive carrier volume. At 120 µL active, it sits well within oriental perfume conventions.

- **Ambermax (50%)**: The warm, rounded amber body at 180 µL of 50% (90 µL active). Chosen over Ambrofix because this composition needs the warm, full-bodied amber register — Ambrofix's crystalline mineral character would fight the balsamic warmth. Ambermax provides the "cozy amber" that fills the space between the oud accord and the musks.

- **Patchouli EO**: The earthy depth at 140 µL — patchouli's camphoraceous-earth character adds another layer to the complex base, connecting the Nagarmortha cypriol to the woody structure. Dosed lower than in the Patchouli Moderne (140 vs 200) because patchouli should support the oud accord, not compete with it.

- **Evernyl (50% in DPG)**: Synthetic moss body at 100 µL of 50% (50 µL active). At this dilution, stays within IFRA compliance while adding the bitter-green-woody dimension that prevents the oriental base from becoming cloying. Chose Evernyl 50% over neat Evernyl to allow more precise dosing in the sub-IFRA range.

- **Ethylene Brassylate**: Creamy depth musk at 280 µL — its lactonic quality softens the IBQ leather and Nagarmortha smoke, providing the skin-level warmth that prevents the oud from reading as aggressive. Chosen over Exaltolide (too fatty for an oriental) because EB's cleaner creaminess balances the dark elements without adding animalic weight.

- **Habanolide**: Warm skin-intimacy musk at 180 µL — the macrocyclic warmth that makes the fragrance feel lived-in and personal rather than performative. In this composition, Habanolide's quiet skin-warmth is the counterpoint to the extroverted IBQ — it whispers while the oud shouts.

- **Romandolide**: Projection musk at 220 µL — diffusive and clean, extends the rose-oud sillage outward. Chosen over Galaxolide (too synthetic-detergent for a luxury oriental) and Zenolide (too cold — would fight the balsamic warmth).

- **Iso E Super**: Molecular skin-cocoon at 480 µL — the highest dose of any Rose formula because an oriental needs the abstract cedar halo to create the "what are you wearing" effect on skin. Chosen over Timberol (too rigid for the flowing oriental structure) and Azarbre (its warmth would blur into the already-warm amber-benzoin base).

- **Vertofix + Cedarwood EO + Sandalore**: The woody bridge triad. Vertofix (180 µL) bridges wood and musk layers with amber-cedar character. Cedarwood EO (140 µL) provides natural woody identity. Sandalore (140 µL) — the fresh citrus-woody sandalwood — adds lightness to prevent the woody base from becoming heavy. Chosen over Javanol (too dry-intimate for an oriental that needs projection), Ebanol (too creamy — would blur with Ethylene Brassylate), and Sandalwood FO (too opaque to control).

- **Ambrofix (30%) + Cashmeran**: The structural duo at 100 + 100 µL. Ambrofix (30 µL active) adds crystalline transparent depth — the cold mineral point that prevents the warm base from collapsing into amorphous sweetness. Cashmeran (100 neat) provides textile-warmth — the "cashmere wrap" effect that adds an additional textural dimension to the oriental warmth.

- **Benzyl Benzoate + Hexyl Salicylate**: Fixative pair. Benzyl Benzoate (220) as near-odorless balsamic mass — adds molecular weight for fixation without contributing character. Hexyl Salicylate (140) as sheer green-floral fixative — holds the bergamot-saffron-cardamom top. No Benzyl Salicylate here — its heavy cosmetic character would clash with the dark oriental direction.

- **Bergamot FCF + Linalool + Linalyl Acetate**: The clean citrus top at 380 + 220 + 80 µL. Bergamot FCF provides classical freshness — chosen over Bergamot FCF Sicilian (too rich — competes with saffron) or other citrus (would fight the oriental character). Linalool adds fresh-lavender lift. Linalyl Acetate extends bergamot persistence.
