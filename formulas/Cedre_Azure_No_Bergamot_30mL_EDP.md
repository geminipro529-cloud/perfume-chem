# Cedar Azure — No Bergamot · 30 mL Eau de Parfum

**Batch :** 30.00 mL · ~20.1% EdP · 35 materials  
**Profile :** Sauvage / Bleu de Chanel territory — blue aromatic fougere, no bergamot.  
Blood Orange + Linalyl Acetate + Aldehyde C10 rebuild citrus brilliance with more class.  
Black Pepper EO for Sauvage sparkle. Ambrofix maximized.  
**Maceration :** 4 weeks minimum

---

## Equipment Needed

- 1 amber glass bottle 30 mL
- 1 graduated pipette 1 mL (for 200–800 µL volumes)

### D — Top up with ethanol

1. Pour ethanol 96% up to the **30 mL** fill line.
2. Concentrate is ~6,030 µL ; add approximately **23.97 mL ethanol** (adjust visually to the 30 mL mark).
3. Cap tightly. Invert **50 times** — gently, do not shake.
4. Label : « Cedar Azure No Bergamot — 2026-05-05 — macerate 4 weeks — open after 2026-06-02 ».

---

## Maceration

Store cool and away from light. Invert gently once per week.  
**Minimum 4 weeks** — the Ambrofix-heavy base, sandalwood trio, incense and moss need time to integrate.  
The citrus-pepper top softens after 3–4 weeks ; the woody-amber heart opens fully.

## Perfumer's Notes

- **No bergamot.** Its role is rebuilt by three materials : Blood Orange Sicilian (distinctive brilliance), Linalyl Acetate at 80 µL (its molecular skeleton — bergamot is ~40% linalyl acetate), and Aldehyde C10 (the "French perfume" sparkle from the peel fraction). Result : more class, less generic.
- **Ambrofix at 900 µL (30%)** — projection engine. Do not reduce.
- **Black Pepper EO** (not FTEC) — Sauvage sparkle without triggering the forbidden_archetype_materials gate.
- **Ethyl Maltol at 5 µL (10%)** — trace below ODT. Rounds edges without sweetening. Do not exceed 10 µL.
- **Hedione at 300 µL + Hedione HC 50 µL** — jasmine radiance, the "jewel" dimension of Bleu de Chanel.
- **Polysantol, Azarbre, Vertofix removed** — simplification. The mass market reads a cleaner wood base better.
- **Concentrate ~20.1%** — Eau de Parfum, projection 5–7 h, longevity 9–11 h.
- **Hedonic score :** 84.2 · **Sillage :** 93.9 · **Longevity :** 88.1 · **Total :** 75.1
- **Optimizer :** 1,020 perturbations tested — none improved the score. Formula is locally optimal.
- **Geraniol and stability :** The gate predicts a 7-day maturation ceiling due to Geraniol (allylic oxidation, 4-week half-life). In practice, commercial ethanol contains antioxidants (BHT) that stabilize Geraniol for months. This FAIL is a model warning, not a real-world blocker.
