## 1. Locked brief and exact claim

Accessible pink-pepper aromatic gourmand: clear pepper and lavender over a roasted-nut/toffee illusion, vanilla, soft suede and warm transparent woods. An original competitor in the Stronger With You Intensely territory, not a clone.

Protected anchors: pink pepper; lavender / clary; roast-vanilla-tonka impression; soft suede / transparent amberwood. Avoid: popcorn/pandan or savory snack; cotton-candy caramel; powder-first iris; hay-heavy coumarin; huge amberwood or extra musk stack.

## 5. Why the composition changed

1. Replace the old mixed stock-volume target with 100% undiluted-product mass. Piperonal is now 24 mg solid per 6 g target, never a neat uL row.
2. Give pepper and lavender explicit compositional priority; mandarin, juniper and cardamom bridge them instead of adding a second opening theme.
3. Make vanilla/Tonkarome/coumarin/benzoin the body of the roast illusion, with pyrazine and ethyl maltol only as small modifiers.
4. Keep Alpha Irone as a deliberately narrow powder bridge. The old reason that AIMI identity was unresolved is obsolete: the owned PW AIMI is now resolved, but is not chosen for this target.
5. Keep Romandolide as the one selected musk. Do not add Ambrox merely because a warmer gourmand is being redesigned.

No larger total, extra musk, higher vapor-pressure spread or ingredient count is treated as richness. Iso E Super is selected for abstract woody space, not identifiable cedar; identifiable cedar/Azarbre is used only where the brief needs that register. Adding Timberol/superambers would require a separate target-linked hypothesis.

## 10. Proposed mixing and bottle action

1. Weigh the wood/musk/solvent scaffold, then Hedione and the known balsamic/coumarin stocks.
2. Add Alpha Irone and vanilla stock. Dissolve the separate 24 mg Piperonal charge using part of the final alcohol allowance and keep that alcohol in the mass ledger.
3. Add Isobutavan and the three trace working stocks; then lavender, clary sage, geranium and linalyl acetate.
4. Add mandarin, pepper, juniper and cardamom last. Close, mix and inspect after equilibration.

AI proposes; the human chooses and confirms the actual measured amounts. Record additions if made. No proposal in these documents has been physically committed and no inventory/bottle ledger is altered. An existing-bottle additive repair would need a separate nonnegative-delta plan.

Stop for an unbound required stock, unreliable dose, undissolved crystal, separation or unexpected off-note. Avoid direct heat/open flames around ethanol. No skin application or commercial release is authorized by this design.

## 11. Small sensory / analytical experiment

Start with LEAD and one discriminating alternative, not three untested 30 mL bottles. Use the same blotter and application amount, code the samples, and assess at **5 minutes, 30–60 minutes, 4 hours and next-day blotter**. Record recognizers, forbidden drift, texture, transition and liking separately; allow ties and “cannot determine.” Repeat a relevant pair across three sessions before making a strong personal-preference claim.

Compare LEAD with ROAST_OMISSION first. If the roast becomes corn chips/pandan, omit/reduce pyrazine before buying more nut materials. If syrupy/powdery, compare DRIER_CONSERVATIVE. Preserve pepper/lavender clarity before increasing woody power.
