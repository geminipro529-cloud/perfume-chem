# Niche House Iris Sombre — Dior Homme Intense Study — 30 mL EdP

**Historical reference:** Dior Homme Intense (2011, François Demachy)
**Concentration:** 20.0% EdP · 6,000 µL concentrate in 30 mL
**Philosophy:** 2011 DHI reimagined through a niche-house lens. The iris-cocoa-leather signature is preserved but rebalanced: real top notes restored (bergamot + lavender, not just a whisper), Alpha Irone leads the iris corps over synthetic ionones, coumarin-vanillin forms the cocoa-tonka body, Cashmeran + Kephalis + Ambrofix build the warm amber spine, and a restrained macrocyclic musk chord replaces the bloated Ethylene Brassylate overfunding. Materials that couldn't achieve perceptible OAV at their doses were either boosted to functional levels or removed.
