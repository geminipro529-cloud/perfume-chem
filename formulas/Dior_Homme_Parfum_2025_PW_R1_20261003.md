# Dior Homme Parfum 2025 — PW Iris–Amber R1

Date: 3 October 2026. Mode: NEW-FORMULA / PW-PRODUCT DEVELOPMENT INTERPRETATION.

Root/petal iris with a dry amber, patchouli/vetiver and hay framework. [Dior's current Parfum page](https://www.dior.com/en_sg/beauty/products/dior-homme-parfum-C099700665.html) and [LVMH's launch notice](https://www.lvmh.com/en/news-lvmh/francis-kurkdjian-revisits-dior-homme) establish the 2025 target as distinct from the old leather version.

Claim: an architecture-matched starting recipe. **Not an authentic Dior formula, an observed similarity result, a safety clearance or a record of physical compounding.** The development lead is not a measured sensory winner.

## Locked brief

restrained aromatic lift → airy yet dense iris heart → dry hay/patchouli/vetiver amber.

- Recognizers: root/petal iris contrast; amber/coumarinic hay; patchouli/vetiver contour.
- Protected design anchors: AIMI; Orris Liquid; Alpha Irone; Coumarin; Patchouli Light; Vetiver EO.
- Avoid: no forced classic leather chassis; no literal coffee/cocoa note imposed by marketing analogy; no giant sandalwood or superamber structure.

## Mixing order

1. Assemble only the matching working stocks. Work by mass in a capped vessel; record any actual deviation.
2. Combine larger Iso E Super, Hedione, musk and wood amounts with resin stocks. Dissolve the separately weighed Ambrox, Coumarin and Vanillin; include weighed Exaltolide in the historical Parfum.
3. Add the iris/ionone module, then prepared small accents. Add citrus/lavender after a uniform stable base is obtained.
4. Withdraw a trial only from a homogeneous master. Stop for persistent solids/sediment, an incorrect stock or a weighing error. Any warming follows supplier handling guidance; do not heat flammable alcohol or work near ignition sources, and cool before dilution.
5. Add the weighed trial alcohol, cap and mix. Check fresh for gross failures, after 48 hours, and optionally at 1–2 weeks. These are useful comparison intervals, not validated shelf-life or optimal maturation claims.

No reservation, inventory deduction or bottle event is committed by these instructions. The design remains PROPOSE; human confirmation, measurement and any atomic bottle commit were not performed.

## First comparison and stop conditions

Start with this lead and **one** appropriate control/alternative, not every candidate. Compare on the same blotter and consistent deposition at 5 minutes, 30–60 minutes, 4 hours and next day. The relevant commercial **edition** is the reference, not a different flanker. A coded comparison is helpful, not a demand for photographs or lots.

Reject for lost iris identity, excessive vanilla/powder, leafy/carrot/cumin drift, overwhelming musk/wood, inappropriate blue freshness, or leather drowning iris. If a simpler control is as good and as close, remove its omitted accents in a new version. Lower-root, lower-Ambrox and matched-total musk alternatives are specified in the shared plan. ## Safety and verification boundary

At nominal 20% w/w, declared-product contribution to the final batch is target % × 0.20. Additional coumarin/rose-ketone/allergen contributions from naturals and mixture ingredients remain unresolved. Category 4 review requires actual product documentation and aggregate constituent assessment. Prioritize Coumarin, alpha damascone, citrus/lavender, naturals/resinoids and the classic leather products. Suederal is an undisclosed mixture; a generic blank supplier declaration is not complete safety evidence.
