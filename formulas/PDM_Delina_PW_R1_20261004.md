## Locked brief and sensory architecture

Tart lychee/rhubarb sparkle around a luminous Damask-rose heart, with a cashmere-like wood/musk finish.

A multiple-facet lychee hypothesis links rose oxide, linalool, geraniol, rose ketones and tiny fruit esters. Rhubofix supplies a separate tart-green axis; actual fruit recognition requires smelling.

Protected anchors: Rose/PEA/Citro/Ger: clear rose protagonism; Rhub/MP: tartness rather than syrup; RO/rose ketones: restrained lychee-rose connection. Forbidden drift: metallic rose oxide; generic fruity shower gel; jammy rose; wood masking the heart.

Temporal design hypotheses, not measured performance:

- 5 min: tart rose-lychee/citrus
- 30-60 min: fresh Damask rose, fruit still identifiable
- 4 h: soft rose, cashmere-like wood and skin musk
- next-day blotter: rose/woody musk, not a vanilla gourmand

## Stock preparation and mixing order

The shared [campaign research and preparation guide](/D:/chatbots/perfume-chem/docs/research/PDM_Featured_Bestsellers_PW_Research_20261004.md) lists all 28 diluted products, exact mass-fraction preparation templates and current-stock differences.

1. Prepare only the working dilutions needed for this formula. Label material, mass fraction and carrier; a nominal v/v stock is not a w/w stock.
2. Add the 5.000 g declared alcohol carrier to a closed mixing vessel. Add the vanilla/coumarin/resin working stocks, weighed Ambrox and other solids, plus the compatible woody and musk products. Stir at room temperature.
3. Add the floral/fruit body, then natural oils and the powerful trace stocks. Reweigh each transfer and keep an actual-mass note.
4. Mix until homogeneous. Do not heat ethanol on a hotplate or use an open flame. If dissolution fails, pause and recalculate any added solvent explicitly.
5. Take the stated master aliquot and add the stated remaining alcohol. Close, mix, and inspect again after standing. A 7–14 day settling period is a practical first comparison schedule, not proof of stability.
6. Compare coded blotters with an authentic sample of the exact reference edition at a matched application amount. No skin-use or release permission follows from this document.

## Hard gates and sensory decisions

Arithmetic and measurement-floor checks are verified separately in the campaign receipt. Sensory identity, dominance, redundancy, solubility, reference similarity and liking remain **NOT TESTED**. Reject a trial if metallic rose oxide, generic fruity shower gel, jammy rose, wood masking the heart displaces the protected anchors. Avoid deleting a row based on OAV or ingredient count.

At 5 minutes, 30–60 minutes, 4 hours and next-day blotter, note: recognizable core, strongest mismatch, texture and whether the reference or trial is preferred; allow tie/cannot determine. Repeat promising pairs on another day; three sessions are preferable before a personal preference claim. There is no blind consumer or population claim.

Use the control to test trace complexity, and the robustness alternative to test woody/musk masking. Multiple musks have distinct intended roles, but their sensory nonredundancy is a hypothesis; test the altered musk balance rather than treating a multi-musk list as inherent quality.
