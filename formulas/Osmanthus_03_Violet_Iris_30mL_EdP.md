# Osmanthus Violet-Iris — 30mL EdP

## Character
A powdery iris-violet composition with osmanthus absolute providing warm apricot-leather depth to what would otherwise be a cold, metallic ionone structure. The osmanthus bridges the green violet-leaf top and the buttery orris drydown, creating a soft-floral-powder effect that reads as vintage cosmetic elegance — think Chanel No. 19's green-iris backbone softened by an apricot-suede heart, or Guerlain Après L'Ondée with more leather and less anise.

**Family**: Iris / Powdery Floral — violet-ionone body, orris butter base, osmanthus warmth

## Material Justification

- **Osmanthus Absolute (550 µL of 10% = 55 µL active)**: The warm-apricot-leather anchor that prevents the ionone structure from reading as cold-metallic. Osmanthus's natural fruit-leather facets add dimensionality that pure ionones lack. At ~1% active, it's clearly present without dominating the iris-violet identity.

- **Musk Ketone (10% in DPG)**: The ONLY musk that reinforces powder directly — essential for the iris-violet character. Chosen as the character-echo musk over all other options because this composition needs talcum-powder reinforcement. The nitro-musk warmth also counterbalances the cool ionone body.

- **Alpha Ionone + Beta Ionone + Alpha Isomethyl Ionone**: The iris-violet trinity at 150 + 120(1%) + 100 µL. Alpha for the rich orris-woody facet, Beta for green-violet leaf, Methyl Ionone Pure for powdery diffusion. Beta at 1% dilution allows precise dosing — at 1.2 µL active it provides the violet-leaf top note without overpowering.

- **Orivone + Ultralia**: Butter-iris depth (Orivone) paired with ghost-iris transparency (Ultralia). Orivone at 70 µL provides the substantive orris body; Ultralia at trace (25 µL) adds the ethereal, transparent iris that floats above the powder.

- **Heliotropal**: Sweet-almond-powdery bridge between ionones and the coumarinic base — chosen over Anisaldehyde because Heliotropal's almond facet pairs better with osmanthus's apricot than anisaldehyde's licorice.

- **DBCA (80 µL)**: Gardenia-rose cosmetic character — adds the "vintage cosmetic" plastic-floral quality that defines the Chanel No. 19 / Après L'Ondée territory. Bridges the ionone powder and the salicylate cushion.

- **Benzyl Salicylate (400 µL)**: Chosen as the heavy fixative over Hexyl-only because iris-powder compositions NEED cosmetic weight. The salicylate cushion amplifies the powder diffusion and adds the waxy-floral body that makes iris perfumes feel "expensive." Hexyl Salicylate at 160 µL provides the lighter, greener top layer of the fixative gradient.

- **Ebanol (70 µL)**: Creamy-soft sandalwood — chosen over Javanol (too cold/dry for this powder context) and over Bacdanol (too heavy). Ebanol's milky warmth supports the orris-butter character from Orivone.

- **Cashmeran (90 µL neat)**: Textile-warmth and cashmere muskiness — chosen at moderate dose to add the "wrapped in fabric" sensation without shifting the family toward oriental. Below the 50 µL family-shifting ceiling at this concentrate volume.

- **Ambrofix (30% w/v, 80 µL = 24 µL active)**: Crystalline-mineral amber at low dose — provides transparent structure beneath the powder without adding warmth. Ambrofix chosen over Amberwood F because the mineral facet pairs with the metallic-cool ionone character.
