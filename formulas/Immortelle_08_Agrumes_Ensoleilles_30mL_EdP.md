# Immortelle Agrumes Ensoleillés — 30mL EdP

## Character
A sun-drenched Mediterranean citrus composition where Immortelle Absolute's honeyed-maple warmth acts as a golden modifier behind a radiant multiple-citrus top. Unlike sharp, fleeting cologne-style citruses, this is a warm, sustained citrus — Bergamot FCF Sicilian, Cedrat, Blood Orange, and Grapefruit create a multi-dimensional hesperidic opening, while immortelle, Hedione, and a light amber-musk base provide the golden warmth that extends the citrus radiance into the heart and early drydown. The immortelle reads as "sunlight through citrus groves" — not a distinct note, but a warm haze that makes the citrus feel three-dimensional and lasting. Think *Acqua di Parma Colonia* meeting a Corsican hillside at golden hour — citrus-forward but substantial, fresh but not fleeting, with immortelle as the olfactory equivalent of the warm Provençal sunlight that ripens the fruit.

**Family**: Citrus Aromatic — multiple citrus top supported by immortelle-golden heart, transparent woody-amber-musk base

## Material Justification

- **Immortelle Absolute (10%)**: The golden glow behind the citrus — 150 µL of 10% = 15 µL active (0.27% of concentrate). At the lower end of modifier range because the citrus should lead — immortelle functions as the "backlight," not the subject. Its honeyed-maple warmth creates a perceptible golden radiance behind the citrus top notes, extending their perceived life and adding depth that pure citrus accords lack. Without immortelle, this is a cologne that dies in 15 minutes; with it, the citrus feels sustained and dimensional.
- **Bergamot FCF Sicilian (350 µL) + Cedrat FCF Sicilian (150 µL) + Blood Orange Sicilian (150 µL) + Grapefruit FCF (120 µL)**: The four-citrus symphony — the largest citrus load in any Immortelle formula (770 µL total). This is explicitly a citrus composition, so the multiple citrus materials are structurally necessary, not decorative.
  - **Bergamot FCF Sicilian**: The lead citrus — richer and more complex than standard Bergamot FCF. Chosen as the star because its rounded, fruity-floral bergamot character is the most compatible with immortelle's golden warmth.
  - **Cedrat FCF Sicilian**: Bitter citron edge — sharper and more mineral, providing the "top" of the citrus register. Cuts through the richness of the Sicilian bergamot.
  - **Blood Orange Sicilian**: Juicy, sweet-tart orange — the "body" of the citrus accord. Its less-terpenic character provides citrus substance without the harshness of D-Limonene.
  - **Grapefruit FCF**: Bitter-clean pink-pith dryness — the "finish." Adds a subtle bitter-clean tail that prevents the citrus from becoming sweet and confectionary.
  - Rejected: Red Mandarin EO (too warm-gourmand for this fresh-citrus concept), standard Bergamot FCF (less complex than Sicilian, chosen Sicilian to maximize richness).
- **Linalool (120 µL) + Linalyl Acetate (100 µL)**: Citrus structure materials. Linalool provides the fresh-lavender-citrus alcohol character that is the molecular core of bergamot. Linalyl Acetate adds the fruity-floral ester that gives bergamot its signature — the "bergaptene-free" character of FCF bergamots is largely linalyl acetate. Together they reinforce the natural citrus materials' fresh-fruity register.
- **Terpinyl Acetate (60 µL)**: Pine-citrus-herbal lift — adds a subtle coniferous-citrus dimension that differentiates this from a simple cologne. The pine facet creates a "Corsican hillside" subtext — citrus trees growing among pines. Chosen specifically to add geographic specificity to the otherwise generic "Mediterranean citrus" character.
- **Dihydromyrcenol (100 µL)**: Synthetic fresh-citrus-lavender lift — enhances the bergamot's brightness and extends its perceived duration. Standard in modern citrus compositions to create the "shampoo-fresh" opening that consumers expect. Used at functional dose — not a character note, but a structural enhancer.
- **Parmavert (30 µL)**: Green violet-leaf freshness — adds a subtle green-sharp dimension that reads as "citrus leaves," not just fruit. Prevents the citrus from being purely juicy — adds the botanical context of the grove. Kept at moderate dose (30 µL neat is well below the "violet leaf bomb" threshold).
- **Clary Sage EO (40 µL) + Geranium EO (40 µL)**: Aromatic-herbal lift. Clary sage provides the dry, tea-like herbal character that gives the citrus-aromatic structure complexity. Geranium EO adds fresh rosy-minty lift — a classic companion to citrus in Mediterranean compositions.
- **Labdanum Resinoid (10%)**: 100 µL of 10% = 10 µL active. The resinous heart — half the dose used in other Immortelle formulas because the citrus should dominate. Labdanum's amber-leather warmth provides the golden-resinous bridge between the citrus top and the woody base. At 10 µL active, it whispers rather than speaks — just enough to add depth to the immortelle's golden glow.
- **Coumarin (20%)**: 60 µL of 20% = 12 µL active. Hay-sweet tonka at restrained dose — pairs with immortelle (proven synergy) but kept low to avoid sweetening the citrus character. Functions as a "sun-warmed hay" background note rather than a distinct coumarin presence.
- **Hedione HC (600 µL) + Hedione (400 µL)**: Radiance amplifier pair at maximum dose (1000 µL total). The highest Hedione load in any Immortelle formula because citrus compositions NEED maximum radiance — Hedione IS the structural material that makes citrus "fly." HC variant provides rapid lift; standard Hedione sustains projection. This is the single most important structural choice in the formula.
- **Iso E Super (600 µL)**: Molecular cocoon — volume and skin-adhesion. Standard dose. Essential in citrus compositions to create the "space" that the volatile citrus occupies — without Iso E, citrus feels thin and evanescent.
- **Cedarwood EO (150 µL) + Vertofix (150 µL) + Koavone (50 µL)**: Warm-transparent woody base. Cedarwood provides natural cedar identity without the darkness of vetiver or patchouli. Vertofix bridges wood to musk. Koavone supports the cedar with balsamic warmth. Together they create a clean, transparent wood register that supports the citrus without adding earth or darkness.
- **Cedramber (80 µL) + Amberwood F (80 µL)**: Cedar-amber + transparent-amber layer. Cedramber bridges the cedar and amber registers — the warm-wood bridge. Amberwood F adds invisible clean-amber warmth — the background glow that extends the citrus' perceived warmth into the drydown. Chosen specifically because they add warmth WITHOUT character — the citrus should remain the star.
- **Ambrofix (30% w/v)**: 80 µL of 30% = 24 µL active. Crystalline-mineral sparkle — adds the "sunlight-through-glass" effect that makes the citrus feel radiant. The mineral character creates a sense of clarity and freshness — the opposite of warm-amber heaviness. Chosen specifically for its cold, crystalline register.
- **Zenolide (150 µL)**: Character-echo musk — the CLEANEST, most citrusy-fresh musk in inventory. This is the strategic musk choice: Zenolide's fresh-citrusy character directly echoes the citrus top at the skin level, creating a sustained "ghost citrus" that persists after the actual citrus top notes have evaporated. Without Zenolide, the drydown would read as generic woods; with it, the citrus memory lingers.
- **Romandolide (160 µL)**: Projection musk — diffusive woody-musk that extends the citrus-Zenolide radiance outward. Chosen over Habanolide (too warm-intimate for a fresh citrus), over Galaxolide (too laundry-detergent, would cheapen the composition), and over Exaltolide (too fatty — would clash with citrus freshness).
- **Ethylene Brassylate (150 µL)**: Depth musk at RESTRAINED dose — lower than typical (150 vs 200-250). Kept low specifically because EB's creamy-lactonic character could clash with the fresh-citrus profile at higher doses. At 150 µL, it provides essential skin-anchoring for the volatile citrus without introducing noticeable creaminess.
- **Hexyl Salicylate (180 µL) + Benzyl Benzoate (120 µL)**: The PERFECT fixative pairing for a citrus composition. Hexyl Salicylate's green-floral transparent film creates diffusion and adhesion for the citrus top — the primary fixative, chosen because its character aligns with the citrus aromatic profile. Benzyl Benzoate provides invisible molecular-weight fixation — the secondary anchor that adds longevity without any character contribution. Benzyl Salicylate rejected (too cosmetic-heavy — would smother the fresh citrus).
- **Ebanol (40 µL)**: Creamy sandalwood accent at trace — adds a soft, creamy texture that prevents the citrus-wood transition from being harsh. The "silk lining" of the citrus composition — you don't smell it, but you notice its absence.
- **Lemonile (30 µL)**: Citrus-extender — a nitrile-based citrus material with surprising tenacity (VP ~0.2 Pa). Adds a clean, long-lasting citrus facet that bridges the volatile natural citruses to the amber-musk base. At 30 µL neat, it's a structural enhancer — extends the citrus illusion without introducing an artificial character.
