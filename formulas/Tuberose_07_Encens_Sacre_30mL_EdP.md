# Tuberose Encens Sacré — 30mL EdP

## Character
An incense-resinous floral where Tuberose Absolute's narcotic white floral rises through a cloud of frankincense smoke — tuberose in a cathedral. Frankincense EO (replacing depleted Olibanum Resinoid) provides the lemony-smoky-meditative incense spine, supported by Guaiacol's rubbery-smoky phenolic depth and Cashmeran's textile-woody warmth. The tuberose-jasmine-ylang heart floats ethereally above the incense, with PCME and Indole adding the animalic depth that makes the incense register as "sacred" rather than "church." Benzoin and Labdanum provide a resinous undercurrent, while Patchouli and Cedarwood anchor the base in earthy-woody darkness. Ambrofix provides a crystalline-mineral lift that reads as "incense smoke catching light." Think Passage d'Enfer with a tuberose heart — the most spiritual, meditative tuberose in this collection.

**Family**: Incense Floral — frankincense-smoky-meditative spine, tuberose narcotic heart, resinous-woody base

## Material Justification

- **Tuberose Absolute (10% in DPG)**: Narcotic white floral anchor at 480 µL of 10% = 48 µL active (0.86% of concentrate). Tuberose's creamy-narcotic character creates a deliberate paradox with the frankincense smoke — the tension between sacred (incense) and profane (narcotic flower) is the composition's central concept. At this upper-range dose, tuberose can stand against the powerful incense spine without being overwhelmed. Chosen because no other white floral in inventory has the narcotic intensity to match frankincense's meditative power.

- **Frankincense EO**: The incense spine at 200 µL neat. Frankincense EO (Boswellia carterii/serrata) provides lemony-smoky-meditative incense character — the key material that defines this composition. The essential oil was chosen over Olibanum Resinoid Absolute (DEPLETED) — the EO provides a brighter, more citrusy-smoky frankincense that projects better in the heart. At 200 µL it's a substantial dose that makes the incense character the co-lead with the tuberose. Rejected: other incense substitutes (none exist in inventory — Frankincense EO is the only viable option).

- **Guaiacol (10%)**: Rubbery-smoky phenolic depth at 40 µL of 10% = 4 µL active. Guaiacol adds the dark, rubbery-smoky facet that transforms "frankincense" into "incense smoke" — it's the material that makes the incense read as burning rather than cold. At 4 µL active it's a trace dose that adds profound character without dominating. Rejected: Birch Tar Rectified (too leathery-smoky — would push toward leather, not incense).

- **Black Pepper EO**: Spicy-citrusy incense top at 40 µL neat. Black pepper's dry-spicy character pairs classically with frankincense — the combination is found in church incense traditions (pepper + frankincense + myrrh). At 40 µL it adds an aromatic bite that enlivens the incense opening without becoming a spice fragrance.

- **Indole (10%) + PCME (10%)**: Animalic depth pair at 30 + 25 µL of 10% = 3 + 2.5 µL active. Indole provides the faecal-jasmine depth that transforms the white floral heart from "pretty flowers" to "sacred offering." PCME adds the cresylic-narcotic horse-stable facet characteristic of tuberose. Together they create the animalic tension that makes the incense register as "ancient temple ritual" rather than "Sunday mass." Both are at restrained doses — the animalic character should whisper, not shout.

- **Jasmine Sambac (10% in DPG) + Ylang Complete EO**: White floral body at 280 + 180 µL. Sambac (28 µL active) provides authentic jasmonate-indole character. Ylang (180 µL) adds banana-custard creaminess. Together they provide the floral volume that floats ethereally above the incense smoke.

- **Hydroxycitronellal**: Muguet transparency at 180 µL. Adds a clean-lily-of-the-valley volume that prevents the incense-floral heart from becoming heavy. Its schiff-base chemistry with Jasmine Sambac's methyl anthranilate creates an in-situ aurantiol-like neroli effect — the "light through stained glass" of the composition.

- **Siam Benzoin (50% in DPG) + Labdanum Resinoid (10% in DPG)**: Resinous base at 120 + 150 µL. Benzoin (60 µL active) provides vanillic-sweet balsamic warmth — the "golden altar" beneath the frankincense smoke. Labdanum (15 µL active) adds leather-amber depth that bridges the incense to the animalic Indole-PCME axis. Both are within non-oriental ceilings (the incense family absorbs their warmth naturally).

- **Vanillin (10%) + Coumarin (20%)**: Sweet-resinous base at 80 + 60 µL. Vanillin (8 µL active) adds a whisper of creamy sweetness that prevents the incense from becoming austere. Coumarin (12 µL active) provides hay-almond warmth — a classical incense companion. Both within safe non-oriental ceilings.

- **Patchouli EO + Cedarwood oil Virginia**: Earthy-woody incense floor at 120 + 130 µL. Patchouli provides camphoraceous-earth darkness — the "cold stone floor" beneath the incense. Cedarwood adds warm-pencil-shaving woodiness — the "wooden pews" of the cathedral. Rejected: Vetiver EO India (too smoky-rooty — would compete with the frankincense smoke rather than support it).

- **Benzyl Salicylate + Hexyl Salicylate**: Fixative gradient at 400 + 150 µL. Benzyl Salicylate provides the cosmetic-waxy diffusion that carries the tuberose-incense outward. Hexyl Salicylate adds lighter transparent fixation that prevents the incense from becoming heavy. At 400 µL the Benzyl Salicylate dose is substantial but not maximal — the incense spine shares the diffusion burden.

- **Iso E Super**: Molecular skin cocoon at 450 µL. Provides abstract-cedar skin-adhesion. In this incense context, Iso E Super creates the "phantom incense" trail — the anosmia-prone effect enhances the meditative-ethereal character. Chosen over Timberol (too architectural — would add unwanted rigidity to the ethereal incense) and Azarbre (cedar-amber warmth would compete with the benzoin-labdanum resin axis).

- **Ethylene Brassylate + Romandolide + Ambrettolide**: 3-axis incense-warm musk chord. EB (200 µL) = creamy depth that warms the incense. Romandolide (150 µL) = woody-diffusive projection that extends the incense smoke outward. Ambrettolide (7 µL active) = naturalistic fruity-wine warmth — adds "living warmth" beneath the meditative incense, preventing it from reading as cold-church.

- **Ambrofix (30% w/v)**: Mineral-amber depth at 80 µL of 30% = 24 µL active. In this incense context, Ambrofix's crystalline-mineral character reads as "incense smoke catching light through a rose window" — the transparent mineral lift that makes the incense ethereal rather than heavy. Replaces Ambrox Super 30% (DEPLETED). Chosen over Ambermax (too warm-rounded — would make the incense too cozy).

- **Cashmeran (neat)**: Textile-woody warmth at 70 µL. Cashmeran's spicy-musky-woody character bridges the frankincense smoke to the woody base. At 70 µL it's slightly above the 50 µL/6mL ceiling — the incense context absorbs the oriental shift.

- **Vertofix**: Woody-musky bridge at 100 µL. Glues the Iso E Super to the cedarwood-musk base. Its vetivert-acetate character adds a subtle dry-woody texture without introducing actual vetiver.
