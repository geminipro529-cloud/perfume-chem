# Tuberose Floral Oriental Poison — 30mL EdP

## Character
A narcotic tuberose-anchored floriental in the Dior Poison lineage — dense, indolic, and unapologetically opulent. Tuberose Absolute forms the narcotic white floral core, amplified by an indolic-animalic white floral heart (Indole, PCME, Methyl Anthranilate) that creates the "toxic flower" character. The oriental base layers benzoin-labdanum-vanillin-coumarin over patchouli-vetiver-cedarwood earth for a dark, resinous drydown. Damascone Beta and Damascenone inject a fruity-rose depth reminiscent of the original Poison's damascone overdose. A heavy Benzyl Salicylate dose (500 µL) provides the cosmetic-plastic diffusion that made Poison a landmark. The musk chord shifts toward warm-intimate-powdery — Habanolide for skin warmth, Musk Ketone for the talcum-powder echo, Ethylene Brassylate for creamy depth.

**Family**: Floriental — white floral + oriental base, heavy cosmetic diffusion, indolic-animalic depth

## Material Justification

- **Tuberose Absolute (10% in DPG)**: The narcotic white floral anchor at 500 µL of 10% = 50 µL active (0.86% of concentrate). At this upper-range dose, tuberose's natural indolic-camphoraceous-methyl benzoate profile dominates the heart. The methyl benzoate and benzyl salicylate facets integrate with the heavy cosmetic fixative layer, creating the "salicylate-tuberose" accord that defines the Poison family. Chosen because no other material delivers the creamy-narcotic intensity needed for a floriental anchor — Tuberalia base was rejected as too clean/synthetic for the Poison direction.
- **Indole (10%) + PCME (10%) + Methyl Anthranilate**: The indolic-animalic triad that creates the "toxic flower" character. Indole (4 µL active) provides the faecal-jasmine depth — at this dose it reads as narcotic white floral, not fecal. PCME (3 µL active) adds the cresylic-narcotic facet characteristic of tuberose absolute and narcissus — the "horse-stable" white floral note, reduced from 5 µL to keep OAV below the 10,000 ceiling. Methyl Anthranilate (30 µL) contributes the orange-blossom-animalic grape character that bridges the indole to the schiff-base chemistry of the aldehydic top. Together, these three materials reproduce the animalic depth of natural tuberose absolute at narcotic intensity.
- **Ylang Comoros Complete EO F3255**: The tropical floral body at 250 µL. At this dose, ylang's banana-custard-benzyl ester profile adds creamy volume to the white floral core. The Complete grade's sesquiterpene fraction adds base tenacity. Chosen over Ylang Extra (brighter top notes, less base) because the base-heavy floriental structure benefits from the Complete grade's depth.
- **Jasmine Sambac (10% in DPG)**: Indolic white floral depth at 350 µL of 10% = 35 µL active. Sambac provides the authentic jasmonate-indole character that reinforces the Indole-PCME-MA axis without needing additional synthetic indole. At 35 µL active it's a substantial dose that makes the jasmine facet clearly perceptible alongside the tuberose.
- **Hydroxycitronellal**: Muguet body at 250 µL. Adds a clean-lily-of-the-valley volume that prevents the indolic-animalic axis from becoming oppressive. The hydroxycitronellal-lily facet also provides a schiff-base partner for Methyl Anthranilate, creating an in-situ aurantiol-like effect. Chosen over Bourgeonal (too sharp-aqueous) and Lilyreal (too cosmetic-transparent — would compete with the Benzyl Salicylate diffusion character).
- **Damascone Beta (10%) + Damascenone (1%)**: The fruity-rose depth pair that defines the Poison DNA. Damascone Beta (6 µL active) provides plum-rose body — the beta isomer was chosen because its fruit-rose character bridges the white floral heart to the oriental base better than the alpha isomer (more metallic-rose). Damascenone (0.4 µL active) provides the intense apple-rose-dried-fruit facet at a trace level characteristic of classic Poison formulations. At 0.4 µL active it reads as a magical fruity depth rather than a distinct rose note.
- **Cinnamaldehyde + Eugenol**: Spice warmth at 10 + 20 µL. Cinnamaldehyde adds a hot-cinnamon heat that burns through the dense floral heart, creating the "spicy Poison" character. Eugenol adds clove warmth that bridges the spice to the patchouli-vetiver base. Both are at ceiling doses for non-oriental contexts per the family-shifting rules — but since this IS a floriental, the ceilings are relaxed. The total spice dose (30 µL neat) is deliberate — enough to read as "spicy oriental" without becoming a spice soliflore.
- **Benzyl Salicylate**: Heavy cosmetic diffusion at 500 µL. This is the defining structural material of the Poison family — benzyl salicylate's waxy-cosmetic-plastic diffusion creates the "cloud of scent" sillage architecture. At 500 µL (8.6% of concentrate) it's a maximum dose that provides both the cosmetic character (desired in this composition) and maximum diffusion volume. Chosen because the composition's entire aesthetic depends on cosmetic-white-floral diffusion — Hexyl Salicylate (too light-transparent) would not provide the necessary "weight."
- **Benzyl Benzoate**: Inert fixative anchor at 150 µL. Adds molecular mass and fixation without contributing any character — the composition already has more than enough character from the tuberose-indolic-oriental axis. The balsamic density of Benzyl Benzoate helps hold the heavy floral heart on skin.
- **Siam Benzoin (50% in DPG)**: Oriental-balsamic warmth at 150 µL of 50% = 75 µL active. Siam Benzoin's vanillic-sweet character provides the balsamic backbone of the oriental base. At 75 µL active per 5.8 mL concentrate, it's above the 30 µL/6mL ceiling for non-oriental contexts — but this IS a floriental, so family-shifting rules are intentionally violated by design. Chosen over Benzoin Sumatra (drier, more cinnamon-smoke) because Siam's sweeter profile supports the vanillin-coumarin axis.
- **Labdanum Resinoid (10% in DPG)**: Amber-resin depth at 150 µL of 10% = 15 µL active. Provides the leather-amber undercurrent that gives the oriental base its warmth. At 15 µL active it's below the threshold where labdanum reads as "church incense" — it supports the benzoin rather than competing with it.
- **Vanillin (10%) + Coumarin (20%)**: Sweet-oriental base pair at 120 + 80 µL. Vanillin (12 µL active) provides the sweet-creamy undercurrent. Coumarin (16 µL active) adds hay-almond warmth and a tobacco facet. Both are intentionally pushed above non-oriental ceilings — the floriental character depends on their sweet-resinous contribution.
- **Patchouli EO + Vetiver EO India + Cedarwood oil Virginia**: The dark earthy-woody base triad at 150 + 100 + 120 µL. Patchouli provides the camphoraceous-earth backbone — essential for anchoring the heavy floral heart without it floating away. Vetiver EO India adds a smoky-rooty depth distinct from standard Vetiver EO — the Indian ruh khus character is darker and more complex. Cedarwood oil Virginia adds warm-pencil-shaving woodiness. Together they create an earthy forest-floor base that contrasts with and supports the sweet oriental resins above.
- **Evernyl**: Oakmoss replacement at 20 µL. Provides a bitter-lichen counterpoint to the sweet oriental resins, preventing the base from becoming cloying. Dosed at 20 µL to stay within IFRA Cat4 limits (0.1% maximum) — the synthetic oakmoss character is potent enough at this level to add a chypre-facet that makes the floriental read as "vintage luxury" rather than "modern gourmand."
- **Habanolide**: Warm-skin intimacy musk at 120 µL. Chosen over Romandolide because this composition needs skin-warmth, not woody projection — the Benzyl Salicylate already handles diffusion. Habanolide's warm macrocyclic trail creates the "come closer" skin-scent effect that contrasts with the aggressive opening sillage. Chosen over Exaltolide (too fatty-lactonic — would compete with EB and the vanillin-coumarin creaminess).
- **Musk Ketone (10% in DPG)**: Powdery character-echo musk at 100 µL of 10% = 10 µL active. The ONLY musk that directly reinforces the powder-talcum register. In a Poison-style floriental, the nitro-musk powder facet is essential — it creates the "vintage cosmetic" powder trail that defined 1980s orientals. Pre-diluted to 10% for accurate dosing of the crystalline solid.
- **Ethylene Brassylate**: Creamy depth musk at 200 µL. The lactonic quality supports the vanillin-coumarin creaminess and provides skin-sitting tenacity. At 200 µL it anchors the musk chord's depth axis without overwhelming the Habanolide intimacy or Musk Ketone powder.
- **Iso E Super**: Molecular skin cocoon at 400 µL. Provides the abstract-cedar skin-adhesion effect. At this dose it creates the "what are you wearing?" halo without adding identifiable wood character — the cedar character comes from Cedarwood oil Virginia instead. Chosen over Timberol (too architectural, would clash with the dark-earthy base) and Azarbre (would add amber warmth that competes with the benzoin-labdanum axis).
- **Aldehyde C12 MNA (1%)**: Aldehydic sparkle at 40 µL of 1% = 0.4 µL active. C12 MNA provides the classic "aldehydic floral" opening — metallic-waxy-citrus lift that cuts through the dense floral heart. At this trace dose it reads as "sparkle" rather than "aldehyde note." Chosen over Aldehyde C10 (too citrus-orange — C12 MNA's metallic-waxy character pairs better with tuberose's natural waxy facet).
- **Bergamot FCF + Linalool**: The classic hesperidic opening at 400 + 200 µL. Bergamot provides the bergaptene-free citrus-linalyl freshness. Linalool adds the floral-citrus bridge that connects the bergamot top to the white floral heart. Together they create a classical opening that contrasts with the dark oriental drydown — the Poison effect of "innocent top, dangerous base."
- **Parmavert**: Green violet-leaf freshness at 25 µL. At this modest dose, Parmavert provides a green-bitter counterpoint to the sweet-aldehydic top, preventing the opening from reading as simple citrus cologne. The violet-leaf character also creates a subtle ionone-like bridge to the damascone rose facet.
- **Vertofix**: Woody-musky bridge at 100 µL. Glues the Iso E Super molecular layer to the patchouli-vetiver-cedar woody base. Its vetivert-acetate character reinforces the Vetiver EO India without redundancy.
- **Cashmeran (neat)**: Textile-spice warmth at 80 µL. Adds a soft cashmere-musky undercurrent that rounds the dark-earthy base. Its spicy-musky character bridges the Eugenol-Cinnamaldehyde spice axis to the musk chord. At 80 µL it's intentionally above the 50 µL/6mL ceiling — the floriental character absorbs the oriental-woody shift.
- **Ambrofix (30% w/v)**: Mineral-amber depth at 80 µL of 30% = 24 µL active. Provides a crystalline-transparent lift to the base, preventing the heavy oriental resins from becoming muddy. Ambrofix replaces Ambrox Super 30% (DEPLETED) with identical effect. The mineral register creates a "cold crystal" contrast with the warm benzoin-labdanum — the glass shard in the honey.
