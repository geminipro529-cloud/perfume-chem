# Rose Patchouli Moderne — 30mL EdP

## Character
A modern woody-rose where Rose de Mai Absolute meets a clean architectural patchouli accord. Unlike the classic chypre's mossy-patchouli darkness, this composition uses Clearwood and Patchouli EO in a transparent, modern register — the patchouli reads as polished earth rather than hippie-damp soil. The rose core is built leaner than the Chypre Classique (Geraniol constrained to 60 µL, Rhodinol to 30 µL), relying on Damascones and Rose Oxide for rose character rather than overdose of rose alcohols. Hedione HC expands the rose into the cedar-amber-woody base, while the musk chord (Ethylene Brassylate depth, Romandolide projection, Ambrettolide character-echo) creates a clean, diffusive trail. Think Le Labo Rose 31 with more natural rose presence — or a transparent, modernized Voleur de Roses.

**Family**: Woody Floral (patchouli-rose) — bergamot-citrus top, rose-patchouli heart, cedar-amber-musk base

## Material Justification

- **Rose de Mai Absolute (10%)**: The natural rose anchor at 420 µL (42 µL active, 0.73% of concentrate). Higher than the Chypre Classique (400 µL) because the modern patchouli accord needs more natural rose weight to balance the earthy base. The absolute's honeyed-spicy warmth provides the organic counterpoint that prevents Clearwood from reading as sterile.

- **Citronellol + Geraniol + PEA + Rhodinol + Nerol**: The molecular rose accord — 5 materials, but with Geraniol radically reduced from 180 to 60 µL and Rhodinol from 150 to 30 µL to prevent OAV overdoses. Citronellol (280) provides fresh-citrus-rose lift — boosted from 250 to compensate for Geraniol reduction. PEA (360) fills honeyed-rose volume. Rhodinol (30) contributes just enough natural geranium-rose richness at the new ceiling. Nerol (90) adds the sweeter, citrusy-rose facet. The leaner rose alcohol profile is compensated by the rose modifiers (Damascone Beta, Damascol, Rose Oxide) delivering character through ketone/minority impact rather than alcohol mass.

- **Damascone Beta (10%) + Damascol (10%) + Rose Oxide (10%)**: The rose modifier triad. Damascone Beta at 90 µL (9 µL active) provides dark, fruity-rose ketone character. Damascol at 70 µL (7 µL active) — the rose-plum damascone alcohol — adds softer, more rounded rose depth than Damascone Beta alone. Rose Oxide at 70 µL (7 µL active) adds metallic-green geranium leaf. Chose Damascol over Damascenone because the patchouli accord benefits from the plum-fruit facet that Damascol provides, whereas Damascenone's honeyed-woody character would blur into the woody base.

- **Hedione HC**: Radiance amplifier at 520 µL — lifts the rose-patchouli heart outward, creating the transparent modern projection that distinguishes this from a vintage patchouli-rose. High-cis chosen for brighter jasmine-rose luminosity.

- **Patchouli EO + Clearwood**: The modern patchouli accord at 200 + 150 µL. Patchouli EO provides the authentic earthy-camphoraceous patchouli character — the root identity that makes this a patchouli-rose, not just a woody-rose. Clearwood adds the clean, modern patchouli replacement — its polished-earthy character removes the "headshop" heaviness of Patchouli EO alone. The 200:150 ratio keeps Patchouli EO dominant for character while Clearwood refines the texture. No Oakmoss or Evernyl — this is a modern patchouli, not a chypre.

- **Hexyl Salicylate + Benzyl Benzoate**: Fixative pair. Hexyl Salicylate (200) as the light green-floral fixative — its transparent film character holds the bergamot-petitgrain top without adding the cosmetic weight of Benzyl Salicylate (which would clash with the clean-modern patchouli direction). Benzyl Benzoate (200) as the inert mass anchor — adds molecular weight for fixation without contributing any character. NO Benzyl Salicylate here — in a modern patchouli-rose, the heavy cosmetic salicylate cushion would pull the fragrance toward vintage territory.

- **Ethylene Brassylate**: Creamy depth musk at 280 µL — sits closest to skin, its lactonic quality echoes the honeyed-rose of PEA and Rose de Mai. Chosen over Exaltolide (too fatty for modern patchouli) and Habanolide (too warm-skin — would blur the clean-modern texture).

- **Romandolide**: Projection musk at 250 µL — clean, diffusive, slightly woody. The best projection musk for modern woody-floral architecture. Extends the rose-patchouli sillage outward without adding synthetic character. Chosen over Galaxolide (too detergent-fruity for a luxury modern context).

- **Ambrettolide (10%)**: Character-echo musk at 200 µL of 10% (20 µL active). The most naturalistic macrocyclic — its fruity-wine warmth connects the rose heart to the earthy patchouli base organically. Chosen over Habanolide because Ambrettolide's slightly fruity character echoes the patchouli's natural camphoraceous-fruity facets better than Habanolide's neutral skin-warmth.

- **Iso E Super**: Molecular skin-cocoon at 420 µL — the abstract cedar halo that creates the "modern" architectural feel. Chosen over Timberol (too rigid for a rose composition) and Kephalis (too powerful — would dominate). Iso E Super's anosmia-prone blur effect creates the transparent woody space that lets the rose and patchouli speak clearly.

- **Vertofix + Cedarwood EO + Azarbre**: The woody bridge triad. Vertofix (200 µL) bridges the wood and musk layers with its amber-cedar character. Cedarwood EO (140 µL) provides natural woody identity — pencil-shaving warmth. Azarbre (120 µL) adds the cedar-amber warmth that fills the gap between the earthy patchouli and the abstract Iso E Super — warmer than Ambrox and softer than Timberol, it creates the rounded woody bed without the crystalline edge of Ambrox.

- **Ambrofix (30%)**: Crystalline mineral amber at 100 µL (30 µL active). Adds transparent depth to the base — the cold structural counterpoint that prevents the patchouli-woods from reading as muddy. Chosen at a modest dose because Azarbre already provides the warm amber register — Ambrofix here is purely the mineral-clean structural element.

- **Bergamot FCF + Linalool + Petitgrain EO Paraguay + Linalyl Acetate**: The clean citrus top at 450 + 240 + 130 + 100 µL. Bergamot FCF's classical freshness cuts through the earthy base. Petitgrain EO Paraguay provides the green-woody citrus lift that bridges bergamot to patchouli — chosen over standard Petitgrain EO (depleted). Linalool adds fresh-lavender lift. Linalyl Acetate extends bergamot persistence into the heart.
