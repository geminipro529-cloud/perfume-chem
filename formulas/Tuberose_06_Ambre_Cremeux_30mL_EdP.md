# Tuberose Ambre Crémeux — 30mL EdP

## Character
A creamy amber oriental where Tuberose Absolute's narcotic white floral meets a warm, resinous amber base — tuberose dipped in honeyed labdanum. The oriental spine (Siam Benzoin → Labdanum → Vanillin → Coumarin → Tonkarome) creates a golden-resinous warmth reminiscent of Ambre Sultan, while the tuberose-jasmine-ylang heart floats luxuriously on top. Ambermax (50%) provides a full-bodied warm amber register, Ebanol delivers creamy sandalwood texture, and Ethylene Brassylate deepens the lactonic-creamy axis. Paradisamide injects a tropical fruit sparkle that prevents the amber from becoming monolithic. A touch of Patchouli EO and Vetiver EO India adds earthy darkness beneath the golden amber. The musk chord shifts toward creamy-warm — EB for lactonic depth, Habanolide for skin-warmth intimacy, Ambrettolide for naturalistic fruity echo. Think Ambre Sultan with a tuberose heart — the warmest, most opulent tuberose in this collection.

**Family**: Amber Oriental — golden-resinous amber base, tuberose narcotic heart, creamy-lactonic musk drydown

## Material Justification

- **Tuberose Absolute (10% in DPG)**: Narcotic white floral anchor at 500 µL of 10% = 50 µL active (0.82% of concentrate). At the upper-range dose, tuberose's creamy-narcotic-methyl benzoate profile creates a opulent white floral heart that floats over the golden amber base. The tuberose-amber pairing is a classical floriental strategy — the white floral needs the amber warmth to read as "opulent" rather than "raw." Chosen over Tuberalia base (too clean-synthetic for an amber oriental — the composition needs tuberose's natural depth).

- **Siam Benzoin (50% in DPG) + Labdanum Resinoid (10% in DPG)**: The amber-resin backbone. Siam Benzoin (90 µL active) provides the vanillic-sweet balsamic character that defines the amber family — its vanillic-sweet-cinnamon profile is the warm "honey" beneath the tuberose. Labdanum (16 µL active) adds leather-amber depth — the animalic-ambergris warmth that gives amber its "golden" character. Together they form the classical amber accord. Chosen over Benzoin Sumatra (drier, more cinnamon-smoke — Siam's sweeter profile supports the creamy concept). This IS a floriental, so the benzoin dose intentionally exceeds non-oriental ceilings.

- **Vanillin (10%) + Coumarin (20%) + Tonkarome (10% in DPG)**: The sweet-resinous trio. Vanillin (15 µL active) provides the sweet-creamy undercurrent that bridges the amber to the tuberose. Coumarin (20 µL active) adds hay-almond warmth and a tobacco facet. Tonkarome (8 µL active) is Firmenich's coumarinic-tonka base — it extends the coumarin register into a more complex, naturalistic tonka bean effect with almond-hay-clove nuances. Together they create the multi-dimensional sweet-resinous base. All doses push above non-oriental ceilings — the floriental character depends on their sweet-resinous contribution.

- **Ambermax (50%)**: Warm-rounded amber at 80 µL of 50% = 40 µL active. Ambermax provides the "cozy" amber register — full-bodied and warm, in contrast to Ambrox's crystalline-mineral coldness. In this amber oriental composition, Ambermax is the appropriate amber choice. Chosen over Ambrofix (too cold-mineral — would create a "cold crystal in warm honey" effect that works in Poison but not in Ambre Crémeux) and Amberwood F (too transparent — this composition needs amber body, not just warmth).

- **Red Mandarin EO**: Warm citrus top at 180 µL neat. Red Mandarin's sweet-tangerine character provides a warm, rounded citrus opening that foreshadows the amber base. Its lower terpene content (vs. Orange Peel EO) provides better tenacity. Chosen over Bergamot FCF Sicilian (too bright-complex — Mandarin's simpler, sweeter profile pairs better with amber) and Blood Orange (too juicy — would introduce an unwanted fruity register).

- **Jasmine Sambac (10% in DPG) + Ylang Complete EO**: White floral body at 300 + 220 µL. Sambac (30 µL active) provides indolic-jasmonate depth. Ylang (220 µL neat — the highest dose in this collection) provides banana-custard-benzyl ester creaminess at a dose that allows it to sing alongside the amber. In an amber oriental, ylang's natural benzyl salicylate and methyl benzoate content integrates with the cosmetic salicylate fixative and tuberose anchor.

- **Hydroxycitronellal**: Muguet volume at 200 µL. Adds clean-lily-of-the-valley transparency that lifts the dense amber-floral heart. Its schiff-base chemistry with Jasmine Sambac's methyl anthranilate creates an in-situ aurantiol-like effect. Chosen over Bourgeonal (too sharp-aqueous for an amber) and Lilyreal (too cosmetic — would compete with the Benzyl Salicylate diffusion).

- **Paradisamide (10%)**: Tropical fruit sparkle at 90 µL of 10% = 9 µL active. Paradisamide's guava-passionfruit character provides a bright counterpoint to the amber's golden heaviness. Its 150hr tenacity means the fruit sparkle persists alongside the amber (not just a top-note flash). At 9 µL active it's clearly perceptible but not candy-like.

- **Patchouli EO + Vetiver EO India + Cedarwood oil Virginia**: Earthy-woody darkness at 100 + 80 + 100 µL. These three materials provide the "dark forest floor" beneath the golden amber — without them, the amber would read as monolithic honey. Patchouli's camphoraceous-earth character provides the essential bitter counterpoint. Vetiver EO India's smoky-rooty depth adds complexity. Cedarwood's warm-pencil-shaving woodiness bridges to the sandalwood. All three are at restrained doses — they support, not dominate.

- **Ebanol + Sandalwood FO**: Creamy sandalwood pair at 100 + 150 µL. Ebanol provides creamy-milky sandalwood that reinforces the lactonic-creamy axis. Sandalwood FO provides warm-sandalwood volume — chosen because precise sandalwood control is less critical when the amber spine provides the primary base character. Rejected: Javanol (too dry-intimate for a creamy amber) and Sandalore (too fresh-light — would be lost in the amber density).

- **Benzyl Salicylate + Benzyl Benzoate**: Fixative pair at 450 + 150 µL. Benzyl Salicylate provides the cosmetic-waxy diffusion that carries the tuberose outward. Benzyl Benzoate adds inert balsamic fixation — in this amber context, Hexyl Salicylate was rejected because its green-transparent character would fight the golden-amber register.

- **Iso E Super**: Molecular skin cocoon at 450 µL. Provides abstract-cedar skin-adhesion. In this amber context, Iso E Super creates the "phantom amber" trail. Chosen over Azarbre (would add cedar-amber warmth that competes with the benzoin-labdanum axis) and Timberol (too architectural — would add unwanted dryness to the creamy amber).

- **Ethylene Brassylate + Habanolide + Ambrettolide**: 3-axis creamy-warm musk chord. EB (250 µL — highest musk dose in this collection) = creamy-lactonic depth that reinforces the vanillin-coumarin creaminess and the tuberose's natural lactonic qualities. Habanolide (150 µL) = warm-skin macrocyclic intimacy — chosen over Romandolide because the amber context needs skin-warmth, not woody projection. Ambrettolide (8 µL active) = naturalistic fruity-wine echo that sustains the Paradisamide tropicality into the musky drydown.

- **Ambrofix (30% w/v)**: Mineral-amber depth at 80 µL of 30% = 24 µL active. Provides a crystalline-transparent contrast to the warm Ambermax — the "cold crystal in warm honey" effect that prevents the amber from becoming monolithic. Replaces Ambrox Super 30% (DEPLETED).

- **Cashmeran (neat)**: Textile-spice warmth at 80 µL neat. Adds a soft cashmere-musky undercurrent. At 80 µL it intentionally exceeds the 50 µL/6mL non-oriental ceiling — the floriental character absorbs the oriental-woody shift.

- **Vertofix**: Woody-musky bridge at 100 µL. Glues the Iso E Super to the sandalwood-musk base. Its vetivert-acetate character reinforces the Vetiver EO India.

- **PCME (10%)**: Cresylic-narcotic facet at 25 µL of 10% = 2.5 µL active. Capped at 25 µL to stay below OAV overdose threshold. The horse-stable white floral character reinforces tuberose's natural complexity in the amber context.
