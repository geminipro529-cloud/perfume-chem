# F2 — Le Chypre Vert · 30 mL Finished EDP

**Family:** Modern Green Chypre — mass-market lane, luxury build
**Bottle:** 30.00 mL Eau de Parfum
**Concentration:** 20 % concentrate in perfumer's ethanol
**Composition of bottle:** 6.00 mL concentrate (35 raw materials) + 24.00 mL ethanol

> **Design intent.** The *profile* sits in the broadly-likeable green-chypre lane (Cristalle / Niki de Saint Phalle / Aliage / Eden Roc) — sparkling bergamot-galbanum top, luminous floral-jasmonate heart, deep mossy-woody-musky base. The *build* uses max-cost materials to push **luxury, depth, texture, and performance** simultaneously: Hedione HC for radiance, Iso E Super at 9.7% of concentrate for velvet halo, Ebanol for sandalwood-musk creaminess, three macrocyclic musks (Galaxolide + EB + Ambrettolide) for 10–12 hr sillage, Ambrofix + Evernyl + Patchouli + Vetiver for the chypre spine.

---

## What you'll need

- 1 × clean 30 mL atomizer or amber bottle (final bottle)
- 1 × clean 10 mL amber vial (concentrate build vial)
- Positive-displacement pipette 10–100 µL (trace doses)
- Standard pipette 100–1000 µL
- 1 mL glass syringe or graduated dropper for larger volumes
- Perfumer's ethanol ≥ 96 %, fragrance-grade
- Optional: 1–2 drops distilled water for final softening

> Build the **6 mL concentrate first**, mature 4+ weeks, then dilute. Macrocyclic musks + galbanum + evernyl all need maturation time to shed their "ingredient-list" reading.

- **Chypre spine:** Patchouli + Vetiver + Cedarwood VA + Evernyl + Coumarin — modernized (no IBQ, no birch tar, no animalic indole) so it stays mass-market-friendly

## Step 2 — Mature the concentrate

1. Cap the 10 mL build vial tightly. Label "F2 concentrate · DD-MM-YYYY".
2. Rest **dark, 18–22 °C, minimum 4 weeks** before dilution. Galbanum + Evernyl + macrocyclic musks all need maturation; Ebanol + Cashmeran take ~3 weeks to fold into the wood-musk halo.

## What it should smell like

- **0–15 min (top):** Sparkling green-citrus — Bergamot Sicilian + Cedrat brightness, Galbanum stem-sap shadow, Triplal fresh-watery diffusion, Hexenol crushed-leaf trace. Reads "expensive perfume" instantly.
- **15 min – 2 hr (heart):** The aldehyde C12 hands the spotlight to a wide jasmonate-floral chord. Hedione HC gives the "shimmer" texture mass-market consumers register as luxury (Cristalle's signature trick); Mayol + Bourgeonal carry green-muguet diffusion; ACA + PCME + Cis Jasmone build the jasmine; Damascol + Geraniol + PEA give a soft rose halo; Ylang Comoros gives narcotic depth.
- **2 hr onward (heart→base bridge):** Iso E Super takes over as the texture spine — velvety-warm-woody halo against which the patchouli/vetiver/cedarwood/evernyl chypre signature emerges.
- **6 hr onward (base):** Mossy-creamy-musky drydown. Ebanol's sandalwood-musk facet becomes legible (this is the "luxury skin" reading). Three musks alternate visibility through hours 6–12. Coumarin shadow keeps it warm without going fougère.

Expected performance on skin (median, 0.5 mL spray, dry skin):
- **Sillage:** 4–6 hr radiant projection
- **Longevity:** 10–14 hr
- **Drydown skin-cling:** 18+ hr (the macrocyclic musks)

## Cost note (per 30 mL bottle, indicative)

The build is dense in premium materials (Hedione HC, ACA, Mayol, PCME, Ebanol, Cedrat Sicilian, Ylang Comoros, Ambrettolide). Per-bottle raw-material cost will land in the **niche/luxury** band, not true mass-market — this is "mass-market profile, niche execution". If you want to *lower* cost while keeping the profile, the cheapest single switch is **Hedione HC → Hedione (drop ~30 %)**, but you lose ~40 % of the luminosity that defines the smell. Don't recommend cutting it.
