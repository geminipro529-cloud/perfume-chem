## DESIGN RATIONALE

**Family:** Chypre — Mineral-citrus opening → jasmine heart → amber-woods drydown  
**Concept:** Rain-on-limestone petrichor yields to jasmine mist over chypre-amber warmth  
**Mineral opening:** Geosmin 1% (4,444 OAV) — petrichor signature + Cedrat citron (972) + Bergamot (667) + Calone marine (2,000) + Scentenal metallic ozone  
**Jasmine heart:** cis-Jasmone (10,000 OAV) + Gamma Undecalactone lactonic bridge (3,333) + Sambac touch + Hedione/HC radiance cloud  
**Orris touch:** Alpha Irone (1,500) — subtle luxury + Beta Ionone 1% (2,381) — woody depth + Orivone buttery support  
**Chypre backbone:** Evernyl at 60 µL — THE chypre material (oakmoss replacement) — prevents floral from collapsing into soft amber  
**Musk chord (3 axes):** Ambrettolide (skin-warmth) + EB (creamy depth) + Cashmeran (textile projection)

## PROCEDURE

1. **Pre-flight.** Label beaker: *Minéral Profond — 30mL — YYYY-MM-DD*. Wipe pipette tips. Have Geosmin, Calone, Scentenal ready (they're trace materials in 1% dilution — verify stock).
2. **Dose Base** (1→5). Swirl after each addition. Evernyl at 60 µL = deliberate chypre backbone. Cashmeran + Ebanol = creamy-textile warm bed for mineral opening.
3. **Dose Heart — Jasmine** (6→13). Swirl after #10, after #13.
   - **CHECK:** After #13, aroma should read as: Hedione radiance cloud (voluminous, diffusive) + cis-Jasmone green-ketone (identifiable jasmine) + Sambac indolic warmth (natural). Should smell like jasmine, not celery-soup. If celery — cis-Jasmone overdose.
4. **Dose Heart — Orris** (14→17). Swirl after #17.
   - **CHECK:** Jasmine + Orris together = warm floral with powdery depth. Beta Ionone woody-violet should be background, not front stage.
5. **Dose Top** (18→27) quickly — ≤90 seconds. Cap immediately.
   - **CHECK:** Freshly capped: Geosmin petrichor (THAT smell), Cedrat flinty-bright, bergamot citrus, Calone watermelon-fresh, aldehydic sparkle. Should smell like "standing on a wet cliff after rain."
6. **Add ethanol** in 3 portions (~8 mL each). Swirl 15s between. Cloudiness clears dramatically as DPG-based dilutions (Alpha Irone, Sambac, Ambrettolide, Indole) dissolve out.
7. **Weight cross-check.** 30 mL × ρ ≈ 23.5–24.0 g (ethanol-dominant, ~80% ethanol × 0.79 + 20% concentrate × ~1.0). Acceptable range: 23.0–24.5 g.
8. **Transfer** to 30 mL amber atomiser. ≤1 mL headspace.
9. **Macerate** 4 weeks minimum, dark, 18-22°C. Geosmin and Alpha Irone need time to develop.

## TROUBLESHOOTING

| Off-note | Likely cause | Fix |
|----------|--------------|-----|
| Beetroot/soil (day 0-7) | Geosmin pre-maceration rawness | **NORMAL.** Wait until day 14 minimum |
| Celery-soup / over-green | cis-Jasmone overdose or Geosmin + Scentenal interaction | Verify #9 dose (150 µL) and #21 (60 µL of 1%) |
| Too woody/dark | Beta Ionone overdose or undiluted stock used | **CRITICAL:** Verify 1% stock (#17), NOT neat Beta Ionone |
| No mineral character | Geosmin depleted or Scentenal missing | Verify #18 and #21 |
| Flat / collapsed | Missing Evernyl (chypre backbone) | Verify #3 — 60 µL Evernyl is structural |
| Too rosy / metallic | Alpha Damascone cross-contamination | Verify no Alpha Damascone in this formula (not included) |
| Melon-bomb / synthetic | Calone overdose | Verify #22 — 60 µL of 1% |
| Sharp alcohol burn at day 28+ | Incomplete maceration | Wait another 14 days |
