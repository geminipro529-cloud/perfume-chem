### Perfumer Analysis

**1. Character** — Modern mineral aromatic-fougère with Ellena-style transparency. Evokes a hybrid of Bleu de Chanel (ambrox-citrus structure) and Terre d'Hermès (mineral-cedar-vetiver backbone) with an Ellena iris signature. Architecture is **top-to-base with a thin heart**: the top (55.7% OAV) and base (23.0% OAV) dominate, while the heart at only 4 materials (22.9% active) is structurally hollow — Hedione is doing all the radiance work alone.

**2. Opening (0-5 min)** — Massive grapefruit-citrus blast. Grapefruit FCF (OAV 16,804) and Pamzest (11,891) dominate alongside Hedione (11,810) — the latter an unusual top-notes performer from its VP 0.5 Pa. Total vapor 119 ppm. The "crushed leaf" Verdox (OAV <1) is sub-threshold at this dose — the 20 µL is too low for perceptible freshness. The Ellena iris ghost (Alpha Irone OAV 5) is at threshold but will register as expensive cosmetic texture.

**3. Heart (30 min-2 hr)** — Hedione takes over as primary OAV carrier (12,181 → 12,965), supported by Iso E Super's slow climb. Lavender (OAV 222 → 233) provides a steady aromatic thread, but the expected fougère heart is thin — the target calls for 40% heart active but only 22.9% is delivered. Geraniol (OAV 21) is moderate. The lavender + linalyl acetate pair are flagged as potentially redundant (JND overlap).

**4. Drydown (2 hr-4 hr+)** — Base dominance: iso e super (9,884), javanol (8,378), hedione (13,640) persist strongly. Coumarin (OAV 86) is above its cliff threshold of 60, meaning **bitter almond emerges aggressively** at drydown — the coumarin dose of 0.4% active is pushing into tonka territory (margin only 4% from boundary). Galaxolide (OAV 9) underwhelms as a projection musk at this dose. Clearwood (OAV <1) and Romandolide (OAV <1) are functionally inert — the "invisible skin musk" concept fails because Romandolide's VP is too low (0.001 Pa) to generate perceptible headspace at 50 µL.

**5. Sillage & Diffusion** — Primary OAV carriers at opening: Grapefruit FCF (16,804), Pamzest (11,891), Hedione (11,810). At drydown: Hedione (13,640), Iso E Super (9,884), Javanol (8,378). The sillage is **citrus-forward initially, then transitions to woody-ambrox projection**. The 72% adaptation overlap risk (top 5 in same medium tier) means the entire composition may seem to collapse to a flat woody-ambrox hum after ~30 min of wear.

**6. Longevity** — 4 hr evaporation: total vapor drops from 119 to 38 ppm (68% loss). Base persistence is strong: Iso E Super (VP 0.15 Pa), Ambrofix (0.05 Pa), Javanol (0.1 Pa) all have long half-lives. 4% of materials have VP < 0.01 Pa, flagged for weak longevity. Expected skin life: **6-8 hours** on skin, limited by the thin heart — once citrus burns off, the skeleton is Iso E Super + Hedione + Javanol.

**7. Balance** — Pyramid: T:25.6% H:22.9% B:51.5% vs target T:20% H:40% B:40%. The heart deficit (−17.1 pp) is the single biggest structural weakness. OAV range: 0 (Romandolide) to 16,804 (Grapefruit FCF). σ‑log contrast = 1.73 (acceptable range 0.8–1.2). Carles ratio violations: Iso E Super dominates Ambrofix (138:1), Galaxolide (980:1), Cashmeran (417:1) — all >8:1 Carles limit. Volatility balance: T:56% H:21% B:23% (target for EdP: 30:35:35).

- **Romandolide sub-threshold** (OAV < 1) — its role as "invisible skin musk" concept is correct but the material is not functional at 50 µL with VP 0.001 Pa; either increase to 200 µL+ or replace with Galaxolide
- **Verdox sub-threshold** (OAV < 1) — "crushed-leaf freshness" is not perceptible at 20 µL; increase to 60 µL+
