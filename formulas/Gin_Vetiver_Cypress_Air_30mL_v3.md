## Target / ideal and latest user boundary

Juniper-led gin and bitter citrus peel; a leafy citrus middle; recognizable Indian vetiver in the drydown, framed by smooth woody body and a little cedar texture. Cypress stays light. The user explicitly permits Hedione while excluding jasmine accords. Hedione remains transparent support, not a jasmine subject. This is not a claim that Hedione itself has no floral or jasmine facet.

The middle is now developed with **Petitgrain EO Paraguay**, supported by Hedione. Clary Sage is omitted because its herbal, tea and tobacco/leather direction would add a different emphasis. No jasmine accord, absolute or base is assembled. Ingredient count is a consequence of selected roles, not a quality target.

## Where the body and middle come from

- **Iso E Super** adds the main new woody fullness. IFF describes rounded, smooth woody-amber character and fullness; that supports its selected role but does not establish this blend's diffusion or liking. [IFF Iso E Super](https://www.iff.com/scent/ingredients-compendium/iso-e-super/)
- **Indian vetiver** remains the defining root-wood subject. The small **Virginia cedar** addition gives a different wood texture alongside the smooth support. Berjé describes Virginia cedar as smoother, with classic cedar character; it remains subordinate here to avoid a cedar-led or creamy drydown. This material-type reference is not an assay or sensory assessment of the owned lot. [Berjé cedarwood oils](https://berjeinc.com/products/cedarwood-oils/)
- **Hedione** remains as explicitly allowed transparent support. Its manufacturer describes floral-jasmine and citrus facets; it is neither odorless nor a guarantee of greater projection. Here it surrounds the citrus-leaf middle rather than constructing a jasmine accord. [dsm-firmenich Hedione](https://studio.dsm-firmenich.com/product/hedioner-pe-964898)
- **Petitgrain Paraguay** contributes the middle's citrus-leaf character. Its supplier reference describes leafy green, citrus, light neroli-like and woody facets. The intended progression is peel to leaf to wood, not a promise that fresh grapefruit will remain dominant for hours. Eden's description is a material-type reference; the owned stock is Aroma&More, not the Eden lot. [Eden Botanicals Petitgrain Paraguay](https://www.edenbotanicals.com/petitgrain-bigarade-paraguay-organic.html)
- **Zenolide** remains the one restrained musk. Its delicate manufacturer-described character is the selection reason; its fruit facet could still soften the gin image and must be judged by smell. [IFF Zenolide](https://www.iff.com/scent/ingredients-compendium/zenolide/)

**Ethanol supplies most of the bottle volume, not the perfume's olfactory body.** The structural allocation is Hedione and Iso E Super around the vetiver subject, with small cedar texture, citrus-leaf detail and a single musk. Volume shares do not measure perceived contribution.

These dose allocations are the parent's new design hypothesis. They were not derived from supplier typical-use figures, invented safe limits, ingredient-count advice or an optimization score. The two independent native reviews supported the qualitative roles; final doses, stock binding and acceptance remain the parent's responsibility.

## Why the nearby additions are not included

Clary Sage would bring herbaceous, tea and tobacco/leather nuances, so it is left out of this citrus-middle version. [Eden Botanicals Clary Sage](https://www.edenbotanicals.com/clary-sage-organic.html)

No Clearwood, Vertofix, extra musk, Hexyl Salicylate, separate Linalyl Acetate, Terpinyl Acetate, resin tincture or sweet creamy multiwood layer is added. Coffee, ginger, magnolia and orris remain outside the brief. Acetylated vetiver and bergamot remain deferred. Depleted Romandolide is not used. Cypress is not increased to create body.
