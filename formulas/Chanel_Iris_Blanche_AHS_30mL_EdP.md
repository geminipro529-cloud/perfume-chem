Alpha Irone at trace only — this is "iris blanc" not "orris butter."

## HOW IT SMELLS

**Opening (0–5 min):** Aldehydic sparkle + mandarin + marine Calone — immediately recognizable as "Chanel fresh." The aldehydes fizz, the mandarin is juicy-sweet, the Calone adds sporty aquatic lift.

**Heart (5 min–2 hrs):** The iris emerges as the aldehydes fade. Alpha Ionone provides clean violet-iris brightness — this is NOT a lipstick powder. It's "white iris" — luminous, fresh, transparent. Dihydro Beta Ionone adds woody warmth without violet dominance. Orivone + Irotyl give green-powdery body. Ultralia is the ghost — you smell "clean iris" not "iris molecule." The white musks (Habanolide + Romandolide) are already present, creating a clean-sporty bed under the iris.

**Drydown (2–6 hrs):** The Coumarin-tonka emerges as the iris fades — hay-almond warmth, the Allure Homme Sport DNA. Vetiver India provides earthy-smoky masculine backbone. Cedarwood pencil-cedar spine. The white musks hold everything in a clean, sporty frame. No darkness. No sweetness. Just clean.

**6+ hrs:** White musk + coumarin + vetiver skin scent. Clean, elegant, masculine.

## MATERIAL SELECTION

| Register | Chosen | Why Chanel Would Choose It | Rejected Alternative |
|----------|--------|----------------------------|---------------------|
| **Clean violet-iris** | Alpha Ionone 250 µL | Bright, luminous, not dark — the "blanche" character | Beta Ionone (too woody-dark violet) |
| **Warm texture** | Dihydro Beta Ionone 200 µL | Woody-fruity without OR5A1 competition — lets Alpha Ionone lead | None — this is the only warm-but-not-dominant ionone |
| **Clean body** | Orivone 250 µL | Buttery-clean — no rooty-metallic edge | Alpha Irone (too expensive-buttery for sport) |
| **Powder** | AIMI 150 µL | Cosmetic-clean — the Chanel polish | Methyl Ionone Pure (too generic) |
| **Fresh iris** | Irotyl 40 µL | Green-powdery lift — the "just-washed" iris | Allyl Ionone (too green-woody) |
| **Transparency** | Ultralia 20 µL | Ghost-iris — you smell "iris" not molecules | Heliotropal (too almond-gourmand) |
| **Trace iris truth** | Alpha Irone 8 µL 30% | Authentic iris — just enough for "real" | Full dose (too expensive-luxury for sport) |
| **White musk** | Habanolide 500 µL | Clean-sporty projection — THE Chanel musk | EB (too creamy-heavy), Ambrettolide (too wine-fruity) |
| **Tonka** | Coumarin 20% 150 µL | The AHS drydown — hay-almond | Vanillin (too gourmand), Tonka FO (pre-blend) |

| **Aldehyde** | C12 MNA 1% 80 µL | Clean sparkle — Chanel aldehydic signature | C10 (too waxy), C11 (too fatty) |
