**Smell:** Bergamot-aldehydic citrus opening at 11,263 ppm with cis-Jasmone threading green-jasmine lift into the first 60 seconds. Within 2 minutes Dihydrojasmone + Jasmine absolute bloom as warm jasmonate flesh inside a 19,308 ppm Hedione+HC radiance cloud — smells like a real flower, not a headspace abstract. The orris register sits behind: ionone powder (10,620 ppm) projects cosmetic-luxury face powder, Orivone adds buttery warmth from a non-ionone pathway, and Alpha Irone at 579 ppm reads as genuine orris butter — expensive, not synthetically clean. The rose trace (Geraniol 1,609 + damascones 130 + Rose Oxide 80) brightens the heart without competing. Drydown is Ambrofix crystal (10,475 ppm) riding the Iso E Super molecular cocoon (32,180 ppm) with Cashmeran textile-warmth and Koavone natural cedar anchoring the synthetic woods. Evernyl prevents collapse into softness. Ambrettolide at 579 ppm finally reads as skin-warmth — the only musk in the chord that bridges Romandolide's projection to EB's creamy depth. Benzoin-Heliotropal-Coumarin sweet-almond powder closes the 4-hour+ tail.
