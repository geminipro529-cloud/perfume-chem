# Bleu Carbon — Upgrade Procedure

**You have:** Bleu Carbon Final v1, 30 mL bottle
**You'll do:** Remove 5 mL → add 21 things → ethanol → done
**You'll get:** 30 mL at 19.8% EdP, 58 materials
**Time:** ~15 minutes

---

## Check You Have These

```
☐ 5 mL syringe or graduated pipette
☐ Empty 5–10 mL bottle (for the removed portion)
☐ 1 mL graduated pipette (100–1000 µL range)
☐ Micro-pipette or 1 mL syringe with 0.01 mL marks (5–90 µL range)
☐ Ethanol 96% (~4 mL)

Check inventory for these 7 — you may not have them:
☐ Benzyl Salicylate (neat)
☐ Cashmeran (neat)
☐ Floralozone (10%)
☐ Amberwood F (neat)
☐ Azarbre (neat)
☐ Cardamom EO (neat)
☐ Polysantol (neat)
```

---

## Procedure

### STEP 1 — Remove 5 mL

Draw exactly **5.00 mL** from the bottle with a syringe or pipette. Put it in a labeled container: `Bleu Carbon Light — ~14% EdC`. Keep it — it's a wearable lighter version.

Your bottle now has ~25 mL.

---

### STEP 2 — Base (11 additions)

Add these directly to the bottle. Swirl 5 seconds between each.

| # | Material | Amount | Pipette to use |
|---|----------|--------|----------------|
| 1 | Ambrofix 30% | **307 µL** | 1 mL graduated |
| 2 | Benzyl Salicylate | **120 µL** | 1 mL graduated |
| 3 | Olibanum Resinoid 10% | **122 µL** | 1 mL graduated |
| 4 | Ethylene Brassylate | **113 µL** | 1 mL graduated |
| 5 | Habanolide | **88 µL** | micro-pipette |
| 6 | Polysantol | **80 µL** | micro-pipette |
| 7 | Cashmeran | **60 µL** | micro-pipette |
| 8 | Javanol | **52 µL** | micro-pipette |
| 9 | Amberwood F | **50 µL** | micro-pipette |
| 10 | Azarbre | **50 µL** | micro-pipette |
| 11 | Evernyl | **34 µL** | micro-pipette |

**Swirl 15 seconds.**

---

### STEP 3 — Heart (7 additions)

| # | Material | Amount | Pipette to use |
|---|----------|--------|----------------|
| 12 | Hedione | **170 µL** | 1 mL graduated |
| 13 | Lavender EO High Altitude | **137 µL** | 1 mL graduated |
| 14 | Hedione HC | **117 µL** | 1 mL graduated |
| 15 | Floralozone 10% | **60 µL** | micro-pipette |
| 16 | Aurantiol 10% | **57 µL** | micro-pipette |
| 17 | Coumarin 20% | **27 µL** | micro-pipette |
| 18 | Cardamom EO | **25 µL** | micro-pipette |

**Swirl 15 seconds.**

---

### STEP 4 — Top (2 additions)

| # | Material | Amount | Pipette to use |
|---|----------|--------|----------------|
| 19 | Black Pepper EO | **38 µL** | micro-pipette |
| 20 | Scentenal 1% | **23 µL** | micro-pipette |

**Swirl 10 seconds.**

---

### STEP 5 — Ethanol

| # | Material | Amount |
|---|----------|--------|
| 21 | Ethanol 96% | **3,270 µL** (3.27 mL) |

Add the ethanol slowly down the inside wall of the bottle. This brings you back to exactly 30 mL.

---

### STEP 6 — Finish

1. Cap tightly.
2. Invert **50 times** — gentle, no shaking.
3. Label: `Bleu Carbon MAX — [today's date] — 19.8% EdP`
4. Store cool and dark.

---

## Maceration

| If your bottle was... | Then... |
|---|---|
| Already macerated 4+ weeks | Wait **2 weeks** before judging. Wearable after 48 hours. |
| Still macerating | Continue the clock. Additions integrate alongside everything. |
| Fresh (not yet macerated) | Wait **4 weeks** from today. |
