## Procedure

### Step 1 — Benzyl Salicylate

Add **80 µL** of Benzyl Salicylate (neat) directly into the bottle.

Use the 1 mL graduated pipette. It's a slightly viscous liquid — draw slowly, dispense fully.

Swirl 5 seconds.

### Step 2 — Cashmeran

Add **40 µL** of Cashmeran (neat).

Use the micro-pipette. Thin liquid, easy to handle.

Swirl 5 seconds.

### Step 3 — Habanolide

Add **30 µL** of Habanolide (neat). The bottle already has 50 µL — this brings it to 80.

Use the micro-pipette.

Swirl 5 seconds.

### Step 4 — Aurantiol 10%

Add **20 µL** of Aurantiol 10%. The bottle already has 40 µL — this brings it to 60.

Use the micro-pipette.

Swirl 5 seconds.

### Step 5 — Cardamom EO

Add **20 µL** of Cardamom EO (neat).

Use the micro-pipette. You'll smell it immediately — green-spice aromatic.

Swirl 5 seconds.

### Step 6 — Musk Ketone

Weigh **20 mg** of Musk Ketone powder on the milligram scale. Tip directly into the bottle.

It will take 12–24 hours to fully dissolve in the ethanol. Swirl gently — you'll see small crystals suspended. They'll disappear overnight.

### Step 7 — Finish

1. **Cap tightly.**
2. **Invert 30 times.** Gentle — no shaking.
3. **Label:** `Bleu Carbon + [today's date]`
4. **Store dark.**
