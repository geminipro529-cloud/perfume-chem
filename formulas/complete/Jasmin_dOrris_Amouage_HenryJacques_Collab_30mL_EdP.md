## SMELL PREDICTION

**Opening (0–10 min):** Cardamom bright-cineolic sparkle + Saffron leather warmth + Bergamot citrus. The brightest, most complex opening of all six formulas. Saffron is the Amouage rum character, Cardamom provides Henry Jacques' clean lift.

**Heart (10 min – 2 hours):** Jasmine Sambac at 60 µL active (full volume) + Paradisamide ghost amplifier (you don't smell guava, you smell "the most beautiful jasmine"). cis-Jasmone cuts through (10,000 OAV). Dihydro Beta Ionone provides woody-fruity texture without violet dominance — the Alpha Irone at 7.5 µL active speaks as rooty-butter orris, not "violet perfume." Orivone fills the butter register. Allyl Ionone adds green-woody concrete edge.

**Drydown (2–6 hours):** Musk bloom timeline: Romandolide at 30 min (clean woody projection), EB at 90 min (creamy depth), Musk Ketone at 120 min (powder-echo iris). Kephalis woody-amber-tobacco at 3,333 OAV provides the Amouage character without harshness. Patchouli dark earth grounds everything. Benzoin sweet-balsamic warmth emerges late. Benzyl Benzoate invisible anchor extends all naturals 2-3 hours.

**6+ hours:** Musk Ketone powder-echo iris + Vertofix woody-musky bridge + Benzoin + Javanol intimate sandalwood skin. Cardamom memory faded. No smoke. No animalics. No harshness. Just beautiful, elegant, complex warmth.

## HOW IT READS

This is what happens when maximalist complexity meets minimalist restraint. The Amouage brought the architecture — the musk bloom timeline, the Paradisamide ghost amplifier, the incense-resin DNA, the dry woods symphony. The Henry Jacques brought the wisdom — drop everything that challenges wearability, let the Alpha Irone speak without violet competition, add Cardamom for brightness, anchor everything with BB for 8+ hour longevity.

**It's not a compromise. It's the best of both.**
