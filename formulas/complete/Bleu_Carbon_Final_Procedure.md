# Bleu Carbon — Final Mixing Procedure

**Batch:** 30 mL · 16.9% EdP · 55 materials
**Time:** ~20 minutes bench work
**Macerate:** 4 weeks minimum

---

## Equipment

| Item | For |
|------|-----|
| 1 amber glass bottle, 30 mL | Final container |
| 1 graduated pipette, 1 mL (100–1000 µL) | Medium doses |
| 1 micro-pipette or 1 mL syringe with 0.01 mL marks | Small doses (4–90 µL) |
| Ethanol 96% (perfumer's grade) | ~25 mL |
| Milligram scale | Musk Ketone powder |
| Label + pen | |

**Rule:** Wipe or swap pipette between materials. Work on a stable surface.

## PHASE 4 — DILUTION & FINISH

| Step | Action | Detail |
|------|--------|--------|
| 56 | **Ethanol 96%** | Add **24,942 µL** (24.94 mL). Pour slowly down the bottle wall. |
| 57 | **Cap tightly** | Full seal — no air gap. |
| 58 | **Invert 50×** | Gentle, no shaking. Aerating citrus accelerates oxidation. |
| 59 | **Label** | `Bleu Carbon — [today's date] — 16.9% EdP — open after [today + 28 days]` |
| 60 | **Store dark** | 15–20°C. Invert gently once per week. |

## If You Already Have the v1 Bottle Mixed

Don't start fresh. Instead, add these 7 materials directly to your existing bottle (no cuts possible — they're already in solution):

| Step | Material | Add | Pipette |
|------|----------|----:|---------|
| 1 | Benzyl Salicylate | **80 µL** | 1 mL graduated |
| 2 | Habanolide | **+30 µL** (50→80) | micro-pipette |
| 3 | Cashmeran | **40 µL** | micro-pipette |
| 4 | Aurantiol 10% | **+20 µL** (40→60) | micro-pipette |
| 5 | Cardamom EO | **20 µL** | micro-pipette |
| 6 | Musk Ketone | **20 mg** | weigh, tip in |

Cap → invert 30× → label `Bleu Carbon +` → wait 1 week if already macerated.

The cuts (Zenolide, DHM, Cedrat, etc.) can't be undone from a mixed bottle. Your formula will have slightly higher doses of these than the optimized version — the difference is negligible for all except DHM (180 vs 130 µL), which will make your opening slightly more "blue" and aquatic. This is a style preference, not a defect.
