## DIRECTION

**L'Apothicaire du Nord** is a forest-gin perfume built on three stars: juniper leads the heart, cardamom binds, vetiver anchors. Grapefruit-bergamot opens bright. Cashmeran warms without sweetening. Ambrofix projects without dominating. DHBI cools the drydown. Myristic acid builds silent structure. Coumarin-tonka emerges at hour three — a seduction, not a dessert.

The brief: *gin & tonic, cardamom holds them together, coumarin darkens the end.* Arrived through 5 formula revisions, 8 OAV audits, 1 nitrile disaster, and 17 house critiques. Compromise is gone. Every material earns its place.

## PROCEDURE

1. Label 60 mL amber bottle: *L'Apothicaire du Nord — 50 mL EDT+ — 2026-05-10*
2. Dose Base (1→9). Swirl after each. Myristic acid: pipette from 15g/10mL stock. DHBI last.
3. Dose Heart (10→14). Juniper + Cardamom — smell for gin-and-tonic clarity.
4. Dose Top (15→16) — ≤30 seconds. Cap immediately.
5. Add ethanol in 3 portions. Swirl 15s between.
6. Macerate 4 weeks minimum, dark, 18–22°C. 6 preferred.
7. Cold-filter if hazy.
