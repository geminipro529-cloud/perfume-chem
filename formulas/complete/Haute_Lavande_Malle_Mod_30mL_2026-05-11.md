## Layer E — Soliflore Transformation → 50 mL (to add)

### Problem Diagnosis

The base concentrate is massive and competitive: 780 µL Iso E Super (abstract cedar cocoon, 12.5%), 750 µL Hedione (jasmine radiance, 12%), 540 µL Romandolide + 540 µL Ambrettolide 10% (musk axis, 10.4% combined), 510 µL Cedarwood VA (natural pencil-cedar, 8.2%). These create a woody-musky-jasmine base that **drowns the lavender** in the heart and drydown. For a soliflore, the lavender must be the first, loudest, and last thing you smell.

**Critical ratio issue:** Natural lavender EO is ~30-40% linalyl acetate and ~25-35% linalool — roughly 1:1. The current formula has linalool at only 24 µL vs. linalyl acetate at 165 µL — a 1:7 ratio. Linalool (ODT 0.51 ppb) is 5.3× more potent in air than linalyl acetate (ODT 2.7 ppb), but its mass is wildly under-represented. The fresh-floral lift of lavender is nearly absent.

**Camphoraceous deficit:** Both HA and Bontaux are L. angustifolia — floral-sweet, low camphor. Real lavender has a subtle camphoraceous edge that gives it botanical authenticity. Under-dosed linalool + zero camphor = the lavender reads as flat, sweet-herbal water rather than the living plant.

## Layer F — Hedonic Finish (to add)

### Hedonic Diagnosis

The soliflore rebuild (Layer E) fixed lavender dominance, but the formula remains aromatically **dry and angular** — linalool's sharp freshness, Spike Lavender's camphor, Rosemary's cineole, and the Iso E Super / Cedarwood base create a herbal-mineral profile with high olfactory "friction." Hedonic perfumery requires **softness, powder, radiance, and richness** to offset aromatic angularity.

The current high-hedonic materials are doing heavy lifting: Hedione (3.0, 750 µL), Javanol (3.5, 75 µL), Bergamot Sicilian (4.0, 80 µL), Lavender EO HA (3.0, 560 µL). But they're competing against a bulk of low-to-moderate hedonic mass: Iso E Super (2.0, 780 µL), Linalool (0.48, 624 µL), Linalyl Acetate (0.32, 1,115 µL), Cedarwood VA (~1.5, 510 µL), Vetiver (1.5, 80 µL).

**Strategy:** Add trace-level high-impact materials that create a soft-powder cushion and rich-floral bloom around the lavender without shifting the aromatic soliflore identity. Work with materials whose ODT is so low that µg-level doses create an outsized olfactory presence — making them highly cost-efficient in hedonic terms.

## What Changed — Full Analysis

### Before (Malle Mod → 30 mL)
The Ellena overlay added photorealistic stem and earth to the warm woody-musky base, but the formula was a **woody-aromatic with lavender** disguised as a lavender fragrance. The lavender sat on top of a massive Iso E Super + Hedione + Cedarwood + Coumarin structure that dominated the drydown. Aromatic, dry, angular — interesting but not beautiful.

### After Layer E (Soliflore → 50 mL)
Lavender established as the **dominant character across all evaporation phases** — the linalool deficit was fixed, the camphoraceous gap was filled, and the herbal-bridge materials (Clary Sage, Rosemary, Bergamot) created a complete lavender plant illusion.

### After Layer G (Longevity Optimized → 50 mL)
The lavender signature now persists through the deep drydown via Terpinyl Acetate + Kephalis structural anchoring:

| Phase | What you smell | Dominant materials |
|-------|---------------|-------------------|
| **Opening (0-15 min)** | Fresh-citrus lavender blast with jasmine sparkle, stem-green snap, ionone shimmer | Linalool (VP 15.8 Pa) + Beta-Pinene (VP 320 Pa) + Bergamot Sicilian + Hedione HC + Beta Ionone |
| **Heart (15 min-3 hr)** | Sweet-herbal lavender body wrapped in rose-plum richness and soft almond powder | Linalyl Acetate (VP 17.5 Pa) + Lavender EOs + Clary Sage + Damascenone + Heliotropal |
| **Late Heart (3-5 hr)** | Herbal-lavender echo, warm clary-ambery bridge, soft skin-musk intimacy | Terpinyl Acetate (VP 0.4 Pa) + Clary Sage + Ambrettolide + Benzyl Benzoate |
| **Base (5-8+ hr)** | Coumarinic-hay powder, transparent woody-amber structure, cool mineral trail | Terpinyl Acetate + Coumarin + Heliotropal + Kephalis + Romandolide + Ambrox |

### Key Hedonic Material Roles

- **Damascenone (0.37 µL active):** The highest OAV-per-µL material in the formula. ODT 0.004 ppb means even sub-µL active creates perceptibility orders of magnitude above threshold. Adds the "dark expensive richness" dimension that makes a formula feel niche rather than functional. The rose facet sits *behind* lavender, not beside it.
- **Heliotropal (15 µL):** Vintage-powder signature. At 0.17% of concentrate, it's below its standalone impact threshold — it adds texture (softness, roundness) rather than an identifiable "heliotrope note." Bridges lavender's herbal freshness to coumarin's hay-sweet base.
- **Hedione HC (100 µL):** The HC isomer has a cleaner, more transparent jasmine-radiance than standard Hedione. Adds sparkle to the top notes without the waxy-fatty body that standard Hedione develops. The combined Hedione system (850 µL total) is now the second-largest material group after lavender.
- **Beta Ionone (0.5 µL active):** ODT 0.007 ppb. At this dose, it creates a transparent violet-ionone powder that's felt as a shimmer rather than a note. Ionones have the unique ability to make entire compositions feel more diaphanous — they "ionize" the air around the fragrance.
- **Terpinyl Acetate (245 µL):** The unsung hero of lavender longevity. VP 0.4 Pa means this is the only material in the formula that carries the herbal-lavender signature into the deep drydown (5-8+ hr). Without it, the lavender character dies at ~2 hr when linalyl acetate evaporates. The boost from 125 → 245 µL extends the "lavender ghost" from a whisper to a clear trail.
- **Kephalis (4 µL):** Transparent structural anchor. At 0.04% of concentrate and ODT ~0.01 ppb, it operates as architecture rather than aroma — felt as "the formula has a spine" rather than smelled as "Kephalis." Adds the missing base-note gravity without introducing a competing character.
- **Ambrettolide (74 µL active):** The deepest skin-reservoir layer. >300 hr tenacity means it's still there when everything else is gone. The fruity-wine warmth adds a skin-intimacy that makes the formula feel personal rather than projected. The boost from 54 → 74 µL active deepens the reservoir without pushing the musk character forward.

### Odor Family
**Aromatic Lavender Soliflore** — Unchanged. The hedonic materials serve as texture and finish, not as competing floral or oriental characters. Heliotropal at 15 µL is well below the family-shifting ceiling (≤21 µL equivalent).

## Scoring — Generic vs. Family-Aware

The pipeline's generic scorer applies identical axis weights to all formulas regardless of fragrance family. This penalizes soliflores for traits they intentionally avoid (low versatility, low stacking depth, low longevity). The family-aware scorer (`engine/family_scorer.py`) applies different axis weights per family — de-emphasizing axes irrelevant to the concept and emphasizing defining axes.

### Composite Comparison

| Scorer | Geometric Total | Delta |
|--------|:--------------:|:-----:|
| Generic (default weights) | 69.2 | — |
| Family-Aware (soliflore weights) | **73.7** | **+4.5** |

### How the Weights Shift

The soliflore preset de-emphasizes axes where soliflores inherently score low and emphasizes axes that define soliflore quality:

| Axis | Generic Weight | Soliflore Weight | Reason |
|------|:------------:|:--------------:|--------|
| Photorealism | 0.7 | **1.0** | +0.3 — must smell like the real flower; the defining axis |
| Hedonic | 0.5 | **0.9** | +0.4 — beauty is the point of a soliflore |
| Sillage | 0.8 | 0.9 | +0.1 — the flower should project |
| Texture | 0.8 | 0.9 | +0.1 — petal-soft, silky |
| Stacking Depth | 0.8 | **0.3** | −0.5 — single subject, not layered architecture |
| Longevity | 0.8 | **0.5** | −0.3 — flowers are ephemeral by nature |
| Luxury | 0.8 | 0.6 | −0.2 — soliflores can be humble or luxurious |
| Skin Performance | 0.7 | 0.5 | −0.2 — moderate intimacy, not the point |

The family-aware composite (73.7) correctly reflects that this formula excels at what matters for a soliflore (photorealism, hedonic quality, sillage, texture) while appropriately down-weighting what doesn't (stacking depth, longevity, luxury).

## Maceration

1. Add all layers in sequence: E (items 29–38), F (items 39–42), G (items 43–48).
2. Add 40.82 mL ethanol 96%.
3. Swirl gently 30 seconds. Store cool and dark.

**Minimum maceration:** 4 weeks. Linalyl acetate and linalool integrate immediately, but the EOs (Clary Sage, Spike Lavender) need time to marry. Heliotropal and Beta Ionone are powder-formers requiring 3–4 weeks to fully diffuse. Kephalis needs curing time to establish its structural role in the base. Benzyl Benzoate reservoir formation improves with age.
