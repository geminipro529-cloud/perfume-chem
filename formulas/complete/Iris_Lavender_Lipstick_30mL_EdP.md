# Iris-Lavender Lipstick — 30 mL EdP — Bench Instructions

**Concept:** Lipstick iris meets haute lavande. The 15 mL pour-out from your HL soliflore build is the lavender-woody-musk skeleton. 18 iris-cosmetic additions transform it into a Chanel No. 19 / Dior Homme axis — powdery orris, waxy cosmetic raspberry, almond-heliotrope, with lavender as the co-star rather than a supporting note.

**Pour-out contents (15 mL at ~20% EdT):** Lavender EO HA + Bontaux (~872 µL), Iso E Super (382), Hedione (368), Romandolide (265), Cedarwood VA (250), Ambrettolide 10% (265), Coumarin 20% (118), Linalyl Acetate (81), Ethyl Linalool (74), Zenolide (66), Vertofix (59), Javanol (37), Geraniol (37), Terpinyl Acetate (37), Vetiver EO India (39), Benzyl Benzoate (39), Ambrox Super ~33% (39), Evernyl (32), Vetival (26), Linalool (12), Damascenone 1% (6), Nagarmotha Oil (4), Petitgrain EO (3), Geosmin 0.1% (2.5), Beta-Pinene (1.5), ~11.9 mL ethanol.

**Target:** 30 mL at 17.2% EdP. 18 additions, no FTEC or Fleuressence.
**Time at bench:** ~30 minutes.
**Wait after:** 4 weeks minimum, 8 weeks ideal (iris needs time).

---

## PREP (do first)

### Prep A — Raspberry Ketone 10% in TEC

Weigh **0.10 g Raspberry Ketone** (neat powder) into a small vial. Add **0.90 g Triethyl Citrate (TEC)**. Swirl. Gentle warming (hand heat or warm water bath) helps dissolution. Label: `Raspberry Ketone 10% in TEC`.

### Prep B — Heliotropal (warm before pipetting)

Heliotropal (Piperonal) melts at ~37°C. Before pipetting, **warm the bottle in your hand or a cup of warm water for 2-3 minutes** until it liquefies. Pipette quickly — it re-solidifies as it cools. It will fully dissolve once ethanol is added.

### Prep C — Antioxidant (strongly recommended)

The lavender EOs and Beta-Pinene give this formula a predicted 7-day shelf life without antioxidant. Add **0.006 g BHT** (~0.02% w/v) or **1 drop Vitamin E (tocopherol)** to extend to >2 years. If you don't have BHT, the formula will still perform beautifully — just store extra cold and use within 3 months.

## STEP 1 — Pre-dissolve solids in ethanol

Before adding materials, pour **5 mL ethanol 96%** into the 15 mL pour-out bottle. This ensures Heliotropal and Vanillin dissolve fully when added. Swirl.

## STEP 3 — Add Remaining Ethanol

Add **7.5 mL ethanol 96%** (12.5 mL total minus the 5 mL pre-added in Step 1). Cap tightly. Invert gently 3-4 times — do NOT shake vigorously (iris materials are fragile, violent agitation can cause micro-emulsion haze).

---

## STEP 4 — Label and Wait

Label: `Iris-Lavender Lipstick — 30 mL EdP — Open after 2026-06-14`

**Minimum wait: 4 weeks.** Iris materials (Alpha Irone, ionones, Heliotropal) are slow integrators. Benzyl Salicylate needs 3-4 weeks for full cushion establishment. Cashmeran and the musk complex require extended curing for structural role development.

**Ideal wait: 8 weeks.** Alpha Irone continues to develop roundness through week 8. The raspberry ketone-ionone receptor priming effect strengthens with maturation.

Store cool (15-20°C) and dark. Do not open during maceration.

## Warnings

### IFRA — Personal Use Only

**Evernyl** is locked at 32 µL (from the original base — cannot remove). At 30 mL final volume this is **0.107% vs 0.1% IFRA Category 4 limit**. The original HL soliflore formula documents this: "Correct in the NEXT batch: Evernyl 7 µL (not 66)."

This batch is for **personal use only**. The IFRA limit is a skin sensitization precaution — the excess is minor (7% over) and many commercial fragrances have operated at 0.1-0.2% Evernyl historically, but do not sell or gift this batch.

### Shelf Life

Without antioxidant, predicted maturation shelf life is **7 days** due to terpene-rich lavender EOs and Beta-Pinene. Add BHT 0.02% w/v (0.006 g) or 1 drop tocopherol to extend to >2 years. If you skip the antioxidant, store at <15°C and use within 3 months.

### Heliotropal — Crystalline Solid

Heliotropal (Piperonal) melts at ~37°C. The "warm before pipetting" step is critical — attempting to pipette cold crystals will give incorrect dose. Once in ethanol, it remains dissolved.
