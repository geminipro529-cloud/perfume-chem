Store cool and dark. Invert gently once per week.

## Accord Architecture

**Top — dark-aromatic, no citrus:** High-altitude lavender's full spectrum opens first — the floral linalyl acetate sweetness, then the herbaceous-camphor edge. Black pepper adds dry spark. Cedarwood Virginia provides pencil-shaving warmth as the structural backbone. No citrus lift — instead, rosemary and juniper berry supply coniferous-herbal freshness, while spike lavender reinforces the camphor facet. This opening says "lavender" unambiguously, but a darker, more serious lavender than any fougère.

**Heart — dry sandalwood, rooty earth:** Javanol's dry-mineral sandalwood precision meets the lavender's persistent herbaceous core. Geraniol bridges with geranium-leaf green-rose brightness — the only softness in the composition. Clary sage adds amber-tobacco herbal depth without sweetness. Vetiver India draws rooty earth through the middle register. Dihydrojasmone provides creamy jasmine radiance at trace. Damascenone and Alpha Damascone whisper rose-narcotic luxury — felt as texture, not identified as a note.

**Base — mossy-mineral, no amber:** Evernyl anchors the drydown with mossy-mineral structure — the oakmoss replacement operating as a character note, not a background fixative. Romandolide projects clean macrocyclic musk outward. Ambrettolide provides quiet natural skin warmth — the only warmth in the composition. Iso E Super creates a molecular cocoon for skin adhesion. Patchouli, Clearwood, and Nagarmortha form an earthy-camphor-mineral triad. Norlimbanol Dextro and Kephalis add architectural woody tenacity. No vanilla. No coumarin. No benzoin. No amber. The drydown is moss, musk, and mineral earth.

## Material Selection Rationale

### Why Romandolide (not Habanolide, not EB)
Projection axis. Romandolide's clean, slightly woody-musk diffusion extends the sillage outward without adding warmth or creaminess. Habanolide would add warm-skin intimacy that contradicts the dry-mineral base. EB would introduce lactonic creaminess that fights the mossy architecture.

### Why Javanol (not Ebanol, not Sandalore)
Dry-mineral, cold, architectural. Javanol's precision sandalwood cuts through the lavender without softening it. Ebanol would add creamy-soft yielding quality — wrong for a structured, serious composition. Sandalore is too light and citrus-adjacent — would pull toward freshness we don't want.

### Why Evernyl at 55 µL (not background level)
Evernyl is a character note here — the mossy-mineral structure IS the base identity. At 55 µL neat, it's clearly perceptible (not subliminal as in Bleu Carbon where it's at 25 µL). The oakmoss-replacement triad (lavender–coumarin–Evernyl) is deliberately broken — we keep lavender and Evernyl but drop coumarin entirely. This is a woody-mossy lavender, not a fougère.

### Why no citrus
Citrus brightness would turn this into a different fragrance — the dark-aromatic opening depends on the absence of bergamot/grapefruit/cedrat sparkle. Juniper and rosemary provide lift without brightness. The black pepper opening is dry and spicy, not fresh.

### Why no DHM
Dihydromyrcenol's blue-aquatic freshness is the signature of the modern blue genre (Sauvage, BdC). Its absence is what separates this formula from that axis. Adding even 50 µL of DHM would pull the top toward "shower gel" and undermine the dark-aromatic character.

### Lavender proportion
At 400 µL, lavender represents 20.2% of the concentrate — the single largest material. In Bleu Carbon, lavender HA is 6.7% of concentrate (340/5044). This formula gives lavender nearly 3× the proportional weight. Combined with Linalyl Acetate and Spike Lavender, the lavender complex is 473 µL (23.9% of concentrate) — the formula IS lavender.

### Lavender complex breakdown
The lavender thematic block (HA + Linalyl Acetate + Spike) = **2,961 OAV (20.6% of total)** — the single largest identifiable group:
- **Linalyl Acetate** (rank #3, OAV 1,925) — the volatile top-note amplifier, giving the opening its pear-sweet lavender character

### Model caveat
This uses the simplified VP × ppm heuristic (same as `_bleu_carbon_oav_recalc.py`), which does NOT apply gamma-corrected activity coefficients or mixture suppression. Low-ODT base materials (Vertofix, Romandolide) are likely over-ranked. The actual headspace under gamma-corrected Raoult's law would push volatile top/heart materials (Linalyl Acetate, Clary Sage, Geraniol) higher and suppress low-VP base materials. Directionally, lavender HA is solidly in the dominant band and will be clearly legible throughout the wear.
