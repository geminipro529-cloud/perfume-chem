# F1 — Le Barbier de Grasse · 30 mL Finished EDP · v2 (Longevity+)

**Family:** Classical Fougère (lavender · coumarin · oakmoss-cedar · musk)
**Bottle:** 30.00 mL Eau de Parfum
**Concentration:** 20.85 % concentrate in perfumer's ethanol
**Composition of bottle:** 6.255 mL concentrate (20 raw materials) + 23.745 mL ethanol

> **What's different from v1:** smell signature preserved exactly. Three additive-only fixative reinforcements in Stage 1A:
> - **+ Ambrettolide** (80 µL) — macrocyclic musk, 3rd axis under EB + Galaxolide
> - **+ Cashmeran** (25 µL) — woody-musky tail extender for Iso E / cedar
> - **+ Ambrox Super** (extra 150 µL of 30 %) — deepens existing ambery anchor
>
> Net effect: estimated **+2–4 hr base persistence** with no perceptible note shift. All additions sit inside the existing musk/amber/wood lane of the formula and are mixture-suppressed by the current chord.

---

## What you'll need

- 1 × clean 30 mL atomizer or amber bottle (final bottle)
- 1 × clean 10 mL amber vial (concentrate build vial)
- Positive-displacement pipette (10–100 µL) for trace doses
- 1 mL glass syringe or graduated dropper for larger doses
- Perfumer's ethanol ≥ 96 %, fragrance-grade
- Optional: 1 × distilled water in dropper (for final softening)

> Build the **6.255 mL concentrate first** in the small vial, mature, then dilute into the 30 mL bottle. Do **not** add raw materials directly to ethanol — the base layer needs time to homogenize neat before dilution.

## Step 2 — Mature the concentrate

1. Cap the 10 mL build vial tightly. Label "F1 v2 concentrate · DD-MM-YYYY".
2. Rest **dark, 18–22 °C, minimum 4 weeks** before dilution. Coumarin + Evernyl + macrocyclic musks (now 3 of them: EB + Galaxolide + Ambrettolide) integrate slowly; rushing produces a thin, "ingredient-list" reading. Cashmeran in particular needs 3+ weeks to fold into the Iso E / cedar chord.

---

## Step 3 — Dilute into the 30 mL bottle

> Once concentrate has matured.

| # | Component | µL | mL |
|---:|---|---:|---:|
| A | Matured concentrate (Step 1) | 6 255 | 6.255 |
| B | Perfumer's ethanol ≥ 96 % | 23 745 | 23.745 |
| | **Finished EDP** | **30 000** | **30.000** |

**Procedure:**
1. Transfer all **6.255 mL concentrate** into the 30 mL atomizer bottle.
2. Add **23.745 mL ethanol**. (Cleanest way: weigh ~18.76 g ethanol on a 0.01 g scale; ρ ≈ 0.79 → 23.74 mL.)
3. *Optional softener:* 1–2 drops distilled water. Cap, invert 10×.
4. Macerate **2 more weeks** dark before evaluation on skin.

## Shortcut — add directly to the already-bottled v1 EDP

If you've already built and matured the v1 30 mL EDP and don't want to rebuild from scratch, pipette these doses **directly into the finished bottle**:

| Add | Form | µL into bottle | Result |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Ambrettolide | neat | **80** | adds the 3rd musk axis |
| Cashmeran | neat | **25** | adds the woody fixative shadow |
| Ambrox Super | 30 % w/v | **150** | deepens existing ambery anchor |
| | | **= 255 µL added** | bottle becomes 30.255 mL |

Then cap, invert 20×, and **re-macerate dark 3 weeks** before re-evaluating. Three weeks because you skipped the neat-base homogenization phase, so the new materials need extra time to integrate into the diluted matrix.

> *Note:* this shortcut keeps the same wt% as the rebuild (because the additions are identical), with one caveat — Ambrettolide and Cashmeran integrate ~30 % faster when added neat-into-neat (Stage 1A) than diluted-into-diluted (bottle direct). If you have the patience, rebuild.
