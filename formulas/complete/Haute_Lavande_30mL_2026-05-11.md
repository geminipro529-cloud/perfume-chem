## Maceration

Store cool and dark. Invert gently once per week.
**Minimum 3 weeks.** Cedarwood-Javanol-Evernyl base needs integration. Lavender top softens at 10-14 days. Ethyl Linalool + Linalool bridge merges at 2-3 weeks.
