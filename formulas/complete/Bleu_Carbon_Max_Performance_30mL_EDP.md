---

### Phase 5 — FINISHING

1. **Cap tightly.** Ensure seal is complete — no air gap.
2. **Invert 50 times** — gently, no shaking. Aerating citrus oils accelerates oxidation.
3. **Label:** `Bleu Carbon Max — YYYY-MM-DD — macerate 4 weeks — open after YYYY-MM-DD+28`
4. **Store cool and dark** — 15–20°C, away from direct light.

---

### Maceration Schedule

| Week | Action | What's happening |
|------|--------|------------------|
| Week 1 | Invert gently 1× at day 4 | Citrus oils emulsify into ethanol; lavender begins integrating with coumarin |
| Week 2 | Invert gently 1× at day 11 | Sandalwood chord (Javanol+Ebanol+Sandalore) blooms; incense opens |
| Week 3 | Invert gently 1× at day 18 | Ambrofix crystallinity settles; Hedione radiance smooths; orris whispers emerge |
| Week 4 | Invert gently 1× at day 25 | Full integration — the formula is ready at day 28 |
| Week 5+ | Optional: age further | The sandalwood-incense heart continues developing through week 8. Peak at 6–8 weeks. |

**Minimum: 4 weeks.** Do not open before day 28 — the frankincense, sandalwood chord, patchouli, and orris require significant integration time.
