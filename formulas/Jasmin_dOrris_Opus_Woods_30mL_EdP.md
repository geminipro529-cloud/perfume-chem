## DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

You don't build Amouage by stacking florals on musks. You build it by creating a **dry-wood concert hall** and seating the jasmine and orris inside it.

**Woods Symphony structure (from headspace architecture):**
- **Top:** Orris, Rum-spice
- **Heart:** Jasmin, Rose, Orris Concrete (multi-ionone accord)
- **Base:** Dry Woods, Animalic trace, Resins

**This formula translates that into inventory materials:**
- The **orris accord is 7 ionone materials** (not 4) — creating waxy-metallic-cool iris concrete character, not cosmetic powder
- The **woods are 9 materials** composing a dry symphony — not "fixation" but the orchestral body
- **Frankincense + balsamic resin** replace gourmand heliotrope-coumarin — Amouage DNA
- **Jasmine is layered IN the woods**, not floating above — Sambac absolute radiates THROUGH the wood grain
- **Animalic trace** (Costus Olifac) replaces clean musk warmth — Opus civet character
- **No gourmand sweet materials** — no coumarin, no heliotropal. The sweetness comes from benzoin and ionone-violet warmth.

## ARCHITECTURAL READING

### Opening (0–15 min)
- Aldehydic sparkle (C12 MNA 267 OAV) + bergamot-petitgrain
- Frankincense cold-resinous (Olibanum 267 OAV — same magnitude as citrus, reads as "incense citrus")
- Saffron leather (Ethyl Safranate 1,333 OAV — the "rum" character)
- Metallic-cold iris edge (Scentenal 27 OAV + Rose Oxide 1,333 OAV + ionones beginning to open)
- **Smells like:** Cold incense + orris concrete + vague citrus

### Heart (15 min – 3 hours)
- **cis-Jasmone cuts through** (10,000 OAV) — green jasminic sparkle riding the woods
- **Alpha Ionone blooms** (10,000 OAV) — violet-berry fruitiness behind the jasmine
- **Beta Ionone woody depth** (4,762 OAV) — the orris concrete register
- Hedione radiance (1,067 OAV) fills the Iso E Super molecular cocoon (2,000 OAV)
- Jasmine absolute warmth (167 OAV) — low OAV but high character weight from indolic richness
- **Smells like:** Jasmine growing through dry cedarwood. Violet-berry orris concrete under the flower. Frankincense cold smoke in the background.

### Drydown (3–8 hours)
- **Kephalis woody-amber-tobacco** (4,000 OAV) — Opus character signature dominates
- **Vertofix woody-musky bridge** (2,667 OAV) — transitions wood to skin
- **Norlimbanol dry-powerful** (1,333 OAV) — the "dry woods" axis
- **Romandolide clean projection** (6,667 OAV) — wood-framed musk
- Benzoin sweet-balsamic warmth (67 OAV — late-emerging due to low VP)
- Jasmine fades → ionone violet-woody remains → dry woods dominate
- **Smells like:** Polished cedarwood, faint tobacco, warm balsamic sweetness, violet-wood memory of orris

### 8+ hours
- Romandolide + Vertofix skin-musk with cedarwood memory
- Benzoin + Javanol intimate sandalwood-balsamic skin scent
- **Smells like:** Dry woods on skin. Faint violet. Warm skin.

## PROCEDURE

1. **Pre-flight.** Label beaker: *Jasmin d'Orris — Opus Woods — 30mL — YYYY-MM-DD*. Verify Beta Ionone 1% stock fresh.
2. **Dose Base — Woods** (#1-9). Swirl after each 3 additions. The woods will be cloudy (vetiver-type materials in ethanol-limited base).
3. **Dose Base — Incense** (#10-12). Swirl. Olibanum is viscous — warm bottle if needed.
4. **Dose Base — Musk** (#12-13). Swirl. Romandolide is projective even pre-dilution — you'll smell it.
5. **Dose Heart — Jasmine** (#14-21). Swirl after #17 (cis-Jasmone), after #21 (Indole). **CHECK:** The jasmine should smell green-ketone fresh (cis-Jasmone) over Hedione radiance cloud, with Sambac indolic warmth. If celery-soup — verify cis-Jasmone dose.
6. **Dose Heart — Orris** (#22-29). Swirl after each 4 materials. **CHECK:** Complex ionone accord — violet-berry (Alpha Ionone) + woody-concrete (Beta Ionone) + buttery (Orivone) + green-woody bite (Allyl Ionone). Should smell like orris concrete, not cosmetic lipstick powder. Cold, waxy, elegant.
7. **Dose Heart — Rose** (#30-33). Swirl. Trace only — Rose Oxide metallic integrates with ionone coolness.
8. **Dose Top** (#34-39) quickly — ≤60 seconds. Cap immediately. **CHECK:** Aldehydic sparkle + frankincense cold + saffron-leather rum + Costus animalic-mineral. Should smell expensive, unusual, Amouage.
9. **Add ethanol** in 3 portions (~7.5 mL each). Swirl 15s between.
10. **Macerate** 6 weeks minimum. This is a 40-material extrait — it needs time. Evaluate at day 42. Peak at day 90-120.
