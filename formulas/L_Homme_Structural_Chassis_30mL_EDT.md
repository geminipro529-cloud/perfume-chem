## Selection and Rejection Ledger

- The three-material musk chord is Ethylene Brassylate for depth, Galaxolide for diffusion, and Exaltolide for skin character.
- Florol was selected as a modern muguet function. Lilial is excluded because it is prohibited in EU cosmetics; Florol is not claimed to be odor-identical.
- Alpha Isomethyl Ionone plus Parmavert gives violet/green structure without turning the chassis into an iris perfume.
- Heavy vanilla, patchouli, leather, incense, and balsams were rejected because they would impede flanker flexibility.
- White pepper is unavailable; Black Pepper EO is reserved for the realistic module at trace scale.
- Habanolide and Muscenone Delta are unavailable and are handled only through the explicit amends below.

## Mixing and Evaluation

1. Preblend the woods, musks, coumarin/tonka materials, and Ambrofix.
2. Add the floral-air and green materials, then the citrus materials.
3. Add DPG last for this structural control.
4. Add 4.5 mL concentrate to 25.5 mL ethanol 96%; invert gently.
5. Mature at least 14 days. Evaluate strip at 0, 5, and 30 minutes, then 2 and 4 hours.
