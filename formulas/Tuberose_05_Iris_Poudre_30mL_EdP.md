# Tuberose Iris Poudré — 30mL EdP

## Character
A powdery iris-tuberose floral where Tuberose Absolute's narcotic creaminess meets the cool, cosmetic elegance of orris. The ionone complex (Alpha Ionone, Beta Ionone, Alpha Isomethyl Ionone, Irotyl, Dihydro Beta Ionone, Orivone, Ultralia) creates a multi-dimensional iris-powder-violet bed that cradles the tuberose heart. Heliotropal and Musk Ketone deepen the powder register into old-fashioned cosmetic talcum. The musk chord shifts toward powdery-intimate — Musk Ketone for talcum character-echo, Ambrettolide for naturalistic warmth, Romandolide for projection. Damascone Beta and Nerol add a rosy bridge, while Vanillin and Coumarin provide a whisper of creamy sweetness. Think Iris Poudre meets Fracas — the most elegant, cosmetic tuberose in this collection.

**Family**: Powdery Floral — iris-violet powder core, tuberose narcotic heart, cosmetic-musk-talcium base

## Material Justification

- **Tuberose Absolute (10% in DPG)**: Narcotic white floral anchor at 450 µL of 10% = 45 µL active (0.79% of concentrate). In this powdery context, tuberose's creamy character provides the "cream" to the iris "powder" — the composition depends on the tuberose-irone contrast. Chosen because tuberose's natural methyl benzoate and benzyl salicylate facets integrate with the cosmetic salicylate fixative layer.

- **Alpha Ionone + Beta Ionone + Alpha Isomethyl Ionone**: The primary ionone triad. Alpha Ionone (120 µL) provides the classic orris-violet character — the most "iris" of the ionones. Beta Ionone (100 µL) adds woody-violet depth with a raspberry undertone. Alpha Isomethyl Ionone / Methyl Ionone Pure (150 µL) provides the substantive, long-lasting iris backbone — the most tenacious ionone. Together they create the multi-dimensional iris bed. Rejected: Allyl Ionone / Ketone V (too aggressive-pineapple for a powdery composition).

- **Irotyl + Dihydro Beta Ionone + Orivone + Ultralia**: The iris-texture quartet. Irotyl (50 µL) adds a specific iris-orris character with a slight fruity nuance. Dihydro Beta Ionone (80 µL) provides a softer, more transparent woody-violet than the parent ionones. Orivone (80 µL) contributes warm-buttery orris depth — the "butter" to the iris "powder." Ultralia (30 µL) adds ghost-iris transparency at a trace level — a barely-there iris facet that adds complexity without weight. Together they create a textured, multi-dimensional iris register.

- **Heliotropal (Piperonal)**: Sweet-almond-heliotrope powder at 50 µL neat. Heliotropal provides the heliotrope-talcum facet that defines classical cosmetic powder. Its almond-vanillic character bridges the ionone iris to the vanillin-coumarin base. Chosen over Anisaldehyde (sharper, more anisic — would read as licorice rather than cosmetic powder).

- **Musk Ketone (10% in DPG)**: Powdery-talcum character-echo musk at 80 µL of 10% = 8 µL active. The ONLY musk that directly reinforces the powder register — its nitro-musk talcum character is essential for a cosmetic-powder composition. Pre-diluted to 10% for accurate dosing of the crystalline solid. Chosen because no other musk (EB, Romandolide, Habanolide, Ambrettolide) provides powder — they add creaminess, projection, warmth, or fruitiness, but not talcum.

- **Ambrettolide (10% in DPG)**: Naturalistic warmth musk at 60 µL of 10% = 6 µL active. The most naturalistic macrocyclic musk adds "quiet skin warmth" beneath the cosmetic powder. This prevents the composition from reading as "makeup powder" — it reads as "powdered skin." Chosen over Habanolide (warmer-skin, would compete with the cosmetic-powder character).

- **Jasmine Sambac (10% in DPG)**: Indolic white floral depth at 200 µL of 10% = 20 µL active. At this restrained dose, Jasmine Sambac provides authentic jasmonate-indole character without dominating — the iris should lead. The dose is lower than in Coco Exotique (300 µL) and Poison (350 µL) because the powdery-iris register requires a lighter jasmine hand.

- **PCME (10%)**: Cresylic-narcotic tuberose reinforcement at 25 µL of 10% = 2.5 µL active. At this dose, PCME adds the horse-stable white floral facet that makes tuberose recognizable without pushing OAV into overdose territory.

- **Nerol**: Rose-citrus alcohol at 80 µL neat. Nerol's sweeter, more citrusy-rose character bridges the aldehydic-citrus top to the powdery-floral heart. Chosen over Geraniol (more straightforward rose, less citrus sparkle) and Rhodinol (too rich — would compete with the iris complexity).

- **Damascone Beta (10%)**: Plum-rose depth at 50 µL of 10% = 5 µL active. The beta isomer's plum-rose character sits between the ionone iris and the PEA-citronellol rose axis. At this dose it adds depth without reading as a distinct damascone note.

- **Ylang Comoros Complete EO**: Tropical floral body at 150 µL. Ylang's banana-custard-benzyl ester profile adds creamy-floral volume that supports the tuberose without competing with the iris. The Complete grade's sesquiterpene fraction adds base tenacity. Dose is restrained (vs. 180–250 µL in other formulas) to prevent ylang from dominating the iris.

- **Bergamot FCF + Aldehyde C12 MNA (1%) + Aldehyde C10 (1%)**: The classic aldehydic opening. Bergamot (380 µL) provides hesperidic-linalyl freshness. C12 MNA (0.4 µL active) adds metallic-waxy sparkle that pairs with tuberose's natural waxiness. C10 (0.3 µL active) provides citrus-orange lift. Together they create the aldehydic "fizz" that defines the Soft Floral / Aldehydic Floral family — Chanel No. 5 territory, but with tuberose as the star.

- **Benzyl Salicylate + Hexyl Salicylate + Benzyl Benzoate**: Triple fixative gradient. Benzyl Salicylate (450 µL) = heavy cosmetic diffusion — essential for a cosmetic-powder composition. Hexyl Salicylate (150 µL) = lighter transparent fixative that prevents the Benzyl Salicylate from becoming oppressive. Benzyl Benzoate (120 µL) = inert balsamic anchor that adds molecular mass without contributing character — the composition already has enough character from the iris-tuberose axis.

- **Iso E Super**: Molecular skin cocoon at 500 µL. Provides abstract-cedar skin-adhesion. In this powdery context, Iso E Super creates the "what are you wearing?" halo without adding identifiable wood. Chosen over Timberol (architectural cedar would clash with cosmetic powder).

- **Ebanol + Sandalwood FO**: Sandalwood pair at 80 + 120 µL. Ebanol provides creamy-milky sandalwood that supports the tuberose's creaminess. Sandalwood FO provides warm-sandalwood volume — chosen because precise sandalwood control is less critical in a cosmetic-powder context where sandalwood is a structural support, not a character note. Rejected: Javanol (too dry-intimate — cosmetic powder needs warmth, not mineral precision).

- **Ethylene Brassylate**: Creamy depth musk at 200 µL. The lactonic quality supports the vanillin-coumarin creaminess and the tuberose's natural creaminess. At 200 µL it anchors the musk chord without overwhelming the Musk Ketone powder character.

- **Vanillin (10%) + Coumarin (20%)**: Sheer creamy-sweet base at 60 + 50 µL. Vanillin (6 µL active) adds a whisper of creamy sweetness. Coumarin (10 µL active) provides hay-almond warmth — a classical cosmetic-powder drydown material that pairs with heliotropin. Both within safe non-oriental ceilings.

- **Ambrofix (30% w/v)**: Mineral-amber depth at 80 µL of 30% = 24 µL active. Provides a crystalline-transparent lift that prevents the powdery base from becoming cloying. Replaces Ambrox Super 30% (DEPLETED).

- **Cashmeran (neat)**: Textile-spice warmth at 60 µL. At this dose, Cashmeran adds a soft cashmere undercurrent that rounds the cosmetic-powder base. Slightly above the 50 µL/6mL ceiling but the powdery-iris context absorbs the oriental shift naturally.

- **Vertofix**: Woody-musky bridge at 80 µL. Glues the Iso E Super molecular layer to the sandalwood-musk base.
