## Revision Decision

Galbanum EO is removed because the user does not own it. Its former 10 µL working-stock transfer is replaced with 10 µL Ethanol 96%, keeping the finished bottle at exactly 30 mL without inventing another material.

The owned Cis-3-Hexenol 1% stock was evaluated and rejected as the automatic replacement: its nominal 0.1 µL active dose is a high-OAV cut-grass hypothesis, while the design brief explicitly excludes cut grass and its carrier/basis are unresolved. No other EO is forced into the accord. Petitgrain would add recognizable bitter-orange leaf, Geranium would pull the flower toward rose, Tagetes would add fruity-herbal marigold, and the available white-floral EOs would compete with PADMA. Magnolia's separate formula retains Magnolia EO; only this Hyacinth revision is EO-free because the owned EOs do not improve the named target.

## Deep-Plane Contract

1. **Identity:** PADMA owns the odor object; all other flowers must read as facets inside hyacinth.
2. **Morphology:** a cool green stem, sweet-green ester surface, broad petal, cool lily edge, and sheer lower film.
3. **Function:** every support has one job; Benzyl Benzoate is structural and is not credited as a smelled note.
4. **Relation:** PADMA carries both the green-hyacinth identity and stem axis, connecting downward through PEA and Benzyl Acetate, sideways through Cyclamen/Mayol/Florol, and into Hexyl Salicylate at the late heart.
5. **Mixture:** the formula must remain one hyacinth-centered floral rather than separable green, rosewater, jasmine, and muguet mini-accords.
6. **Temporal:** Benzyl Acetate and the cool muguet edge appear first; PADMA and PEA broaden the heart; Florol and Hexyl Salicylate carry the late floral film.
7. **Spatial:** a centered, cool flower with moderate petal width and no wide jasmine cloud.
8. **Physicochemical:** raw-volume arithmetic is exact; ppm and OAV remain model-bound proxies.
9. **Biological:** no receptor, adaptation, irritation, or population-response claim is promoted.
10. **Hedonic:** beauty and liking are independent sensory criteria and remain NOT TESTED.
11. **Inventory/build:** every source material occurs in the live inventory; Galbanum is explicitly unavailable. PADMA's name crosswalk remains a stock-authority limitation.
12. **Evidence/authority:** the design is final; smell, safety, stability, performance, and release authority are withheld.
13. **Experiment:** first resolve PADMA causality and Hedione takeover with separate constant-total omission arms; a Cis-3-Hexenol variant is optional research, not the final build.

## Material Decisions

- **Cis-3-Hexenol 1%:** rejected from the final build. Its nominal 10 µL raw / 0.1 µL active trial is modeled as a strong, separable cut-grass channel and its carrier/basis are unresolved. It may be tested only as a controlled variant against the omission build.
- **Liffarome 10% w/w:** rejected; green pear would compete with the hyacinth subject, and its carrier is unresolved.
- **Cis-3-Hexenyl Salicylate 20% w/w:** rejected; it would add a persistent tomato-leaf film, and its carrier is unresolved.
- **Caryophyllene Acetate 20% w/w:** rejected; no target-linked dry spicy-woody job, and its carrier is unresolved.
- **Other EOs:** rejected for this formula because they introduce orange-leaf, rose, aromatic, marigold, or another named flower rather than a neutral hyacinth support.
- Benzyl Salicylate, musks, woods, ambers, ozonics, marine materials, aldehydic lift, Rose Oxide, and damascones remain excluded.
- Farnesol remains omitted because its modeled role at the compounding floor is sub-threshold and the formula already has two lower floral films.

Evaluate blind for hyacinth identity, non-hyacinth takeover, transition continuity, and liking as separate criteria.
