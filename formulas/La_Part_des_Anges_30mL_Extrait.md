# La Part des Anges — Cognac-Boisé Oriental Vanilla
**Date:** 2026-06-09
**Batch Size:** 10.00 mL (33% concentrate for extrait-style projection)
**Equipment:** 20–200 µL micropipette, 0.01g precision scale
**Density assumption:** ≈ 1.0 g/mL (1 µL ≈ 1 mg)

> **Tribute reference:** Kilian *Angels' Share* (2020, Benoist Lapouza) — "la part des anges," the cognac that evaporates from oak barrels. Direct construction: cinnamaldehyde + coumarin + maple lactone + tobacco absolute + cedarmber for the boozy oak-barrel effect; hedione to keep the cognac accord from collapsing into syrup (this is the exact structural role Hedione plays in the original).

> **Dilution reminder:** All amounts are the *diluted material* to dispense. Active ingredient is reduced by the dilution factor (e.g., 300 µL of Coumarin at 20% = 60 µL active coumarin). Amounts < 1 mL shown in µL.

---

## Concept

The liquid that rises from the cellar. Opens with the sharp bite of cinnamaldehyde (the actual molecule of cinnamon bark) over a coumarinic-tobacco veil, hedione preventing the boozy accord from collapsing into syrup. The middle is tonka-cedarmber smoke with a soft lift from cashmeran. The drydown is a sandalwood-iso e super molecular cocoon wrapped in ethyl maltol caramel and a three-material musk chord (EB depth + Romandolide projection + Musk Ketone powder).

This is **Oriental Vanilla** at 50% concentrate, the way a 30 mL extrait bottle behaves — liquid, dense, tenacious, instantly readable as luxury.

**Family:** Oriental Vanilla (Soft Amber, Boozy Gourmand direction)
**Concentration:** 33.3% in ethanol (Extrait)
**Luxe Differentiators:** Hedione in the heart (the exact structural play of the original), Coumarin + Maple Lactone + Tonkarome as the tonka-cognac triad, Tobacco Absolute as a literal cigar-barrel association, three-material musk chord.

## Accord Breakdown

- **Boozy spice top:** Cinnamaldehyde (cinnamon bite, the actual molecule) + Bergamot FCF Sicilian (citrus frame, the **richer more complex** bergamot, not the default FCF — chosen because the original Angels' Share's citruses are also "Sicilian" quality) + Ethyl Safranate (golden saffron-spice lift)
- **Tonka-cognac heart:** Coumarin at heavy dose (boozy-honeyed, the cognac-amber primary) + Hedione (radiance, prevents syrup-collapse) + Tonkarome (true tonka-coumarin base) + Tobacco Absolute (cigar-barrel cellar)
- **Cashmere-cedar bridge:** Cashmeran (textile warmth) + Cedramber (cedar-amber bridge)
- **Sandalwood-musk base:** Sandalore (light-fresh sandalwood, the correct register for the cognac-wood pairing) + Iso E Super (molecular cocoon) + Vertofix (woody-musk bridge) + 3-material musk chord: EB (depth) + Romandolide (projection) + Musk Ketone (character-echo)
- **Cognac-maple accent:** Vanillin (real vanilla) + Ethyl Maltol (caramel-cotton-candy) + Maple Lactone (maple-cognac depth) + Labdanum (resinous anchor) + Benzyl Salicylate (luxury diffusion cushion)

## Dosing & Mixing Notes

1. **Order of addition:** Add ethanol last. Dispense concentrate materials largest-volume-first (Iso E Super, Coumarin, Hedione, Benzyl Salicylate, Sandalore), then work down to trace amounts (Maple Lactone, Labdanum Resinoid, Tobacco Absolute).
2. **Cinnamaldehyde at 40 µL** is the micropipette's lower comfort zone — use a fresh tip, draw slowly.
3. **Tobacco Absolute at 80 µL of 10%** is a high-impact trace material; dispense before ethanol to ensure even distribution.
4. **Maceration:** Allow minimum 4 weeks before evaluating. This is an extrait — the coumarin-tonka-musks need significant time to fully marry. The opening will read "spicy-hot" for the first 2 weeks.
5. **Viscous materials (Labdanum Resinoid, Benzyl Salicylate):** Pre-warm gently to 30-35°C to reduce viscosity.
6. **Crystalline solids:** None in this formula. All materials are liquids or pre-dissolved dilutions.
