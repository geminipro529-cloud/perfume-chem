## Target and Design Decision

A living Magnolia EO surface and a magnolia-soft PEDMC upper flower fold into a clean peony corolla, then into a two-temperature satin film. This must remain distinct from the PADMA-green Hyacinth and from the natural Rose de Mai perfume. Hedione is reduced from v1's 600 µL field to a 40 µL aperture. PEA is reduced from 900 µL to 320 µL so it cannot rebuild a generic Rose accord. The v1 DPG padding is removed and the actual aromatic-stock charge is reported honestly.

## Deep-Plane Contract

1. **Identity:** Magnolia EO supplies living natural irregularity and PEDMC owns magnolia softness; Peonile/Hydroxycitronellol form the peony co-subject without a Rose outline.
2. **Morphology:** living natural surface, green-sweet upper petal, magnolia veil, fleshy interior, rounded peony corolla, cool/warm satin underside.
3. **Function:** every row has one nonredundant plane; the two salicylates form a temperature gradient rather than duplicate bulk.
4. **Relation:** Magnolia EO and Stralyl pass into PEDMC; PEDMC passes through Jessemal into the peony cushion; the cushion settles into Hexyl/Benzyl Salicylate and Benzyl Benzoate.
5. **Mixture:** magnolia and peony must remain legible as one continuous two-flower object, not jasmine, gardenia, Rose, or muguet.
6. **Temporal:** upper-petal flash, magnolia/peony heart, then satin floral residue.
7. **Spatial:** broad soft petals with a close, smooth trail; no huge Hedione cloud.
8. **Physicochemical:** raw arithmetic is exact; concentration and OAV are model-bound volume proxies.
9. **Biological:** no receptor, adaptation, irritation, or population-response claim is promoted.
10. **Hedonic:** satin texture, beauty, and liking remain separate bench criteria.
11. **Inventory/build:** all selected labels occur in the live inventory; Magnolia EO species, part, supplier, strength, and amount are unrecorded; Peonile's current profile and an older generated graph disagree on molecular identity.
12. **Evidence/authority:** design authority is final; quantitative, sensory, safety, stability, performance, and release authority are withheld.
13. **Experiment:** Peonile omission is the first causality test; a later Hedione omission checks whether aperture becomes takeover.

## ODT/OAV Takeover Screen

Because the live Magnolia EO record lacks constituent, MW, VP, ODT, and stock-strength data, a complete composite-natural total vapor and OAV are **UNAVAILABLE**. The known-material active-concentrate screen at 305 K gives this opening order: Hedione **435.7**, PEDMC **407.9**, Jessemal **68.3**, Stralyl Acetate **64.0**, PEA **15.3**, Hexyl Salicylate **6.76**, Hydroxycitronellol **2.14**, Benzyl Salicylate **1.29**, Peonile **1.21**, and Benzyl Benzoate **0.056**. Magnolia EO is intentionally not assigned a monomolecular surrogate OAV.

Interpretation is deliberately limited:

- PEDMC and Hedione are nearly coequal modeled leaders; Hedione is capped at 40 µL and must not be raised before bench testing.
- Magnolia EO must use composite-natural treatment once its physical identity and composition are known. Its current 2,000 ppm figure is raw-stock volume only, not active ppm.
- Peonile and Hydroxycitronellol are only threshold-level in this model. Their named-flower effect is a physical-test question, not a numerical conclusion.
- The Peonile supplier/profile identity and the older generated-graph identity conflict. The OAV value is therefore provisional.
- Benzyl Benzoate is sub-threshold and is admitted only as a structural hypothesis.
- OAV does not establish perceived share, beauty, similarity, or longevity.

## Explicit Exclusions

- **Liffarome 10% w/w:** rejected; its green-pear direction would overlap the Hyacinth and its carrier is unresolved.
- **Cis-3-Hexenyl Salicylate 20% w/w:** rejected; it would add a cut-leaf film and its carrier is unresolved.
- **Caryophyllene Acetate 20% w/w:** rejected; no required spicy-woody bridge and its carrier is unresolved.
- Freesia HDI is rejected because it adds a third named floral instead of deepening magnolia–peony.
- DBCA and ACA are rejected because they drive gardenia/cosmetic white-floral drift.
- Dihydrojasmone, Florol, Mayol, Nympheal, Cyclamen Aldehyde, Lilyreal ND, and Bourgeonal are rejected to prevent green-jasmine or muguet overlap with the Hyacinth.
- Geraniol, Citronellol, Nerol, Rhodinol, Rose Oxide, damascones, Rose de Mai Absolute, musks, woods, ambers, lactones, vanilla materials, and resins are excluded.
- Champaca Flower, Ylang, Geranium, Neroli, Petitgrain, and other essential oils are excluded because Magnolia EO already provides the target-specific natural surface without introducing another named flower.
