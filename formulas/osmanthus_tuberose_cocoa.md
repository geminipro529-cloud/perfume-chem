# Osmanthus Tuberose Cocoa — 10 mL EdP

**Character:** A gourmand-floral composition built around three signature naturals — Osmanthus Absolute (apricot-tea-leather), Tuberose Absolute (narcotic white floral), and Cocoa Absolute (bitter-chocolate warmth). The structure layers ionones and damascones for fruity-floral depth, a jasminate heart for radiance, and a warm woody-amber base with benzoin-vanilla tonka sweetness. The result is a rich, complex oriental-gourmand with a transparent floral opening that dries down to a creamy cocoa-sandalwood skin scent.

**Family:** Oriental Gourmand — floral top, gourmand heart, woody-amber base

**Concentration:** ~3.1% concentrate — light EdP / cologne strength

## Material Justification

- **Osmanthus Absolute (10% in DPG)**: The star material at 15 µL of 10% = 1.5 µL active. Osmanthus carries β-ionone, damascones, and lactones that create its signature apricot-tea-leather profile. Chosen because no synthetic can replicate its composite character. The low dose is deliberate — osmanthus is potent and can dominate at higher levels.
- **Tuberose Absolute (10% in DPG)**: Narcotic white floral at 10 µL of 10% = 1 µL active. Tuberose adds creamy-camphoraceous depth that pairs with the cocoa gourmand base. Its natural methyl benzoate content integrates with the jasmine heart.
- **Cocoa Absolute (10% in TEC)**: Bitter-chocolate gourmand anchor at 10 µL of 10% = 1 µL active. Cocoa provides the "cocoa" in the name — a dark, slightly bitter chocolate note that prevents the base from reading as purely vanilla-sweet. Pairs with Patchouli for an earthy-chocolate drydown.
- **Beta Ionone + Alpha Ionone**: The violet-ionone pair that defines osmanthus' woody-tea facet. Beta Ionone (2 µL) provides the primary violet-woody backbone; Alpha Ionone (5 µL) adds iris-powder support. Together they create the "tea" dimension of osmanthus.
- **Alpha Damascone (10%)**: Fruity-rose ripeness at 20 µL of 10% = 2 µL active. Damascone amplifies the apricot-fruit facet of osmanthus without adding a separate rose note. The beta isomer was rejected because Alpha's more metallic-rose character better matches osmanthus' tea-rose profile.
- **Hedione**: Jasmonate radiance at 10 µL. Expands the jasmine-tuberose heart outward into sillage. At this low dose it provides lift without creating a jasmine-dominant composition.
- **Jasmine Sambac (10% in DPG)**: Indolic depth at 10 µL of 10% = 1 µL active. Provides the narcotic white floral gravity that supports tuberose. Chosen over Jasmine FO for authentic indolic character.
- **Jessemal (Jasmonyl)**: Warm-fatty jasmine body at 5 µL. The tetrahydropyran acetate structure provides a different jasmine facet than Hedione — more body, less radiance. Creates a jasmine chord with Hedione and Sambac.
- **Paradisamide (10%)**: Tropical guava-passionfruit at 5 µL of 10% = 0.5 µL active. At this trace level it adds a fruity sparkle that brightens the osmanthus heart without pulling the composition into fruity-floral territory. Its 150hr tenacity means it persists into the drydown.
- **Methyl Pamplemousse (10% in TEC)**: Grapefruit-rhubarb tartness at 5 µL of 10% = 0.5 µL active. Provides a sharp-fresh counterpoint to the creamy gourmand base. The rhubarb facet pairs with Paradisamide's guava character.
- **Dihydrojasmone**: Warm jasmine-ketone at 5 µL. Bridges the floral heart into the woody base. Its ketone structure provides a slightly fruity-warm transition that prevents the heart-base junction from feeling disjointed.
- **Heliotropal (Piperonal)**: Sweet-almond-heliotrope powder at 5 µL. Used as the inventory substitute for Heliotropin (not in stock). Provides the powdery-gourmand bridge between the floral heart and the cocoa-vanilla base. Pairs with Vanillin for a classic heliotrope-vanilla accord.
- **Siam Benzoin (50% in DPG)**: Balsamic-vanilla warmth at 20 µL of 50% = 10 µL active. The primary gourmand anchor — benzoin's vanillic-cinnamic profile creates the "warmth" in the cocoa base. Chosen over Benzoin Sumatra Resinoid (10%) because the 50% dilution provides more volume at this dose.
- **Vanillin (10%)**: Sweet-creamy undercurrent at 5 µL of 10% = 0.5 µL active. Reinforces the benzoin-vanilla axis. At this trace level it adds sweetness without pushing the composition into gourmand-overdose territory.
- **Tonka Bean FO**: Pre-blended coumarinic warmth at 5 µL. Provides hay-almond tonka character as a quick coumarin source. Chosen over Tonkarome (20% in TEC) because the FO is neat and more straightforward for a small batch.
- **Patchouli EO**: Earthy-chocolate depth at 5 µL. Patchouli's earthy-camphoraceous profile bridges the cocoa absolute to the woody base. At this low dose it adds depth without creating a "patchouli perfume."
- **Labdanum Resinoid (10% in DPG)**: Amber-leather warmth at 5 µL of 10% = 0.5 µL active. Provides a subtle ambery fixative that extends the drydown. Its leather facet echoes the osmanthus leather note.
- **Ambrox Super (~33% in DEP:EtOH) — Woods section**: Crystalline amber at 40 µL of ~33% = ~13.2 µL active. Provides transparent mineral depth to the woody base. Combined with the Base section Ambrox Super (20 µL), total amber active is ~19.8 µL — a substantial amber foundation that supports the cocoa without overwhelming it.
- **Ambrox Super (~33% in DEP:EtOH)**: Mineral amber at 20 µL of ~33% = ~6.6 µL active. Second amber layer — slightly different character from Ambrofix, adds complexity to the amber base. The DEP:EtOH dilution provides some solubility advantage for the small batch.
- **Iso E Super**: Molecular skin cocoon at 30 µL. Provides abstract cedar skin-adhesion that anchors the entire composition. At this low dose it adds the "your skin but better" effect without creating an Iso E-dominant perfume.
- **Cedarwood EO**: Natural cedar warmth at 20 µL. Adds identifiable cedar character to the woody base. Chosen over Cedarwood oil Virginia because the standard EO provides a more classic pencil-shaving cedar profile.
- **Sandalwood FO**: Pre-blended warm sandalwood at 30 µL. Provides quick sandalwood volume. At this low dose in a 10 mL batch, it adds warmth without competing with the cocoa base. Rejected: Javanol (too dry-mineral), Ebanol (too creamy-milky, would fight the cocoa), Bacdanol (too heavy).
- **Petitgrain EO Paraguay**: Green-citrus-neroli leaf at 10 µL. Provides the green bridge between the aldehyde top and the white floral heart. Its lighter, greener character (compared to standard Petitgrain) pairs well with the osmanthus tea facet.
- **Aldehyde C12 MNA (1%)**: Aldehyde sparkle at 2 µL of 1% = 0.02 µL active. Extreme trace — provides a subtle metallic-fatty aldehyde lift at the opening. At this level it's perceptible but not harsh. The 1% dilution is necessary because the neat material would be too powerful at this scale.
- **Ethyl Linalool**: Woody-floral lift at 5 µL. Softer and more tenacious than Linalool. Provides a transparent floral top that foreshadows the osmanthus heart. Chosen over Linalool because its woody character better matches the sandalwood-cedar base.

## Concentration Note

This formula is designed at **3.09% concentrate** (309 µL in 10 mL ethanol). This is a light concentration — suitable for an EdC or a very light EdP. If a higher concentration is desired (e.g., 15% EdP = 1,500 µL concentrate), multiply all amounts by approximately **4.85×**.

---

## Musk / Fixative Note

No dedicated musk materials are included in this formula. The musk-like qualities come from:
- **Ambrofix + Ambrox Super**: Amber-musk skin adhesion
- **Iso E Super**: Molecular skin-cocoon effect
- **Siam Benzoin**: Balsamic fixative warmth
- **Labdanum**: Amber-leather fixative

If a dedicated musk chord is desired for a scaled-up version, consider adding:
- **Ethylene Brassylate** (depth axis — creamy-lactonic)
- **Romandolide** (projection axis — diffusive woody-musk)
- **Ambrettolide** (character-echo — naturalistic fruity-wine warmth)
