# Cis Jasmone Collection Vol. 2 — 30 More Cis Jasmone Variations

**Date:** 2026-04-03  
**Batch Size:** 10.00 mL each  
**Format:** Ready-to-mix trial formulas  
**Purpose:** A second collection that keeps `Cis Jasmone` in clear feature or sub-feature position, but explores more suede, resin, peach, aldehydic, sporty, and mineral territory than the first set.

This sequel is split into:
- `NL-31` to `NL-40`: niche-luxury
- `LM-41` to `LM-50`: luxury-mass
- `NM-51` to `NM-60`: niche-mass bridge formulas

All formulas use only current inventory materials from [`inventory.txt`](/d:/chatbots/perfume-chem/inventory.txt).

---

## Cis Jasmone Positioning

- `Star` formulas use roughly `45-60 µL` cis Jasmone per 10 mL formula.
- `Sub-Star` formulas use roughly `30-35 µL` cis Jasmone per 10 mL formula.
- This volume is intentionally broader than the first collection, but the material is still kept visible in every build.

### Niche-Luxury
