## Phase 6 — The Secret Note

### 🔑 Vanillin (10%) — "The Invisible Warmth"

| Step | Material | % | mL | Tool | Notes |
|------|----------|---|-----|------|-------|
| **35** | **Vanillin (10%)** | **+1.5** | **0.148** | **P** | **The deep secret. 148 µL.** |

**Why this works — the hidden synergy:**

Vanillin (10%) does NOT add obvious vanilla sweetness at 1.5% of formula
(only ≈0.015 mL active in 10 mL concentrate). What it actually does:

1. **Activates the Coumarin** — Vanillin + Coumarin together create
   authentic tonka bean richness that neither produces alone. Your
   Coumarin (20%) at 2.5% is currently "flat tonka." Adding Vanillin
   turns it into *three-dimensional* tonka with depth and warmth.

2. **Bridges iris to musk** — Vanillin's molecular structure (4-hydroxy-3-
   methoxybenzaldehyde) hydrogen-bonds with macrocyclic musks, creating
   a "warm haze" transition layer between the iris heart and musk cloud
   that doesn't exist in the formula currently. Without it, there's a
   slight gap between the powdery iris and the clean musks. With it,
   the iris *melts* into the musk.

3. **Extends perceived longevity** — Vanillin at trace levels doesn't
   smell like vanilla — it smells like *warmth itself*. It makes the
   brain interpret the entire composition as richer, more expensive,
   and longer-lasting than the actual sillage suggests.

4. **The Guerlain secret** — Guerlain has used vanillin as a "ghost note"
   in iris compositions for decades (Mitsouko, L'Heure Bleue). Not
   enough to smell vanilla. Enough to feel luxury.

**Concentrate complete:** 103.5% = ~10.2 mL. Cap the vessel.

## Phase 7 — Carrier System (dilution to EdP)

**This perfume is built as a ready-to-wear 20% EdP.**  
The carrier isn't just dilution — IPM and TEC are performance enhancers.

| Step | Material | mL | Tool | Purpose |
|------|----------|----|------|---------|
| 36 | **Isopropyl Myristate (IPM)** | 1.500 | S | Skin-feel enhancer. Reduces alcohol bite. Aids absorption. Acts as co-fixative — slows evaporation of base notes. |
| 37 | **Triethyl Citrate (TEC)** | 1.500 | S | Longevity extender. Smooth dry-down. Deodorizing carrier. Helps musks radiate. |
| 38 | **Perfumer's Alcohol (Ethanol 96%)** | 37.000 | Cyl | Primary carrier. Use graduated cylinder. |

> **Order matters:** Add IPM and TEC to the concentrate first.
> Stir for 30 seconds. *Then* add the alcohol slowly in 3 pours
> (~12 mL each), swirling between pours. This prevents shock-
> precipitation of heavier musks and resins.

**Carrier subtotal:** 40.000 mL  
**Grand total:** 10.6 mL concentrate + 40.0 mL carrier = **50.6 mL EdP**

## Maturation

1. **Cap immediately** after adding all carrier.
2. **Day 1–3:** Invert the bottle gently once per day. The musks and
   woods are marrying. It will smell rough and boozy — that's normal.
3. **Day 7:** First evaluation on skin. The iris should start floating
   above the wood. If it's buried, the ionones haven't bloomed yet — wait.
4. **Day 14:** The Vanillin bridge should be fully integrated. The
   "gap" between iris and musk should be gone. The alcohol harshness
   fades. IPM and TEC are now fully homogenized.
5. **Day 21–30:** Full maturation. The 5 wood layers should be
   individually distinguishable on a smelling strip at the 2-hour mark.
   The IPM gives it a silky skin-feel that raw alcohol EdPs lack.

> **No separate dilution step.** This is already a finished 20% EdP
> ready to wear from the bottle after maturation.

### The Three Hidden Synergies Already in This Formula

1. **Iso E Super × Galaxolide/Habanolide** — ISO E amplifies
   polycyclic and macrocyclic musks by 3-5×. Your 3% ISO E is
   effectively turning 4% Galaxolide into the projection of 12%.
   This is why the formula projects despite "only" 18% musk.

2. **Hedione × Bergamot FCF** — Hedione lifts citrus 2-3× beyond
   its natural projection. Your 6.5% Hedione is projecting that
   2.5% Bergamot like it's 7%. Combined with Aldehyde C11, the
   opening is much bigger than the 10% top allocation suggests.

3. **Coumarin × Vanillin (secret)** — The synergy that doesn't
   exist until you add Step 35. Alone, Coumarin smells like
   fresh-cut hay. Alone, Vanillin smells sweet. Together at these
   sub-threshold doses, they create *tonka bean absolute* — a
   $400/kg material — from $15 worth of synthetics.

### Carrier Synergy (Phase 7)

4. **IPM × Macrocyclic Musks** — IPM's ester structure creates a
   lipophilic micro-environment on skin that keeps macrocyclics
   (Ambrettolide, Exaltolide, Habanolide) from evaporating too
   fast. Your musks will last 2-3 hours longer than in pure alcohol.

5. **TEC × Coumarin/Vanillin** — TEC slows the release of small
   aromatic molecules. The Coumarin-Vanillin tonka synergy unfolds
   gradually over hours instead of hitting all at once. This turns a
   single-moment effect into a slow-developing revelation.
