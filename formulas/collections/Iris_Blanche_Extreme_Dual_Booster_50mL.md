# Iris Blanche Extreme — Dual Booster System For An Existing 50 mL Bottle

**Goal:** Push an existing iris-inspired fragrance toward:
- `Allure Homme Edition Blanche` style lemon-cream brightness
- `Allure Homme Sport Eau Extreme` style clean sporty polish and longer wear
- a clear `light wood` vs `heavy wood-ambrox` contrast
- preserved iris identity

This is a **two-part addition system**, not a full clone formula.

Because your inventory does not include some Chanel-signature materials, this is optimized for the **direction** rather than a literal duplication.

---

## Architecture

### Part A — Blanche Lift Booster

This is the bright side:
- cedrat / bergamot lemon brightness
- sporty clean freshness
- creamy blanche warmth in trace
- transparent light-wood support
- iris continuity so the top booster does not feel detached from your bottle

### Part B — Extreme Wood-Ambrox Booster

This is the dark side:
- dense amber-wood push
- stronger persistence
- heavier masculine drydown
- dry modern wood spine under the lighter top booster

The whole point is contrast:
- **Part A** gives the sparkling, creamy, polished opening and bridge
- **Part B** gives the heavy base pressure and longevity

### What Part A Is Doing

- `Cedrat + Bergamot` give the blanche lemon profile
- `Dihydromyrcenol` adds the sport-clean, high-polish freshness
- `Linalyl Acetate + Linalool` smooth the citrus so it feels expensive, not sharp
- `Cardamom FTEC` gives the familiar modern masculine sparkle
- `Hedione + Benzyl Salicylate` create the airy bridge into the body
- `Iso E Super + Bacdanol` are the **light woods**
- `Alpha Irone + I-IRIS F-TEC` keep the booster tied to the iris idea
- `Coumarin + Vanillin` supply a small blanche / tonka-cream echo without turning it dessert-like

- freshness: `5.51`

## How To Test The System

### Best First Step: 5 mL Decants

Do not modify the full bottle first.

#### Balanced Chanel-Iris Test
- `4.60 mL` original perfume
- `0.15 mL` Part A — Blanche Lift Booster
- `0.25 mL` Part B — Extreme Wood-Ambrox Booster

This is the **recommended starting point**.

#### Fresher Blanche-Leaning Test
- `4.60 mL` original perfume
- `0.22 mL` Part A
- `0.18 mL` Part B

Use this if you want more lemon / polish and less dark base takeover.

#### Darker Eau Extreme / Wood Test
- `4.60 mL` original perfume
- `0.10 mL` Part A
- `0.30 mL` Part B

Use this if your real priority is base weight and persistence.

## Full 50 mL Bottle Adjustment

### Recommended Starting Ratio

Remove `4.0 mL` from the bottle, then add:
- `1.5 mL` Part A — Blanche Lift Booster
- `2.5 mL` Part B — Extreme Wood-Ambrox Booster

This is the version I would call:
**Edition Blanche x Sport Eau Extreme x Iris, with a heavy modern wood base**

### Fresher Version

Remove `4.0 mL`, then add:
- `2.2 mL` Part A
- `1.8 mL` Part B

### Darker Version

Remove `4.0 mL`, then add:
- `1.0 mL` Part A
- `3.0 mL` Part B

## Expected Effect

### Opening

Sharper lemon-bergamot brightness, but rounded by smoother blanche cream and sporty clean lift.

### Heart

The iris should feel more connected to the fresh top because Part A contains a small iris bridge instead of just citrus.

### Drydown

The base becomes much denser, drier, and more expensive-smelling. You should get a real contrast between:
- airy polished upper structure
- dark wood-amber lower structure

That contrast is the point of this version.

## Tuning

### If The Top Still Feels Too Weak

Next time:
- raise `Part A` dose by `0.3-0.5 mL` in the 50 mL bottle
- or raise `Cedrat FCF oil Sicilian` in Part A from `1000 µL` to `1200 µL`
- or raise `Dihydromyrcenol` in Part A from `450 µL` to `550 µL`

### If The Base Is Too Dry / Harsh

Next time:
- reduce `Norlimbanol Dextro` in Part B from `80 µL` to `40 µL`
- reduce `Cedramber` from `400 µL` to `300 µL`
- increase `Hedione` in Part B from `250 µL` to `350 µL`

### If You Want Even More Iris

Next time:
- raise `I-IRIS F-TEC` in Part A from `150 µL` to `220 µL`
- or raise `Alpha Irone (10%)` from `120 µL` to `180 µL`
