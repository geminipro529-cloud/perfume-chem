## Root Causes Identified

### 1. Alpha Irone Cross-Adaptation (the "missing heart" explanation)

Alpha Irone is DOMINANT in the base. At sustained high concentration, it saturates OR11A1 — the primary ionone receptor. The brain adapts and stops perceiving the iris heart within 20–30 minutes. The existing ionone additions (Beta Ionone 150µL, DHBI 100µL, Orivone 30µL) compound this: they feed into the SAME receptor pool without creating perceptual separation.

**The solution is NOT more ionones.** It is non-ionone heart materials that create heart-body through DIFFERENT receptor pathways — specifically the vanilloid/benzaldehyde pathway (Heliotropin) and the hydroxycitronellal/lily pathway (Florol). These allow the brain to perceive "heart" even after ionone adaptation.

### 2. Zero Carles top band (1.8% vs 20% target)

The only top-band materials are Linalool (15µL) and C11 aldehyde (60µL at 1% = 0.6µL active). The perfume starts in the heart-base register. Without a volatile opening, there is no temporal arc — the nose has no point of entry, and the composition reads as "flat" despite having depth.

### 3. Floral dimension underweight (−3.2)

Character radar shows floral at 2.1 against a target of 5.3 for the amber style the engine detected. The heavy musk chord (730µL total across 6 musks) and salicylate architecture (820µL) are burying the floral character.

### 4. IPM skin-anchoring (0.75 mL = 2.5%)

Creates a dense lipophilic reservoir absorbing Hedione's radiance. At current 350µL Hedione (1.17%), the IPM layer solvates most of the radiance lift. Need to push Hedione past the solvation threshold (~1.5%).

### 5. Subliminal base materials

BB (0.02 perceptibility), Exaltolide (0.04), MK (0.43), Bacdanol (0.5) — contributing mass/fixation but not perceptible scent. These are irreversible; V3 focuses on adding PERCEPTIBLE materials to improve the ratio of audible to subliminal.

## V3 Strategy

**The formula doesn't need more materials in every register — it needs PERCEPTUAL ARCHITECTURE.** 38 materials exist but many are subliminal. The strategy:

1. **Break the ionone cross-adaptation** with non-ionone heart materials (Heliotropin, Florol)
2. **Create a temporal arc** with a volatile opening material (Neroli EO)
3. **Push radiance past IPM solvation** (Hedione increase)
4. **Add spatial projection** (Floralozone) to extend far-field
5. **Create structural contrast** (Timberol) so the formula reads as architecture, not wall
6. **Boost skin performance** (Ebanol increase) per engine recommendation
7. **Extend perceived duration** (Beta Ionone louder heart + EB/Romandolide above perceptibility)
8. **Add balsamic-resinous depth** (Ambermax + Olibanum) for the "it smells expensive" quality
9. **Strengthen orris luxury signal** (Orivone increase) for perceived iris quality
10. **Activate saffron spice axis** (Ethyl Safranate increase) for metallic-warm luxury coding
11. **ODT subliminal design polish** (C12 MNA, Eugenol, Ethyl Maltol, cis-3-Hexenol, Rose Oxide, Carrot Seed) — finishing layer dosed between detection and recognition threshold for perceived depth, naturalness, luxury, and craftsmanship
