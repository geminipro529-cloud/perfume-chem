# THAI TRUE MASS-MARKET EIGHTEEN — 6 Families × 3 Sub-Families, 9 W/U + 9 M

**Date:** 2026-05-02
**Format:** 18 × 30 mL — true Thai mass-market tier (Mistine, Karmart, Twelve Plus, BSC, Watsons, Eveandboy private label; 99–890 THB retail). Body-mist / EDT / EDP-light formats. Gender split: 9 women / unisex + 9 masculine.
**Differs from v1 (Mass_Market_Thailand_Eighteen):** v1 was Western global mass-market with Thai botanical localization (champaca naturals, Mysore-stack sandalwood, Coromandel patchouli — these are SE Asian niche/prestige). This v2 reflects what actually moves volume at Thai dispensary, drug-store and dept-store mid-mass tier:
- **Body-mist format** (5–7 % EDT, ~30 mL spray) for the women's teen tier
- **FO/FTEC-heavy** builds (Oud Fleuressence, Sandalwood FO, Jasmine FO, Tonka Bean FO are mass-tier production tools)
- **Simple sandalwood** (1 FO + benzoin + vanilla, not 5-material Mysore reconstruction)
- **Oud as its own tier** (Karmart Oud / BSC Oud / Vasu Oud Lite — explicit Thai mass mid-tier category)
- **Lower concentrations across the board** (Thai mass averages EDT 6–10 %; v1 was running 11–17 %)
