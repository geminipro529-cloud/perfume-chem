## Why This Version Is Better

### 1. The iris stays centered

The crystal accord still carries the Opus V identity, but the shell is now lighter and more wearable.

### 2. Jasmine is implied, not declared

The floral lock is built from:
- `Hedione`
- `Phenethyl Alcohol`
- `Nympheal`
- `Lilyreal ND`
- `Freesia HDI`

That gives a polished white-floral impression without using `Jasmine FO`.

The wood/musk structure is intentionally premium:
- `Iso E Super`
- `Clearwood`
- `Cedramber`
- `Vertofix`
- `Habanolide`
- `Ethylene Brassylate`
- `Romandolide`

That reads more like luxury fabric and mineral polish than a heavy amber base.

## Mixing Order

1. Pre-blend the base first:
   `Iso E Super`, `Clearwood`, `Cedramber`, `Vertofix`, `Habanolide`, `Ethylene Brassylate`, `Romandolide`, `Galaxolide`, `Ambrofix`, `Bacdanol`, `Hexyl Salicylate`.
2. Add the floral lock:
   `Hedione`, `Phenethyl Alcohol`, `Nympheal`, `Lilyreal ND`, `Freesia HDI`.
3. Add the iris center:
   `Opus V Iris Crystal`.
4. Add the top lift:
   `Bergamot`, `Cedrat`, `Linalool`, `Aldehyde C12 MNA`, `Rose Oxide`.
5. Rest `7` to `14` days before judging.

---

## Practical Notes

- Do not add `Jasmine FO`; it is intentionally replaced by the structural floral lock.
- If the perfume feels too dry, raise `Hedione` by `20 uL` and lower `Iso E Super` by `20 uL`.
- If it feels too musky or too soft, reduce `Ethylene Brassylate` by `20 uL` and raise `Cedramber` by `20 uL`.
- If you want more sparkle, raise `Bergamot` by `20 uL` and lower `Clearwood` by `20 uL`.
