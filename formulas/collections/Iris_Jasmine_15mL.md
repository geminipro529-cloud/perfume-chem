# IRIS–JASMINE 15 mL

**Batch:** 15.00 mL EDP @ ~22% concentrate in ethanol 96%.
**Concept:** Iris–jasmine dialogue — orris powder-suede meeting indolic white-floral radiance. Classical lineage: Chanel No. 19 (iris + galbanum + jasmine), Serge Lutens A La Nuit (heavy jasmine), Amouage Epic Woman (iris + jasmine + woody-amber).
**This batch:** built as the other 15 mL half of the same 30 mL hand-build, using iris heart inventory already in-stock + the full jasmine chord.
**Optimized axes:** depth, sillage, luxury, texture, longevity, photorealism.
**OAV-guard audited:** all 36 materials pass `check_proportional_scaling()` — no pipette-floor violations, no ODT crossings.
**Constraint:** no FTECs, aroma chemicals + naturals only.

---

## 1. Composition architecture

Three architectural layers, each with a **defined iris↔jasmine dialogue**:

| Layer | Iris voice | Jasmine voice | Bridge |
|---|---|---|---|
| **TOP** | Leafovert + crushed-leaf green | Methyl Benzoate (fruity-jasmone lift) | Bergamot Sicilian |
| **HEART** | α-Irone 30% + Irotyl + Orivone + 3-ionone chord | Hedione (heavy) + Hedione HC + Indole 10% + Benzyl Acetate + ACA + Jasmine FO + Ylang Extra | Cis Jasmone (both chords share this molecule) |
| **BASE** | Ebanol creamy sandalwood + Koavone iris-extender | Benzyl Salicylate (cosmetic white-floral cushion) | Hexyl Salicylate (transparent layer) |

**Musk triad (all 3 axes covered):**
- **Depth:** Ethylene Brassylate (lactonic-creamy, echoes orris butter + jasmine indoles)
- **Projection:** Romandolide (pushes jasmine outward without laundry-synthetic Galaxolide)
- **Character-echo:** Musk Ketone 10% (powdery — reinforces iris-powder register)

## 3. Multi-axis optimization audit

### DEPTH
- **Iris side:** α-Irone + 3-ionone chord + Orivone + Irotyl + Carrot Seed + Myristic lipid
- **Jasmine side:** Indole 10% (animalic) + Ethylene Brassylate (lactonic echo of indolic cream) + Ambrettolide (fruity-musky warmth)
- **Bridge:** Azarbre (cedar-amber-warm) + Ebanol (creamy sandalwood)
- **Rejected:** Javanol/Ambrox crystalline would clash with warm-floral heart; Vetival/Norlimbanol would add dry-suede that breaks creamy register.

### SILLAGE
- **2-material projection chord:** Hedione (620 µL — 22% of concentrate, Roudnitska jasmine radiance ceiling) + Romandolide (95 µL — outward projection musk)
- ACA (15 µL, IFRA-capped) as diffusion-lift for white floral body
- Iso E Super (130 µL) as molecular carrier
- Ambrox Super 30% (40 µL trace) for crystalline lift without breaking warmth
- **Rejected Galaxolide 80% as projection:** would make this read like fabric conditioner under Hedione overdose

### LUXURY
- α-Irone 30% DEP (premium grade, not α-ionone substitute)
- Hedione + Hedione HC pairing (full cis spectrum, Firmenich premium)
- Irotyl (rare Symrise material)
- Jasmine FO + Ylang Comoros Complete (naturals, not synthetic jasmine accord alone)
- Ambrettolide 10% (naturalistic macrocyclic)
- Bergamot Sicilian (character-grade citrus)

### TEXTURE
- **Fatty-lipid:** Myristic 20% DEP (100 mg) + IPM 250 µL + Ethylene Brassylate (lactonic creamy mass)
- **Cosmetic cushion:** Benzyl Sal + Hexyl Sal gradient (heavy→light) + Benzyl Benzoate (invisible weight)
- **Powder:** Musk Ketone + Ultralia + Heliotropal-free (heliotropal deliberately absent to keep jasmine-iris distinct from almondy-powder accords)
- **Suede:** Irotyl
- **Buttery:** Orivone + Exaltolide

### LONGEVITY
- **Musk triad across all 3 axes:** EB (depth) + Romandolide (projection) + Musk Ketone (character-echo) + Exaltolide (intimacy) + Ambrettolide (naturalistic-warmth)
- **Iris receptor extender:** Koavone — maintains iris perception into 5–8 hr mark
- **Lipid matrix:** IPM + Myristic Acid extend skin-release curve +30–60 min
- **2-fixative gradient:** Benzyl Sal (heavy) + Hexyl Sal (light) + Benzyl Benzoate (invisible) — 3-tier anchor system
- **Predicted on-skin longevity: 12–16 hours**

### PHOTOREALISM
- **Iris photorealism:** α-Irone + 3-ionone + Orivone + Irotyl + Carrot Seed + Myristic → full Kraft orris olfactophore coverage
- **Jasmine photorealism:** Benzyl Acetate (25% of real absolute) + Methyl Benzoate + Indole + Cis Jasmone + ACA + Jasmine FO + Ylang Comoros → all 6 major jasmine-absolute GC peaks represented
- **Bridge molecules:** Cis Jasmone + Violet Fleuressence + Leafovert bridge both chords naturally

## 5. Preparation protocol

### Pre-dilutions required (if not already on hand)

- **Myristic Acid 20% in DEP** — same as Photorealistic Iris half; share the 5 mL stock.
- **Musk Ketone 10% in DPG** — same as Photorealistic Iris half.
- **Indole 10% in DPG** — 200 mg Indole crystal + 1.80 mL DPG, warm 45°C until dissolved.
- **Paradisamide 10% in DPG** — as per existing stock.

### Compounding sequence

1. **Lipid matrix:** pipette Myristic 20% DEP (500 µL) + IPM (250 µL) into empty 15 mL bottle.
2. **Iris heart:** α-Irone 30% (280), α-Ionone, β-Ionone, Isomethyl Ionone, Irotyl, Orivone, Ultralia, Carrot Seed, Violet Fleuressence, Farnesol (per table).
3. **Jasmine heart:** Hedione (620), Hedione HC (95), Benzyl Acetate (80), Methyl Benzoate (25), Cis Jasmone (15), ACA (15), Jasmine FO (35), Ylang (35), Paradisamide 10% (5), Indole 10% (8).
4. **Top lift:** Bergamot Sicilian, Methyl Pamplemousse 10%, Leafovert, AAG, Ethyl Linalool.
5. **Base + cosmetic cushion:** Benzyl Sal, Hexyl Sal, Benzyl Benzoate, Ebanol, Koavone, Azarbre, Iso E Super.
6. **Musk chord + amber:** EB, Romandolide, Musk Ketone 10%, Exaltolide 10%, Ambrettolide 10%, Ambrox 30%.
7. **Ethanol to 15 mL.** Cap, invert 50×.

### Maceration

- Minimum **6 weeks** in cool dark storage (longer than iris-only build due to indolic jasmine aging — indole needs time to integrate)
- Weekly gentle inversion
- Evaluate at 2, 4, 6, 8 weeks — indole character softens from sharp to creamy-floral between 2 and 6 weeks
