# Le Barbier de Grasse — 30 mL EDP Mixing Guide

*The classical aromatic fougère — boulevard, leather glove, hot shave-shop towel.*

**Source:** `_fougere_5_named_v2.py` — direction-optimised, DE/rand/1/bin 50 generations.
**Score delta vs initial:** +7 789 774

> **Why mix this alongside Vol d'Ambre v4?**
> This is the *opposite end* of the fougère family. Where v4 is amber-vanilla-oriental,
> Le Barbier is coumarin-oakmoss-dry. Smelling both back-to-back defines what "fougère" means.

Add to a clean 10 mL vial in this order. Swirl between each addition for heavy materials.

| Step | Material | Stock | Measure | Notes |

## Finish

1. Seal vial; swirl 30 s; **macerate 48 h minimum** before evaluating.
2. Transfer concentrate to the 30 mL bottle.
3. Fill to **30 mL** with ethanol 96% (**~23.2 mL**).
4. Macerate finished EDP **2–4 weeks** — Evernyl and Coumarin need time to integrate.

Evernyl is the key ingredient to smell. It smells like wet stone, moss, damp forest floor — nothing else in your inventory does this. It is what gives the classical fougère its distinctive bitter-green shadow that Layton does not have.

## Safety Notes

- **Evernyl:** oakmoss-type allergen. IFRA Cat 4 limit 0.02 % in finished product. At 2.31 % of concentrate in 20 % EDP → 0.46 % in EDP — **exceeds IFRA limit for leave-on skin use.** For evaluation/testing purposes this is acceptable; do not wear daily. Evernyl's limit is 0.1 % neat in EDP (= 0.5 % in concentrate at 20 % EDP) — just on the edge. If reducing, cut to 70 µL (1 %) in concentrate.
- **Eugenol:** IFRA Cat 4 limit ~1.0 % in finished product. At 1.48 % of concentrate × 20 % = 0.30 % in EDP. Safe.
- **Bergamot FCF:** FCF = furanocoumarin-free, no phototoxicity risk.
- **Coumarin:** IFRA Cat 4 limit 3.5 % of concentrate. At 5.32 % in concentrate — **slightly over IFRA limit.** Reduce to 210 µL (= 3.5 % of 6000 µL) if IFRA compliance is required. For evaluation/testing batches the current level is acceptable.
