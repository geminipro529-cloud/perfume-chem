# Iris Rêverie Lactée

**Batch:** 10.00 mL EDP · **Concentrate:** 23.34 % (2 334 µL) · **Ethanol 96 %:** 7.67 mL
**Character:** Soft iris powder → hydroxycitronellal muguet white floral → coumarin-vanillin-heliotropin-lactone cream. A dreamy single-direction synergy: every register is warm, soft, sweet-powder-creamy; no angular breaks. AIMI kept low (user spec). Sub-ODT traces (Geosmin, Indole, Damascol, Aurantiol, Isoeugenol) add hedonic depth and textural complexity beneath the audible structure.

**Single direction:** powdery-muguet-creamy-vanillic-heliotropinic. No green/galbanum bomb. No leather/smoke. No bright citrus aldehyde. No oakmoss. The whole composition resolves as ONE perfume, not top+heart+base stacked.

## Accord Architecture — Why Each Material

### TOP — deliberately minimal (85 µL total, 3.6 % of concentrate)
A dreamy opening requires restraint. A bright aldehydic/hesperidic top would break the powder-cream direction before the iris speaks.

- **Bergamot FCF Sicilian (50 µL)** — chosen over 8 citrus alternatives. Rejected: Grapefruit FCF (bitter pink-pith dryness would clash with cream), Cedrat (too sharp-mineral), D-Limonene (fleeting terpenic burst, no diffusion), Red Mandarin (warm-tangerine pulls gourmand-oriental, not dreamy), Terpinyl Acetate (pine-herbal breaks direction), Methyl Pamplemousse (synthetic-tart, wrong register), plain Bergamot FCF (too light-cologne). Sicilian FCF gives RICHER bergamot character at LOWER dose — a whispered fan, not a citrus announcement.
- **Ethyl Linalool (30 µL)** — softer, more woody-floral and more tenacious than Linalool; bridges citrus fan into iris-woody heart without leaving a bare gap.
- **Scentenal 1 % (5 µL → 0.05 µL active)** — sub-ODT ozonic-metallic transparency trace. Adds "dreamy" air under the powder without contributing an audible ozone note.

### IRIS POWDER CORE — 448 µL, 19.2 % of concentrate
The hero register. All 9 iris/violet materials deployed across different texture axes.

- **Alpha Irone 30 % (180 µL → 54 µL active)** — THE orris hero. Most expensive iris material in inventory; chosen because only Alpha Irone delivers the authentic Mysore-orris deep-powder-butter character. Ionones alone read as "violet-candy"; irone reads as "iris absolute".
- **Alpha Ionone (25 µL)** — round, warm, rosy-violet — softens the precision of Alpha Irone.
- **Beta Ionone (8 µL)** — powdery-woody orris echo at trace, widens the iris spectrum without adding a violet candy sweetness.
- **Irotyl (25 µL)** — sweet-irone carrier, bridges iris → cream register beneath the audible powder.
- **Orivone (45 µL)** — buttery-iris structural base. **Critical bridge material:** Orivone is the single molecule that seamlessly connects iris powder to the creamy-lactonic drydown. Without it the iris would sit above the cream instead of melting into it.
- **Ultralia (25 µL)** — ghost iris at trace concentration, powdery transparency carrier; widens the halo of the iris.
- **Carrot Seed EO (20 µL)** — natural carrot-iris bridge; adds a faint earthiness that grounds the synthetic iris chord without breaking dreaminess.
- **Alpha Isomethyl Ionone (40 µL)** — kept LOW per user spec. Adds a light violet-iris powder volume and an AIMI "cosmetic-ghost" signature but never becomes the centerpiece.
- **Musk Ketone 10 % (80 µL → 8 µL active)** — THE powder-axis musk. Chosen specifically because Musk Ketone is the only musk in inventory that directly reinforces a powdery register. Rejected as primary musks here: Habanolide (no powder contribution), Galaxolide (synthetic-laundry clash), Romandolide (projects outward — wrong for intimate iris powder). Musk Ketone is the *character-echo* musk in this formula.

### WHITE FLORAL HEART — 532 µL, 22.8 % of concentrate
Hydroxycitronellal register (muguet / white lily / gardenia), kept CLEAN and transparent — this is the "dreamy" white-flower cloud that floats between iris and cream.

- **Hydroxycitronellal (70 µL)** — THE muguet-white-lily signature molecule, the hydroxycitronellal register the user explicitly requested.
- **Mayol (35 µL)** — post-Lyral muguet; cleaner, fresher muguet character than Hydroxycitronellal alone. Together Mayol + Hydroxycitronellal + Bourgeonal build a full muguet chord.
- **Lilyreal ND (25 µL)** — clean synthetic muguet, cosmetic character — layered above for transparency.
- **Bourgeonal (10 µL)** — aldehydic muguet, completes the muguet chord at low dose (powerful material).
- **DBCA (30 µL)** — gardenia-rose cosmetic-clean white floral cushion; chosen over raw Jasmine FO (pre-blend, opaque, not precise) and Methyl Benzoate (wrong ester direction).
- **Freesia HDI (20 µL)** — green-floral transparency; bridges iris's faint earthiness (Carrot Seed) into the white floral register without adding leafy green.
- **PEDMC (20 µL)** — rose-magnolia soft-clean; chosen over PEA (too raw-rosy) for a dreamier magnolia facet.
- **Hedione (280 µL)** — radiance amplifier and volume expander; the Diorella-engine that gives the white floral heart its diffusion and keeps the whole composition luminous. Without Hedione the iris + muguet + cream would sit heavy-flat on skin.
- **Hedione HC (40 µL)** — high-cis Hedione for the jasmine-core facet; complements plain Hedione with a deeper rounded jasmine register.
- **Farnesol (2 µL)** — trace lily-muguet fixative; IFRA-restricted, used at the lowest effective dose. Adds a subtle muguet-fixation that extends Hydroxycitronellal's volatile lifespan.

### CREAM / HELIOTROPIN / COUMARIN / VANILLIN / LACTONE — 505 µL, 21.6 %
The luxury drydown register — explicitly per user spec. All materials chosen to integrate into ONE creamy-vanillic-heliotropinic-powdery mass.

- **Heliotropal / Piperonal (50 µL)** — THE heliotropin-powder-cream anchor. Sweet-almond-heliotrope-powdery. Chosen over Heliotropin Fleuressence (pre-blend, opaque). Heliotropal bridges iris powder (Musk Ketone + Alpha Irone) into vanillic-cream.
- **Coumarin 20 % (200 µL → 40 µL active)** — creamy-hay-tonka register. Neat powder Coumarin used via 20 % dilution for dosing precision.
- **Vanillin 10 % (120 µL → 12 µL active)** — vanilla cream body.
- **Ethyl Vanillin neat (5 µL)** — trace amplifier (3× stronger than Vanillin); the whisper of Ethyl Vanillin deepens the vanilla register without pulling the formula into an overt gourmand.
- **Ethyl Maltol 10 % (40 µL → 4 µL active)** — kept LOW; just enough caramel-candy lift to add hedonic sweetness under the cream without making it "edible". This is explicitly a luxury creamy perfume, not a gourmand.
- **Anisaldehyde (8 µL)** — sweet-powdery heliotrope echo; reinforces the heliotropin-powder axis at low dose.
- **Gamma Undecalactone (12 µL)** — peach-dairy lactone; contributes the creamy-lactonic character the user specified.
- **Delta Decalactone (10 µL)** — milk-coconut lactone; paired with Undecalactone for a full lactonic register (dairy + peach skin).
- **Benzoin 50 % DPG (60 µL → 30 µL active)** — balsamic-vanillic depth; deepens Vanillin's drydown and integrates it into the coumarinic hay register. Chosen over Siam Benzoin (50% too) for the cleaner balsamic profile.

### MUSK / FIXATIVE / STRUCTURAL COCOON — 735 µL, 31.5 %
Multi-axis musk chord (depth + projection + character-echo) plus 2-layer salicylate diffusion.

- **Benzyl Salicylate (140 µL)** — heavy cosmetic diffusion cushion; THE fixative bridge between white floral and cream. Chosen for its deep-waxy-cosmetic-warm character matching the luxury brief.
- **Hexyl Salicylate (60 µL)** — lighter green-floral salicylate layered ABOVE Benzyl Sal; creates a gradient salicylate fixation (light over heavy) that maintains transparency.
- **Ethylene Brassylate (150 µL)** — **PRIMARY musk, depth axis.** Chosen because EB is the ONLY musk in this inventory whose character (creamy, lactonic, slightly thick) directly REINFORCES the cream register. Habanolide was evaluated and rejected as primary — its macrocyclic skin-warmth does not echo the cream. Galaxolide rejected — synthetic-laundry clashes with luxury niche brief. Zenolide rejected — citrus-fresh wrong register. EB + the lactones (Undecalactone + Decalactone) + Exaltolide form a single fused lactonic-creamy mass.
- **Exaltolide 10 % (80 µL → 8 µL active)** — depth axis, skin-fatty intimate; adds natural skin-oil quality that lets the formula read as "her skin, but more luxurious".
- **Ambrettolide 10 % DPG (60 µL → 6 µL active)** — **character-echo axis**, most naturalistic macrocyclic, fruity-musky-wine-like; adds quiet skin warmth and a naturalistic edge that counterweights the synthetic precision of EB.
- **Habanolide (70 µL)** — RESTRAINED. Chosen as secondary intimate-skin musk; not the hero (cream needs lactonic EB, not plain macrocyclic). Adds intimate-trail under EB's projection.
- **Ebanol (55 µL)** — creamy-sandalwood. Chosen over Javanol (too cold-dry-mineral for cream direction), Bacdanol (too heavy-traditional for dreamy register), Sandalore (too fresh-light, doesn't echo cream). Ebanol's milky-creamy character matches and extends the cream register. **Sandalwood register chosen: creamy-soft, not dry-intimate or round-heavy.**
- **Iso E Super (90 µL)** — molecular skin-cocoon for skin adhesion and the "what are you wearing" halo effect. Kept moderate (not the 300–500 µL overdose) because the formula has audible woody-creamy character (Ebanol + Benzoin + EB); Iso E's abstract-cedar is a SUPPORT not a structural pillar here.
- **Ambrox Super 30 % (30 µL → 9 µL active)** — TRACE only. Crystalline-mineral amber register chosen at minimal dose for subliminal depth. Ambermax was evaluated and rejected — its warm-rounded amber would dominate and fight the cream (adds a *second* warmth that clashes with Benzoin + coumarin + vanillin). Ambrox at trace adds depth without adding character.

### SUB-ODT COMPLEXITY TRACES — 29 µL, 1.2 %
These sit at or below odor detection threshold. They are not audible as discrete notes — they enrich the hedonic, textural, and luminous character of the audible structure. Per user spec: "positive direction of scent".

- **Aurantiol (5 µL)** — orange-blossom-powder Schiff base trace; reinforces the white-floral-powder bridge between muguet heart and cream base.
- **Damascol 10 % (15 µL → 1.5 µL active)** — rose-plum damascone; softer and more rounded than Alpha/Beta Damascone. Chosen specifically because the formula already has enough fruit character from Ambrettolide + Undecalactone — Damascol's *rounded plum* reinforces hedonic softness without adding a fruity character note.
- **Geosmin 1 % TEC (3 µL → 0.03 µL active)** — petrichor / rain-on-earth trace at the absolute lower limit (max recommended 5 µL of 1 % per 100 mL; here 0.3 µL-equivalent per 10 mL). ODT ≈ 6 ppt — sub-threshold. Adds the "dreamy" mineral-earth undertone the user asked for; overdose would destroy the composition (beet-soil), hence the extreme restraint.
- **Indole 10 % (3 µL → 0.3 µL active)** — trace indolic depth under the white florals; provides the "living" quality of white flowers without reading as urinous or animalic. Sub-ODT.
- **Isoeugenol (3 µL)** — trace carnation-halo; bridges coumarin + vanillin + heliotropin into a spicy-balsamic unity. Sub-ODT; just enough to widen the drydown's hedonic register.

## Why This Reads as ONE Perfume (Single-Direction Synergy)

Every register pulls toward the same direction: **powder → cream → vanillic-coumarinic luxury**. The bridges are deliberate, overlapping, and redundant:

1. **Iris → White Floral bridge:** Orivone (buttery iris) + Hedione + DBCA + Carrot Seed create a gradient; no gap.
2. **White Floral → Cream bridge:** Hydroxycitronellal + Heliotropal + Farnesol + Anisaldehyde; the muguet-lily register contains intrinsic powder (Hydroxycitronellal) that prepares the nose for Heliotropal's almond-heliotrope-powder.
3. **Cream → Powder closure:** Musk Ketone (powder axis musk) + Heliotropal + Coumarin + Vanillin + Ethylene Brassylate form a fused mass; they share character so the composition reads as ONE creamy-powdery cloud, not as "heart + base".
4. **Structural cocoon:** Benzyl Sal + Hexyl Sal + Ethylene Brassylate + Iso E Super + Ebanol create a continuous skin film that holds all three registers in mutual diffusion.
5. **Sub-ODT resonance:** Aurantiol + Damascol + Geosmin + Indole + Isoeugenol add subliminal hedonic depth at 5 different micro-axes (orange-flower powder, rose-plum, earth, indolic, carnation) that the nose perceives as "richness" without identifying them.

**Expected performance:** long-lasting (12–16 hr projection, 24+ hr skin trail driven by EB + Exaltolide + Ambrettolide + Benzoin + Benzyl Sal macrocyclic/balsamic fixation); strong projective sillage first 4–6 hr (Hedione + Hexyl Sal diffusion + Romandolide-class projection from EB); textured drydown (cream + lactone + powder + balsamic layering).

## Mixing Order (recommended)

1. Weigh solid Coumarin into Coumarin 20 % dilution bottle first (prepare separately if not already done).
2. Add neat powerhouses first (Hedione, Benzyl Salicylate, Ethylene Brassylate, Iso E Super, Habanolide) into a clean 10 mL amber bottle.
3. Add dilutions next (Alpha Irone 30 %, Musk Ketone 10 %, Coumarin 20 %, Vanillin 10 %, Exaltolide 10 %, Ambrettolide 10 %, Ambrox Super 30 %, Ethyl Maltol 10 %, Scentenal 1 %, Damascol 10 %, Indole 10 %, Geosmin 1 %).
4. Add remaining neat materials (ionones, floral materials, heliotropal, lactones, musks, citrus).
5. Add traces last (Farnesol, Aurantiol, Ethyl Vanillin, Isoeugenol) using a clean micropipette.
6. Add 7.67 mL ethanol 96 %. Cap, shake 30 s, rest.
7. **Maceration: 4–6 weeks minimum.** Coumarin + Vanillin + Heliotropal + Benzoin + Musk Ketone all require extended maceration for full integration — this is not a formula to evaluate at 48 hr.

## Rejected Alternatives — Design Record

| Reflex pick | Rejected because | Used instead |
|---|---|---|
| Javanol (sandalwood) | Cold-dry-mineral clashes with cream direction | Ebanol (milky-creamy matches) |
| Habanolide (musk default) | No lactonic echo to cream; no powder echo to iris | EB primary (lactonic depth) + Musk Ketone (powder echo) |
| Ambermax / Ambrofix | Warm-rounded amber fights Benzoin + vanillin (double warmth) | Ambrox Super trace (crystalline depth only) |
| Galaxolide | Laundry-synthetic breaks luxury niche character | Ethylene Brassylate (lactonic creamy) |
| Bergamot FCF plain | Too light-cologne, no dreamy weight | Bergamot FCF Sicilian (richer at lower dose) |
| Jasmine FO | Pre-blend opaque — can't tune | Hedione + Hedione HC + Indole 10 % trace + DBCA (controllable jasmine facet) |
| Heliotropin Fleuressence | Pre-blend opaque | Heliotropal (precise Piperonal) + Anisaldehyde echo |
| High-dose AIMI | User spec: low AIMI only | AIMI 40 µL (modest), Alpha Irone + Alpha Ionone carry the iris |
| Oakmoss / Evernyl | Breaks single-direction synergy into chypre | Omitted entirely |
| Patchouli EO | Adds earthy-camphor facet, wrong direction | Omitted |
| Leather / Styrax / IBQ | Smoky-animalic breaks dreamy powder | Omitted |
