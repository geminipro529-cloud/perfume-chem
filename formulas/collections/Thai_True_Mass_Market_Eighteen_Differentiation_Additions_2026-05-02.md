**Date:** 2026-05-02
**Companion to:** [Thai_True_Mass_Market_Eighteen_2026-05-02.md](Thai_True_Mass_Market_Eighteen_2026-05-02.md)
**Purpose:** stock-aware, small-dose overlays for the 18 Thai true mass-market formulas. Each overlay was screened twice: first against the actual input tables with `FormulaScorer`, then by perfumer logic so the move still fits the formula's category.

**Mass-market constraint:** these are not prestige rebuilds. Keep the profile direct, familiar, hot-weather wearable, and retail-plausible. For a retail-faithful trial, replace equal volume from broad filler mass first: Galaxolide, Iso E Super, Ethylene Brassylate, Dihydromyrcenol, or FO volume. For a "beat the benchmark" trial, add directly and accept a small concentration bump.

**Input checked:** current [inventory.txt](../../inventory.txt). Depleted Exaltolide is not used. Geosmin and Costus were removed from the final engine-checked overlay because their trace romance was not worth the scoring or handling risk here.
