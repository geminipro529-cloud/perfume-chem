# Iris Blanche Extreme — Wood-Ambrox Addition For An Existing 50 mL Bottle

> If you want the newer **split architecture** with a Chanel-leaning bright booster plus a separate heavy base booster, use [Iris_Blanche_Extreme_Dual_Booster_50mL.md](/d:/chatbots/perfume-chem/Iris_Blanche_Extreme_Dual_Booster_50mL.md).

**Use Case:** You already have a finished iris-inspired sporty blanche-style perfume and want:
- longer wear
- a much heavier wood-ambrox backbone
- more drydown support without fully rebuilding the formula

This is an **addition concentrate**, not a full perfume formula.

---

## What This Addition Does

It pushes the fragrance in a more structural direction:
- stronger amber-wood drydown
- more persistence on skin and fabric
- cleaner, denser modern masculine backbone
- slightly less emphasis on airy freshness

If your original already smells balanced and you only want more support, this is the correct move.

---

## Why This Works

- `Iso E Super` gives the large dry woody frame
- `Amberwood F` is the main ambrox-style backbone here
- `Ambermax (50%)` adds warm amber body behind the cleaner amberwood line
- `Cedramber` and `Norlimbanol Dextro` create the long, dry, expensive wood spine
- `Habanolide` keeps the base clean and persistent rather than muddy
- `Benzyl Salicylate` and `Hedione` stop the addition from choking the iris heart
- `Bacdanol`, `Javanol`, `Cashmeran`, and `Suederal` make the base feel plush rather than skeletal
- trace `Coumarin` and `Vanillin` give a small Allure-style warmth without turning it gourmand

---

## Recommended Dosing

### Best Method: Decant Trial First

Test the booster in a small sample before touching the full bottle.

#### 5% Addition Test
- `4.75 mL` original perfume
- `0.25 mL` booster

#### 10% Addition Test
- `4.50 mL` original perfume
- `0.50 mL` booster

For your stated goal, **10% is the more likely winner**.

---

## Full 50 mL Bottle Adjustment

If you want to modify the full bottle directly:

### Moderate Version
- remove `3.0 mL` from the bottle
- add `3.0 mL` booster

### Heavy Wood-Ambrox Version
- remove `4.0 mL` from the bottle
- add `4.0 mL` booster

For the effect you described, I would start with **4.0 mL swap-in**.

Do not add the booster on top of a full bottle without removing perfume first unless you want the total volume to increase and the balance to get less predictable.

---

## Mixing Order For The Booster

1. Add `Iso E Super`
2. Add `Amberwood F`
3. Add `Ambermax (50%)`
4. Add `Cedramber`
5. Add `Norlimbanol Dextro`
6. Add `Bacdanol`
7. Add `Javanol`
8. Add `Cashmeran (20%)`
9. Add `Suederal (10%)`
10. Add `Benzyl Salicylate`
11. Add `Habanolide`
12. Add `Hedione`
13. Add `Coumarin (20%)`
14. Add `Vanillin (10%)`
15. Stir for `45-60 seconds`

Let the booster sit for `24 hours` before adding it to the perfume if possible.

---

## Maturation After Addition

- `Day 0-2`: sharper, woods more obvious than integrated
- `Day 5-7`: drydown starts to read as one structure
- `Day 10-14`: best first serious evaluation window
- `Day 21`: full result usually clearer

---

## Expected Effect On The Perfume

### What Increases
- longevity
- woody amber authority
- drydown thickness
- skin and fabric persistence

### What Decreases Slightly
- airy brightness
- transparency
- some of the sporty freshness in the top

The iris should still be there, but now sitting on a much more assertive modern wood-amber floor.

---

## If It Gets Too Dry

For the next booster batch:
- reduce `Norlimbanol Dextro` from `70 µL` to `40 µL`
- reduce `Cedramber` from `350 µL` to `250 µL`
- increase `Hedione` from `250 µL` to `350 µL`

## If It Is Still Too Soft

For the next booster batch:
- increase `Amberwood F` from `1000 µL` to `1200 µL`
- increase `Iso E Super` from `1250 µL` to `1400 µL`
- increase `Habanolide` from `400 µL` to `500 µL`
