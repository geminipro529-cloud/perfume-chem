## 1. Conceptual architecture

Real orris butter is **not a floral — it is a fatty lipid matrix carrying ionones/irones.** A photorealistic reconstruction must supply:

| Layer | Real orris butter component | % natural | This formula |
|---|---|---:|---|
| **Fatty acid matrix** | Myristic acid (solid fatty acid) | 40–50% of butter | Myristic Acid Powder |
| **Fatty ester matrix** | Methyl myristate / myristate esters | 30–40% of butter | **Isopropyl Myristate (IPM)** — new v6 |
| **Iris identity** | α-irone + γ-irone + ε-irone (all isomers) | 10–15% | α-Irone 30% DEP |
| **Violet-woody body** | α-ionone + β-ionone + allyl ionone | 2–5% | 4 ionone chord |
| **Jasmonate radiance** | Methyl jasmonate + cis-jasmone trace | <1% | Hedione + Hedione HC + Cis Jasmone |
| **Rhizome earth** | Rooty terpenes + **geosmin traces** + pyrazines | <0.5% | Carrot Seed EO + Cyclamen Ald + **Geosmin 1%** — new v6 |
| **Aldehydic cold head** | Metallic-green aldehyde trace | <0.5% | Cyclamen Ald + Scentenal 1% |
| **Powder haze** | (emergent from fatty-ionone diffusion) | structural | Heliotropal + Ultralia + Musk Ketone |
| **Skin-fat extension** | (natural extension on warm skin) | structural | Ambrettolide + Exaltolide + Ethylene Brassylate |

**This is the first formulation in this session where every real orris butter facet has a molecular counterpart in the bottle.**

## 4. Preparation protocol

### 4.1 Pre-dilutions required

**Myristic Acid in DEP 20% w/v** (one-time prep, makes 5 mL stock):
1. Weigh 1.00 g Myristic Acid Powder into a 10 mL glass vial
2. Add 4.00 mL DEP
3. Heat vial in warm water bath (45–50°C) with gentle swirling until fully dissolved (~5 min)
4. Solution stays liquid at room temp; re-warm briefly if any cloudiness appears before dosing
5. Label: "Myristic Acid 20% in DEP — prep [date]"

**Musk Ketone 10% in DPG** (one-time prep, makes 10 mL stock):
1. Weigh 1.00 g Musk Ketone powder into 20 mL glass vial
2. Add 9.00 g DPG (9.0 mL at density ~1.02)
3. Magnetic stir 30 min OR warm to 45°C with swirling until clear
4. Label: "Musk Ketone 10% in DPG — prep [date]"

### 4.2 Compounding order (critical for photorealism)

Use a **20 mL glass graduated vial** for concentrate, then transfer to 30 mL bottle with ethanol.

**Step 1 — Lipid matrix (the foundation of photorealism):**
- Myristic Acid 20% DEP: **1500 µL**
- **Isopropyl Myristate (IPM): 500 µL** (fatty-ester matrix completion)
- Orivone: 120 µL
- **Koavone: 200 µL** (iris-receptor extender)
- **Azarbre: 100 µL** (cedar-amber-suede bridge)
- Ebanol: 230 µL
- Iso E Super: 280 µL
- Habanolide: 550 µL
- Ethylene Brassylate: 380 µL
- Exaltolide 10%: 300 µL
- Ambrettolide 10%: 250 µL
- Romandolide: 120 µL
- Ambrox Super 30%: 100 µL
- Heliotropal: 80 µL
- Musk Ketone 10%: 200 µL
- **Geosmin 1% in TEC: 1 µL** (use a Hamilton microsyringe or 10 µL pipette set to 1 µL — measure carefully)

Swirl gently 1 min. Let sit 5 min for fatty-acid + fatty-ester integration. IPM acts as a co-solvent for the solid myristic acid dispersion AND as functional lipid-matrix material. Koavone+Azarbre dissolve fully into the unified fatty matrix and bond to the ionone chord added in Step 2.

**Step 2 — Ionone/irone chord:**
- α-Irone 30% DEP: 500 µL

- Allyl Ionone: 30 µL

- Ultralia: 80 µL

Swirl 30 sec.

**Step 3 — Radiance and rhizome:**
- Hedione: 650 µL
- Hedione HC: 80 µL
- Cis Jasmone: 20 µL
- Carrot Seed EO: 60 µL
- Cyclamen Aldehyde: 30 µL
- Farnesol: 10 µL
- Violet Fleuressence: 30 µL

Swirl 30 sec.

**Step 4 — Top lift (last, preserves volatility):**
- Bergamot FCF Sicilian: 200 µL
- Grapefruit FCF: 60 µL
- Leafovert: 8 µL
- Ethyl Linalool: 140 µL
- Dihydromyrcenol: 100 µL
- Allyl Amyl Glycolate: 20 µL

**Step 5 — Finish:**
- Transfer entire ~7,100 µL (concentrate + DEP carrier) to 30 mL amber bottle
- Top up to 30.00 mL with Ethanol 96%
- Cap, invert 20× gently
- Macerate minimum **4 weeks at 18–20°C in darkness** (myristic acid + ionones need extended maturation; α-irone specifically benefits from 6–8 weeks)
- Chill-filter at week 4 if any haze (myristic acid may precipitate below 15°C; warm to 22°C before filtering through 0.45 µm PTFE)

## 5. Expected smell — vignette by stage

**Spray and first breath:** A cold sparkle — grapefruit zest edge, bergamot richness, a flash of aldehyde lifting something floral. Crushed iris leaf greenness for 2 seconds.

**5–15 minutes:** The iris arrives. Cold, powdery, faintly rooty. Not soap, not candy — recognizably orris. Hedione creates a diffuse floral halo around it. Carrot seed and cyclamen aldehyde add a wet-rhizome shadow.

**30 minutes – 2 hours:** The formula settles into its full orris butter bloom. Myristic acid + IPM are now melting as a unified lipid matrix on your skin, releasing retained irones and ionones in a slow, continuous exhalation that lasts noticeably longer than any non-lipid formula could achieve. Irotyl and Koavone add a suede-powder-woody modulation specific to premium orris bases. Violet Fleuressence underlines the ionone chord with a green-natural authenticity. Far underneath, a geosmin whisper — the smell of an old cellar after rain, read as "authentic rhizome" at conscious level. The effect is dense but transparent, like looking through frosted glass at something cold, wet, and expensive.

**2–6 hours:** Drydown powder unfolds — Heliotropal's almond-heliotrope note wraps around Musk Ketone and Ethylene Brassylate's creamy lactonic base. Ebanol lends warm-skin sandalwood intimacy. Ambrettolide's fruity-wine naturalism warms the musk stack without animal heaviness. **Koavone and Azarbre become the iris signal-keepers** — maintaining a recognizable iris-suede perception even as actual α-irone concentration falls below detection.

**6–16 hours:** The final skin signature — quiet, powdery-creamy-woody, with orris still faintly perceptible at close range due to the IPM+myristic lipid matrix AND receptor co-activation from Koavone. Exaltolide and Habanolide form a skin-close musk film. You smell of yourself, but better — and the iris echo lasts hours longer than a comparable formula without receptor extenders and without a genuine dual fatty-acid + fatty-ester lipid matrix.

**References it evokes:** 70% *Iris Silver Mist* (cold rooty iris), 20% *Hiris* (minimalist skin transparency), 10% *Iris Poudre* (aldehydic powder drydown).
