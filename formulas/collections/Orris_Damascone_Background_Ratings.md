# Orris + Damascone Background Ratings

**Date:** 2026-04-06  
**Purpose:** score the 90 background frames for one specific task: turning your dry `orris + damascone` accord into a finished perfume.  
**Scoring basis:** `10 = strongest fit`, `8-9 = very good`, `6-7 = situational`, `4-5 = weak fit`, `<4 = wrong direction for this accord`.

This is **not** a rating of how good the backgrounds are in general. It is a rating of how well they serve **your current accord**.
