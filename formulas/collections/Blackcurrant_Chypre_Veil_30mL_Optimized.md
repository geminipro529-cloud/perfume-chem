## Intent

This is the finished `Blackcurrant Chypre Veil` build in full bottle-build format.

The logic is simple:
- the strange `orris + damascone` module is already the niche center
- the outer frame should smell like a real perfume
- the frame here is `dark fruit + clean chypre spine + dry modern woods + polished musk`

This formula is not trying to rebuild the accord outside the accord.
It is deliberately doing the opposite.

What the fixed module is assumed to already provide:
- the dry orris body
- the violet/ionone shadow
- the damascone fruit-strangeness
- the powder-root ambiguity
- the unusual niche signature

What the additions are doing:
- `Blackcurrant FTEC` gives the fruit shadow
- `Bergamot FCF + Petitgrain EO` make the opening readable and clean
- `Patchouli EO + Evernyl + Labdanum` build the chypre spine
- `Iso E Super + Cedramber` provide the dry modern architecture
- `Habanolide` gives polish and wearability
- `Benzoin + Hedione` keep it from smelling too skeletal or too dry

## Why This Version Works

### 1. The blackcurrant stays dark, not jammy

`Blackcurrant FTEC` is held at `0.28 mL`.

That is enough to create:
- cassis shadow
- tart dark-fruit lift
- a bridge into the damascone strangeness

It is not enough to turn the perfume into a berry perfume.

### 2. The chypre spine is real, but cleaned up

The real chypre skeleton is:
- `Bergamot FCF oil Sicilian`
- `Patchouli EO`
- `Evernyl`
- `Labdanum`

That gives the perfume a recognizable classical shape.

But it is cleaned and modernized by:
- `Iso E Super`
- `Cedramber`
- `Habanolide`

So it reads more like:
- polished modern chypre
- dry-fruity prestige

and less like:
- dusty old-school moss perfume

### 3. The fixed accord is not being smothered

No extra violet overload.
No extra coumarin-vanillin powder block.
No aggressive smoke.
No leather push.

That matters because the central module is already doing the weird work.

### 4. The formula stays finished, not skeletal

`Benzoin Resinoid` and `Hedione` are small but important:
- `Benzoin` softens the hard edges of the dry chypre frame
- `Hedione` keeps the center breathing

Without them, this build would be cleaner but more brittle.

Do **not** do a direct `1:1` swap with `Labdanum Absolute (10%)`.

Why:
- the current formula uses `280 uL` of **neat** `Labdanum`
- `Labdanum Absolute (10%)` is much weaker by active content
- a straight `280 uL` swap would make the perfume much thinner and less resinous

The cleanest no-`Labdanum` fix is to redistribute that `280 uL` across materials already in the frame.

Remove:
- `Labdanum` — `280 uL`

- `Patchouli EO` `+70 uL`

This keeps the perfume:
- chypre
- dark-fruity
- modern
- wearable

But it will smell:
- slightly cleaner
- slightly less resin-thick
- slightly more woody-dry

## If You Want To Adjust It Later

If it is too fruity:
- reduce `Blackcurrant FTEC` by `0.05-0.08 mL`
- move that volume into `Bergamot FCF` or `Iso E Super`

If it is too dry:
- raise `Benzoin Resinoid` by `0.03-0.05 mL`
- lower `Cedramber` by the same amount

If it is too classical:
- lower `Evernyl` by `0.02-0.03 mL`
- raise `Habanolide` or `Iso E Super`

If it lacks lift:
- raise `Bergamot FCF oil Sicilian` by `0.03-0.05 mL`
- lower `Labdanum` by the same amount

If it feels too clean and not dark enough:
- raise `Patchouli EO` by `0.03-0.05 mL`
- lower `Hedione` or `Iso E Super`
