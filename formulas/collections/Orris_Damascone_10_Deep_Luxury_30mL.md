# Orris + Damascone — 10 Deep Luxury Directions (30mL)

**Date:** 2026-04-06  
**Purpose:** 10 genuinely different finished-perfume directions for your premade `dry orris + damascone accord`, all optimized to feel `deep`, `luxurious`, and fully composed.

## Build Logic

All 10 use the same core structure:

- `4.50 mL` your premade `orris + damascone accord`
- `3.00 mL` additions
- `22.50 mL` ethanol 96%
- total `30.00 mL`
- final concentration `25.0%`

This keeps the accord dominant while letting the background direction actually show.

If your accord is already diluted in solvent, the math needs adjustment.

## Full Build Rule For All 10

For each prototype:

1. Measure the listed `3.00 mL` additions into a clean beaker.
2. Stir until homogeneous.
3. Add `4.50 mL` of your premade `orris + damascone accord`.
4. Stir again for `30-60 seconds`.
5. Add `22.50 mL` ethanol.
6. Stir thoroughly and bottle.
7. First check at `7 days`.
8. Best read at `14-21 days`.
