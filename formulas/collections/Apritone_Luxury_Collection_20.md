Together they create a citrus head with both warmth (apricot) and architecture (citron). This is the only formula where using both is structurally necessary.  
**Evernyl at 80 µL:** The highest Evernyl dose across AP-11 to AP-30. A chypre IS oakmoss — without sufficient moss, it's just a woody-amber. At 80 µL, you'll smell "forest floor after rain" as a distinct character, not a background modifier.

# RATINGS & DESCRIPTORS

## Rating Criteria

| Metric | What It Measures |
|--------|-----------------|
| **Originality** | Concept novelty + use of unusual material combinations. Bonus for first-use materials |
| **Wearability** | How practical for regular wear? Mass-appeal scores higher |
| **Longevity** | Material-stack tenacity analysis. What % of concentrate is base materials? |
| **Sillage** | Projection potential based on volatility, salicylate/Hedione content, concentration |
| **Cohesion** | Do the materials work as a unified accord, or is it an ingredient list? |
