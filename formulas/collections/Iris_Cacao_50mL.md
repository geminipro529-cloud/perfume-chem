# Iris Cacao — Chocolate Iris EDP

**Style:** Buttery-opaque iris on cocoa-sweet powder, generous musk, layered woody depth  
**Batch:** 50.00 mL EDP @ ~19.3% concentrate in ethanol 96%.  
**Reference axis:** 60% Dior Homme (iris-cocoa transparency), 25% Iris Silver Mist (cold rooty), 15% Valentino Uomo (iris-leather-cocoa warmth)

---

## 1. Accord architecture

Six functional layers, each with a specific chemical role:

1. **Bitter-Spice Opening** — Cedrat bitterness bridges to dark cocoa; lavender creates the historical iris-coumarin axis (Jicky lineage); cardamom spice bridges to eugenol-cocoa heart
2. **Iris Opacity Core** — AIMI saturated to DHI-level waxy-powdery opacity; α-Irone for real orris-butter quality; 5-ionone/irone isomer chord for dimensionality
3. **Cocoa Chord** — Heliotropal as the cocoa-bridge molecule (piperonal's methylenedioxybenzene skeleton); Coumarin for dry-powder tonka dimension; Eugenol/Isoeugenol for the spice-clove facet present in real cocoa absolute
4. **7-Layer Wood Architecture** — six yielding woods + one hard-wood pillar (molecular-skin → textile-warm → creamy-sandalwood → milky-round → cedar-amber → woody-musk bridge → architectural-dry cedar)
5. **6-Axis Musk Foundation** — generous musk (~12.6% active of concentrate) optimized for cocoa-sweet compatibility across depth, skin-intimacy, projection, skin-fatty, character-echo, and naturalistic warmth
6. **Balsamic Bed** — Benzoin balsamic warmth supporting the cocoa-vanillic dimension

## 3. Material selection rationale

### 3.1 Citrus — Why Cedrat and not Bergamot

Cedrat FCF Sicilian is the sole citrus. Its **bitter-mineral citron** character bridges directly to the bitter dimension of dry cocoa powder. Bergamot FCF's bright-optimistic-cologne quality would fight the dark character. Blood Orange is too sweet-juicy. Grapefruit FCF is too clean-bright for an opaque composition.

### 3.2 Iris core — AIMI at saturation dose

AIMI at 1,400 µL (16.2% of concentrate) is the formula's identity. This is DHI-level saturation — the waxy-powdery ionone opacity that creates the "lipstick" association. At this dose AIMI becomes opaque-purple-buttery rather than merely violet. The 5-isomer ionone/irone chord (α-Irone, α-Ionone, β-Ionone, Allyl Ionone, DHBI) prevents flatness by creating dimensional complexity within the ionone register. Irotyl adds suede-powder texture; Ultralia at trace creates a transparent iris ghost that hovers above the opacity.

**Why Orivone and not Orris FTEC:** Orivone provides warm buttery iris base character from a single molecule, giving more predictable control than the pre-blended FTEC which would add facets we can't separate.

### 3.3 Cocoa chord — pyrazine-free reconstruction

Real cocoa absolute contains pyrazines (2-methylpyrazine, tetramethylpyrazine) that create the true chocolate dimension and iris-enhancing cross-receptor activation. These are NOT available in our inventory. Our cocoa chord reconstructs ~65% of the cocoa *smell* using:

- **Heliotropal (piperonal)** — THE cocoa bridge molecule. Its methylenedioxybenzene skeleton gives cherry-almond-powder that reads as "cocoa powder" in iris context. 200 µL = sufficient for clear cocoa read without overwhelming the iris.
- **Coumarin (20%)** — dry-powder tonka; creates the DRY dimension of cocoa (vs. sweet chocolate). 400 µL (80 µL active) synergizes with AIMI for iris-cocoa opacity. The hay-almond aspect of coumarin is present in real cocoa absolute.
- **Eugenol** — clove-spice at trace (30 µL). Present in real cocoa absolute at 0.5–2%. Darkens the heliotropal cocoa effect. At this dose it is NOT identifiable as clove — it reads as "spice warmth."
- **Isoeugenol** — sweeter, more refined than eugenol (20 µL). Clove-cream modifier also present in real cocoa. Adds warm-balsamic quality without eugenol's sharpness.
- **Vanillin (10%)** — MINIMAL dose (150 µL = 15 µL active). The brief is dry-cocoa, not sweet chocolate. Just enough to suggest the sweet axis of cocoa without pulling toward gourmand.

**What's missing:** Pyrazines (no available source), cocoa butter fatty acids (partially compensated by IPM + Benzoin), polyphenol-derived molecules. This gap is irreducible with current inventory — accept it as the limit of synthetic cocoa reconstruction.

### 3.4 Wood architecture — 7 registers, sandalwood-dominant + one hard pillar

Cocoa-sweet-iris demands **creamy, warm, yielding** woods. Angular, dry, or architectural woods fight the sweet-butter concept. The wood chord is deliberately sandalwood-heavy (Ebanol + Bacdanol = 550 µL of 1,440 total) because milky-creamy sandalwood is the single most compatible wood class with cocoa-butter and orris-butter.

| Wood | Register | Role in cocoa-sweet-iris |
|------|----------|----------------------|
| **Iso E Super** (450 µL) | Molecular-skin | Abstract cedar cocoon for skin-adhesion. Reduced from 500→450 because with the expanded musk chord, less Iso E is needed for the skin-adhesion role. Still creates the molecular halo that makes the wearer "smell like themselves but better." |
| **Cashmeran neat** (120 µL) | Textile-warm-spice | Switched from 20% dilution (400 µL / 80 active) to neat (120 µL / 120 active). More active cashmere-spice per µL. The spicy facet bridges directly to the eugenol-cocoa axis; the textile warmth wraps the sweetness in "expensive cashmere." Cashmeran's musky undertone also bridges wood→musk layers. |
| **Ebanol** (400 µL) | Creamy-buttery sandalwood | INCREASED from 250→400. The primary wood for this concept. At 400 µL, Ebanol's creamy-milky character becomes the dominant wood texture — it mirrors both cocoa-butter and orris-butter registers. Chosen over Javanol (too dry-mineral, wrong texture for sweet-buttery) and Sandalore (too light-fresh). |
| **Bacdanol** (150 µL) | Milky-round sandalwood | NEW. Heavier, more traditional milky sandalwood that creates a sandalwood CHORD with Ebanol: Ebanol gives soft cream, Bacdanol gives full milky weight. Together they read as "rich sandalwood" with more dimensionality than either alone. The milky quality amplifies the sweet-cocoa dimension. Closest to natural Mysore sandalwood character in this inventory. |
| **Azarbre** (200 µL) | Cedar-amber-warm | Unchanged. Smooth cedar-amber bridge connecting wood and balsamic layers. The warm amber facet is sweet-compatible; the cedar facet prevents the wood chord from being entirely soft (needs some structure). Warmer than Ambrox (no crystalline edge), softer than Timberol (no angular rigidity). |
| **Vertofix** (120 µL) | Woody-musky bridge | NEW, replacing Koavone. Vertofix is the connective tissue between wood and musk layers — its amber-cedarwood-musk character prevents the two sections from reading as separate chords. Better integration than Koavone's generic warm-wood, which was redundant with Azarbre's cedar warmth. |
| **Timberol** (80 µL) | Architectural-dry cedar | The single hard-wood pillar. At 80 µL this is deliberately restrained — enough to create a dry cedarwood skeleton that prevents the 6 soft/creamy/warm woods from collapsing into shapeless butter. The angular rigidity of Timberol gives the listener's nose something to "grab" — architectural contrast inside a yielding composition. Without it, the wood chord reads as "soft" from every angle; with it, there's one moment of dry structure that makes the surrounding cream read creamier by contrast. Think of it as the bone inside the flesh. |

**Removed: Koavone** — provided warm-cedar support but no unique dimension that Azarbre + Vertofix don't already cover. Every µL must contribute a distinct functional effect.

**Rejected woods:**
- **Clearwood** — earthy facet competes with cocoa
- **Javanol** — dry-mineral, wrong textural register for buttery-sweet
- **Sandalore** — too light-fresh for an opaque sweet composition
- **Norlimbanol** — too rigid, needs softness not structure
- **Cedarwood EO** — identifiable "pencil cedar" competes with cocoa-iris identity
- **Cedramber** — redundant with Azarbre's cedar-amber register
- **Vetival** — dark-suede dryness fights sweet

### 3.5 Musk foundation — 6 axes, optimized for cocoa-sweet

Total active musk: 1,200 µL = 12.6% of concentrate (up from 6.4%). The musk chord is now generous — optimized for **best smell** with cocoa-sweet-iris, not minimized. Each musk was selected for specific chemical compatibility with the cocoa-butter/iris-butter/sweet-powder accord.

| Musk | Axis | Volume µL | Active µL | Role in cocoa-sweet-iris |
|------|------|-----------|-----------|----------------------|
| **Ethylene Brassylate** (neat) | DEPTH | 500 | 500 | Lactonic-creamy anchor — THE musk for cocoa-sweet. Its lactone quality IS cocoa-butter. At 500 µL it creates a full creamy bed that envelops the entire drydown. Increased from 350 because the sweet-buttery concept demands a deeper lactonic foundation. |
| **Habanolide** (neat) | SKIN INTIMACY | 350 | 350 | NEW. Warm, skin-like macrocyclic. Creates "cocoa-on-warm-skin" — the intimate quality that makes sweet fragrances feel sensual rather than gourmand-edible. Previously rejected for "low musk" brief; now essential for "best smell." Habanolide's warmth is no longer redundant — with the sandalwood-dominant wood chord, its SKIN quality adds a dimension that Ebanol/Bacdanol (which read as "wood") cannot. |
| **Romandolide** (neat) | PROJECTION | 250 | 250 | Increased from 150→250. The formula's voice projecting outward. More Romandolide = more sillage = more people notice the cocoa-iris. Its slightly woody-clean character doesn't fight sweetness — it extends it into space. |
| **Exaltolide** (10%) | SKIN-FATTY | 350 | 35 | NEW. Pairs with EB to create a layered lactonic chord: EB gives CREAM, Exaltolide gives SKIN-OIL. Together they have more dimension than either alone — the cream/fat combination is literally how real cocoa butter smells (triacylglycerol + lactone). |
| **Musk Ketone** (10% in DPG) | CHARACTER-ECHO | 300 | 30 | Slightly increased from 250→300. Powdery-talcum quality directly reinforces BOTH cocoa-powder AND iris-powder. The only musk that mirrors the formula's powder axis. At 30 µL active this is still a ghost — "expensive powder" not "baby powder." |
| **Ambrettolide** (10% in DPG) | NATURALISTIC | 350 | 35 | Increased from 300→350. Fruity-wine-musk warmth, the most natural-feeling macrocyclic. Its fruity facet enhances the sweet dimension; its wine-like depth adds "this can't be synthetic" quality. At 35 µL active it whispers rather than speaks. |

**Rejected musks:**
- **Galaxolide / Tonalide** — polycyclic detergent musks; fight luxury cocoa concept; pull toward "laundry" not "chocolate"
- **Zenolide** — citrusy-fresh character fights warm sweet cocoa; useful in fresh accords, wrong here
- **Macrolide (10%)** — too gentle; with 6 musks already contributing, a 7th quiet musk adds nothing

### 3.6 Radiance and fixative

- **Hedione** (700 µL) — radiance amplifier and volume expander. At 8.1% of concentrate this is moderate — enough to bloom the iris-cocoa chord without the "Hedione veil" that drowns character at higher doses.
- **Benzyl Salicylate** (400 µL) — cosmetic-heavy salicylate fixative. The lipstick-cosmetic association is DELIBERATE: historical lipstick formulations = cocoa butter + synthetic iris/violet. This material reinforces the iris-cocoa-lipstick olfactive tradition. Chosen over Hexyl Salicylate (too light/transparent for this opaque formula).
- **IPM** (500 µL) — fatty ester matrix for skin adhesion. Recreates part of the orris butter lipid matrix. Also acts as carrier for the cocoa-spice molecules, extending their release curve on skin.

### 3.7 Balsamic bed

- **Benzoin Resinoid 50%** (300 µL = 150 µL active) — balsamic-vanillic warmth; cinnamic acid esters in benzoin echo the spice of the cocoa chord
- **Siam Benzoin 50%** (200 µL = 100 µL active) — smoother benzoin variant with less cinnamic sharpness; creates a warm-sweet pillow under the cocoa-wood base

Together these benzoins provide the warm-balsamic-vanillic foundation that supports the cocoa dimension without needing excessive vanillin.

## 5. Pre-dilution protocol

### 5.1 Musk Ketone preparation

Musk Ketone is a crystalline solid (pure powder). Pre-dilute before use:

1. Weigh **0.500 g** Musk Ketone powder on precision scale
2. Add to a **5 mL** amber vial
3. Add **4.500 mL** DPG (dipropylene glycol)
4. Cap, shake vigorously for 2 minutes
5. Let stand at room temperature 24 hours with occasional shaking until fully dissolved
6. Result: **10% w/v Musk Ketone in DPG** — use 250 µL of this solution

## 7. Smell vignette

**0–30 minutes:** Cedrat bitterness cuts through immediately — not a cheerful citrus opening but a dark, mineral-bitter edge that says "this is going somewhere serious." Lavender and cardamom create a brief aromatic bridge that flashes for 10 minutes before the iris tide rises. Within 15 minutes the AIMI opacity begins to saturate — that distinctive waxy-purple-buttery quality that DHI lovers recognize instantly. The Heliotropal cocoa chord arrives simultaneously, threading cherry-almond-powder through the iris matrix. You smell lipstick, expensive powder, and something dark-brown.

**30 minutes – 3 hours:** Full iris-cocoa bloom. AIMI at saturation dose creates an opaque purple-brown cloud. The 5-ionone/irone isomer chord prevents flatness — you get dimensionality, shifting facets, a sense of depth rather than a wall of one note. Coumarin's dry-powder tonka quality merges with Heliotropal to create cocoa-sweet powder — the sweet-roasted-bitter character of dark cocoa with a powdery vanillic halo. Eugenol + Isoeugenol at trace provide a clove warmth that darkens everything without being identifiable as spice. The 6 wood layers are beginning to differentiate: Cashmeran's textile-spice warmth arrives first, then Azarbre's smooth cedar-amber, then the Ebanol/Bacdanol sandalwood chord — a milky-creamy duet that mirrors cocoa-butter. And somewhere in between, a flash of Timberol's dry cedarwood angularity — brief, structural, like a cedarwood beam in a room of silk.

**3–8 hours:** The wood architecture reveals itself fully. Iso E Super's molecular cocoon wraps the iris-cocoa in an abstract cedar halo — the "second skin" effect. The Ebanol/Bacdanol sandalwood chord reaches full projection: creamy-milky, buttery, supporting both iris-butter and cocoa-butter. Timberol persists as a dry architectural edge — the one hard line that prevents the soft woods from reading as formless. Vertofix bridges seamlessly into the 6-axis musk foundation. And now the musks bloom: EB's lactonic cream envelops the drydown; Habanolide adds warm-skin intimacy (the "cocoa on warm skin" effect); Romandolide pushes sillage outward; MK's ghost-powder reinforces the cocoa-powder register; Exaltolide's skin-fatty quality merges with EB to create a layered lactonic chord that reads as real cocoa-butter. Benzoin warmth rises from the balsamic bed. This is the formula's sweet spot — opaque, woody-chocolate-iris, generous and sensual.

**8–16 hours:** Terminal drydown resolves to Iso E + Azarbre + EB + Habanolide — an abstract woody-amber-musk skin scent with persistent iris echo. The generous musk chord keeps the fragrance alive and present on skin far longer than the previous low-musk version. Ambrettolide's fruity-wine naturalness and Exaltolide's skin-oil quality blend into the EB/Habanolide bed for a rich, naturalistic, "expensive skin" drydown. Bacdanol's milky weight persists as a faint sandalwood warmth underneath. You smell like warm cashmere draped over cocoa-butter skin.
