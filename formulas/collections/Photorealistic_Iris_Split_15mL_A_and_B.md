# PHOTOREALISTIC IRIS — 30 mL OVERDOSE SALVAGE → 2 × 15 mL SPLIT OPTIMIZATION

**Source 30 mL bottle:** `formulas/collections/Photorealistic_Iris_v4_Final.md` (internally v6 final — orris-butter molecular reconstruction, 39 materials, 21.4 % concentrate + 4 % lipid matrix).

**Chat-log of the overdose diagnosis:** `formulas/collections/perfume_a_discussion.md`, lines 4115–4174 — IPM at ~2.3 % of bottle volume "dampens sillage", "slows the C11 aldehyde sparkle", "makes the perfume feel smoother, less alcohol bite", and "creates a skin-scent bias" against radiance additions.

---

## 1. Diagnosis — what was overdosed

The Photorealistic Iris v6 formula is designed around a **dual lipid matrix** that physically mimics real orris butter:

| Lipid-matrix material | Dose in 30 mL | % of bottle | Role |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Isopropyl Myristate (IPM) | 500 µL | **1.67 %** | Fatty-ester half (methyl myristate fraction of natural butter) |
| Myristic Acid Powder (in DEP 20 %) | 300 mg (1,200 µL DEP) | 1.00 % active + 4.00 % DEP | Fatty-acid half (40–50 % of real orris butter) |
| **Total non-odorant lipid load** | **~1,700 µL + 300 mg** | **~6 %** | Skin-anchor / slow-release matrix |

At that load the bottle is photorealistically correct as a *skin-release kinetics* mimic, but the matrix over-weights the **fixative / skin-anchor axis** so severely that the chat log's explicitly named failure modes appear:

- **Projection crushed** — lipid film holds volatile molecules below their natural headspace VP
- **Sillage crushed** — aldehyde / Hedione diffusion is dampened on emission
- **Cold-head flash muted** — Scentenal-1 %, grapefruit pith, Ald-C11 would provide — but C11 was never added, and Scentenal at 0.24 µL active is below break-through with this much lipid on top
- **Hedonic loss for non-wearer** — "crowd-pleaser" index drops because the accord now rewards only close-range reading

This is exactly the mechanism the chat log diagnoses for IPM at this concentration range. **It is a design overdose of the lipid carrier system, not a safety overdose.**

---

## 2. Split protocol — 30 mL → 2 × 15 mL

Both halves inherit 1/2 of every material at identical concentration. Additions are **only ever additive** — you cannot subtract IPM from a bottle that already contains it. Corrective strategy: shift the ratio by adding *radiant / projective / diffusive* materials to recover sillage, without dilution-killing the iris identity.

**Physical procedure:**
1. Gently invert the 30 mL bottle 10× (do not shake — bubble-oxidation risks the trace Ald-C11 if added earlier, and agitates the macrocyclic musks)
2. Let settle 30 min
3. Decant **15.00 mL** via graduated glass cylinder or 10-mL pipette (2× 7.5 mL) into two clean 20 mL amber bottles labelled **A** and **B**
4. Both bottles now contain:
   - Concentrate: ~3,207 µL
   - DEP carrier (from myristic acid prep): ~600 µL
   - Ethanol: ~11,193 µL
   - Total: 15,000 µL
5. Apply corrective additions per Formula A and Formula B below
6. Let each rest **72 h minimum** before re-evaluation (macrocyclic musks + new Ambrox need full integration time)
