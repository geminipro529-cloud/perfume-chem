## Concept Brief

Vol d'Ambre v3c raises the luxury ceiling of v3b. Ethyl Vanillin is tripled to carry headspace-active vanilla where Vanillin alone could not. Benzyl Salicylate adds a soft jasmine-floral cushion and a high-logP skin fixative. Cashmeran provides a creamy cashmere mid-drydown, introducing the temporal novelty the pure Layton-linear v3b lacked. Lavender and Dihydromyrcenol are trimmed to reduce aromatic-OR cluster over-recruitment (GR6). BHT antioxidant is added post-optimisation to guard lavender/limonene peroxide formation. (Beta-Ionone was considered but its ODT is ~700× more potent than Alpha Isomethyl Ionone; even 0.6% would dominate the powdery axis disastrously — kept out.)

## Post-Mix Base Booster (add to finished 30 mL bottle)

Applied after v3c was mixed and evaluated. Formula was correct structurally but the amber/woody base was severely underpowered: aromatic OAV ~7000 (lavender + Hedione dominated), amber OAV only ~59 in base vs target 420. The booster addresses this without remaking the formula.

### Thermodynamic rationale

| Material | VP (Pa) | clogP | ODT (µg/m³) | or_family | Function in base |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| Ambrox Super | 0.0003 | 4.92 | 0.3 | amber | Skin-depot amber — fixed reservoir, not aerial |
| Azarbre | 0.005 | 4.80 | 2.0 | amber | Aerial amber projection — VP 17× higher than Ambrox, fills the air column Ambrox cannot |
| Javanol | 0.001 | 3.50 | 3.0 | woody | Creamy sandalwood bridge; logP 3.5 operates heart-to-base temporal zone |
| Norlimbanol Dextro | 0.001 | 5.20 | 0.5 | woody | Hard dry woody backbone; highest skin substantivity (logP 5.2) |

GR6 note: Ambrox + Azarbre share or_family=amber (compete at same ORs) but are separated by GR1 in the time domain — Azarbre (VP 0.005) dominates early aerial base; Ambrox (VP 0.0003) dominates late skin-contact phase. Javanol + Norlimbanol share or_family=woody and blend as a complementary woody accord.

### Addition protocol

Decant ~1 mL from the bottle first to avoid overflow. Add in order:

| Step | Material | Stock | Add | EDP% after addition |
|---:|---|---|---:|---|
| 1 | Ambrox Super | 30% | **900 µL** | 0.456% → ~1.33% |
| 2 | Azarbre | neat | **150 µL** | +0.49% |
| 3 | Javanol | neat | **100 µL** | 0.75% → ~1.07% |
| 4 | Norlimbanol Dextro | neat | **40 µL** | +0.13% — **hard cap: do not exceed 50 µL total; GR5 harshness cliff at ~0.17%** |

Cap bottle, invert 10×, macerate **24–48 h** before re-evaluating.

Expected outcome: amber OAV in base rises from ~59 to ~230–250 (thermodynamic ceiling given VP of Ambrox). Will not reach Layton parity — for that, v4 needs Ambrox floored at 10% of concentrate and Hedione capped at 5%. This is the best correction achievable in the finished bottle.

## Post-Booster Evaluation & Masculinity Fix (added 2026-04-27)

After four booster steps the amber band (Ambrox + Azarbre) is at ~1.82% EDP — receptor-saturated. The remaining weakness is a **sweetness vs. dryness imbalance**: Vanillin + Coumarin + Benzoin + Cashmeran + Ethyl Vanillin dominate the profile with no angular woody backbone to counter them. Norlimbanol is hard-capped at 0.13%.

**Do NOT add any of these:**
- **More Ambrox** — amber ORs saturated; pushes toward sweaty/mineral rather than masculine
- **Amberwood F** — smooth/rounded amber-wood, makes the profile *softer*, not tougher

**Recommended addition — Timberol 100 µL neat:**
- OR family = **woody** (no competition with amber-band Ambrox/Azarbre)
- Dry, warm, slightly vetiver-like backbone — directly counters sweet vanilla-core
- Per inventory note on Azarbre: *"softer than Timberol"* — Timberol is the harder pole
- Safe: no harshness cliff like Norlimbanol
- Add 100 µL neat to bottle → +0.33% EDP; cap, invert 10×, macerate 24–48 h

**Why not Cedramber?** Cedramber contains an **amber facet** (the name is *cedr-amber*) that overlaps with the saturated amber OR band (Ambrox + Azarbre at ~1.82%). Additionally, Cedramber is only stocked at **10%** — a 100 µL neat-equivalent dose would require 1,000 µL of stock, too much volume for a finished 30 mL bottle. Timberol is available **neat**, requires only 100 µL, and operates cleanly in the woody OR family with no amber competition.

**Backup if more structure needed after Timberol:**
- Vertofix Coeur 60–80 µL neat → +0.20–0.27% EDP — cedar-vetiver-leather, very dry/angular

## Safety Notes

- **BHT:** cosmetic antioxidant, 0.02% is well within limits (typical range 0.01–0.1%).  Protects linalool and limonene against peroxide formation during storage.
- **Benzyl Salicylate:** IFRA cat 4 limit ~1.7% in finished product; v3c is ~0.4% in finished EDP. Safe.
- **Cashmeran:** no current IFRA restriction; conservative perfumer practice caps at 5% in concentrate; v3c ~1.5%.
- Store in dark glass; BHT lifespan ~12–18 months before antioxidant is depleted by air exposure.
