## Procedure

1. In a clean 20 mL beaker, add materials in the order listed (macros → traces).
2. Add ethanol 96% to bring to 10 mL.
3. Stir 30 s, cap, rest 24–48 hr (maceration is important for iris — the waxes need time to integrate).
4. Pour into the bottle and swirl.

## What you're giving up vs the full 33-material formula

- **Top notes** (Bergamot, Ethyl Linalool, Dihydromyrcenol): the opening will be quieter, more straight-to-iris. The simplified version skips the brief citrus-airy lift.
- **Accessory ionones** (Alpha, Beta, Dihydro Beta, Orivone): slightly less multidimensional iris — you lose some of the violet-backdrop shimmer around the Alpha Irone.
- **Musk chord** (Habanolide, Exaltolide, Ambrettolide, Musk Ketone): just Ethylene Brassylate carries all musk duty — less layered skin-trail.
- **Trace florals** (Ultralia, Cyclamen Aldehyde, Farnesol, Cis Jasmone): no floral halo around the iris.

**Net:** ~80–85% of the photorealistic iris effect, ~25% of the work.
