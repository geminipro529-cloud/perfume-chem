# Orris + Damascone — 30mL Addition Prototypes

**Date:** 2026-04-06  
**Purpose:** convert 3 of the highest-rated but **more differentiated** background directions from [Orris_Damascone_Background_Ratings.md](/d:/chatbots/perfume-chem/Orris_Damascone_Background_Ratings.md) into actual `30 mL` finished perfumes built around your premade `dry orris + damascone accord`.

## Assumption

These formulas assume:

- your `orris + damascone accord` is a **neat concentrate module**
- you want a finished `30.00 mL` perfume at `25.0%` concentration
- total aromatic concentrate = `7.50 mL`
- ethanol = `22.50 mL`

To keep the accord clearly dominant, each prototype uses:

- `4.50 mL` your premade accord
- `3.00 mL` additions
- `22.50 mL` ethanol

If your accord is already diluted in ethanol or DPG, these numbers need adjustment.

---

## 1. Clean Plum Powder

**Background score:** `9.4`  
**Target:** plush fruit-powder luxury-mass  
**Effect:** turns the accord into a soft plum-powder perfume with cosmetic warmth and easy diffusion

### Additions

| Material | Amount (mL) | Amount (uL) | Role |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Peonile | 0.45 | 450 | soft plum-peony bloom |
| Heliotropin Fleuressence | 0.55 | 550 | powdered almond-vanilla compact effect |
| Cashmeran (20%) | 0.35 | 350 | plush textile warmth |
| Hedione | 0.80 | 800 | air and radiance |
| Galaxolide (80%) | 0.55 | 550 | smooth clean musk spread |
| Habanolide | 0.30 | 300 | clean skin finish so it stays modern |
| **Total Additions** | **3.00** | **3000** |  |

### Full 30mL Build

| Component | Amount (mL) |
|---|---:|
| Your `orris + damascone accord` | 4.50 |
| Additions above | 3.00 |
| Ethanol 96% | 22.50 |
| **Total** | **30.00** |

### Read

This is still the safest `luxury + mass` version. It should read like dry plum dusted with expensive face powder rather than literal iris or rose.

## Mixing Order

For all 3:

1. Add the `3.00 mL` additions into a clean beaker.
2. Stir until fully homogeneous.
3. Add `4.50 mL` of your premade `orris + damascone accord`.
4. Stir again for `30-60 sec`.
5. Add `22.50 mL` ethanol.
6. Stir thoroughly.
7. Rest at least `7 days`.
8. Best read: `14-21 days`.
