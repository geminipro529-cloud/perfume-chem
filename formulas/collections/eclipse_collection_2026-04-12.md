# Eclipse Collection — 10 Cosmic Compositions
**Date:** 2026-04-12  
**Batch Size:** 10.00 mL each  
**Equipment:** 20–200 µL micropipette, 0.01g precision scale  
**Density assumption:** ≈ 1.0 g/mL (1 µL ≈ 1 mg, 1 mL ≈ 1 g)  
**Unifying Material:** Galaxolide — the synthetic-clean polycyclic musk threading every composition, recontextualized as cosmic void, solar wind, space-suit interior, or ethereal veil depending on the accord architecture surrounding it.

> **Dilution reminder:** All amounts are the *diluted material* to dispense. The active perfumery ingredient is reduced by the dilution factor (e.g., 300 µL of Ambrox Super at 30% = 90 µL active ambroxan). Amounts < 1 mL shown in µL; amounts ≥ 1 mL shown in mL.

> **Galaxolide Strategy:** Galaxolide's fruity-floral synthetic-clean character typically reads as "detergent" in warm/natural contexts. In this collection, it is deliberately reframed by pairing it with metallic-mineral, ozonic, abstract-molecular, or dark-smoky materials that shift its register from "laundry" to "cosmic." Each formula states HOW Galaxolide is being recontextualized.

### Accord Architecture

**Corona Flash Layer (Top):**
- Aldehydes C11 + C12 MNA at 80 + 60 µL of 1% dilution — the waxy metallic shimmer of the corona's edge. C11 provides the classic "radiance lift" while C12 MNA adds warm waxy body. At 1% these are trace active — just sparkle, no soap.

**Radiant Glow Layer (Heart):**

**Cosmic Void Layer (Base):**
- Galaxolide (80%) at 250 µL as the space vacuum behind the corona — its synthetic-clean character, paired with metallics and aldehydes, reads as "pristine cosmic emptiness" rather than laundry. The aldehydic context reframes it.
- Iso E Super at 350 µL as molecular skin cocoon — abstract cedar halo, the dark matter holding the corona together. Chosen for molecular-skin register: the corona should live ON skin. Rejected: Timberol (too identifiably cedar), Clearwood (too earthy).
- Ambrox Super at 250 µL of 30% as crystalline mineral amber — cold cosmic depth behind the light. Crystalline register chosen: the void is crystalline, not warm. Rejected: Ambermax (too warm/cozy), Amberwood F (too transparent for structural void).
- Cashmeran (20%) at 150 µL as textile-warm bridge — prevents the composition from becoming cold sterile; adds a "cosmic blanket" comfort.
- Benzyl Salicylate at 150 µL as diffusion cushion and cosmetic volume — maximum projection for the corona effect. Chosen over Hexyl Sal because the corona needs to PROJECT, not whisper.
