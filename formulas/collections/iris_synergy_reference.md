## How to Read This File

This file covers **every iris/violet-axis material in your inventory** and documents how each interacts with the others. Each synergy includes:

- **Chemical effect** — what happens when the materials meet
- **Dosing at 4 batch sizes** (10 mL / 30 mL / 50 mL / 100 mL) at 4 intensity levels
- **Ratio guidance** — how to balance materials against each other
- **What goes wrong** — overdose behaviour, dead zones, clashes

> **Intensity levels:** Whisper (subliminal) · Audible (detectable) · Statement (clearly present) · Dominant (leading the composition)

> **All values in µL of your stock solution** (dilution already factored in — volumes are what you pipette from your bottle).

## PART 2: PAIRWISE SYNERGIES — THE CORE INTERACTIONS

### Synergy 1: ALPHA IRONE + ORIVONE (The Orris Butter Engine)
**Effect:** Alpha Irone provides cold-metallic orris identity; Orivone adds buttery-fatty body underneath. Together they create the "cold butter on warm toast" effect that IS realistic orris root — neither alone achieves it. Orivone fills the hollow middle of Alpha Irone's profile.

| Material | Dilution | Whisper | Audible | Statement | Dominant |
|----------|----------|---------|---------|-----------|----------|
| | | 10 / 30 / 50 / 100 | 10 / 30 / 50 / 100 | 10 / 30 / 50 / 100 | 10 / 30 / 50 / 100 |
| Alpha Irone | 30% DEP | 15/45/75/150 | 50/150/250/500 | 100/300/500/1000 | 200/600/1000/2000 |
| Orivone | neat | 8/24/40/80 | 20/60/100/200 | 50/150/250/500 | 100/300/500/1000 |

**Ratio:** Alpha Irone (of solution) : Orivone = 3:1 to 5:1. Going heavier on Orivone (below 2:1) makes the accord fatty and loses the cold-metallic top that Alpha Irone provides. Going lighter on Orivone (above 6:1) leaves Alpha Irone thin and acidic.

**Active ingredient note:** At 30% dilution, 100 µL of Alpha Irone solution = 30 µL active irone. So at Statement (10 mL), you're getting ~30 µL active irone vs 50 µL neat Orivone — the real active ratio is approximately 1:1.7 (irone:orivone), which is what creates the balanced butter.

**Overdose:** Too much Orivone → rancid/fatty/margarine. Too much Alpha Irone → thin, acidic, "sniffing a perfume strip, not skin."

### Synergy 2: ALPHA IRONE + ALPHA ISOMETHYL IONONE (Realism vs. Powder)
**Effect:** Alpha Irone brings orris realism (buttery-metallic). AIMI brings powdery-cosmetic volume. This is the fundamental split in iris perfumery: **irone-forward = niche/realistic; AIMI-forward = commercial/powdery.** The ratio between these two materials determines which camp a composition falls into.

| Material | Dilution | Whisper | Audible | Statement | Dominant |
|----------|----------|---------|---------|-----------|----------|
| | | 10 / 30 / 50 / 100 | 10 / 30 / 50 / 100 | 10 / 30 / 50 / 100 | 10 / 30 / 50 / 100 |
| Alpha Irone | 30% DEP | 15/45/75/150 | 50/150/250/500 | 100/300/500/1000 | 200/600/1000/2000 |
| AIMI | neat | 15/45/75/150 | 50/150/250/500 | 120/360/600/1200 | 200/600/1000/2000 |

**Ratio spectrum:**

| Irone (active) : AIMI Ratio | Character | Reference |
|-----------------------------|-----------|-----------|
| 3:1 or higher | Irone-dominant. Buttery, metallic, waxy, realistic orris root. | Amouage Opus V, Chanel No.19 |
| 2:1 | Irone-led with powder support. Rich, dimensional, niche. | Malle Iris Poudre |
| 1:1 | Balanced. Both realism and powder present equally. | Prada Iris, Infusion |
| 1:2 | Powder-led with orris ghost. Cosmetic, elegant. | Dior Homme territory |
| 1:3 or lower | AIMI-dominant. Lipstick iris, face-powder, commercial. | Most designer iris fragrances |

**Critical:** Remember Alpha Irone is at 30% dilution. To calculate the active ratio: multiply your Alpha Irone µL by 0.3, then compare to your neat AIMI µL.

**Example (10 mL, Statement):** 100 µL Alpha Irone solution × 0.3 = 30 µL active irone. 120 µL AIMI = 120 µL active. Real ratio = 1:4 (powder-led). To get irone-forward at Statement: use 200 µL Alpha Irone (60 active) vs 60 µL AIMI = ~1:1, then Orivone fills the body.

### Synergy 3: THE VIOLET CASCADE (Alpha Ionone → Beta Ionone → Dihydro Beta Ionone)
**Effect:** Three ionones that cover different time phases and olfactive registers, creating a "violet waterfall" from bright-transparent (Alpha) through dark-woody (Beta) to dry-cedar (Dihydro Beta). This cascade gives iris accords temporal depth — the composition evolves instead of being static.

| Material | Dilution | Whisper | Audible | Statement | Dominant |
|----------|----------|---------|---------|-----------|----------|
| | | 10 / 30 / 50 / 100 | 10 / 30 / 50 / 100 | 10 / 30 / 50 / 100 | 10 / 30 / 50 / 100 |
| Alpha Ionone (bright flash) | neat | 5/15/25/50 | 15/45/75/150 | 40/120/200/400 | 80/240/400/800 |
| Beta Ionone (dark depth) | neat | 3/9/15/30 | 10/30/50/100 | 30/90/150/300 | 60/180/300/600 |
| Dihydro Beta Ionone (dry base) | neat | 5/15/25/50 | 15/45/75/150 | 50/150/250/500 | 80/240/400/800 |

**Ratio:** Alpha Ionone : Beta Ionone : Dihydro Beta Ionone = 1 : 0.7 : 1.2

Alpha Ionone flashes first (top-note violet introduction, 15–45 min). Beta Ionone sustains through the heart (dark violet, 1–4 hr). Dihydro Beta Ionone persists into the base as dry woody-cedar-violet.

**Without Alpha Ionone:** The iris starts dark and heavy. Useful for noir compositions.
**Without Beta Ionone:** The middle is hollow — you get flash then immediately dry wood. Only useful when Orivone fills the heart.
**Without Dihydro Beta Ionone:** The iris evaporates too cleanly — no woody anchor in the drydown.

**Overdose (any single one):** Alpha Ionone → too watery, dilutes the butter. Beta Ionone → too dark and woody, lose the iris identity. Dihydro Beta → too cedar-dry, iris becomes indistinguishable from wood.

### Synergy 4: ALLYL IONONE (Ketone V) — THE WOODY BRIDGE
**Effect:** Allyl Ionone has less violet character and more woody-warm quality than the other ionones. It bridges the iris chord toward wood materials (Iso E Super, Timberol, Cedarwood). Without it, iris accords and woody bases can feel like two separate compositions layered on top of each other.

| Material | Dilution | Whisper | Audible | Statement | Dominant |
|----------|----------|---------|---------|-----------|----------|
| | | 10 / 30 / 50 / 100 | 10 / 30 / 50 / 100 | 10 / 30 / 50 / 100 | 10 / 30 / 50 / 100 |
| Allyl Ionone | neat | 5/15/25/50 | 15/45/75/150 | 40/120/200/400 | 80/240/400/800 |

**Best pairings:**
- Allyl Ionone + Iso E Super (1:8 ratio) — seamless iris-into-molecular-wood transition
- Allyl Ionone + Dihydro Beta Ionone (1:1.5 ratio) — reinforced dry-woody iris base
- Allyl Ionone + Timberol (1:3 ratio) — architectural iris-cedar

**When to include vs. omit:** Include when the composition has a wood base. Omit in purely powdery or floral-iris compositions where no wood bridge is needed.

### Synergy 5: ULTRALIA — THE GHOST LAYER
**Effect:** Ultralia creates an iris "halo" that extends around the perceptible iris chord. At proper dosing, it's not consciously detected as a separate note — it widens the perceived cloud of iris, making the composition feel more 3D. Think of it as atmospheric fog around the iris core.

| Material | Dilution | Whisper | Audible | Statement | Dominant |
|----------|----------|---------|---------|-----------|----------|
| | | 10 / 30 / 50 / 100 | 10 / 30 / 50 / 100 | 10 / 30 / 50 / 100 | 10 / 30 / 50 / 100 |
| Ultralia | neat | 3/9/15/30 | 8/24/40/80 | 20/60/100/200 | 35/105/175/350 |

**Ratio to iris core:** Ultralia should be 1–3% of total iris concentrate volume. More than 5% and it starts to read as its own note rather than as a halo.

**Key synergies:**
- Ultralia + Musk Ketone (10% DPG): The powder-ghost combination. Musk Ketone provides tangible powder; Ultralia provides the invisible iris cloud around it. Together: vintage face-powder-counter effect.
- Ultralia + Hedione: Hedione pushes the ghost layer outward — wider sillage iris halo. The composition seems to project iris even when the actual iris dosing is modest.
- Ultralia + Ethylene Brassylate: The ghost meets the skin-musk. Creates "iris on warm skin" perception.

**Overdose:** Above 5% of iris concentrate → Ultralia reads as a cheap synthetic ionone instead of a subliminal halo. The magic is in the barely-there zone.

## PART 3: IRIS + NON-IRIS SYNERGIES — CRITICAL PARTNERS

### Synergy 9: IRIS + HEDIONE (The Radiance Engine)
**Effect:** Hedione pushes iris materials outward around the wearer. Without radiance amplification, iris accords sit close to skin and read as intimate-only. Hedione at proper dosing creates a 3D "iris cloud" that projects without becoming loud.

| Material | Dilution | Whisper | Audible | Statement | Dominant |
|----------|----------|---------|---------|-----------|----------|
| | | 10 / 30 / 50 / 100 | 10 / 30 / 50 / 100 | 10 / 30 / 50 / 100 | 10 / 30 / 50 / 100 |
| Hedione | neat | 40/120/200/400 | 100/300/500/1000 | 200/600/1000/2000 | 350/1050/1750/3500 |

**Ratio to iris chord:** Hedione should be 15–20% of the total iris accord concentrate. Below 10%: iris stays close to skin. Above 25%: Hedione's own jasmonic-floral character becomes audible and dilutes the iris identity.

**Hedione vs. Hedione HC:** Hedione HC (high cis) is more powerfully jasmonic. For iris work, standard Hedione is preferred — it provides radiance without jasmine intrusion. Hedione HC at half-dose can substitute when you want slight jasmonic warmth blended into the iris.

**Critical formula positions (from Opus V Crystal):** Hedione at 17% of 1000 µL accord = 170 µL per 10 mL batch equivalent.

### Synergy 10: IRIS + MUSK KETONE (The Powder Echo)
**Effect:** Musk Ketone is the ONLY musk that directly reinforces iris powder. All other musks provide generic skin-adhesion or projection — Musk Ketone actually echoes the talcum-dry face-powder quality that AIMI and Ultralia create. Forms a closed feedback loop: AIMI creates powder → Musk Ketone echoes powder → the composition reads as "powdery throughout" rather than "iris with musk."

| Material | Dilution | Whisper | Audible | Statement | Dominant |
|----------|----------|---------|---------|-----------|----------|

> *Crystalline solid. Pre-dilute to 10% in DPG before use. Values shown assume 10% dilution.*

**Without Musk Ketone:** The powder effect stays in the heart only (from AIMI/Ultralia). With Musk Ketone: powder extends into the base and creates a continuous powder trail. This is the difference between "iris perfume" and "powdery iris perfume."

**Partner chain:** AIMI (powder generator) → Ultralia (powder ghost) → Musk Ketone (powder echo in musk layer) → Ethylene Brassylate (creamy skin-adhesion underneath). This is the complete powder architecture.
