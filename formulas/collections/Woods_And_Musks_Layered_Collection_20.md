# Woods And Musks Layered Collection — 20 Ideas for 30 mL Bottles
**Date:** 2026-04-02  
**Batch Size:** 30.00 mL each  
**Concentration:** 30% extrait-style structure  
**Concentrate:** 9.0 mL  
**Ethanol 96%:** 21.0 mL

> **Design rule:** these formulas are engineered so woods and musks remain the only clearly perceivable architecture. Any citrus, aldehyde, spice, incense, smoke, or floral material is present only as trace polish or edge-shaping.
>
> **Layering principle:** each formula uses a release ladder rather than a single wood or single musk.
> - **Lift woods / opening frame:** Iso E Super, Cedramber, aldehydes, tiny bergamot or rose-oxide traces
> - **Heart woods / textile body:** Bacdanol, Ebanol, Sandalore, Koavone, Javanol, Cashmeran, Suederal
> - **Drydown anchors:** Timberol, Norlimbanol Dextro, Vertofix, Vetival, Clearwood, Amberwood F, Evernyl
> - **Musk ladder:** Habanolide, Zenolide, Romandolide, Galaxolide, Ethylene Brassylate, Exaltolide, Macrolide, Tonalide, Musk Ketone
>
> **Use note:** all percentages below are of the **9.0 mL concentrate**. At this batch size, **1% = 90 uL** of your stock material.
>
> **Current inventory note:** this regenerated set removes earlier `Ambrettolide` and `Vertofix Coeur` references. Their roles are redistributed across `Exaltolide`, `Macrolide`, `Zenolide`, `Habanolide`, and `Vertofix`.
>
> **Coverage goal:** across this set, the collection uses essentially the full available woods/amber/structure and musk palette so you can study different release stories rather than one repeated base.

## Top 5 Must-Builds

1. **LW-14 Atelier Blonde**  
   Best overall balance of elegance, texture, and wearability in the current set.
2. **NM-10 Smoked Paper Woods**  
   Strongest niche concept with real character and a stable structure.
3. **NM-03 Vetiver Lattice**  
   Best green-dark woody architecture with excellent longevity.
4. **LW-16 Ambered Dry Woods**  
   Best warm luxury option without drifting into sweetness.
5. **LW-17 Velvet Vetiver Musk**  
   Best tailored elegant woody-musky build.
