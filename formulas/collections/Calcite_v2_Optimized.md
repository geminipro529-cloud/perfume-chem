## Optimization Rationale

### Why NOT Dihydromyrcenol
DHM (3,7-dimethyloct-6-en-1-ol) is the defining molecule of the sport-fresh genre: Cool Water (1988), Sauvage (2015), every aquatic-sport fragrance since the late '80s. Its metallic-citrus-brisk character would hijack Calcite's Ellena-minimalist mineral identity and push it toward generic designer-masculine territory. The formula needs radiance lift that stays **crystal-metallic-botanical**, not brisk-metallic-aquatic.

### The Replacement: Cyclamen Aldehyde
2-Methyl-3-(4-isopropylphenyl)propanal. Metallic-green-floral with a crystalline mineral quality. Provides the same radiance function as DHM (metallic sparkle, top-note lift) but with a green-floral-mineral character that **reinforces** Calcite's transparent blossom heart:

- Cyclamen Aldehyde + Cedrat FCF = mineral-crystalline citron (Ellena-approved pairing)
- Cyclamen Aldehyde + Nympheal + Lilyreal = green-metallic muguet shimmer (not sport-fresh muguet)
- Cyclamen Aldehyde + Hexyl Salicylate = metallic-green transparency over salicylate cushion

### What Each New Material DOES

**Neroli EO (120 µL, neat) — Indolic Citrus-Floral Depth**
- THE luxury citrus in perfumery — distilled bitter orange blossom. Contains trace indole, linalool, linalyl acetate, nerol, geraniol — actual molecular complexity vs. bergamot's simpler terpene profile
- Replaces Bergamot FCF: Bergamot rounds and softens (every cologne uses it). Neroli deepens and complicates. The indolic undertone creates a citrus-floral bridge that connects directly into the muguet heart (Nympheal, Lilyreal)
- Known synergies: Neroli + Hedione = the radiance engine from Eau Sauvage and Hermès school — luminous jasmonoid volume amplified by neroli's linalool
- Neroli + Cedrat FCF = mineral-indolic-bitter citrus — the most sophisticated citrus pairing in niche perfumery (see: Bigarade Concentrée, Concentré d'Orange Verte)
- Neroli + Cyclamen Aldehyde = metallic-indolic shimmer — the two metallic materials interact to create a faceted crystalline opening
- At 120 µL (~4.7% of concentrate), neroli provides depth without dominating — you smell the structural effect, not "orange blossom"

**Petitgrain EO (80 µL, neat) — Bitter-Leaf Architecture**
- Distilled bitter orange leaf and twig — NOT the fruit, NOT the flower. This is the intellectual citrus modifier
- Replaces Blood Orange: Blood Orange is designer-fruity and too sweet for Calcite's mineral concept (already dropped in 30mL version). Petitgrain adds the dry, green-bitter, almost herbal-woody dimension from Bigarade Concentrée (Malle/Ellena)
- Pairs with Cedrat FCF for bitter-mineral citrus architecture — the same pairing Ellena uses extensively
- At 80 µL, it structures the citrus opening with intellectual dryness rather than fruity sweetness

**Cyclamen Aldehyde (50 µL, neat) — Metallic-Green Radiance Lift**
- Functional replacement for DHM without the sport-fresh character
- Creates the metallic shimmer above the Cedrat FCF citron opening
- Bridges citrus → floral through its green-floral facet
- Known pairing: Cyclamen Aldehyde + muguet synthetics (Lilyreal, Nympheal) = green-metallic lily-of-the-valley with crystalline edge
- At 50 µL (2% of concentrate) it's a modifier, not a dominant — you smell the *effect* not the material

**Aldehyde C12 MNA (50 µL of 1%) — Mineral Halo Sparkle**
- 0.5 µL active aldehyde = the definition of trace-level
- Creates the luminous "expensive skin" aura — the same olfactive code as Chanel No. 5's aldehyde halo but with mineral-waxy rather than soapy character
- C12 MNA specifically is the metallic-mineral aldehyde (vs. C11's fatty-waxy)
- At trace, it doesn't read as "aldehydic" — it reads as "luminous"
- Synergy: C12 MNA + Amberwood F = mineral-architectural depth

**Benzyl Salicylate (80 µL, neat) — Diffusion Cushion + Fixative Bridge**
- Creates the double-salicylate base with Hexyl Salicylate (already present at 80 µL)
- Hexyl Sal = lighter, green-floral transparent fixative
- Benzyl Sal = heavier, diffusion engine, cosmetic-clean character
- Together: the blossom heart has a **cushion** to sit on — it doesn't just evaporate off the woody base
- Known synergy: Hedione + Benzyl Salicylate = the radiance-diffusion engine of modern niche perfumery (Narciso Rodriguez For Her DNA)
- Also extends the longevity of the blossom heart materials (Nympheal, DBCA, Freesia HDI all sit on the salicylate matrix)

**Hedione Increase (160 → 280 µL) — Radiance Amplifier Push**
- At 160 µL in the original, Hedione was present but not assertive in a 2290 µL concentrate (~7%)
- At 280 µL in 2530 µL (~11.1%), it becomes the dominant jasmonoid engine — the fragrance's *texture* rather than a background note
- +60 µL from the optimization push, +60 µL from Paradisamide volume redistribution
- Hedione + Aldehyde C12 MNA = luminous halo (the aldehydes give sparkle, Hedione gives 3D volume)
- Hedione + Cyclamen Aldehyde = metallic-floral bloom (the aldehyde opens, Hedione makes the opening persist)

## Tuning Notes

**If the metallic-green opening is too assertive:**
- Reduce Cyclamen Aldehyde from 50 → 30 µL. It's potent — 30 µL may be sufficient for the metallic shimmer without dominating.

**If you want more mineral character:**
- Add Scentenal (1%) at 30 µL — metallic-green ozone at ppb trace level. Reinforces the "Calcite" mineral effect beneath the Cyclamen Aldehyde. Optional because it's borderline sub-threshold at this dose.

**If the blossom heart needs more body:**
- Increase DBCA from 100 → 130 µL. DBCA's gardenia-rose accord creates floral body that the salicylate cushion can project.

**If longevity disappoints on skin:**
- Increase Benzyl Salicylate from 80 → 120 µL. It's both fixative and diffusion engine — more of it makes the blossom heart persist without changing the character.
