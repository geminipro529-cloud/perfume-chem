# MASS-MARKET SIX — One Perfume Per Olfactory Family

**Date:** 2026-05-02
**Format:** 6 × 30 mL — one perfume drawn from each of the 6 main olfactory families, each anchored in one of the top-3 mass-market popular sub-designations of its parent family.
**Positioning:** mass-market, not niche/luxury. Concentrate strengths kept at EDT 10–12 % or EDP 15–17 %. Material counts 18–28 per formula (vs. 35–45 for the niche-luxury builds in this collection). Recognizable reference targets named for each.
**Constraint:** all 6 use only materials currently in [inventory.txt](../../inventory.txt) as of 2026-04-30. Aroma chemicals + naturals + FTEC/FO accord bases all permitted (mass-market houses use these freely).

## Mass-market vs. niche trade-offs explicitly accepted

- **Galaxolide used in 5 of 6** despite the "laundry-synthetic" critique — because mass-market audiences read this as "clean perfume." (The niche/luxury formulas in this collection like Iris-Jasmine 15 mL deliberately rejected Galaxolide as projection musk; this collection embraces it.)
- **FTECs used liberally** (Pink Pepper Base, Tobacco FTEC, Blackcurrant FTEC, Cardamom FTEC, Black Pepper FTEC, Dewberry FTEC) because mass-market houses use FTECs as standard cost-control tools.
- **No α-Irone, no Javanol, no Polysantol, no Ambrettolide, no Suederal** — the iris/luxury sandalwood/luxury musk inventory is reserved for niche-luxury builds. This collection sticks to ≤ $0.50/g raw materials wherever possible.
- **Concentrate strengths intentionally lower** than this project's niche builds (which typically run 22–28 %). Mass-market EDT 10–12 % and EDP 15–17 % match commercial product norms.

## Compounding notes (apply to all 6)

Each formula assumes the dilutions listed are already on hand from the project's standard pre-dilution stock. Compounding sequence per formula:

1. Open empty 30 mL bottle on tared scale or volumetric pipette setup.
2. Pipette **base layer** materials first (highest-MW, lowest-volatility) — gives them maximum maceration time.
3. Pipette **heart layer** next — naturals (Patchouli EO, Vetiver EO, Lavender EO etc.) before aroma chemicals to let them disperse into the lipid matrix.
4. Pipette **top layer** last (citruses, aldehydes) to minimize their volatile loss during compounding.
5. Add ethanol 96 % to the 30 mL fill line, cap, invert 50×.
6. Macerate **3–4 weeks** for the 4 mass-market builds without indolic content (Pink Pearls, North Compass, Glacial Field, Surface Calm); **6 weeks** for Velvet Bloom and Plum Velvet (indole/heavy-resin builds need indole-resin integration time).

## Why these specific sub-family choices

- **Floral → Fruity Floral** rather than White Floral or Powdery: Fruity Floral has been the #1 women's-launch sub-family every year since 2010. (White Floral is more polarizing; Powdery is a niche-leaning sub-family in the 2020s mass market.)
- **Oriental → Vanilla Amber** rather than Spicy Amber or Floral Amber: vanilla-gourmand orientals (Black Opium, La Vie est Belle, Good Girl) drive the bulk of women's amber-family revenue. Spicy Amber leans more masculine; Floral Amber is a smaller commercial slice.
- **Woody → Aromatic Woody** rather than Sandalwood or Mossy Woody: Sauvage and Bleu de Chanel single-handedly anchor men's mass-market 2015–2025. Sandalwood (Egoiste-style) is more niche-leaning today; Mossy Woody (Aramis-style) is heritage-only.
- **Chypre → Fruity Chypre** rather than Floral Chypre or Green Chypre: post-2009 oakmoss restriction reset the chypre family — Fruity Chypre (Coco Mademoiselle, Sì) became the dominant modern chypre because the fruit + patchouli + Evernyl signature reads correctly to mass audiences without true oakmoss.
- **Fougère → Aromatic Fougère** rather than Sweet Fougère or Fresh Fougère: the lavender + coumarin + woody-mossy aromatic fougère is the textbook men's mass-market structure (Cool Water, Brut, Drakkar Noir, Azzaro). Sweet Fougère (Pour Homme Intense) and Fresh/Aquatic Fougère are smaller sub-slices.
- **Fresh → Aquatic** rather than Citrus or Green: Aquatic anchors the summer/unisex mass-market segment. Pure citrus (4711 cologne) is heritage-only; Green (Chanel No. 19 type) is much more niche-leaning today.
