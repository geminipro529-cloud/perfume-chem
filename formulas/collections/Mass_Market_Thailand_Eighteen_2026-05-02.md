# MASS-MARKET EIGHTEEN — Thailand-Anchored, 6 Families × 3 Sub-Families

**Date:** 2026-05-02
**Format:** 18 × 30 mL EDT/EDP — 3 mass-market sub-families per main olfactory family.
**Market positioning:** Thailand mass-market (Mistine, Karmart, Twelve Plus, BSC, Eveandboy private-label tier; 99–890 THB price band) with selective use of mid-tier materials. Most builds also reference a global mass-market touchstone for shape recognition.
**Climate envelope:** Bangkok ambient 28–35 °C, RH 65–85 %, indoor AC 22–25 °C — favors aquatic/citrus/light floral/powder; disfavors heavy oriental cloy. Concentrations skew lower than Western mass market: EDT 8–10 %, EDP 12–15 %, with only Floral Amber and Vanilla Amber pushing 15 %.
**Inventory constraint:** [inventory.txt](../../inventory.txt) as of 2026-04-30; FTECs/FOs permitted (mass-market tools).

## Why these specific 18 sub-family choices for Thailand

- **Floral × 3:** White Floral (jasmine garlands are everywhere), Fruity Floral (teen/young-women dominant), Powdery Floral (Thai "น้ำหอมแป้ง" cultural category — strong) — chosen over Aldehydic Floral (too 1980s for Thai taste) and Soliflore (too mono).
- **Oriental × 3:** Vanilla Amber (gourmand-popular, Black Opium copies dominate), Floral Amber (champaca/temple direction is regionally authentic), **Soft / Skin Amber** (Alien / Narciso For Her are top-5 Thai prestige feminines — distinct from Vanilla Amber by being lower on sweetness, higher on Cashmeran-musk-iris) — chosen over Spicy Amber (lower commercial volume in Thailand outside niche) and Tobacco Oriental (niche-only in Thailand).
- **Woody × 3:** Sandalwood (cultural anchor — temple sandalwood), Aromatic Woody (Sauvage-tier dominates men's mass), Soft Creamy Woody (Cashmeran-led skin-scent — fast-growing) — chosen over Mossy Woody (heritage-only) and Leather (niche).
- **Chypre × 3:** Fruity Chypre (modern feminine dominant — Coco Mademoiselle / Sì), Floral Chypre (sophisticated office-wear — Mon Guerlain), **Patchouli Chypre** (Coco Noir / Coromandel-direction — patchouli-anchored modern chypre is the post-2010 commercial powerhouse, with cultural patchouli-affinity in Thailand) — chosen over Green Chypre (heritage with smaller commercial volume), Aromatic Chypre (overlaps Fougère), and Leather Chypre (niche).
- **Fougère × 3:** Aromatic Fougère (lemongrass-localized), Fresh/Aquatic Fougère (Cool Water — peak Thai-beach-resort men's), Sweet Fougère (Pour Homme Intense — gift-pack tier) — chosen over Spicy Fougère (overlaps Spicy Amber) and Floral Fougère (rare commercially).
- **Fresh × 3:** Aquatic (peak Thai climate fit), Citrus (kaffir lime localized), Green (pandan localized) — chosen over Ozonic (overlaps Aquatic too much) and Mineral (niche-only).
