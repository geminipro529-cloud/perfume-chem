### Accord Architecture

- **Aldehydic sparkle (C10+C11+C12 MNA at 60 µL of 1% + C11 undecylenic 21 µL neat):** The four-aldehyde triad was deliberately seeded at audible dose (~0.6 µL active each + 21 µL undecylenic) so the Chanel-style soap-and-candle-wax sparkle reads as a clear character signature, not a whisper. C10 (waxy-orange), C11 (floral-fatty), C12 MNA (clean-waxy), C11 undecylenic (sharper, snappy).
- **Hesperidic frame (Bergamot Sicilian 51 + Neroli 50 + Linalool 60):** Bergamot FCF Sicilian picked over plain Bergamot FCF for the richer-complex bergamot identity suited to an aldehydic classicism. Neroli EO bridges citrus and white floral. Linalool extends the citrus-floral top with transparent lavender-muguet transparency.
- **White floral radiance (Hedione 183 + Hedione HC 120 + PEDMC 42 + Ylang 28 + ACA 35):** Hedione as the primary radiance amplifier / jasmine-volume pump; Hedione HC (cis-isomer enriched) for additional jasmine-pear diffusion; PEDMC as jasmine-indolic lift; Ylang at restrained dose so the oily-tropical facet supports rather than dominates; ACA as waxy-floral diffusant (kept ≤35 µL under IFRA allergen cap).
- **Rose/iris inflection (Alpha Irone 80 @ 30% + Rose Oxide 25 @ 10% + Alpha Damascone 25 @ 10% + Alpha Isomethyl Ionone 42):** Alpha Irone at 24 µL active for a substantial orris whisper; Rose Oxide for the metallic-green rose top-note tear; Alpha Damascone for warm-fruity rose depth; Alpha Isomethyl Ionone for powdery violet shim.
- **Salicylate cushion (Benzyl Sal 79 + Hexyl Sal 90):** Two-tier salicylate gradient — Hexyl Sal as the lighter transparent-floral adhesion layer sitting over Benzyl Sal's deeper cosmetic-waxy cushion. Total salicylate 169 µL / 10 mL = 1.7%, well under the IFRA combined cap.
- **4-axis musk chord (Ethylene Brassylate 200 + Habanolide 200 + Ambrettolide 120 @ 10% + Musk Ketone 34):** Ethylene Brassylate provides creamy-lactonic depth on skin; Habanolide adds warm-skin intimate trail; Ambrettolide (at 12 µL active) contributes the wine-musky-fruity naturalism; Musk Ketone echoes the powdery register of Alpha Isomethyl Ionone + Alpha Irone — Musk Ketone is the only musk here that directly reinforces powder, serving as the character-echo axis.
- **Amber-wood cocoon (Iso E Super 250 + Ambrox Super 15 @ 30% + Sandalore 34 + Cashmeran 30 @ 20% + Vetiver EO 50):** Iso E at maximum-cap 250 µL for the molecular skin-cocoon; Ambrox Super at trace (4.5 µL active) for crystalline mineral whisper without cold-over-cosmetic conflict; Sandalore as the light-fresh sandalwood support; Cashmeran for textile-warm powder underlining; Vetiver grounding the base with green-earth weight.
- **Sweet anchor (Vanillin 60 @ 10% + Coumarin 60 @ 20% + Ethyl Maltol 2 @ 10%):** Restrained gourmand anchor — Vanillin at 6 µL active, Coumarin at 12 µL active for the hay-tonka drydown, Ethyl Maltol at trace (0.2 µL) as a micro-caramelic bridge that lifts the sweet register without tipping into candy.

### Accord Architecture

- **Tight top (Bergamot Sicilian 61 + Pink Pepper Base 40 + Aldehyde C11 30 µL @ 1% + Linalool 60):** Deliberately minimal citrus so the rose-leather axis reads immediately. Bergamot FCF Sicilian picked for its richer bergamot body rather than generic Bergamot FCF; Pink Pepper Base adds a spicy-red-berry lift that prefigures the damascone rose; Aldehyde C11 at sub-trace (0.3 µL active) for a vintage lift signature; Linalool for linking the citrus and floral transitions.
- **3-damascone rose (Alpha Damascone 70 + Damascol 60 + Damascenone 12):** The triad is a proven luxury rose architecture — Alpha Damascone (7 µL active) for warm-fruity plum-rose; Damascol (6 µL active) for honeyed-jammy depth; Damascenone (0.12 µL active, at 1% dil) for the dried-apple-tea facet. Active damascone total ~13 µL — audible character without crossing into jammy overdose.
- **Rose-alcohol naturals (Rose Oxide 30 @ 10% + Geraniol 24 + Citronellol 39 + Phenethyl Alcohol 17):** Rose Oxide at 3 µL active for the metallic-green tear-of-rose top-note; Geraniol + Citronellol kept under IFRA allergen label caps (24+39 µL) to shape the natural rose alcohol component; PEA at a deliberately low 17 µL (cut from a v1 overdose of 120) — enough honey-rose body without the yeasty-rose overdose character.
- **Hedione engine (Hedione 310):** The single largest heart material — chosen specifically as radiance amplifier to drive the rose outward through the leather. Near the 350 µL cap but below it, kept under 35% of concentrate to prevent flattening.
- **Leather spine (Suederal 207 µL @ 10% = 20.7 µL active + IBQ 20 @ 10% = 2 µL active + Evernyl 10):** Suederal is the protagonist — a suede-leather accord material providing 20.7 µL active (0.9% of concentrate). IBQ at 2 µL active is in the *hint* zone (0.09% of concentrate) — enough for smoky-animalic shadow, below the harsh quinoline overdose threshold that killed v2/v3. Evernyl at exactly 10 µL neat = 0.1% of EDP, the IFRA Cat 4 ceiling, delivering oakmoss-chypre bone without violation. (Birch Tar dropped: scorer flags even 2 µL as creosote-overdose, and the smoke register is already covered by IBQ + Evernyl + Cedarwood.)
- **Salicylate gradient (Hexyl Sal 190 + Benzyl Sal 45):** Light-over-heavy gradient — Hexyl Sal as the dominant transparent-floral adhesion layer (1.9% of EDP, well under IFRA), Benzyl Sal in supporting role for cosmetic-waxy depth. Combined 235 µL = 2.35% of EDP, comfortably under the salicylate combined cap.
- **Wood-amber bed (Iso E Super 250 + Cedarwood Virginia 51 + Vetival 80 + Ebanol 30 + Ambermax 61 @ 50% + Ambrox Super 30 @ 30% + Cashmeran 105 @ 20%):** Iso E at max-cap 250 µL for molecular skin-cocoon. Ebanol picked over Javanol for the creamy-soft sandalwood register — Javanol's cold-dry character would fight the damascone rose; Ebanol's milky-yielding texture cradles it. Ambermax (30.5 µL active) for warm-rounded amber bed (NOT Ambrox-cold); Ambrox Super (9 µL active) supplies a minority crystalline facet for dimensional depth. - **3-axis musk chord (Ambrettolide 150 @ 10% + Habanolide 125 + Ethylene Brassylate 102):** Ambrettolide as the depth axis — 15 µL active macrocyclic providing wine-fruity natural skin warmth ("quiet skin warmth" signature). Habanolide as projection + intimate-skin bridge. EB as character-echo amplifying the creamy-lactonic register that links to Cashmeran and the salicylates. No Musk Ketone here (would pull toward powder, conflicting with leather), no Galaxolide (would pull toward laundry-clean, wrong register).
- **Micro-gourmand bridge (Ethyl Maltol 2 µL @ 10% = 0.2 µL active):** A homeopathic caramel whisper that lifts the rose-damascone warmth marginally without tipping into gourmand territory.

## Optimization Methodology

Both formulas were produced by an iterative optimize → critique → fix loop:

1. **v1 greedy optimizer** (geo across 9 axes, no safety weighting) → reached geo 83.9 / 83.6 but produced chemistry-illegal outputs (Iso E 600 µL, 41 ingredients, Birch Tar 10 µL).  
2. **v1 critique** identified: missing safety in weights, no caps, Ambrox+Ambrofix redundancy, trace-dose aldehydes inaudible, PEA/IBQ/Birch Tar overdose.  
3. **v2 optimizer** — added safety weight 1.2 + per-material caps + mutual-exclusion + MAX=30 ingredients + perfumery-seeded aldehyde doses. Discovered scoring-engine bug: `_science_ingredients` normalized concentrate µL → treated as final-product %, making every salicylate read as a critical IFRA violation.  
4. **v3 optimizer** — overrode scorer's broken safety axis with externally-computed `score_ifra_compliance` on the actual 10 mL batch. Both formulas reached geo ≥ 82.8 with safety ≥ 88. Remaining flags: Evernyl 0.25% IFRA violation, IBQ + Birch Tar overdose on Suede.  
5. **v4 targeted fix** — Evernyl 25→10 µL (= IFRA Cat 4 ceiling), IBQ 45→20 µL (exits overdose zone), Birch Tar dropped entirely (scorer flags even 2 µL; smoke register covered by IBQ + Evernyl + Cedarwood). Vetival + Suederal compensated for leather loss. Convergence reached: both formulas clean across IFRA, allergen labels, dose-response, psychophysics channel count, diffusion field distribution.
