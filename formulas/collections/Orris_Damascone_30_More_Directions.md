# Orris + Damascone — 30 More Deep Directions

**Date:** 2026-04-06  
**Purpose:** 30 additional direction ideas for your fixed `dry orris + damascone accord`.

## Ground Rule

The weird part is already the accord.

Everything around it should do one of these jobs:
- make it easier to wear
- give it depth and complexity
- frame it in a recognizable perfume family
- stay normal enough that the perfume still smells good

These are **direction briefs**, not final formulas yet.

If you later want to prototype them the default bottle logic is still:
- `4.50 mL` premade `orris + damascone accord`
- `3.00 mL` additions
- `22.50 mL` ethanol 96%

---

## Fruit / Floral / Cosmetic

### 1. Pear Muguet Skin
**Why:** clean pear-lily lift makes the accord feel polished instead of dusty.  
**Frame:** `Bourgeonal`, `Helional`, `Cyclamen Aldehyde`, `Hedione`, `Linalyl Acetate`, `Habanolide`, `Iso E Super`

### 2. Apricot Neroli Cream
**Why:** a soft apricot-orange blossom shell makes the accord feel plush and easy to like.  
**Frame:** `Apritone (10%)`, `Gamma Undecalactone`, `Neroli EO`, `Petitgrain EO`, `Heliotropin Fleuressence`, `Benzoin Resinoid (50% in DPG)`, `Ambrox Super (30% w/v)`

### 3. Blackcurrant Rose Satin
**Why:** dark fruity rose gives the accord a richer, more dressed heart without going old-fashioned.  
**Frame:** `Blackcurrant FTEC`, `Rose Oxide (10%)`, `Phenethyl Alcohol (PEA)`, `Geraniol`, `Citronellol`, `Peonile`, `Hexyl Salicylate`, `Galaxolide (80%)`

### 4. Mandarin Peony Glow
**Why:** bright peony-citrus makes the center feel modern and luminous.  
**Frame:** `Red Mandarin EO`, `Bergamot FCF oil Sicilian`, `Peonile`, `Hedione`, `Benzyl Acetate`, `Aurantiol`, `Habanolide`, `Iso E Super`

### 5. Berry Musk Petal
**Why:** fruity-musky softness gives depth without relying on heavier woods or resins.  
**Frame:** `Dewberry FTEC`, `Blackcurrant FTEC`, `Peonile`, `Methyl Anthranilate`, `Romandolide`, `Galaxolide (80%)`, `Hedione`

### 6. Solar Coconut Bloom
**Why:** creamy tropical floral framing turns the accord warm and plush instead of rooty-strange.  
**Frame:** `Gamma Decalactone`, `Delta Decalactone`, `Allyl Amyl Glycolate`, `Ylang Comoros Complete EO F3255`, `Heliotropin Fleuressence`, `Hedione`, `Benzyl Acetate`, `Habanolide`

### 7. Peach Skin Sandal
**Why:** soft lactonic fruit plus sandalwood makes the accord feel silky and expensive.  
**Frame:** `Gamma Undecalactone`, `Delta Decalactone`, `Sandalore`, `Javanol`, `Hedione`, `Galaxolide (80%)`, `Ethylene Brassylate`

### 8. Dewberry Blossom Musk
**Why:** juicy berry top over clean musks gives the accord a smoother, friendlier silhouette.  
**Frame:** `Dewberry FTEC`, `Peonile`, `Hedione`, `Galaxolide (80%)`, `Romandolide`, `Habanolide`, `Iso E Super`
