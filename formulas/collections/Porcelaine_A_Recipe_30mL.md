0# Porcelaine Poudre — Version A — 30 mL Mixing Recipe

**Batch:** 30.00 mL · 27 % EdP · 23 materials  
**No IFRA adjustment applied** (personal-use batch)

---

## What You Need

- 1 × 30 mL glass spray bottle (amber or clear)
- 1 mL graduated glass pipettes (for large pours)
- Micro-pipettes or 1 mL syringes with 0.01 mL marks (for small pours)
- Small glass beaker or vial for dissolving Musk Ketone
- Milligram scale (for Musk Ketone powder)
- Ethanol 96 % (perfumer's grade or pharma-grade)

---

## Mixing Steps

### A — Dissolve Musk Ketone first

Musk Ketone is a crystalline powder. It must be fully dissolved before adding anything else.

1. Measure **30 mg** of Musk Ketone powder on the milligram scale.
2. Pour roughly **5 mL** of ethanol 96 % into your beaker.
3. Drop the Musk Ketone into the ethanol.
4. Swirl gently for 2–3 minutes until no crystals remain. It dissolves easily at this concentration (~6 mg/mL, well under solubility limit).
5. Set the beaker aside — this is your "ethanol + MK pre-mix."

---

### G — Add the ethanol + Musk Ketone

1. Pour the **MK pre-mix** (from step A) into the bottle.
2. Measure the remaining ethanol: you used ~5 mL for the pre-mix, so add another **≈ 16.89 mL** of ethanol 96 % to bring the total ethanol to **21.89 mL**.
3. Cap the bottle tightly.

**Final volume: 8.11 mL concentrate + 21.89 mL ethanol = 30.00 mL**

---

### H — Seal and mature0

1. **Cap tightly.** Invert the bottle 10 times — do not shake hard.
2. Store upright, away from light, at room temperature (~20–25 °C).
3. **Invert 10 times** again at day 1, day 3, and day 7.
4. **Minimum maturation: 2 weeks** before evaluating on skin.
5. **Optimum: 3–4 weeks** — the Musk Ketone + Coumarin + Heliotropin powder matrix fully forms by week 3.

## Mixing Order Summary

```
Base → Salicylates → Heart florals → Powder → Top → Ethanol+MK
```

Heavy/slow materials first, light/fast materials last, ethanol last of all. This gives the base time to pre-blend before volatiles are introduced.
