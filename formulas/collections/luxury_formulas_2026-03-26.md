## 7. Porcelaine — Luxury Cosmetic Plastic Floral

**Accord Profile:** Salicylate Cushion · DBCA Gardenia-Rose · Muguet Transparency · Hedione Radiance → Clean Amber-Musk Skin Effect  
**Commercial Reference:** Byredo Blanche × Narciso Rodriguez For Her (the "cosmetic plastic" genre perfected)

Linalyl Acetate provides the laundered lift that reads as "expensive clean." Hedione at heavy dose expands the floral bouquet into three-dimensional radiance. The base is minimal by design: Iso E Super as molecular cocoon, Ambrox for crystalline mineral depth, Habanolide for clean-skin musk. Wears close, linear, addictive — like burying your face in freshly laundered high-thread-count sheets.

## Mixing Notes

1. **Order of addition:** Add ethanol last. Dispense concentrate materials largest-volume-first, then work down to trace amounts. This minimizes error accumulation.
2. **Maceration:** Allow a minimum of 2 weeks before evaluating. These are dense formulas — the amber and musk bases in particular need 4–6 weeks to fully marry.
3. **Viscous materials (Benzoin Resinoid, Birch Tar):** Pre-warm gently to 30–35°C to reduce viscosity before pipetting.
4. **Crystalline solids (Ethyl Vanillin):** Weigh directly in mg or pre-dissolve into a 10% ethanol solution for pipetting convenience.
5. **Micropipette technique:** Use fresh tips between materials. For EOs, pipette quickly — they evaporate rapidly from the tip.
