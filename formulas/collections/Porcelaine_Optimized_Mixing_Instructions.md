# Porcelaine — Step-by-Step Mixing Instructions (Optimized)

**Finished Volume:** 10.00 mL Eau de Parfum  
**Concentrate:** 3.48 mL (20 materials) + **Carrier:** 6.52 mL ethanol  
**Effective Concentration:** ~26% EdP (1.00 mL of concentrate is aldehyde dilution solvent)  
**Active Aroma Load:** 2.48 mL  
**Difficulty:** Beginner-Intermediate — micropipette + syringe work, no viscous resinoids  

**Formula Identity:** Luxury Cosmetic Plastic Floral — the scent of expensive beauty products. Not a specific flower, but the abstract floral effect created when salicylates meet muguet synthetics and DBCA's gardenia-rose character. Aldehydic sparkle amplifies radiance. Wears close, linear, addictive — like burying your face in freshly laundered high-thread-count sheets.

**Commercial Reference:** Byredo Blanche × Narciso Rodriguez For Her

## Phase 1 — Clean Musk Foundation (build the invisible skin bed)

Musks bloom slowly. Dispense these first and let them start marrying while you build the rest. These three materials create the "clean laundry" base that makes Porcelaine feel expensive on skin.

## Phase 3 — Cosmetic Plastic Floral Core (the soul of Porcelaine)

This **IS** Porcelaine. Add in order of tenacity — heaviest fixatives first, most transparent materials last. The salicylate cushion goes in first because it's both the fixative AND the primary character material.

## Phase 5 — Carrier System

| Step | Material | mL | Tool | Purpose |
|------|----------|----|------|---------|
| 21 | **Ethanol 96%** | **6.52** | Cyl | Primary carrier. Pour into the concentrate, stir for 60 seconds until fully homogeneous. |

**Grand total:** 3.48 mL concentrate + 6.52 mL ethanol = **10.00 mL EdP**

> **Volume accounting:** The 1.00 mL of DPG from the aldehyde dilutions (Steps 15–16) acts as a secondary carrier within the concentrate — smooth, slightly viscous, helps musks radiate. Total carrier system: 1.00 mL embedded DPG + 6.52 mL ethanol = 7.52 mL effective carrier.

### Optional: Carrier Enhancement

For improved skin feel and extended musk radiance, replace 1.00 mL of ethanol with performance enhancers. This is particularly effective for Porcelaine's skin-scent character:

| Material | mL | Purpose |
|----------|----|---------|
| Isopropyl Myristate (IPM) | 0.50 | Skin-feel enhancer, co-fixative, reduces alcohol bite. Makes the "cosmetic" character feel more like actual skin cream |
| Triethyl Citrate (TEC) | 0.50 | Longevity extender, smooth drydown. Helps the musk cloud radiate more evenly |
| Ethanol 96% | 5.52 | Remaining carrier |

> **If using IPM + TEC:** Add them to the concentrate FIRST. Stir 30 seconds. Then add ethanol in 2 pours (~2.7 mL each), swirling between pours.

## Olfactive Profile (Week 2+)

### Opening (0–15 min)
Aldehydic shimmer over laundered citrus. C12 MNA creates a metallic-warm halo while C11 adds waxy roundness. Dihydromyrcenol provides modern metallic freshness. Bergamot FCF Sicilian frames the citrus without dominating. But it's Linalyl Acetate that delivers the true character here — the "clean laundry on expensive sheets" impression. The aldehydes make this opening read as fine fragrance, not fabric softener.

### Heart (15 min – 2 hours)
The cosmetic-plastic floral core in full bloom. DBCA's gardenia-rose character meets the dual salicylate cushion — Benzyl Salicylate providing heavy diffusive volume while Hexyl Salicylate adds a sheer, transparent skin layer above it. Hedione at 300 µL creates three-dimensional radiance: the florals hover around you, not sitting flat on skin. The muguet triad (Nympheal bloom + Lilyreal body + Hydroxycitronellal wax) with Freesia transparency and Bourgeonal's watermelon-lily facet. This is Porcelaine's identity phase: abstract, polished, unmistakably "cosmetic."

### Drydown (2–6 hours)
Clean abstract base. Iso E Super molecular cocoon creates "skin but better" — the effect you notice when someone smells amazing and you can't identify why. Ambermax adds ambergris depth without reading as amber. The musk triad (Habanolide clean skin + Galaxolide laundry projection + Cashmeran cashmere warmth) creates intimate-but-present sillage. Not detectably woody, smoky, or animalic — pure skin effect with salicylate memory.

### Far Drydown (6–12 hours)
Sheer musk veil with salicylate ghost. Benzyl Salicylate's fixative power keeps a cosmetic-clean aura on skin — the last thing you'll smell at the end of the day. Very intimate, close-wearing, addictive. The aldehydes are long gone but the musks remember them.

## Troubleshooting

### "Too soapy / fabric softener"
- **Cause:** Linalyl Acetate or Hydroxycitronellal dominating, or insufficient DBCA character
- **Fix:** Mature 2 weeks. If persistent at Week 2: increase DBCA by 30 µL next batch (it's the gardenia-rose character that separates Porcelaine from generic clean-floral)

### "I don't smell the florals, just musk / nothing"
- **Cause:** Partial anosmia to Hedione and/or Iso E Super (very common — both are partially anosmic)
- **Fix:** Ask someone else to evaluate. These materials register subconsciously even when you can't consciously detect them. Others will smell a beautiful floral; you may only smell "clean skin"

### "Aldehydes too sharp / metallic"
- **Cause:** C12 MNA unmellowed, or evaluation too early
- **Fix:** Needs 2+ weeks. C12 MNA integrates slowly into the salicylate cushion. If still metallic at Week 3: reduce C12 MNA to 300 µL in next batch

### "Too weak / close to skin"
- **Cause:** By design — Porcelaine is a skin-scent composition. Iso E Super + Habanolide create intimate projection
- **Fix:** If you want more throw: +50 µL Benzyl Salicylate (more diffusion cushion) and +30 µL Galaxolide (more musk projection)

### "Smells generic / like any clean floral"
- **Cause:** DBCA understaged or masked by aldehydes
- **Fix:** DBCA is the character-defining material. Increase to 180 µL next batch. Also check that your DBCA bottle hasn't oxidized

### "Hedione smells watery / synthetic"
- **Cause:** Fresh Hedione can read as slightly metallic-watery before marriage
- **Fix:** Hedione + salicylates + time = radiant bloom. At Week 2 the watery edge disappears completely
