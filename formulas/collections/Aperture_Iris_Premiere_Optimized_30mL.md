## Design Goal

This is the revised premiere shell for a bright iris launch:
- crystalline rather than dark
- luxurious rather than sporty
- iris-first, with the shell acting as framing glass
- no `Jasmine FO`

The floral illusion is carried by:
- `Nympheal`
- `Bourgeonal`
- `Hedione`
- `Hexyl Salicylate`
- trace `Rose Oxide`

The musk frame is upgraded toward a more luxurious clean register:
- `Habanolide`
- `Romandolide`
- a reduced `Galaxolide`

The woods stay polished and transparent:
- `Iso E Super`
- `Cedramber`
- `Clearwood`
- low `Ambrofix`

## Why These Swaps

### Musks

The graph-backed luxury fit for this brief is:
- `Habanolide` as the clean structural musk
- `Romandolide` as the most luxurious bright-premiere fit
- `Galaxolide` only as a small familiar bridge

I did not add `Ethylene Brassylate` to the main shell. It reads smoother and more powdery, but for this brief it softens the crystal edge more than I want.

### Floral Locks

`Jasmine FO` was removed. The jasmine illusion is now implied by:
- `Nympheal` for luminous floral lift
- `Bourgeonal` for transparent muguet-like polish
- `Hedione` for diffusion and brightness
- `Hexyl Salicylate` for a soft cosmetic floral cushion
- `Rose Oxide` for a cool metallic floral edge

### Woods

The wood frame is intentionally light:
- `Iso E Super` for the main crystal-wood scaffold
- `Cedramber` for dry polished persistence
- `Clearwood` for a cleaner vetiver-patchouli style veil
- `Ambrofix` only as a small ambergris-like extender

That keeps the shell bright and jewel-like instead of warm and heavy.

## Mixing Order

1. Combine the iris center first: `Opus V Iris Crystal`.
2. Add the main wood scaffold: `Iso E Super`, `Clearwood`, `Cedramber`, `Ambrofix`.
3. Add the musk frame: `Habanolide`, `Romandolide`, `Galaxolide`.
4. Add the bright floral lock: `Hedione`, `Nympheal`, `Bourgeonal`, `Hexyl Salicylate`.
5. Add citrus top materials: `Bergamot FCF`, `Cedrat FCF`, `Linalool`.
6. Add trace materials last: `Aldehyde C12 MNA`, `Rose Oxide`.
7. Add ethanol, mix thoroughly, then rest at least `7` days.

Best read is usually after `14` to `21` days.

## Optional Tweaks

If you want it even brighter:
- reduce `Iso E Super` by `50-100 uL`
- raise `Bergamot FCF` or `Cedrat FCF` by the same amount

If you want a slightly softer luxury skin effect:
- add `20-30 uL` `Javanol`
- remove the same amount from `Iso E Super`

If you want more floral glare:
- add `20 uL` `Phenethyl Alcohol`
- remove `20 uL` from `Galaxolide`
