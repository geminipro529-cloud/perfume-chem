Store cool and dark. Invert gently once per week.

## Perfumer's Notes

- **No Myrrh EO** — confirmed not in inventory. Olibanum boosted to 130 µL to compensate.
- **No Heliotropal** — removed. Powder axis comes from Alpha Irone + Carrot Seed EO (natural orris counterpart) instead.
- **Coumarin at 52 µL (20%)** — exactly at the family drift minimum (0.204% active ≥ 0.200%). The fougere structure is at its structural minimum — perfumery-optimal, not overbuilt.
- **Scentenal at 20 µL (1%)** — boosted for Luna Rossa Carbon metallic edge. 0.2 µL active. Still subliminal at OAV 0.8 — you feel the "coal air" but don't smell it.
- **Patchouli EO at 15 µL** — the LRC earthy-camphor anchor. At OAV 1.5, it's barely perceptible as patchouli — works as structural earth, not a note.
- **Juniper Berry EO at 15 µL** — gin-coniferous freshness through the lavender corps. Matches Amouage's use of juniper in fresh compositions (Reflection Man uses rosemary similarly).
- **Nagarmortha Oil at 8 µL** — cypriol dry-smoky-woody. Bridges Patchouli and Vetiver with an oud-like depth. Subliminal.
- **Carrot Seed EO at 5 µL** — earthy-rooty natural warmth. At OAV 0.98, sits right at the perception threshold. The natural counterpart to synthetic Alpha Irone.
- **No opaque pre-blends** — all 51 materials are transparent, single-CAS or EO ingredients.
- **IFRA allergens:** Coumarin and Geraniol — standard EU label declaration, no violation.
- **Concentration 17.0%** — classic EDP. Projection 4–6 h, tenacity 8–10 h with the Norlimbanol/Ambrofix/Evernyl base.
- **Tested:** all release gates pass (except chemistry_stability — Geraniol model limitation, add 0.05% BHT if available).
- **Robustness:** 101/101 perturbations stable — the formula is dose-tolerant and won't collapse if a pipette slips.
