## How to read the card

**All liquid µL amounts are approximate.** Supplier/other-lot relative-density references are used as nominal g/mL estimates, not measurements of the actual bottle. Rows marked † are working solutions: some use explicitly assumed densities and others use another supplier's reference. Their exact volumetric stock equivalence remains unresolved.

Conversion: `planning µL = source stock mass g / assumed stock density g/mL × 1000`.

Rounding is normally to 5 µL. Patchouli stays at 28 µL to avoid a larger rounding error. Piperonal remains a weighed solid. No gram-to-mL equality or solid-volume inference is used.
