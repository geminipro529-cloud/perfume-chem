# Niche House Iris-Sandalwood-Amber — Dior Homme Parfum 2025 Study — 30 mL EdP

**Historical reference:** Dior Homme Parfum 2025 (Francis Kurkdjian reformulation). The 2025 DHP kills the heavy leather-oud of the 2014 Demachy version and elevates iris back to the star — now riding on a transparent sandalwood-ambroxan base. Cleaner, brighter, more modern. A niche house would amplify the iris with premium orris, use real sandalwood materials, and add ambergris generously.

**2025 vs 2014:** Leather gone. Oud gone. Iris IS the star. Sandalwood replaces leather in the base. Ambroxan provides radiance. Much lighter and more transparent.

**Concentration:** 20.0% EdP · 6,000 µL concentrate in 30 mL
