## Dosing Notes

- **Iso E Super at 2,107 µL (29.3%)**: THE skeleton. FdB uses 45.5% — our 29% leaves room for DHP's leather-rose-oud additions while keeping the holographic cocoon. Below 1,800 µL: the Demachy effect collapses.
- **IBQ 10% at 420 µL**: 42 µL active = 0.58%. The leather anchor. At this dose: dark, smoky, dominant. Archetype requires >=0.74% raw = passes at 5.8% of concentrate as 10% dilution.
- **Birch Tar Rectified 10% at 80 µL**: 8 µL active = 0.11%. At 10% dilution in DPG — easy to pipette. Below 50 µL: no scorched leather. Above 100 µL: barbecue.
- **Galaxolide 50% at 680 µL**: FdB's 21.5% benchmark. With macrocyclic counterbalance, reads as warm-skin not laundry.
- **Orange Peel EO at 150 µL + Bergamot 100 + Mandarin 50 = 300 µL citrus**: Drives limonene solidly to #3 allergen. The "Italian orange" opening.
- **Farnesol at 15 µL neat**: 0.21% of concentrate. IFRA Category 4 limit: 1.0% in finished product → 0.21% × 24% concentration = 0.05% in finished product. Well within limits. Below 10 µL: imperceptible muguet.
- **Rose de Mai Absolute 10% at 220 µL**: 22 µL active = 0.31%. The lavender→rose swap IS DHP.
- **Maturation**: 8 weeks minimum. The DHP skeleton (Iso E + leather + ionones + birch tar + guaiacol + vanillin) requires longer integration than DHI. Test at 4 weeks: leather dominates harshly. At 8 weeks: burnt iris blooms, smoky vanilla integrates, leather softens into amber base.
