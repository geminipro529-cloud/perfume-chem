# Vert Éclat — Optimized Green Chypre Axis · 30 mL EDP

**Family:** Green Chypre (Mossy Woods × Green) — mass-market lane, green-axis optimized
**Bottle:** 30.00 mL Eau de Parfum
**Concentration:** 20 % concentrate in perfumer's ethanol
**Composition:** 6.00 mL concentrate (34 raw materials) + 24.00 mL ethanol

> **Design intent.** This formula is built on a single structural principle: **the green chypre axis is the identity, not a decoration layer.** Every material exists to serve either the green-chypre spine or the mass-market accessibility that makes it wearable. The F2 formula (Le Chypre Vert) has a lush 14-material floral heart that reads as a floral chypre with green topnotes. This formula strips the floral heart down to transparent jasmine-muguet radiance (Hedione HC + Hedione + Mayol + Hydroxycitronellal) and reinvests the material budget into a stronger, more persistent green character that carries from opening through drydown.

> **What changed vs F2:**
> - **Stronger green throughout:** Galbanum + Dynascone + Hexenol in the top; Verdox + Cyclamen Aldehyde + Parmavert in the heart extend green persistence beyond the first 20 minutes.
> - **Leaner floral heart:** No ACA, Benzyl Acetate, Ylang, PCME, Damascone, Damascol, Cis Jasmone, Geraniol, or Bourgeonal. The heart is transparent jasmine-muguet on Iz E Super velvet — the Cristalle trick — not a lush floral bouquet.
> - **Warmer amber base:** Ambermax + Azarbre replace Ambrofix. Green chypre reads best with warm rounded amber (the "moss on warm stone" effect), not cold crystalline amber.
> - **Coumarin in chypre dose:** 40 µL active — warm without crossing into fougère territory.
> - **No animalic shock materials:** No IBQ, Birch Tar, Indole, Skatole. Polished drydown for mass market.

> **The three structural chords:**
> 1. **Green Chypre Axis:** Galbanum → Hexenol → Dynascone → Verdox → Cyclamen Aldehyde → Evernyl → Patchouli → Vetiver → Cedarwood Virginia. Green character persists through all three pyramid stages, not just the top.
> 2. **Jasmonate Radiance + Muguet Transparency:** Hedione HC + Hedione (mustard/wine full-spectrum jasmonate; not defaulting — HC specifically for its depth dimension), with Mayol + Hydroxycitronellal as transparent muguet (the No. 19/Cristalle heart strategy).
> 3. **3-Axis Musk + Texture Cushion:** Galaxolide (projection axis) + Ethylene Brassylate (depth axis) + Ambrettolide (character-echo axis — warm-green naturalistic skin), with Iso E Super as molecular velvet and Cashmeran as textile warmth in the drydown.

## What it should smell like

- **0–15 min (top):** Bright bergamot-citrus opening with immediate galbanum stem-sap bitterness. Triplal adds a watery-aldehydic sparkle. Hexenol gives the "crushed leaf" flash. Cedrat provides mineral-citron tension. The Dynascone extends green persistence past the first 5 minutes. Aldehyde C12 MNA provides metallic-green effervescence. Reads "expensive green perfume" within 30 seconds.
- **15 min–2 hr (heart):** The jasmonate-muguet heart blooms. Hedione HC gives the full-spectrum radiance (mustard/wine dimension that regular Hedione lacks). Hydroxycitronellal + Mayol form a transparent muguet-lily axis (the No. 19/Cristalle trick). Verdox and Parmavert keep green character alive in the mid where it would normally vanish. Cyclamen Aldehyde adds metallic-green floral edge. Iso E Super's molecular velvet coats everything.
- **2 hr onward (heart→base bridge):** Iso E Super takes over as the texture spine. Evernyl's oakmoss character emerges alongside Patchouli's earthy darkness. Vetiver's dry persistence anchors the mossy-green character. The green axis is still reading — Dynascone's persistence ensures green character hasn't collapsed.
- **6 hr onward (base):** Mossy-warm-creamy drydown. Ebanol's sandalwood-musk creaminess reads as "luxury skin." Ambermax provides warm rounded amber (the "moss on warm stone" effect). Azarbre bridges cedar and amber seamlessly. Coumarin shadow keeps it warm without going fougère. Three musks alternate visibility through hours 6–14. Green echo from Ambrettolide's wine-like warmth and Dynascone's lingering green trace.

Expected performance on skin (median, 0.5 mL spray, dry skin):
- **Sillage:** 4–5 hr radiant projection
- **Longevity:** 10–14 hr
- **Drydown skin-cling:** 18+ hr (macrocyclic musks)

## If you want to adjust it later

If it is **too green**:
- Reduce Galbanum Resinoid by 20–30 µL of 10% (2–3 µL active)
- Reduce Dynascone by 10 µL of 10% (1 µL active)
- Raise Ambermax by 20–30 µL of 50%

If it is **too harsh/dry**:
- Raise Benzoin Resinoid by 30–50 µL of 50%
- Raise Ethylene Brassylate by 50 µL
- Lower Evernyl by 20–30 µL

If it is **too floral/lush** (reading more like F2):
- Reduce Hydroxycitronellal by 30 µL
- Reduce Hedione HC by 50 µL
- Raise Patchouli by 50 µL
- Raise Evernyl by 20 µL

If it **lacks green persistence past 2 hours**:
- Raise Dynascone by 10–15 µL of 10% (1–1.5 µL active increase)
- Raise Verdox by 30 µL
- Raise Parmavert by 15 µL

If it is **too clean/modern and not chypre enough**:
- Raise Patchouli by 50–80 µL
- Raise Evernyl by 30–50 µL
- Lower Hedione HC by 80–100 µL

If it is **too heavy/dark**:
- Raise Dihydromyrcenol by 30–50 µL
- Raise Bergamot FCF Sicilian by 30–50 µL
- Lower Patchouli by 50–80 µL
