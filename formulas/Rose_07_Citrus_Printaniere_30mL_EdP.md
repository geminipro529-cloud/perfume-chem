# Rose Citrus Printanière — 30mL EdP

## Character
A spring-fresh citrus rose where Rose de Mai Absolute is wrapped in a bright, effervescent citrus bouquet. This is the lightest, most transparent rose composition — a hesperidic-floral that prioritizes freshness and luminosity over depth or complexity. The citrus accord uses four distinct materials (Bergamot FCF Sicilian for richness, Blood Orange for juicy sweetness, Lime Distilled EO for cold tartness, Methyl Pamplemousse for grapefruit-rhubarb bite) to create a multi-dimensional opening that feels like walking through a citrus grove in bloom. The rose core is the leanest of all formulas (Geraniol at 60 µL, no Rhodinol, PEDMC for clean magnolia-rose) because the rose must shimmer through the citrus, not anchor it. Damascenone at trace levels provides just enough rose depth. The base is deliberately transparent — Sandalore, Ambrofix, and light Iso E Super create a weightless woody framework, while Zenolide's citrusy-fresh musk projection keeps the entire composition airy. Think Diptyque Eau Rose brightened with more citrus verve, or a fresher, more modern Annick Goutal Rose Absolue.

**Family**: Citrus Floral (fresh-rose) — multi-citrus top, rose-citrus heart, transparent musk-wood base

## Material Justification

- **Rose de Mai Absolute (10%)**: The natural rose anchor at 350 µL (35 µL active, 0.62% of concentrate). The lowest dose of any Rose formula because a citrus-rose must let the hesperidic materials dominate — the absolute provides the natural rose presence without competing for olfactory bandwidth. At 0.62% of concentrate, the absolute whispers rose rather than declaring it, creating the "rose through citrus" transparency that defines the concept.

- **Citronellol + Geraniol + PEA + PEDMC + Nerol**: The molecular rose accord — 5 materials but deliberately omitting Rhodinol. Citronellol (280) provides fresh-citrus-rose lift — the most citrus-compatible rose alcohol, whose natural citronellal character connects directly to the citrus top. Geraniol (60) at maximum — sweet-geranium body. PEA (340) fills honeyed-rose volume — reduced slightly from the usual 350-400 because less volume is needed when the citrus materials provide so much headspace. PEDMC (170) — boosted because its clean magnolia-rose character is more transparent and citrus-compatible than Rhodinol's geranium richness. Nerol (100) — boosted because Nerol's sweet citrusy-rose facet is the single best bridge between rose and citrus. The citrus-rose architecture is the one formula where the molecular rose ALCOHOLS (not damascones) are the primary character carriers.

- **Damascenone (1%) + Rose Oxide (10%)**: Minimal rose modifiers at 60 + 50 µL (0.6 + 5 µL active). Damascenone at the lowest dose of any formula — only 0.6 µL active — because the honeyed-woody depth it adds would pull the citrus-rose toward heavier territory. Rose Oxide at 5 µL active adds just enough metallic-green freshness to connect the rose to the Leafovert-Parmavert green accents. No Damascone Beta or Damascol because their fruity-rose character would compete with the citrus fruits.

- **Bergamot FCF Sicilian + Blood Orange Sicilian + Lime Distilled EO + Methyl Pamplemousse (10%)**: The multi-dimensional citrus bouquet — 4 materials, each contributing a different citrus register. Bergamot FCF Sicilian (400 µL) — chosen over standard Bergamot FCF because the Sicilian variant's richer, more complex character deserves to be the STAR citrus (the concept explicitly de-emphasizes bergamot-as-default). Blood Orange Sicilian (220 µL) provides juicy, sweet-tart warmth — the least terpenic citrus, adding fruit body without phototoxic furanocoumarin risk. Lime Distilled EO (120 µL) adds cold, tart lime freshness — the "gin and tonic" clarity that makes the opening feel effervescent. Methyl Pamplemousse at 80 µL of 10% (8 µL active) provides grapefruit-rhubarb tartness — the synthetic citrus edge that extends citrus tenacity beyond what any natural EO can achieve. Four citrus materials in one formula is justified because the concept IS a citrus soliflore with rose.

- **Leafovert + Parmavert**: The green accent at 20 + 15 µL. Leafovert provides sharp cut-grass freshness — the green leaf stem that makes the rose-citrus feel botanical rather than synthetic. Parmavert adds the green violet-leaf facet — a whisper of green that pairs with the ionone facets of the citrus (limes naturally contain ionone-related compounds). Both at trace doses because the citrus materials are the primary green element. Chosen over Dynascone (too powerful — would overwhelm) and Triplal (too watery-aldehydic — would fight the citrus).

- **Hedione HC**: Radiance amplifier at 560 µL — the highest Hedione dose of any Rose formula because a citrus-rose lives and dies by its projection. High-cis chosen for maximum brightness — the jasmine-citrus radiance that makes the entire composition feel luminous. Hedione HC's citrus-compatible character (Hedione itself possesses a grapefruit-magnolia facet) makes it the ideal radiance amplifier for this concept.

- **Linalool + Linalyl Acetate**: The bergamot co-factors at 220 + 100 µL. Linalool provides fresh-lavender-citrus lift that pairs with bergamot's own linalool content. Linalyl Acetate extends the bergamot persistence into the heart. Essential because the citrus-rose heart is thin — without Linalyl Acetate, there would be a gap between the citrus opening and the rose emergence.

- **Zenolide**: Character-echo + projection musk at 240 µL — the star musk of this composition. Zenolide's pale citrusy-fresh character makes it the ONLY musk that can sit at the top of a citrus composition without dragging it toward warmth or powder. Its clean brightness extends the citrus sillage into the heart. Chosen because no other musk can fulfill this role: Romandolide is too woody, Galaxolide too synthetic-fruity, Habanolide too warm, Ethylene Brassylate too creamy.

- **Romandolide**: Clean diffusion musk at 220 µL — bridges the Zenolide citrus projection to the skin-level Habanolide. Its slightly woody-musk character adds structure without warmth.

- **Habanolide**: Gentle skin-warmth musk at 140 µL — the lowest Habanolide dose of any formula because a citrus-rose should not sit heavy on skin. At 140 µL, Habanolide provides just enough skin-intimacy to make the fragrance feel personal, while letting the citrus and rose do the heavy lifting. Chosen over Ambrettolide (too fruity-wine — would clash with the citrus fruits) and Macrolide (too gentle to register).

- **Sandalore**: The lightest sandalwood at 180 µL — its fresh citrus-woody character makes it the ONLY sandalwood compatible with a citrus-rose. Javanol is too dry-intimate (pulls inward), Ebanol too creamy (adds weight), Bacdanol too heavy. Sandalore's citrus-woody freshness extends the citrus theme into the base without adding oriental warmth.

- **Ambrofix (30%) + Iso E Super + Vertofix**: The transparent structural trio. Ambrofix (140 µL, 42 active) provides crystalline depth — the cold mineral point that lifts the composition. Iso E Super (360 µL) — the lowest dose of any Rose formula because a citrus-rose needs less woody architecture. Vertofix (160) bridges wood and musk. Cashmeran (80 µL) — minimal dose to add a whisper of textile-warmth without introducing the warmth-oriental character that would fight the citrus freshness.

- **Hexyl Salicylate + Benzyl Benzoate**: Fixative pair. Hexyl Salicylate (200) as the green-floral fixative — the ONLY rose formula where Hexyl Sal IS the primary fixative because Benzyl Salicylate's cosmetic heaviness would destroy the citrus transparency. Benzyl Benzoate (220) as inert mass. This is the lightest fixative system of any Rose formula — appropriate for a composition designed for freshness over longevity.
