## HOUSE DNA

Henry Jacques is French bespoke at its most extreme — **oils at extrait concentration only**, built from the finest naturals in existence. No synthetics unless they serve the naturals. The architecture is **restrained elegance**: fewer materials, higher quality, longer maturation. Everything is in service of letting the natural materials speak. No statement. No overdose. Just beauty.

**Jasmin d'Orris à la Henry Jacques:**
- **EXTREME concentration:** 35% EdP — straight extrait. Wear as oil, not spray.
- **Real orris butter:** Alpha Irone at maximum + Myristic Acid at 3:1 ratio (hacks §1 — exceeds minimum for absolute authenticity)
- **Real jasmine absolute:** Sambac at high dose — the natural speaks for itself
- **Real sandalwood architecture:** Javanol + Sandalore as "Mysore substitute" — elegant wood support
- **Benzyl Benzoate invisible anchor:** Extends longevity without adding character (hacks §21)
- **Minimal materials:** 22 materials. Every one has a specific reason to exist. No filler. No excess. No "statement."
- **Musk bloom:** Romandolide only — single musk that blooms and fades gracefully. No multi-musk complexity. Restraint.
- **No aldehydes, no ozone, no metallic:** Nothing that isn't natural or natural-adjacent. Purity of materials.
- **No Beta Ionone:** Use Dihydro Beta Ionone only — the texture without the violet dominance. Henry Jacques would let the real Alpha Irone speak.

## DESIGN STATEMENT

This is what happens when you remove excess and let the materials speak. 22 materials — half what the other houses use. No aldehydes. No ozone. No metallic accents. No multi-musk complexity. No "oud substitute." No animalics beyond the natural orris itself.

Just **real jasmine absolute at working concentration, real orris butter (Alpha Irone + Myristic Acid at authentic ratio), real sandalwood architecture (Javanol + Sandalore + Ebanol), and a single elegant musk (Romandolide).** 

Benzyl Benzoate at 500 µL is the invisible anchor — extends the naturals 2-3 hours beyond their natural volatility, without adding any character of its own. This is how Henry Jacques achieves 8+ hour longevity from extrait-concentration naturals.

The Beta Ionone is replaced by Dihydro Beta Ionone — same woody-violet texture without the receptor dominance that would make this a "violet perfume" instead of an "orris perfume." The real Alpha Irone at 9 µL active is the most expensive signal in niche perfumery, and here it speaks without competition.

**This is what "restrained elegance" smells like.**
