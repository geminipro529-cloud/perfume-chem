## Selection and Rejection Ledger

- The four-material musk chord is Ethylene Brassylate for depth, Galaxolide for diffusion, Exaltolide for skin character, and Zenolide for transparent fresh diffusion.
- Florol, Mayol, and Bourgeonal form a three-material muguet accord: Florol for volume, Mayol for transparency, Bourgeonal for aldehydic lift. Lilial is excluded because it is prohibited in EU cosmetics.
- Alpha Isomethyl Ionone plus Parmavert and Alpha Ionone gives violet/green structure with iris polish.
- Hydroxycitronellol replaces depleted Hydroxycitronellal — distinct rose-peony diol, not the same odor but closest available muguet-rose bridge.
- Heliotropal (Piperonal) replaces Heliotropin Fleuressence — sweet-almond-heliotrope character at neat dose.
- Oranger Crystals + Nerolin Bromelia + Neroli EO form a three-material orange blossom proxy; Orange Blossom Absolute is not stocked.
- Patchouli EO replaces Patchouli Heart (not stocked) at reduced dose to avoid overdosing the earthy facet.
- Vetiver EO (India) replaces Vetiveryl Acetate (not stocked) — rootier, darker profile.
- Sandalore replaces Bacdanol (not stocked) — creamier, less milky sandalwood register.
- Cedramber and Amberwood F provide the cedar-amber bridge without Ambrox Super (depleted).
- Cashmeran was removed because it is a forbidden material in the prada_clean_iris archetype (advisory guideline; not release-blocking but corrected for archetype compliance). Its warm textile-wood depth was redistributed across Sandalore (+10 µL), Cedramber (+10 µL), and Ambrofix (+10 µL) to preserve structural continuity.
- Heavy vanilla, leather, incense, and balsams were rejected because they would pull the composition toward oriental territory away from the clean-iris brief.
- Muscenone Delta is not stocked; Exaltolide 10% carries the skin-musk function at increased dose.
- Isoraldeine 95 is not stocked; Alpha Isomethyl Ionone (Methyl Ionone Pure) compresses both ionone effects into one stock material.

## Mixing and Evaluation

1. Preblend the woods, musks, coumarin, and Ambrofix.
2. Add the floral-air and green materials, then the citrus materials.
3. Add the ionone-violet and iris materials.
4. Add DPG last for this structural control.
5. Add 4.5 mL concentrate to 25.5 mL ethanol 96%; invert gently.
6. Mature at least 14 days. Evaluate strip at 0, 5, and 30 minutes, then 2 and 4 hours.
