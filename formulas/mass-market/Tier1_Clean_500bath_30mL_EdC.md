# Tier 1 — "Clean" — <500 THB — 30 mL EdC

**Batch:** 30.00 mL · 3.0% EdC · 7 materials
**Target:** Drugstore body-splash tier. Cheap, clean, inoffensive. DPG as filler.
**Maceration:** 2 weeks

## Procedure

### Concentrate

| Order | Material | Dilution | Volume | Role |
|------:|---------|----------|-------:|------|
| 1 | DPG | neat | 300 µL | filler |
| 2 | Iso E Super | neat | 150 µL | abstract wood |
| 3 | Galaxolide | 50% in DEP | 150 µL | clean musk |
| 4 | Hedione | neat | 100 µL | radiance |
| 5 | Linalool | neat | 80 µL | fresh floral |
| 6 | Bergamot FCF | neat | 70 µL | citrus top |
| 7 | Dihydromyrcenol | neat | 50 µL | fresh lift |

**Total concentrate:** 900 µL

### Finish

1. Add 900 µL concentrate to bottle.
2. Add **29.1 mL ethanol 96%** to 30 mL line.
3. Cap, invert 30 times.
4. Label: "Clean — 30 mL EdC — <500 THB"
