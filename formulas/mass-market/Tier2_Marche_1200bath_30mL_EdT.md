# Tier 2 — "Marché" — 800–1,500 THB — 30 mL EdT

**Batch:** 30.00 mL · 8.0% EdT · 10 materials
**Target:** Entry-level real perfume. Clean, modern, minimal. Compete with CK One/Davidoff at lower cost.
**Maceration:** 2 weeks

## Procedure

### Concentrate

| Order | Material | Dilution | Volume | Role |
|------:|---------|----------|-------:|------|
| 1 | Iso E Super | neat | 500 µL | abstract wood |
| 2 | Hedione | neat | 400 µL | radiance amplifier |
| 3 | Galaxolide | 50% in DEP | 350 µL | clean projection musk |
| 4 | Bergamot FCF | neat | 300 µL | citrus top |
| 5 | Linalool | neat | 200 µL | fresh floral-lavender |
| 6 | Dihydromyrcenol | neat | 150 µL | fresh lift |
| 7 | Lavender EO | neat | 150 µL | aromatic heart |
| 8 | Cedrat FCF | neat | 150 µL | bitter citrus backbone |
| 9 | Florol | neat | 100 µL | clean muguet |
| 10 | Geraniol | neat | 100 µL | rose brightness |

**Total concentrate:** 2,400 µL

### Finish

1. Combine all materials. Total concentrate: **2,400 µL**.
2. Add approximately **27.6 mL ethanol 96%** to the 30 mL line.
3. Cap, invert 40 times.
4. Label: "Marché — 30 mL EdT — 1,200 THB"
