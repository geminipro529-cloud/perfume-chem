# Tier 3 — "Pure" — 1,500–2,000 THB — 30 mL EdT

**Batch:** 30.00 mL · 8.0% EdT · 8 materials
**Target:** Transparent citrus-clean. CK One / Elizabeth Arden territory.
**Maceration:** 2 weeks

## Procedure

### Concentrate

| Order | Material | Dilution | µL | Role |
|------:|---------|----------|----:|------|
| 1 | Iso E Super | neat | 500 | abstract wood |
| 2 | Hedione | neat | 450 | radiance |
| 3 | Galaxolide | 50% in DEP | 400 | clean musk |
| 4 | Bergamot FCF | neat | 350 | citrus top |
| 5 | Linalool | neat | 250 | fresh floral |
| 6 | Dihydromyrcenol | neat | 200 | fresh lift |
| 7 | Florol | neat | 150 | clean muguet |
| 8 | Nerol | neat | 100 | refined citrus-rose |

**Total concentrate:** 2,400 µL

### Finish

1. Combine all. Total concentrate: **2,400 µL**.
2. Add ethanol 96% to 30 mL line.
3. Cap, invert 40 times.
4. Label: "Pure — 30 mL EdT — 1,800 THB"
