# Tier 1 — "Splash" — <1,000 THB — 30 mL EdC

**Batch:** 30.00 mL · 3.0% EdC · 6 materials
**Target:** Drugstore body splash. Je'Ve / Mistine territory. Sweet clean musk.
**Maceration:** 1 week

## Procedure

### Concentrate

| Order | Material | Dilution | µL | Role |
|------:|---------|----------|----:|------|
| 1 | Galaxolide | 50% in DEP | 350 | clean musk |
| 2 | Iso E Super | neat | 250 | abstract wood |
| 3 | Hedione | neat | 100 | radiance |
| 4 | Linalool | neat | 80 | fresh floral |
| 5 | Bergamot FCF | neat | 70 | citrus top |
| 6 | Dihydromyrcenol | neat | 50 | fresh lift |

**Total concentrate:** 900 µL

### Finish

1. Add 900 µL concentrate to 30 mL bottle.
2. Fill to line with ethanol 96%.
3. Cap, invert 30 times.
4. Label: "Splash — 30 mL EdC — <1,000 THB"
