### Accord Breakdown (v2 — GC-MS informed)

- **Grojsman backbone (41% active):** ISO E Super (18.8%) + Hedione (12.5%) + Galaxolide (9.4%) — the exact "hug me" accord skeleton of the original. This IS the Angels' Share architecture.
- **Musk chord (30% active):** EB (9.4%) + Cashmeran (4.4%) + Tonalide (2.3%) + Ambrettolide (1.3%) + Musk Ketone (3.1%) — 5-material musk system. Substituting Musk Ketone for Muscenone to provide the warm-animal-powdery diffusion. Total musk loading hits ~30%, matching the original's 28.7%.  
- **Gourmand-praline (12% active):** Coumarin (4.1%) + Ethyl Maltol (3.1%) + Vanillin (2.0%) + Tonka Bean FO (1.6%) + Ethyl Vanillin (1.3%) + Heliotropal (0.4%). The real praline illusion — Ethyl Maltol at 3.1% is the highest single gourmand dose.  
- **Oak-cognac (9% active):** Ambrox Super (3.1%) + Cedramber (1.6%) + Linalyl Acetate (2.5%) + Orange Peel EO (1.3%) + Evernyl (0.5%) + Labdanum (0.1%). The boisée-barrel accord.  
- **Cinnamon-spice (0.9%):** Cinnamyl Alcohol (0.78%) + Cinnamaldehyde (0.09%). Replaces ~1.1% of missing materials (cinnamon EOs, Nerolidol) with the correct cinnamon-character materials that ARE in inventory.  
- **Trace lift (0.9%):** Helional + Alpha Irone + Ethyl Linalool + Damascone Beta + Linalool for the ozone-floral-fruity aura that prevents the boozy-gourmand accord from collapsing into syrup.

### Dosing & Mixing Notes

1. **Coumarin is crystalline:** Weigh 130 mg directly on the scale. Do not attempt to pipette. Pre-dissolve in 1 mL warm ethanol if easier.
2. **Ethyl Maltol at 1000 µL of 10%** is the largest single line. Ensure the 10% solution is well-mixed — Ethyl Maltol can precipitate in cold ethanol.
3. **Order of addition:** Largest volume first: ISO E Super → Hedione → Galaxolide → EB → Coumarin → Tonalide → Cashmeran → Ethyl Maltol → Vanillin → Ambrettolide → Ethyl Vanillin → Ambrox Super → Tonka Bean → Linalyl Acetate → Cedramber → trace materials. Ethanol last.
4. **Maceration:** Minimum 4 weeks. This is a dense Grojsman-acord + musk chord — the Galaxolide-Hedione-IES synergy needs significant time to fully bloom. The opening will read "sharp/diffusive" for the first 10-14 days as the musks marry.
5. **Cinnamyl Alcohol 50%** is viscous — pre-warm gently to 30°C before pipetting.
