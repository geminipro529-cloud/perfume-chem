## Family-to-function map and chassis

The proposed chassis is:

```text
restrained citrus / pink pepper / aldehydic air
  -> iris-root and quiet petals
  -> ambrette seed and powder/skin musk
  -> pale woody-musky persistence hypothesis
```

All links are perfumery design hypotheses, not measured synergistic interactions or receptor affinities. The protected design recognizers are tactile musk, iris behind it, quiet seed/root texture, and a small pepper frame. Mobility belongs first to Galaxolide's cotton-clean share and the seed/root traces. The wood block must remain subordinate.

## Mixing order

1. Weigh the vessel and record actual additions. Use a verified balance; the 0.001 g scale is suitable for these working-stock doses, not for direct weighing of the 1.2 mg aldehyde target.
2. Dissolve Ambrox crystals and Exaltolide in a portion of the final alcohol and/or the compatible liquid perfumery base. Keep their weighed masses; do not turn them into liquid dose equivalents.
3. Add the other musks, woody framework and Hexyl Salicylate; mix.
4. Add iris/ionone products, Piperonal stock, the quiet floral bridge and natural seed/root stocks; mix.
5. Add citrus, pepper, Coriander and aldehyde stock last.
6. Add the remaining alcohol to the selected **mass** or **measured-volume** endpoint. Do not mix endpoints.
7. Close, mix and inspect after equilibration. Persistent haze, sediment or crystals is a hold, not permission to assume all odorant remains dissolved.
8. Keep ethanol away from ignition; do not heat open ethanol. Compare initially after a common resting period, then again after about one week. Maturation time is an experimental convention, not a proven optimum.

If starting a physical bottle later: propose -> human confirm -> record actual measurements -> commit exactly once. This document does not create a bottle event or inventory movement.

## Failure modes and next decisions

- **Laundry dominates:** compare the lower-Galaxolide trial before adding more ingredients.
- **Lipstick/violet dominates:** reduce ionone body in a new version, preserving the iris/seed question; do not compensate with vanilla.
- **Carrot/celery dominates:** range-test the Carrot Seed trace; an earthy ingredient is not automatically luxury.
- **Seed smells musty:** compare seed omission or a lower seed dose rather than increasing clean musk automatically.
- **Sweet almond powder dominates:** range-test Piperonal; keep amber/vanilla excluded.
- **Dry woody amber dominates:** inspect Ambrox, Cashmeran and Iso E roles separately.
- **Blend is soft but featureless:** omission/alternative tests determine whether a musk or floral seam is redundant.
- **Haze/crystals:** resolve stock identity, solvent compatibility and dissolution before any claimed performance.
