### The "Creamy Citrus" Accord — Deconstructed

The "lemon meringue pie" character that defines Edition Blanche is NOT ethyl maltol and NOT a vanilla bomb. It is a **four-layer accord**:

1. **Layer 1 — Sharp Citrus (Top):** Sicilian Lemon oil (high Citral) + Calabrian Bergamot (Linalool) + Aldehydes C10/C12 MNA (Chanel sparkle)
2. **Layer 2 — Powdery-Woody Cream (Heart):** **Alpha-Isomethyl Ionone** (violet-powder creaminess) + **Sandalwood Synthetics** (Sandalore + Polysantol + Ebanol — Polge's sandalwood compound from Égoïste) 
3. **Layer 3 — Sweet-Hay Cream (Base):** **Coumarin** (tonka, restrained) + **Vanillin** (ethereal, not heavy)
4. **Layer 4 — Diffusion Cushion:** **Benzyl Salicylate** (cosmetic-salicylate volume — confirmed on INCI)

## Accord Breakdown

### Lemon-Bergamot-Aldehyde Opening (35% active)
Lemon FCF Sicilian (7.6%) + Bergamot FCF Sicilian (5.5%) + Lemonile (1.5%) + DHM (6.1%) + Hedione (7.6%) + Linalool (1.8%) + Linalyl Acetate (1.5%) + Helional (1.2%) + Aldehydes (0.02%) + AAG (0.08%) + Cardamom EO (0.5%) + Black Pepper (0.6%) + Galbanum (0.06%). The citrus is real — Sicilian lemon oil with high Citral, sustained by Lemonile into the heart. AAG completes the neo-fougère framework (DHM-AAG-Helional-salicylates-coumarin-ambroxan per Yudov). Cardamom EO provides the ginger-like perky freshness (shares cineole).

### Sandalwood-Cream Heart (35% active)
ISO E Super (9.1%) + **Polge's Sandalwood Compound** — Sandalore (7.6%) + Polysantol (3.6%) + Ebanol (1.8%) + Javanol (0.09%) — **total sandalwood = 13.1% of concentrate** + **Alpha Isomethyl Ionone (4.5%)** as the "creamy" powdery-violet texture + **Geranium EO (1.5%)** — Yudov's "generous portion" differentiating heart material + **Chanel Muguet-Neroli Accord** — Hydroxycitronellal (3.0%) + Methyl Anthranilate (0.5%) + Aurantiol (0.9%) + Citronellol (0.9%). The "cream" character is NOT ethyl maltol — it is the ionone-powder layered over the sandalwood compound, with Hydroxycitronellal providing transparent volume.

### Tonka-Musk-Vetiver Drydown (30% active)
**Chanel White Musk (12.1%)** — Habanolide (7.6%) + Romandolide (4.5%) + Coumarin (2.1% — RESTRAINED) + Vanillin (0.6% — ETHEREAL) + Benzyl Salicylate (6.1%) + Ambrox Super (1.8%) + Vetiver EO (1.5%) + **Patchouli EO (0.75%)** + Cedarwood EO (1.8%) + Farnesol (0.6%) + Cashmeran (0.9%) + Frankincense (0.3%) + **Labdanum Resinoid (0.09%)**. Yudov's "patchouli, sandalwood, Iso E Super, vetiver" massive woody accord is now complete. Labdanum provides the "noticeable portion of resins."

## Dosing & Mixing Notes

1. **Order of addition (largest to smallest):** Hedione → ISO E Super → Lemon FCF → Sandalore → Habanolide → DHM → Benzyl Salicylate → Bergamot FCF → Vanillin (10%) → Romandolide → Polysantol → Alpha Isomethyl Ionone → Hydroxycitronellal → Benzyl Benzoate → Coumarin → Ebanol → Ambrox Super → Cedarwood → Linalool → **Geranium EO** → Vetiver → Lemonile → Linalyl Acetate → Helional → Aurantiol → Cashmeran → Citronellol → **Patchouli EO** → Farnesol → **Labdanum Resinoid** → Cardamom EO → Frankincense → Black Pepper → AAG → Aldehydes → Galbanum. Ethanol last.
2. **Coumarin is crystalline:** Weigh 70 mg directly. Pre-dissolve in 1 mL warm ethanol.
3. **Geranium EO at 50 µL:** The "generous portion" Yudov describes. This is the differentiating heart material — do not reduce. Provides menthone (cool-mint) that works with DHM to create the "crystalline" cold dimension Polge described.
4. **Labdanum Resinoid 10% at 30 µL (3 µL active):** The "noticeable portion of resins." At this dose, structural warmth — double to 60 µL if you want more pronounced amber-leather.
5. **Patchouli EO at 25 µL:** Completes the Yudov "massive woody accord: patchouli, sandalwood, Iso E Super, vetiver." Earthy-chocolate anchoring. Do not increase — Patchouli can dominate.
6. **AAG 10% at 25 µL:** The missing link in the neo-fougère framework. Green-pineapple-galbanum. At 2.5 µL active, it's structural. Double to 50 µL of 10% if you want the "galbanum-green" dimension more prominent.
7. **Cardamom EO at 15 µL:** Ginger substitute. Cineolic-cool perky freshness. Do not increase — Cardamom above 20 µL pushes toward fougère territory.
8. **Javanol at 3 µL (reduced from 5):** At ODT ~2 ppt, even 3 µL provides the eternal sandalwood trail. Do NOT increase — this is a trace-tenacity material, not a structural sandalwood.
9. **Aurantiol (Schiff base):** Sticky, viscous. Pre-warm to 30°C.
10. **Maceration:** 3 weeks minimum. The Geranium EO needs time to marry with the sandalwood compound. The Hydroxycitronellal + Methyl Anthranilate Schiff base formation continues in-bottle. Testing before 2 weeks: the citrus will dominate and the geranium-sandalwood cream won't be integrated.
