## APPLICATION

1. **Check headspace.** If bottle is filled to rim, decant ~1.5 mL into a clean vial (keep as archive — do not discard until evaluation).
2. **Dosing order.** Add materials #1–13 in the listed order. Swirl after each addition. No heat, no shaking — gentle swirl.
3. **Wait.** After all 13 additions, swirl 30 seconds. Re-cap. Let settle **minimum 7 days** before first evaluation. Peak integration at **14–21 days**.
4. **First read at day 7.** The Benzyl Salicylate + Iso E Super cascade will dominate initially. This is normal. The salicylate-Hedione synergy needs time to fuse into the existing base. Do not judge on day 1.
5. **Full evaluation at day 14.** Expect: brighter opening (Cardamom + Aldehyde C12 MNA sparkle), more radiant jasmine heart (salicylate cascade lifting Hedione), richer iris (Heliotropal × Alpha Irone × Cashmeran wrapping the ionone core), and deeper fixative drydown (3-musk chord + Ambrofix blooming).

## IMPORTANT — Iris-Jasmine Salvage Logic

This addition set preserves and enhances the **identity of the original bottle** rather than overwriting it:

- **Jasmine identity preserved:** Dihydrojasmone reinforces (not replaces) cis-Jasmone. Ylang adds a white-floral companion without diluting jasmine signal. No new jasmine materials compete with the existing Sambac/Hedione/cis-Jasmone core.
- **Iris identity preserved:** Heliotropal × Alpha Irone synergy strengthens the orris character through a non-ionone pathway. Cashmeran adds textural warmth without competing for violet receptors. No Beta Ionone added (respects the Dihydro Beta Ionone-only decision). No new ionones dilute the Alpha Irone signature.
- **Musk restraint preserved:** Only 2 new musks (EthBrass + Habanolide). Together with existing Romandolide, this creates a 3-musk chord (depth + body + echo) without the exaggeration of a 4-musk system. Still reads as restrained elegance — just with better fixative function.
- **No challenging notes:** No indole, no animalics, no smoke, no ozone, no metallic materials. Everything is Henry Jacques-compatible: clean, natural-adjacent, elegant.
