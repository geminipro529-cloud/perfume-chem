# Jasmine Sambac Vivante — Natural Petal / Warm Skin — 30 mL V4

Date: 2026-09-10.

**Status: PRE-MIX COMPUTATIONAL DESIGN COMPLETE / REALISM AND HEDONIC OUTCOME NOT YET SMELLED / NOT SKIN-RELEASED.**

This is a separate successor to `Jasmine_Sambac_Clair_30mL_V3_Optimized.md`; it does not overwrite V3 and is not an instruction to modify an existing bottle. The target is a fuller, more flower-like Jasmine Sambac EDP with natural petal depth, a quiet green edge, warm white-floral body, and a soft skin drydown. Sambac remains the single natural jasmine identity rather than being blurred with a second species or a generalized white-floral bouquet.

The user's direct sensory evidence is decisive: the natural jasmine extracts and Sambac blossoms read as jasmine, while the separate Benzyl Acetate stock read as sour/waxy glue and the previous benzyl-acetate formula smelled bad. **Isolated Benzyl Acetate therefore remains at 0 µL.** Benzyl acetate occurring inside a natural jasmine absolute remains part of that integrated natural mixture; it is not equivalent to adding the rejected isolated stock.

## V3 to V4 design changes

- Jasmine Sambac Absolute 10% w/w in DPG: **1350 -> 2100 µL supplied stock**, or **135 -> 210 µL-equivalent nominal active absolute**, a **55.6%** identity-strength increase.
- Jasmine Absolute (Grandiflorum) is held outside the executable formula. It is a legitimate separate flower material, but the current stock says only 10% in DPG without a mass/volume fraction basis. The pipeline must not invent that basis merely to admit a second absolute.
- Hedione: **1290 -> 750 µL**. It remains at 12.5% of the concentrate for transparent petal space but is no longer the oversized center of the formula.
- Benzyl Salicylate, Phenethyl Alcohol, and Nerolidol are added as three non-identical body layers: warm white-floral cushion, living floral volume, and floral-woody persistence.
- cis-Jasmone and Methyl Anthranilate are used only as small Sambac-character corrections. Florhydral is used at **20 µL / 0.33% of concentrate** as fresh green-petal polish, not as a muguet accord.
- V3's free Linalool, Linalyl Acetate, Bergamot FCF Oil Sicilian, and Iso E Super are removed. Petitgrain is halved to a small bitter-leaf edge. This reduces aromatic, citrus, and abstract-wood competition around the natural flower.
- V3's three-musk stack is replaced by one selected musk, Ethylene Brassylate, for a rounded floral skin base. Habanolide and Zenolide are not needed in this version.
- Sandalore is reduced and Ebanol is added at only 1% of concentrate as a creamier wood detail. The woods remain a substrate, not a competing theme.
- Romandolide is not used. It is owned again, but the new bottle's stock strength and carrier are still unstated, so it is not quantitatively executable.

## Batch and concentration basis

- Final target: **30.00 mL**.
- Supplied-stock concentrate: **6000 µL / 6.00 mL nominal**.
- Ethanol 96%: **q.s. to 30.00 mL final volume** only after the pre-mix review is complete.
- Nominal fragrance-stock loading: **20% v/v by dispensed stock volume**, an EDP design target; it is not exact active aromatic w/w.
- Jasmine Sambac Absolute stock: **10% w/w in DPG**, bound to the current executable DPG-carried stock.
- Do not substitute the selected Sambac stock with the separate ambiguous-carrier 10% row, Grandiflorum, neat absolute, Jasmine FO, or dried blossoms.
- Every other formula row is the current **neat / as-supplied** stock.
- No selected stock declares DEP, and DEP is not deliberately added. This is a stock-record statement, not an analytical trace-absence claim.

The active-ppm values below are screening conversions in the supplied-stock concentrate, assuming 1.000 g/mL for every transferred stock. Neat rows count at 100%; Sambac counts at its declared 10% w/w fraction. These are not measured concentrations.
