# Tuberose Aquatique Blanc — 30mL EdP

## Character
A sheer aquatic floral where Tuberose Absolute's narcotic creaminess is lifted by an ozonic-marine freshness — tuberose meets the ocean. Helional and Floralozone create an airy, sea-mist ozonic canopy over the white floral heart, while Dihydromyrcenol and Cyclamen Aldehyde add clean-metallic transparency. The tuberose-jasmine-ylang heart floats weightlessly over a minimal, transparent base of Ambrofix (ambergris-mineral) and Zenolide (cold citrusy musk). Paradisamide injects a tropical-fruit sparkle that reads as "sea spray on tropical flowers" rather than candy. Think L'Eau d'Issey with a tuberose heart — the most transparent, sheer tuberose in this collection.

**Family**: Aquatic Floral — ozonic-marine top, tuberose white floral heart, mineral-ambergris transparent base

## Material Justification

- **Tuberose Absolute (10% in DPG)**: The narcotic white floral anchor at 450 µL of 10% = 45 µL active (0.77% of concentrate). In this aquatic context, tuberose's creaminess is deliberately lightened by the ozonic-marine canopy — the goal is "tuberose by the sea," not "tuberose in a hothouse." Chosen over Tuberalia base (too synthetic-clean — the aquatic materials already provide cleanliness; tuberose's natural depth is needed as counterweight).

- **Helional + Floralozone (10%) + Cyclamen Aldehyde**: The ozonic-marine triad. Helional (140 µL neat) provides sea-mist mineral freshness — the key marine material after Calone. Floralozone (10 µL active) adds ozonic-violet freshness that bridges the aquatic top to the floral heart. Cyclamen Aldehyde (80 µL neat) contributes metallic-green floral sparkle. Together they create an aquatic canopy that lifts rather than drowns the tuberose. Calone (DEPLETED) is not needed — Helional provides sufficient marine character.

- **Dihydromyrcenol**: Clean-lime-metallic freshness at 280 µL. The workhorse aquatic-fresh material — provides the "clean" register that makes aquatics read as "water" rather than "seaweed." At 280 µL it's a substantial dose that carries the ozonic freshness through the heart. Chosen because no other material in inventory provides the same clean-lime-metallic freshness with this tenacity.

- **Bergamot FCF Sicilian**: Rich citrus top at 380 µL. Sicilian bergamot was chosen over standard Bergamot FCF because its richer, more complex profile pairs better with the aquatic freshness — standard Bergamot would read as too simple against the Helional-Floralozone complexity. Rejected: Grapefruit FCF (too bitter-dry for an aquatic floral), Cedrat (too sharp-mineral).

- **Methyl Pamplemousse (10% in TEC)**: Grapefruit-rhubarb tartness at 60 µL of 10%. Adds a tart-fresh counterpoint that prevents the aquatic-floral heart from becoming flat. The rhubarb facet reads as "sea spray acidity."

- **Aldehyde C11 undecylenic**: Aldehydic sparkle at 30 µL neat. C11 undecylenic provides a clean-metallic-aldehydic lift distinct from C10 (orange) and C12 MNA (waxy). Its "fresh laundry" character pairs with the aquatic transparency. Chosen over C12 MNA (too waxy-cosmetic for this sheer composition).

- **Lilyreal ND + Mayol + Hydroxycitronellal**: The muguet-lily transparent heart triad. Lilyreal (180 µL) provides clean synthetic muguet body. Mayol (150 µL) adds transparent muguet-lily freshness — chosen as the post-Lyral muguet for its cleaner character. Hydroxycitronellal (150 µL) adds lily-of-the-valley volume and schiff-base chemistry with Jasmine Sambac's methyl anthranilate. Together they create a transparent floral body that supports tuberose without competing.

- **Jasmine Sambac (10% in DPG) + Ylang Complete EO**: White floral volume at 250 + 150 µL. Sambac (25 µL active) provides authentic jasmonate-indole depth at a restrained dose — in an aquatic context, too much jasmine would pull toward indolic-oriental. Ylang (150 µL) adds banana-custard creaminess that bridges tuberose to the muguet materials.

- **Paradisamide (10%)**: Tropical fruit sparkle at 80 µL of 10% = 8 µL active. Paradisamide's guava-passionfruit character reads as "sea spray on tropical flowers" in this aquatic context. Its 150hr tenacity means the fruit sparkle persists into the drydown. At 8 µL active it's perceptible but not candy-like.

- **Hexyl Salicylate**: Light transparent fixative at 220 µL. Chosen as the primary fixative because Benzyl Salicylate's heavy cosmetic character would drag down the aquatic transparency. Hexyl Salicylate's green-floral sheer film creates adhesion without weight. Rejected: Benzyl Benzoate (too inert — the composition needs the green-floral character contribution of Hexyl Sal).

- **Benzyl Salicylate**: Medium fixative at 350 µL. Provides the cosmetic diffusion base but at a reduced dose (vs. 450–500 µL in heavier compositions). The salicylate cushion is needed to carry the tuberose heart, but is deliberately dialed back to preserve aquatic transparency.

- **Iso E Super**: Molecular skin cocoon at 500 µL. Provides abstract-cedar skin-adhesion. In this aquatic context, Iso E Super's anosmia-prone "what are you wearing?" effect creates the "phantom floral" trail characteristic of modern aquatics. Chosen over Timberol (too architectural — would add unwanted dryness).

- **Ebanol + Sandalore**: Sandalwood pair at 80 + 60 µL. Ebanol provides creamy-milky sandalwood that supports the tuberose creaminess in the base. Sandalore adds fresh-citrus-woody lift that pairs with the aquatic freshness. At these modest doses they provide sandalwood texture without weight. Rejected: Javanol (too dry-mineral for an aquatic), Bacdanol (too heavy-milky).

- **Ambrofix (30% w/v)**: Mineral-ambergris depth at 80 µL of 30% = 24 µL active. In an aquatic context, Ambrofix's crystalline-mineral character reads as "ambergris on the beach" — the transparent marine depth that defines modern aquatics. Replaces Ambrox Super 30% (DEPLETED). Chosen over Amberwood F (too warm — would pull toward amber) and Cedramber (cedar facet would intrude).

- **Zenolide**: Cold citrusy-fresh character-echo musk at 100 µL neat. Zenolide is the coldest musk in inventory — its clean, slightly citrusy-fresh character reinforces the aquatic transparency. Chosen over Galaxolide (too synthetic-clean, would make the composition read as "laundry detergent aqua") and Tonalide (similar polycyclic character but less fresh).

- **Romandolide**: Woody-diffusive projection musk at 200 µL. Romandolide's outward projection extends the aquatic-floral sillage architecture. At 200 µL it's the dominant musk by volume — chosen because this composition needs projection (aquatics should diffuse), not intimacy.

- **Ethylene Brassylate**: Creamy depth musk at 150 µL. The modest dose provides skin-sitting tenacity without the lactonic thickness that would clash with aquatic transparency. At 150 µL it anchors the musk chord without weighing it down.

- **Vanillin (10%) + Heliotropal + Coumarin (20%)**: Sheer sweet base at 60 + 40 + 50 µL. Vanillin (6 µL active) adds a whisper of creamy sweetness. Heliotropal (40 µL neat = piperonal) adds almond-heliotrope powder that bridges to the muguet heart. Coumarin (10 µL active) provides hay-almond warmth at a restrained dose. All three are intentionally low to prevent the base from becoming sweet-oriental — the aquatic transparency must dominate.

- **Cashmeran (neat)**: Textile warmth at 50 µL. At this restrained dose, Cashmeran adds a soft cashmere undercurrent that prevents the aquatic-transparent base from reading as clinical. Safely at the 50 µL/6mL non-oriental ceiling.

- **Vertofix**: Woody-musky bridge at 80 µL. Glues the Iso E Super molecular layer to the sandalwood-musk base.
