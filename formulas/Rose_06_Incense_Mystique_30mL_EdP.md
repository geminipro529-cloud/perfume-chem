# Rose Incense Mystique — 30mL EdP

## Character
A meditative incense rose where Rose de Mai Absolute meets Frankincense EO in a cool, contemplative space. Rather than the warm, balsamic incense of church tradition (Olibanum Resinoid is depleted), this composition uses the bright, citrus-terpenic character of Frankincense EO (Boswellia carterii/serrata) as the incense core, supported by a cool cedar-violet backbone of Alpha Isomethyl Ionone, Juniper Berry EO, and Cedarwood Virginia. The rose is kept luminous and transparent (Geraniol at 60 µL, Rhodinol at 30 µL, Nerol adding brightness) so it floats through the incense smoke rather than competing with it. Guaiacol provides a whisper of liturgical smoke — just enough to suggest burning resin without crossing into barbecue territory. The musk chord (Romandolide cool projection, Zenolide pale-fresh clarity, Ambrettolide naturalistic warmth) creates a transparent, uplifting trail. Think Comme des Garçons Avignon softened with rose, or a rose-forward take on Heeley Cardinal.

**Family**: Incense Floral (rose-encens) — juniper-citrus top, rose-incense heart, cedar-musk-amber base

## Material Justification

- **Rose de Mai Absolute (10%)**: The natural rose anchor at 380 µL (38 µL active, 0.67% of concentrate). Slightly lower than the Chypre Classique (400 µL) because the incense architecture demands that the rose float through the frankincense, not sit on top of it. The absolute's honeyed-spicy character provides the organic warmth that prevents the cool incense-cedar structure from reading as clinical.

- **Citronellol + Geraniol + PEA + Rhodinol + Nerol**: The molecular rose accord — 5 materials at constrained doses. Citronellol (280) provides fresh-citrus-rose lift — the brightest rose alcohol, chosen to shine through the incense. Geraniol (60) at maximum — sweet-geranium body. PEA (360) fills honeyed-rose volume. Rhodinol (30) at maximum — natural geranium-rose richness that bridges the rose to the botanical incense materials. Nerol (100) — boosted from the usual 80-90 because Nerol's sweet citrusy-rose character pairs naturally with the citrus-terpenic frankincense.

- **Damascenone (1%) + Rose Oxide (10%)**: The rose modifier pair at 70 + 60 µL. Damascenone (0.7 µL active) provides honeyed-woody rose depth — the incense connection, as the woody facet of Damascenone resonates with the cedar-violet structure. Rose Oxide (6 µL active) adds metallic-green freshness. Only two modifiers because the incense materials (Frankincense EO, Alpha Isomethyl Ionone) already provide complexity — adding more rose modifiers would create competition.

- **Frankincense EO**: The incense core at 200 µL — Boswellia carterii/serrata essential oil, steam-distilled from resin. Provides the bright, citrus-terpenic, slightly piney incense character that defines this composition. Chosen over Olibanum Resinoid Absolute (depleted) — Frankincense EO is lighter, brighter, and more transparent, which suits the modern incense-rose concept better than the heavy, balsamic resinoid. The EO's natural limonene and alpha-pinene content connects directly to the Juniper Berry and Bergamot top notes.

- **Alpha Isomethyl Ionone**: The incense-ionone bridge at 140 µL — Methyl Ionone Pure provides the woody-violet character that has been a classical partner to incense accords since L'Heure Bleue. Here it functions as the transparent "incense-wood" that fills the space between the frankincense and the cedar base. Chosen over Beta Ionone (more powdery — would soften the incense too much) and Alpha Ionone (more floral — would compete with the rose).

- **Juniper Berry EO + Black Pepper EO**: The cool-spicy top at 100 + 60 µL. Juniper Berry EO provides the fresh-green-coniferous-gin clarity that announces "cool incense" before the frankincense even emerges. Black Pepper EO adds the dry-spicy terpene facet that bridges the juniper freshness to the frankincense. Together they create the opening impression of entering a cool stone cathedral.

- **Guaiacol (10%)**: The liturgical smoke trace at 40 µL of 10% (4 µL active) — provides the phenolic-smoky whisper that suggests burning resin without crossing into smoke-dominant territory. At this dose, Guaiacol reads as "incense smoke" rather than "campfire" or "barbecue." Essential for creating the meditative atmosphere that distinguishes this from a simple woody-rose.

- **Cedarwood Virginia + Iso E Super + Vertofix + Azarbre + Ambrofix**: The cedar-incense woody base. Cedarwood Virginia (180 µL) provides natural cedar — the pencil-shaving warmth that anchors frankincense. Iso E Super (420 µL) creates the molecular skin-cocoon. Vertofix (180) bridges wood and musk. Azarbre (140) adds the warm cedar-amber bed — warmer than Ambrofix, it fills the architectural space without the crystalline edge that would fight the incense warmth. Ambrofix (120 µL, 36 active) provides transparent depth — the cold mineral point that lifts the entire composition.

- **Evernyl (50% in DPG)**: Synthetic moss body at 120 µL of 50% (60 µL active). At this dilution, stays within IFRA compliance while adding the bitter-green-woody dimension that makes incense compositions feel "serious." Evernyl's synthetic moss character echoes the green facets of frankincense and juniper.

- **Hedione**: Radiance amplifier at 520 µL — standard Hedione chosen over Hedione HC because the softer, less-bright jasmonate character pairs better with meditative incense than the high-intensity HC variant. Expands the rose heart into the frankincense top, creating the luminous transparency that prevents incense from reading as heavy.

- **Romandolide**: Cool projection musk at 260 µL — the highest Romandolide dose of any Rose formula because incense compositions need maximum outward projection. Romandolide's clean, slightly woody-musk character extends the incense-rose sillage without adding warmth (which would fight the cool-incense concept).

- **Zenolide**: Character-echo musk at 180 µL — the coldest, most citrusy-fresh musk in the inventory. Its pale-clean character reinforces the cool-transparent incense register. Chosen because no other musk can echo this concept: Galaxolide is too detergent-sweet, Habanolide too warm-skin, Musk Ketone too powdery.

- **Ambrettolide (10%)**: Depth musk at 200 µL of 10% (20 µL active). The warmest element in the musk chord — its naturalistic fruity-wine warmth adds the "human" dimension that prevents the incense from reading as sterile. Chosen over Ethylene Brassylate (too creamy — would fight the cool incense), Exaltolide (too fatty), and Habanolide (too skin-warm — would pull the incense toward oriental territory).

- **Benzyl Benzoate + Hexyl Salicylate**: Fixative pair. Benzyl Benzoate (220) as inert mass. Hexyl Salicylate (160) as sheer fixative for the top notes. No Benzyl Salicylate — its cosmetic-heavy character would destroy the transparent incense architecture.

- **Bergamot FCF + Linalool**: The clean citrus top at 380 + 200 µL. Bergamot FCF provides classical cologne freshness — chosen over more exotic citrus because frankincense and juniper ARE the top-note character; bergamot simply provides the clean frame. Linalool adds fresh-lavender lift.
