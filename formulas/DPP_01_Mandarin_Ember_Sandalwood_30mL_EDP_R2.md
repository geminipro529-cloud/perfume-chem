## R2 Concept Lock

A glowing mandarin over a classical resin amber and a broad sandalwood body. **Amber means labdanum, benzoin, olibanum, opoponax, Peru balsam, and myrrh.** Ambrox Super is retained only as a subordinate crystalline-mineral drydown and performance support; it does not own the amber identity. Amber Xtreme remains excluded because its extreme superamber register and sub-threshold modeled dose do not justify a separate voice. Azarbre and Kephalis remain differentiated wood-transition materials, not substitutes for the resin amber.

The R1 Amber Xtreme dose was not numerically excessive: 50 µL of the 10% stock supplied a 5 µL active-volume proxy and modeled opening OAV 0.64. It remains omitted. The 150 µL Ambrox Super dose supplied a 37.5 µL active-volume proxy and modeled opening OAV 24.0, so it is retained as a moderate, clearly perceptible return voice under the resin and sandalwood structure.

R2 strengthens the classical resins, retains subordinate Ambrox support, and preserves the exact 6,000 µL raw concentrate total by reducing two modeled sub-threshold structural materials:

- Amber Xtreme 10% w/w in DEP: 50 → 0 µL.
- Ambrox Super 25% w/w in DPG/IPM/ethanol: retained at 150 µL.
- Labdanum Resinoid 10% in DPG: 200 → 344 µL.
- Olibanum Resinoid 50% w/w in DPG: 400 → 456 µL.
- Bacdanol: 650 → 550 µL.
- Benzyl Benzoate: 250 → 200 µL.

This is an intentional target rebalance, not active-dose equivalence. The resin increments remain the amber identity; the retained Ambrox provides a cool mineral return and persistence beneath them. Bacdanol remains the largest sandalwood material at 550 µL alongside 400 µL Ebanol, while Benzyl Benzoate remains a 200 µL residue anchor.

Recursive Deep Plane Perfumery remains intact: every macro-plane contains a deep seed, body, contour, upper veil, and return thread. The table follows physical basket order first and descends by raw transfer volume inside each basket; it does not describe literal unmixed liquid layers.

## Basket Compounding Procedure

1. Use one clean amber vial and add rows 1–30 in exact basket-first order, completing each basket before moving to the next.
2. Inside each basket, keep repeated-volume settings together and turn the dispenser knob only down. Reset it only when starting the next basket. Cap and gently roll periodically until homogeneous. The DPP planes describe functional relationships, not physical stratification.
3. After the concentrate reaches 6,000 µL, mix until uniform.
4. Add approximately 22 mL ethanol 96%, mix, then add ethanol q.s. to the 30.00 mL final-volume mark.
5. Inspect clarity and color immediately, at 24 hours, 7 days, and 14 days. The saffron infusion and resinoids may produce deep color or later haze.
6. Evaluate on blotters at 0, 5, and 30 minutes; 2, 6, 24, and 48 hours. The decisive R2 question is whether the late residue returns as olibanum-labdanum-saffron-mandarin rather than as generic amberwood.

## Material Selection and Rejection Rationale

- **Resin amber:** Labdanum is the leathery seed; Siam Benzoin supplies sweet balsamic volume; opoponax and myrrh darken and dry the interior; Peru balsam rounds the middle; olibanum provides the luminous incense return.
- **Rejected Amber Xtreme:** its modeled R1 OAV was 0.64, below the pipeline perceptibility threshold, and its extreme superamber pressure role remains both functionally weak and conceptually unnecessary.
- **Supporting Ambrox Super:** the user explicitly permits ambrox character for distinction and performance when it remains subordinate. The 150 µL current stock dose provides a 37.5 µL active-volume proxy and historically modeled OAV 24.0. Its crystalline-mineral return sits beneath the labdanum-benzoin-olibanum identity. Ambrofix is rejected because its owned liquid stock remains heterogeneous and non-executable; Ambermax would add a warmer synthetic amber body that competes more directly with the resins; Amberwood F is too transparent to justify another row.
- **Retained woody transitions:** Ebanol and Bacdanol build the sandal kernel and mass. Azarbre connects cedar to resin without a crystalline edge; Kephalis supplies dry tobacco-wood tension. Their basket category does not grant them amber-identity ownership.
- **Single musk:** Ethylene Brassylate supplies creamy, low-frequency diffusion without adding a clean projecting chord.
- **Fixative gradient:** Benzyl Benzoate supplies quiet mass while Benzyl Salicylate carries the residue upward toward the floral-incense plane.
