# Osmanthus Aromatic Fougère — 30mL EdP

## Character
Osmanthus-centered aromatic fougère. Lavender and coumarin form the frame — not the lead. Osmanthus absolute carries the apricot-tea-leather center. Tobacco at trace level reads as dried tea leaf, not smoke. Grapefruit FCF + Methyl Pamplemousse open with bitter citrus, burning off by 30 minutes. The creamy line — gamma decalactone through ebanol to ambrofix — blooms as the bitter fades. Rose de Mai provides geraniol for the fougère skeleton and eugenol warmth. Vetival and Vertofix add refined woods. No oud, no leather, no oakmoss. Clean, modern, unisex.

**Family**: Aromatic Fougère (modern)
**Materials**: 26
**Concentration**: 14.5% EdP

## Architecture

```
BITTER ── Grapefruit 50 + MP 60 → Nagar Motha 60 → Theaspirane (osmanthus)
OSMANTHUS (center) ← Rose de Mai 120 (geraniol + eugenol)
CREAMY ── γ-Decalactone 50 → Ebanol 60 → Timberol 80 → Cedramber 60 → Ambrofix 80
FRAME   ── Lavender HA 35 + Ethyl Linalool 25 + Coumarin 100 + Tobacco 10% 20
VOLUME  ── Hedione HC 600 + Iso E Super 800
WOODS   ── Vetival 35 + Vertofix 80
MUSK    ── Romandolide 300 + Ethylene Brassylate 200 + Zenolide 120 + Ambrettolide 10% 45
FIX     ── Benzyl Salicylate 250
SPARKLE ── Eugenol 1% 10
```
