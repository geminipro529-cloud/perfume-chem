# Osmanthus Dark Crystal - 30 mL EdP

## Brief

Modern woody-aromatic sport luxury: a cold bergamot/lime cut opens onto an
apricot-tea osmanthus heart, then mineral Ambrox, clean cedar woods, patchouli,
and a quiet bitter-cocoa shadow. Osmanthus is the named character. Cocoa is
background texture, not a gourmand theme.

Montblanc Explorer is used only as an architectural reference (bright aromatic
top, patchouli/Ambrox heart, vetiver-leather-cedar base). No proprietary formula
or measured Explorer OAV ratios are available, so this is not represented as a
clone or quantitative reconstruction.

## Evidence and uncertainty

- Distilled-lime OAV uses a conservative composite based on published
  commercial-oil composition and GC-O evidence (Chisholm, Wilson & Gaskey,
  2003, DOI 10.1002/ffj.1172).
- Osmanthus uses the repository's published GC-O composite model.
- There is no Osmanthus EO in the live inventory. The in-stock cost substitute
  is neat `Osmanthus Absolute (volume grade)`, documented as a lower-beta-ionone
  structural grade. Its conservative composite uses the published osmanthus
  fingerprint at 25% characterized potency, matching the repository's existing
  0.5 ppb premium / 2.0 ppb volume-grade effective-ODT ratio. Exact supplier
  batch GC-MS/GC-O remains unavailable.
- Premium 10% osmanthus stock falls from 1,300 to 300 uL (76.9% less stock;
  130 to 30 uL active premium absolute). The 200 uL neat volume grade supplies
  economical tea-apricot body. The modeled combined osmanthus fingerprint is
  about 61.5% of the former load; the pipeline OAV below is authoritative for
  the new mixture.
- Cocoa Absolute is neat user stock and is distinct from Cocoa CO2 Extract.
  The 40 uL neat absolute supplies the premium bitter-roasted definition; the
  250 uL of 7.7% CO2 Extract supplies economical creamy chocolate body.
- Both cocoa naturals now use separate, literature-derived composite models.
  At the opening, the neat Cocoa Absolute resolves to OAV 21.88 from its broad
  cocoa-liquor aldehyde/pyrazine fingerprint; the 7.7% Cocoa CO2 stock resolves
  to OAV 0.0034 from the conservative supercritical-fluid trimethylpyrazine
  floor. These are non-normalized lower-bound estimates, not supplier batch
  assays. The CO2 result is sub-threshold as a standalone character note at this
  dose, but does not disprove a structural/body effect that requires sensory
  testing. The two materials never share one composite.
- The Ambrox stock is the available ~33% w/v DEP:EtOH stock; the depleted 30%
  stock is not used.
- Romandolide is depleted. Zenolide supplies the clean red-fruit musk veil,
  Ethylene Brassylate supplies fixation, and 10% Ambrettolide supplies a small
  osmanthus-compatible fruity/skin accent. These low-vapor musks do not claim
  to reproduce Romandolide's former high-vapor projection.

## Trial protocol

Make a 10 mL evaluation first: divide every raw dose by 3, giving 1,746.67 uL
raw concentrate plus 8.2533 mL ethanol. Evaluate at 24 hours, 7 days,
and 21 days. Judge the cocoa accord by blind blotter/skin comparisons against
one control without Cocoa Absolute and one without Cocoa CO2 Extract. Do not
increase either material from the pipeline's conservative OAV alone.
