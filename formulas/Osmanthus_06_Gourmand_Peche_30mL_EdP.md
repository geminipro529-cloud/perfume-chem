# Osmanthus Gourmand Pêche — 30mL EdP

## Character
A gourmand composition built around osmanthus absolute's natural apricot-peach lactonic character, amplified into a caramelized-peach dessert accord. Gamma Decalactone and Apritone expand the peach register, while Ethyl Maltol and Vanillin create a cotton-candy-vanilla cloud. The patchouli-amber base provides the necessary non-edible anchor that prevents tipping into food-flavoring territory. Think Thierry Mugler Angel's gourmand-patchouli architecture with osmanthus replacing the red-berry accord, or a more sophisticated Lancôme La Vie est Belle.

**Family**: Gourmand — caramelized peach, vanilla-coumarin, patchouli anchor

## Material Justification

- **Osmanthus Absolute (550 µL of 10% = 55 µL active)**: The natural anchor — osmanthus's apricot-leather-tea character is the bridge between the fruity top and the gourmand base. At this dose, it's clearly present as the character note while leaving room for the gourmand cloud.

- **Gamma Decalactone (80 µL) + Apritone (10%, 150 µL = 15 µL active)**: The peach axis — Gamma Decalactone provides the lactonic peach-skin character; Apritone adds the fresh-apricot juiciness. Together they amplify osmanthus's natural apricot facet. Gamma Decalactone at 80 µL neat is a substantial dose that makes the peach character unmistakable.

- **Paradisamide (10%, 60 µL = 6 µL active)**: Tropical-fruity complexity — guava/passionfruit character at modest dose adds depth to what would otherwise be a one-dimensional peach. NOT a muguet material — placed here specifically for its fruit-modifying role. At 6 µL active, it's supportive, not dominant.

- **Ethyl Maltol (10%, 120 µL = 12 µL active)**: The cotton-candy cloud — the signature gourmand molecule. At moderate dose, it creates the caramelized-sugar top note without becoming cloying. Placed in the top section to create immediate gourmand recognition.

- **Cocoa Absolute (10% in TEC, 60 µL = 6 µL active)**: Chocolate-patchouli bridge — the dark, bitter cocoa facet adds depth to the patchouli base and prevents the vanilla from reading as simple frosting. At trace dose, it's a shadow, not a chocolate note.

- **Patchouli EO (250 µL)**: The non-edible anchor — essential for gourmand architecture. Without patchouli (or an equivalent dark-earthy material), the fragrance tips into food-flavoring territory. At 250 µL, it's the structural spine that makes the composition read as perfume, not dessert.

- **Ambermax (50%, 100 µL = 50 µL active)**: Warm, rounded, full-bodied amber — chosen over Ambrofix because gourmands need cozy warmth, not crystalline mineral. The "cozy" amber register supports the caramel-vanilla base.

- **Ethylene Brassylate (160 µL)**: Creamy-lactonic depth musk — the lactonic quality deepens the peach-cream character. Chosen over Habanolide because the lactonic facet of EB pairs specifically with the Gamma Decalactone peach-skin effect.

- **Heliotropal (50 µL neat)**: Sweet-almond-powdery bridge — connects the vanilla-coumarin base to the floral heart. Chosen over Anisaldehyde because almond pairs naturally with peach (think peach-almond tart).
