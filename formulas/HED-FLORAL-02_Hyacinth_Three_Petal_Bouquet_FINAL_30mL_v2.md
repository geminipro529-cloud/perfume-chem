## Target and Design Decision

This revision makes PADMA the protected hyacinth subject. Rosewater petal, jasmine-like floral ester, and soft muguet/lily are subordinate facets rather than coequal flowers. The result is intended to be cool, green-stemmed, honeyed-floral, and dimensional without becoming detergent muguet, cut grass, aquatic floral, Rose perfume, or a Hedione perfume.

The v1 DPG padding is removed. It did not deepen the flower and its only purpose was to make a nominal 6,000 µL concentrate phase. The v2 label therefore reports the actual aromatic-stock charge rather than claiming a padded 20% concentrate.

## Deep-Plane Contract

1. **Identity:** PADMA owns the odor object; all other flowers must read as facets inside hyacinth.
2. **Morphology:** living bitter-green stem, fresh ester surface, broad petal, lily edge, and a sheer lower film.
3. **Function:** every support has one job; Benzyl Benzoate is structural and is not credited as a smelled note.
4. **Relation:** Galbanum opens PADMA's living stem; PADMA connects downward through PEA and Benzyl Acetate, sideways through Cyclamen/Mayol/Florol, and into Hexyl Salicylate at the late heart.
5. **Mixture:** the formula must remain one hyacinth-centered floral rather than four separable mini-accords.
6. **Temporal:** PADMA and Benzyl Acetate lead; PEA and the muguet set broaden the heart; Florol/Hexyl Salicylate carry the late floral film.
7. **Spatial:** a centered, cool flower with moderate petal width and no wide jasmine cloud.
8. **Physicochemical:** raw-volume arithmetic is exact; ppm and OAV remain model-bound proxies.
9. **Biological:** no receptor, adaptation, irritation, or population-response claim is promoted.
10. **Hedonic:** beauty and liking are independent sensory criteria and remain NOT TESTED.
11. **Inventory/build:** every source material occurs in the live inventory; prepare and label the Galbanum 10% v/v working stock before dosing. PADMA's name crosswalk remains split between the formula science profile and V5 stock record.
12. **Evidence/authority:** the design is final; smell, safety, stability, performance, and release authority are withheld.
13. **Experiment:** first resolve PADMA causality and Hedione takeover with separate constant-total omission arms.

## ODT/OAV Takeover Screen

The existing `build_formula_state` model at 305 K gives an active-concentrate screening total vapor of approximately **126.26 ppm**. Its opening OAV order is: PADMA **614.8**, Hedione **459.9**, Cyclamen Aldehyde **60.2**, Galbanum EO composite **56.7**, Mayol **48.5**, PEA **30.3**, Benzyl Acetate **26.2**, Hexyl Salicylate **5.11**, Florol **2.04**, and Benzyl Benzoate **0.025**.

Interpretation is deliberately limited:

- PADMA is the modeled leader and the principal identity channel.
- Hedione remains a strong takeover alarm even at 40 µL; it must not be increased before a physical comparison.
- Galbanum is dosed through a 10% working stock because 10 µL neat modeled near PADMA and risked turning the perfume into galbanum. At 1 µL nominal EO active it remains a natural green-stem hypothesis.
- Cyclamen Aldehyde and Mayol are potent despite their small raw volumes; detergent/muguet drift is an explicit failure mode.
- Benzyl Benzoate is sub-threshold and is admitted only as a structural hypothesis.
- OAV is not perceived percentage, similarity, beauty, or proof of performance.

## Explicit Exclusions

- **Liffarome 10% w/w:** rejected; green pear would compete with the hyacinth subject, and its carrier is unresolved.
- **Cis-3-Hexenyl Salicylate 20% w/w:** rejected; it would add a persistent cut-leaf film, and its carrier is unresolved.
- **Caryophyllene Acetate 20% w/w:** rejected; no target-linked spicy-woody floral job, and its carrier is unresolved.
- Benzyl Salicylate, musks, woods, ambers, ozonics, marine materials, aldehydic lift, Rose Oxide, and damascones are excluded.
- Petitgrain, Tagetes, Eucalyptus, Geranium, and other essential oils are excluded because Galbanum already supplies the only required natural green-stem function.
- Farnesol is omitted because its modeled role at the compounding floor is sub-threshold and the formula already has two lower floral films.
