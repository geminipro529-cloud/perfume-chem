# Bleu Luxe Aldéhydé v2 — 30 mL Eau de Parfum

**Batch:** 30.00 mL · ~20.0% EdP · 57 materials
**Profile:** Bleu de Chanel axis — citrus-woody-amber modern chypre, elevated with aldehyde sparkle, orris luxury, and naturalistic musk depth.
**Differentiation:** seven luxury-material axes absent from mainstream Bleu-type fragrances:
1. Aldehyde cocktail C10–C12 (Chanel-sparkle DNA)
2. Full orris register — α-Irone + α-Isomethyl Ionone + β-Ionone
3. Four-material sandalwood chord (Javanol dry / Ebanol creamy / Sandalore fresh / Polysantol floral)
4. Ambrettolide — macrocyclic naturalistic skin warmth (warmer than Habanolide)
5. Oranger Crystals + Ylang Extra — neroli-orange blossom luxury without FO
6. Labdanum + Benzoin + Olibanum — resinous amber warmth replacing FO myrrh
7. Paradisamide + DBCA — tropical-gardenia radiance at trace

**Opening:** sparkling aldehydes + bergamot-cedrat, radiant orris-rose-jasmine heart, multi-layer sandalwood + resinous amber base.
**Maceration:** 4 weeks minimum
**V1→V2:** removed Lime Distilled (max 2 citrus rule), Géosmine 5→2 µL (ODT threshold), corrected base arithmetic (3 435, not 3 385), heart +50 µL to hold 20% concentration.

### D — Top up with ethanol

1. Pour ethanol 96% to the **30 mL** mark.
2. Concentrate is ~5 997 µL; add approximately **24.00 mL ethanol** (adjust visually to the 30 mL line).
3. Cap tightly. Invert **50 times** — gently, no shaking.
4. Label: "Bleu Luxe Aldéhydé v2 — 2026-05-07 — macerate 4 weeks — open after 2026-06-04."

---

## Maceration

Store cool and dark. Invert gently once a week.
**Minimum 4 weeks** — the heavy Ambrofix base, multi-layer sandalwood chord, resins, and moss need time to integrate.
The aldehydic-citrus top softens after 3–4 weeks; the orris register and labdanum fully bloom at 6 weeks.

## Accord Architecture

### Top — Aldehydic Citrus Sparkle (892 µL, 14.9% of concentrate)
The opening delivers the luxury contract immediately: the C10–C12 aldehyde cocktail (190 µL of dilution, 1.9 µL total active) produces the waxy-clean sparkle signature of Chanel perfumes — champagne effervescence at first sniff. Bergamot EO + Cedrat Sicilian = two natural citrus oils (max 2 rule). Methyl Pamplemousse + Floralozone provide modern brightness and ozonic transparency without a third citrus. Petitgrain + Linalyl Acetate bridge into the floral heart. Geosmin 2 µL dilution trace = petrichor at perception threshold — mineral without rooty earth.

### Heart — Orris-Rose-Jasmine Radiance (1 690 µL, 28.2%)
The heart is built around the luxury orris register: α-Isomethyl Ionone (190 µL) provides radiant powdery-violet sparkle, β-Ionone (80 µL) adds woody-floral depth, α-Irone 30% (30 µL) contributes the most noble and expensive iris material in the inventory. The trio is pyramidal for a clear iris reading, not a violet fog.

Rose reads through PEA (petal) + Geraniol (brightness) + Rose Oxide 10% (metallic) + α-Damascone 10% (rich ketone). Damascenone 1% trace complexifies with apple-rose facets.

Jasmine is suggested, not literal: Hedione + Hedione HC for jasmonate radiance, Cis Jasmone trace for green-jasmine depth, ACA trace for jasmine-muguet waxy body (IFRA-compliant at 0.033% of finished product).

FO substitution: Oranger Crystals 10% + Petitgrain replace Neroli FO with a more sophisticated orange-blossom-grape effect. Clary Sage + Spike Lavender replace Lavender FO — Sage brings herbal-coumarin roundness, Spike the camphoraceous lift.

DBCA (70 µL) injects gardenia-rose cosmetic character — clean luxury, not detergent. Ylang Extra (40 µL) contributes creamy-salicylate floral opulence, softening the heart-to-base transition. Paradisamide 10% (40 µL) is the secret tropical sparkle — guava-passionfruit at the perception threshold, only detectable in the sillage. Freesia HDI + Cyclamen Aldehyde create green-floral transparency.

Fixation: Benzyl Salicylate (200 µL, heavy cosmetic cushion) + Hexyl Salicylate (100 µL, lightweight transparent film) in gradient — heavy under light for layered diffusion.

### Base — Sandalwood-Amber-Chypre (3 415 µL, 56.9%)
The base is anchored by a 4-material sandalwood chord — each molecule occupies a distinct textural register:
- **Javanol (20 µL):** dry-mineral-intimate
- **Ebanol (120 µL):** creamy-silky soft envelopment
- **Sandalore (150 µL):** fresh-woody column lift
- **Polysantol (80 µL):** floral-creamy heart bridge

Wood framework: Iso E Super (700 µL, abstract molecular cocoon) + Cedarwood EO (250 µL, natural bite) + Timberol (40 µL, architectural precision) + Vertofix (50 µL, fixative bridge).

FO substitution: Clearwood (80 µL, clean-earthy) replaces Patchouli FO — same earthy function without heavy camphor. Vetival (30 µL, suede-vetiver dryness) + Nagarmortha (15 µL, smoky cypriol) replace Vetiver FO — more sophisticated, less raw-rooty.

Amber warmth: Azarbre (80 µL, cedar-amber smooth) + Cashmeran (100 µL, textile warmth) + Labdanum 10% (100 µL, amber-resinous-animalic) + Benzoin 50% (150 µL, balsamic sweetness). Olibanum Resinoid (30 µL) spaces the structure with incensed transparency. Coumarin 20% (60 µL) + Heliotropal (30 µL) form the powdered-tonka accord — echo of the heart's orris register.

3-axis musk accord:
- **Projection:** Romandolide (250 µL) — best sillage musk in inventory, projects outward
- **Depth:** Ethylene Brassylate (150 µL) — creamy-lactonic, anchors on skin + Ambrettolide 10% (80 µL) — macrocyclic skin-wine natural, intimate warmth
- Ambrettolide also contributes the fruity-wine facet that echoes Paradisamide in the heart

**Why not Habanolide:** the register is luxury-warm-natural; Habanolide pulls toward synthetic-clean. Ambrettolide + Ethylene Brassylate produce more sophisticated skin warmth.

Projection: Ambrofix 30% (600 µL) — crystalline projection head. Kephalis (30 µL) structures with minimal woody-amber power.

Chypre anchor: Evernyl (20 µL) locks the structure — without it, the base collapses into soft amber.

## Why Each FO Substitution

| FO Avoided | Replacement | Reasoning |
|-----------|------------|-----------|
| Neroli FO | Oranger Crystals 10% + Petitgrain EO | Oranger Crystals = authentic neroli-grape; Petitgrain = bitter orange leaf freshness. More sophisticated, less "soap." |
| Lavender FO | Clary Sage EO + Spike Lavender EO | Sage = herbal-coumarin roundness; Spike = camphoraceous lift. Two real essential oils covering the lavender spectrum without FO. |
| Vetiver FO | Vetival + Nagarmortha | Vetival = suede-vetiver dryness; Nagarmortha = smoky-earthy cypriol. More niche, less raw-rooty. |
| Patchouli FO | Clearwood | Modern clean-earthy — patchouli function without heavy camphor. More transparent, better woody integration. |
| Myrrh FO | Labdanum Absolute 10% + Benzoin 50% | Labdanum = amber-resinous-animalic; Benzoin = balsamic sweetness. Covers resinous warmth + softness. |
| Champaca Absolute | Ylang Extra + DBCA + Oranger Crystals | Ylang = creamy floral opulence; DBCA = gardenia; Oranger Crystals = orange blossom. Trio covers exotic white floral register without absolute. |
