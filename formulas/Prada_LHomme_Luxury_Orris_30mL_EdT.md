## Concept Lock

This is the same clean neroli-iris, geranium, pepper, amber, cedar, and patchouli architecture as the control. Luxury comes from substitution inside the iris module: part of the large synthetic methyl-ionone body is removed and replaced by Orris Liquid plus Alpha Irone. It is not an additive pile-up. Exaltolide replaces part of Ethylene Brassylate to add a soft skin-fatty echo under the rooty iris while Zenolide retains clean projection.

The active-ppm column is a volume-basis screening value: `active uL / 5000 uL x 1,000,000`. Orris Liquid's 30% stock basis and carrier remain incompletely documented; the values below use the declared fraction for screening and must not be represented as exact ppm w/w. Headspace OAV remains modeled, and natural mixtures use composite OAV.

## Selection and Rejection Audit

All non-iris registers retain the control choices so the experiment has one interpretable variable. Irotyl and Ultralia were rejected because they would stylize the powder axis; Carrot Seed EO was rejected because it could exaggerate earthy-carrot character; Beta Ionone was kept very low because its OAV can overwhelm Orris Liquid. Orris FTEC and I-IRIS FTEC are not in the live inventory. Romandolide is explicitly depleted and is not used. Ambrettolide was rejected here because its fruity-wine warmth would compete with the clean pressed-shirt direction; Exaltolide is the more coherent character-echo musk.

## Bench Boundary

Run a coded 5 mL or smaller trial before committing 30 mL. Compare control and luxury variants blind after identical maturation. Do not add more Orris Liquid directly to the source bottle if the first trial is weak: use the immutable addition-only rescue contract and test a separate aliquot after documenting bottle mass, stock basis, carrier, density, and safety category.
