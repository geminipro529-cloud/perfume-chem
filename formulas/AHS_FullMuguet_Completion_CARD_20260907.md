## Test boundary and decision

The absolute amount of existing aldehydes is unchanged. Adding 0.600 mL to nominal 30.840 mL reduces their volume-based concentration by only about **1.91%**. Any larger sensory softening would have to come from a mixture effect, which is NOT TESTED. Nothing chemically cancels or removes the aldehydes. The sour impression has not been established as an acid or degradation diagnosis.

For a controlled bench comparison, use separate coded samples: untreated current perfume; an equal 5.140 mL portion plus 100 µL of the same ethanol; and the full module above. The ethanol arm controls volume dilution, not exact solvent composition. Compare equal blotter applications with preparation ages matched at 5/30 minutes and 2/4 hours; repeat after 24–48 hours. Do not spray on skin under this unreleased design, and do not return test portions to the main bottle.

Accept only if connected lily petal fullness improves, sour/aldehydic discomfort does not increase, and mandarin/wood identity stays recognizable. Reject a rose-led, oily/waxy, soapy, greener/sharper or detached-floral result. Separate equal-volume omission comparisons of PEDMC, added Hedione and Farnesol are required before claiming they each improve the accord.

Scaling after a successful, otherwise-cleared comparison uses the **measured remaining current-parent volume R**, not the original 30 mL label: multiply the nominal equivalent doses by R/30.840. Sampling and spraying reduce R. The component-sum bottle history is 30.740 mL, 0.100 mL lower than the user-called volume; no missing top-up is assumed.
