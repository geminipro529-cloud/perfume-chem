# Osmanthus Tabac Fumé — 30mL EdP

## Character
A warm tobacco-coumarinic composition where osmanthus absolute provides fruity-apricot contrast to the hay-like, smoky tobacco base. The osmanthus's leather-tea facet pairs naturally with cured tobacco leaf, creating a sophisticated tobacco-gourmand that avoids the heavy sweetness of Tobacco Vanille clones. Ethyl Safranate adds a saffron-leather dimension, while the coumarin-tonka base provides warm-hay depth. Think Serge Lutens Chergui with osmanthus replacing honey, or a lighter, more transparent Tom Ford Tobacco Vanille.

**Family**: Tobacco / Coumarinic — cured tobacco leaf, warm hay, osmanthus fruit contrast

## Material Justification

- **Tobacco Absolute (10%, 150 µL = 15 µL active)**: The cured-tobacco-leaf character — warm, slightly animalic, hay-like. At 15 µL active, it's clearly present without dominating. Tobacco Absolute at 10% dilution allows precise control — the material is potent and easily overdosed.

- **Ethyl Safranate (40 µL neat)**: Saffron-leather complexity — adds the spicy-leather facet that distinguishes sophisticated tobacco fragrances from simple sweet-tobacco. Chosen because osmanthus's leather facet already provides a partial leather register; Ethyl Safranate bridges the osmanthus leather with the tobacco hay.

- **Tonkarome (10%, 100 µL = 10 µL active)**: Firmenich coumarinic-tonka base — a pre-built tonka accord that provides the warm-hay sweetness without requiring high Coumarin dosing. Complements the Coumarin (20%, 100 µL = 20 µL active) for a layered coumarinic effect.

- **Habanolide (80 µL neat)**: Warm-skin macrocyclic musk — chosen specifically for tobacco compositions because the warm-intimate character pairs with cured tobacco's natural warmth. The "what are you wearing" effect of Habanolide creates the skin-close intimacy that tobacco fragrances need.

- **Nagarmotha Oil (40 µL)**: Cypriol profile — dry, earthy, smoky-woody. Functions as a dark-smoke accent beneath the tobacco. At modest dose, it prevents the tobacco-coumarin from becoming too sweet-gourmand.

- **Red Mandarin EO (150 µL)**: Sweet-tangerine citrus — chosen over Bergamot as the secondary citrus because Red Mandarin's warm, rounded sweetness pairs with tobacco and oriental registers. Bergamot provides the classical-fresh lift; Red Mandarin provides the warm-orange depth.

- **Cashmeran (140 µL neat)**: Textile-warmth at moderate dose — adds the "wrapped in cashmere" sensation that defines the tobacco-gourmand territory. Within the family-shifting ceiling for this concentrate volume.

- **Coumarin (20%, 100 µL = 20 µL active)**: The warm-hay sweet-tobacco backbone. At this dose, it's substantial enough to define the coumarinic character without shifting the composition to full oriental.
