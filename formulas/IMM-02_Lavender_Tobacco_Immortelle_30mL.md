## E. CONTROLLED TEST PLAN

1. Compound at **one-tenth scale** first. Keep every stock and total stock-blend mass fixed.
2. Run the recognizer ladder with Immortelle Absolute at **0×, 0.5× and 1×** while Helichrysum EO remains fixed; replace removed stock mass with the same exact-stock carrier.
3. Evaluate blind at **5 min, 30 min, 2 h, 8 h and 24 h** for immortelle recognition, honey/hay/curry balance, sock-like or greasy excess, aromatic lift and drydown continuity.
4. Repeat the winning arm on blotter and skin because the temporal model is a local headspace sensitivity screen, not human longevity or sillage.
5. Record physical liking, target similarity, stability, safety and release status as **NOT TESTED** until real evidence exists.
