## Selection and Rejection Ledger

- The three-musks chord covers depth, diffusion, and skin character with Ethylene Brassylate, Galaxolide, and Exaltolide.
- Lemon and bergamot are supported by official notes and public material ranking; Cedrat adds the bitter citron contour available in inventory.
- Literal Basil EO was rejected after the pipeline exposed missing composite OAV coverage. Indian basil literature documents multiple materially different chemotypes, and no supplier-batch assay identifies this bottle; Terpinyl Acetate is a transparent functional bridge, not an odor-identical basil substitute.
- Calone stays at 0.2 active uL so it behaves as humidity and lift rather than melon-water.
- Florol supplies safe modern muguet volume; Lilial is excluded.
- Nutmeg, coriander, cinnamon, mint, and elemi appear in the public secondary list but were rejected from this compressed study because they are trace-ranked and would add uncontrolled channels.
- No claim is made that Black Pepper EO is odor-identical to white pepper.

## Mixing and Evaluation

1. Preblend woods, musks, coumarin/tonka materials, and Ambrofix.
2. Add floral-air, violet, and green materials.
3. Premix the six-material signature module separately; add it immediately before the remaining citrus.
4. Add 4.5 mL concentrate to 25.5 mL ethanol 96%; invert gently.
5. Mature at least 14 days. Compare directly with the structural chassis at 0, 5, 30 minutes, 2 hours, and 4 hours.
