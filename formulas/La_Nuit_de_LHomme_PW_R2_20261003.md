Official architecture is documented, but proportions below are creative development targets. ## Executive decision and locked brief

The development lead is a soft, green-cardamom aromatic-woody composition: bergamot and cardamom first, an airy lavender/ionone bridge, then restrained coumarin over smooth cedar, polished vetiver and musks. It deliberately does not turn the EDT into a blue shower-gel fragrance, a dense vanilla gourmand or a superamber bomb.

Protected recognizers: cardamom; soft aromatic heart; restrained tonka warmth; cedar texture. Negative space: eucalyptus medicine, pencil-shaving harshness, iris lipstick, loud muguet, laundry-musk takeover, smoke/leather, caramel and aggressive amberwoods. These are **sensory gates to test**, not claims already passed.

YSL's US page identifies cardamom, cedar and coumarin and describes iris/tonka; its international page describes bergamot, lavender/cedar and vetiver. These pages establish intended architecture and limited ingredient presence, not exact amounts or a single authenticated commercial batch. [YSL US](https://www.yslbeautyus.com/fragrance/mens-fragrances/la-nuit-de-lhomme/la-nuit-de-lhomme-eau-de-toilette-spray/426YSL.html), [YSL international](https://www.yslbeauty.com/int/fragrance/fragrance-for-him/la-nuit-de-l-homme/la-nuit-de-l-homme-eau-de-toilette/426YSL.html).

## What this retry changes

1. Replaces mixed-volume arithmetic and fixed 25.5 mL alcohol with explicit product-mass, stock-carrier and final-endpoint accounting.
2. Uses **neat crystal Ambrox Super**, not the depleted historical 30% Ambrofix liquid.
3. Restores **Vetiveryl Acetate** as its own polished-vetiver function. Natural vetiver and Vertofix do not become chemical substitutes for it.
4. Uses PW **Alpha Isomethyl Ionone 3IW00300** on its own identity. It is not asserted identical to depleted Gamma Coeur.
5. Chooses two musk roles, Galaxolide for clean bulk and Exaltolide for softer skin/powder, rather than the inherited three-musk stack. Controlled alternatives must test their nonredundancy.
6. Separates small literal cedar grain from abstract woods; avoids repeating the old Luxe formula's unusually large raw cedar allocation. Old volume percentages are not converted to this formula's mass percentages.
7. Uses low-level natural/trace detail only as a testable block. The simpler control can win.
8. Does not import 50 names from an unauthenticated reconstruction roster merely to inflate richness. Missing Amberwood F, Trimofix, Muscenone Delta, Edenolide and other proprietary grades are not quietly replaced or declared equivalent.

## Product-mass basis — read this once

The target totals **100 parts by mass of undiluted perfumery-product equivalents**. Whole oils retain their unknown natural constituents, and commercial grades retain their product identity. This is not 100 parts of analytically identified pure odorants.

The weighed recipe below prepares **5.000 g product equivalent in 5.945 g actual working concentrate**. Its **0.945 g working carrier** is tracked separately. Prefer neat major materials here to avoid unnecessarily large DPG/DEP loads. Existing diluted bottles are not assumed to have been prepared on a mass basis.

Every table amount is **grams**, except the explicit Ambrox note in mg. No stock-specific densities were measured, so no fictional µL equivalents are printed. If a real stock density is available, µL = 1000 × grams / density in g/mL.

## Chassis and family-to-function map

The chassis is aromatic softness plus transparent cedar/musk, not maximum woody power.

| Block | Protected / mobile status | Intended transition | Important failure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cardamom EO + terpinyl acetate, trace pepper | Cardamom identity protected; ester/pepper mobile | Citrus/cool spice → aromatic heart | Medicinal cineole, pine or generic pepper |
| Bergamot + linalyl acetate + linalool, limited DHM | Bergamot entrance protected; DHM mobile | Peel freshness → aromatic bridge | Blue detergent/shower gel |
| Ethyl linalool + Grosso + Hedione + limited Florol | Aromatic continuity protected; exact grade/radiance mobile | Lavender air → softly powdered wood | Camphor, white-floral or muguet takeover |
| AIMI + coumarin + trace vanillin/petal/fruity detail | Restrained tonka warmth protected; decoration mobile | Soft powder/tonka → skin warmth | Lipstick iris, hay dominance or candy |
| Iso E + Vertofix + small cedar, acetate and natural vetiver | Soft cedar/vetiver frame protected; drywood load mobile | Diffuse woods → restrained root texture | Pencil shavings, dry scratch or earthy vetiver |
| Sandalore/Bacdanol/Cashmeran/controlled Ambrox | Mobile rounding/texture | Wood warmth without giant amber pressure | Generic woody-amber perfume |
| Galaxolide + Exaltolide | Distinct proposed roles, not both mandatory by dogma | Clean body → soft intimate skin texture | Laundry, powder washout, redundant musks |

## Mixing order and practical stop conditions

1. Confirm the actual product labels and working-stock fractions used. Missing a trace bottle? Use the explicitly simpler control, not an improvised substitute.
2. Weigh Galaxolide and the bulk soft woods into a clean, closable mixing vessel. Add Exaltolide and crystal Ambrox by mass; disperse into the liquid base. Add coumarin and the other weighed solids/solutions. Do not confuse a stock-preparation recipe with a perfume dose.
3. Add the remaining heart materials and declared trace stocks; then natural aromatic/citrus oils. Cardamom and bergamot can go near the end to minimize open-vessel handling.
4. Mix and check homogeneity at room temperature before taking an aliquot. **Do not portion a master containing settled crystals.**
5. If extra solvent is needed to dissolve the master, add and record an exact mass of the same perfumer's alcohol stock. Let the master become homogeneous and use the general dilution formula below. Do not add an unrecorded 400 µL TEC or arbitrary IPM.
6. Keep ethanol away from flame, ignition and heated open equipment. Do not use a household microwave/hot plate to accelerate dissolution.
7. Label the finished sample as PW-R2, date, loading basis and actual stocks. Keep a small master reserve if convenient.
8. If precipitation, persistent clouding, unexpected stratification or a mass-accounting discrepancy occurs, pause rather than compensate blindly. Solubility/stability have not been tested.

## Lightweight test plan

Smell one lead prototype after dissolution/settling, then revisit after about one week under the same conditions. Aging is a comparison variable, not a guarantee of improvement. Keep the reference and target on identical blotters; use the **actual original EDT bottle** you want to match.

If comparing two or three trials, use coded labels and record actual applied mass or the same controlled procedure. Equal product loading does not automatically imply equal spray deposition.

Evaluate around 5 minutes, 30–60 minutes, 4 hours and next-day blotter. Record cardamom recognition, aromatic softness, cedar dryness, tonka sweetness, laundry risk, closeness to your reference and personal liking **separately**. Repeat a promising comparison on another day; formal causal claims need stronger controlled repetition.

| Observation | Next test, not an automatic additive fix |
|---|---|
| Pleasant but generic blue/shower gel | Cardamom-clear alternative; inspect DHM/Florol |
| Cineolic, medicinal or piney | Lower/replace the cardamom oil lot or lower Grosso/terpinyl module; do not add more sweetness as camouflage |
| Dry/scratchy and spice disappears | Soft-wood alternative; evaluate Vertofix/Cedramber/Cashmeran block |
| Laundry/powder dominates | Musk alternative; then controlled single-musk omission |
| Tonka reads as hay or candy | Reduce coumarin/vanillin in a new target version |
| Lead not discernibly better than control | Keep control and do not buy trace details for paper complexity |
| Reference is smoother or differs greatly at heart | Update the target from actual blind observations, not an invented GC-MS ranking |

You cannot remove ingredients from a completed bottle. This campaign is a new-formula comparison, not a negative-delta bottle rescue. A later additive modification needs a measured current-bottle state and a separately confirmed proposal.
