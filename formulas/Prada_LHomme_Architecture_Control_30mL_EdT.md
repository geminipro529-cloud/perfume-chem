## Concept Lock

The north star is a clean, pressed-shirt iris: neroli and black pepper at the entrance, a smooth violet-iris and geranium heart, then transparent amber, cedar, patchouli, and a clean musk trail. The control uses inventory synthetics for the iris body so the luxury study can replace part of that same module with Orris Liquid rather than merely stacking expensive material on top.

The active-ppm column is a volume-basis screening value: `active uL / 5000 uL x 1,000,000`. Exact ppm w/w remains withheld wherever stock basis or density is incomplete. Headspace OAV is calculated by the pipeline from mole fraction, vapor pressure, activity coefficient, and air ODT; natural materials use composite OAV.

## Accord Architecture

- Neroli-linen opening: Neroli EO, linalool, linalyl acetate, black pepper, and two trace aldehydes.
- Clean iris heart: Alpha Isomethyl Ionone, Alpha/Beta Ionone, Dihydro Beta Ionone, Orivone, Mayol, Cyclamen Aldehyde, and Bourgeonal.
- Official wood-amber base: cedar, Timberol, restrained Iso E Super, Amberwood F, Ambrofix, and patchouli.
- Two-axis musk chord: Ethylene Brassylate supplies powder-compatible depth; Zenolide supplies outward clean diffusion and echoes the fresh opening.

## Selection and Rejection Audit

| Register | Chosen | Inventory alternatives evaluated and rejected |
|---|---|---|
| Orange flower | Actual Neroli EO | Aurantiol, Oranger Crystals, and Nerolin Bromelia were not allowed to replace the literal official marker; they would add sweeter or naphthyl orange-flower color |
| Iris body | AIMI, Alpha/Beta Ionone, Dihydro Beta Ionone, Orivone | Orris Liquid and Alpha Irone are reserved for the luxury substitution; Irotyl and Ultralia would add a more stylized or ghost-powder facet |
| Clean floral | Mayol, Cyclamen Aldehyde, Bourgeonal, restrained Hedione | Hydroxycitronellal is depleted; Lilyreal/Lilial carry a less desirable safety and regulatory profile; Florol and Nympheal are modeled sub-threshold at practical support doses |
| Cedar | Cedarwood Virginia, Timberol, limited Iso E Super | Cedarwood EO is less specifically documented in this inventory; Himalayan cedar shifts darker; Koavone and Azarbre add warmth not wanted in a pressed-shirt profile |
| Amber | Amberwood F and Ambrofix | Ambermax and Amber Core are warmer and more opaque; Ambrox Super stock identity is mixed and one stock is depleted |
| Fixation | Hexyl Salicylate and Benzyl Benzoate | Benzyl Salicylate is heavier and more cosmetic; the selected pair gives a light film over a quiet anchor |
| Musk | Ethylene Brassylate and Zenolide | Romandolide is depleted; Ambrettolide and Exaltolide add naturalistic/fatty warmth better reserved for other briefs; Habanolide is not part of the user's current selected musk set |
| Sandalwood | Sandalore | Javanol is drier and more mineral; Ebanol/Bacdanol are creamier and would make the base richer than the clean reference architecture |
