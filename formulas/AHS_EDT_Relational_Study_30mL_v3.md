## Concept Lock

Target the original Allure Homme Sport EDT product identity, not Eau Extreme, Edition Blanche, a heavier Luxe perfume or a Versace hybrid. No historical bottle year or batch has been authenticated. The reference contract named above is explicitly **pending/unregistered**; a missing-contract failure is expected and must not be bypassed by calling the fragrance unclaimed or treating a generic-family pass as proof of identity.

This v3 is one experimental alternative to the unchanged v2, not an accepted improvement or an exact copy. Complexity means target-linked richness, depth, integration, contrast and temporal reveal; none has been observed in this candidate. The materials and their proposed functions are not independent perceptual channels. Safety, stability, liking, sillage, longevity, strict similarity and release remain NOT TESTED/HOLD.

## TARGET / IDEAL — independent of inventory

Seek a recognizable mandarin/citrus foreground, a connected rather than standalone aquatic impression, transparent floral/aromatic continuity into cedar, and a controlled almond-tonka/white-musk finish. These are evidence-informed design objectives, not measured observations. A disclosed natural or chemical class does not establish its quantity or require a separate recipe row. Sandalore is a chosen synthetic interpretation, not equivalence to Santalum album oil; local Vertofix is not proven to be a specific supplier grade.

| Proposed observation window | Foreground hypothesis | Background and continuity question | Reject this direction if |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opening | mandarin with aromatic freshness and peel definition | does the water/floral suggestion belong to the same overall image? | mandarin is obscured or replaced by generic cleaner/melon |
| 5 minutes | citrus/aromatic freshness remains recognizable | floral/ozonic texture may connect the opening into wood | an independent green/cyclamen/hay note takes over |
| 30 minutes | transparent floral and cedar relationship | does the opening feel connected to the emerging body? | a hollow break, detached floral cloud or blur develops |
| 2 hours | wood texture with restrained coumarinic warmth | does the earlier freshness remain echoed without extra citrus dose? | dry disconnected wood or gourmand sweetness replaces the target |
| 4 hours | clean musk/wood and almond-tonka contour | is the same identity still recognizable? | the base becomes a different perfume |

The windows are a planned evaluation schedule, not established burn-off times. The official description does not provide these timings. Actual foreground/background, richness, depth and reveal are NOT TESTED.

## One intentional relationship exchange

Dihydromyrcenol neat changes from 350 to 300 microlitres; Helional neat changes from 50 to 100 microlitres. Every other stock label and raw amount is retained. This is a 50 microlitre exchange chosen for an exploratory comparison, not a literature-derived optimum. The hypothesis is more floral/ozonic connection relative to fresh aromatic/lemon support, without more Calone, sweeter base, additional musks or new ingredients.

The contrast changes two ingredients together. Any sensory result belongs to that complete contrast, not either ingredient in isolation or an established synergy. Equal raw stock volume does not preserve exact active mass, odor strength, mole fractions or evaporation. No post-hoc strength normalization is part of this comparison.

## Material selection and alternatives

- **Citrus identity:** retain Red Mandarin as the proposed leader with Bergamot definition and Petitgrain leaf/heart connection. Lemon, lime, Cedrat, grapefruit, blood orange, D-limonene and specialty citrus extenders were considered but not added: they would change the citrus hypothesis while the water/floral contrast is being tested. No absence claim about Chanel follows.
- **Aromatic connection:** retain Linalyl Acetate, Linalool and the exact Lavender/geranium traces. Do not switch Lavender grades or add rosemary/sage/coriander merely to resolve a machine mapping problem. Dihydromyrcenol is the only reduced aromatic axis; the chosen reduction is uncalibrated.
- **Floral/ozonic relationship:** increase existing neat Helional, whose IFF description supports this direction and its possible green/hay failure. Keep Calone and both aldehyde stocks unchanged. Cyclamen Aldehyde, Florol, Mayol, Freesia HDI, Floralozone, Scentenal, Adoxal, Triplal, Verdox and specialty greens were not added because new material identity and stock effects would complicate this bounded comparison. This does not establish that they are inferior reconstruction tools.
- **Transparent heart:** retain Hedione, Neroli, Benzyl Salicylate and the restrained ionone/geranium support as control. Do not introduce Hedione HC, Aurantiol, additional orange-flower bases, jasmine or other floral layers without evidence of a missing role. Benzyl Salicylate versus Hexyl Salicylate/Benzyl Benzoate is a potential separate texture test, not part of v3.
- **Woods:** retain Iso E Super, Vertofix, Cedarwood, Sandalore, Patchouli and Alpha Isomethyl Ionone without claiming their proposed roles are proven nonredundant. Bacdanol, Ebanol, Javanol, sandalwood bases/oil, Cedramber, Timberol, Koavone, Azarbre, Clearwood, Cashmeran, Kephalis, guaiacwood and stronger amberwoods were considered but not introduced: v3 tests the fresh-heart relationship, not a simultaneous change in base identity or intensity. Ownership does not require inclusion. Ambrox Super is not needed for this particular contrast; no unavailable liquid Ambrofix is substituted.
- **Musks:** retain the Galaxolide/Romandolide pair as the existing control, with nonredundancy untested. Ethylene Brassylate, Habanolide, Zenolide, Exaltolide and Ambrettolide are alternatives for a separate supported comparison, not automatic extras. Tonalide, Macrolide and Musk Ketone remain excluded. There is an evidence gap, not a demonstrated musk gap.
- **Warmth and spice:** keep the explicit Coumarin/Vanillin/Black Pepper contour. Tonka Absolute, Tonkarome, Heliotropal, resins and additional spice/sweetening would confound the primary relationship contrast; they are not needed merely to claim complexity.

## Stock and model limitations

Six selected diluted stocks lack a confirmed fraction basis: Galaxolide, Coumarin, Vanillin, C10, C12 MNA and Calone. Vanillin, C10, C12 MNA and Calone also lack a confirmed carrier. Exact stock-solution densities and measured finished mass are not established. Numeric active-quantity and OAV outputs are legacy screening proxies, not exact current-bottle accounting.

The current material data also conflict with supplier VP records for Dihydromyrcenol and Helional, at different stated temperatures; Vertofix identity/MW/VP and exact Lavender coverage remain unresolved. These are recorded in the decision document. Neither the data nor the simulator is recalibrated here. Low modeled C12 MNA OAV or absent Lavender OAV does not prove sensory absence. Natural oils must use composite models; missing lot-specific composition further limits them.

## Controlled comparison and decision

The selected baseline remains the unmodified v2. Compare only v2 versus v3 initially, with consistent lots, final vehicle definition, preparation age and application, anonymous codes, balanced order and repeated observations after execution/safety holds are resolved. Use free description before separate ratings of integration, depth, richness, temporal contrast, intensity, blur/harshness and liking. Judge reference similarity only alongside an authenticated EDT sample. Record ties and uncertainty.

Reject v3 promotion if mandarin identity is obscured, an independent green/floral/ozonic/hay impression appears, or coherent freshness weakens without a useful connection. If there is no reliable useful difference, retain v2. No unobserved score selects a winner. Calone omission is the next optional diagnostic only if needed and only after its carrier/basis permit a controlled replacement; wood/musk changes are deferred.
