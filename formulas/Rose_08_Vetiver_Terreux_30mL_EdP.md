# Rose Vetiver Terreux — 30mL EdP

## Character
An earthy, rooty rose where Rose de Mai Absolute meets the dry, smoky depth of Indian Vetiver EO (ruh khus) and Vetival's suede-vetiver dryness. This is the most textural rose composition — the vetiver accord (Vetiver EO India, Vetival, Vetikon) creates a rough-woven fabric of root, earth, and mineral dryness beneath the rose. The rose core is deliberately warm and honeyed (Citronellol boosted to 300, PEA at 380, Rhodinol at 30 µL for natural richness) to create a maximal contrast with the dry vetiver base. Damascone Beta and Damascenone together provide fruity-woody rose depth that bridges the floral to the rooty-woody. Vetiver EO India — the ruh khus from North India — provides the deep, complex vetiver character that defines this composition: smokier and more layered than standard Vetiver EO. Orivone adds buttery-orris warmth to soften the vetiver austerity, while Amberwood F provides transparent warmth without adding an identifiable amber note. The musk chord (Ethylene Brassylate creamy depth, Romandolide projection, small Ambrettolide earthy warmth) supports the earthy character. Think a rose version of Terre d'Hermès, or the vetiver-rose axis of Frederic Malle Portrait of a Lady, stripped to its earthy essentials.

**Family**: Woody Floral (vetiver-rose) — grapefruit-citrus top, rose heart, vetiver-woody-musk base

## Material Justification

- **Rose de Mai Absolute (10%)**: The natural rose anchor at 400 µL (40 µL active, 0.69% of concentrate). The absolute's honeyed-spicy warmth is the yin to the vetiver's yang — the sweet-rose counterpoint that makes the vetiver-rose pairing one of the great classical contrasts in perfumery. At 400 µL, the absolute provides enough natural rose presence to stand up to the powerful vetiver accord without descending into rose-soliflore territory.

- **Citronellol + Geraniol + PEA + Rhodinol + Nerol**: The molecular rose accord — 5 materials with the warmest, richest configuration. Citronellol (300, boosted) provides maximum fresh-citrus-rose lift — the bright counterpoint to the dark vetiver. Geraniol (60) at maximum — sweet-geranium body. PEA (380, boosted) fills maximum honeyed-rose volume — essential because the vetiver materials absorb olfactory space, and the rose must project through them. Rhodinol (30) at maximum — the natural geranium-rose richness adds the organic complexity that prevents the rose from reading as synthetic against the natural vetiver. Nerol (90) adds sweet citrusy-rose lift that connects the rose to the Grapefruit FCF top.

- **Damascone Beta (10%) + Damascenone (1%) + Rose Oxide (10%)**: The full rose modifier triad — the only Rose formula using all three primary modifiers. Damascone Beta at 90 µL (9 µL active) provides dark fruity-rose ketone — the plum-apple facet that creates the fruity bridge to the rooty vetiver. Damascenone at 70 µL (0.7 µL active) provides honeyed-woody depth — the woody facet that connects rose to the cedar-vetiver base. Rose Oxide at 60 µL (6 µL active) adds metallic-green freshness — the leaf-stem character that prevents the rose from reading as jammy against the dry vetiver. This is the most complex modifier configuration because the vetiver-rose contrast demands maximum rose character depth.

- **Hedione**: Radiance amplifier at 480 µL — standard Hedione chosen over HC because the softer jasmonate character pairs better with the earthy vetiver than the bright HC variant. Lifts the rose heart into the grapefruit-bergamot top, creating the luminous layer that floats above the dark vetiver base.

- **Vetiver EO India + Vetival + Vetikon**: The vetiver accord at 220 + 140 + 100 µL. Vetiver EO India (220 µL) — the ruh khus from North India — provides the deep, authentic vetiver character: smoky, rooty, slightly leathery, more complex than standard Vetiver EO. Chosen as the primary vetiver material because Indian ruh khus has the smoky depth that creates the maximal contrast with the rose. Vetival (140 µL) adds the suede-vetiver dryness — the textural dimension that makes the vetiver read as "suede" rather than "root." Vetikon (100 µL) provides the clean, woody-vetiver structural element — the linear vetiver that extends the accord's longevity without adding more smoke. Three vetiver materials used deliberately: each covers a different vetiver axis (natural depth, suede texture, structural longevity).

- **Patchouli EO + Orivone**: The earthy bridge at 120 + 80 µL. Patchouli EO (120 µL) adds the camphoraceous-earth character that connects the vetiver rootiness to the woody base — at this dose, patchouli supports rather than competes. Orivone (80 µL) provides the buttery-orris warmth — the iris-butter character that softens the vetiver's austerity without adding sweetness. The orris-vetiver pairing is classical in masculine perfumery (Terre d'Hermès uses an orris-vetiver structure), and here it makes the vetiver feel polished rather than raw.

- **Cedarwood EO + Iso E Super + Vertofix + Amberwood F**: The woody base. Cedarwood EO (140 µL) provides natural woody identity. Iso E Super (420 µL) creates the molecular skin-cocoon. Vertofix (180 µL) bridges wood and musk. Amberwood F (140 µL) — chosen over Ambrofix because Amberwood F provides transparent warmth without identifying as "amber" — it is the most invisible structural material, adding warmth solely as a textural quality rather than a note. In a vetiver-rose, the warmth must come from the vetiver and rose themselves, not from a declared amber material.

- **Evernyl (50% in DPG)**: Synthetic moss at 100 µL of 50% (50 µL active). Adds the bitter-green-woody dimension that connects the vetiver's dryness to the woody base. At IFRA-compliant dose.

- **Benzyl Salicylate + Hexyl Salicylate + Benzyl Benzoate**: The three-layer fixative system — the only Rose formula using all three fixatives because vetiver needs a layered fixation architecture. Benzyl Salicylate (200) as the cosmetic cushion — diffuses the rose heart. Hexyl Salicylate (160) as the sheer fixative for the top. Benzyl Benzoate (200) as inert mass for the heavy vetiver base.

- **Ethylene Brassylate**: Creamy depth musk at 260 µL — the lactonic quality echoes the honeyed-rose of PEA and the absolute, providing the skin-level softness that prevents the vetiver from reading as harsh. Essential at this dose because vetiver-rose without a creamy musk would feel like "dry roots with flowers" — EB makes it feel like "warm earth with petals."

- **Romandolide**: Projection musk at 240 µL — the diffusive woody-musk character extends the vetiver-rose sillage outward. Its slight woodiness resonates with the cedar-vetiver base naturally.

- **Ambrettolide (10%)**: Character-echo musk at 180 µL of 10% (18 µL active). The most naturalistic musk — its slightly earthy, wine-like warmth echoes the vetiver's rooty character. Chosen because its naturalistic quality matches the Indian vetiver's artisanal character better than any synthetic musk could.

- **Grapefruit FCF + Bergamot FCF**: The dual citrus top at 300 + 300 µL. Grapefruit FCF provides the bitter-clean, pink-pith dryness that introduces the vetiver's dry character before the vetiver even emerges — grapefruit's natural sulfur compounds (grapefruit mercaptan) share olfactive territory with vetiver's rooty-sulfurous facets. Bergamot FCF provides classical citrus freshness. Chose Grapefruit FCF over other citrus because it is the ONLY citrus that directly prefigures the vetiver base: Grapefruit → Vetiver is a well-established pairing in perfumery (Terre d'Hermès uses grapefruit and vetiver as its central axis).
