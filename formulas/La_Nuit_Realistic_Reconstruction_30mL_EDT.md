## Concept Lock

This is a from-scratch reconstruction hypothesis for YSL La Nuit de L'Homme EDT based on the 50-row suite target. The fixed identity is a warm-spicy-woody composition built on a cardamom-lavender top, a sweet-herbal floral heart, and a deep woody-amber-musk base with restrained sweetness. The final 50 uL is a neutral DPG flanker slot for the cardamom module.

The active-ppm column is `active uL / 4500 uL x 1,000,000`, a volume-basis proxy. Exact ppm w/w remains unavailable without a complete density chain. OAV is generated by the release pipeline from mole fraction, vapor pressure, activity coefficient, and ODT; natural materials use composite OAV.

## Flanker Interface

Replace the 50 uL DPG slot one-for-one; do not stack a module on top.

| Direction | 50 uL replacement module | Expected shift |
|---|---|---|
| Realistic cardamom study | Cardamom EO 30 + Black Pepper EO 10 + Clary Sage EO 10 | Intensified cardamom signature with peppery-aromatic support |
| Electric cardamom-lavender | Cardamom EO 20 + Lavender EO BONTAUX 15 + Spike Lavender 10 + Terpinyl Acetate 5 | Brighter, more aromatic-electrical cardamom-lavender accord |
| Dry vetiver-cedar | Vetiver EO (India) 20 + Cedarwood oil Virginia 15 + Norlimbanol Dextro 10 + Clearwood 5 | Drier, woodier flanker with reduced sweetness |

Every module changes OAV, allergen load, and family balance and must be rerun through the pipeline before mixing.

## Selection and Rejection Ledger

- The three-material musk chord is Ethylene Brassylate for depth, Galaxolide for diffusion, and Exaltolide for skin character, with Zenolide adding transparent fresh diffusion and Ambrettolide adding naturalistic fruity-musky warmth.
- Cardamom EO is the signature La Nuit note — green spicy-cineolic, dosed at 60 uL for perceptible character without dominating.
- Lavender EO (BONTAUX SAS) replaces Lavandin Grosso (not stocked) — softer, less camphoraceous lavender profile.
- Vetiver EO (India) replaces both Vetiveryl Acetate and Vetiverol (neither stocked) — rootier, darker profile.
- Sandalore replaces Bacdanol (not stocked) — creamier, less milky sandalwood register.
- Ambrettolide 10% replaces Edenolide (not stocked) — fruity-musky naturalistic warmth.
- Zenolide replaces Silvanone Supra (not stocked) — clean citrusy-fresh musk.
- Lemon FCF oil Sicilian replaces Litsea Cubeba (not stocked) — bright citrus lift.
- Black Pepper EO replaces Pink Peppercorn EO (not stocked) — spicy aromatic lift.
- Geraniol neat replaces Methyl Geranate (not stocked) — rosy-floral volume.
- Parmavert replaces Cis-3-Hexenyl Salicylate (not stocked) — crushed green leaf snap.
- Indole 10% is used instead of Indole 1% — dose adjusted down.
- Bourgeonal neat replaces Bourgeonal 10% — dose adjusted down.
- BHT neat replaces BHT 10% — dose adjusted down.
- Citronellol neat replaces Citronellol 10% — dose adjusted down.
- Heavy vanilla, gourmand, and leather materials were rejected because they would pull the composition away from the warm-spicy-woody La Nuit character.
- Muscenone Delta is not stocked; Exaltolide 10% carries the skin-musk function at increased dose.
- Trimofix is not stocked; Vertofix provides the dry woody-amber structural spine.

## Mixing and Evaluation

1. Preblend the woods, musks, coumarin, vanillin, and Ambrofix.
2. Add the floral-air and green materials, then the citrus materials.
3. Add the spice and aromatic materials (cardamom, black pepper, clary sage, lavender).
4. Add DPG last for this structural control.
5. Add 4.5 mL concentrate to 25.5 mL ethanol 96%; invert gently.
6. Mature at least 14 days. Evaluate strip at 0, 5, and 30 minutes, then 2 and 4 hours.
