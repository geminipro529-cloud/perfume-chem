# Immortelle Chypre Automne — 30mL EdP

## Character
An autumnal chypre where Immortelle Absolute's curry-maple-honey warmth transforms the classical bergamot-oakmoss-patchouli-labdanum skeleton into a golden-autumn landscape. The composition opens with crisp bergamot and clary sage, descends through an immortelle-coumarin heart (evoking sun-warmed fallen leaves), and settles into a mossy-earthy base where Patchouli, Oakmoss, Evernyl, and Labdanum create the classic chypre foundation that immortelle's maple sweetness enriches. Unlike the bright-green spring chypres (Mitsouko, Givenchy III), this is a chypre for October — golden, dry, and slightly melancholy. Think *Guerlain Chypre de Paris* reimagined through an autumn woodland lens, with immortelle replacing the peach-lactone as the fruity-sweet bridge. Elegantly restrained — no gourmand excess, just the natural sweetness of autumn decay.

**Family**: Chypre — bergamot top, oakmoss-patchouli-labdanum base, immortelle-coumarin golden heart. Classical chypre structure with autumnal immortelle modulation.

## Material Justification

- **Immortelle Absolute (10%)**: The autumn heart — 180 µL of 10% = 18 µL active (0.3% of concentrate). At modifier level, it transforms the classical chypre skeleton into an autumnal composition. The curry-maple-honey character is the olfactory equivalent of golden sunlight on fallen oak leaves — it takes the chypre's mossy-bitter-green register and warms it toward gold. Paired with Coumarin for the tonka-immortelle synergy and with Labdanum for the resinous-amber bridge. The maple facet specifically evokes the "sweet decay" of autumn.
- **Oakmoss Absolute (10%) + Evernyl (50%)**: The chypre moss axis — these are THE definitive chypre materials. Oakmoss at 25 µL active provides the bitter-lichen forest-floor character — the "damp earth" beneath the fallen leaves. Evernyl at 90 µL active adds the synthetic mossy body and brightness that modern chypres require. Together they create a moss register that is both authentic (Oakmoss) and substantial (Evernyl). Higher doses than in other formulas because a chypre DEMANDS moss presence.
- **Patchouli EO (180 µL) + Clearwood (100 µL)**: Dual patchouli — the second pillar of the chypre base. Patchouli EO provides the dark, camphoraceous earth that anchors the moss. Clearwood adds the cleaner, modern-earthy patchouli character — the bright side of earth. Together they create the chypre's dark foundation at a dose appropriate for the genre (280 µL combined).
- **Labdanum Resinoid (10%)**: 180 µL of 10% = 18 µL active. The third chypre pillar — labdanum's resinous amber-leather warmth bridges the mossy bitterness to the golden immortelle heart. Without labdanum, the oakmoss-patchouli-immortelle triangle would feel disjointed — labdanum is the resinous "glue" of the classical chypre. Matched in active dose with immortelle (18 µL each) — co-hearts of the composition.
- **Coumarin (20%) + Vanillin (10%)**: The sweetness axis — essential in a chypre to balance the bitter oakmoss. Coumarin at 24 µL active provides the hay-sweet tonka character that pairs with immortelle (proven synergy) and complements the oakmoss — the classic coumarin-oakmoss axis of fougères and chypres. Vanillin at 8 µL active adds just a whisper of vanilla warmth. Both kept moderate — a chypre should be mossy first, sweet second.
- **Bergamot FCF (450 µL)**: The canonical chypre top note at substantial dose. Bergamot's hesperidic-lavender freshness creates the essential contrast against the mossy base — the classical chypre tension. Chosen over other citruses because bergamot IS the chypre top — it pairs naturally with oakmoss and labdanum. The high dose (450 µL vs typical 300-350) is justified because the moss-patchouli base is substantial and needs corresponding citrus brightness.
- **Clary Sage EO (50 µL) + Dihydromyrcenol (60 µL)**: Aromatic-green top support. Clary sage provides the herbal-aromatic lift that bridges bergamot to the oakmoss heart — drier and more tea-like than lavender, suited to the chypre's restrained elegance. Dihydromyrcenol adds synthetic fresh-citrus-lavender lift — useful in chypres to enhance the bergamot's brightness without adding identifiable citrus character.
- **Hedione HC (500 µL) + Hedione (300 µL)**: Radiance amplifier pair. Hedione's synergy with immortelle expands the golden heart character outward. Essential in a chypre because the mossy base can feel weighty and grounded — Hedione provides the lift that makes the chypre "fly." 800 µL total is appropriate for a modern chypre.
- **Vetiver EO India (80 µL)**: Vetiver accent at trace — adds a subtle smoky-rooty depth to the chypre base that enhances the autumnal character. Not enough to read as "vetiver" — just enough to add complexity to the moss-patchouli foundation. The Indian ruh khus vetiver's smoky facet aligns with the "burning autumn leaves" subtext.
- **Cedarwood EO (200 µL) + Iso E Super (500 µL) + Vertofix (180 µL)**: Woody backbone. Cedarwood provides warm natural cedar — the "autumn woodland" architecture. Iso E Super provides the molecular cocoon — volume and the "what are you wearing" skin resonance. Vertofix bridges the wood to the musk layer — essential in chypres where the moss-wood-musk transition can feel segmented.
- **Azarbre (80 µL) + Koavone (50 µL)**: Warm-cedar amber layer. Azarbre provides cedar-amber warmth — warmer than Ambrox, complementing the golden-autumn character. Koavone supports the cedar accord with balsamic warmth. Together they add a warm-golden glow to the woody base without introducing the crystalline brightness of Ambrox at high dose.
- **Ambrofix (30% w/v) + Amberwood F (60 µL)**: Clean amber accents. Ambrofix at 21 µL active provides a crystalline sparkle that cuts through the mossy density — the visual equivalent of sunlight through autumn canopy. Amberwood F adds invisible transparent warmth — the "background glow" that prevents the chypre from becoming dark and gloomy.
- **Ethylene Brassylate (220 µL)**: Depth musk — creamy lactonic skin-sit that anchors the mossy-patchouli base. The lactonic quality softens the oakmoss's bitter edge without making it "sweet" — just rounder, more wearable. Standard dose for a chypre.
- **Romandolide (120 µL)**: Projection musk — diffusive woody-musk that extends the chypre's architecture outward. Chosen over Habanolide (too intimate) and over Galaxolide (too laundry-detergent — would clash with the chypre's elegance). Romandolide's slightly woody character aligns with the cedar-vertfix backbone.
- **Musk Ketone (10% in DPG)**: Character-echo musk — 100 µL of 10% = 10 µL active. The ONLY musk that reinforces immortelle's powdery-maple character AND simultaneously bridges to the oakmoss powder of the classical chypre drydown. This dual-axis character echo is strategically essential — it creates continuity between the golden heart and the mossy base.
- **Benzyl Salicylate (180 µL) + Hexyl Salicylate (120 µL)**: Dual salicylate fixative gradient. Benzyl Salicylate provides the cosmetic-warm cushion — the classic chypre fixative, adding white-floral volume to the oakmoss trail. Hexyl Salicylate provides the light green-floral film for the bergamot-immortelle heart. Both justified — a chypre needs the full salicylate gradient.
- **Orivone (50 µL)**: Buttery orris warmth — adds a subtle iris-butter richness that makes the chypre base feel more luxurious. The orris character is the olfactory equivalent of the velvety texture of fallen leaves — adding depth without introducing violet powder (which would clash with Musk Ketone's powder axis).
- **Rhodinol ex Citronella (60 µL) + Geranium EO (50 µL) + Phenethyl Alcohol (80 µL)**: Rose-geranium heart trace. The subtle floral-rose dimension adds complexity to the chypre heart without introducing identifiable florals. Geranium EO provides fresh rosy-minty lift — period-correct for a classical chypre structure.
- **Damascone Beta (10%) + Damascenone (1%)**: Rose-ketone accents. Damascone Beta at 4 µL active adds subtle metallic-rose depth. Damascenone at 0.2 µL active provides microscopic dried-fruit warmth. Both at trace to enrich the Labdanum-Vanillin axis — subliminal complexity rather than identifiable notes.
