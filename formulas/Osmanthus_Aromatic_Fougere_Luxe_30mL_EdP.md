# Osmanthus Aromatic Fougère Luxe — 30mL EdP

## Character
Osmanthus-led aromatic fougère. The osmanthus carries apricot-tea-leather at the center — the loudest identifiable voice. Lavender and tonkarome form the quiet frame. Tobacco and nagar motha provide dark smoke without oud or birch tar. IBQ adds leather depth. Rose de Mai mediates lavender to coumarin. Heliotropal-tonkarome creates the sweet-powder accord. Labdanum resin fills the mid-base. Alpha Ionone deepens the ionone-violet backbone. Vetival anchors the woody base. No citrus competition — bergamot at whisper level. Dark, modern, unisex.

**Family**: Aromatic Fougère (dark luxury)
**Materials**: 24
**Concentration**: 14.6% EdP
