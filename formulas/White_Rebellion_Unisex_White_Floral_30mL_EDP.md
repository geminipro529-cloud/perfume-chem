# White Rebellion — Unisex White Floral — 30 mL Strong EDP

**Status:** New-formula design; not yet physically compounded or smelled  
**Concept:** A direct market competitor in the narcotic orange-blossom, tuberose, and apricot-osmanthus territory of Good girl gone Bad by KILIAN, rebuilt as a genuinely unisex floral through bitter-green aromatics, dry woods, mineral musk, vetiver, and crystal Ambrox.  
**Family:** Dry narcotic white floral / floral woody musk  
**Target:** Opulent and sensual, but neither sweet-gourmand nor barbershop-fougère; the flower remains the identity and the dry structure changes its gender coding.  
**Finished volume:** 30.0 mL, measured after all materials dissolve and return to room temperature  
**Liquid stock charge:** exactly 6,000 µL  
**Separate solid charge:** exactly 240 mg Ambrox Super crystals  
**Alcohol:** Ethanol 96%, q.s. to a measured final volume of 30.0 mL; do not assume that adding exactly 24.0 mL will produce the final volume  
**Documentary reference only:** The commercial reference's proprietary formula and proportions are unknown. Its official orange-blossom/osmanthus/tuberose architecture is used as a market brief, not copied as analytical composition.

## Ambrettolide neat-stock rebase

The user confirmed that the currently owned Ambrettolide is neat. The previous design used 100 µL of a nominal 10% stock, so transferring 100 µL neat would create an unintended tenfold nominal dose. This version instead uses **10 µL neat Ambrettolide plus 90 µL DPG**. That preserves the intended nominal 10 µL Ambrettolide dose and the original 100 µL liquid-module volume. It is a volume-preserving personal-compounding rebase; a commercial weight formula still requires the neat material's density or a weighed stock preparation.

## Why this reads more unisex

- The recognizable perfume remains tuberose, orange blossom, and apricot-tea osmanthus. The formula does not hide the flower behind a generic masculine base.
- Petitgrain, pink pepper, cardamom, and a restrained clary-sage trace replace overt sweetness with bitter, green, and aromatic contrast.
- Cedarwood, vetiveryl acetate, Haitian vetiver, Cedramber, Iso E Super, and crystal Ambrox give the drydown a tailored mineral-wood line.
- The musk chord has three nonredundant jobs: Habanolide supplies dry mineral projection, Romandolide supplies woody diffusion, and trace neat Ambrettolide links osmanthus fruit to warm skin.
- Vanilla, ethyl maltol, coumarin, benzoin, heavy leather, smoke, and aquatic-blue materials are intentionally absent.

## Compounding order

1. In the final glass vessel, weigh **240 mg Ambrox Super crystals**. Add roughly 5 mL of the final ethanol allocation. Swirl and use only a gently warm water bath if needed; apply no direct heat. Continue only when the solution is clear.
2. Combine **10 µL neat Ambrettolide** with the formula's **90 µL DPG** carrier allocation, or add those two measured transfers consecutively. Do not transfer 100 µL neat Ambrettolide.
3. Resolve the Aurantiol bottle strength from its label and prepare the single 250 µL module using the matching branch above. If the label cannot be checked yet, do not guess: compound the other **5,750 µL** of liquid stocks, leave this 250 µL module outstanding, and do not bring the bottle to final volume until the correct module has been added.
4. Add the remaining base liquid stocks in table order, then the heart stocks in table order, then the top stocks in table order. At the Aurantiol row, add the complete 250 µL module. Swirl gently after each layer.
5. Add Ethanol 96% to approach the final mark. Mix, let the solution return to room temperature, then bring the bottle to an accurately measured **30.0 mL final volume**.
6. Cap and invert gently. Check after 24 hours for haze, sediment, or Ambrox recrystallization. If crystals appear, warm gently and redissolve before judging the scent.
7. First useful smell check: 48 hours. Better structural check: 14 days. Main decision point: 4 weeks. Evaluate on the same substrate at 5 minutes, 30 minutes, 2 hours, and 4 hours.

## Expected sensory trajectory — hypothesis, not measured fact

- **Opening:** bergamot and mandarin light, immediately dried by petitgrain, pink pepper, cardamom, and clary sage.
- **Heart:** orange blossom opens first; tuberose becomes the central narcotic flower; osmanthus adds apricot skin and tea rather than overt fruit sweetness.
- **Drydown:** the white-floral silhouette remains visible while crystal Ambrox, cedar, vetiveryl acetate, Iso E Super, Habanolide, and Romandolide form a clean, dry, persistent trail.

## First adjustment policy

Do not redesign immediately. Smell the matured bottle first. If it is too floral/feminine, the first controlled add-only comparison is a small dry-structure delta, not more musk at random. If it is too dry/masculine, the first comparison is a small tuberose–osmanthus–Hedione bridge. Any change becomes a new recorded bottle-state delta; it does not require making a second full bottle.
