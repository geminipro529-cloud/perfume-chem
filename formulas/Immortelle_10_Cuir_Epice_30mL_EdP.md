# Immortelle Cuir Épicé — 30mL EdP

## Character
A dark, spiced leather where Immortelle Absolute's curry-honey-maple warmth bridges a sharp spice heart (Cardamom, Black Pepper, Cinnamaldehyde) to a multi-textured leather base (IBQ, Suederal, Guaiacol, Leather FO). Unlike the Tobacco Cuir formula (which relied on tobacco sweetness and Birch Tar smokiness), this composition uses SPICE as the bridge between immortelle's honeyed warmth and the leather's animalic depth — the curry facet of immortelle providing the olfactory logic that connects cardamom's green-spice to IBQ's dirty-leather bite. The leather itself is constructed as a layered accord: IBQ for animalic bite, Suederal for smooth suede texture, Guaiacol for phenolic-smoke depth, and Leather FO as the blended leather body. Labdanum and Patchouli provide the dark-resinous foundation. Think *Knize Ten* reimagined through a spice-market lens — leather that has traveled the silk road, infused with centuries of cardamom, cinnamon, and immortelle's golden warmth.

**Family**: Spicy Leather — cardamom-pepper-cinnamon spice heart over IBQ-Suederal-Guaiacol leather base, immortelle as curry-honey modulator

## Material Justification

- **Immortelle Absolute (10%)**: The curry-honey bridge — 200 µL of 10% = 20 µL active (0.35% of concentrate). At the upper modifier range because its curry facet is the olfactory logic for the spice focus. Immortelle's curry character provides the common ground between Cardamom's green-spice, Cinnamaldehyde's warm-spice, and the leather's animalic depth — without immortelle, the spice + leather would read as discordant; with it, they read as a unified "spice route leather." Pairs with Ethyl Safranate (saffron-curry synergy) and Coumarin (proven immortelle partner).
- **Isobutyl Quinoline (10%)**: 40 µL of 10% = 4 µL active. The quintessential dirty-leather material — the animalic bite that defines classic leathers. At 4 µL active, it's clearly present but not overwhelming — the "signature" of leather rather than the whole composition. Higher than the Tobacco Cuir formula (3.5 µL) because this is a LEATHER composition with spice accent, not a leather-accented tobacco. IBQ provides the raw, urbane edge that prevents the composition from becoming a "spice perfume with leather undertones."
- **Suederal (10%)**: 100 µL of 10% = 10 µL active. Suede-leather without smoke — the refined, chamois-soft texture that contrasts with IBQ's animalic rawness. At double the IBQ dose, it creates the balanced leather register: animalic bite (IBQ) + suede softness (Suederal) = natural leather that's both angry and elegant. Chosen because Suederal's suede-smooth character is the perfect counterpoint to IBQ's harshness.
- **Guaiacol (10%)**: 50 µL of 10% = 5 µL active. Phenolic-smoke depth — the "burning wood" facet of leather that gives it the dark, almost medicinal intensity of a well-tanned hide. At 5 µL active, it's the strongest phenolic dose in any Immortelle formula — justified because leather without phenolic depth reads as "plastic." Pairs with Patchouli for the dark-earthy-smoky axis.
- **Leather FO (150 µL)**: Pre-blended leather body — the "filler" leather that provides volume and recognizability. Leather FO gives the composition an immediately identifiable leather character, while IBQ + Guaiacol + Suederal add the complexity and specificity that a pre-blend alone would lack. This layered approach — pre-blend for body, individual materials for character — is the professional perfumer's strategy for leather accords.
- **Cardamom EO (40 µL) + Black Pepper EO (35 µL) + Cinnamaldehyde (7 µL)**: Spice triad. Cardamom provides green-cineolic-spicy brightness — the lead spice, chosen because its green-spicy character synergizes with immortelle's curry facet. Black Pepper adds dry-piquant sparkle that cuts through the leather density. Cinnamaldehyde at micro-dose (7 µL neat) provides a whisper of cinnamon warmth — not enough to read as "cinnamon leather," just enough to add spice complexity. All three at moderate-to-trace doses — the spice should serve the leather, not dominate it.
- **Ethyl Safranate (25 µL) + Eugenol (12 µL)**: Spice nuance. Ethyl Safranate at 25 µL neat provides saffron's leathery-iodine warmth — the most natural bridge between spice and leather. Saffron's own leathery character means it merges WITH the IBQ-Suederal accord rather than sitting on top of it. Eugenol at 12 µL neat adds a faint clove-spice undertone that enriches the Cinnamaldehyde warmth. Both at trace — nuanced spice accents, not front-line character notes.
- **Labdanum Resinoid (10%)**: 180 µL of 10% = 18 µL active. The dark-resinous heart that connects the spice to the leather. Labdanum's own leather-amber complexity provides the common ground between the Cardamom spice and the IBQ leather — without labdanum, these two registers would read as adjacent rather than integrated. Matched in active dose with immortelle (18 vs 20 µL) — co-hearts of the composition.
- **Coumarin (20%) + Vanillin (10%)**: Sweetness axis at restrained doses. Coumarin at 20 µL active provides hay-sweet tonka that pairs with immortelle and softens the spice edge. Vanillin at 10 µL active adds a whisper of vanilla warmth — kept low to avoid sweetening the leather into gourmand territory. Both at lower doses than other Immortelle formulas because leather should be dark, not sweet.
- **Patchouli EO (150 µL) + Oakmoss Absolute (10%, 120 µL) + Evernyl (50%, 100 µL)**: Dark-earthy foundation. Patchouli at 150 µL provides the camphoraceous earth that anchors the leather — the "old wooden chest" that the leather goods are stored in. Oakmoss at 12 µL active adds bitter-lichen depth — the chypre-inflected darkness. Evernyl at 50 µL active adds synthetic mossy body. Together they create a dark, complex foundation that prevents the leather from floating above the skin — this leather is rooted in earth, not sanitized.
- **Iso E Super (500 µL) + Vertofix (180 µL) + Cashmeran (90 µL)**: Woody backbone. Iso E Super provides the molecular cocoon — volume and the anosmic skin resonance that makes leather compositions "huffable." Vertofix bridges wood to musk. Cashmeran adds textile-warmth — the "wool lining" of the leather, creating a tactile quality that makes the leather feel lived-in. Chosen at moderate dose (90 µL) because the leather-spice character is already warm — Cashmeran should support, not amplify.
- **Azarbre (80 µL) + Cedramber (70 µL) + Amberwood F (60 µL)**: Warm-amber woody layer. Azarbre provides cedar-amber warmth — warmer than Ambrox, complementing the dark-leather character. Cedramber bridges cedar and amber. Amberwood F adds invisible clean warmth. Together they create a warm-woody bed beneath the leather without introducing crystalline brightness.
- **Kephalis (25 µL)**: Woody-amber-tobacco structural accent at trace — the olfactory rivet that pins the leather to the wood. Its tobacco-iris undertone adds a subtle complexity that enriches the leather without introducing actual Tobacco Absolute. Chosen over Ambrofix (which would add cold-mineral sparkle — wrong for a dark spice leather) and over Norlimbanol (too rigid-structural — would make the leather feel stiff).
- **Ethylene Brassylate (240 µL)**: Depth musk at elevated dose. The lactonic creaminess softens IBQ's animalic bite and Guaiacol's phenolic intensity — keeps the leather dark but wearable, animalic but not feral. The higher dose (240 vs typical 200) is needed because the IBQ + Guaiacol combination is more aggressive than the materials in other Immortelle formulas — EB is the "civilizing force" that prevents the leather from becoming unwearable.
- **Romandolide (140 µL)**: Projection musk — diffusive woody-musk that extends the leather-spice character outward. Chosen over Habanolide (too warm-intimate for a leather that should project power) and over Galaxolide (too laundry-clean — would whitewash the dark leather character). Romandolide's woody facet aligns with the cedar backbone.
- **Ambrettolide (10% in DPG)**: Character-echo musk — 80 µL of 10% = 8 µL active. Naturalistic fruity-musky warmth that bridges immortelle's honey and the leather's animality. The "warm leather jacket after years of wear" impression — leather that has absorbed the warmth of the body wearing it. Essential to prevent the leather from reading as a new, unworn product.
- **Hedione HC (500 µL) + Hedione (250 µL)**: Radiance amplifier pair. Hedione's synergy with immortelle expands the curry-spice character outward. Essential in a leather composition because leather tends to read as dense and ground-level — Hedione provides the "lift" that makes the leather breathe. 750 µL total is the right volume for a dark composition that needs projection.
- **Bergamot FCF (350 µL) + Clary Sage EO (40 µL)**: Bright-aromatic opening contrast. Bergamot provides the classic citrus brightness — essential tension against the dark leather base. Clary sage provides the herbal-aromatic bridge from citrus to spice — the transition material that makes bergamot→cardamom→leather feel logical rather than abrupt.
- **Benzyl Salicylate (200 µL) + Hexyl Salicylate (120 µL)**: Dual salicylate fixative gradient. Benzyl Salicylate provides the cosmetic-warm cushion that anchors the leather trail — the "perfume" quality that makes this a fine fragrance, not a leather-scented product. Hexyl Salicylate provides the light film for the spice-citrus top — adhesion without cosmetic weight. Both justified — the salicylate gradient is the standard fixative architecture for complex dark compositions.
