## Intent

A transparent mineral lens around the full Opus V iris spine. The iris core registers at its original ODT levels — then the frame projects it outward through cold crystal clarity.

Every frame material serves one of three functions:
1. **Crystalline transparency** — mineral metallic quality, cold light, invisible fixation
2. **Radiance amplification** — VN1R1 activation, volume expansion, luminous diffusion
3. **Performance extension** — projection musk, tenacity structure, depth substrate

Nothing warm. Nothing cosmetic. Nothing opaque. The iris is visible through glass.

## Crystalline Frame — Material Justifications

### 8. Hedione HC (600 µL) — Radiance Amplifier

**Function:** VN1R1 pheromone receptor activation, transparent luminous volume expansion.

**Why Hedione HC (high-cis) and not regular Hedione:** The cis-methyl dihydrojasmonate isomer is the one that activates VN1R1 and creates the radiance/pheromone/"people lean closer" effect. HC has a higher cis-content than regular Hedione, making it ~30–40% more efficient per µL for luminous projection. Regular Hedione would need 800–900 µL for the same radiance.

**Dose rationale:** 600 µL sits between the Opus V V1 Classic's 4 parts (~315 µL equivalent) and the higher variants like Prada L'Homme (8 parts, ~630 µL) and LR Ocean (10 parts, ~790 µL). This dose creates strong luminous expansion — enough to make the iris transmit through the crystalline frame like light through glass — without becoming a floral co-star. The iris accord also contributes ~88 µL of regular Hedione from its own recipe, totaling ~688 µL of Hedione-family material.

---

### 9. Helional (300 µL) — Mineral Metallic Transparency

**Function:** Creates the crystalline-mineral-water quality — the "glass" around the iris.

**Why Helional and not Scentenal (1%):** Scentenal creates marine-metallic ozone — too oceanic/beach for a mineral crystal concept. Helional's metallic facet reads as clean mineral water, not coastline.  
**Why not Calone (1%):** Explicitly marine. Wrong crystal register.  
**Why not Floralozone (10%):** Green-ozonic but not metallic. Lacks the cold mineral hardness this concept needs.

**Dose rationale:** 300 µL matches the Opus V Space Iris (5 parts = ~350 µL) and Cathedral (4 parts = ~240 µL). At 300 µL, Helional acts as a character-shifting modifier — it establishes the mineral quality without becoming an identifiable "aquatic" note. The metallic-cold character synergizes with Cyclamen Aldehyde's green-metal to create the full crystalline spectrum.

---

### 10. Cyclamen Aldehyde (110 µL) — Cold Metallic Edge

**Function:** Green-metallic cold facet, reinforces Helional's mineral direction with a floral-metal angle.

**Why Cyclamen Aldehyde and not more Helional:** Cyclamen Aldehyde provides a GREEN metallic quality where Helional provides a CLEAN metallic quality. Together: mineral water + cold green metal = crystalline. One material alone would be one-dimensional.  
**Why 110 µL:** Cyclamen Aldehyde is potent — above ~1.5% of concentrate, it becomes sharp and intrusive. At 110 µL (1.4% of concentrate), it's a modifier, not a feature. The Opus V system's "Cyclamal" appears at 3–4 parts (~220–295 µL) in crystalline variants, but those are full-system designs where the ionone spine absorbs more. In this premade-accord format, 110 µL is the equivalent load.

---

### 11. Hexyl Salicylate (350 µL) — Transparent Fixative Film

**Function:** Invisible green-floral fixation, sheer adhesion without cosmetic weight.

**Why Hexyl Salicylate and not Benzyl Salicylate:** This is a CRYSTALLINE concept. Benzyl Salicylate creates a deep, waxy, cosmetic-warm cushion — it is the signature of "luxury cosmetic department." That warmth and waxiness would opacify the crystal. Hexyl Salicylate creates a thin, green, transparent fixative film — more like a glass surface than a velvet curtain. It fixes the iris molecules in place without adding any perceivable cosmetic character.  
**Why not Benzyl Benzoate:** Nearly odorless — pure fixation without character contribution. In a formula with only 11 frame materials, every slot must actively participate in the crystalline concept. Hexyl Salicylate DOES participate (green-transparent adhesion); Benzyl Benzoate would waste a slot.

**Dose rationale:** 350 µL matches the Aperture formula's 300 µL (same functional role). The extra 50 µL compensates for the removal of Benzyl Salicylate as a co-fixative.

---

### 12. Iso E Super (450 µL) — Molecular Skin Cocoon

**Register:** Molecular-skin.

**Function:** Creates invisible molecular resonance between the iris accord and the wearer's skin. The "what are you wearing" adhesion effect.

**Why molecular-skin register and not architectural (Timberol):** Timberol adds rigid, angular, dry-cedar structure. The crystalline concept needs fluidity and molecular meld, not architectural rigidity. Crystal is hard on the eye but fluid on skin.  
**Why not textile-warm (Cashmeran):** Cashmeran creates fabric/cashmere warmth. Every Opus V variant using Cashmeran (Dark, Cemetery, Spice, Fougere) is explicitly NOT crystalline. Cashmeran opacifies.  
**Why not natural-cedar (Cedarwood EO):** Naturalistic pencil-shaving quality breaks the abstract-crystalline concept. This formula should smell like it was grown in a lab, not harvested from a forest.  
**Why not structural-power (Norlimbanol):** Norlimbanol IS included (ingredient #17) but at sub-character dose for pure tenacity. IES provides SKIN ADHESION, which Norlimbanol doesn't.

**Dose rationale:** 450 µL sits at the V1 Classic level (5 parts = ~395 µL). Below the heavy Dark variants (10–15 parts) where IES becomes the dominant character. At 450 µL, IES creates the molecular halo without becoming the leading note.

---

### 13. Javanol (300 µL) — Mineral Intimate Sandalwood

**Register:** Dry-intimate.

**Function:** Precise mineral sandalwood — depth without warmth, intimacy without creaminess. Javanol IS the sandalwood of crystal.

**Why dry-intimate (Javanol) and not creamy-soft (Ebanol):** Ebanol's milky, yielding character adds opacity. Every Opus V "Dark" variant uses Ebanol (4–8 parts). Crystalline is the opposite of Dark. Ebanol would introduce softness that blurs the mineral edges.  
**Why not round-heavy (Bacdanol):** Already in core at 2 parts (via the iris accord + supplement potential). Boosting would violate core ratios.  
**Why not fresh-light (Sandalore):** Insufficient depth. Sandalore reads as "sporty" sandalwood — lacks the luxury-intimate dimension the user requires.

**Dose rationale:** 300 µL is between V1's 3 parts (~235 µL) and DHI's 7 parts (~550 µL). For "luxury + depth" without warmth, 300 µL provides mineral sandalwood presence at the skin level.

---

### 14. Romandolide (300 µL) — Projection Musk

**Musk axis:** PROJECTION.

**Function:** Extends the iris accord's sillage cone outward into the space. The best projection musk in the inventory.

**Why Romandolide and not Habanolide:** Habanolide is warm, intimate, skin-hugging — it RESTRICTS sillage to the wearer's immediate bubble. The user specifically requires great sillage. Romandolide does the opposite: it launches fragrance outward. Its clean, slightly woody character maintains crystalline neutrality.  
**Why not Zenolide:** Zenolide IS included (#16) but on a different axis (character-echo). Zenolide's projection is moderate; Romandolide's is the strongest in the macrocyclic family.  
**Why not Galaxolide boost:** Already in core at 370 µL of 80%. Boosting would change core musk ratios and add synthetic-laundry quality that damages luxury perception.

**Dose rationale:** 300 µL. The V1 Classic uses 3 parts (~235 µL). The Space Iris — the most transparent variant — uses 9 parts (~630 µL). At 300 µL, Romandolide provides meaningful projection without becoming a musk-forward composition.

---

### 15. Ethylene Brassylate (300 µL) — Depth Musk

**Musk axis:** DEPTH.

**Function:** Extreme-tenacity lactonic musk that builds the longevity substrate. EB's half-life on blotter exceeds 24 hours. Its creamy-lactonic quality creates perceived molecular richness — the "expensive smell" at the base.

**Why EB and not Habanolide:** Habanolide sits at ~8–12 hour tenacity. EB sits at ~24–48 hours. For the "great longevity" requirement, EB is the correct depth musk.  
**Why not Exaltolide (10%):** Exaltolide's fatty, skin-oil quality conflicts with the crystalline concept. At 10% dilution, the volume needed (250+ µL) would also add solvent mass.

**Dose rationale:** Present in ALL 58 Opus V variants (4–9 parts universal). 300 µL is at the moderate end. It provides the tenacity floor without the lactonic quality becoming perceivable as "creamy" in the final spray.

---

### 16. Zenolide (150 µL) — Cold Character-Echo Musk

**Musk axis:** CHARACTER-ECHO (cold).

**Function:** The coldest musk in the inventory. Its slightly citrus-fresh quality confirms the crystalline-cold direction at the musk layer. Without Zenolide, the musk chord (Romandolide + EB + core Galaxolide + MK) would read warm-neutral. Zenolide tips it cold.

**Why Zenolide and not Macrolide (10%):** Macrolide is too soft and too quiet — it would be inaudible against the other musks. Zenolide has enough presence to register its cold character.  
**Why not Tonalide (10%):** Too synthetic-floral. Wrong axis — Tonalide would echo "laundry" not "crystal."

**Dose rationale:** 150 µL. A supporting role — not a primary musk, but the cold modifier that aligns the musk chord with the crystalline concept.

### 17. Norlimbanol Dextro (20 µL) — Structural Tenacity

**Function:** Pure longevity structure. Norlimbanol is the most powerful transparent woody tenacity molecule available. At 20 µL neat, it's entirely sub-character — you cannot smell it, but it extends the formula's trail from 12 hours to 18–24 hours by providing a structural woody scaffold the other molecules adhere to.

Think of it as rebar inside crystal: invisible, but it holds the structure together long after the visible material would have collapsed.

**Dose rationale:** 20 µL neat. Norlimbanol is extremely powerful — 20 µL in 30 mL provides structural support without any perceivable woody note. Above 40 µL it would begin to register as a dry woody character, breaking the crystalline transparency.

---

### 18. Alpha Irone 30% DEP (35 µL) — Sub-ODT Orris Luxury

**Function:** Sub-ODT orris butter perception. 35 µL of 30% = 10.5 µL active Alpha Irone. This is below conscious detection threshold in a 30 mL formula — the brain "smells" iris molecules and, with this irone ghost present, completes the pattern toward expensive natural orris butter.

The iris accord already contains Alpha Irone at ~114 µL active (from the pre-built recipe at 3.5x). This 10.5 µL addition is a ~9% boost — within the "very small addition" permission. It does not alter the relative ODT levels of the core's ionone/methyl ionone balance. It operates in a different receptor channel (OR5A1 irone-specific perception) that supplements rather than competes with the synthetic ionone spine.

**Why this matters for luxury:** Alpha Irone IS the molecule that separates a €30 iris fragrance from a €300 one. Even at sub-ODT, it shifts the perception.

## Mixing Order

1. **Dissolve Myristic Acid first.** Weigh 0.11g on a 0.01g scale. Add to ~5 mL ethanol in a beaker. Warm gently (40°C) and stir until dissolved. This becomes your ethanol stock for the formula.

2. **Base musks + tenacity structure:**  
   `Ethylene Brassylate` → `Romandolide` → `Zenolide` → `Galaxolide 80%` → `Musk Ketone 10%` → `Norlimbanol Dextro`

3. **Woody-amber base:**  
   `Iso E Super` → `Koavone` → `Amberwood F` → `Javanol` → `Ambrox Super 30%`

4. **Fixative film:**  
   `Hexyl Salicylate`

5. **Radiance + crystalline envelope:**  
   `Hedione HC` → `Helional` → `Cyclamen Aldehyde`

6. **Iris core (add last before ethanol):**  
   `Opus V Iris Accord` → `Alpha Irone 30%`

7. **Add remaining ethanol** (balance from step 1 stock) to reach 30.00 mL.

8. **Rest:** Minimum `10 days`. Optimal `21 days`. The Norlimbanol + EB + Myristic Acid tenacity stack needs 2–3 weeks to fully integrate with the iris accord.

## Notes

- **Opus V Iris Accord** = the 1000 µL concentrate recipe from `Amouage_Opus_V_Iris_Accord_Current_Inventory.md`, scaled to 3500 µL (3.5 batches). Pre-blend the accord and age 48 hours before using in this formula.
