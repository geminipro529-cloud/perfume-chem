**Date:** 2026-04-05  
**Batch Size:** `30.00 mL`  
**Concentration:** `25.0%` in ethanol  
**Core Rule:** `Opus V Iris Crystal` stays at the center; no `Jasmine FO`

---

## Intent

This version is built to show the iris core with:
- crystal clarity
- jewelry-like polish
- premium musky lift
- a clean floral illusion instead of a literal jasmine accord

The center stays `Opus V Iris Crystal`. The shell is tuned to frame it, not bury it.

## Why This Version Works

- `Hedione + Nympheal + Lilyreal ND + DBCA + Cis Jasmone + Phenethyl Alcohol` gives the jasmine impression without using `Jasmine FO`.
- `Habanolide + Romandolide + Ethylene Brassylate` gives a cleaner, more expensive musk halo than a heavy Galaxolide-led base.
- `Iso E Super + Clearwood + Cedramber + Kephalis` gives vertical wood architecture and a polished mineral edge.
- `Hexyl Salicylate` keeps the center smooth and blooming without tripping the benzyl salicylate limit.
- `Aldehyde C12 MNA` and `Rose Oxide` keep the surface bright and crystalline.

---

## Mixing Order

1. Add the woods and musks first:
   `Iso E Super`, `Clearwood`, `Cedramber`, `Kephalis`, `Habanolide`, `Romandolide`, `Ethylene Brassylate`, `Ambrofix`, `Bacdanol`, `Hexyl Salicylate`.
2. Add the floral frame:
   `Hedione`, `Phenethyl Alcohol`, `Cis Jasmone`, `Nympheal`, `Lilyreal ND`, `DBCA`.
3. Add the opening:
   `Bergamot FCF oil Sicilian`, `Cedrat FCF oil Sicilian`, `Linalool`, `Aldehyde C12 MNA`.
4. Add `Opus V Iris Crystal`.
5. Add `Rose Oxide (1%)` last.
6. Add ethanol.
7. Rest at least `7 days`; `14 days` is better.

## What Not To Add

Avoid these if the goal is crystal clarity and luxury framing:

- `Jasmine FO` at any meaningful level
- `Benzyl Salicylate` above trace levels
- `Galaxolide` as the main musk body
- `Musk Ketone`
- `Ethyl Maltol`
- `Vanillin`
- `Ethyl Vanillin`
- `Maple Lactone`
- `Coumarin`
- `Ambrox Super` as a major block
- `Ambrofix` as a major block
- `Vetival`
- `Vetiver EO` at more than a trace
- `Patchouli EO`
- `Birch Tar Rectified`
- `Guaiacol`
- `Isobutyl Quinoline`

These push the formula toward sweet, smoky, leathery, or heavy amber territory, which works against the Aperture brief.

## Adjustment Knobs

If it feels too dense:
- reduce `Cedramber` by `20 µL`
- reduce `Ambrofix` by `20 µL`
- raise `Hedione` by `20-30 µL`

If it needs more jasmine illusion:
- raise `Nympheal` by `10-15 µL`
- raise `Lilyreal ND` by `10 µL`
- raise `Cis Jasmone` by `5 µL`

If it needs more jewelry sparkle:
- raise `Rose Oxide (1%)` to `20 µL`
- raise `Aldehyde C12 MNA (1%)` to `25-30 µL`

If it needs more wood polish:
- raise `Kephalis` by `10-20 µL`
- raise `Vertofix Coeur` by `10-20 µL` in the next variant
