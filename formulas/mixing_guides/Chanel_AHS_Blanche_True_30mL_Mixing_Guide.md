**Minimum wait: 3 weeks.** Ionones + coumarin + musks are slow integrators. The lavender needs 2–3 weeks to fully bind into the ionone-coumarin matrix. Ambrofix at 30% integrates faster than Ambrox Super (depleted) but still needs 3 weeks for full diffusion bed formation.

**Ideal wait: 6 weeks.** Coumarin-tonka develops full almond-hay bloom through week 4–6. The aldehydic opening becomes more integrated with the citrus-hedione heart. The triple-musk spine (Habanolide + Galaxolide + Ambrofix) reaches full structural diffusion bed formation at week 6.

## Why This Works (Pipeline-Verified)

1. **Dihydromyrcenol OAV 9,449** — THE "sport" material. Rapid evaporation flash creates Chanel-style sport freshness that never reads as teenager-body-spray because it's balanced by tonka + iris sophistication.

2. **Ambrofix OAV 21** — Clean ambergris backbone. Non-negotiable AHS DNA. Brighter and cleaner than depleted Ambrox Super, which suits the "Blanche" (white) concept.

3. **Alpha Ionone OAV 424 at 3.0% headspace** — Iris as supporting player, not lead. Enough for Chanel polish and cosmetic texture. Not enough to dominate the lavender or drown the citrus. This is the single most important dose in the formula.

4. **Lavender EO HA OAV 14** — Clean herbal-floral fougere heart. High Altitude angustifolia avoids the camphoraceous edge of Spike Lavender. The authentic AHS masculine spine.

5. **Hedione HC OAV 619** — High-cis jasmonate radiance. Gives the formula spatial expansion, air, and bloom. The "transparency" in AHS's transparent citrus-woody structure.

6. **Coumarin OAV 48** — Tonka-hay warmth. THE Allure Homme Sport Edition Blanche signature drydown. At 70 µL active (350 µL at 20%), it's clearly perceptible but not gourmand.

7. **Habanolide 650 µL + Galaxolide 175 µL active** — Clean white musk bed. Habanolide provides warm skin-musk (THE Chanel sport musk), Galaxolide provides soapy-clean lift.

8. **Iso E Super OAV 4,448** — Molecular spatial cocoon. The invisible volume that makes Chanel fragrances feel "full" without reading as woody. High OAV but low perceived intensity due to anosmic adaptation.

9. **Red Mandarin at 80 µL OAV 4,337** — Chanel-cologne citrus recognition at the opening. Present but not dominating. The bergamot + cedrat carry the citrus architecture.

10. **Aldehydes C10 + C11 + C12 MNA** — Chanel aldehydic sparkle at trace doses (sub-µL active). The Chanel DNA that separates "French perfume" from everything else. Flash-evaporates in seconds but defines the opening character.
