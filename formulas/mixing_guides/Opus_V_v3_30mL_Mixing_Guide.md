## Temporal Architecture — What You Should Smell

### Act I: Lush Iris (0–10 min)
**Engine says:** Beta Ionone (OAV 4390), Alpha Irone (128), Rose Oxide (40), Alpha Ionone (38)  
**What you smell:** Lush, warm, buttery orris with metallic sparkle. The rhum accord's Ethyl Vanillin and Eugenol flash briefly — warm, boozy, spiced — then yield to the iris within minutes. Rose Oxide creates the "platinum shavings" metallic lift. Hedione amplifies everything into a radiant cloud that fills the room.  
**Sillage:** Maximum — 150 cm projection, sillage cloud level.

### Act II: Oud Takeover (10 min–2 hr)
**Engine says:** Character remains woody(24%)/floral(20%)/powdery(18%) — highly linear  
**What you smell:** The "real" orris emerges — Alpha Irone and Orivone's buttery-powdery-rooty character. Guaiacol's smoke starts seeping through. Jasmine FO and Phenethyl Alcohol add narcotic-floral dimensionality. Patchouli and Myrrh darken the orris with oud-like resinous earth.  
**Sillage:** Still maximum — 148 cm at 1 hr.

### Act III: Dirty Leather (2–5 hr)
**Engine says:** Iso E Super (OAV 40.4), Ambrox Super (38.4) increasingly dominant as lighter materials evaporate  
**What you smell:** "Delicious dirty leather, old wood of the barn." Iso E Super's molecular cocoon wraps everything. IBQ's animalic leather emerges. Suederal adds suede texture. Evernyl's dry oakmoss joins. The iris becomes a powdery echo within the woody-leather frame.  
**Sillage:** 147 cm — still projecting strongly into the room.

### Act IV: Woods Symphony (5+ hr)
**Engine says:** All heavy base materials at sustained OAV. Character stable. Musks subliminal but present.  
**What you smell:** Intimate woody-amber-musk skin scent. Ambrox Super (ODT 0.003 ppb) radiates crystalline amber when everything else has softened. The musk bed (Galaxolide, Habanolide, EB, Romandolide) creates a velvet cushion close to skin. The iris is a ghostly powdery memory carried in the amber-musk-wood matrix.  
**Sillage:** Gradually declining from 147 cm → more intimate. Near-skin at 24+ hr.

## Engine Caveats

1. **Character linearity:** The engine shows woody(24%)/floral(20%)/powdery(18%) from opening straight through drydown. In practice, the 4-act evolution WILL occur — the engine's additive model doesn't fully capture how the nose prioritizes novelty (the "surprise" of dark materials emerging) or how synergistic interactions shift character perception. Real wearing will show more evolution than the steady-state model predicts.

2. **Beta Ionone dominance:** OAV 4390 is extraordinary. Beta Ionone's ODT of 0.007 ppb means it punches enormously above its 1.8% concentration. In reality, at such high OAV, the nose partially adapts (anosmia), and Beta Ionone functions more as a "background violet" than a screaming soloist. The engine correctly models the physical chemistry but overestimates the sustained perceptual impact.

3. **Rose Oxide overestimation:** The engine treats Rose Oxide with dilution factor 0.1 (10%), but the inventory uses 1% solution. Actual Rose Oxide OAV is closer to 4 (still perceptible but not #4 in the hierarchy). In practice, Rose Oxide provides a metallic sparkle in the first 30 minutes.

4. **Subliminal musks:** All 4 musks register subliminal individually. This is CORRECT — musks function as a collective bed that's perceived as "skin warmth" and "clean closeness" rather than as individual odorants. The 7% musk bed creates a coherent textural foundation.
