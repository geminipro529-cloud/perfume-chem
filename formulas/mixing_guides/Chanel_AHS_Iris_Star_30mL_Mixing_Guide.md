## Why This Works

1. **Alpha Ionone 250 µL OAV 2,381** — same dose as the original formula, but now framed by AHS structure. The iris leads because the sport-citrus-ambrox architecture supports it, creating a complete fragrance instead of a one-note accord.

2. **Orivone 150 µL OAV 1,248** — clean buttery iris body. The double-ionone anchor (Alpha + Orivone) gives the iris weight and substance that holds through the heart.

3. **AHS structural materials intact** — DHM (sport), Bergamot + Cedrat + Mandarin (Chanel cologne), Ambrofix (ambergris), Coumarin (tonka), Habanolide + Galaxolide (white musk). The sport-citrus opening and ambrox-tonka drydown are still here — iris replaces lavender as the heart.

4. **Hedione HC 180 µL OAV 696** — radiance gives the iris spatial bloom. Without it, iris sits flat and monolithic. With it, iris has transparency and air.

5. **Ultralia 30 µL OAV 127** — ghost-iris echo that extends the iris character into the late drydown without adding density. The "just-washed" iris freshness.

6. **Dihydro Beta Ionone 100 µL** — woody-fruity iris warmth for extended drydown. Low headspace OAV but high tenacity. Bridges iris to the woody base.

7. **Citrus restrained, not removed** — Bergamot 250 (not 330), Mandarin 50 (not 80), DHM 80 (not 210). Enough for AHS recognition. Not enough to fight the iris.

8. **Coumarin 70 µL active OAV 53** — AHS Blanche tonka signature. The hay-almond warmth that makes the drydown recognizable.
