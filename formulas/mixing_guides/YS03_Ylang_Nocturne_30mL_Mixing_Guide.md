## Step-By-Step Mixing Instructions

### 1. Build the base first

Add these into your mixing bottle:

1. `Benzyl Salicylate` — `0.42 mL`
2. `Iso E Super` — `0.66 mL`
3. `Cashmeran (20%)` — `0.36 mL`
4. `Vanillin (10%)` — `0.24 mL`
5. `Coumarin (20%)` — `0.21 mL`
6. `Benzoin Resinoid (50% in DPG)` — `0.27 mL`
7. `Habanolide` — `0.30 mL`
8. `Galaxolide (80%)` — `0.45 mL`

Swirl or stir for `30-45 seconds` until the base looks uniform.

### 2. Add the floral heart

Add:

1. `Ylang Comoros Complete EO F3255` — `0.60 mL`
2. `Ylang Comoros III EO F3295` — `0.24 mL`
3. `Methyl Benzoate` — `0.30 mL`
4. `Hedione` — `0.78 mL`
5. `Benzyl Acetate` — `0.24 mL`
6. `Phenethyl Alcohol (PEA)` — `0.12 mL`
7. `Indole (10%)` — `0.12 mL`

Swirl or stir again for `45-60 seconds`.

### 3. Add the top notes

Add:

1. `Bergamot FCF oil Sicilian` — `0.27 mL`
2. `Linalool` — `0.12 mL`

Swirl gently for `20-30 seconds`.

### 4. Add the alcohol

Add:

1. `Ethanol 96%` — `24.30 mL`

Cap the bottle and shake thoroughly for `60 seconds`.

## Resting Instructions

### Minimum rest

- Let it rest `7 days` before first evaluation.

### Better read

- `14 days` is better.

### Best read

- `3-4 weeks` will give the smoothest result.

This formula especially benefits from rest because:
- `ylang` can smell rough or overly oily when fresh
- `benzoin`, `vanillin`, and `coumarin` need time to fuse into the floral body
- `indole` settles into the flower more naturally after maceration

---

## What To Expect

### Opening

- bright bergamot flash
- immediate rich yellow-white floral body

### Heart

- the ylang becomes thick, creamy, banana-floral, and softly narcotic
- `Methyl Benzoate + Benzyl Acetate + Hedione` make it feel more luxurious and expansive

### Drydown

- warm ambered floral skin scent
- soft musk halo
- sweet resin cushion underneath the flower

## If You Want To Adjust It

If you want it less sweet:
- reduce `Vanillin (10%)` by `0.05 mL`
- reduce `Coumarin (20%)` by `0.03 mL`
- raise `Iso E Super` by `0.08 mL`

If you want it more floral and less woody:
- raise `Hedione` by `0.10 mL`
- raise `Methyl Benzoate` by `0.05 mL`
- reduce `Iso E Super` by `0.10 mL`

If you want it dirtier / more narcotic:
- raise `Indole (10%)` by `0.02-0.04 mL`

Do not raise `Indole (10%)` aggressively on the first batch.
