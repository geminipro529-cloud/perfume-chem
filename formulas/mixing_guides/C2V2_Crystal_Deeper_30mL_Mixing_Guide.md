## Design

**DHM Removal Rationale:** DHM VP@32°C = 0.7148 mmHg — 150× higher than any iris molecule. At 10.3% of concentrate, DHM accounts for **94.4% of headspace** at skin temperature. The first 30–45 minutes would smell like a citrus sport fragrance, not a luxury iris. The xlsx Molecule Physics sheet confirms DHM receptor = "Multiple" (generic olfactory) with **no TRPV1/TRPA1/VN1R1 pathway**. Unlike Ambrox ("TRPV1 thermal depth sub-ODT") and Cashmeran ("TRPA1 cashmere texture sub-ODT"), DHM has no useful sub-ODT mechanism. Sub-ODT volume = <1 µL (0.016% of concentrate). **Removed entirely; 620 µL redistributed proportionally (×1.115) across remaining 16 materials.**

The crystalline identity now comes from **Helional (OR2W1 cold)** + **Cyclamal (OR2W1 floral)** — the actual crystal molecules with moderate VP values that blend with the iris headspace rather than overwhelming it.

**Inventory dilution adjustment:** xlsx assumes Galaxolide 50%; inventory has Galaxolide 80%. Volume adjusted to match active dose (152 µL active Galaxolide). All other dilutions match (Ambrox 30%, Alpha Irone 30%).

**What changed vs xlsx Crystalline V2: Deeper:**
- **REMOVED:** DHM — 94.4% headspace dominance creates citrus sport, not iris crystal. No sub-ODT utility.
- **SCALED:** All 16 remaining materials ×1.115 proportional to fill 6,000 µL. Iris DNA ratios preserved (8:4:3:2.5:2:3:2.5).
- **ADDED:** Premium Luxury Architecture — 7 crystal-compatible materials (+1,070 µL). Hedione 400µL (diffusion), Cashmeran 250µL (TRPA1 depth), IES 200µL (molecular cocoon), Javanol 80µL (mineral sandalwood), Coumarin 80µL@20% (powdery register), Ebanol 40µL (creamy counter-texture), Maple Lactone 20µL@20% (lactonic dimension).
- **CONCENTRATION:** 20.0% → 24.0% EDP (within luxury EDP range). Iris DNA absolute volume unchanged; percentage diluted by luxury + synergy additions.
- **SYNERGY RESOLUTION:** +Musk Ketone powder 0.02g (powder-character echo musk for iris register) + Hexyl Salicylate 120µL (transparent salicylate fixative for skin effect). Two broken synergies repaired.
- **RESULT:** Iris DNA 41.9% of concentrate (was 50.3%). Crystal transparency 11.8% (was 14.1%). Luxury score +21.1 with only −2.9 clarity loss. Opening is metallic crystal iris with expanded diffusion radius.

## Mixing Instructions

### Step 1 — Weigh Myristic Acid
Weigh 0.22g Myristic Acid powder into a clean 50mL beaker. Set aside.

### Step 2 — Build the Iris DNA Core
Pipette ingredients 1–7 into a separate vial (3,015 µL total). Swirl gently. This is the iris concentrate — handle with care, it's the irreducible identity.

### Step 3 — Add Crystal Transparency Layer
Add Helional (485 µL) and Cyclamen Aldehyde (360 µL). These two OR2W1 agonists define the crystalline spatial effect.

### Step 4 — Add Orris Anchor
Add Alpha Irone 30% (180 µL). This is 54 µL active genuine orris character.

### Step 5 — Add VN1R1 + Thermal Depth
Add Hedione HC (240 µL) and Ambrox Super 30% (250 µL).

### Step 6 — Add B1 Ozonic Mineral Base
Add Habanolide (725 µL), Ethylene Brassylate (275 µL), Romandolide (280 µL), Galaxolide 80% (190 µL).

### Step 6.5 — Add Premium Luxury Architecture
Add in order of descending volume:
1. Hedione (400 µL) — radiance diffusion amplifier
2. Cashmeran neat (250 µL) — invisible cashmere depth
3. Iso E Super (200 µL) — molecular skin-cocoon
4. Javanol (80 µL) — mineral sandalwood
5. Coumarin 20% (80 µL) — powdery register (16 µL active)
6. Ebanol (40 µL) — creamy counter-texture
7. Maple Lactone 20% (20 µL) — lactonic dimension (4 µL active)

### Step 6.7 — Add Synergy Resolution
Add:
1. Musk Ketone powder (0.02g) — weigh and set aside; will dissolve with Myristic Acid in Step 7
2. Hexyl Salicylate neat (120 µL) — transparent salicylate fixative; creates salicylate × musk skin-effect synergy (zero salicylate before this addition)

### Step 7 — Combine with Ethanol
Pour the Myristic Acid and Musk Ketone powder into the beaker. Add ~5 mL warm (40°C) ethanol, stir until both solids dissolve completely. Pour in the 7,190 µL liquid concentrate. Rinse all vials with remaining ethanol. Top up to exactly 30.00 mL with ethanol (22,810 µL).

### Step 8 — Maceration
Seal and store in dark. **Minimum 3 weeks.** The Helional/Cyclamen Aldehyde crystal dyad needs time to equilibrate with the iris DNA core — the spatial geometry of the crystalline effect only emerges after molecular reorganization. Alpha Irone transforms dramatically: the initial metallic sharpness softens into authentic orris butter. Judge no earlier than day 21.

## Temporal Evolution — Opus V Iris Accord by Vapor Pressure

Materials ordered by volatility (highest VP evaporates first). The iris reads differently at each phase — not because materials "appear," but because faster-evaporating molecules unmask slower ones underneath.

**Phase 1 — Violet-Ketonic Flash (0–30 min)**
The highest-VP iris materials hit first:
- **Cyclamen Aldehyde** (360µL) — cold metallic geometric edge; the crystalline signature
- **Alpha Ionone** (485µL) — violet-orris flash, powdery and bright; bulk iris volume in the opening
- **Beta Ionone** (360µL) — darker violet depth; the nose is ~140× more sensitive to Beta Ionone than to Alpha Ionone, so it dominates perceived character despite equal volume
- **AIMI** (970µL) — iris body and powdery volume; sheer mass compensates for low volatility
- **Hedione** (400µL) — air and expansion around the iris materials; opens the diffusion radius

This reads as a **violet-powder cloud with a metallic edge** — the declared-orris top of Opus V.

**Phase 2 — Buttery-Metallic Orris Heart (30 min–2h)**
As the violet flash thins, the true orris character emerges:
- **Alpha Irone** (54µL active) — the real orris signature; only 54µL active but the nose detects it at concentrations 20× lower than Alpha Ionone — this is why orris reads as orris and not just violet
- **Beta Ionone** — still strongly perceived; darker violet undertow beneath the irone
- **AIMI** (970µL) — continues as iris body
- **Ultralia** (360µL) — ghost-iris halo and coolness; transparent powder-cold
- **Helional** (485µL) — cold crystal spatial frame; Cyclamen Aldehyde's opening flash has thinned but Helional sustains the crystalline cavity the orris sits inside — the OR2W1 glass architecture persists
- **Hedione** (400µL) — projects the iris outward

This reads as **buttery-metallic orris concrete inside a crystal frame** — the heart character of Opus V. Alpha Irone is the identity; Beta Ionone is the violet depth; Ultralia is the cold halo; Helional is the spatial architecture.

**Phase 3 — Warm Iris Radiance (2–6h)**
The iris darkens and warms:
- **AIMI** (970µL) — still holding iris body
- **Orivone** (300µL) — buttery-metallic orris body emerges fully; the warm fatty orris-concrete character
- **Alpha Irone** — persists; the orris metallic thread continues
- **Hedione HC** (240µL) — VN1R1 pheromone channel; intimate warmth-perception beneath the iris
- **IES** (200µL) — molecular cocoon; abstract cedar skin-halo wrapping the iris
- **Cashmeran** (250µL) — invisible cashmere depth; warm textile under the orris

This reads as **warm iris with dark wood undertow** — where the declared orris starts bridging into the dry-wood accord.

**Phase 4 — Dark Iris-Wood Drydown (6–12h)**
The lowest-VP iris materials finally read:
- **DHBI** (300µL) — woody-violet persistence; the nose needs 100× more DHBI than Alpha Irone to perceive it, so it only reads once louder materials have faded
- **Koavone** (240µL) — warm woody iris bridge; iris-to-wood transition material
- **Habanolide** (725µL) — warm macrocyclic skin depth
- **Galaxolide** (152µL active) — ionone-perception lift; pushes fading iris molecules back above detection threshold, maintaining the iris shadow into the drydown
- **Ambrox** (75µL active) — small crystalline amber-wood undertow
- **Javanol** (80µL) — mineral sandalwood; dry intimate skin-scent
- **Ebanol** (40µL) — creamy sandalwood counter-texture
- **Hexyl Salicylate** (120µL) — transparent salicylate film; adhesion layer that keeps the drydown polished and creates musk × salicylate skin-effect synergy

This reads as **dark root-wood with iris shadow** — the Opus V base structure where iris meets dry woods. Galaxolide ensures the ionone stack remains perceptible; without it, iris would vanish at 6h.

**Phase 5 — Macrolide Skin Trail (12–24h+)**
- **EB** (275µL) + **Romandolide** (280µL) — macrolide musk bed; longest tenacity materials
- **IES** (200µL) — molecular halo persists; ghostly iris memory
- **Musk Ketone** (12µL active) — powder-character echo musk; the nitro-musk powdery sweetness reinforces the fading iris powder register. ODT 0.005 ppb — perceived as talcum-powder even at trace airborne concentrations. This is why Phase 5 still reads "iris" and not just "clean musk."
- **Myristic Acid** — skin depot; fatty anchor extending the silage on warm skin

This reads as **clean skin with iris ghost** — the intimate signature.
