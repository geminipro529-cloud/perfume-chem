## The Problem With the Original Formula

The original Eclipse VII dosed materials by **mL intuition** — pouring what "feels right." Stevens' Power Law ($PI = OV^{0.6}$, where $OV = \text{Conc}_{ppm} / ODT_{eth}$) reveals that perceived smell ≠ volume poured:

- **Ambrox Super** (250 µL at 30%) + **Norlimbanol** (80 µL neat) = only 15.8% of the liquid but **44.7% of what you actually smell** — their ODT is 0.05 ppm, 1000× more potent than Benzyl Salicylate
- **Suederal** (the core leather note) was **1.8% of perceived scent** — nearly invisible
- **Guaiacol** (the campfire smoke) was **0.8%** — sub-perceptual in the mixture
- **Galaxolide** (the "sterile void" — the concept anchor) was only **5.7%** — too quiet to carry the black hole metaphor

The formula read as a crystalline amber-woody bomb, not leather-smoke.

## Musk Chord Analysis

2-material musk chord serving distinct axes:

| Musk | Axis | µL (30mL) | Active µL | Perceived | Function |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| **Galaxolide 80%** | Projection + concept | 840 | 672 | 10.0% | The "sterile void" — cold, synthetic, clean nothingness. Extends the event horizon outward. Projection axis. At ODT 1.0 ppm, needs volume. |
| **Ethylene Brassylate** | Depth + warmth | 600 | 600 | 4.8% | Creamy lactonic substrate beneath the leather. Depth axis — the warm gravity well below the cold void. At ODT 3.0 ppm, needs mass. |

**Why not Habanolide:** The composition needs cold sterility (Galaxolide) and warm gravity (EB). Habanolide's "warm skin" quality would soften the conceptual tension.  
**Why not Romandolide:** Clean-woody diffusion would blur the Galaxolide void with another clean musk layer. Two distinct temperatures, not two clean musks.

## Amber Register

**Crystalline-mineral** (Ambrox Super 30%) — not warm-rounded (Ambermax), not transparent (Amberwood F). The event horizon demands mineral coldness, not comfort. At ODT 0.05 ppm, Ambrox anchors 15.7% of the scent from only 3.8% of the concentrate — the most efficient material in the formula.

## Fixative Strategy

**Heavy fixation** (Benzyl Salicylate, 450 µL neat) — chosen for maximum diffusion cushion and molecular weight in a dark composition. At ODT 50.0 ppm, barely perceptible — pure structural function; sillage architecture without olfactive character.

**Why not Hexyl Salicylate:** Too light/transparent for this darkness level — Hexyl Sal creates a sheer film; this formula needs a deep waxy cushion.  
**Why not Benzyl Benzoate:** This formula already has Evernyl + Guaiacol + IBQ providing balsamic darkness — adding more near-odorless fixative would displace active materials.

## Mixing Instructions

### Step 1 — Prepare Workspace
Clean 50mL beaker, 1mL graduated pipette, 5mL graduated cylinder. Have ethanol measured out at 23.70 mL. All materials at room temperature.

### Step 2 — Build the Leather-Smoke Heart First
Pipette into the beaker in this order:
1. Evernyl — 450 µL (thick; move slowly)
2. Vetiver EO — 450 µL
3. Suederal 10% — 600 µL
4. Guaiacol 10% — 240 µL
5. IBQ 10% — 30 µL

> Build the dark complex first so the campfire-phenolic-animalic materials integrate.

### Step 3 — Add the Molecular-Amber Architecture
6. Iso E Super — 1,050 µL (use 5mL graduated cylinder: 1.05 mL)
7. Norlimbanol Dextro — 30 µL (**caution:** ultra-potent, measure precisely)
8. Ambrox Super 30% — 240 µL
9. Timberol — 300 µL
10. Cashmeran 20% — 450 µL

### Step 4 — Add the Void Musks
11. Galaxolide 80% — 840 µL
12. Ethylene Brassylate — 600 µL

### Step 5 — Add Fixative
13. Benzyl Salicylate — 450 µL

### Step 6 — Add the Spice-Citrus Top Last
14. Ethyl Safranate — 90 µL
15. Black Pepper FTEC — 240 µL
16. Cedrat FCF Sicilian — 240 µL

> Top notes last to preserve their volatile character during maceration.

### Step 7 — Combine With Ethanol
Pour concentrate into the ethanol (23.70 mL). Rinse beaker with a few mL of the ethanol, add back. Swirl gently — do not shake. Top up to exactly 30.00 mL.

### Step 8 — Maceration
Seal tightly. Store in a cool, dark place. **Minimum 2 weeks.** The vetiver-evernyl-guaiacol smoke complex needs time to integrate with the Ambrox-Norlimbanol mineral core. The leather becomes coherent around day 14. The musk void fills out around day 21.

> **Safety note:** Guaiacol and IBQ are skin sensitizers at neat concentration. At 10% dilution in a 21% EDP, the final skin dose is well within IFRA limits, but avoid prolonged neat contact during mixing.

## Adjustment Guide

| If it smells... | Adjust | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Too much saffron / metallic | Reduce Ethyl Safranate to 60 µL | ODT 0.1 ppm — small cuts have large perceptual effect |
| Not dark enough | Add 30 µL Birch Tar Rectified (neat) | Adds actual tar-smoke. Trace level only. |
| Too abstract / woody | Reduce Iso E Super to 750 µL | Cut molecular haze to reveal leather-smoke clarity |
| Norlimbanol too loud | Reduce to 15 µL | Even 15 µL = 6%+ perceived. Extremely potent. |
| Needs more animalic edge | Increase IBQ to 60 µL (10%) | 6 µL active = OV 30,000. Only if you want confrontational. |
| Musk void too clean | Add 150 µL Habanolide | Warm-skin intimacy in the void. Changes character slightly. |
| Suede invisible after maceration | Increase Suederal to 900 µL (10%) | Some Suederal integrates into the smoke complex and disappears. |
| Too heavy / cloying | Reduce Cashmeran to 300 µL, EB to 400 µL | Strips textile warmth and lactonic mass |
| Needs more citrus bite | Add 120 µL Grapefruit FCF | Bitter-pink pith. Keeps dark character. |
