---

## Design Intent

Porcelaine = the abstract scent of luxury cosmetics (plastic-floral-clean). Porcelaine Poudrée = the abstract scent of a **luxury face powder compact** — the iris-violet-heliotropin poudré character of Guerlain Météorites married to the DBCA cosmetic-plastic DNA.

**What changed from original Porcelaine:**

| Shift | Original Porcelaine | Porcelaine Poudrée |
|-------|--------------------|--------------------|
| **Character axis** | Cosmetic plastic floral (muguet-dominant) | Cosmetic iris powder (ionone-dominant) |
| **Salicylate** | Benzyl Salicylate 250µL (OUT OF STOCK) | Hexyl Salicylate 180µL — lighter, greener, transparent |
| **Muguet layer** | 5 materials, 570µL total | 2 materials, 100µL — minimized to connector (HCA 60, Lilyreal 40) |
| **Iris/powder** | Absent | 7 materials, 690µL — AIMI 120 + Methyl Ionone Pure 80 + Orivone 100 + Heliotropin 150 + I-IRIS 80 + Orris FTEC 60 + Ultralia 100 |
| **Coumarinic base** | Absent | Coumarin 250µL (20%) — hay-sweet powder drydown, increased for base-level powder anchoring |
| **Amber** | Ambrox Super (crystalline mineral) | Removed — replaced by coumarinic warmth |
| **Musk character** | Clean-skin | Powdery — Ethylene Brassylate added |
| **Iso E Super** | 300µL (molecular cocoon dominant) | 200µL — pulled back so powder reads over wood |

**Porcelaine DNA retained:** DBCA gardenia-rose cosmetic character, Hedione radiance amplification, salicylate cushion diffusion, laundered citrus lift architecture, clean musk base.

## Reference Formula (10mL Batch)

**Accord Profile:** Laundered Lift · DBCA Cosmetic Character · Ionone Iris-Powder · Heliotropin Face-Powder · Coumarinic Warmth → Powdery Musk Skin  
**Commercial Reference:** Prada Infusion d'Iris × Byredo Blanche — the iris-powder cosmetic genre  
**Wearing Impression:** Open a vintage Guerlain powder compact and press your nose into the puff. That powdery-violet-almond warmth — rendered in modern synthetics. DBCA anchors the cosmetic-product character from original Porcelaine but now the heart is a powder-optimized iris accord: Alpha Isomethyl Ionone split with Methyl Ionone Pure (whose powder-to-floral ratio is inverted vs AIMI), Orivone for buttery orris warmth, Heliotropin for the almond-vanilla face-powder effect, I-IRIS FTEC for iris complexity, Orris FTEC as pure powder reinforcement (powder score 8 — highest in inventory), Ultralia as ghost-iris gossamer. The muguet layer from original Porcelaine is minimized to bare connector dose — Hydroxycitronellal at 60µL and Lilyreal ND at 40µL bridge DBCA cosmetic to iris-powder without cool-floral competition. Hexyl Salicylate provides a lighter, greener salicylate cushion (Benzyl Sal is out of stock, but Hexyl Sal is actually better here — its transparency lets the powder read clean). The base shifts from crystalline-mineral (Ambrox) to coumarinic-warm (Coumarin 250µL + Cashmeran), and Ethylene Brassylate adds a powdery dimension to the musk accord. Wears soft, close, intimate — makeup counter at a luxury department store.

## Step-by-Step Mixing Instructions

### Phase 1: Prepare Workspace (5 min)

1. **Clean workspace** — Wipe down with ethanol-dampened paper towel
2. **Arrange materials** — Lay out all 21 aroma materials + ethanol in order of addition
3. **Label your bottle** — "Porcelaine Poudrée / 30mL / 24.5% / [Date]"
4. **Put on gloves** — Hexyl Salicylate and Heliotropin can be irritating neat

### Phase 2: Build Top Notes (3 min)

**Target:** Soft citrus-lavender lift (0.75 mL total)

5. **Add Bergamot FCF Sicilian** — Pipette 0.24 mL (240 µL) into beaker  
   *Olfactive check: Citrus-bergamot freshness — lighter dose than original Porcelaine*

6. **Add Linalyl Acetate** — Pipette 0.36 mL (360 µL) into beaker  
   *Olfactive check: Lavender-acetate clean-laundry lift — still reads "laundered" but won't overpower the powder*

7. **Add Linalool** — Pipette 0.15 mL (150 µL) into beaker  
   *Olfactive check: Floral-woody transparency, bridges citrus to heart*

8. **Stir gently** — 10 seconds with stirring rod  
   **Check:** Soft laundered citrus — cleaner and lighter than original Porcelaine's top

---

### Phase 3: Build Cosmetic Iris-Powder Heart (10 min)

**Target:** Full iris-powder floral core (4.05 mL total)

**Step 3A — Cosmetic Foundation**

9. **Add DBCA** — Pipette 0.39 mL (390 µL) into beaker  
   *Material note: Gardenia-rose accord, THE cosmetic-plastic character material. This IS the Porcelaine DNA.*

10. **Add Hedione** — Pipette 0.75 mL (750 µL) into beaker  
    *Material note: Radiance amplifier — heavy dose projects the iris-powder into three-dimensional volume*

11. **Add Hexyl Salicylate** — Pipette 0.54 mL (540 µL) into beaker  
    *Material note: Lighter salicylate cushion than Benzyl Sal — green-transparent, lets the powder character read clearly. This is the diffusion engine + fixative.*

12. **Stir for 20 seconds** — These 3 materials are the cosmetic foundation  
    **Check:** Should smell like "luxury beauty products" — the original Porcelaine DNA, but more transparent

**Step 3B — Iris-Powder Accord (OPTIMIZED FOR POWDER DOMINANCE)**

13. **Add Alpha Isomethyl Ionone** — Pipette 0.36 mL (360 µL) into beaker  
    *Material note: Violet-powder ionone. Reduced from 600µL because AIMI is floral(6)/powder(5) — its floral contribution slightly exceeds its powder. Still the primary face-powder voice, but now balanced by Methyl Ionone Pure.*

14. **Add Methyl Ionone Pure** — Pipette 0.24 mL (240 µL) into beaker  
    *Material note: THE powder-biased ionone — powder(6)/floral(5), inverting AIMI's ratio. The dual-ionone blend (AIMI + MIP) creates a more faceted violet-powder with net powder dominance. MIP's slightly woodier character also bridges into the Iso E Super base.*

15. **Add Orivone** — Pipette 0.30 mL (300 µL) into beaker  
    *Material note: Warm buttery orris depth. Powder score of 7 — highest among the iris materials. Provides the "butter on suede" orris effect beneath the violet-powder.*

16. **Add Heliotropin Fleuressence** — Pipette 0.45 mL (450 µL) into beaker  
    *Material note: Almond-vanilla powder character. The classic poudré material — think old-fashioned face powder, marzipan-powdery without sweetness when dosed here.*

17. **Add I-IRIS F-TEC** — Pipette 0.24 mL (240 µL) into beaker  
    *Material note: Pre-built iris accord, powder(7) — adds faceted iris complexity that individual ionones can't achieve alone*

18. **Add Orris F-TEC** — Pipette 0.18 mL (180 µL) into beaker  
    *Material note: Powder score of **8** — THE highest powder-scoring material in inventory. Orris-creamy-woody character fills the volume gap left by reducing muguet, without adding any cool-floral drag.*

19. **Add Ultralia** — Pipette 0.30 mL (300 µL) into beaker  
    *Material note: Ghost iris — gossamer powdery transparency (powder 6). At this dose it creates an iris "halo" that makes the powder effect three-dimensional.*

20. **Stir for 30 seconds**  
    **Check:** THIS is the optimized powder shift. Dual-ionone blend + Orivone + Orris FTEC should read as pure powder-iris without cool-floral competition. More "Guerlain compact" than "department store muguet."

**Step 3C — Muguet Connector (Minimized)**

21. **Add Hydroxycitronellal** — Pipette 0.18 mL (180 µL) into beaker  
    *Material note: Waxy muguet at minimum viable dose. HCA is floral(7)/powder(2) — every extra µL drags character toward cool-floral. At 180µL it retains its bridging function (DBCA→iris) without competing with the powder frame.*

22. **Add Lilyreal ND** — Pipette 0.12 mL (120 µL) into beaker  
    *Material note: Cosmetic muguet body at minimum connector dose. Lilyreal is floral(7)/powder(2) — the biggest powder-drag per-µL in the original formula. At 120µL it barely whispers — enough to maintain the cosmetic-cleanness code without readable lily-of-the-valley.*

23. **Stir for 20 seconds**  
    **Check:** The muguet should be invisible as a discrete note — present only as a textural softener beneath the dominant powder

---

### Phase 4: Build Coumarinic Powder-Musk Base (5 min)

**Target:** Warm powdery musk skin effect (2.55 mL total)

24. **Add Coumarin (20%)** — Pipette 0.75 mL (750 µL) into beaker  
    *Material note: Hay-sweet powder drydown. Increased from original to fill the base-weight gap left by muguet reduction. 150µL active coumarin at 20% dilution. This is the anchor that makes the powder frame persist into late drydown.*

25. **Add Iso E Super** — Pipette 0.60 mL (600 µL) into beaker  
    *Material note: Molecular cocoon, abstract cedar. Reduced from original Porcelaine (0.90→0.60) so the powder character reads over the wood.*

26. **Add Cashmeran (20%)** — Pipette 0.45 mL (450 µL) into beaker  
    *Material note: Suede-cashmere warmth. Increased from original — its musky-powdery character supports the iris-powder accord. 30µL active Cashmeran.*

27. **Add Galaxolide (80%)** — Pipette 0.30 mL (300 µL) into beaker  
    *Material note: Clean laundry musk projector — maintains the cosmetic-clean code*

28. **Add Habanolide** — Pipette 0.24 mL (240 µL) into beaker  
    *Material note: Clean macrocyclic musk, skin-scent character*

29. **Add Ethylene Brassylate** — Pipette 0.21 mL (210 µL) into beaker  
    *Material note: Macrocyclic musk with powdery dimension — THIS is the musk that reads "face powder." It bridges the iris-powder heart into the musk base.*

30. **Stir for 30 seconds**  
    **Check:** The base should be warm, powdery, musky — coumarin + cashmeran + powdery musks creating a "cashmere face powder" effect

---

### Phase 5: Dilute and Bottle (3 min)

31. **Add ethanol 96%** — Measure 22.65 mL and add to beaker  
    *This creates 24.5% concentration*

32. **Stir vigorously** — 60 seconds until fully homogeneous  
    *Coumarin (20% in DPG) may create slight cloudiness — normal, will clear during maceration*

33. **Transfer to bottle** — Pour through funnel into 30mL amber glass bottle

34. **Cap tightly** — Wipe any drips from bottle threads

---

### Phase 6: Maceration

35. **Store** — Dark, room temperature, upright
36. **Week 1** — Shake gently once daily for 30 seconds
37. **Week 2** — Shake every other day
38. **Week 3–4** — Leave undisturbed
39. **First evaluation** — At 3 weeks. The iris-powder accord needs time for the ionones and coumarin to integrate with the DBCA cosmetic foundation.
40. **Full maturation** — 4–6 weeks. Heliotropin and coumarin bloom slowly — the powder character deepens significantly after week 4.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
|-------|-------|-----|
| Too sweet/vanilla | Heliotropin dominating | Reduce Heliotropin to 0.30 mL, increase Hexyl Sal to 0.66 mL |
| Not powdery enough | Ionone dose too low | Increase Methyl Ionone Pure to 0.30 mL or Orris FTEC to 0.24 mL |
| Too flat/no projection | Hedione too low | Increase Hedione to 0.90 mL |
| Iris too aggressive | Ultralia or I-IRIS FTEC too high | Reduce each by 0.06 mL |
| Coumarin too "old-fashioned" | Coumarin dominating base | Reduce to 0.45 mL, increase Iso E Super to 0.75 mL |
| Muguet competing with powder | HCA + Lilyreal too present | Reduce HCA to 0.12 mL and Lilyreal to 0.09 mL |
| Drydown too woody | Iso E Super reading over powder | Reduce to 0.45 mL, increase Ethylene Brassylate to 0.30 mL |
| Methyl Ionone Pure too sharp | Powder reading chalky | Reduce to 0.18 mL, redistribute back to AIMI |

### Temporal Architecture — Prose Timeline

**Act I: Laundered Citrus-Powder Opening (0–15 min)**
Engine says: Bergamot FCF Sicilian (OAV 273), Linalool (45), Iso E Super (44), Cashmeran (35), Hedione (27). Character: transparency/floral/freshness.
What you smell: Bergamot and linalool create an initial clean-citrus burst, but the ionone powder is already present underneath — this isn't a "citrus first, then powder" formula. It's powder with a citrus wash over it. The Iso E Super cocoon and Cashmeran warmth are already active at the base level. Methyl Ionone Pure and Orris FTEC work subliminally/structurally to tilt the ionone cluster toward powder. Very different from original Porcelaine, where the muguet reads immediately.

**Act II: Iris-Powder Plateau (15 min–4 hr)**
Engine says: Bergamot fading (273→150), but AIMI (~20), Orivone, Heliotropin, and Hedione all holding steady. Character: transparency(22%)/floral(15%)/freshness(13%).
What you smell: The citrus wash thins and the full powder compact opens. Alpha Isomethyl Ionone's violet-powder, supported by Methyl Ionone Pure's powder-biased character, creates a richer ionone strata than the pre-optimization formula. Orivone's buttery orris and Heliotropin's almond-vanilla deepen the powder. Orris FTEC (powder 8) adds pure powder reinforcement. Hedione provides the radiance that makes the powder three-dimensional. The muguet connector (HCA + Lilyreal) is now subliminal — it bridges DBCA cosmetic to the iris-powder core without any discrete lily-of-the-valley competition.

**Act III: Coumarinic Powder-Musk Skin (4–12 hr)**
Engine says: Bergamot still perceptible (150→83) but no longer proportionally dominant. Heart materials supremely stable. Coumarin (increased to 250µL) and base musks now increasingly important.
What you smell: This is the "face powder compact left open on the vanity" phase. Coumarin's hay-sweet drydown — now 25% stronger than pre-optimization — merges with the ionone powder. Cashmeran adds suede warmth. The character dimension begins shifting: powdery starts climbing toward 12% while freshness fades. Extremely linear — someone smelling you at hour 8 smells essentially the same composition as hour 2, but with more powder weight.

**Act IV: Intimate Powder Skin (12–24 hr)**
Engine says: All major materials still above OAV 2. Character: transparency(22%)/floral(15%)/**powdery(12%)**. Projection ~70 cm.
What you smell: Close-range powder-skin effect. The optimization pays off here: powdery is now the tertiary character (replacing freshness, which has faded with the bergamot). Iso E Super's molecular cocoon, the macrocyclic musks (Habanolide, subliminal Galaxolide and Ethylene Brassylate), and the ionone-coumarin powder core create an intimate "this person smells expensive" trail. The DBCA cosmetic character operates subliminally — you can't pick it out, but it's why the whole thing reads "luxury cosmetics" rather than "floral perfume." The powder optimization means the final impression is genuinely powdery, not merely "clean floral with some powder." This is the Guerlain Météorites effect.
