# Iris Allure Homme Sport Luxe — 30 mL Mixing Guide

**Date:** 2026-04-03  
**Target Style:** `Chanel Allure Homme Sport` direction with more longevity, depth, texture, and luxury  
**Floral Rule:** The floral identity is built as `iris only`  
**Concentration:** `25%` in ethanol  
**Batch Size:** `30.00 mL`

This is not a literal Chanel clone. It is a current-inventory build aimed at:
- sparkling citrus-sport lift
- creamy polished masculine body
- long-lasting wood-amber-musky base
- an iris heart replacing the usual white-floral support

---

## F-TEC Decision

I checked `I-IRIS F-TEC` and `Orris F-TEC` before using them.

What is defensible from the supplier documents:
- `I-IRIS F-TEC` is described as a soft, wet, faint-wood iris base and the official documents show it is heavily `Alpha Isomethyl Ionone` led, with smaller supporting amounts of `Alpha Irone`, `DBCA`, `Tonalide`, `Nerolidol`, `Alpha Damascone`, and other trace support materials.
- `Orris F-TEC` official documents disclose a much smoother texture profile built around `Nerolidol`, `Alpha Isomethyl Ionone`, and `Iso E Super`.

That means:
- `I-IRIS F-TEC` is safe to use as a **small structural iris helper**
- `Orris F-TEC` should be treated as an **optional texture experiment**, not as the backbone of the iris

So the main perfume uses `I-IRIS F-TEC` modestly, and the `Orris F-TEC` is pushed into separate small-scale trials at the end.

## Amber Dilution Prep

Prepare these as **separate `10.00 mL` stock solutions** before mixing the perfume.

### Ambrox Super — `30% w/v`

- `Ambrox Super`: `3.00 g`
- `IPM`: `1.00 mL`
- `DPG`: `0.10 mL`
- `Ethanol 96%`: `q.s. to 10.00 mL total volume`

### Ambrofix — `30% w/v`

- `Ambrofix`: `3.00 g`
- `IPM`: `1.00 mL`
- `DPG`: `0.10 mL`
- `Ethanol 96%`: `q.s. to 10.00 mL total volume`

### How To Make The Stocks

1. Put the solid amber material in a clean `10 mL` vial or graduated mixing vessel.
2. Add `DPG`, then `IPM`, and swirl to wet the crystals.
3. Add part of the `ethanol 96%`, warm gently if needed, and swirl until the material is fully dissolved.
4. Add more `ethanol 96%` until the **final total volume** reaches exactly `10.00 mL`.
5. Cap and shake well.
6. If any crystals appear after standing, warm gently and shake again before use.

The `150 µL` entries in the main formula refer to these prepared `30% w/v` stocks, not neat material.

## Why This Works

### 1. It keeps the Allure Homme Sport silhouette
- `Cedrat + bergamot + blood orange` give the Chanel-style sparkling citrus shell
- `Dihydromyrcenol + Helional + trace Floralozone` create the polished sport-air effect
- `Cardamom + black pepper` provide the sleek masculine lift
- `Coumarin + vanillin + Amber Core Accord` give the warm creamy finish without turning it into dessert

### 2. The flowers stay iris
- There is no neroli, rose, jasmine, peony, muguet, or ylang architecture carrying the floral theme
- The floral body is the iris stack:
  `Alpha Irone + I-IRIS F-TEC + Methyl Ionone Pure + Alpha Isomethyl Ionone + Irotyl + Dihydro Beta Ionone + Ultralia + Carrot Seed EO`
- `Hedione` is used here as radiance and expansion, not as a jasmine statement

### 3. The base is built for maximum completion and wear
- `Amberwood F + Ambermax + Cedramber + Ambrox Super + Ambrofix + Norlimbanol Dextro + Timberol` create the long-wear modern woody-amber backbone
- `Ambrox Super (30% w/v)` adds the brighter crystalline ambergris lift while `Ambrofix (30% w/v)` adds a drier cleaner fixation line, so the base gets more expensive and persistent without losing the sporty outline
- `Iso E Super + Bacdanol + Sandalore + Javanol + Clearwood` give texture, depth, and luxury movement
- `Habanolide + Galaxolide + Ethylene Brassylate + Benzyl Salicylate` keep the drydown plush, diffusive, and expensive instead of dry and hollow

---

## Expected Wearing Arc

- `0–20 min`: bright sport citrus, airy polished freshness, pepper-cardamom sparkle
- `20–120 min`: the iris moves forward and turns the Chanel skeleton colder, silkier, and more luxurious
- `2+ hours`: the drydown becomes creamy wood-amber-musk with a cleaner ambergris trace, while ghost iris stays visible over a denser backbone

This should read like:
`Allure Homme Sport made denser, smoother, and more luxurious, with the floral axis replaced by iris`

## Mixing Order

1. Add `Iso E Super`, `Amberwood F`, `Cedramber`, `Ambermax (50%)`, `Ambrox Super (30% w/v)`, `Ambrofix (30% w/v)`, `Bacdanol`, `Sandalore`, `Javanol`, `Clearwood`, `Amber Core Accord`.
2. Add `Habanolide`, `Galaxolide (80%)`, `Ethylene Brassylate`, `Benzyl Salicylate`, `Cashmeran (20%)`, `Norlimbanol Dextro`, `Vetival`, `Timberol`.
3. Add `Coumarin (20%)` and `Vanillin (10%)`.
4. Add the iris block:
   `Alpha Irone (10%)`, `I-IRIS F-TEC`, `Methyl Ionone Pure`, `Alpha Isomethyl Ionone`, `Irotyl`, `Dihydro Beta Ionone`, `Allyl Ionone (Ketone V)`, `Ultralia`, `Carrot Seed EO`, `Hedione`.
5. Add the top:
   `Cedrat FCF oil Sicilian`, `Bergamot FCF oil Sicilian`, `Blood Orange oil Sicilian`, `Dihydromyrcenol`, `Linalyl Acetate`, `Linalool`, `Cardamom FTEC (10%)`, `Black Pepper FTEC`, `Helional`, `Floralozone (10%)`, `Methyl Pamplemousse (10%)`, `Aldehyde C11 (1%)`, `Aldehyde C12 MNA (1%)`.
6. Stir thoroughly for `60–90 seconds`.
7. Add `Ethanol 96%`.
8. Shake well.
9. Rest `14 days` minimum.
10. Best read: `3–4 weeks`.

### How To Read The Trials

- If `Control` is best, leave the main formula exactly as written.
- If `Trial A` is best, in the main formula replace `0.10 mL` of `Methyl Ionone Pure` with `0.10 mL` of `Orris F-TEC`.
- If `Trial B` is best, replace `0.15 mL` of `Methyl Ionone Pure` plus `0.05 mL` of `Hedione` with `0.20 mL` of `Orris F-TEC`.
- Do not push `Orris F-TEC` beyond that until you have smelled the result on skin.

## Fast Adjustment Knobs

If you want it fresher:
- raise `Cedrat FCF oil Sicilian` by `100 µL`
- raise `Dihydromyrcenol` by `80 µL`
- reduce `Amberwood F` by `80 µL`
- reduce `Coumarin (20%)` by `20 µL`

If you want more iris body:
- raise `Methyl Ionone Pure` by `50 µL`
- raise `Irotyl` by `20 µL`
- reduce `Dihydromyrcenol` by `30 µL`
- reduce `Iso E Super` by `40 µL`

If you want more drydown luxury:
- raise `Bacdanol` by `40 µL`
- raise `Habanolide` by `40 µL`
- raise `Benzyl Salicylate` by `40 µL`
- reduce `Dihydromyrcenol` by `40 µL`
