## Intent

A transparent mineral lens around the full Opus V iris spine. Every ionone-family molecule from the xlsx universal core is listed individually — no premade accord, no external recipe file. The iris core registers at the xlsx's original relative ODT levels, with two necessary substitutions (AMI → Alpha-ISO ×1.3, DBI → Dihydro Beta Ionone) documented by the xlsx's Fallback Substitutions sheet.

The crystalline frame projects it outward through cold crystal clarity.

Every frame material serves one of three functions:
1. **Crystalline transparency** — mineral metallic quality, cold light, invisible fixation
2. **Radiance amplification** — VN1R1 activation, volume expansion, luminous diffusion
3. **Performance extension** — projection musk, tenacity structure, depth substrate

Nothing warm. Nothing cosmetic. Nothing opaque. The iris is visible through glass.

## Core Substitutions (from xlsx Fallback Sheet)

### AMI (Amyl Methyl Ionone) — 16 parts → NOT IN INVENTORY

The xlsx Fallback Substitutions sheet specifies:  
> *"AMI → Alpha Isomethyl Ionone × 1.3. Quality Loss: Low. Receptor: OR5A1 maintained. AMI is more tenacious; increase Alpha-ISO and add Koavone ×0.5 extra."*

**Applied:**
- Alpha-ISO addition: 16 × 1.3 = **20.8 parts** (added to core's existing 8 parts → total **28.8 parts**)
- Koavone addition: 16 × 0.5 = **8 parts** (added to core's existing 6 parts → total **14 parts**)

**⚠ OR5A1 adaptation note:** The xlsx Anti-Anosmia sheet warns: *"HIGH — rapid adaptation. Limit to ≤8 parts; use AMI+DBI for different conformer."* The original core uses AMI (16) + Alpha-ISO (8) as two distinct OR5A1 conformers. This substitution collapses both into one conformer (Alpha-ISO) at 28.8 parts. Mitigation:
- Dihydro Beta Ionone (10 parts) provides a secondary ionone conformer with different OR5A1 binding geometry
- Koavone (14 parts) presents a third OR5A1 binding geometry via the musky-woody facet
- If ionone anosmia is perceived after maceration, add 100 µL Alpha Ionone (~1.5 parts) for a fourth conformer

### DBI (Delta Beta Isomethyl Ionone) — 10 parts → NOT IN INVENTORY

No direct xlsx fallback provided for DBI. **Dihydro Beta Ionone** is the closest ionone-family material in the inventory — powdery iris character, OR5A1 activation. Used at 1:1 ratio (10 parts unchanged). This substitution may read slightly less "orris-dry" and slightly more "powdery-violet" than the original DBI — a small character shift within the same ionone register.

### Galaxolide 50% IPM → Galaxolide 80%

The xlsx specifies "Galaxolide 50% IPM" at 8 parts.  
Active content: 8 × 0.50 = 4 parts active Galaxolide.  
User inventory has Galaxolide 80%: 4 / 0.80 = **5 equivalent parts** of the 80% dilution.

## Crystalline Frame — Material Justifications

### 13. Hedione HC (450 µL, 7 parts) — Radiance Amplifier

**Function:** VN1R1 pheromone receptor activation, transparent luminous volume expansion.

**Why Hedione HC (high-cis) and not regular Hedione:** The cis-methyl dihydrojasmonate isomer is the one that activates VN1R1 and creates the radiance/"people lean closer" effect. HC has higher cis-content than regular Hedione, ~30–40% more efficient per µL for luminous projection.

**Why not something else:** The xlsx Sub-ODT sheet explicitly identifies Hedione HC as the VN1R1 activator — *"3–6 pts in concentrate; critical for sillage opening without 'jasmine' ID."* No other inventory material activates VN1R1.

**Dose rationale:** 7 parts matches the xlsx Space Iris variant (Hedione HC 7 parts). At 7 parts, this operates just at the perception boundary — maximum VN1R1 activation while barely registering as jasmine character.

---

### 14. Helional (320 µL, 5 parts) — Mineral Metallic Transparency

**Function:** Creates the crystalline-mineral-water quality — the "glass" around the iris.

**Why Helional and not Scentenal (1%):** Scentenal creates marine-metallic ozone — too oceanic for mineral crystal. Helional reads as clean mineral water, not coastline.  
**Why not Calone (1%):** Explicitly marine.  
**Why not Floralozone (10%):** Green-ozonic but not metallic. Lacks cold mineral hardness.

**Dose rationale:** 5 parts matches the xlsx Space Iris (Helional 5 parts) and Cathedral (4 parts) crystalline variants. The metallic-cold character synergizes with Cyclamen Aldehyde to form the full crystalline spectrum.

**⚠ IFRA note:** Helional IFRA limit = 0.5%. At 320 µL in 30 mL = 1.07% — exceeds IFRA for leave-on products. The xlsx Space Iris variant itself specifies Helional at 5 parts which also exceeds IFRA at standard EDP concentration. **For personal use: acceptable. For sale/distribution: reduce to 150 µL (0.50%).**

---

### 15. Cyclamen Aldehyde (195 µL, 3 parts) — Cold Metallic Edge

**Function:** Green-metallic cold facet. This is the same molecule as "Cyclamal" in the xlsx (CAS 103-95-7).

**Why Cyclamen Aldehyde and not more Helional:** Cyclamen Aldehyde provides a GREEN metallic quality where Helional provides a CLEAN metallic quality. Together: mineral water + cold green metal = crystalline. One alone would be one-dimensional.

**Dose rationale:** The xlsx Crystalline Dark uses Cyclamal at 3 parts; Space Iris at 4 parts. At 3 parts, it's a potent modifier creating the cold edge without sharpness.

---

### 16. Hexyl Salicylate (320 µL, 5 parts) — Transparent Fixative Film

**Function:** Invisible green-floral fixation, sheer adhesion without cosmetic weight.

**Why Hexyl Salicylate and not Benzyl Salicylate:** Crystalline concept requires transparency. Benzyl Salicylate creates deep, waxy, cosmetic-warm cushion — it opacifies the crystal. Hexyl Salicylate creates a thin, green, transparent fixative film — more like a glass surface than a velvet curtain.  
**Why not Benzyl Benzoate:** Nearly odorless — in a formula with only 11 frame materials, every slot must actively contribute to the crystalline concept.

---

### 17. Iso E Super (320 µL, 5 parts) — Molecular Skin Cocoon

**Register:** Molecular-skin. NOT architectural (Timberol), NOT textile (Cashmeran), NOT natural (Cedarwood EO).

**Function:** Invisible molecular resonance between the iris core and the wearer's skin. The xlsx Synergy Reference documents: *"Iso E Super + Cashmeran: OR5A1 + TRPA1 = 'niche signature'"* — but this formula skips Cashmeran to maintain crystalline transparency. IES functions alone as the molecular cocoon.

**Dose rationale:** 5 parts. The xlsx Sub-ODT sheet says *"3–5 pts ONLY when used as aura; at 10+ pts becomes identifiable."* At 5 parts, IES creates the molecular halo at the boundary of perception — maximum adhesion before it reads as "cedar."

### 18. Javanol (260 µL, 4 parts) — Mineral Intimate Sandalwood

**Register:** Dry-intimate.

**Function:** Precise mineral sandalwood — depth without warmth, intimacy without creaminess. The xlsx Synergy Reference documents: *"Ambroxan + Javanol: TRPV1 + OR2AT4 = Baccarat Rouge structural basis"* — at 3 parts Ambrox + 4 parts Javanol, this amber-sandalwood dyad creates warm-skin-sandalwood perception.

**Why Javanol and not Ebanol:** Ebanol's milky, yielding character adds opacity. Every Opus V "Dark" variant uses Ebanol. Crystalline is anti-dark.  
**Why not more Bacdanol:** Already in core at 2 parts. Boosting violates core ratios.  
**Why not Sandalore:** Insufficient depth. Reads "sporty."

---

### 19. Romandolide (320 µL, 5 parts) — Projection Musk

**Musk axis:** PROJECTION.

**Function:** Extends the iris core's sillage cone outward into the space.

**Why Romandolide and not Habanolide:** Habanolide is warm, intimate, skin-hugging — restricts sillage to personal bubble. The user requires great sillage. Romandolide launches fragrance outward. Clean, slightly woody character maintains crystalline neutrality.  
**Why not Zenolide:** Zenolide IS included (#21) on a different axis (character-echo). Its projection is moderate; Romandolide's is the strongest in the macrocyclic family.

**Dose rationale:** 5 parts. Space Iris uses 9 parts; this formula uses less because Hedione HC already provides substantial radiance/projection through VN1R1.

### 20. Ethylene Brassylate (260 µL, 4 parts) — Depth Musk

**Musk axis:** DEPTH.

**Function:** Extreme-tenacity lactonic musk, >24 hours on blotter. Builds the longevity substrate. Its creamy-lactonic quality creates perceived molecular richness — the "expensive smell" at the base.

**Why EB and not Habanolide:** Habanolide tenacity ~8–12 hours. EB tenacity ~24–48 hours. For great longevity, EB is the correct depth musk.  
**Why not Exaltolide (10%):** Exaltolide's fatty skin-oil quality conflicts with crystalline transparency.

**Xlsx synergy:** *"Galaxolide + EB: OR5A2 bimodal — polycyclic + macrolide prevents single-receptor fatigue."* The core's Galaxolide (5 parts) paired with EB (4 parts) creates bimodal OR5A2 stimulation.

## Mixing Order (per xlsx 12-Step Protocol, adapted)

The xlsx Mixing Protocol specifies molecular order by vapor pressure and LogP. Adapted for this formula's individual core molecules:

1. **Dissolve Myristic Acid first.** Weigh 0.10g on a 0.01g scale. Add to ~5 mL ethanol in a beaker. Warm gently (40°C) and stir until dissolved. This becomes your ethanol stock for the formula.

2. **Base/depot molecules (LogP > 4):**  
   `Amberwood F` → `Bacdanol`  
   *Per xlsx protocol: "High LogP = low VP = no evaporative loss during weighing."*

3. **Ionone spine (the iris identity backbone):**  
   `Alpha Isomethyl Ionone` → `Dihydro Beta Ionone` → `Koavone` → `Orivone` → `Ultralia` → `Beta Ionone`  
   *Per xlsx protocol: "Iris accord is the identity backbone. These are the least volatile ionone-family; stable during handling."*  
   *Visual check: clear pale yellow solution.*

4. **Heart molecules (mid-VP bridge):**  
   `Iso E Super` → `Javanol` → `Hexyl Salicylate` → `Hedione HC`  
   *Per xlsx protocol: "Mid-VP molecules that bridge top and base."*  
   *Smell check: iris + cedar + jasmine diffusion.*

5. **Musk stack (full OR5A2 coverage):**  
   `Ethylene Brassylate` → `Romandolide` → `Zenolide` → `Galaxolide 80%` → `Musk Ketone 10%` → `Norlimbanol Dextro`  
   *Per xlsx protocol: "5-family musk stack covers all OR5A2 binding conformers."*  
   *Smell check: clean white musk cloud emerging.*

6. **TRPV1 somatosensory molecule:**  
   `Ambrox Super 30%`  
   *Per xlsx protocol: "TRPV1 agonists amplify all preceding notes via somatic warmth. Add after musk stack."*

7. **Volatile top-note molecules (add last):**  
   `Helional` → `Cyclamen Aldehyde` → `Alpha Irone 30%`  
   *Per xlsx protocol: "High VP — add at end to prevent evaporative loss. Seal immediately after adding."*

8. **Add remaining ethanol** (balance from step 1 stock) to reach 30.00 mL.

9. **Close, shake 30s, rest 10 min, reshake.** Per xlsx: *"Raoult's Law mixing: all partial pressures reach equilibrium. Ionone molecules need contact time with musk matrix for H-bond bridge formation."*

10. **Macerate at room temp, dark.** Minimum `14 days`. Optimal `21 days`. Per xlsx: *"Ester formation equilibria; oxidative maturation of ionones; H-bond network formation. REQUIRED — unmacerated = harsh."*

11. **Optional chill-filter:** 0–4°C for 48h. Per xlsx: *"Removes waxes (Myristic Acid precipitates at 0°C if excess). Prevents cloud formation in EtOH solution."*

12. **Already diluted** — ethanol was added at step 8, so the xlsx's separate dilution step is integrated. Concentration is 24.7% (between EDP and Parfum).

## Notes

- **No premade accord used.** All 13 xlsx universal core molecules are listed individually. AMI and DBI substituted per the xlsx Fallback Substitutions sheet.
- **Galaxolide 80%** replaces the xlsx's "Galaxolide 50% IPM." Volume adjusted: 5 parts of 80% = 4 parts active = same as 8 parts of 50%.

- **If sillage needs boost:** Increase `Romandolide` by 50–100 µL. It scales linearly without affecting core ratios.
- **Xlsx synergy applied:** Myristic Acid + Amberwood F (DEPOT × TRPV1) creates the "Solid-Lacquer" skin adhesion effect documented in the xlsx Synergy Reference.
