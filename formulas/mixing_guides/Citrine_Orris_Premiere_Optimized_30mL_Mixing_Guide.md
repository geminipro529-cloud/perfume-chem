## Optimization Summary

This final version was optimized from the first `Citrine Orris` concept by:
- tightening the balance between top lift and woody base support
- raising `Hedione` so the iris stayed luminous under the citrus
- reducing some of the heavier wood-amber ballast
- swapping `Benzyl Salicylate` to `Hexyl Salicylate` so the local validator would stop hard-failing the formula

## Why This Version Wins

### 1. The opening is market-facing but still expensive

The top is bright, but not cheap-fresh:
- `Bergamot FCF oil Sicilian`
- `Cedrat FCF oil Sicilian`
- `Petitgrain EO`
- `Linalool`
- `Aldehyde C12 MNA (1%)`
- `Black Pepper FTEC`

This gives:
- immediate lift
- polish
- prestige movement
- enough brightness to welcome people into the line

### 2. The iris still owns the center

The whole point was not to bury the iris under citrus.

That is why:
- `Opus V Iris Crystal` stays high at `2.25 mL`
- `Hedione` lifts the heart instead of obscuring it
- `Jasmine FO` and trace `Rose Oxide` act as framing light, not as separate themes

### 3. The base is modern, dry, and expensive

The drydown frame is:
- `Iso E Super`
- `Cedramber`
- `Clearwood`
- `Habanolide`
- `Galaxolide`
- `Ambrofix`
- `Ambrox Super`
- `Cashmeran`
- `Vetival`

So the perfume lands as:
- luxury
- woody-clean
- diffusive
- contemporary

without going:
- syrupy
- leathery
- smoky
- heavy

### 4. Hexyl Salicylate was the right substitution

The earlier candidate used `Benzyl Salicylate`.

Here it was replaced with `Hexyl Salicylate` because:
- the local validator stops hard-failing `Benzyl Salicylate`
- `Hexyl Salicylate` keeps the floral-cushion function
- it suits the brighter launch direction better anyway

## Mixing Order

1. Add the diffusive woody base:
   `Iso E Super`, `Cedramber`, `Clearwood`, `Habanolide`, `Galaxolide (80%)`, `Ambrofix (30% w/v)`, `Ambrox Super (30% w/v)`, `Cashmeran (20%)`, `Vetival`, `Benzyl Benzoate`.
2. Add the floral cushion:
   `Hexyl Salicylate`, `Hedione`, `Jasmine FO`.
3. Add the premade `Opus V Iris Crystal`.
4. Add the citrus and bright modifiers:
   `Bergamot FCF oil Sicilian`, `Cedrat FCF oil Sicilian`, `Petitgrain EO`, `Linalool`, `Black Pepper FTEC`.
5. Add `Aldehyde C12 MNA (1%)`.
6. Add `Rose Oxide (1%)` last.
7. Stir or shake thoroughly.
8. Rest `7 days` minimum.
9. Best read: `14-21 days`.

## Adjustment Knobs

If it is too citrus-led:
- reduce `Bergamot FCF oil Sicilian` by `80-120 uL`
- raise `Opus V Iris Crystal` by `150 uL`

If it is not airy enough:
- raise `Hedione` by `100-150 uL`
- raise `Linalool` by `20-30 uL`

If it feels too abstract and less iris-centered than you want:
- raise `Opus V Iris Crystal` from `2.25 mL` to `2.40 mL`
- reduce `Iso E Super` by `100-150 uL`

If the drydown feels too clean and not textured enough:
- raise `Cashmeran (20%)` by `30-50 uL`
- raise `Vetival` by `10-20 uL`

If you want a darker prestige flanker later:
- keep the same shell
- replace part of the crystal center with the richer `Signature` branch from [Amouage_Opus_V_Iris_Accord_Current_Inventory.md](d:\chatbots\perfume-chem\Amouage_Opus_V_Iris_Accord_Current_Inventory.md)
