## Mixing Instructions — 50mL Expansion

> **Starting point:** Sealed 30mL batch (mixed April 12, 2026) + 1,000 µL AIMI added = 31,000 µL current.

> **Transfer to 50mL bottle** if the current 30mL vessel is too small. Rinse the original bottle with ethanol to recover all material.

### Step 7 — Re-maceration
Seal and store in dark. **Minimum 21 days from this addition date.** The 2× iris mass, fresh ionones, and new materials need full maceration to integrate. The original 30mL base already has some maturation; the new additions need time to equilibrate. Judge no earlier than day 21.
