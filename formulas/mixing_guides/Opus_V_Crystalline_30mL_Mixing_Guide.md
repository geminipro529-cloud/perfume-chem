## Intent

The xlsx "universal core" is not an iris accord. It is a **complete fragrance base** spanning four functional layers: ionone spine, musk cushion, wood-amber substrate, and epidermal depot. The iris character comes from 6 of the 13 molecules. The other 7 provide performance infrastructure.

v2 mislabeled all 13 as "IRIS CORE" then duplicated the core's musks and woods in the frame — adding IES (warm cedar) and EB (waxy musk) on top of Koavone (warm woody) and Galaxolide (clean musk) that were already in the core. The result: 51% warm/opaque materials, 7% crystalline materials. It would smell like a large powdery violet-iris cloud, not a crystal.

**v3 fixes this:**

1. **Honest labeling** — core split into Ionone Spine / Core Musks / Core Skin Substrate / Depot
2. **Frame deduplication** — removed Iso E Super (warm woody cocoon duplicating Koavone's bridge role) and Ethylene Brassylate (waxy lactonic musk duplicating Galaxolide's OR5A2 foundation)
3. **Crystalline mass boost** — Helional increased from 5→8 parts, Cyclamen Aldehyde from 3→5 parts, Zenolide from 2→3 parts
4. **Result:** Crystalline/transparent materials = 22% of concentrate (was 7%). Zero warm materials in the frame.

### AMI (Amyl Methyl Ionone) — 16 parts → NOT IN INVENTORY

The xlsx Fallback Substitutions sheet specifies:  
> *"AMI → Alpha Isomethyl Ionone × 1.3. Quality Loss: Low. Receptor: OR5A1 maintained. AMI is more tenacious; increase Alpha-ISO and add Koavone ×0.5 extra."*

**Applied:**
- Alpha-ISO addition: 16 × 1.3 = **20.8 parts** (added to core's existing 8 → total **28.8 parts**)
- Koavone addition: 16 × 0.5 = **8 parts** (added to core's existing 6 → total **14 parts**)

**⚠ OR5A1 adaptation note:** The xlsx Anti-Anosmia sheet warns: *"HIGH — rapid adaptation. Limit to ≤8 parts; use AMI+DBI for different conformer."* The original core uses AMI (16) + Alpha-ISO (8) as two distinct OR5A1 conformers. This substitution collapses both into Alpha-ISO at 28.8 parts. Mitigation:
- Dihydro Beta Ionone (10 parts) — secondary ionone conformer with different OR5A1 binding geometry
- Koavone (14 parts) — third OR5A1 geometry via musky-woody facet
- If ionone anosmia is perceived after maceration: add 100 µL **Alpha Ionone** for a fourth conformer

### DBI (Delta Beta Isomethyl Ionone) — 10 parts → NOT IN INVENTORY

No direct xlsx fallback provided. **Dihydro Beta Ionone** is the closest ionone-family material — powdery iris character, OR5A1 activation. Used at 1:1 (10 parts). May read slightly more "powdery-violet" and less "orris-dry" than original DBI — a small character shift within the same register.

### Galaxolide 50% IPM → Galaxolide 80%

xlsx specifies 50% IPM at 8 parts → 4 parts active. Inventory has 80%: 4/0.80 = **5 equivalent parts**.

## Crystalline Frame — Material Justifications

### 13. Hedione HC (460 µL, 7 parts) — Radiance Amplifier

**Function:** VN1R1 pheromone receptor activation, transparent luminous volume expansion.

The cis-methyl dihydrojasmonate isomer activates VN1R1 — the "people lean closer" receptor. HC has ~30–40% more efficient cis-content than regular Hedione. At 7 parts (matching xlsx Space Iris), it operates at the perception boundary: maximum VN1R1 activation while barely registering as jasmine character.

**Olfactive contribution:** *Cold luminous radiance.* This is NOT warmth — VN1R1 activation is perceived as spatial expansion and attractiveness, not temperature. It makes the iris feel luminous and projecting without adding any warm character. Crystalline-compatible.

---

### 14. Helional (530 µL, 8 parts) — Mineral Metallic Water ⬆ BOOSTED

**Function:** The primary crystalline character material. Creates mineral-water-metallic coldness — the "glass" around the iris.

**Why boosted from 5→8 parts:** At 5 parts (v2), Helional was 320 µL competing against 1850 µL Alpha-ISO. The iris powder overwhelmed the mineral edge. At 8 parts (530 µL), Helional has enough olfactive mass to register as a dominant frame quality rather than a trace modifier. Helional's per-µL impact is HIGH (low ODT, distinctive metallic character), so 530 µL of Helional can recontextualize 1900 µL of softer Alpha-ISO.

**Why Helional and not Scentenal (1%):** Scentenal = marine-metallic ozone → oceanic. Helional = clean mineral water → crystalline.  
**Why not Calone (1%):** Explicitly marine.

**Dose:** xlsx Space Iris uses 5 parts; this formula exceeds it to compensate for the crystalline frame carrying the concept alone (no DHM or other cold helpers from Space Iris).

**⚠ IFRA:** Helional limit = 0.50%. At 530 µL in 30 mL = **1.77%** — exceeds IFRA 3.5×. **For personal use: acceptable. For distribution: reduce to 150 µL (0.50%).**

---

### 15. Cyclamen Aldehyde (330 µL, 5 parts) — Cold Green Metal ⬆ BOOSTED

**Function:** Green-metallic cold edge. Same molecule as xlsx's "Cyclamal" (CAS 103-95-7).

**Why boosted from 3→5 parts:** Together with Helional, this is 50% of the crystalline concept. At only 3 parts (v2), the metallic quality was a whisper behind the powdery ionone mass. At 5 parts, the Helional-Cyclamen Aldehyde dyad totals 13 parts — enough mineral-metallic mass to clearly frame the 61-part ionone spine as "seen through cold glass."

**Complementarity:** Helional = CLEAN metallic (mineral water). Cyclamen Aldehyde = GREEN metallic (cold vegetable metal). Together = full crystalline spectrum. One alone is one-dimensional.

**Dose:** xlsx Space Iris uses Cyclamal at 4 parts. This formula uses 5 parts for the same boost-vs-compensation rationale as Helional.

---

### 16. Hexyl Salicylate (330 µL, 5 parts) — Transparent Fixative Film

**Function:** Invisible green-floral fixation. Creates adhesion without cosmetic weight — glass surface, not velvet curtain.

**Why Hexyl Salicylate and not Benzyl Salicylate:** BenSal creates deep, waxy, cosmetic-warm cushion that opacifies. HexSal creates a thin, green, transparent film. This is the ONLY fixative that actively contributes to transparency.

**Why not Benzyl Benzoate:** Near-odorless. Every frame slot must actively serve the crystalline concept. With only 9 frame materials (reduced from 11), no room for passive fillers.

---

### 17. Javanol (265 µL, 4 parts) — Mineral Dry Sandalwood

**Register:** Dry-intimate. The ONLY wood in the frame.

**Function:** Precise mineral sandalwood. The core has milky-warm Bacdanol (2 parts) for skin adhesion — Javanol provides the OPPOSITE sandalwood axis: cold, dry, precise, mineral. This creates a sandalwood gradient from warm core (Bacdanol) to cold frame (Javanol) that reinforces the crystalline direction.

**xlsx synergy:** *"Ambroxan + Javanol: TRPV1 + OR2AT4 = Baccarat Rouge structural basis."* The core's Ambrox (3 parts) + Javanol (4 parts) activates this dyad.

**Why not Ebanol:** Milky, yielding, opaque. Every Opus V "Dark" variant uses Ebanol. Crystalline is anti-dark.  
**Why not Sandalore:** Too thin. Reads "sporty."  
**Why not more Bacdanol:** Already in core. Boosting violates core ratios.

### 18. Romandolide (330 µL, 5 parts) — Clean Projection Musk

**Musk axis:** PROJECTION.

**Function:** Launches the iris outward. Clean, slightly woody, cold-neutral character. The ONLY macrocyclic musk in the frame, serving as the Galaxolide partner for polycyclic + macrocyclic OR5A2 coverage — replacing EB in that role.

**Why Romandolide replaces EB as the macrolide partner:** The xlsx synergy "Galaxolide + EB = OR5A2 bimodal" requires one polycyclic + one macrocyclic. Romandolide IS macrocyclic, so it fulfills the structural-class pairing. But Romandolide is clean/projecting where EB is waxy/intimate. For crystalline: projection over wax.

**Why not Habanolide:** Warm, intimate, skin-hugging. Restricts sillage to personal bubble. User requires great sillage.

## Mixing Order (per xlsx 12-Step Protocol, adapted)

1. **Dissolve Myristic Acid.** Weigh 0.10g. Add to ~5 mL ethanol, warm gently (40°C), stir until clear. This is your ethanol stock.

2. **Base/depot molecules (LogP > 4):**  
   `Amberwood F` → `Bacdanol`  
   *xlsx: "High LogP = low VP = no evaporative loss during weighing."*

3. **Ionone spine (iris identity):**  
   `Alpha Isomethyl Ionone` → `Dihydro Beta Ionone` → `Koavone` → `Orivone` → `Ultralia` → `Beta Ionone`  
   *xlsx: "Iris accord is the identity backbone."*  
   *Visual check: clear pale yellow.*

4. **Heart molecules (mid-VP):**  
   `Javanol` → `Hexyl Salicylate` → `Hedione HC`  
   *xlsx: "Mid-VP molecules that bridge top and base."*  
   *Smell check: iris + mineral sandalwood + jasmine diffusion.*

5. **Musk stack:**  
   `Romandolide` → `Zenolide` → `Galaxolide 80%` → `Musk Ketone 10%` → `Norlimbanol Dextro`  
   *xlsx: "Cover OR5A2 binding conformers."*  
   *Smell check: clean cool musk cloud.*

6. **TRPV1 somatosensory:**  
   `Ambrox Super 30%`  
   *xlsx: "TRPV1 agonists amplify all preceding notes via somatic warmth."*

7. **Volatile top molecules (add last, seal immediately):**  
   `Helional` → `Cyclamen Aldehyde` → `Alpha Irone 30%`  
   *xlsx: "High VP — add at end to prevent evaporative loss."*

8. **Add remaining ethanol** to reach 30.00 mL.

9. **Close, shake 30s, rest 10 min, reshake.** *xlsx: "Raoult's Law mixing. Ionone molecules need contact time with musk matrix for H-bond bridge formation."*

10. **Macerate dark, room temp.** Minimum 14 days. Optimal 21 days. *xlsx: "Ester formation; oxidative ionone maturation; H-bond network. REQUIRED."*

11. **Optional chill-filter:** 0–4°C, 48h. *xlsx: "Removes wax precipitates. Prevents cloud formation."*

12. **Already diluted** at step 8. Concentration: 24.7%.

## What It Should Actually Smell Like

**Opening (0–30 min):** Cold mineral flash from Helional + Cyclamen Aldehyde. Metallic water with green facets. The iris is immediately visible through the mineral wall but you smell the glass first — like looking at flowers through a window.

**Heart (30 min–4 hr):** Hedione HC expands the iris outward. The ionone spine (Alpha-ISO dominated) fills the radiance bubble with soft powdery violet-iris, but the metallic aldehydes *maintain their frame* at this concentration. The Opus V character is recognizable — powdery, orris-ish, the ionone cascade — but perceived through a mineral-cold filter. Javanol adds dry intimacy at skin level without warmth. Not Dior Homme (no IES velvet, no coumarin blanket). More Cartier L'Envol territory: mineral iris.

**Base (4–20 hr):** Romandolide projects clean musk outward while Zenolide maintains cold echo. Ambrox at sub-ODT provides that "expensive warmth" the xlsx describes. Norlimbanol scaffold keeps the trail legible. The crystal frame gradually recedes as Helional evaporates, leaving the deeper ionone spine + Galaxolide warm musk as the skin scent. The last 4–6 hours will be closer to the warm core character than the cold frame — this is the mineral iris turning back into a skin iris at the end.
