# Porcelaine Poudre — Flanker 30mL Mixing Guide

**Formula Intent:** cosmetic powder-white flanker of Porcelaine  
**Batch Size:** 30.00 mL  
**Concentration:** 24.45% EdP  
**Concentrate:** 7.335 mL  
**Ethanol 96%:** 22.665 mL  
**Design Basis:** Porcelaine flanker built specifically for a powder-white cosmetic effect

---

## Why This Is A Flanker, Not A Bottle Add

I checked the active `post_mix` path and also simulated the obvious powder additions directly on the current Porcelaine bottle:
- `Heliotropin Fleuressence`
- `Alpha Isomethyl Ionone`
- `Methyl Ionone Pure`
- `Irotyl`
- `Musk Ketone (10%)`
- `Exaltolide (10%)`

None of them moved the current bottle far enough. The biggest bottle-add tests only nudged the powdery character from about `1.33` to roughly `1.36-1.38`, and usually at a small score loss.

So the honest answer is:
- **No, I do not think this can be added in right now in a meaningful way**
- **Yes, the next Porcelaine should be treated as a flanker**

---

## Engine Direction

This version was chosen because it gives a real powder-white shift while keeping the perfume recognizably Porcelaine.

### Compared With Porcelaine Blanche

- Total score: `75.9` → `75.1`
- Powdery radar: `1.33` → `1.63`
- Freshness: `3.05` → `2.66`
- Floral: `4.04` → `3.94`
- Sillage: `84.8` → `82.1`
- Balance: `84.2` → `81.1`

That is the right trade:
- a little less glaze and freshness
- more cosmetic powder
- still white, still polished, still clean

---

## What Changed Artistically

To make Porcelaine feel **cosmetic powder white** instead of **glazed white surface**, the formula does four things:

1. **Reduces the sparkling top**
   - less bergamot
   - less linalyl acetate
   - less linalool

2. **Reduces the watery muguet glare**
   - less `Freesia HDI`
   - less `Nympheal`
   - less `Bourgeonal`

3. **Builds an actual powder axis**
   - `Heliotropin Fleuressence`
   - `Alpha Isomethyl Ionone`
   - `Musk Ketone (10%)`

4. **Makes the base more cosmetic-fabric**
   - more `Ethylene Brassylate`
   - same clean musk architecture
   - no darker woods, no resin, no warmth overload

## Accord Architecture

### 1. Powder Glaze
- `Bergamot FCF oil Sicilian`
- `Linalyl Acetate`
- `Linalool`
- `Aldehyde C12 MNA (1%)`
- `Rose Oxide (1%)`

This is still the surface polish, but quieter than Porcelaine Blanche. It should read as white cosmetic finish, not bright gloss.

### 2. Cosmetic Powder Core
- `DBCA`
- `Benzyl Salicylate`
- `Hexyl Salicylate`
- `Heliotropin Fleuressence`
- `Alpha Isomethyl Ionone`
- `Hedione`

This is the actual difference-maker. It turns the formula from glossy beauty-product air into powder-white cosmetic material.

### 3. White Floral Veil
- `Lilyreal ND`
- `Hydroxycitronellal`
- `Freesia HDI`
- `Nympheal`
- `Bourgeonal`

Still fabricated and clean, but softened so it stops pushing the formula too far into watery white floral.

### 4. Powder-Fabric Base
- `Iso E Super`
- `Ambermax (50%)`
- `Habanolide`
- `Galaxolide (80%)`
- `Cashmeran (20%)`
- `Ethylene Brassylate`
- `Musk Ketone (10%)`

This should feel like a luxury powder compact, not like an overt wood or amber base.

## Step-by-Step Mixing Order

### Phase 1: Powder-Fabric Base

Add and stir for `30` seconds:

1. `Habanolide` — `0.3750 mL`
2. `Galaxolide (80%)` — `0.3375 mL`
3. `Ethylene Brassylate` — `0.1800 mL`
4. `Musk Ketone (10%)` — `0.0900 mL`
5. `Ambermax (50%)` — `0.2625 mL`
6. `Cashmeran (20%)` — `0.1275 mL`
7. `Iso E Super` — `0.9000 mL`

This should smell like clean white fabric, skin powder, and soft cosmetic diffusion.

### Phase 2: Cosmetic Powder Core

Add and stir for `30-45` seconds:

8. `Benzyl Salicylate` — `0.9000 mL`
9. `Hexyl Salicylate` — `0.3000 mL`
10. `DBCA` — `0.4125 mL`
11. `Hedione` — `1.0500 mL`
12. `Heliotropin Fleuressence` — `0.1200 mL`
13. `Alpha Isomethyl Ionone` — `0.1500 mL`

This is the heart of the formula. It should smell cosmetic, powdery, and white rather than glossy or watery.

### Phase 3: White Floral Veil

Add and stir for `30` seconds:

14. `Hydroxycitronellal` — `0.4500 mL`
15. `Lilyreal ND` — `0.4500 mL`
16. `Freesia HDI` — `0.1800 mL`
17. `Nympheal` — `0.1200 mL`
18. `Bourgeonal` — `0.1200 mL`

This should whiten the formula without reintroducing too much watery muguet brightness.

### Phase 4: Surface Polish

Add and stir gently for `20` seconds:

19. `Bergamot FCF oil Sicilian` — `0.1800 mL`
20. `Linalyl Acetate` — `0.3300 mL`
21. `Linalool` — `0.1500 mL`
22. `Aldehyde C12 MNA (1%)` — `0.0900 mL`
23. `Rose Oxide (1%)` — `0.0600 mL`

This should feel like a pale lacquer, not like a fresh citrus opening.

### Phase 5: Final Dilution

24. Add `Ethanol 96%` — `22.6650 mL`
25. Stir or shake thoroughly for `60` seconds
26. Bottle in amber glass

## Maturation Curve

### Day 0
- detached
- sharp
- powder core not yet connected to the florals

### Day 3
- cosmetic body appears
- heliotropin and ionone begin to sit inside the salicylate structure

### 1 Week
- first real evaluation point
- the formula should feel more powder-white than Porcelaine Blanche
- less gloss, more compact-powder feel

### 2 Weeks
- best first wearing window
- the powder and white floral veil should feel joined instead of layered

### 1 Month
- fully integrated
- the intended effect is white cosmetic powder on clean skin

## Expected Character

This version should smell:
- whiter
- drier
- more cosmetic
- more powder-compact
- less glossy
- less watery
- less beauty-counter lacquer

It should **not** smell:
- iris-heavy
- vintage violet perfume
- almond-gourmand
- dark
- woody

It is still Porcelaine, but as a powder-white flanker instead of the glaze-white original.
