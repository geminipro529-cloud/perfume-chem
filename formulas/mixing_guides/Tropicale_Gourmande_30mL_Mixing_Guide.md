# Tropicale Gourmande — Pineapple · Coconut · Vanilla · 30 mL Mixing Guide
## 16 Materials · 17.5% EdP · Pipeline-Verified OAVs

> **Pre-requisites:** Aldehyde C10 1% in EtOH, Maple Lactone 20% in DPG, Vanillin 10% in EtOH, Coumarin 20% in EtOH, Ambrettolide 10% in DPG

## STEP 1 — BASE: Vanilla Gourmand Bed (add FIRST into empty beaker)
### Sorted by µL (largest → smallest)

| # | Material | µL | mL | Notes |
|---|----------|----|----|-------|
| 1 | Vanillin 10% | 1,200 | 1.20 | Vanilla body mass. 10% dilution = 120 µL active. OAV 10 — structural body, not the primary vanilla driver. |
| 2 | Ethyl Vanillin | 500 | 0.50 | **PRIMARY vanilla.** Neat. Higher VP than Vanillin — OAV 45. The vanilla you actually smell. |
| 3 | Iso E Super | 450 | 0.45 | Transparent cedar volume. Structural anchor for the drydown. |
| 4 | Ethylene Brassylate | 400 | 0.40 | Creamy depth-axis musk. OAV 7 — soft, lactonic, pairs with coconut. |
| 5 | Coumarin 20% | 350 | 0.35 | Tonka-hay bridge — 20% dilution = 70 µL active. OAV 58. Holds the vanilla-coconut transition. |
| 6 | Benzyl Salicylate | 300 | 0.30 | Salicylate cushion. Low headspace OAV (0.2) — functions as a diffusion bed at skin level. |
| 7 | Ambrettolide 10% DPG | 250 | 0.25 | Natural skin warmth — 10% dilution = 25 µL active. OAV 5. Shake stock before use. |

**Σ Base = 3,450 µL** — add in this order (largest first). Swirl 10 seconds after every 3 additions. Expect slight cloudiness from DPG in Ambrettolide.

**CHECK after base:** Smell the beaker. Vanilla-almond sweetness (Ethyl Vanillin + Coumarin), creamy musk (Ethylene Brassylate), transparent-woody volume (Iso E Super). Sweet but not cloying. The coconut lactones aren't in yet — this should smell like a vanilla-tonka skin scent right now.

### Sorted by µL (largest → smallest)

| # | Material | µL | mL | Notes |

| 8 | Hedione | 500 | 0.50 | Jasmine-radiance amplifier. OAV 13,222 — the biggest single OAV in the formula. Transparent — adds lift without adding jasmine character. |
| 9 | Gamma Decalactone | 300 | 0.30 | **COCONUT STAR.** Peach-lactone with creamy coconut character. OAV 551 — the primary coconut voice. |
| 10 | Delta Decalactone | 200 | 0.20 | Creamy coconut depth. Low VP → low headspace OAV (0.2) — functions on skin, not in air. The "coconut cream" texture. |
| 11 | Maple Lactone 20% | 200 | 0.20 | Maple-caramel cream — 20% dilution = 40 µL active. OAV 269. Bridges coconut to vanilla with a warm, gourmand bridge. |
| 12 | Gamma Undecalactone | 180 | 0.18 | Peach-coconut bridge. OAV 1.2 — trace-level connector between Gamma Decalactone and the vanilla base. |

**Σ Heart = 1,380 µL** — swirl 15 seconds. Wait 30 seconds before smelling.

**CHECK after heart:** Now the coconut is present. Gamma Decalactone (peach-coconut creamy) leads. Maple Lactone adds caramel warmth. Hedione makes everything bloom. Smells like coconut cream stirred into vanilla pudding — not sunscreen, not artificial. The Delta Deca is felt as texture, not smelled directly.

| 16 | Ethyl 2-Methylbutyrate | 15 | 0.015 | **PINEAPPLE.** Ultra-potent fruity ester. OAV 5,394 at just 15 µL. Do not exceed — this molecule overwhelms everything. |

**Σ Top = 415 µL — cap immediately.** Work ≤60 seconds. The top is dangerously volatile.

**CHECK after top:** Smell immediately. Pineapple bursts first (Ethyl 2-MB), then green-tropical body (Allyl Amyl Glycolate), then bergamot citrus, all lifted by aldehydic sparkle. Behind it: coconut cream + vanilla. This is a pineapple-coconut-vanilla smoothie, not a synthetic fruit candy. The aldehydes will flash off within 3–5 minutes, revealing the coconut heart.

## STEP 4 — Add Ethanol + Antioxidant

1. Add **6 mg BHT** (or 1 drop tocopherol) directly into the beaker. Swirl until dissolved.
2. Add **24,785 µL ethanol 96%**.
3. Cap tightly. Invert gently 5 times.

## Why This Works

1. **Ethyl 2-Methylbutyrate at 15 µL (OAV 5,394)** — the pineapple molecule. Ultra-low dose (0.05% of concentrate) delivers massive opening impact. Do not overdose — this is a 1-drop-wonder.
2. **Gamma Decalactone at 300 µL (OAV 551)** — the coconut star. Peach-lactone with creamy coconut character. This is the single most important material for the "coconut" register.
3. **Maple Lactone at 200 µL 20% (OAV 269)** — caramel-cream bridge. Connects coconut to vanilla without reading as maple syrup. At 20% dilution, it's creamy-sweet, not brown-sugar.
4. **Ethyl Vanillin at 500 µL (OAV 45)** — primary vanilla driver. Higher VP than Vanillin → actually perceptible in headspace. Vanillin 10% at 1,200 µL is body mass (OAV 10) — structural, not aromatic.
5. **Coumarin at 350 µL 20% (OAV 58)** — tonka bridge. Holds the coconut → vanilla → drydown transition. Without it, coconut and vanilla read as separate layers.
6. **Ethylene Brassylate at 400 µL (OAV 7)** — creamy-lactonic musk. Pairs with coconut lactones — same texture, same softness. Ambrettolide at 250 µL 10% (OAV 5) extends the intimate skin trail past 6 hours.
7. **Bergamot FCF Sicilian at 200 µL (OAV 2,024)** — citrus top lift. Makes the opening bright and fresh rather than heavy-fruity. Pineapple + bergamot = tropical, not candy.
8. **Hedione at 500 µL (OAV 13,222)** — transparent radiance. Makes everything bloom without adding jasmine character. The coconut lactones read through it rather than being buried by it.
