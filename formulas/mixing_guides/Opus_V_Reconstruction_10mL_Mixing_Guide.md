## Mixing Order

### Step 1 — Base & Structure (heaviest, least volatile)

Pipette into a clean 15 mL glass vial:

1. `Benzyl Salicylate` — 65 µL
2. `Benzyl Benzoate` — 30 µL
3. `Iso E Super` — 190 µL
4. `Clearwood` — 50 µL
5. `Vertofix` — 60 µL
6. `Bacdanol` — 25 µL
7. `Vetival` — 20 µL
8. `Evernyl` — 25 µL
9. `Ambrox Super (30%)` — 120 µL
10. `Galaxolide (80%)` — 55 µL
11. `Habanolide` — 40 µL
12. `Ethylene Brassylate` — 35 µL
13. `Romandolide` — 25 µL
14. `Cashmeran (20%)` — 15 µL
15. `Coumarin (20%)` — 25 µL

*Swirl gently. Let sit 2 minutes.*

16. `Orivone` — 125 µL
17. `Methyl Ionone Pure` — 110 µL
18. `Alpha Ionone` — 65 µL
19. `Beta Ionone` — 50 µL

*Swirl gently. The iris core is now set.*

### Step 3 — Dark Accord (oud, leather, resin)

36. `Suederal (10%)` — 20 µL
37. `Guaiacol` — 12 µL ← **tiny dose, use micropipette**

### Step 4 — Floral Frame

38. `Hedione` — 180 µL
39. `Hydroxycitronellal` — 30 µL
40. `Jasmine FO` — 35 µL
41. `Phenethyl Alcohol` — 25 µL

### Step 6 — Dilute

*Cap tightly. Invert 10× to mix. Do NOT shake.*
