# Temple Root Original — 30mL Mixing Guide

**Date:** 2026-04-04  
**Series Role:** founding original built around your `Opus V Iris Accord` module  
**Target Style:** dark rooty iris with incense, mineral woods, and quiet luxury diffusion  
**Concentration:** `25.0%` in ethanol  
**Batch Size:** `30.00 mL`

This is the best first original to build from your accord because it does three things at once:
- uses the accord where it is strongest: dark, waxy, buttery, metallic, rooty iris
- does **not** lean too hard into direct Opus V mimicry like rhum, rose, or leather-first builds
- gives you the cleanest sequel path later: you can branch into `Rhum`, `Rose`, `Suede`, `Bright Citrus`, or `Amber` flankers from this base

If you want one perfume to establish the line, this is the right move:
- serious
- original enough
- wearable enough
- unmistakably iris-led

---

## Concept

`Temple Root` is not a clone and not a Dior-style iris.

It is built around:
- cold waxy orris concrete
- root-earth darkness
- incense air
- dry polished woods
- soft skin diffusion under the structure

The result should feel like:
`orris concrete in a stone room with incense smoke in the air and dry wood built underneath`

### Accord Mixing Order

1. Add the structural cushion:
   `Bacdanol`, `Coumarin (20%)`, `Ambrox Super (30% w/v)`, `Vetival`, `Heliotropin Fleuressence`.
2. Add the heavy iris body:
   `Orivone`, `Methyl Ionone Pure`, `Orris F-TEC`, `I-IRIS F-TEC`, `Allyl Ionone (Ketone V)`, `Dihydro Beta Ionone`, `Irotyl`.
3. Add the irone-violet section:
   `Alpha Ionone`, `Beta Ionone`, `Alpha Irone (10%)`.
4. Add `Hedione`.
5. Add `Carrot Seed EO`.
6. Add `Ultralia`.
7. Add `Rose Oxide (1%)` last.
8. Shake or stir thoroughly.
9. Rest `7 days` minimum.
10. Best read: `14-21 days`.

## Why This Is The Best First Original

### 1. It makes the iris the identity, not the reference

The accord is already doing the noble work:
- irone-led butter
- metallic concrete chill
- waxy root body
- dry shadow underneath

So the outer perfume should not compete with that by becoming:
- a loud rum scent
- a rose perfume
- a suede perfume
- an amber bomb

This formula instead frames the accord.

### 2. Incense and root are the cleanest original direction

`Olibanum Resinoid`, `Myrrh EO`, `Patchouli EO`, `Vetiver EO`, `Cedramber`, and `Clearwood` all reinforce the accord's best qualities:
- dryness
- depth
- mineral austerity
- earthy luxury

That is more original than simply copying Opus V's declared note story more literally.

### 3. It gives you the best sequel path

If this becomes your first release, you can later build:
- a `Rhum` flanker by pushing `Maple Lactone`, `Benzoin`, and `Labdanum`
- a `Rose` flanker by adding `PEA`, `Citronellol`, and a little more `Hedione`
- a `Suede` flanker by adding `Suederal`
- a `Bright` flanker by pushing `Bergamot`, `Cedrat`, and diffusion
- an `Amber` flanker by increasing resin and amber structure

That makes this the strongest foundation perfume, not just the strongest single formula.

## Expected Wearing Arc

- `0-20 min`: cold incense air, tiny bergamot lift, black-pepper edge, metallic iris opening
- `20-120 min`: waxy orris concrete, rooty darkness, frankincense and dry woods fully merge
- `2+ hours`: polished mineral woods, soft musk cushion, lingering iris dust and resin shadow

It should read as:
`dark luxury iris, built from roots and incense rather than lipstick and sweetness`

## Mixing Order

### Phase 1: Dry Wood Structure

Add and stir:
1. `Iso E Super` — `1.10 mL`
2. `Cedramber` — `0.54 mL`
3. `Clearwood` — `0.42 mL`
4. `Ambrofix (30% w/v)` — `0.18 mL`
5. `Vetival` — `0.10 mL`
6. `Bacdanol` — `0.15 mL`

This should smell dry, polished, slightly mineral, and expensive.

### Phase 2: Resin-Root Frame

Add and stir:
7. `Olibanum Resinoid` — `0.45 mL`
8. `Myrrh EO` — `0.24 mL`
9. `Labdanum Absolute (10%)` — `0.24 mL`
10. `Patchouli EO` — `0.18 mL`
11. `Vetiver EO` — `0.18 mL`

This is where the perfume stops being generic woody and becomes temple-like.

### Phase 3: Diffusion Cushion

Add and stir:
12. `Benzyl Salicylate` — `0.33 mL`
13. `Habanolide` — `0.27 mL`
14. `Galaxolide (80%)` — `0.27 mL`
15. `Hedione` — `0.18 mL`
16. `Heliotropin Fleuressence` — `0.06 mL`
17. `Coumarin (20%)` — `0.09 mL`

This phase keeps the perfume refined instead of harsh or skeletal.

### Phase 4: Iris Core

Add and stir thoroughly:
18. `Opus V Iris Accord` — `2.40 mL`

Once this goes in, the whole build should pivot from dry incense woods into a true iris perfume.

### Phase 5: Top Polish

Add last before dilution:
19. `Bergamot FCF oil Sicilian` — `0.09 mL`
20. `Black Pepper FTEC` — `0.03 mL`

These are there only to sharpen the opening edge, not to create a classical citrus top.

### Phase 6: Final Dilution

21. Add `Ethanol 96%` — `22.50 mL`
22. Stir or shake thoroughly for `60-90 seconds`
23. Bottle in glass

## Maturation Curve

### Day 0
- sharp
- resin sections may sit apart
- iris may feel more metallic than buttery

### Day 3-7
- incense and woods start integrating
- iris becomes fuller and less segmented

### Day 14
- first real judgment point
- the perfume should begin reading as one shape rather than separate layers

### Day 21-28
- ideal reading
- dry woods, resins, and iris should feel fused

## If You Want To Tune It After First Wear

If it is too dry or severe:
- reduce `Vetiver EO` by `30-60 µL`
- reduce `Cedramber` by `60-90 µL`
- raise `Benzyl Salicylate` by `60 µL`

If it is not incense-heavy enough:
- raise `Olibanum Resinoid` by `60-90 µL`
- raise `Myrrh EO` by `30 µL`

If it is not rooty enough:
- raise `Opus V Iris Accord` by `150-300 µL`
- reduce `Iso E Super` by the same amount

If it feels too close to Opus V:
- reduce `Labdanum Absolute (10%)` by `60 µL`
- reduce `Myrrh EO` by `30 µL`
- raise `Clearwood` or `Cedramber` instead

If you want a slightly warmer version:
- add `60-90 µL` `Benzoin Resinoid (50% in DPG)`

## Bottom Line

If you are about to launch a series from this accord, make **this** first:
- not the rum version
- not the leather version
- not the rose version

Make the **incense-root original** first.

It gives the accord the most defensible artistic shell, and it gives your later perfumes the clearest room to expand.
