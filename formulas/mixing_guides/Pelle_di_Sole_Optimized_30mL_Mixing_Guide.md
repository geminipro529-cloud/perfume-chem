## *Blood Orange Sandalwood Skin Scent*

> "Skin of sun" — as if your skin naturally smelled of sun-warmed blood orange and creamy sandalwood. Whisper-close, intimate, nearly invisible. Now with Paradisamide locking the blood orange signal into the late drydown, Gamma Decalactone bridging fruit into sandalwood with a lactonic peach-skin, Sandalwood FO adding a natural-illusion opening, and Zenolide as a fifth musk voice for the skin-scent cocoon.

**Concentration:** 18.6% EdP  
**Reference:** Glossier You × Le Labo Santal 33, sun-kissed  
**Wear:** Unisex / Summer / Intimate / "What are you wearing?"

### Changes from Original BO-10

| Change | Why |
|--------|-----|
| +Paradisamide (30 µL) | Blood orange fades in 30 min. Paradisamide's cassis-tropical signal (150-hr tenacity) carries the fruity identity into Act III. Without it, this becomes "generic sandalwood musk" by hour 2 |
| +Gamma Decalactone (30 µL) | Lactonic peach-skin bridge between blood orange (fruit) and sandalwood (cream). They share warm-golden character; the lactone is the molecular handshake |
| +Sandalwood FO (30 µL) | Natural-illusion topnote for the synthetic sandalwood quad. Adds the first 15 minutes of "real sandalwood" before the synthetics develop |
| +Zenolide (40 µL) | Fifth musk voice: clean-powdery-floral. Galaxolide (white) + Habanolide (metallic) + Ambrettolide (warm) were 3 voices. Zenolide adds the "clean laundry" skin-scent dimension |
| Coumarin 20 → 30 µL | 20 µL of 20% = 4 µL active was subliminal. At 30 µL (6 µL active), the powder-warmth bridge becomes discernible — just enough to round the sandalwood |
| Iso E Super 150 → 160 µL | Slight increase for better molecular cocoon support in the drydown |
| Hexyl Sal 80 → 60 µL | Slight reduction — the double salicylate was slightly heavy for an 18% skin scent. Still present as transparent green-floral fixative alongside Benzyl Sal |

### Step-by-Step Mixing Instructions

#### Equipment
- 20–200 µL variable micropipette + tips (one per material)
- 1000 µL (1 mL) micropipette for volumes > 200 µL (or measure in two pulls)
- 30 mL amber glass bottle with cap
- Small glass beaker for pre-mixing (optional but helpful)
- Label + pen
- Paper towels for spill management

#### Important Notes
- All volumes doable with a 200 µL pipette (max single pull = 200 µL; larger volumes = multiple pulls)
- Pull Galaxolide slowly — it's viscous at 80%
- Cashmeran 20% pours easily
- Work in a ventilated area — you're handling ~5.6 mL of concentrate

# PART 3 — Ratings & Descriptions

## Rating Criteria

| Metric | What It Measures |
|--------|-----------------|
| **Originality** | How novel is the concept? Is this just another fresh-blue or does it do something genuinely different? |
| **Wearability** | How practical for daily/regular wear? Compliment-getters score high; challenging art-scents score lower |
| **Longevity** | Material-stack analysis: what % of the concentrate is tenacious base materials? How long before the concept fades? |
| **Sillage** | Projection potential based on molecular volatility, salicylate/Hedione content, and concentration |
| **Cohesion** | Do the materials work together as an accord, or is this an ingredient list? |

## BO-10 — Pelle di Sole v2 (Optimized)

> **"Your skin, but sun-warmed."** Creamy five-sandalwood accord glowing with blood orange warmth, anchored by Paradisamide for all-day fruit echo. Whisper-close intimacy, designed to be discovered, not announced.

| Metric | Score | Notes |
|--------|-------|-------|
| Originality | 7/10 | Sandalwood skin scent is a known archetype, but five-sandalwood + blood orange + Paradisamide fruit anchor is distinctive engineering |
| Wearability | 9/10 | Universally flattering. Office, date, summer heat, winter layers — works everywhere |
| Longevity | 7/10 | 18% with strong sandalwood + musk base = 6–8 hr total. Berry/citrus character lasts 3–4 hr (Paradisamide), intimate skin musk persists longer |
| Sillage | 5/10 | By design. This is a "lean-in" scent, not a room-filler. Sillage at arm's length for first hour, then conversation distance only |
| Cohesion | 9/10 | Blood orange → lactone bridge → creamy sandalwood → clean musk = a single seamless arc. Every material serves one story |
| **Overall** | **8/10** | The best "everyday signature" in the collection. Low projection is intentional, not a weakness |
