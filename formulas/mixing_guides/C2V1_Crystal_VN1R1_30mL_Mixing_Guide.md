## Mixing Instructions

### Step 1 — Weigh Myristic Acid
Weigh 0.12g Myristic Acid powder into a clean 50mL beaker. Set aside.

### Step 2 — Build the Iris DNA Core
Pipette ingredients 1–7 into a separate vial (3,000 µL total). Swirl gently. This is the iris concentrate.

### Step 3 — Add Orris Signature
Add Alpha Irone 30% (200 µL), Suederal 10% (200 µL), Carrot Seed EO (65 µL).

### Step 4 — Add Crystal Frame
Add Helional (400 µL) and Cyclamen Aldehyde (335 µL).

### Step 5 — Add VN1R1 Luxury Radiance
Add Hedione (400 µL), Hedione HC (135 µL), Ambrox Super 30% (265 µL).

### Step 6 — Add Perception Luxury
Add Javanol (135 µL), Cashmeran 20% (135 µL), Hexyl Salicylate (135 µL).

### Step 7 — Add Standard Base
Add Galaxolide 80% (335 µL) and Habanolide (265 µL).

### Step 7.5 — Add Synergy Resolution
Add in order:
1. Romandolide (180 µL) — clean projection musk; fills the zero-projection-macrolide gap
2. Iso E Super (150 µL) — molecular skin-cocoon via OR5A1; restores IES × Ambrox depth synergy and activates 6 additional dormant synergy pairs
3. Musk Ketone powder (0.02g) — weigh and set aside; will dissolve with Myristic Acid in Step 8

### Step 8 — Combine with Ethanol
Drop ~3mg BHT (one tiny grain) into the beaker. Pour the Myristic Acid and Musk Ketone powder into the beaker. Add ~5 mL warm (40°C) ethanol, stir until all solids dissolve completely. Pour in the 6,335 µL liquid concentrate. Rinse all vials with remaining ethanol. Top up to exactly 30.00 mL with ethanol (23,665 µL).

> **Why BHT:** Helional, Cyclamen Aldehyde, and the 5 ionones are oxidation-vulnerable. Over 3+ weeks of maceration, oxidation dulls aldehydes and browns ionones. BHT at ~0.01% (3mg/30mL) is standard practice — scentless, prevents degradation.

### Step 9 — Maceration
Seal and store in dark place. **Minimum 3 weeks.** Alpha Irone + ionones transform dramatically — the metallic edge softens, the orris butter emerges. The Myristic Acid depot needs time to equilibrate with skin-binding ionones. Judge no earlier than day 21.
