## Step-by-Step Mixing Instructions

### Phase 1: Prepare Workspace (5 min)

1. **Clean workspace** — Wipe down with ethanol-dampened paper towel
2. **Arrange materials** — Lay out all 16 aroma chemicals + ethanol in order of addition
3. **Label your bottle** — "Porcelaine (Optimized) / 30mL / 25% / [Date]"
- **Put on gloves** — Some materials (Benzyl Salicylate) are thick/sticky

### Phase 2: Build Top Notes (3 min)

**Target:** Laundered citrus lift (0.99 mL total)

5. **Add Bergamot FCF Sicilian** — Pipette 0.30 mL (300 µL) into beaker  
   *Olfactive check: Bergamot-citrus freshness, slightly herbal*

6. **Add Linalyl Acetate** — Pipette 0.45 mL (450 µL) into beaker  
   *Olfactive check: Lavender-acetate lift, "clean laundry" character emerges*

7. **Add Linalool** — Pipette 0.24 mL (240 µL) into beaker  
   *Olfactive check: Floral-woody transparency, rounds the lavender*

8. **Stir gently** — 10 seconds with stirring rod  
   **Check:** Top accord should smell like expensive fabric softener — citrus-lavender cleanness

---

### Phase 3: Build Floral Heart (8 min)

**Target:** Cosmetic plastic floral core (4.11 mL total)

9. **Add DBCA** — Pipette 0.45 mL (450 µL) into beaker  
   *Material note: DBCA is THE cosmetic-plastic character creator (gardenia-rose)*

10. **Add Hedione** — Pipette 1.20 mL into beaker **[OPTIMIZED DOSE]**  
    *Material note: This is the radiance amplifier — heavy dose creates volume*

11. **Add Benzyl Salicylate** — Pipette 0.75 mL (750 µL) into beaker  
    *Material note: Diffusion cushion + fixative, slightly viscous*

12. **Stir for 20 seconds** — These 3 materials are the cosmetic-plastic foundation  
    **Check:** Should already smell like "luxury beauty products" — abstract floral salicylate

13. **Add Freesia HDI** — Pipette 0.36 mL (360 µL)  
    *Adds green-floral transparency*

14. **Add Lilyreal ND** — Pipette 0.46 mL (460 µL)  
    *Synthetic muguet, clean cosmetic character — boosted to fill muguet body*

15. **Add Nympheal** — Pipette 0.20 mL (200 µL)

    **Check:** Base is clean, abstract, skin-like — not detectably "woody" or "ambery"

### Phase 5: Dilute with Ethanol (2 min)

25. **Measure concentrate volume** — Should be 7.20 mL in beaker (24% of final)

26. **Add Ethanol 96%** — Pour 22.80 mL ethanol into beaker  
    *Use the 30mL bottle to pre-measure ethanol if your beaker lacks markings*

27. **Stir thoroughly** — 60 seconds, making sure no undissolved oils remain at bottom

28. **Check clarity** — Liquid should be crystal clear with no cloudiness (no water contamination)

### Phase 6: Bottle & Mature (2 min)

29. **Transfer to bottle** — Use funnel, pour concentrate + ethanol mixture into 30mL amber glass bottle

31. **Shake vigorously** — 30 seconds to fully homogenize

32. **Label details** — Write: "Porcelaine (Opt.) / 25% / 30mL / [Date] / +Hedione"

## Technical Notes

### Concentration Choice (25% EdP)
- **24% → 25%** after Hedione optimization (still EdP range: 15-40%)
- Higher concentration would make the salicylates and musks overwhelming
- Lower concentration would lose the "luxury cosmetic" richness

### Hedione Chemistry
- **Original dose:** 0.90 mL (12.0% of 7.50 mL concentrate)
- **Optimized dose:** 1.20 mL (16.0% of concentrate)
- **Why this works:** Hedione (methyl dihydrojasmonate) is a radiance amplifier and volume expander. It synergizes with salicylates to create diffusion, and with florals to create three-dimensional bloom. At 16%, it acts as both a heart-note material AND a structural supporting player. Safe to dose heavily — it's gentle, transparent, non-cloying.

### Material Synergies in This Formula
- **DBCA + Benzyl Salicylate + Hedione** = The cosmetic-plastic floral foundation (gardenia-rose salicylate cushion with radiant volume)
- **Nympheal + Lilyreal + Freesia HDI** = Transparent muguet triad (radiant diffusive muguet bloom without sweetness)
- **Iso E Super + Ambermax + Habanolide** = Abstract skin-effect base (molecular cocoon + ambergris depth + clean musk)
- **Linalyl Acetate + Linalool + Bergamot** = Laundered citrus lift (fabric softener opening)

### Dilution Handling
Three materials are pre-diluted:
- **Ambermax (50%)** — ambergris-type material in DPG, dose at 0.30 mL = 0.15 mL active (replaces Ambrox Super, which is out of stock)
- **Galaxolide (80%)** — Near-neat polycyclic musk, dose at 0.30 mL = 0.24 mL active
- **Cashmeran (20%)** — Moderate dilution, dose at 0.30 mL = 0.06 mL active

All doses account for these dilutions. No further adjustment needed.

## Troubleshooting

### "It smells too soapy/aldehydic"
- **Cause:** Linalyl Acetate or Hydroxycitronellal overdosed, or insufficient maturation
- **Fix:** Let mature 2 weeks. If still too soapy, reduce Linalyl Acetate by 0.10 mL next batch

### "I don't smell the florals, just musk"
- **Cause:** Nose blindness to Hedione or Iso E Super (common — both are partially anosmic)
- **Fix:** Ask someone else to smell it. The musks may be dominating YOUR perception, but others will smell the full floral bouquet

### "It's too weak/close to skin"
- **Cause:** High Iso E Super dose creates intimate skin-scent effect by design
- **Fix:** This is intentional. If you want more projection, increase Benzyl Salicylate by 0.15 mL and Galaxolide by 0.10 mL in next batch

### "The Hedione smells too synthetic/sharp"
- **Cause:** Fresh Hedione can smell slightly metallic-watery before marriage
- **Fix:** Mature for 2 weeks minimum. Hedione + salicylates + time = radiant floral bloom

### "It changed character after a month"
- **Cause:** Normal maturation. Top notes mellow, base integrates more
- **Fix:** Not a problem — enjoy the evolution. Month-old batches are often the best

## Wearing Recommendations

### Best For
- Daily wear in professional/intimate settings (office, dates, close social)
- Spring/summer/early fall (cool-warm weather, 15-25°C)
- When you want to smell "expensive clean" without being loud
- Layering under heavier perfumes (acts as a radiant skin-scent base)

### Application
- **Pulse points:** Wrists, inner elbows, behind ears
- **Textile targets:** Spray lightly on hair, scarf, collar (musks + salicylates anchor to fabric)
- **Dosage:** 2-3 sprays total (this is a close-wearing formula by design)

### Avoid
- Heavy spray (will overwhelm the subtle transparency)
- Hot/humid weather >28°C (may turn soapy-cloying)
- Mixing with heavily fragranced body products (will clash with the "clean" character)
