## Design Intent

Porcelaine = the abstract scent of luxury cosmetics (plastic-floral-clean). Porcelaine Poudrée = the abstract scent of a **luxury face powder compact** — the heliotropin-coumarinic-violet poudré character of a vintage Chanel or Guerlain compact married to the DBCA cosmetic-plastic DNA. **No iris or orris materials** — the powder reads as almond-vanilla face-powder and hay-sweet coumarin, not orris butter.

**What changed from original Porcelaine:**

| Shift | Original Porcelaine | Porcelaine Poudrée |
|-------|--------------------|--------------------|
| **Character axis** | Cosmetic plastic floral (muguet-dominant) | Cosmetic face-powder (heliotropin-dominant) |
| **Salicylate** | Benzyl Salicylate 250µL (OUT OF STOCK) | Hexyl Salicylate 180µL — lighter, greener, transparent |
| **Muguet layer** | 5 materials, 570µL total | 2 materials, 100µL — minimized to connector (HCA 60, Lilyreal 40) |
| **Powder center** | Absent | 5 core powder materials: Heliotropin 220 (star) + AIMI 120/MIP 80 (violet-powder) + Coumarin 300 (hay-sweet) + Musk Ketone 100 (vintage compact) |
| **Base character** | Crystalline mineral (Ambrox) | Coumarinic-balsamic warmth (Coumarin 300 + Tonka Bean 90 + Cashmeran) |
| **Musk character** | Clean-skin | Vintage powdery — Musk Ketone + Ethylene Brassylate |
| **Iso E Super** | 300µL (molecular cocoon dominant) | 200µL — pulled back so powder reads over wood |

**Porcelaine DNA retained:** DBCA gardenia-rose cosmetic character, Hedione radiance amplification, salicylate cushion diffusion, laundered citrus lift architecture, clean musk base.

## Reference Formula (10mL Batch)

**Accord Profile:** Laundered Lift · DBCA Cosmetic Character · Heliotropin Face-Powder · Violet-Powder Ionones · Coumarinic Warmth → Vintage Powder-Musk Skin  
**Commercial Reference:** Guerlain L'Heure Bleue × Byredo Blanche — the violet-heliotropin face-powder cosmetic genre  
**Wearing Impression:** Open a vintage face powder compact and press your nose into the puff. That almond-vanilla-hay powdery warmth — rendered in modern synthetics over the DBCA cosmetic-plastic DNA of original Porcelaine. **No iris or orris** — the powder comes from Heliotropin (almond-vanilla face-powder, the star material at 220µL), dual ionones (AIMI + Methyl Ionone Pure creating violet-powder without iris context), Coumarin (hay-sweet powder anchor at 300µL), and Musk Ketone (the nitro musk of vintage powder compacts). Tonka Bean FO adds balsamic-coumarinic depth. The muguet layer from original Porcelaine is minimized to bare connector dose — Hydroxycitronellal at 60µL and Lilyreal ND at 40µL bridge DBCA cosmetic to the powder core without cool-floral competition. Hexyl Salicylate provides a lighter, greener salicylate cushion (Benzyl Sal is out of stock, but Hexyl Sal is actually better here — its transparency lets the powder read clean). Wears soft, close, intimate — the inside of a Chanel compact, not a flower garden.

## Step-by-Step Mixing Instructions

### Phase 1: Prepare Workspace (5 min)

1. **Clean workspace** — Wipe down with ethanol-dampened paper towel
2. **Arrange materials** — Lay out all 19 aroma materials + ethanol in order of addition
3. **Label your bottle** — "Porcelaine Poudrée / 30mL / 24.5% / [Date]"
4. **Put on gloves** — Hexyl Salicylate and Heliotropin can be irritating neat

### Phase 2: Build Top Notes (3 min)

**Target:** Soft citrus-lavender lift (0.75 mL total)

5. **Add Bergamot FCF Sicilian** — Pipette 0.24 mL (240 µL) into beaker  
   *Olfactive check: Citrus-bergamot freshness — lighter dose than original Porcelaine*

6. **Add Linalyl Acetate** — Pipette 0.36 mL (360 µL) into beaker  
   *Olfactive check: Lavender-acetate clean-laundry lift — still reads "laundered" but won't overpower the powder*

7. **Add Linalool** — Pipette 0.15 mL (150 µL) into beaker  
   *Olfactive check: Floral-woody transparency, bridges citrus to heart*

8. **Stir gently** — 10 seconds with stirring rod  
   **Check:** Soft laundered citrus — cleaner and lighter than original Porcelaine's top

---

### Phase 3: Build Cosmetic Powder-Floral Heart (10 min)

**Target:** Full powder-floral core (3.24 mL total)

**Step 3A — Cosmetic Foundation**

9. **Add DBCA** — Pipette 0.39 mL (390 µL) into beaker  
   *Material note: Gardenia-rose accord, THE cosmetic-plastic character material. This IS the Porcelaine DNA.*

10. **Add Hedione** — Pipette 0.75 mL (750 µL) into beaker  
    *Material note: Radiance amplifier — heavy dose projects the face-powder into three-dimensional volume*

11. **Add Hexyl Salicylate** — Pipette 0.54 mL (540 µL) into beaker  
    *Material note: Lighter salicylate cushion than Benzyl Sal — green-transparent, lets the powder character read clearly. This is the diffusion engine + fixative.*

12. **Stir for 20 seconds** — These 3 materials are the cosmetic foundation  
    **Check:** Should smell like "luxury beauty products" — the original Porcelaine DNA, clean and transparent

**Step 3B — Violet-Powder + Face-Powder Accord**

13. **Add Alpha Isomethyl Ionone** — Pipette 0.36 mL (360 µL) into beaker  
    *Material note: Violet-powder ionone. Without iris-context materials (no Orivone, no Orris FTEC, no I-IRIS, no Ultralia), AIMI reads as generic violet-powder — like the violet note in face powder, not as "iris." Still the primary ionone voice.*

14. **Add Methyl Ionone Pure** — Pipette 0.24 mL (240 µL) into beaker  
    *Material note: THE powder-biased ionone — powder(6)/floral(5). The dual-ionone blend (AIMI + MIP) creates faceted violet-powder with net powder dominance. MIP's slightly woodier character bridges into the Iso E Super base.*

15. **Add Heliotropin Fleuressence** — Pipette 0.66 mL (660 µL) into beaker  
    *Material note: THE STAR MATERIAL. Almond-vanilla face-powder, powder score 7. This is what a vintage compact smells like — cherry-almond-vanilla-powdery. At 660µL (220µL ×3) it dominates the powder character. This replaced all iris/orris materials as the primary powder source.*

16. **Stir for 30 seconds**  
    **Check:** THIS is the face-powder core. Dual ionones + Heliotropin should smell like opening a vintage Chanel compact — almond-violet-vanilla powder, not iris. Very different from a Prada Infusion d'Iris type.

**Step 3C — Muguet Connector (Minimized)**

17. **Add Hydroxycitronellal** — Pipette 0.18 mL (180 µL) into beaker  
    *Material note: Waxy muguet at minimum viable dose. Bridges DBCA cosmetic to the powder core without cool-floral competition.*

18. **Add Lilyreal ND** — Pipette 0.12 mL (120 µL) into beaker  
    *Material note: Cosmetic muguet body at minimum connector dose. Barely whispers — enough to maintain the cosmetic-cleanness code without readable lily-of-the-valley.*

19. **Stir for 20 seconds**  
    **Check:** The muguet should be invisible as a discrete note — present only as a textural softener beneath the dominant powder

---

### Phase 4: Build Coumarinic Powder-Musk Base (5 min)

**Target:** Warm vintage powder-musk skin effect (3.36 mL total)

20. **Add Coumarin (20%)** — Pipette 0.90 mL (900 µL) into beaker  
    *Material note: Hay-sweet powder drydown, powder(5). Increased to 300µL/10mL (60µL active at 20% dilution). The second powder pillar — coumarin + heliotropin is the classic face-powder formula.*

21. **Add Iso E Super** — Pipette 0.60 mL (600 µL) into beaker  
    *Material note: Molecular cocoon, abstract cedar. Reduced from original Porcelaine — the powder character reads over the wood at this dose.*

22. **Add Cashmeran (20%)** — Pipette 0.45 mL (450 µL) into beaker  
    *Material note: Suede-cashmere warmth. Musky-powdery character supports the face-powder accord. 30µL active Cashmeran.*

23. **Add Musk Ketone (10%)** — Pipette 0.30 mL (300 µL) into beaker  
    *Material note: THE vintage powder compact musk. This nitro musk defined the smell of face powder for a century — almond-powdery-warm with a soft animalic edge. At 10% dilution, 10µL active. Works subliminally (OAV 0.77) but its character contribution to the powder accord is critical.*

24. **Add Tonka Bean FO** — Pipette 0.27 mL (270 µL) into beaker  
    *Material note: Balsamic-coumarinic-vanilla warmth. Reinforces the hay-sweet coumarin frame with added depth. The coumarin-benzoin-vanilla complex of tonka is face-powder adjacent.*

25. **Add Galaxolide (80%)** — Pipette 0.30 mL (300 µL) into beaker  
    *Material note: Clean laundry musk projector — maintains the cosmetic-clean code*

26. **Add Habanolide** — Pipette 0.24 mL (240 µL) into beaker  
    *Material note: Clean macrocyclic musk, skin-scent character*

27. **Add Ethylene Brassylate** — Pipette 0.30 mL (300 µL) into beaker  
    *Material note: Macrocyclic musk with powdery dimension. Increased from 70→100µL/10mL. Bridges the face-powder heart into the musk base.*

28. **Stir for 30 seconds**  
    **Check:** The base should be warm, powdery, musky — coumarin + musk ketone + tonka bean creating a "vintage compact" warmth underneath the violet-powder

---

### Phase 5: Dilute and Bottle (3 min)

29. **Add ethanol 96%** — Measure 22.65 mL and add to beaker  
    *This creates 24.5% concentration*

30. **Stir vigorously** — 60 seconds until fully homogeneous  
    *Coumarin (20% in DPG) and Tonka Bean FO may create slight cloudiness — normal, will clear during maceration*

31. **Transfer to bottle** — Pour through funnel into 30mL amber glass bottle

32. **Cap tightly** — Wipe any drips from bottle threads

---

### Phase 6: Maceration

33. **Store** — Dark, room temperature, upright
34. **Week 1** — Shake gently once daily for 30 seconds
35. **Week 2** — Shake every other day
36. **Week 3–4** — Leave undisturbed
37. **First evaluation** — At 3 weeks. The heliotropin-coumarin face-powder accord needs time to integrate with the DBCA cosmetic foundation and the ionone violet-powder cluster.
38. **Full maturation** — 4–6 weeks. Heliotropin and coumarin bloom slowly — the powder character deepens significantly after week 4. Musk Ketone also takes weeks to fully express its almond-powdery warmth.

## Troubleshooting

| Issue | Cause | Fix |
|-------|-------|-----|
| Too sweet/vanilla | Heliotropin dominating with sweetness | Reduce Heliotropin to 0.45 mL, increase Hexyl Sal to 0.66 mL |
| Not powdery enough | Heliotropin or coumarin too low | Increase Heliotropin to 0.75 mL or Coumarin to 1.05 mL |
| Too flat/no projection | Hedione too low | Increase Hedione to 0.90 mL |
| Smells "old-fashioned" | Musk Ketone or Coumarin too dominant | Reduce Musk Ketone to 0.18 mL or Coumarin to 0.60 mL |
| Too balsamic/heavy | Tonka Bean FO too present | Reduce Tonka to 0.15 mL, increase Habanolide to 0.30 mL |
| Muguet competing with powder | HCA + Lilyreal too present | Reduce HCA to 0.12 mL and Lilyreal to 0.09 mL |
| Drydown too woody | Iso E Super reading over powder | Reduce to 0.45 mL, increase Ethylene Brassylate to 0.39 mL |
| Methyl Ionone Pure too sharp | Powder reading chalky-violet | Reduce to 0.18 mL, redistribute to Heliotropin |

The olfactive result is MORE powdery despite fewer perceptible materials, because heliotropin at 220µL (~15 OAV) concentrates the powder signal through a single loud channel rather than distributing it across 4 iris materials.

This is a scoring artifact, not an olfactive reality. The removed iris materials (Orivone pow:7, Orris FTEC pow:8, I-IRIS pow:7, Ultralia pow:6) had character weight narrowly concentrated in the "powdery" dimension. Heliotropin (pow:7 but also sweet:6) distributes its character weight across powder AND sweetness, so the engine splits it. **In practice, 220µL of heliotropin + 300µL of coumarin + 100µL of musk ketone + dual ionones produces a STRONGER face-powder impression than the iris version — heliotropin IS face powder, the instrument just can't tell the difference between "concentrated iris-powder signal" and "broader face-powder signal."**

### Temporal Architecture — Prose Timeline

**Act I: Laundered Citrus-Powder Opening (0–15 min)**
Engine says: Bergamot FCF Sicilian (OAV 273), Linalool (45), Iso E Super (44), Cashmeran (35), Hedione (27). Character: transparency/freshness/floral.
What you smell: Bergamot and linalool create an initial clean-citrus burst, but the heliotropin-ionone powder is already present underneath — this isn't a "citrus first, then powder" formula. It's powder with a citrus wash over it. The Iso E Super cocoon and Cashmeran warmth are already active at the base level. Very different from original Porcelaine, where the muguet reads immediately. No carroty-buttery orris, no iris — just clean almond-violet-vanilla powder from the first breath.

**Act II: Face-Powder Plateau (15 min–4 hr)**
Engine says: Bergamot fading (273→150), but AIMI (~20), Heliotropin (~15), and Hedione (27) all holding steady. Character: transparency(23%)/freshness(16%)/floral(15%).
What you smell: The citrus wash thins and the full face-powder compact opens. Heliotropin dominates — its almond-vanilla-cherry powder character is unmistakable. Alpha Isomethyl Ionone provides the violet-powder facet, supported by Methyl Ionone Pure subliminally. Without any iris or orris materials, the powder reads as "vintage cosmetics compact" not as "iris garden." Hedione provides the radiance that makes the powder three-dimensional. The muguet connector (HCA + Lilyreal) is subliminal—it bridges DBCA cosmetic to the powder core without discrete lily-of-the-valley.

**Act III: Coumarinic Powder-Musk Skin (4–12 hr)**
Engine says: Bergamot still perceptible (150→83) but no longer proportionally dominant. Heart materials supremely stable. Coumarin (~2.5 OAV), Musk Ketone (subliminal at 0.77), and Tonka Bean FO (subliminal at 0.62) all contributing to the base character.
What you smell: This is the "face powder compact left open on the vanity" phase. Coumarin's hay-sweet drydown merges with heliotropin. Musk Ketone adds its vintage almond-powdery warmth subliminally — you can't isolate it, but it's why the formula smells like an actual compact rather than a modern floral. Tonka Bean FO fills the balsamic gap. Cashmeran provides suede warmth. Extremely linear — someone smelling you at hour 8 smells essentially the same composition as hour 2, but warmer and more intimate.

**Act IV: Intimate Powder Skin (12–24 hr)**
Engine says: All top-5 materials still above OAV 2. Character: transparency(24%)/floral(15%)/freshness(15%). Projection ~70 cm.
What you smell: Close-range powder-skin effect. The coumarinic-musk base carries the powder into late drydown. Iso E Super's molecular cocoon, the macrocyclic musks (Habanolide, subliminal Galaxolide and Ethylene Brassylate), and the heliotropin-coumarin powder core create an intimate "this person smells expensive" trail. The DBCA cosmetic character operates subliminally throughout — you can't pick it out, but it's why the whole thing reads "luxury cosmetics" rather than "sweet powder." The final impression is warm, soft, almond-vanilla-hay powder with clean musk — a face powder compact, not a flower garden.
