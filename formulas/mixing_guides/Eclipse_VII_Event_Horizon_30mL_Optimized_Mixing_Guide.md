## Mixing Instructions

### Equipment
- Clean 50mL beaker or amber glass bottle
- 1mL graduated pipette (for most materials)
- 5mL graduated cylinder (for Hedione, Galaxolide, Iso E Super, EB)
- Micropipette or 0.1mL syringe (for IBQ, Norlimbanol)
- 25mL graduated cylinder (for ethanol)

### Step 1 — Build the Molecular-Wood Architecture
Pipette into the beaker:
1. **Iso E Super** — 564 µL (0.56 mL). The molecular foundation. Everything sits on this.
2. **Cedarwood EO** — 161 µL. Natural cedar structure.
3. **Timberol** — 113 µL. Angular dry cedar geometry.
4. **Clearwood** — 81 µL. Modern dark earthy depth.
5. **Patchouli EO** — 64 µL. Earthy-sweet dark foundation.
6. **Cashmeran 20%** — 242 µL. Textile warmth.
7. **Norlimbanol Dextro** — 16 µL. **Caution: ultra-potent.** Measure precisely with micropipette.

> The structural skeleton. Everything else drapes over this framework.

### Step 2 — Add the Amber Triad
8. **Ambrox Super 30%** — 129 µL. Crystalline mineral.
9. **Ambrofix 30%** — 97 µL. Smoother mineral echo.
10. **Amberwood F** — 81 µL. Transparent warmth.

> Three distinct amber registers: crystalline → smooth mineral → transparent warm.

### Step 3 — Add the Sandalwood Chord
11. **Javanol** — 113 µL. Dry mineral precision.
12. **Ebanol** — 97 µL. Creamy yielding silk.

### Step 4 — Add the Leather-Smoke Heart
13. **Evernyl** — 242 µL. Oakmoss-chypre darkness.
14. **Vetiver EO** — 242 µL. Dry earthy smoke.
15. **Suederal 10%** — 322 µL. Suede leather pull.
16. **Guaiacol 10%** — 129 µL. Phenolic campfire.
17. **Eugenol** — 64 µL. Clove-spicy dark heat.
18. **IBQ 10%** — 16 µL. **Caution: most potent material at ODT 0.02 ppm.** Micropipette only.

> Build the dark complex together so the smoke-leather-animalic materials integrate before anything else.

### Step 5 — Add the Musk Void
19. **Galaxolide 80%** — 676 µL (0.68 mL). The sterile synthetic void.
20. **Ethylene Brassylate** — 483 µL (0.48 mL). Creamy lactonic depth.
21. **Habanolide** — 193 µL. Warm skin intimacy.
22. **Exaltolide 10%** — 129 µL. Skin-fatty animalic depth.
23. **Zenolide** — 68 µL. Cold fresh void.

### Step 6 — Add the Graduated Fixatives
24. **Benzyl Benzoate** — 129 µL. Invisible mass.
25. **Benzyl Salicylate** — 169 µL. Cosmetic diffusion cushion.
26. **Hexyl Salicylate** — 129 µL. Transparent skin adhesion.

### Step 7 — Add the Balsamic-Resinous Layer
27. **Benzoin Resinoid 50%** — 64 µL. Sweet-smoke balsamic depth.
28. **Coumarin 20%** — 64 µL. Hay-tonka dark warmth.

### Step 8 — Add the Radiance Amplifier
29. **Hedione** — 725 µL (0.73 mL). This is the single biggest new addition. It pushes the entire leather-smoke complex outward with radiance without introducing non-family freshness. Add it here so it integrates with the heart and base, not the top.

### Step 9 — Add the Spice-Citrus Top Last
30. **Pink Pepper Base** — 97 µL. Aromatic dark peppercorn.
31. **Black Pepper FTEC** — 129 µL. Terpene-metallic heat.
32. **Cedrat FCF Sicilian** — 129 µL. Bitter mineral citron.
33. **Ethyl Safranate** — 48 µL. Saffron metallic crimson.

> Top notes last to preserve volatile character during maceration.

### Step 10 — Combine With Ethanol
Pour concentrate into ethanol (24.00 mL). Rinse beaker with a few mL of ethanol, add back. Swirl gently — do not shake. Top up to exactly 30.00 mL if needed.

### Step 11 — Maceration
Seal tightly. Store cool and dark. **Minimum 3 weeks.** This formula has more stacking depth than the original (75 vs. 57) — the additional layers (Benzoin, Coumarin, Patchouli, Clearwood, sandalwood chord) need time to integrate. The musk void fills out around day 14. The full amber triad becomes coherent around day 21. The sandalwood chord (Javanol + Ebanol) smooths around day 28.

> **Safety note:** Guaiacol, IBQ, and Eugenol are skin sensitizers at neat concentration. At 10% dilution (Guaiacol, IBQ) or trace dose (Eugenol at 64 µL/30mL = 0.21%) in a 20% EDP, all are within IFRA limits. Avoid prolonged neat contact during mixing.

## Adjustment Guide

| If it smells... | Adjust | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Too much Hedione / floral-fresh | Reduce Hedione to 500 µL | 725 is near the family threshold; dial back if radiance overpowers leather |
| Saffron dominates | Reduce Ethyl Safranate to 32 µL | ODT 0.1 ppm — tiny cuts, large perceptual effect |
| Not dark enough | Add 30 µL Birch Tar Rectified or 30 µL Styrax FTEC | Actual tar-smoke or balsamic-leather smoke |
| Too abstract / woody | Reduce Iso E Super to 400 µL | Cut molecular haze to reveal leather-smoke clarity |
| Musks too busy / cluttered | Remove Zenolide (68 µL) | Simplify to 4-musk chord; Zenolide is the most expendable |
| Needs more animalic | Increase IBQ to 32 µL (10%) | 3.2 µL active = OV 16,000. Confrontational. |
| Suede disappears after maceration | Increase Suederal to 480 µL (10%) | Suederal integrates into smoke complex and can vanish |
| Too warm / sweet | Reduce Benzoin to 32 µL, Coumarin to 32 µL | Both add warmth; halving them cools the drydown |
| Needs more mineral bite | Increase Ambrox to 193 µL (30%) | Extra crystalline mineral — watch for dominance |
| Hedione is fine but want more projection | Increase Cedarwood EO to 242 µL | The sillage champion; stacks with Hedione for maximum throw |
