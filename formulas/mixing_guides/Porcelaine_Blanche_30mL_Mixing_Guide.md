## Design Brief

This is not a patch on the older Porcelaine. It is a **current-inventory reconstruction** of the original artistic idea:

- porcelain-white floral abstraction
- luxury cosmetic-plastic finish
- clean skin diffusion
- polished surface sheen
- no obvious bouquet, no obvious wood perfume, no laundry-detergent collapse

The formula keeps the original genre, but the architecture is cleaner:

- **dual salicylate cushion** instead of relying only on Benzyl Salicylate
- **micro-polish built in from the start** instead of forcing a late aldehyde overload
- **more intentional skin lacquer base** using Habanolide, Galaxolide, and Ethylene Brassylate
- **current-inventory structure** with `Ambermax (50%)` in place of unavailable ambergris materials

## Accord Architecture

### 1. Surface Glaze
- `Bergamot FCF oil Sicilian`
- `Linalyl Acetate`
- `Linalool`
- `Aldehyde C12 MNA (1%)`
- `Rose Oxide (1%)`

This is not a top note accord in the classical sense. It is the **surface finish**: clean lacquer, cold floral sheen, expensive cosmetic brightness.

### 2. Cosmetic Plastic Core
- `DBCA`
- `Hedione`
- `Benzyl Salicylate`
- `Hexyl Salicylate`

This is the actual identity. `DBCA` gives the gardenia-rose cosmetic body, `Benzyl Salicylate` gives the beauty-product cushion, `Hexyl Salicylate` makes the salicylate effect more transparent and skin-like, and `Hedione` keeps it floating instead of sitting flat.

### 3. White Muguet Veil
- `Freesia HDI`
- `Lilyreal ND`
- `Nympheal`
- `Bourgeonal`
- `Hydroxycitronellal`

This is the white, fabricated floral veil. It should read as **clean luxury product air**, not lily of the valley soliflore.

### 4. Skin Lacquer Base
- `Iso E Super`
- `Ambermax (50%)`
- `Habanolide`
- `Galaxolide (80%)`
- `Cashmeran (20%)`
- `Ethylene Brassylate`

This base is deliberately quiet. It gives the formula a polished skin aura, not a woody-musky statement drydown.

## Step-by-Step Mixing Order

### Phase 1: Skin Lacquer Base

Add and stir for 30 seconds:

1. `Habanolide` — `0.3750 mL`
2. `Galaxolide (80%)` — `0.3375 mL`
3. `Ethylene Brassylate` — `0.0750 mL`
4. `Ambermax (50%)` — `0.2625 mL`
5. `Cashmeran (20%)` — `0.1275 mL`
6. `Iso E Super` — `0.9000 mL`

This phase should smell like clean skin, soft lacquer, and abstract white warmth.

### Phase 2: Cosmetic Plastic Core

Add and stir for 30-45 seconds:

7. `Benzyl Salicylate` — `0.9000 mL`
8. `Hexyl Salicylate` — `0.3000 mL`
9. `DBCA` — `0.4125 mL`
10. `Hedione` — `1.2000 mL`

At this point, the formula should start reading as **luxury beauty products**, not floral perfume.

### Phase 3: White Muguet Veil

Add and stir for 30 seconds:

11. `Hydroxycitronellal` — `0.4500 mL`
12. `Lilyreal ND` — `0.4500 mL`
13. `Freesia HDI` — `0.3000 mL`
14. `Nympheal` — `0.2250 mL`
15. `Bourgeonal` — `0.2100 mL`

This should whiten the floral body without making it naturalistic.

### Phase 4: Surface Glaze

Add and stir gently for 20 seconds:

16. `Bergamot FCF oil Sicilian` — `0.2250 mL`
17. `Linalyl Acetate` — `0.4125 mL`
18. `Linalool` — `0.1875 mL`
19. `Aldehyde C12 MNA (1%)` — `0.0900 mL`
20. `Rose Oxide (1%)` — `0.0600 mL`

This layer should feel like a polished cool finish, not like a citrus opening or rose note.

### Phase 5: Final Dilution

21. Add `Ethanol 96%` — `22.5000 mL`
22. Stir or shake thoroughly for 60 seconds
23. Bottle in amber glass

## What Makes This More Luxurious

- `Bergamot FCF oil Sicilian` gives a finer surface than a generic bergamot push
- `Hexyl Salicylate` makes the salicylate architecture feel more translucent and modern
- `Rose Oxide (1%)` is used as cold gloss, not as a rose note
- `Ethylene Brassylate` makes the drydown more like expensive fabric and less like plain laundry musk
- `Hedione` stays dominant, but the formula no longer relies on Hedione alone for elegance

---

## What Makes This More Niche

- it smells like a designed object, not a flower bouquet
- the floral effect is fabricated and surface-based
- the accord is restrained and abstract
- the luxury comes from **finish, texture, and aura**, not from loud complexity

This is the correct direction for Porcelaine. It should feel like:

- cool polished ceramic
- expensive skin cream
- white lacquer
- clean floral air suspended over skin

not:

- rose perfume
- detergent musk
- woody amber skin scent
