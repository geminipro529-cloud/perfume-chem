## Step-by-Step Mixing Instructions

### Phase 1: Fixative-Wood Matrix — The Foundation

**Why base-first:** The salicylate cushion + woody fixatives form the structural matrix that everything else sits on. By mixing these first and swirling, you create the bonding surface for the heart and top to adhere to. This gives better integration than top-down addition.

---

**Step 1 — Benzyl Salicylate (240 µL neat)**
> Diffusion cushion. This is the foundation of the entire formula's radiance projection — the molecule that makes everything above it project outward. Add first so every subsequent material bonds into its matrix.

- [ ] Pipette 240 µL into beaker
- [ ] Swirl gently

**Step 2 — Hexyl Salicylate (240 µL neat)**
> Lighter salicylate partner. Green-floral transparent fixative. Together with Benzyl Sal, these two form the double-salicylate diffusion base — Hexyl for transparency, Benzyl for body.

- [ ] Pipette 240 µL into beaker
- [ ] Swirl to combine with Benzyl Sal

**Step 3 — Iso E Super (1170 µL = 1.17 mL neat)**
> The molecular cocoon. Largest single component of the concentrate (15.4%). This is the abstract cedar skeleton — everything else drapes over it. Use 1 mL pipette or scale: 1.17 mL ≈ 1.17 g.

- [ ] Measure 1.17 mL (or weigh 1.17 g)
- [ ] Add to beaker
- [ ] Stir slowly with glass rod — blend into salicylate base

**Step 4 — Vertofix Coeur (480 µL neat)**
> Woody-musky bridge. Acetyl cedrene fixative that anchors the Iso E Super and prevents it from going "ghostly." Creates the cedar-amber warmth in the drydown.

- [ ] Pipette 480 µL (or 2 × 240 µL)
- [ ] Stir into the Iso E Super + salicylate matrix

**Step 5 — Vetival (240 µL neat)**
> Suede-vetiver dryness. Not green vetiver — this is the dry, sueded facet that gives Calcite its leather-mineral drydown. Needs time to bond with Vertofix and Iso E.

- [ ] Pipette 240 µL
- [ ] Stir

**Step 6 — Habanolide (240 µL neat)**
> Clean macrocyclic musk. Transparent, laundry-clean, skin-close. Bonds into the salicylate cushion and creates the "clean skin" baseline. Slow to open — benefits from early addition.

- [ ] Pipette 240 µL
- [ ] Stir

**Step 7a — Ambermax 50% (300 µL)**
> Warm amber body. Composed amber accord at 50% concentration — 300 µL = 150 µL active amber material. Replaces Ambrox Super which is no longer in inventory. Dosed at 300 µL to provide sufficient amber warmth and body behind Amberwood F's mineral architecture. The 50% concentration means each µL carries real amber weight, not DPG carrier.

- [ ] Pipette 300 µL
- [ ] Stir

**Step 7b — Amberwood F (570 µL neat)**
> Mineral-transparent amber architecture — THE amber backbone. Clean, smooth, architectural amber-wood character without sweetness or warmth. At 570 µL this is the dominant amber material, maintaining the mineral transparency that defines Calcite. Amberwood F's architectural quality replaces Ambrox Super's crystalline edges with a broader, smoother mineral platform.

- [ ] Pipette 570 µL (or weigh 0.57 g)
- [ ] Stir

**⏸ PAUSE — Swirl the beaker for 30 seconds.** You now have the complete fixative-wood matrix: 3.49 mL of base material. Active amber: 150 µL (from Ambermax) + 570 µL Amberwood F neat = 870 µL total amber volume. Amberwood F dominates with mineral-transparent architecture; Ambermax provides the warm amber body. Should smell woody-mineral-clean with smooth amber warmth behind the architectural transparency.

### Phase 2: Blossom Heart — The Transparent Floral Layer

**Why this order:** Hedione goes in first within the heart because it's the radiance amplifier — it needs to bond into the woody-salicylate matrix before the character florals (Nympheal, DBCA) are added. Then the muguet materials layer on. Cyclamen Aldehyde goes last in this phase because it's a bridging material between heart and top.

---

**Step 8 — Hedione (900 µL neat)**
> Radiance amplifier. Methyl dihydrojasmonate — the molecule that makes everything around it project as a luminous three-dimensional cloud. Adding it directly onto the salicylate-wood matrix creates the Hedione × Benzyl Sal synergy (Narciso Rodriguez For Her DNA). Boosted from 660 µL to fill volume freed by Paradisamide removal.

- [ ] Pipette 900 µL (or weigh 0.90 g)
- [ ] Stir slowly — let it integrate into the base

**Step 9 — Nympheal (300 µL neat)**
> Radiant diffusive muguet. The most projecting muguet material available — creates the transparent white-blossom bloom that defines Calcite's floral heart. Luminous and airy without sweetness or tropical fruit character.

- [ ] Pipette 300 µL (or weigh 0.30 g)
- [ ] Stir

**Step 10 — DBCA (300 µL neat)**
> Gardenia-rose accord. Cosmetic-clean white floral character material. Not generic rose — DBCA is the specific gardenia-cyclamen-rose molecule that creates the "luxury cosmetic" read.

- [ ] Pipette 300 µL
- [ ] Stir

**Step 11 — Freesia HDI (360 µL neat)**
> Green-floral transparency. Reinforces Nympheal's muguet effect with a greener, more botanical dimension. Together they create the "white blossom" accord. Boosted +60 µL for fuller green-floral register.

- [ ] Pipette 360 µL
- [ ] Stir

**Step 12 — Lilyreal ND (240 µL neat)**
> Clean synthetic muguet. Cosmetic muguet character — pairs with Freesia HDI and Nympheal for the three-voice muguet chorus.

- [ ] Pipette 240 µL
- [ ] Stir

**Step 13 — Ultralia (120 µL neat)**
> Ghost iris. Ultra-diffusive, subliminal violet-powdery iris effect at trace level. You won't smell it directly — it creates a cool iris shimmer behind the muguet that your brain registers as "mineral coolness."

- [ ] Pipette 120 µL
- [ ] Stir gently

**Step 14 — Cyclamen Aldehyde (150 µL neat)**
> Metallic-green radiance bridge. THIS is the DHM replacement — provides the metallic sparkle without sport-fresh character. Goes last in the heart phase because it bridges upward into the citrus top. Its green-metallic character links the muguet florals to the incoming citrus.

- [ ] Pipette 150 µL
- [ ] Stir — notice the metallic shimmer appearing over the floral base

**⏸ PAUSE — Swirl for 30 seconds.** Heart + base now combined: 6.86 mL. Should smell like a mineral-woody-floral: abstract cedar through transparent white blossom with metallic edges. The citrus will complete the picture.

### Phase 3: Citrus Top — The Volatile Opening

**Why this order:** Linalyl Acetate first (it's a semi-volatile ester that bridges heart→top and has moderate tenacity). Then the EOs from heaviest to lightest. Aldehyde C12 MNA goes dead last because it's the most fugitive sparkle effect and would be buried if added too early.

---

**Step 15 — Linalyl Acetate (150 µL neat)**
> Lavender-clean acetate bridge. Reduced from 240 µL — functions purely as the ester that links the floral heart's muguet into the citrus. Not lavender per se, but the crisp laundered facet that smooths the heart→top transition. Goes first because it's the most tenacious semi-volatile in this phase.

- [ ] Pipette 150 µL
- [ ] Stir

**Step 16 — Neroli EO (200 µL neat) ← LUXURY INDOLIC CITRUS**
> Indolic citrus-floral depth — the luxury engine. Neroli replaces bergamot to elevate Calcite from "citrus cologne" to "mineral-indolic depth study." Distilled bitter orange blossom containing trace indole, linalool, linalyl acetate, nerol — actual molecular complexity that bergamot's terpene-dominant profile can't match. Neroli's indolic warmth creates tension against Cedrat's mineral bitterness (warm glow behind cold stone). Known synergy: Neroli + Hedione = the radiance pairing from Eau Sauvage and the Hermès school. Neroli + Cyclamen Aldehyde = metallic-indolic shimmer.

- [ ] Pipette 200 µL
- [ ] Stir

**Step 17 — Cedrat FCF Sicilian (640 µL neat) ← LEAD CITRUS**
> Bitter citron — THE mineral citrus signature. Promoted to absolute lead at 640 µL. This is Ellena-school perfumery: citron over bergamot, mineral over rounded. Cedrat's sharp, almost metallic bitterness is what makes Calcite read as architectural citrus rather than generic cologne-fresh. Reference: Terre d'Hermès, Concentré d'Orange Verte.

- [ ] Pipette 640 µL (or weigh 0.64 g)
- [ ] Stir

**Step 18 — Petitgrain EO (200 µL neat)**
> Bitter orange leaf architecture. NOT the fruit — the leaf and twig. This is the niche move: petitgrain brings a dry, green-bitter, almost herbal-woody dimension that you'd find in Bigarade Concentrée (Malle/Ellena) or Hermès Eau de Néroli. It structures the citrus with intellectual dryness. Pairs with Cedrat to create a bitter-mineral citrus accord that reads high-end.

- [ ] Pipette 200 µL
- [ ] Stir

**Step 19 — Methyl Pamplemousse 10% (200 µL)**
> Grapefruit-rhubarb tartness. 200 µL of 10% = 20 µL active. This synthetic adds a modern, dry, tart facet — the pink-grapefruit-rhubarb sharpness you find in MFK Aqua Universalis and Diptyque's greener fragrances. It's NOT the same as Grapefruit FCF — Methyl Pamplemousse has a rhubarb stem quality that adds sophistication without juiciness.

- [ ] Pipette 200 µL
- [ ] Stir

**Step 20 — Aldehyde C11 1% (100 µL)**
> Fatty-waxy aldehyde sparkle — first layer of double-aldehyde halo. 100 µL of 1% = 1 µL active. C11 (undecanal) has a clean, waxy, slightly citrus-peel character — think Chanel No. 5's upper register. Paired with C12 MNA, you get a multi-dimensional aldehyde shimmer: C11 is warmer and waxier, C12 is cooler and more metallic. Together they create the luminous "expensive skin" aura.

- [ ] Pipette 100 µL
- [ ] Stir gently

**Step 21 — Aldehyde C12 MNA 1% (150 µL)**
> Metallic mineral halo — second layer of double-aldehyde sparkle. LAST material added. 150 µL of 1% = 1.5 µL active. C12 MNA (2-methylundecanal) is the cooler, more metallic partner to C11. This goes on top of everything else so it sits at the surface. The C11+C12 combination creates a multi-faceted luminous halo in the first 30 seconds — waxier warmth (C11) giving way to cool mineral shimmer (C12).

- [ ] Pipette 150 µL
- [ ] Stir very gently — you want this to stay near the "surface" of the concentrate

**⏸ PAUSE — Your concentrate is complete: 7.49 mL total.** Should smell intensely of bitter-mineral citron over transparent floral over woody base. The double-aldehyde halo will sparkle on top. It will be sharp and concentrated — don't judge yet.

---

### Phase 4: Ethanol Dilution

**Step 22 — Ethanol 96% (22.51 mL)**
> Dilution to 25.0% EdP concentration.

- [ ] Measure 22.51 mL ethanol (weigh 18.01 g if using scale — ethanol density 0.80 g/mL)
- [ ] **Add ethanol slowly** — pour about 5 mL first into the concentrate, swirl to dissolve
- [ ] Add remaining 17.51 mL
- [ ] Stir thoroughly for 60 seconds
- [ ] Transfer to 30 mL amber glass bottle via funnel
- [ ] Cap tightly
- [ ] Label: **"Calcite v2 Optimized / 30 mL / 25.0% EdP / [DATE]"**
