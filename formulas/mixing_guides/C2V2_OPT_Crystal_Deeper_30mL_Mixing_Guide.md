## Mixing Instructions

### Step 1 — Weigh Powders
Weigh **0.22g Myristic Acid** + **0.15g Musk Ketone** into a clean 50mL glass beaker. Both are crystalline solids (MA: white waxy; MK: yellow crystals). Keep together — they dissolve in the same ethanol batch.

### Step 2 — Hot Dissolution (60°C)
Add ~8 mL ethanol (96%) to the beaker. Place in a water bath and warm to **60°C** (not boiling — ethanol BP is 78°C). Stir continuously for **10–15 minutes** until both powders dissolve completely.

- **Myristic Acid** (MP 54°C) dissolves quickly — melts then dissolves at 60°C.
- **Musk Ketone** (MP 135.5°C) dissolves slowly even at 60°C — it goes into solution by solubility in hot ethanol, not by melting. Continue stirring until the solution is **perfectly clear** — no visible crystals or turbidity. If MK crystals persist after 15 min, increase to 65–70°C briefly.

Allow to cool to room temperature. Solution should remain clear (MK at 0.15g in 8mL = ~1.9% w/v, well within ethanol solubility at room temp). If crystals reappear on cooling, reheat briefly and add 1 mL more ethanol.

### Step 3 — Build the Iris DNA Core
Pipette ingredients 1–7 into a separate vial (3,070 µL total). Swirl gently. This is the iris concentrate — the irreducible identity.

### Step 4 — Add Crystal Transparency Layer
Add Helional (485 µL) and Cyclamen Aldehyde (360 µL). These two OR2W1 agonists define the crystalline spatial effect.

### Step 5 — Add Orris Anchor
Add Alpha Irone 30% (180 µL). This is 54 µL active genuine orris character.

### Step 6 — Add VN1R1 + Thermal Depth
Add Hedione HC (300 µL) and Ambrox Super 30% (350 µL).

### Step 7 — Add Sub-ODT Luxury Matrix
Add **Cashmeran neat (170 µL)** and **Amberwood F neat (300 µL)**. These two materials operate below the conscious smell threshold — Cashmeran activates TRPA1 cashmere tactile luxury on warm skin, Amberwood F activates TRPV1 168h depot longevity. Neither changes the iris-crystalline-transparent family. **Blend cold** (room temperature) per Mixing Science protocol.

### Step 8 — Add B1 Ozonic Mineral Base
Add Habanolide (725 µL), Ethylene Brassylate (275 µL), Romandolide (280 µL), Galaxolide 80% (240 µL).

### Step 9 — Combine with Ethanol
Pour the dissolved powder solution (from Step 2) into a 30mL+ bottle. Add the 6,735 µL liquid concentrate (Steps 3–8). Rinse all vials with remaining ethanol. Top up to exactly **30.00 mL** with ethanol 96%.

### Step 10 — Maceration
Seal and store in dark. **Minimum 4 weeks** (extended from C2V2's 3 weeks — Cashmeran TRPA1 effect requires 4+ weeks to stabilize per Mixing Science R23). During maceration:
- Helional/Cyclamal crystal dyad equilibrates with iris DNA core
- Alpha Irone metallic sharpness softens into authentic orris butter
- Cashmeran dipole-dipole associations form with skin-contact molecules
- Amberwood F + Myristic Acid co-depot lipid pathway establishes
- MK slow integration into the OR5A2 adj receptor-relevant molecular cloud

Judge no earlier than **day 28**.
