## Technical Notes — Section by Section

### TOP — Orange Blossom Trinity (270 µL)

The single biggest weakness of V1 was the 2-material top: Blood Orange + Linalool = bright flash that's gone by minute 15. V2 constructs a proper **orange blossom accord**:

- **Blood Orange Sicilian** (160 µL) — the juice, the blush of warm citrus fruit. Reduced from 180 because Neroli and Petitgrain now share the citrus load. Still the dominant opening character.
- **Neroli EO** (40 µL) — authentic orange blossom naturale. Provides the hesperidic-floral bridge that Aurantiol (synthetic) hints at. Neroli's indolic-orange blossom character makes Blood Orange read as "blossom" rather than "fruit juice." This is the material J'adore uses for its orange-floral opening.
- **Petitgrain EO** (30 µL) — bitter orange leaf: linalyl acetate + citrusy-green. Extends the orange arc by 20+ minutes because citrus-leaf materials have longer tenacity than citrus-peel materials. Also adds a herbal crispness that prevents the top from reading "sweet."
- **Linalool** (40 µL) — the olfactory bridge between citrus and floral. Reduced from 60 because Neroli already contains significant linalool. Provides natural transition from orange blossom top → white floral heart. Still enough for the first 10-minute sparkle.

**Why this works:** Blood Orange = fruit. Neroli = flower. Petitgrain = leaf. Three parts of the same tree, creating a complete botanical portrait rather than a single-note citrus blast.

### HEART — White Floral Bouquet (820 µL, 16 materials)

The heart is the formula's identity. V1 had 10 heart materials; V2 has 16. Every addition solves a specific gap.

**The Muguet Quintet (190 µL total):**
- **Hydroxycitronellal** (60 µL) — watery, dewy muguet. The classic lily-of-the-valley material since Dior's Diorissimo. Provides the aqueous transparency that makes muguet smell like "white petals in rain."
- **Lilyreal ND** (50 µL, up from 40) — clean synthetic muguet. Where Hydroxycitronellal is watery, Lilyreal is "cosmetic counter" muguet. The two together = "fresh lilies in a department store." Increased because it's the backbone of the cosmetic-clean effect.
- **Bourgeonal** (30 µL, down from 40) — lily-aquatic, cyclamen-tinged. Provides the green-watery dimension. Reduced because Florol and Freesia HDI now share this role.
- **Florol** (30 µL, NEW) — waxy-dewy, lily-green, with a soft iris-like facet. The "morning dew on petals" quality that V1 lacked entirely. In a muguet accord, Florol adds the transparent, almost-violet dewiness that separates luxury muguet from functional muguet.
- **Freesia HDI** (20 µL, NEW) — green-floral brightness with a cool, transparent lift. Prevents the muguet quintet from reading dense or heavy. Opens the accord "upward" the way that cutting flowers lets air into a vase.

**The Gardenia-Waterlily Anchor:**
- **DBCA** (70 µL, up from 60) — gardenia-rose transparency, cosmetic-clean. At 70 µL, this is the single-material character anchor of the white floral. DBCA is what makes a white floral read "gardenia" rather than generic. The increase from 60 accounts for the expanded floral field — more competition for attention means the anchor needs more presence.
- **Nympheal** (30 µL) — waxy waterlily, lotus effect. Unchanged. Provides the waxy-aquatic-floral dimension that no muguet material can create.

**The Orange Blossom Bridge:**
- **Aurantiol** (50 µL, up from 40) — orange blossom clean, with a powdery-aldehydic lift. THE molecular bridge between the blood orange top and the white floral heart. Without Aurantiol, blood orange and muguet would read as two separate fragrances. At 50 µL, its powdery-citrus character makes the transition from "orange" to "white floral" feel inevitable. Increased because it now works in concert with Neroli above.

**The Jasmine Narcosis:**
- **Benzyl Acetate** (40 µL) — jasmine sparkle-fruity, top-heart transition material. Lighter than fleshy jasmine, provides the crisp bright top of a jasmine accord.
- **Jasmine FO** (30 µL) — jasmine body, indolic-rich. The full-bodied jasmine depth. Together with Benzyl Acetate + Indole, creates a three-dimensioned jasmine.
- **Indole 10%** (30 µL = 3 µL active) — narcotic animalic belly. At 3 µL active, this is subliminal: you don't consciously smell "indole," but its absence would make the white floral read "laundry detergent." The thin line between "elegant white floral" and "fabric softener" is exactly this 3 µL of indole.

**The Rose Whisper (NEW section, 50 µL total):**
- **PEA** (30 µL, NEW) — rose-hyacinth body. Every luxury white floral has a rose facet — Dior J'adore, Chanel No. 5, Joy. PEA adds the rosy-hyacinth body that reads "complete bouquet" rather than "three white flowers." It also provides a powdery cushion that supports the muguet materials.
- **Citronellol** (20 µL, NEW) — fresh natural rose, citrusy-green. The complement to PEA's body: where PEA is plush, Citronellol is crisp-rosy. Together they sketch a rose that says "I was in the bouquet all along."

**The Luxury Signals:**
- **Gamma Decalactone** (20 µL, NEW) — peachy-creamy lactone. The molecular bridge between the airy floral heart and the creamy sandalwood base. Without it, there's a textural gap: florals feel "light" and sandalwood feels "creamy," and the transition is abrupt. Gamma Decalactone makes the journey seamless — "white petals landing on warm skin."
- **Ultralia** (10 µL, NEW) — ghost iris at trace. Powdery, transparent, imperceptible as a standalone note. At 10 µL, Ultralia doesn't add "iris" to the composition. It adds *expense*. The same way a whisper of real iris root makes a €300 fragrance smell different from a €30 one. The "there's something quietly extraordinary about this" signal.

**The Radiance Engine:**
- **Hedione** (300 µL, up from 280) — radiance amplifier, volume expander, the "air" in the bouquet. At 300 µL (13.6% of concentrate), Hedione serves three functions: (1) makes every floral material above radiate outward, (2) creates perceived volume without heaviness, (3) provides the jasmine-muguet undertone that ties the bouquet together. The 20 µL increase accounts for the expanded floral field needing more amplification.

### BASE — Skin-Effect Architecture (1110 µL, 12 materials)

V1's base was functional but generic: Iso E Super dominated at 250 µL with no sandalwood for skin contact, no wood bridge between floral heart and musk bed. V2 replaces brute force with architecture.

**The Sandalwood Duo (80 µL, NEW section):**
- **Javanol** (50 µL, NEW) — premium sandalwood, skin-scent intimacy. Javanol is why people lean in — it smells like the best version of your own skin. At 50 µL, it's the drydown destination: once the florals thin out, you're wearing Javanol-on-skin. This is the material that separates "perfume" from "fine fragrance."
- **Ebanol** (30 µL, NEW) — creamy sandalwood, distinct from Javanol. Where Javanol is "skin," Ebanol is "cream." The two together create dimensional sandalwood rather than a single-note wood. Ebanol's milky quality makes the peach lactone (Gamma Decalactone) bloom in the drydown.

**The Woody Framework:**
- **Iso E Super** (200 µL, down from 250) — abstract-cedar molecular cocoon. Still substantial but no longer the only wood. At 200 µL, it provides the transparent woody envelope that makes everything else smell "present" without dominating. Reduced because Javanol + Ebanol + Vertofix now share the structural load.
- **Vertofix Coeur** (40 µL, NEW) — woody-musky bridge, cedarwood-amber fixative. The architectural connector between the floral heart and the musk bed. Without Vertofix, there's a gap between "flowers" and "skin musk." With it, the transition reads as one continuous material.
- **Cashmeran 20%** (100 µL = 20 µL active, down from 150 µL / 30 µL active) — cashmere textural warmth. Reduced because Javanol and Ebanol now provide the "soft texture" that Cashmeran was doing alone. At 20 µL active, still provides the cashmere-velvet quality without competing with the sandalwood duo.
- **Ambermax 50%** (50 µL = 25 µL active) — transparent warm amber. Unchanged in function. Provides the amber warmth without resinous heaviness.

**The Salicylate Cushion (240 µL total):**
- **Benzyl Salicylate** (160 µL, up from 150) — THE cosmetic diffusion material. At 160 µL, it's the invisible backbone of the "expensive beauty counter" effect. Provides fixation, volume, and the specific quality perfumers call "cosmetic plastic floral." When you smell a Dior or Chanel counter and can't identify what smells expensive, it's often Benzyl Salicylate.
- **Hexyl Salicylate** (80 µL, unchanged) — lighter salicylate, green-floral transparent fixative. Less heavy than Benzyl, provides the sheer top layer of the salicylate cushion. Together: Hexyl (light, green) over Benzyl (dense, cosmetic) = a salicylate gradient from transparent to cushioned.

**The Drydown Sweetness:**
- **Coumarin 20%** (50 µL = 10 µL active, up from 40 µL / 8 µL active) — hay-tonka powder, drydown sweetness. V1's 8 µL active was borderline subliminal. At 10 µL active, Coumarin provides the warm, slightly sweet finish that makes people say "you smell nice" in hour 6 without understanding why.

**The Musk Triad (350 µL total):**
- **Galaxolide 80%** (220 µL = 176 µL active, reduced from 250 / 200) — clean white musk, cosmetic backbone. Still the primary musk but slightly reduced to make room for Ambrettolide's natural character. At 176 µL active, Galaxolide IS the "clean skin" baseline.
- **Habanolide** (100 µL, reduced from 120) — musky-woody macrolide, metallic-clean. Pairs with Galaxolide: where Galaxolide is "laundry musk," Habanolide is "skin musk." Slightly reduced because Ambrettolide now carries part of the skin-musk role.
- **Ambrettolide 10%** (30 µL = 3 µL active, NEW) — natural musk character, animalic-skin. At 3 µL active, this is the "living skin" quality that separates a fragrance that smells "worn" from one that smells "sprayed." The faintest animalic dimension in the musk bed, detectable only as warmth.

## Post-Mixing Protocol

1. **Seal immediately** — cap tightly or cover with Parafilm
2. **Store upright, dark, room temperature** — away from direct sunlight and heat
3. **Maceration schedule:**
   - **48 hours** — first test spray: citrus top should be bright, white floral should be emerging, base will still be rough
   - **1 week** — salicylate cushion integrates with florals. Muguet quintet becomes cohesive rather than "5 separate materials." The "beauty counter" effect appears
   - **2 weeks** — optimal. Neroli + Aurantiol bridge fully integrates. Sandalwood duo blooms. The drydown destination (Javanol-on-skin) should be clear from hour 4 onward
   - **4 weeks** — fully mature. Coumarin + Ultralia powder note settles. The fragrance reads as one integrated arc, not sections
