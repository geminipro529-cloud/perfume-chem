## CONCEPT

Gin-tonic transparency over a dark-spicy heart. Bergamot-grapefruit opens. Juniper-gin leads the 15-min to 2-hour window. Cardamom-vetiver-cedarwood builds a woody-spicy-aromatic scaffold. Coumarin-ambrofix-musk carries 4-10+ hours as a sweet-dark skin scent. Single bold risk — coumarin at 300 µL of 20% turns hour-4 from "ghost of gin" into "tonka-vanilla seduction." No nitriles. No pre-blends. No jasmine.

Family: Woody Aromatic · Character: Gin-cardamom-vetiver · Target: 13% EDP

## PROCEDURE

1. Dose Base (1→8). Swirl after each. Evernyl at 80 µL — deliberate chypre anchor.
2. Dose Heart (9→13). Swirl after juniper+cardamom, after vetiver+cedar.
3. Dose Top (14→15) — ≤30 seconds. Cap immediately.
4. Add ethanol in 3 portions. Swirl 15s between.
5. Macerate 4 weeks minimum, dark, 18-22°C.
6. Label: **Gin & Tonic Nocturne — 50 mL EDP — 2026-05-10**
