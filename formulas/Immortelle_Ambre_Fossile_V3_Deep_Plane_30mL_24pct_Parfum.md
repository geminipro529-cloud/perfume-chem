## Named identity and hedonic contract

**North star:** a golden immortelle flower caught in amber while still alive. Bitter pollen and dry hay remain visible at the surface; restrained maple and cured leaf lead inward; a mineral-balsamic fossil holds the same flower through the late drydown.

**Hedonic commitment:** pleasure is seated in the base instead of sprayed across the opening. The formula should feel abundant because the rear plane yields under the flower—warm resin grain, textile wood, slow sandalwood body, dry mineral contour, and a restrained skin echo—not because it becomes sweeter, louder, cleaner, or more diffusive.

**Subject hierarchy:** Immortelle first. Amber is its enclosure and transformed state. Wood, tobacco, salicylate, musk, maple, and vanilla are supporting planes and must never become separate subjects.

**Forbidden takeovers:** pancake syrup; curry seasoning; vanilla gourmand; tobacco perfume; generic amberwood; sandalwood cream; laundry musk; cosmetic/sunscreen salicylate; incense; leather; iris; floral bouquet.

**Protected nucleus:** Immortelle Absolute + Coumarin + Maple Lactone + Ethyl Safranate + Tobacco Absolute + Anisaldehyde. Carrot Seed gives desiccated root/stem anatomy but must not read as orris.
