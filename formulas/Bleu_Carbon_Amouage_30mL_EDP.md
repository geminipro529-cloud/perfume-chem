# Bleu Carbon — 30 mL Eau de Parfum — Amouage

**Batch:** 30.00 mL · ~17.3% EdP · 40 materials
**Profile:** Amouage does Sauvage/Luna Rossa Carbon — ultra-luxury modern mineral aromatic fougere.
Under the cold mineral freshness: Amouage's frankincense-orris-sandalwood DNA.
A blue fragrance executed with 40 materials, a four-material sandalwood chord, orris powder luxury,
perceptible incense depth, five-musk macrocyclic chord, and resinous-ambergris warmth.
**Maceration:** 4 weeks minimum — this formula has density that needs time.

---

## Equipment

- 1 amber glass bottle 30 mL
- 1 graduated pipette 1 mL (for 100–300 µL volumes)
- 1 micro-pipette or 1 mL syringe graduated 0.01 mL (for 5–80 µL volumes)
- Ethanol 96% (perfumer's grade)

## Maceration

Store cool and dark. Invert gently once per week.
**Minimum 4 weeks.** The frankincense, sandalwood chord, labdanum, and orris need significant integration time.
The top softens at 3 weeks; the incense-orris-sandalwood heart blooms at 4+ weeks.

### Chemistry stability note (same as designer version)

Geraniol at ~2.3% active mass triggers the conservative oxidation model (4-week half-life, `bht_protected=False`). Add 0.05% BHT or reduce Geraniol to ~90 µL to clear the gate. This is a known model limitation — Geraniol at 2% is standard in commercial fougeres with antioxidants.

### The Amouage "trick" — density without heaviness

Amouage packs 40 materials without reading heavy because each dense material (labdanum, benzoin, myrrh, costus, heliotropal) is dosed at its **subliminal threshold** — you feel the richness but cannot name it. The formula has more depth-per-µL than the designer version without any single material dominating.

- **Labdanum 10% at 30 µL** = 3 µL active = 100 ppm in finished product. Adds ambergris-resinous warmth without identifying as "amber."
- **Costus Olifac 10% at 15 µL** = 1.5 µL active = 50 ppm. Adds "expensive skin" without reading animalic.
- **Heliotropal at 8 µL neat** = 267 ppm. Adds almond-opulence without reading gourmand. The orris-almond axis IS the Amouage luxury signature.
- **Myrrh EO at 10 µL** = 333 ppm. Bitter-resinous depth without reading medicinal.
- **Ethyl Safranate at 8 µL** = 267 ppm. Saffron-leather whisper — distinctly Middle Eastern luxury without any spice heaviness.

## Perfumer's Notes

- **Romandolide replaces Galaxolide** as primary projection musk. This is THE single biggest change that elevates the formula from designer to luxury. Galaxolide reads "laundry" on skin after 30 minutes; Romandolide reads "your skin, but expensive." Givaudan designed Romandolide for exactly this purpose — a post-Galaxolide clean musk for luxury formulations.
- **The sandalwood chord** is not decorative — it's structural. Javanol provides dry precision, Ebanol creamy softness, Sandalore fresh-woody light, Bacdanol round mass. Together they approximate what real Mysore sandalwood does in an Amouage formula: a warm-woody-creamy bed that supports everything above it.
- **Incense at 180 µL of 10%** (18 µL active) is the inflection point where frankincense becomes a perceptible note rather than a whisper. This is the Amouage DNA — they never hide their incense.
- **Alpha Irone (30%) at 50 µL** = 15 µL active = the expensive orris powder that says "I cost more than your rent." This is in every Amouage, even their freshest compositions.
- **Ethyl Safranate at 8 µL** is a deliberate Amouage signature — the saffron nuance gives Middle Eastern luxury context without any of the heavy spice the user specifically does NOT want.
- **No opaque preblends** — all 40 materials are transparent, CAS-traceable ingredients.
- **Chemistry stability gate** same limitation as designer version — Geraniol oxidation model. Documented.
