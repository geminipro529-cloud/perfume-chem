## Target / Ideal

A soft creamy-petal, waxy-satin magnolia-peony rather than the sharper flower-specific green of the hyacinth bouquet or the recognizable rose-alcohol spine of the Rose de Mai formula. The target excludes PADMA, cut-stem greens, muguet materials, ozonics, musks, woods, ambers, vanilla, lactones, resins, added rose recognizers, white-floral takeover materials, and every essential oil.

Peonile would ordinarily be an obvious peony diagnostic, but the current repository assigns it incompatible identities: a C14H15N nitrile in the live profile/data spine versus a damascone-family C13H20O ketone in the generated graph. It is excluded from the primary formula until the physical bottle and data paths are reconciled.

## Current-Inventory Build

PEDMC supplies the magnolia-soft veil; Hydroxycitronellol supplies the mild rose-peony cushion; PEA supplies quiet petal body; Hedione supplies air; Hexyl Salicylate supplies a light waxy film. Dihydrojasmone is omitted because its green-jasmine character is not neutral here and its V5 stock authority is unresolved.

Several rows lack authoritative density, and some ODT/VP records are derived or conflicted. Exact active ppm w/w remains `UNKNOWN / HOLD`; modeled OAV is screening evidence only.
