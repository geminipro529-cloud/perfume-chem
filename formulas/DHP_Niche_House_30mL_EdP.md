# Niche House Leather-Iris-Oud Oriental — Dior Homme Parfum Study — 30 mL EdP

**Historical reference:** Dior Homme Parfum (2014, François Demachy). Reimagined with premium niche materials — bold leather, real iris-orris, rose heart, and a dark oud-like base.

**DHP vs DHI:** DHP has leather (absent in DHI), oud (absent in DHI), and rose (absent in DHI). The iris is present but secondary to the leather-oud axis. DHP is darker, heavier, and longer-lasting.
