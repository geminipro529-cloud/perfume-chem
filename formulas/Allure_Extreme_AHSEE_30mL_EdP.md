# Allure Extrême — 30 mL EdP

**Luxury reinterpretation of Chanel Allure Homme Sport Eau Extrême (Jacques Polge, 2012).**  
**Family:** Woody Aromatic · Tonka Musk  
**Concentration:** ~18% EdP · ~5,400 µL concentrate in 30 mL  
**Character:** Cool juniper-mandarin-mint opening, single black pepper heart, massive tonka-sandalwood-musk drydown with muguet air.

## What the Chanel Label Reveals

Official AHSEE allergen list (current formula), descending:

> LIMONENE · LINALOOL · **COUMARIN** · ALPHA-ISOMETHYL IONONE · CITRAL · GERANIOL · CITRONELLOL · CINNAMAL · FARNESOL · **HYDROXYCITRONELLAL**

**Key confirmations**: Coumarin is the #3 allergen — the tonka loading is massive, more than the ionone powder. Hydroxycitronellal appears (muguet freshness — unique to AHSEE vs the original AHS which lacks it). No benzyl salicylate (unlike AHS EDT). Alpha-isomethyl ionone present (Polge's Chanel powder signature).

## Architecture

AHSEE is a **two-stage perfume**: cool aromatic opening (mint + mandarin + juniper-cypress + sage) → creamy warm drydown (tonka + sandalwood + white musk + cedar). The pepper heart is the single bridge note between both stages. The structural skeleton is the same neo-fougère template as AHS EDT (dihydromyrcenol → helional → coumarin → ambroxan) but with heavier tonka-sandalwood loading and muguet freshness replacing the aldehydic sparkle.

## Dosing Notes

- **Juniper Berry EO + Pine EO at 75 µL total**: The cypress substitute. Juniper provides the gin-clarity; Pine adds the forest depth. Do NOT increase Pine beyond 15 µL — it will dominate and read as "air freshener." The two together create a more luxurious coniferous opening than cypress alone.
- **Geranium EO + Cardamom EO at 80 µL total**: The mint substitute. Geranium's menthone content provides the metallic-cool mint character; Cardamom's 1,8-cineole amplifies the coolness. This is NOT a sweet-peppermint mint — it's a metallic, savory-cool opening like "expensive Japanese restaurant hand soap."
- **Coumarin 20% at 200 µL (40 µL active)**: This plus Tonkarome (70 µL active) = 110 µL total tonka-coumarin actives. The massive coumarin loading is confirmed by the allergen label (coumarin before ionone). Do not reduce.
- **Black Pepper EO at 100 µL**: The only heart note. The original AHSEE has pepper as its sole heart material — unusual in commercial perfumery. At this dose: prominent but smooth, not aggressive. The pepper bridges the cool opening to the warm base.
- **Maturation**: 4 weeks minimum sealed, cool, dark. The tonka-coumarin-vanillin complex requires integration with the ionones and musks. Testing before 2 weeks: the opening will read as disjointed cool-aromatic and the base as raw-tonka without the blending.

## Material Rejections

- **No aldehydes** — AHSEE drops the AHS EDT aldehyde sparkle. Mint + juniper replace it.
- **No marine/calone** — AHSEE drops the AHS EDT sea notes. Juniper + helional provide the fresh-air effect without aquatic character.
- **No benzyl salicylate** — not on the AHSEE label. Benzyl Benzoate is the fixative.
- **No Galaxolide** — laundry-detergent kills the luxury. Full macrocyclic musk chord instead.
- **No synthetic mandarin** — Red Mandarin EO provides the real thing.
- **No DHMOL overdose** — present at 350 µL for the fresh scaffold but the juniper + geranium + cardamom carry the aromatic character.
- **No orange/citrus beyond mandarin + bergamot support** — AHSEE is mandarin-led, not citrus-medley.

| **Opening** | Aldehydes + Blood Orange + Red Mandarin + Bergamot | Juniper + Geranium-Mint + Red Mandarin + Clary Sage |
