# Demachy — Dior Homme Cologne Style — 30 mL Eau de Cologne

**Batch:** 30.00 mL · ~9.5% EdC · 16 materials
**Profile:** François Demachy's transparent neroli-cologne style. Bergamot-grapefruit-mandarin citrus head, Aurantiol + Oranger Crystals + Nerolin Bromelia neroli heart, clean macrocyclic musk base.
**Maceration:** 1 week (cologne — no heavy bases needing integration)

---

## Equipment

- 1 amber glass bottle 30 mL
- 1 graduated pipette 1 mL (for 100–600 µL)
- 1 micro-pipette (for 5–80 µL)
- Ethanol 96%

| Layer | Materials | Effect |

| **Heart** | Aurantiol 100, Oranger Crystals 200, Nerolin Bromelia 30, Methyl Anthranilate 40, Nerol 40, Hedione 300, Linalool 80 | five-material neroli chord — Nerol is a primary neroli constituent |

## Demachy's neroli chord explained

| Material | Dose | What it contributes |
|----------|------|-------------------|
| **Aurantiol** | 100 µL | The body — methyl anthranilate + hydroxycitronellal schiff base gives the sweet orange blossom core |
| **Oranger Crystals** | 200 µL (10%) | The grape-neroli facet — crystalline neroli effect |
| **Nerolin Bromelia** | 30 µL | The naphthyl-floral depth — makes the neroli feel "expensive" rather than "detergent" |
| **Nerol** | 40 µL | Primary neroli constituent — named after neroli itself. Adds the sweet citrusy-rose facet that IS neroli's character |
| **Methyl Anthranilate** | 40 µL | The fruity-floral booster — bridges citrus top to floral heart |
| **Petitgrain** | 100 µL | The green-bitter structure — gives neroli its "green twig" character |
| **Linalool** | 80 µL | Another primary neroli constituent — neroli oil is 30-50% linalool |

Demachy would use real Neroli EO directly, but with your inventory the six-material chord recreates neroli's molecular profile: Aurantiol (body) + Oranger Crystals (grape facet) + Nerolin Bromelia (depth) + Nerol (citrusy-rose) + Linalool (fresh floral) + Methyl Anthranilate (fruity bridge).
