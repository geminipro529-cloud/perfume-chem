# Osmanthus Iris-Jasmine Triptych — 15mL EdP

## Character
A three-part floral composition where the classical osmanthus flower is deconstructed across its three closest olfactive cousins: osmanthus (apricot-leather warmth), iris (violet-ionone powder), and jasmine (radiant white floral). The result is a multifaceted floral that refuses to sit in a single fragrance family — it's simultaneously fruity-floral, powdery-woody, and white-floral radiant. Think of it as an osmanthus soliflore that has been expanded into a full floral symphony, where each movement highlights a different facet of the original flower's complex identity.

**Family**: Floral — osmanthus-iris-jasmine triptych, powdery-violet heart, apricot-leather warmth, radiant white florals

## Concept: The Triptych Structure

The original osmanthus absolute (300 µL of 10% in the base skeleton) is redistributed across three floral registers:

| Register | Materials | Total Dose | Function |
|----------|-----------|------------|----------|
| **Osmanthus** | Osmanthus Absolute 10%, Gamma Decalactone, Damascone Beta | 275 µL dilution | Apricot-leather warmth, the original flower's signature |
| **Iris** | Alpha Ionone, Beta Ionone 1%, Alpha Isomethyl Ionone, Orivone, Ultralia | 252 µL | Violet-ionone powder, buttery orris depth, ghost-iris transparency |
| **Jasmine** | Jasmine Sambac 10%, Dihydrojasmone, Hedione HC, Benzyl Acetate | 505 µL | Radiant white floral, jasmonate volume, jasmine brightness |

The jasmine register receives the largest allocation because Hedione HC (325 µL) serves as the primary radiance amplifier and volume expander for the entire composition — it's the architectural backbone that lets the osmanthus and iris shine without being overshadowed.

## Material Justification

### Why these three registers?

Osmanthus absolute naturally contains all three facets: apricot-fruit (gamma-decalactone family), violet-ionone (beta-ionone is a major constituent), and jasmine-radiance (hedione-like jasmonates). By splitting the formula across these three registers, we're amplifying what the natural absolute already contains — creating an "osmanthus in HD" where each facet gets its own dedicated materials.

### Key choices:

- **Osmanthus Absolute (200 µL of 10% = 20 µL active)**: Reduced from the base skeleton's 300 µL to make room for the iris and jasmine registers. At 20 µL active (~0.67% of concentrate), it's clearly present as the warm-apricot-leather anchor without dominating.

- **Hedione HC (325 µL neat)**: The architectural backbone. Chosen over regular Hedione because the high-cis isomer provides superior radiance and floral volume. At 10.8% of concentrate, it's the dominant material by volume but reads as invisible radiance rather than a distinct note.

- **Alpha Ionone + Alpha Isomethyl Ionone**: The iris-violet trinity. Alpha Ionone (75 µL) for rich orris-woody depth, Alpha Isomethyl Ionone (50 µL) for powdery-diffusive radiance. Together they create a two-layer iris: substantive body + ethereal powder.

- **Beta Ionone 1% (60 µL = 0.6 µL active)**: The green-violet leaf accent. The 1% dilution allows sub-microgram precision — at 0.6 µL active, it provides the violet-leaf top note without the woody-ionone body that higher doses would introduce.

- **Orivone (35 µL) + Ultralia (12 µL)**: Butter-iris depth (Orivone) paired with ghost-iris transparency (Ultralia). This is the same iris architecture used in Osmanthus_03_Violet_Iris, proven to work in this family.

- **Jasmine Sambac 10% (60 µL = 6 µL active)**: True jasmine depth with natural indolic warmth. Chosen over Jasmine FO because the absolute provides more complexity and natural indole character. Kept at modest dose to avoid a "jasmine perfume" reading.

- **Dihydrojasmone (30 µL)**: The warm-fatty jasmine ketone — adds the "jasmine tea" facet that pairs beautifully with osmanthus's tea-like character. Synergizes with Hedione HC for a full jasmonate chord.

- **Benzyl Acetate (40 µL)**: Fresh jasmine brightness — the green-floral top of jasmine that prevents the jasmine register from reading as too heavy or indolic.

- **Musk Ketone 10% (65 µL = 6.5 µL active)**: The powdery-sweet talcum nitro musk — the ONLY musk that directly reinforces powder registers. Essential for the iris-violet character. Chosen as character-echo musk over all other options.

- **Ambrettolide 10% (30 µL = 3 µL active)**: Macrocyclic fruity-musky warmth — adds naturalistic skin warmth that bridges the osmanthus fruit and the iris powder. Pairs with Ethylene Brassylate for a full lactonic chord.

- **Ebanol (35 µL)**: Creamy-soft sandalwood — chosen over Javanol (too cold/dry for this powder context) and over Bacdanol (too heavy). Supports the orris-butter character from Orivone.

- **Cashmeran (45 µL neat)**: Textile-warmth and cashmere muskiness — adds the "wrapped in fabric" sensation. Kept below the 50 µL family-shifting ceiling.

- **Ambrofix 30% (40 µL = 12 µL active)**: Crystalline-mineral amber at low dose — provides transparent structure beneath the powder without adding warmth. Pairs with the metallic-cool ionone character.

## Safety Notes

- **Musk Ketone 10%**: 65 µL of 10% = 6.5 µL active. Nitro musks are IFRA-restricted; this dose is well below restriction limits for EdP.
- **Ambrettolide 10%**: 30 µL of 10% = 3 µL active. Macrocyclic musk, no IFRA restrictions at this dose.
- **Coumarin 20%**: 25 µL of 20% = 5 µL active. Well below IFRA Cat 4 limit (0.1% in leave-on = 1,500 µL active per 15mL).

## Batch Instructions

1. Weigh/measure all materials in the order listed (top → heart → base)
2. Combine in a 20 mL glass vial
3. Agitate gently for 30 seconds
4. Add ethanol 96% to the 15 mL mark
5. Cap and invert 10 times
6. Let mature 48 hours minimum before evaluation

### Perfumer Analysis

This formula reads as a citrus-rose-aromatic floral rather than the intended osmanthus-iris-jasmine triptych. The headspace is dominated by Bergamot FCF (OAV 27,559) with Geraniol (8,914) and Orivone (6,581) as secondary leaders. The iris register (Alpha Ionone 1,242; Beta Ionone 454; Alpha Isomethyl Ionone 134; Orivone 6,581) is the strongest of the three, but Orivone reads as orris-butter rather than violet-ionone.
