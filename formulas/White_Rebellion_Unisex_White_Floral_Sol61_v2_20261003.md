Approachable gender-open white floral in the Good girl gone Bad territory: tuberose, orange blossom and apricot-osmanthus remain the heart. Bitter-green citrus, mineral/skin musk and pale woods frame the flowers without making a dry masculine amberwood scent.

## 5. Why the composition changed

1. Put the tuberose-orange-osmanthus axis ahead of the dry framework; this is an intentional new mass target, not a volume-equivalent conversion.
2. Keep Tuberlia's reproducible commercial body and natural tuberose's texture separate. The base remains opaque; it is not a molecule or an analytical tuberose surrogate.
3. Use only Habanolide (mineral-air hypothesis) and Ambrettolide (fruit-skin hypothesis). Romandolide is a substitute test, not a third default musk.
4. Remove Cedramber and extra Haiti vetiver from the lead. Keep polished Vetiveryl Acetate, small cedar/Clearwood and only 2% crystal Ambrox.
5. Use the user's newer neat Ambrettolide statement explicitly, while leaving the stale 10% inventory entry untouched. Aurantiol's uncertain strength remains an explicit branch.

No larger total, extra musk, higher vapor-pressure spread or ingredient count is treated as richness. Iso E Super is selected for abstract woody space, not identifiable cedar; identifiable cedar/Azarbre is used only where the brief needs that register. Adding Timberol/superambers would require a separate target-linked hypothesis.
