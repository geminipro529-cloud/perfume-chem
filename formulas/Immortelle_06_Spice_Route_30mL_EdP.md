# Immortelle Spice Route — 30mL EdP

## Character
A warm-spiced oriental where Immortelle Absolute's curry-honey-maple character serves as the olfactory bridge between aromatic spices (Cardamom, Black Pepper, Cinnamaldehyde, Ethyl Safranate) and a sweet-resinous base (Labdanum, Benzoin, Vanillin). The composition evokes an ancient spice market at golden hour — cardamom and saffron floating above wood crates, immortelle's maple sweetness as the distant scent of honeyed dates, cinnamon drifting from a tea stall. Unlike aggressive spice-bombs (Opium, Cinnabar), this is a refined, luminous spice — immortelle's golden warmth prevents the spices from reading as "kitchen" or "holiday." Think *L'Artisan Parfumeur Safran Troublant* reimagined through a Corsican lens — warm, expressive, but elegant.

**Family**: Spicy Oriental — cardamom-pepper-saffron spice heart over labdanum-benzoin-vanilla base, immortelle as honeyed modulator

## Material Justification

- **Immortelle Absolute (10%)**: The honeyed bridge — 200 µL of 10% = 20 µL active (0.34% of concentrate). At the upper modifier range because this concept depends on immortelle's curry-honey character to integrate the spices into the oriental base. Without immortelle, Cardamom + Cinnamaldehyde + Eugenol + Saffron + Benzoin = a confused spice rack. With immortelle, those same spices read as a unified "spice route" — immortelle's curry facet gives all the spices a common olfactory language.
- **Cardamom EO (40 µL) + Black Pepper EO (30 µL)**: The spice opening — green cardamom's cineolic-spicy lift and black pepper's dry-piquant sparkle. Cardamom chosen as the lead spice because its green-spicy character is the most compatible with immortelle's herbal-curry facet — they amplify each other rather than competing. Black pepper adds the dry counterpoint without introducing the overwhelming heat of pink pepper. Both at moderate doses — these are spice NOTES, not spice BOMBS.
- **Cinnamaldehyde (8 µL) + Eugenol (15 µL)**: Warm spice trace. Cinnamaldehyde at micro-dose provides a whisper of cinnamon — not enough to read as "cinnamon roll," just a warm-spice shimmer at the top. Eugenol at 15 µL neat adds clove-spice warmth — the classic oriental-spice material. Both kept at trace specifically to AVOID the "Christmas candle" effect that plagues spice-heavy compositions. These are seasonings, not ingredients.
- **Ethyl Safranate (30 µL)**: Saffron effect at heart — the most luxurious spice in perfumery. Ethyl Safranate provides the leathery-iodine-saffron character that elevates the composition from "spice market" to "silk road." At 30 µL neat, it's clearly present but not dominant — saffron should whisper of luxury, not shout. Pairs with Labdanum for the classic saffron-labdanum oriental accord.
- **Labdanum Resinoid (10%)**: 200 µL of 10% = 20 µL active. The resinous-amber heart that connects the spices to the benzoin base. Labdanum's complex leather-amber warmth is the essential oriental anchor — without it, the spice-benzoin axis would read as "potpourri" rather than "perfume." Matched in active dose with immortelle (20 µL each) — they are co-hearts of this composition.
- **Siam Benzoin (50% in DPG)**: 300 µL of 50% = 150 µL active. The sweet-vanilla-balsamic foundation — the largest single dose after Iso E Super. Benzoin provides the "golden glow" that unifies the spices into a warm whole. Siam at 50% chosen over Sumatra 10% for maximum warmth at manageable volume. Benzoin is the olfactory "container" for the spice route — the wooden crate in which the treasures travel.
- **Vanillin (10%) + Coumarin (20%)**: Sweetness axis. Vanillin at 15 µL active provides the classic-vanilla anchor. Coumarin at 20 µL active adds hay-sweet tonka that pairs with immortelle (proven synergy) and bridges the vanilla to the spice heart. Together they create the sweet-resinous depth that makes the spices feel sophisticated rather than culinary.
- **Gamma Decalactone (50 µL)**: Lactonic peach-skin bridge — adds creamy-fruity adhesion between the spicy heart and the sweet base. Prevents the spice-to-vanilla transition from being abrupt. The creamy texture also softens the spices' sharp edges — cardamom through a lactonic lens reads as "creamy chai," not "raw spice."
- **Hedione HC (500 µL) + Hedione (250 µL)**: Radiance amplifier pair. Hedione's known synergy with immortelle expands the spice-honey character outward. Essential in a spice composition because spices tend to read as "dense" and "ground-level" — Hedione lifts them into the air, creating sillage rather than closeness. 750 µL total is the right volume for a spice-oriental.
- **Ethylene Brassylate (280 µL)**: Depth musk at elevated dose. The creamy-lactonic character deepens the benzoin-vanilla warmth and synergizes with Gamma Decalactone for a full lactonic foundation. The slightly-thick quality of EB at 280 µL gives the spicy composition a satisfying "weight" — like a heavy silk scarf, not a cotton shirt.
- **Romandolide (140 µL)**: Projection musk — diffusive woody-musk that extends the spice character outward. Chosen over Habanolide (too intimate for a spice-oriental that should project) and over Galaxolide (too synthetic-laundry, would clash with the natural spice character). Romandolide's slight woody facet aligns with the cedarwood backbone.
- **Musk Ketone (10% in DPG)**: Character-echo musk — 120 µL of 10% = 12 µL active. The ONLY musk that reinforces immortelle's powdery-maple character through the drydown. As the spice notes fade and the benzoin-vanilla base remains, Musk Ketone's talcum-powder sweetness echoes the memory of the opening's golden warmth.
- **Bergamot FCF (350 µL) + Red Mandarin EO (60 µL)**: Dual-citrus golden opening. Bergamot provides the classic bright-spicy top — essential contrast against the warm base. Red Mandarin adds sweet tangerine warmth that foreshadows the oriental base — the citrus equivalent of immortelle's honey. Chosen over Cedrat (too sharp-mineral for this warm composition) and Grapefruit (too bitter-clean — this is golden, not pink).
- **Cedarwood EO (150 µL) + Iso E Super (500 µL) + Cashmeran (90 µL)**: Woody backbone. Cedarwood provides natural cedar warmth — the "spice boxes" that the materials travel in. Iso E Super provides the molecular cocoon — volume and skin-adhesion. Cashmeran adds textile-warmth at moderate dose — the "wool cloak" effect that wraps the spices. Together they create a warm-woody vessel for the spice heart.
- **Rhodinol ex Citronella (50 µL) + Geraniol (40 µL) + Phenethyl Alcohol (80 µL)**: Rose-geranium heart trace. Not enough to read as "floral" — just enough rose complexity to prevent the spices from reading as purely culinary. These function as the "rose petals scattered at the spice merchant's stall" — a subtle floral dimension that elevates the composition from craft to art.
- **Ebanol (50 µL) + Orivone (50 µL)**: Creamy-buttery texture layer. Ebanol adds creamy sandalwood softness — the silk lining of the spice route. Orivone adds warm orris-butter richness. Together they make the base feel luxurious without introducing powder (which would conflict with Musk Ketone's powder axis — orris is buttery, not powdery).
- **Benzyl Salicylate (180 µL) + Hexyl Salicylate (120 µL)**: Dual salicylate fixative gradient. Benzyl Salicylate provides the cosmetic-warm cushion that anchors the spice trail — the "boudoir" quality that makes this a perfume, not a kitchen. Hexyl Salicylate provides the light transparent film for the spice top notes — adhesion without cosmetic weight.
- **Damascenone (1%)**: 20 µL of 1% = 0.2 µL active. Microscopic rose-ketone — adds the barest whisper of dried-fruit depth that enriches the Benzoin-Vanillin axis. Subliminal at this dose — you won't identify damascenone, but the base will feel "complete" in a way it wouldn't without it.
