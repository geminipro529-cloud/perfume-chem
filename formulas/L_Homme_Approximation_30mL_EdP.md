---

## Perfumer Philosophy

**Anne Flipo**: "I like to keep it simple. My style is abstract but always very finished."
**Dominique Ropion**: "A perfume must always be explicit." Architectural precision, balancing excessive doses of powerful ingredients with meticulously measured subtler accords.
**Pierre Wargnye**: "His affinity for woods, especially patchouli, cedar, vetiver, and sandalwood." Clean aromatic openings → rich woody bases.

**Key insight**: L'Homme is a transparent woody-amber disguised as a fresh citrus. The citrus opening burns off in 15 minutes, revealing the real composition: Iso E Super + Cedarwood + Vetiver + Alpha-Isomethyl Ionone.

## Perfumer Analysis

**Character**: Woody Floral Musk. Not a "freshie" despite the citrus opening.

**Opening (0-5min)**: Limonene + Citral + Linalool burst — bright, citrusy, clean. Ginger adds warmth without sweetness. Calone provides ozonic freshness. Total vapor: ~27 Pa.

**Heart (30min-2hr)**: Alpha-Isomethyl Ionone + Hedione + Iso E Super — violet-iris-molecular. Geraniol provides floral body. Violet leaf adds green-ozonic quality. Basil and white pepper add subtle spice.

**Drydown (2hr-4hr+)**: Cedarwood + Vetiver + Tonka + Galaxolide — woody-amber-musk. "Tonka bean is subtle and nutty, vanilla cookies." This is the real composition — the citrus was just a decoy.

**Sillage & Diffusion**: Intimate skin scent. Only Limonene, Citral, and Linalool project strongly, and they burn off in 15 minutes. After that, Hedione + Iso E Super + Galaxolide provide molecular diffusion at arm's length.

**Longevity**: 5-7 hours on skin. Base materials (VP < 0.15 Pa) persist. Projection moderate for first 3 hours, then intimate.

**Balance**: Transparent woody-amber architecture. The "transparent" quality comes from Hedione + Iso E Super creating a molecular cocoon that makes the woods feel airy rather than heavy.

## Instructions

1. Add ethanol to 30mL bottle: 24 mL
2. Add concentrate materials in order listed
3. Shake gently to mix
4. Let mature 2-4 weeks minimum
5. Test spray on strip and skin
