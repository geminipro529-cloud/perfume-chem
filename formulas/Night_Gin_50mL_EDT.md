## PROCEDURE

1. **Pre-flight.** Label 60 mL amber bottle: *Night Gin — 50 mL EDT — 2026-05-10*. Clean pipette tips. Verify all materials against inventory.
2. **Dose Base** (1→7). Swirl after each addition. Iso E Super first — the canvas. Evernyl at 80 µL — deliberate chypre bite. Coumarin 20% — no pre-dissolve needed, it's a free-flowing liquid.
3. **Dose Heart** (8→12). Juniper + Cardamom — swirl, smell: should read gin-and-tonic, not pine-cleaner. Vetiver — swirl. Black Pepper last.
4. **Dose Top** (13→14) — ≤30 seconds. Cap immediately.
5. **Add ethanol** (15) in three portions (~15 mL each). Swirl 15s between. Cloudiness clears as DPG-based dilutions dissolve.
6. **Macerate** 4 weeks minimum, 6 preferred. Dark, 18–22°C. Cold-filter at 4°C after maceration if hazy.
7. **Spray:** 5 sprays = EDP-level skin dose.

## LESSONS FROM THIS BUILD

- **No embedded OAV tables in formula markdown.** The parser reads them as dosing tables and corrupts analysis.
- **Alias every material variant.** Jasmine Sambac Abs, Cedrat FCF, Vetiver EO (India) — all needed explicit entries.
- **Nitriles at >0.3% of concentrate kill perception.** Lemonile at 1% blocked juniper and grapefruit. Avoid entirely or dose at trace.
- **Concentration % ≠ longevity.** Ambrofix + coumarin + vetiver + musks at 7.4% outlasts citrus-hedione at 20%. The base determines wear time.
- **Juniper at #1 was the goal from the start.** It took 5 formula revisions, 4 OAV re-runs, and a nitrile disaster to get there. Worth it.
