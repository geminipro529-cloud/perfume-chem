## Procedure — Layer D: Complete with ethanol

1. Pour ethanol 96% to the **30 mL** fill line.
2. Concentrate is ~4,651 µL (51 materials); add approximately **25.3 mL ethanol**.
3. Cap tightly. Invert **50 times** — gently.
4. Label: **"Bleu Carbon — [date] — macerate 4 weeks — open after [date+4 weeks]"**

---

## Maceration

Store cool and dark. Invert gently once per week.
**Minimum 4 weeks.** Ambrofix crystals, incense, sandalwood chord, and orris need time.

## Notes

- **Ambrofix neat crystals** — weigh 210 mg on balance. Do not attempt to pipette solid crystals. Dissolve directly in the concentrate; the ethanol addition will complete dissolution.
- **No Vetiver EO** — removed from inventory. Vetival (suede-vetiver) replaces it at 20 µL.
- **No Myrrh EO** — not in inventory. Olibanum boosted to 130 µL.
- **Coumarin at 52 µL (20%)** — precisely at the family drift minimum (0.204% active). Structural, not perceptible as tonka.
- **IFRA allergens:** Coumarin, Geraniol — standard EU label declaration. Zero violations.
- **Geraniol oxidation:** the only gate failure. Add 0.05% BHT if available, or reduce Geraniol to 80 µL to clear the chemistry stability gate.
