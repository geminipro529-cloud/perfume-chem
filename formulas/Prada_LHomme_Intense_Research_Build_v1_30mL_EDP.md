# Prada L'Homme Intense — inventory interpretation v1, 30 mL EDP trial

Date: 2 October 2026. Status: PERSONAL_RESEARCH_DESIGN; physically unmade and unsmelled.
This is a new from-scratch interpretation, not Prada's recovered proprietary formula or a measured clone.

## Brief and quantity boundary

Powdery iris first, then warm roasted-tonka/amber, patchouli and sandalwood; clean floral air and a restrained suede texture rather than a dark leather perfume. Prada's official architecture supports iris, amber, patchouli and tonka, with sandalwood and roasted tonka also listed. Suede and the specific raw materials below are formulation hypotheses, not identified Prada ingredients. [Official product](https://www.prada.com/ww/en/p/lhomme-prada-intense-edp-100-ml/2A1252_2HC1_F0Z99_P_ML100), [Pradasphere](https://www.prada.com/ww/en/pradasphere/fragrances/la-femme-et-l-homme-prada/la-femme-et-l-homme-prada-intense.html).

- Final measured volume: 30.0 mL.
- Liquid-stock concentrate total: 6,000 µL, including carriers already in working stocks.
- Separate weighed solids: 120 mg Ambrox Super crystals and 20 mg Heliotropal/Piperonal.
- Carrier: 96% perfumery ethanol q.s. to measured final volume.
- Nominal liquid-stock charge: 20% of final volume. This is not a demonstrated 20% neat-aromatic or w/w composition.
- No mass and volume are added together. No exact 24 mL ethanol addition is prescribed.
- Lineage: ROOT_DESIGN, no immediate parent. Earlier Prada drafts remain untouched.

## Mixing procedure

1. In a clean glass mixing vessel, add approximately 4 mL of the ethanol allocation. Weigh the two solids separately and dissolve them in this solvent precharge with gentle agitation at room temperature. If either remains undissolved, stop and resolve dissolution; do not bottle a heterogeneous mixture as a completed trial. No flame or improvised heating.
2. Add liquid stocks in the table's basket order. Mix gently between baskets. Record the actual dose only if it differs; no photograph or formal receipt is needed for this personal design trial.
3. Transfer and rinse into a suitably calibrated final vessel. Add ethanol gradually to a measured 30.0 mL after the mixture has equilibrated to room temperature. Dissolved solids, carrier composition and volume contraction mean the ethanol addition is not exactly 24.0 mL.
4. Mix, close and label with design ID PRADA-LHOMME-INTENSE-RESEARCH-BUILD-V1-20261002 and date. Check clarity again after resting. A practical first blotter check can be made after 48 hours, then repeated after 1–2 weeks; these waiting periods are trial conventions, not established optimum maturation.
5. Initially compare on blotters: opening, 30 minutes, 2 hours and 4 hours. If an authentic Intense sample is available, evaluate it alongside this trial at comparable application. Skin suitability and commercial safety have not been established.

## What to smell for

Working hypotheses, not simulated sensory findings:
- Opening: a little citrus/cool spice over clean floral air, without turning into citrus cologne.
- Heart: a clear powdery iris-violet accord; avoid coarse woody earthiness or lipstick-like sweetness.
- Drydown: coumarinic tonka, rounded amber and sandalwood, with patchouli present but not dominating and suede kept in the background.

Do not modify several blocks before the first observation. The useful short report is: iris clarity, sweetness, suede prominence, patchouli prominence, and whether the amber feels smooth or scratchy. No untested additive delta is committed here.

## Selection decisions

The iris construction uses AIMI for body, dihydro beta ionone for woody continuity, alpha ionone for a separate floral contour, and a small Orivone accent. Alpha Irone and Orris Liquid are omitted from this design; their presence or absence in the commercial Prada product is not established. Depleted Gamma Coeur, Polysantol, unowned Kephalis and banned/depleted legacy musks are not substituted by name.

One musk, Ethylene Brassylate, is used deliberately for powder-soft cushioning. Sandalore supplies sandalwood body and the smaller Ebanol dose adds a distinct persistent contour. Natural patchouli gives the recognizable earthy facet; Clearwood supplies a cleaner amber-patchouli extension. Javanol/Amber Xtreme/Norlimbanol were not selected because this first hypothesis should not be built around high-impact woody dryness. Tonka absolute and coumarin serve different jobs; the light vanillin/benzoin/labdanum/piperonal accents are rounding and warmth, not a gourmand replacement for iris.
