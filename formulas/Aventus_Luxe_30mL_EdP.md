### TARGET / IDEAL

One continuous layered object, not a pineapple soliflore:

1. **Overture:** Sicilian bergamot and lemon over a distinct blackcurrant-leaf/cassis counterpoint; pineapple reads as a short green-fruit transition, never the subject.
2. **Head into heart:** black pepper, cardamom and juniper connect the citrus head into a transparent Hedione/Iso E Super interior.
3. **Interior:** a restrained luxe floral layer — jasmine, Rose de Mai, osmanthus, orris, ionones, rose oil, geranium, a jasmine ketone trace — sits inside Hedione radiance rather than on top of it.
4. **Heart into base:** patchouli, olibanum, cypress and Evernyl propose the smoked-mossy return; cedar, vetiver, sandalwoods and Vertofix carry the woody spine.
5. **Base:** Ambrox mineral ambergris, a four-musk macrocyclic chord, cashmeran texture, coumarin/tonka/ethyl vanillin sweetness, salicylate fixatives.

**Deliberate exclusions under live authority** (stock/rule constraints, not aesthetic rejections): Birch Tar Rectified (IFRA-prohibited stock), Oakmoss Absolute and Labdanum 10% (non-executable holds), Suederal 10% and IBQ 10% (non-executable holds), Pink Pepper EO (pinned inventory gap; Black Pepper EO used), Cedrat FCF (gap), Ylang and Dihydrojasmone (unresolved), Cassis Base 345B (unresolved), Cade Oil (absent from pinned authority), Guaiacwood EO / Myrrh EO / Sandalwood EO (missing natural composite or threshold authority). Smoke, suede and moss functions are mapped non-equivalently to cypress + olibanum + patchouli + Evernyl; sandalwood is carried by Sandalore, Ebanol and Javanol instead of the unavailable Sandalwood EO.

## Design notes

- **Hedione 19.4% and Iso E Super 11.8%** match published Aventus platform proportions; Hedione is the largest single material, as in the reference.
- **Pineapple is ~1.2% active** across AAG, Dynascone, ethyl 2-methylbutyrate, ethyl maltol, melonal, paradisamide and lactone, plus the blackcurrant absolute. It should read as a transition, not a fruit theme — the main correction over the older 5.5% Pineapple Chypre Luxe.
- **Ambrox active is 4.2%** — inside the 3-5% target, constrained by the 25% w/w stock; the reference's higher level is not reachable without neat Ambrox.
- **Musk architecture is four materials, not five**: Habanolide (diffusion), Ethylene Brassylate (clean persistence), Ambrettolide (skin warmth), Galaxolide (volume), with Cashmeran as texture rather than a fifth musk.
- **Moss and smoke are mapped non-equivalently**: Evernyl crystals at 5 µL, patchouli, cypress and olibanum stand in for the excluded oakmoss/birch functions. Expect a cleaner, less tarry result than the reference.
- **Blackcurrant absolute** is the highest-headspace material in the build; if the opening reads sulfurous or dominates, trim it, not the bergamot.
- **Romandolide** is physically owned and user-confirmed neat on 2026-09-11, but the pinned V5 authority still holds the restocked bottle, so it is excluded from the gated table. If an authority overlay is cut later, use it as a partial swap for Galaxolide/Ethylene Brassylate, not an added fifth musk.

## Mixing protocol

1. Confirm every bottle label, strength and basis against `inventory.txt` and the pinned current-stock authority before transferring anything.
2. Combine Baskets 1–4 in one clean vessel, swirling gently between additions. These form the transparent platform.
3. Add Basket 5 (cassis/pineapple) and Basket 6 (florals) and swirl. These are the character and luxe layers.
4. Add Basket 7 (chypre/smoke), then Basket 8 (woods), then Basket 9 (sweet fixatives), swirling after each basket.
5. The 8 µL AAG, 5 µL Evernyl and 5 µL cis-Jasmone transfers require a verified pipette or prepared dilutions; do not free-drop micro-volumes.
6. Add 24.0 mL ethanol 96% to the concentrate, seal, and macerate in the dark 2–4 weeks. Chill-filter if haze appears, then bottle.
7. Do not substitute neat Suederal for the 10% stock (it is not in this build), and do not add undocumented carriers.

## Adjustment knobs

- **Too fruity / pineapple forward:** cut AAG to 5 µL and blackcurrant to 240 µL before lowering Dynascone.
- **Not fruity enough after 24 h:** raise Dynascone in 10 µL steps; keep AAG below 15 µL to avoid laundry-green.
- **Smoke too dry or coniferous:** lower cypress first, then olibanum; keep patchouli fixed.
- **Moss reads thin:** raise Evernyl in 2 µL steps (mass-preferred); there is no oakmoss stock to substitute.
- **Woods too flat:** raise Vertofix 10–15 µL or Ebanol 10 µL before adding another wood, and check the Sandalore/Javanol balance.
- **Sweetness creeping forward:** reduce Ethyl Vanillin first, then tonka; leave coumarin for the chypre spine.
- **Longevity short:** confirm Hedione timing before adding fixatives; the platform already carries the drydown.
