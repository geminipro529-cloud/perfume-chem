## DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Jasmine and orris are **layers inside a dry-wood concert hall**, not floating above a musky base. The orris accord reads as **waxy-metallic-cool iris concrete** (not cosmetic powder) thanks to 7 ionone materials + Myristic Acid H-bond softening. Musk Ketone provides the **powder-echo axis** that distinguishes iris perfumes from violet perfumes.

## PROCEDURE

1. **Pre-dilute:** Beta Ionone 1%, Musk Ketone 10%, Myristic Acid 1% (all stock solutions ready).
2. **Label beaker:** *Jasmin d'Orris — Opus Woods v2 — 30 mL — YYYY-MM-DD*.
3. **Dose Base — Woods** (#1-9). Swirl after each 3 additions.
4. **Dose Base — Incense** (#10-11). Olibanum is viscous — warm bottle if needed.
5. **Dose Base — Musk** (#12-14). Musk Ketone 10% may be cloudy — normal for DPG suspension.
6. **Dose Heart — Jasmine** (#15-22). Swirl after #18 (cis-Jasmone), after #22 (Indole).
7. **Dose Heart — Orris** (#23-30). Swirl after each 4 materials.
8. **Dose Heart — Rose** (#31-34). Trace only.
9. **Add Myristic Acid 1%** (#42). Swirl gently — this is 396 µL ethanol + 4 µL MA.
10. **Add Hexyl Salicylate** (#41). Swirl.
11. **Dose Top** (#35-40) quickly — ≤60 seconds. Cap immediately.
12. **Add ethanol** in 3 portions (~7.4 mL each). Swirl 15s between.
13. **Macerate** 6 weeks minimum. Peak at 90-120 days.
