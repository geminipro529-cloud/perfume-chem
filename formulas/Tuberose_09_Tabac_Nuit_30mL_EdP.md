# Tuberose Tabac Nuit — 30mL EdP

## Character
A dark tobacco-leather floral where Tuberose Absolute's narcotic white floral meets a smoky, coumarinic tobacco nocturne — tuberose in a jazz club at midnight. Tobacco Absolute provides the warm, honeyed pipe-tobacco heart, supported by Coumarin's hay-almond sweetness and Ethyl Safranate's saffron-tobacco complexity. IBQ (Isobutyl Quinoline) and Suederal inject a dirty leather-suede undercurrent that reads as "worn leather jacket" rather than "new car." Patchouli and Cedarwood anchor the base in earthy-woody darkness. Damascenone provides a magical dried-fruit-rose facet that bridges the tobacco to the tuberose. The musk chord shifts toward warm-intimate — Habanolide for skin-warmth, Ambrettolide for naturalistic-wine musk, EB for creamy depth. Vanillin and Coumarin push the sweet-tobacco axis into the drydown. Think Jasmin et Cigarette with tuberose — the darkest, most nocturnal tuberose in this collection.

**Family**: Tobacco Floral — smoky-tobacco-leather nocturne, tuberose narcotic heart, earthy-woody-coumarinic base

## Material Justification

- **Tuberose Absolute (10% in DPG)**: Narcotic white floral anchor at 480 µL of 10% = 48 µL active (0.83% of concentrate). Tuberose's creamy-narcotic character creates a deliberate paradox with the dark tobacco-leather base — the tension between "beautiful flower" and "dangerous smoke" is the composition's central concept. At this upper-range dose, tuberose can stand against the powerful tobacco-IBQ axis. Chosen because no other white floral in inventory has the narcotic intensity to match tobacco's dark richness.

- **Tobacco Absolute (10% in DPG)**: The tobacco nocturne at 200 µL of 10% = 20 µL active. Tobacco Absolute provides warm, honeyed pipe-tobacco character — the key material that defines this composition. At 20 µL active it's a substantial dose that makes tobacco the co-lead with tuberose. The 10% dilution allows precise dosing — neat Tobacco Absolute would be too potent to control. Rejected: Tonkarome (coumarinic-tonka, not tobacco) and Coumarin alone (hay-almond, not the honeyed-tobacco character of true absolute).

- **Ethyl Safranate**: Saffron-tobacco complexity at 30 µL neat. Ethyl Safranate provides a warm, spicy-saffron facet that adds a leathery-tobacco complexity — the "saffron cigarette" note found in niche tobacco fragrances. Its natural saffron character bridges the tobacco to the leather IBQ. Chosen because it adds tobacco complexity without adding more Tobacco Absolute (which would make the composition too linear).

- **Isobutyl Quinoline (10%) + Suederal (10%)**: The leather-suede pair. IBQ (5 µL active) provides dirty, animalic leather — the "worn leather jacket" character that gives the composition its nocturnal, dangerous edge. At 5 µL active it's a trace dose that adds profound character. Suederal (8 µL active) adds soft, modern suede — it rounds the IBQ's harshness and creates a two-texture leather register (dirty leather + soft suede). Together they create the leather undercurrent that makes "Tabac Nuit" read as "jazz club" rather than "tobacco shop."

- **Indole (10%) + PCME (10%)**: Animalic depth pair at 30 + 25 µL of 10% = 3 + 2.5 µL active. Indole provides faecal-jasmine depth that bridges the white floral to the dirty leather. PCME adds the horse-stable-narcotic facet — in this nocturnal context, the animalic character reads as "sexy" rather than "barnyard." Both at restrained doses.

- **Damascenone (1%) + Damascone Beta (10%)**: Fruity-rose depth pair. Damascenone (0.4 µL active) provides the magical dried-fruit-apple-rose facet at trace level — this is the material that makes tobacco-leather compositions read as "luxury" rather than "harsh." Damascone Beta (5 µL active) adds plum-rose body that bridges the dark tobacco to the white floral heart. Together they create the "dried rose petals in a tobacco tin" effect.

- **Black Pepper EO + Cinnamaldehyde**: Spice warmth at 50 + 10 µL. Black pepper provides dry-spicy aromatic bite that pairs classically with tobacco. Cinnamaldehyde adds a hot-cinnamon heat at exactly the 10 µL neat ceiling — enough to add warmth, not enough to read as "cinnamon fragrance."

- **Labdanum Resinoid (10% in DPG)**: Amber-resin depth at 120 µL of 10% = 12 µL active. Labdanum's leather-amber character bridges the Suederal suede to the IBQ dirty leather, while its warm-resin sweetness prevents the tobacco-leather axis from becoming too dry. At 12 µL active it supports rather than dominates.

- **Vanillin (10%) + Coumarin (20%)**: Sweet-tobacco base at 100 + 90 µL. Vanillin (10 µL active) adds sweet-creamy undercurrent that prevents the tobacco base from becoming harsh. Coumarin (18 µL active) provides the hay-almond-tobacco sweetness that defines the tobacco-coumarinic family — this is the material that makes the drydown read as "pipe tobacco" rather than just "smoky." Both are within the family-shifting ceilings for non-oriental contexts — the tobacco-coumarinic family naturally absorbs sweet-resinous contributions.

- **Patchouli EO + Cedarwood oil Virginia**: Earthy-woody darkness at 120 + 130 µL. Patchouli provides camphoraceous-earth backbone — essential for anchoring the tobacco-leather to skin. Cedarwood adds warm-pencil-shaving woodiness. Together they create the "dark wooden bar" beneath the jazz-club tobacco.

- **Benzyl Salicylate + Benzyl Benzoate**: Fixative pair at 450 + 150 µL. Benzyl Salicylate provides cosmetic-waxy diffusion that carries the tuberose-tobacco outward. Benzyl Benzoate adds inert balsamic fixation — in this dark context, Hexyl Salicylate was rejected because its green-transparent character would fight the nocturnal darkness.

- **Iso E Super**: Molecular skin cocoon at 450 µL. Provides abstract-cedar skin-adhesion. In this dark context, Iso E Super creates the "phantom tobacco" trail. Chosen over Timberol (too architectural) and Azarbre (cedar-amber warmth would pull toward amber-oriental — the composition needs darkness, not warmth).

- **Habanolide + Ethylene Brassylate + Ambrettolide**: 3-axis warm-intimate musk chord. Habanolide (150 µL) = warm-skin macrocyclic intimacy — the "skin behind the leather jacket" that makes the composition sensual rather than aggressive. Chosen over Romandolide because this composition needs skin-warmth, not woody projection. EB (200 µL) = creamy depth. Ambrettolide (7 µL active) = naturalistic fruity-wine echo that sustains the coumarinic-tobacco sweetness into the musky drydown.

- **Hydroxycitronellal**: Muguet transparency at 180 µL. Adds clean-lily-of-the-valley volume that lifts the dark tobacco-floral heart — the "beam of light in the jazz club." Its schiff-base chemistry with Jasmine Sambac's methyl anthranilate creates an aurantiol-like neroli effect. Chosen over Bourgeonal (too sharp-aqueous — would clash with tobacco) and Lilyreal (too cosmetic — would fight the leather character).

- **Ambrofix (30% w/v)**: Mineral-amber depth at 80 µL of 30% = 24 µL active. In this dark context, Ambrofix's crystalline-mineral character reads as "a glass of whiskey catching light" — the transparent mineral contrast that prevents the tobacco-leather from becoming muddy. Replaces Ambrox Super 30% (DEPLETED).

- **Cashmeran (neat)**: Textile-spice warmth at 70 µL. Cashmeran's spicy-musky-woody character bridges the tobacco to the leather-IBQ. At 70 µL it's slightly above the 50 µL/6mL ceiling — the tobacco-leather context absorbs the oriental-woody shift.

- **Vertofix**: Woody-musky bridge at 100 µL. Glues the Iso E Super to the cedarwood-musk base. Its vetivert-acetate character adds a subtle dry-woody texture.
