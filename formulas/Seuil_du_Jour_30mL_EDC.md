# Seuil du Jour — 30 mL Eau de Cologne Moderne — v3 Classique

**Batch:** 30.00 mL · ~16.7% EdC · **18 materials** (5 EOs/naturals)
**Profile:** Dior Homme Cologne radical minimalism × the invisible luxury layer DHC omits
**Target consumer:** The DHC buyer who wants the same transparency but with a "what IS that?"
quality — the difference between clean and polished.
**Maceration:** 3 weeks minimum

---

## What "Classy" Means in This Formula

v2 (13 materials) was clean, correct, minimal. But clean is not the same as classy.
Classy means:

1. **Sparkle you don't notice** — Aldehyde C10 at 0.0008% active. Below identification threshold.
   Creates champagne effervescence at the opening without smelling "aldehydic."
2. **Warmth without a source** — Oranger Crystals at 0.012% active. Not neroli, not orange blossom —
   just a glow behind the grapefruit blossom.
3. **The rose halo** — Damascenone at 0.0005% active. Not rose. Just the $400-fragrance halo.
   This is what Dior, Chanel, and Hermes put in their expensive fresh fragrances that their
   cheaper flankers omit. It's what makes you go "this smells expensive" without knowing why.
4. **Skin warmth that reads as YOU** — Ambrettolide, the most naturalistic musk in inventory,
   replaces the Ebanol/Coumarin Edition Blanche reference. Instead of borrowing another fragrance's
   creaminess, it creates its own.
5. **The ghost of orris** — Alpha Irone at 0.005% active. Below identification threshold.
   The classiest 10 microlitres you can add to any formula. Ellena, Sheldrake, Kurkdjian all use
   this trick — a whisper of iris that no one can name but everyone can feel.
6. **Superior radiance** — Hedione HC replaces some standard Hedione for cleaner jasmonate bloom.

## Accord Architecture

### Layer 1 — Refined Citrus Sparkle (Top, ~29% of concentrate)
Still bergamot-dominant (22% of concentrate) — one citrus defines the opening, DHC-style.
Bergamot FCF Sicilian is the premium grade. Linalyl Acetate extends bergamot freshness.
Cardamom EO provides micro-aromatic lift so the opening doesn't read as "citrus cleaner."
Aldehyde C10 at 1% dilution adds champagne effervescence — invisible, untraceable, essential.

### Layer 2 — Luminous Blossom Heart (Heart, ~40% of concentrate)
Petitgrain EO + Paradisamide remains the grapefruit blossom core — DHC's signature register.
Oranger Crystals at 60 µL of 10% dilution adds a neroli glow behind the blossom — not a second
floral note, just warmth. Damascenone at 25 µL of 1% dilution creates the expensive-rose halo
that DHC lacks. Hedione + Hedione HC provide jasmonate radiance at two clarity levels — standard
for volume, high-cis for brilliance. Ethyl Linalool bridges citrus-floral into the base with
woody-floral tenacity.

### Layer 3 — Polished Skin Musk (Base, ~31% of concentrate)
Romandolide (projection) + Ethylene Brassylate (depth) + Ambrettolide (natural warmth) —
a 3-material musk chord where each operates on a different axis. Ambrettolide is the key
classiness upgrade: it replaces v2's Ebanol + Coumarin Edition Blanche reference with a
naturalistic skin warmth that belongs to THIS fragrance, not to Allure Homme Edition Blanche.
Iso E Super at 8% is the molecular skin cocoon. Ambrofix at 3% dilution provides crystalline
mineral transparency. Ebanol at 0.6% is a whisper — sandalwood as texture, not as a note.
Alpha Irone at 10 µL of 30% dilution (0.06% active in concentrate) is the ghost of orris —
the "what IS that?" at the threshold of perception.

## Maceration

Store cool and dark. Invert gently once per week.
**Minimum 3 weeks.** Longer than v2 because Damascenone (Schiff base formation with Hedione),
Alpha Irone, and Ambrettolide need integration time. The bergamot softens at 1 week.
The blossom heart blooms at 2 weeks. The iris-musk base reaches full integration at 3 weeks.

## Material Selection — Classiness Justifications

### Why Aldehyde C10 at 0.0008% active
This is the single most impactful classiness upgrade. At this dose, Aldehyde C10 is imperceptible
as a "note" — it doesn't smell like Chanel No. 5 or classical aldehydic fragrances. It functions
as a pure volatility enhancer: it lifts the bergamot opening into a champagne effervescence that
reads as "polished" rather than "raw citrus." This is a standard trick in luxury fresh fragrances
(MFK Aqua Universalis, Hermes Eau de Citron Noir, Byredo Bal d'Afrique). None of these are
"aldehydic" — they just sparkle.

### Why Oranger Crystals
DHC's grapefruit blossom is beautiful but one-dimensional. Oranger Crystals at 0.012% active adds
a neroli glow BEHIND the blossom — warmth without a second identifiable floral note. Think of this
as backlighting the grapefruit blossom rather than adding a flower. Aurantiol was rejected — too
honeyed, would read as oriental rather than transparent. Neroli EO not in inventory; Oranger Crystals
is the closest neroli-orange-blossom material available and at this dose it contributes texture only.

### Why Damascenone at 0.0005% active
This is the single most class-signaling trace material in perfumery. Damascenone (and its relatives
Beta Damascone, Alpha Damascone, Damascol) create what perfumers call the "rose ketone halo" —
a narcotic floral transparency that makes everything smell more expensive. Used in virtually every
luxury fresh fragrance at doses below 0.001% active. Beta Damascone was considered (slightly
fruitier, more apple-rose) but Damascenone was chosen for its narcotic depth — it reads as
"expensive rose absolute" rather than "fruity floral."

### Why Ambrettolide replaces Ebanol + Coumarin
v2 used Ebanol + Coumarin to reference Allure Homme Edition Blanche's sandalwood-tonka creaminess.
This was a borrowed reference — the formula smelled expensive because it reminded the wearer of
another expensive fragrance. Ambrettolide creates its OWN naturalistic skin warmth. It's the most
luxurious musk in inventory — macrocyclic, fruity-winey, warm without being animalic. At 0.12%
active in concentrate, it creates a "your skin but better" effect that is more classy than
referencing Edition Blanche. Ebanol stays at a reduced dose (0.6% vs 1.4%) as pure texture.

### Why Alpha Irone at 0.005% active
The ultimate Ellena trick. Alpha Irone at the threshold of perception creates an "orris whisper"
that nobody can identify but everybody interprets as "expensive." Used this way in Terre d'Hermes
(iris at trace), Dior Homme (the original — iris as character, not as note), and virtually every
Frederic Malle fresh fragrance. At 10 µL of 30% dilution, this is 0.006% active in concentrate —
below the identification threshold for orris (~0.01% is where people start to detect it) but
sufficient to create the "what IS that?" effect.

### Why Hedione HC added
v2 used 950 µL of standard Hedione. v3 splits this into 1050 µL standard + 100 µL HC. HC (high-cis)
has approximately 8× the radiance of standard Hedione. The additional 100 µL of HC creates superior
jasmonate bloom without increasing volume. This is a premium-but-invisible upgrade — the fragrance
simply radiates better, without the wearer knowing why.

### What was deliberately excluded from v3
- **Coumarin**: removed. The Edition Blanche reference is now internal (Ambrettolide + micro-Ebanol) rather than borrowed.
- **Muguet materials**: same as v2. DHC has no muguet.
- **Additional citruses**: same as v2. One citrus = DHC signature.
- **Additional woods**: same as v2. DHC has no wood character.
- **Scentenal, Calone, Floralozone**: marine/ozone contradict DHC transparency.
- **Galaxolide, Tonalide, Zenolide**: polycyclic musks contradict naturalistic luxury.
- **Habanolide**: warm-skin intimacy is now served by Ambrettolide, which is more naturalistic.
- **Exaltolide**: depleted. Would have been a candidate for additional fatty-skin depth but Ambrettolide covers this register.
- **DBCA, Aurantiol, ACA**: these are white-floral cosmetics that contradict DHC minimalism. Oranger Crystals at micro-dose provides warmth without the cosmetic register.
- **Alpha Damascone, Beta Damascone, Damascol**: the rose-ketone slot is filled by Damascenone at a more narcotic, less fruity register.
- **Benzyl Salicylate, Hexyl Salicylate**: unnecessary fixatives in an 18-material formula. Musk + IES + Ambrofix handle adhesion.
