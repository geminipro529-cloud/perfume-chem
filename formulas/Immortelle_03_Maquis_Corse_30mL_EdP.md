# Immortelle Maquis Corse — 30mL EdP

## Character
A bright-aromatic composition evoking the Corsican maquis — the wild Mediterranean scrubland where immortelle (Helichrysum italicum) grows freely among rosemary, juniper, and pine. Immortelle Absolute's curry-honey-maple warmth acts as the golden heart modulator, bridging the camphoraceous-herbal top notes (Rosemary, Juniper Berry, Lavender) to a dry labdanum-oakmoss base. The composition reads as sun-warmed herbs on dry earth — not green-fresh, but aromatic-golden, like walking through the maquis at late afternoon. Think *Goutal Eau d'Hadrien* meets a herbal *L'Occitane Immortelle de Corse* — more aromatic than citrus, more wild than polished.

**Family**: Aromatic Herbal — rosemary-lavender-juniper aromatic top over immortelle-labdanum heart, oakmoss-patchouli-cedar base

## Material Justification

- **Immortelle Absolute (10%)**: The golden heart modulator — 180 µL of 10% = 18 µL active (0.3% of concentrate). Its curry-honey-maple character is the olfactory signature of Corsican maquis. At modifier level, it sits at the heart, bridging the aromatic-herbal top to the labdanum-oakmoss base. Kept at this dose because the maquis is about herbs first, immortelle second — a landscape, not a soliflore.
- **Rosemary EO (60 µL) + Juniper Berry EO (50 µL) + Pine EO (30 µL)**: The maquis aromatic triad. Rosemary provides the dominant camphoraceous-herbal character — the wild rosemary that carpets Corsican hillsides. Juniper Berry adds gin-like coniferous clarity with a green-spicy edge. Pine EO contributes the terpenic pine-needle lift that completes the "scrubland" impression. All three at moderate doses — they define the top but shouldn't overwhelm the immortelle heart. Rejected: Beta-Pinene (too one-dimensional compared to the complex Pine EO).
- **Lavender EO BONTAUX (80 µL) + Spike Lavender EO (40 µL)**: Dual lavender — BONTAUX provides the honeyed-floral lavender sweetness that echoes immortelle's own honey facet. Spike Lavender adds the sharper, camphoraceous-aromatic edge that connects the lavender to the rosemary-juniper axis. Together they create a wild, uncultivated lavender character — the lavender of Corsican hillsides, not Provençal fields.
- **Clary Sage EO (40 µL)**: Herbal-aromatic bridge between the citrus top and the herbal heart. Drier and more tea-like than lavender, it smooths the transition from bergamot-citrus freshness to rosemary-camphor depth.
- **Bergamot FCF (350 µL) + Cedrat FCF Sicilian (120 µL)**: Dual-citrus opening. Standard Bergamot provides the classic citrus brightness. Cedrat adds bitter citron edge — sharper and more mineral, evoking the dry, rocky soils of Corsica rather than lush orchards. Chosen over Blood Orange (too juicy for this dry-aromatic composition).
- **Galbanum EO (8 µL)**: Trace green bomb — just enough to add a sharp-green resinous crunch at the top that reads as "crushed wild herbs." Kept at micro-dose because Galbanum easily dominates. This is the olfactory equivalent of snapping a rosemary twig.
- **Labdanum Resinoid (10%)**: 150 µL of 10% = 15 µL active. The resinous heart of the maquis — Corsica is one of the world's primary labdanum-producing regions. Labdanum provides the warm, animalic-amber glue between the herbal top and the oakmoss base. Its natural leather-amber complexity mirrors the wild, untamed character of the maquis.
- **Coumarin (20%)**: 80 µL of 20% = 16 µL active. Hay-sweet tonka character that pairs with immortelle (proven synergy) and softens the camphoraceous herbal notes. Not enough to tilt toward fougère or gourmand — just enough to add a sun-warmed-grass sweetness.
- **Oakmoss Absolute (10%) + Evernyl (50%)**: Chypre-moss layer. Oakmoss at 20 µL active provides the bitter-lichen character — the dark forest-floor undertone of the maquis. Evernyl at 75 µL active adds synthetic mossy brightness and body. Both at restrained doses — this is an aromatic, not a chypre, but the moss is essential for the "earth" beneath the herbs.
- **Patchouli EO (120 µL) + Clearwood (80 µL)**: Dual patchouli. Patchouli EO provides the dark, camphoraceous-earthy base — the rich soil of the maquis. Clearwood adds a cleaner, more modern patchouli character without the heavy camphor — bridges the patchouli to the Iso E Super backbone.
- **Cedarwood oil Virginia (250 µL)**: Natural cedar identity — the dominant wood of the maquis. Virginia cedarwood provides the warm, slightly pencil-shaving character that anchors the aromatic herbs. Higher dose than usual to provide the structural wood backbone. Chosen over Cedarwood EO (generic) and Atlas cedar (not in inventory).
- **Iso E Super (600 µL)**: Molecular cocoon — volume and skin-adhesion without identifiable wood character. Creates the "landscape" effect — not smelling specific woods, but feeling surrounded by them. Standard dose for a modern aromatic.
- **Vertofix (150 µL) + Azarbre (80 µL) + Koavone (60 µL) + Cedramber (80 µL)**: Warm-cedar amber layer. Vertofix bridges wood to musk. Azarbre adds cedar-amber warmth (warmer than Ambrox, chosen because we want warmth, not mineral sparkle). Koavone supports the cedar accord. Cedramber bridges cedar and amber registers. Together they create a warm-woody bed beneath the aromatic herbs — the golden afternoon glow of the maquis.
- **Ambrofix (30% w/v)**: 70 µL of 30% = 21 µL active. Clean amber sparkle at moderate dose — adds lift to the mossy-earthy base without dominating. The crystalline mineral effect creates a sense of "clear mountain air" above the maquis.
- **Ebanol (40 µL)**: Creamy sandalwood accent — adds a subtle creamy-soft texture that prevents the aromatic-woody base from becoming too dry and scratchy. A small touch of silk in the rough wool of the maquis.
- **Romandolide (160 µL)**: Projection musk — diffusive woody-musk that extends the herbal-aromatic signature outward. Chosen over Galaxolide (too laundry-detergent for a wild landscape) and over Habanolide (too warm-intimate — the maquis should project, not embrace).
- **Zenolide (100 µL)**: Character-echo musk — the cleanest, most citrusy-fresh musk in inventory. Echoes the herbal-camphoraceous brightness of the maquis top notes at the skin level. Without Zenolide, the drydown would read as generic woods; with it, a ghost of rosemary-juniper persists.
- **Ethylene Brassylate (180 µL)**: Depth musk — creamy lactonic skin-sit that anchors the highly volatile aromatic top notes. The lactonic quality softens the camphoraceous rosemary-lavender without making it "creamy" — just enough to prevent harshness.
- **Hexyl Salicylate (150 µL) + Benzyl Benzoate (120 µL)**: Dual fixative. Hexyl Salicylate provides a light, green-floral transparent fixative for the herbal top notes — adhesion without cosmetic weight. Benzyl Benzoate provides invisible molecular-weight fixation — pure anchoring without any character contribution. Chosen over Benzyl Salicylate (too cosmetic-warm for this wild-aromatic composition).
- **Geranium EO (50 µL) + Rhodinol ex Citronella (40 µL)**: Rose-geranium heart trace. Geranium provides the fresh rosy-green-minty lift that is a classic companion to lavender and rosemary. Rhodinol adds natural geranium-rose richness. Together they add a subtle floral dimension that prevents the aromatic herbs from becoming purely culinary.
