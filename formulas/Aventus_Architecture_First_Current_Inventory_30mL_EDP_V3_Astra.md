## TARGET / IDEAL

The primary subject is dry woody-musky continuity with a green-citrus head, a restrained floral interior, and a smoky-leathery patchouli return. Pineapple is a subordinate transition, not a fruit theme. Intended relationships, all untested:

1. **Opening:** bergamot with a distinct blackcurrant-leaf/cassis counterpoint; lemon supplies a smaller edge. The absolute used below is not the proprietary reference leaf accord.
2. **Head into heart:** a small pineapple accent, pink pepper, and bitter petitgrain connect the opening to floral and woody material; no separate aromatic-cologne theme.
3. **Interior:** Hedione supplies transparent jasmine-citrus space; a restrained Dihydrojasmone dose proposes more definite jasmine-fruity body. One hexyl-salicylate layer proposes a slower green-floral connection. Their joint value is a testable hypothesis, not an established synergy.
4. **Heart into base:** cedar and patchouli provide identifiable wood and earth; Kephalis proposes a tobacco-amber connection to suede. Iso E Super supplies the abstract wood framework, Ambrox mineral contrast, and Romandolide a single musky continuity layer. Cashmeran is a small texture modifier, not another main woody block.
5. **Base:** smoke, suede, moss, wood, earth, mineral amber, and musk are intended interacting facets, not seven guaranteed separately perceptible layers. Avoid a generic amberwood cloud, opaque floral body, sugary fruit, or an overt leather perfume.

The reference birch function remains in TARGET / IDEAL. The current build excludes Birch Tar Rectified because the inventory marks that stock prohibited. Cade plus Suederal is a non-equivalent smoke/suede mapping, not birch tar. Neat *Schinus molle* EO is the authoritative owned pepper; the older EO/CO₂ requirement remains a separate GAP.

## Why these relationships, and what was rejected

- **Bergamot, cassis, lemon, petitgrain, AAG, pepper:** bergamot and blackcurrant are chosen by the target, not as defaults. Lemon retains the reference citrus edge. Petitgrain provides a smaller leaf/twig hypothesis rather than stacking added linalyl acetate and coriander onto the same transition. Grapefruit, cedrat, mandarin, orange, lavender, and a second pineapple material are omitted because their extra character is not needed by this brief. Too much cassis can become sulfurous/catty; AAG can become green-laundry or fruit-led. These are risks, not measured outcomes.
- **Hedione and Dihydrojasmone:** supplier descriptions distinguish transparent jasmine/citrus diffusion from more specific floral-jasmine/fruity/minty character. Dihydrojasmone is reduced from V2 rather than treated as equal-volume floral reinforcement. Predicted omission loss is reduced identifiable interior body with Hedione continuity retained. Failure mode is a persistent floral theme instead of a woody chypre. Natural jasmine, Jessemal, indole, and muguet alternatives would introduce more literal flower, fatty, animalic, or different floral character; none is needed solely to raise complexity.
- **One salicylate:** retain hexyl salicylate's proposed green-floral connection; omit benzyl salicylate's additional cosmetic/waxy register. The two are not declared interchangeable. Predicted omission loss is less continuity after the bright head. Failure mode is a generic floral film or no detectable benefit. Benzyl benzoate is not inserted as a silent replacement. No rule requiring two fixatives overrides target-linked nonredundancy.
- **Wood, earth, mineral, and musk:** Iso E Super supplies abstract continuity while Virginia cedar and patchouli supply character hypotheses that it cannot simply stand in for. Ambrox supplies mineral contrast; warmer Ambermax, forceful Norlimbanol/Amber Xtreme, creamy sandalwoods, and a further generic amberwood are not selected. Romandolide is the sole musk for its documented delicate musky/drydown-volume character, not because it is the 'best projection musk'. Habanolide, Zenolide, ethylene brassylate, Galaxolide, and the diluted macrocyclic stocks are alternatives, not automatic additions. Musk overlap is not needed to meet a count target.
- **Kephalis, Cashmeran, Vertofix:** retain Kephalis for a proposed tobacco-amber bridge; reduce Cashmeran to a small spicy-texture modifier. Omit Vertofix because this draft does not need another cedar-vetiver/musky bridge alongside cedar, Iso E Super, patchouli, Kephalis, and musk. This is a design decision, not proof Vertofix is inaudible from its historical OAV. Predicted Kephalis omission loss is a weaker floral-to-dark-wood connection; failure mode is tobacco/amber taking over.
- **Smoke, suede, moss:** retain Cade and Suederal unchanged so a missing Cade model is not 'repaired' by increasing smoke. Evernyl is a smaller moss contour, not a new dominant classical-chypre requirement. Guaiacol, IBQ, tobacco absolute, labdanum, and oakmoss would change the proposed effect or bring additional data/stock questions; none is inserted to obtain a marker PASS. Actual smoke/suede effectiveness remains an open hypothesis.
