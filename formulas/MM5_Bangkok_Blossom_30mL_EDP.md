**5. Sillage & Diffusion** — OAV by family: floral 42%, aromatic 25%, citrus 13%, woody 13%. Hedione is the primary projection carrier.

**6. Longevity** — 10% evap over 4h. Vapor 81.2 → 53.0 ppm. Hedione grows 18670 → 21779 over time.

**7. Balance** — T 15.6% / H 49.1% / B 35.3%. Block ratio 7:1. Sigma-log 1.52. 18/20 materials perceptible.
