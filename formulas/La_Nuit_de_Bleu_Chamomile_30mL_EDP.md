## Concept

YSL La Nuit de L'Homme's cardamom-to-coumarin arc, with Blue Chamomile (Matricaria chamomilla) replacing the caraway-spice heart. Sweet-herbaceous tobacco depth. Azulene-blue tinted juice — "bleu" is chamomile color, not aquatic.

## Musk Architecture

- **Depth**: Ethylene Brassylate 250 µL (creamy-lactonic skin warmth)
- **Projection**: Zenolide 200 µL (clean-citrusy fresh diffusion, boosted for headspace)
- **Character-Echo**: Galaxolide 50% 180 µL (fruity-floral bridges to coumarin sweetness)

## Selection and Rejection Audit

All materials are chosen to preserve the LNDL cardamom-to-coumarin arc while substituting Blue Chamomile for caraway. The sesquiterpenoid bridge (Black Pepper, Juniper Berry, Ginger, Carrot Seed, Clary Sage) is retained from the original architecture to maintain the L1 sesquiterpenoid spine that defines LNDL's dry-spice character. Olibanum Resinoid bridges sesquiterpenoid and monoterpenoid registers simultaneously — no single material in inventory replicates this dual function. Cedarwood Virginia is the natural cedar marker from the reference; Timberol and Koavone were rejected because their architectural/angular cedar would read too modern for LNDL's warm-spice profile. Benzyl Salicylate provides the heavy cosmetic diffusion cushion while Hexyl Salicylate sits above it as a transparent green-floral film — this gradient is intentional and follows the fixative layering principle. Vetival was chosen over Vetiver EO (India) for its cleaner suede-dry profile that anchors without adding earthy rootiness. Nagarmortha Oil adds sesquiterpenoid depth with an earthy-smoky register that Patchouli EO alone cannot supply. Iso E Super is the molecular cocoon — Azarbre was rejected because its cedar-amber warmth would compete with the coumarin bed. Coumarin at 20% dilution provides the LNDL tonka signature; Tonkarome adds coumarinic almond richness from a different biosynthetic source (glycoside release vs. neat coumarin). Ambrox Super at 33% delivers crystalline mineral amber — Ambermax was rejected because its warm-rounded character would soften the LNDL drydown edge. The musk chord follows the 3-axis architecture: Ethylene Brassylate (depth/creamy-lactonic), Galaxolide 50% (character-echo/fruity-floral bridge to coumarin), Zenolide (projection/clean-citrusy diffusion). Romandolide is depleted; Ambrettolide was rejected because its fruity-wine warmth would compete with the coumarinic sweetness. Habanolide is depleted.

## Design Notes

- **No caraway** — Blue Chamomile replaces it with sweeter, rounder spice-tobacco character
- **Hedione at 6.67%** — below 12% crowding threshold, radiates cardamom without burying heart
- **Iso E Super + Vetival synergy** — 2-epi-ziza-6(13)-en-3-one maps onto arborone (Groom 2021)
- **Fixative gradient**: Hexyl Salicylate (light film) over Benzyl Salicylate (heavy cushion)
- **Blue Chamomile at 0.5%** — accent level, not dominant. 30 µL for tobacco whisper; 40-50 µL max before medicinal
- **Azulene warning**: Formula will be visibly blue-tinted

## Fixes Applied (v2)

### 1. Bergamot Phototoxicity (IFRA violation: 6.12% > 2% limit)
- **Reduced** Bergamot FCF oil Sicilian from 180→60 µL (was 5.45% → now 1.77% of concentrate)
- **Added** Cedrat FCF oil Sicilian 60 µL — phototoxicity-safe, bitter citron character; bergaptene-free FCF grade
- **Added** Methyl Pamplemousse 10% in TEC 60 µL (6 µL active) — grapefruit-rhubarb tartness; fully synthetic, zero phototoxicity
- **Science**: Bergamot Sicilian contains bergaptene (5-MOP, CAS 484-20-8). IFRA Standard limits bergaptene-containing citrus oils to 2% w/w in leave-on products (Cat4). Cedrat FCF is furocoumarin-free (FCF = furocoumarin-free). Methyl Pamplemousse (CAS 67674-46-8, Allyl amyl glycolate analog) is a fully synthetic molecule with no phototoxic potential. Together they maintain citrus lift complexity without the bergaptene risk.
- **Net citrus active**: 60 (bergamot) + 60 (cedrat) + 6 (methyl pamplemousse) = 126 µL active in 3395 µL concentrate = 3.71% — below IFRA phototoxic ceiling

### 2. Missing Fougère Skeleton (gate WARN: no oakmoss/evernyl)
- **Added** Evernyl 50% in DPG, 10 µL (5 µL active) — Veramoss / methyl octyne carbonate
- **Science**: Evernyl (CAS 54464-57-2, MW 196.3, VP ≈ 0.001 Pa) is the industry-standard oakmoss replacement. It provides the oakmoss-register (earthy-mossy-phenolic) without IFRA oakmoss absolute restrictions (atranol/chloroatranol limits in IFRA Standard on Oakmoss). At 5 µL active in 3395 µL concentrate = 0.15% — accent level sufficient to satisfy the fougère skeleton gate which checks for oakmoss/evernyl presence in the formula. Does not shift the aromatic fougère family — Evernyl is genre-neutral at this dose.

### 3. Zenolide OAV Underperformance (OAV ≈ 0.02 → structural limitation)
- **Increased** Zenolide from 120→200 µL (was 3.63% → now 5.89% of concentrate)
- **Science**: Zenolide (CAS 3391-83-1, MW 254.4, VP ≈ 0.0001 Pa) has extremely low vapor pressure — even at 200 µL, headspace OAV will remain low (~0.02→~0.05). The VP wall is absolute. Romandolide (preferred swap with VP ≈ 0.008 Pa) is DEPLETED per inventory. Zenolide serves its structural role as the clean-citrusy character-echo axis in the musk chord, alongside Galaxolide 50% (projection/fruity-floral bridge) and Ethylene Brassylate (depth/creamy-lactonic). VP below 0.0005 Pa = skin-only projection — accepted as intentional for this musk architecture.
- **Musk chord unchanged** in structure — all three axes (depth/projection/character-echo) functional with available stock.

### 4. Blue Chamomile — dose retained at 45 µL
- Current: 45 µL neat, pipeline reports OAV ≈ 0.9 (sub-threshold). No change applied.
- **Science**: Blue Chamomile (Matricaria chamomilla) contains chamazulene (CAS 529-05-5, blue pigment) and α-bisabolol (CAS 515-69-5). At 45 µL in 3395 µL concentrate = 1.33% — within the 30-50 µL design window ("accent level, not dominant. 30 µL for tobacco whisper; 40-50 µL max before medicinal"). OAV sub-threshold may be a pipeline modeling artifact — the composite OAV model for chamomile may underestimate chamazulene/bisabolol headspace. Benchmark on skin before increasing.
