# Rose Fougère Rosier — 30mL EdP

## Character
A rose-centric fougère where the classical lavender-coumarin-oakmoss fougère structure is rebuilt around Rose de Mai Absolute as the primary floral character rather than the traditional geranium. The fougère skeleton remains intact (Lavender EO BONTAUX for floral-herbaceous top, Coumarin for hay-like warmth, Oakmoss Absolute for bitter-green lichen depth), but the rose heart replaces the usual geranium-lavender axis. Geranium EO is included at a bridging dose (60 µL) to connect the lavender to the rose — geranium's rosy-green-minty character is the natural translator between the two worlds. The rose core uses the full 5-material accord (Citronellol, Geraniol, PEA, Rhodinol, Nerol) at constrained doses. Terpinyl Acetate provides pine-citrus-herbal coniferous lift in the top, reinforcing the fougère's aromatic architecture. Azarbre anchors the base with cedar-amber warmth — warmer than Ambrofix's crystalline edge, it creates the rounded warmth that makes this fougère feel modern rather than barbershop. The musk chord (Romandolide projection, Galaxolide clean projection for fougère structure, Habanolide gentle skin-warmth) keeps the composition structured and fresh. Think a rose through the lens of a modern fougère — or what would happen if you replaced the geranium in a classical aromatic fougère with a complete rose accord.

**Family**: Fougère Floral (rose-fougère) — lavender-citrus top, rose-geranium heart, coumarin-oakmoss-musk base

## Material Justification

- **Rose de Mai Absolute (10%)**: The natural rose anchor at 380 µL (38 µL active, 0.65% of concentrate). A fougère typically uses Geranium EO as its sole floral-rose element, so introducing Rose de Mai Absolute is the concept's entire premise — replacing abstract rose-geranium with actual rose. The absolute's honeyed-spicy warmth provides the organic rose presence that distinguishes this from a standard aromatic fougère.

- **Citronellol + Geraniol + PEA + Rhodinol + Nerol**: The full 5-material rose accord at constrained doses. Citronellol (270) provides fresh-citrus-rose lift — the most fougère-compatible rose alcohol because its fresh-citrus character pairs with the bergamot-lavender top. Geraniol (60) at maximum — sweet-geranium body, bridging the rose to the Geranium EO. PEA (350) fills honeyed-rose volume. Rhodinol (30) at maximum — essential for the fougère because Rhodinol's natural geranium-rose richness translates between the rose accord and the geranium-lavender axis. Nerol (80) adds sweet citrusy-rose lift. This is the only rose formula where Rhodinol is functionally essential rather than optional.

- **Geranium EO**: The fougère bridge at 60 µL — the material that connects the rose heart to the lavender top. Geranium EO (Pelargonium graveolens) naturally shares olfactory territory with both rose (via geraniol and citronellol) and lavender (via its minty-herbaceous linalool content). At 60 µL, it provides the functional translation layer without overwhelming the rose — in a standard fougère, Geranium EO would be dosed at 100-200 µL. Reduced to 60 µL here because the rose accord handles the floral work.

- **Damascone Beta (10%) + Rose Oxide (10%)**: The rose modifier pair at 80 + 60 µL (8 + 6 µL active). Damascone Beta provides dark fruity-rose ketone — the plum-apple facet that adds richness to the rose accord. Rose Oxide adds metallic-green geranium leaf — the facet that connects the rose to the geranium-lavender greenness. No Damascenone because its honeyed-woody depth would pull the fougère toward oriental territory.

- **Hedione**: Radiance amplifier at 500 µL — standard Hedione chosen because the softer jasmonate character pairs better with the aromatic lavender-coumarin structure than bright HC. Lifts the rose heart into the lavender-bergamot top, creating the luminous transparency that distinguishes a modern floral-fougère from a barbershop classic.

- **Lavender EO BONTAUX**: The fougère top's aromatic foundation at 200 µL — chosen because this premium French Lavandula angustifolia has a floral-herbaceous character with soft honeyed sweetness that pairs more gracefully with rose than the camphoraceous Spike Lavender or the simpler standard Lavender EO. The BONTAUX lot's refined floral character makes it the only lavender that can sit alongside a rose accord without fighting.

- **Terpinyl Acetate + Beta-Pinene**: The coniferous-aromatic lift at 100 + 60 µL. Terpinyl Acetate provides pine-citrus-herbal lift — the coniferous brightness that defines the fougère's aromatic top architecture. Beta-Pinene adds the green-pine terpene freshness — the natural pine needle character that extends the lavender's aromatic register into the coniferous realm. Chosen over Pine EO (too complex — competes with the rose-lavender axis) and Spike Lavender EO (too camphoraceous — would dominate).

- **Coumarin (20%) + Oakmoss Absolute (10%) + Evernyl (50%)**: The fougère base trinity. Coumarin at 200 µL of 20% (40 µL active) provides the hay-like coumarinic warmth — the defining material of the fougère family. Within safe limits even for non-oriental contexts (40 µL active at ~20% dilution = well within the ~32 µL active ceiling for a 5.8 mL concentrate). Oakmoss Absolute at 180 µL of 10% (18 µL active) provides bitter-green lichen character — the soul of any fougère. Evernyl at 120 µL of 50% (60 µL active) extends the moss tenacity beyond natural oakmoss — at IFRA-compliant dose.

- **Patchouli EO**: The earthy depth at 100 µL — patchouli's camphoraceous-earth character adds a masculine dimension to the fougère base. Dosed modestly because the fougère's identity comes from the coumarin-oakmoss axis, not patchouli.

- **Azarbre + Vertofix + Cedarwood EO + Iso E Super + Ambrofix**: The woody base. Azarbre (180 µL) is the star — its cedar-amber-warm register is the ideal fougère woody material: warmer than Ambrofix (no crystalline edge), softer than Timberol (too rigid for fougère), warmer than Iso E Super (which is too abstract). Azarbre provides the rounded warmth that makes this fougère feel modern and luxurious rather than 1980s barbershop. Vertofix (180) bridges wood and musk. Cedarwood EO (140) provides natural woody identity. Iso E Super (380) creates the molecular skin-cocoon. Ambrofix (100 µL, 30 active) adds crystalline depth — the cold mineral point that lifts the warm coumarin-azarbre base.

- **Benzyl Salicylate + Benzyl Benzoate**: Fixative pair. Benzyl Salicylate (220) as the cosmetic cushion — diffuses the rose-lavender heart. Chosen over Hexyl Salicylate because a fougère benefits from the heavier, more cosmetic salicylate character — Hexyl Sal would be too transparent for the structured fougère architecture. Benzyl Benzoate (220) as inert mass.

- **Romandolide**: Projection musk at 260 µL — clean, diffusive woody-musk. The best projection musk for a fougère because its woody character resonates with the cedar-vertifix structure.

- **Galaxolide (50% in DEP)**: Character-echo musk at 280 µL of 50% (140 µL active). In a fougère, the clean-laundry musk is not just acceptable — it is traditional. Galaxolide's synthetic-clean-fruity character is the musk that defines the modern fougère's fresh-shower-gel projection. Chosen at 50% dilution per inventory rather than neat for more controlled dosing.

- **Habanolide**: Warm skin-intimacy at 140 µL — the warmth that humanizes the structured fougère architecture. Essential because without a warm-skin musk, the coumarin-oakmoss base can read as cold and impersonal.

- **Bergamot FCF + Linalool + Linalyl Acetate**: The classical fougère citrus top at 400 + 200 + 80 µL. Bergamot FCF provides the classical cologne freshness — the traditional fougère opening. Linalool adds fresh-lavender lift (linalool is the primary constituent of lavender). Linalyl Acetate extends bergamot persistence into the lavender heart.
