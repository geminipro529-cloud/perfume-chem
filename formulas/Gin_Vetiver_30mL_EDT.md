## PROCEDURE

1. Bottle has steps 1–9 already. Swirl.
2. Add Grapefruit FCF (10). Swirl.
3. Add Bergamot FCF (11). Swirl.
4. Add Vetiver EO India (12). Swirl.
5. Top ethanol 96% to 30 mL mark. Cap. Shake 30s.
6. Macerate 2 weeks minimum, dark, 18–22°C.
7. Label: **"Gin Vétiver — 2026-05-10 — 13.4% EDT"**

## PERFUMER'S ASSESSMENT — 7/10

Strong gin-tonic concept. Citrus opening is bright and correct. Vetiver at 667 OAV is the right volume for an earth-echo — present but not the star. The ceiling is locked at 7 by the original Iso E Super overdose (20,000 OAV). Without it, this is an 8. With it, it's still a very good EDT.
