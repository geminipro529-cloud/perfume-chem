# Cuir Obscur Luxe — 30 mL Parfum

**Dior Homme Parfum reconstruction (François Demachy, 2014).**  
**Family:** Leather · Woody Floral  
**Concentration:** ~20% Parfum · 6,000 µL concentrate in 30 mL  
**Formula Code (reference):** 08642/A — unchanged for 10 years  
**Character:** Dark, scorched leather over burnt iris, Turkish rose heart, smoky vanilla-incense drydown — the "bad boy" Dior Homme.

## The Dior Allergen Label — What We're Matching

Official #08642/A label, descending concentration:

> **ALPHA-ISOMETHYL IONONE** · LINALOOL · LIMONENE · COUMARIN · CITRONELLOL · EUGENOL · GERANIOL · BENZYL BENZOATE · FARNESOL · CITRAL · CINNAMAL · BENZYL CINNAMATE

**Key signals**: Ionone leads (#1 — iris dominates chemically). Eugenol = real rose absolute. Coumarin #4 (tonka present but less than DHI where coumarin is #3). Benzyl Benzoate = Demachy's fixative. Farnesol #9 = deliberate muguet/lily accord (IFRA-restricted — not a byproduct). Limonene #3 = substantial orange-led citrus top (Dior: "Italian orange").

## The Demachy Construction Method

DHP inverts DHI's structure: **leather-first, iris as servant, rose replacing lavender, bitter chocolate replacing sweet cocoa.**

| DHI (Polge/Demachy) | DHP (Demachy) |
|---|---|
| Lavender aromatic top | **No lavender** — rose absolute replaces it |
| Iris = star, lipstick, creamy | **Iris = servant to leather, burnt, dry** |
| Pear + Ambrette heart | **Turkish Rose Absolute** (eugenol on label) |
| Sweet milk chocolate + vanilla | **Bitter dark chocolate** (guaiacol smoke + restrained patchouli) |
| Frankincense only | **Frankincense + Labdanum** (darker incense) |
| Standard patchouli | **Patchouli + Guaiacol** (phenolic smoke) |
| No oud | **Nagarmortha (cypriol)** as budget-oud bridge |
| Galaxolide-heavy musk | **Galaxolide + macrocyclic musk chord** |
| Iso E ~8% | **Iso E ~30%** — the Féminité du Bois skeleton |
| No muguet | **Farnesol + Mayol** (allergen #9, IFRA-restricted lily accord) |

## Dosing Notes

- **Iso E Super at 1,800 µL (30%)**: THE architectural skeleton. The Shiseido Féminité du Bois GCMS — the ancestral template Demachy built DHP from — shows Iso E Super at 45.5%. At 30%, this is the molecular cocoon that makes the leather-iris float. Do NOT reduce — below 1,200 µL the holographic scaffold collapses and you lose the "what are you wearing" Demachy effect.
- **IBQ 10% at 380 µL**: 38 µL active = 0.63%. Commercial-realistic dose (archetype minimum: 0.74% raw volume = 5.7% of concentrate as 10% dilution → 342 µL). At this dose: dark, smoky quinoline leather that dominates perceptually without overwhelming. The niche-amplified 550 µL (0.92% active) was a deliberate reinterpretation — this is the reconstruction dose.
- **Birch Tar Rectified at 6 µL**: TRACE at 0.1% — IFRA-realistic. The "pyrogenic Russian leather char." Below 4 µL: no scorched effect. Above 8 µL: campfire. Dior's own words confirm birch tar as the leather material.
- **Galaxolide 50% at 540 µL**: The FdB skeleton at 21.5% Galaxolide confirms this is DHP's musk backbone. At 4.5% active with macrocyclic counterbalance (EB + Romandolide + Ambrettolide), the musk chord reads as warm-skin, not laundry-detergent. Macrocyclics prevent the polycyclic "clean" signature from dominating.
- **Farnesol at 12 µL**: Allergen #9 — deliberately present. IFRA-restricted muguet/lily fixative. Below 10 µL: imperceptible. Above 20 µL: rancid-waxy at overdose. Synergizes with Mayol + Hedione for the lily dimension.
- **Alpha Isomethyl Ionone at 250 µL**: 4.2% — blended iris. DHP's iris is more integrated than DHI's 17.8% opaque wall. The #1 allergen by concentration, but the leather dominates perceptually.
- **Rose de Mai Absolute 10% at 180 µL**: 18 µL active = 0.3%. The lavender→rose swap is Demachy's defining structural decision. Eugenol on the label from this material.
- **Citrus: Bergamot 80 + Orange 107 + Mandarin 40 = 227 µL**: Drives limonene to the #3 allergen position. Dior's "Italian orange" confirmed. The larger citrus pool creates the recognizable DHP opening.
- **Maturation**: 6 weeks minimum sealed, cool, dark. DHP requires MORE maturation than DHI. The leather (IBQ + birch tar) needs time to integrate with the ionone powder. Testing before 4 weeks: leather dominates and reads harsh. At 6-8 weeks: burnt-iris effect blooms and leather softens into vanilla-amber base. The "smoky vanilla" (vanillin + coumarin + birch tar + guaiacol) requires 8+ weeks to fully integrate — this is the Persolaise Dior Parfum trinity.

## Material Rejections

- **No lavender** — Demachy removed it. Rose absolute replaces it. This IS the DHP structural innovation.
- **No Hedione HC** — period-correct standard Hedione. HC was not standard in 2014. DHP projects via Iso E Super mass (30%), not hedione airiness.
- **No cardamom** — not confirmed in any DHP GCMS or review. The spice character comes from eugenol + cinnamal.
- **No benzyl salicylate** — Demachy's fixative choice was benzyl benzoate. Confirmed on the label.
- **No calone / marine** — DHP has zero aquatic character. Dark, leather, incense.
- **No pear** — DHI's pear note is absent from DHP. The fruity register comes from rose absolute + alpha ionone's raspberry facet.
- **No real oud oil** — Nagarmortha (cypriol) + Ambrettolide create the "oud impression." Real oud at effective doses would cost more than the entire formula.
- **No Iso E Super stinginess** — 30% is the floor, not the ceiling. The FdB skeleton uses 45%. This is not a niche reinterpretation — this IS the Demachy architecture.

## The Burnt Iris Mechanism

What makes DHP's iris "burnt" rather than "lipsticky":

1. **Leather overload**: IBQ + birch tar create a smoky-char layer the ionone powder pushes through. The powder is present but "scorched" by the leather.
2. **Reduced vanillin-coumarin cushion**: DHI uses vanilla-cocoa as a soft bed for the iris. DHP reduces this cushion — the iris sits directly against leather-wood-smoke.
3. **Guaiacol**: Phenolic smoke that darkens every material it touches. The ionone reads as charred violet, not cosmetic powder.
4. **Kephalis**: Woody-amber-tobacco with natural iris-undertone. The knowledge graph confirms this as "the signature DHP iris-leather-amber transition."
5. **Nagarmortha**: Dry, earthy, smoky cypriol. The iris reads as pulled from the earth, not from a makeup bag.
