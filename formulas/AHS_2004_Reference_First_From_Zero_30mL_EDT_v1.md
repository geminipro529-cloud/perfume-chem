## Authority boundary

Chanel's current public description supports a mandarin-to-cedar structure with an aquatic dimension and an almond-tonka/white-musk finish. Its current ingredient disclosure supports several chemical classes used below. It does not expose Chanel's formula, percentages, trade grades, headspace, production batch or reformulation history.

This v1 is a **reconstruction hypothesis**, not a recovered formula and not evidence that it is already an almost-exact copy. No public authenticated quantitative analysis of the original EDT was found in the bounded research. The pipeline has no target-specific AHS reference contract; a generic family screen cannot adjudicate identity.

The as-stock volumes below are the formula definition. For stocks whose percentage basis or solution density is unresolved, the percentage printed on the user's label is preserved without converting it into exact active mass, exact ppm w/w or exact carrier displacement. Do not silently treat every percentage as v/v or assume density = 1.

## TARGET / IDEAL architecture

- **Opening:** recognizable sweet mandarin and peel brightness, polished by linalyl acetate/linalool; brisk and lively rather than lemon-cleaner sharp.
- **Fresh-aquatic connection:** a restrained watery/aldehydic shimmer integrated with the citrus, never a standalone watermelon or laundry accord.
- **Heart:** transparent orange-flower/aromatic volume that lets cedar become perceptible without a hollow break.
- **Wood plane:** smooth woody-amber volume plus an acetyl-cedrene-like dry cedar spine, moderated by real cedar and creamy sandalwood.
- **Drydown:** almond-coumarinic warmth, a small vanilla contour and clean white-musk volume; less dense and less sweet than Eau Extreme.

## Why this is not the old Luxe formula

The historical Allure_Homme_Sport_Luxe_30mL_EdP.md deliberately replaces the proposed AHS structure with a richer three-citrus opening, a five-musk chord, multiple luxury orange-flower materials, amber/resin additions and a denser EdP-style base. It also makes unsupported claims about a Calone-heavy and Galaxolide-dominated original. None of its amounts were copied into this file.

This v1 keeps two citrus oils, one main white-musk body plus one lower-dose diffusion axis, one sandalwood modifier, and no Ambrox Super, benzoin, labdanum, iris accord, pineapple, grapefruit, blood orange, cardamom, luxury resin stack or aggressive amberwood.

## Material decisions and rejected alternatives

- **Red Mandarin + Bergamot:** selected as a two-oil citrus structure. Lemon FCF and Blood Orange were rejected from v1 to prevent a three-oil generic citrus pile and to preserve mandarin as the name-linked leader.
- **Calone 1% + Helional:** separate trace marine cue and floral-water bridge. Floralozone was rejected because adding another ozonic material would make it easier to optimize “aquatic” numerically while drifting into a generic blue accord.
- **Hedione:** used as a transparent bridge, not evidence that Chanel used it. Hedione HC was rejected because its different isomer enrichment would add another unverified grade assumption.
- **Iso E Super + Vertofix + Cedarwood:** three nonredundant wood functions—volume, dry acetyl-cedrene-like spine and natural grain. The exact local Vertofix identity must be reconciled before its modeled OAV is trusted.
- **Sandalore:** selected alone for a controlled creamy sandalwood effect. Bacdanol is owned neat but rejected from v1 because its heavier sandalwood body risks making the sensual plane richer and more tenacious than the EDT target.
- **Galaxolide + Romandolide:** Galaxolide supplies broad clean cushion; the lower-dose Romandolide dose supplies diffusion. Their nonredundancy is a hypothesis requiring omission trials. Tonalide, Macrolide and Musk Ketone remain excluded under project policy and current stock state.
- **Coumarin + Vanillin:** used directly because the current disclosure and Chanel's almond-tonka description support the contour. Tonkarome and Tonka Absolute were rejected so a proprietary base or natural mixture would not obscure the first controlled trial.
- **Ambrox Super:** excluded. The official architecture does not require it, and the owned 25% w/w stock is mass-dose ready but not volume-convertible without stock density.

## Pre-registered sensory comparisons

Do not call v1 close, similar or successful from a pipeline result. Against an authenticated bottle of the original EDT, evaluate coded samples at opening, 5 minutes, 30 minutes, 2 hours and 4 hours. Record identity similarity separately from liking, strength, diffusion and smoothness.

The first diagnostic omissions are:

1. Remove the lower-dose Romandolide axis and replace its raw volume with odorless final-batch diluent; this tests whether it adds a useful trail or merely duplicates Galaxolide.
2. After its carrier is documented, remove the Calone stock with its carrier-matched replacement; this tests whether the aquatic identity needs Calone specifically.
3. Compare the Vertofix-containing build with a constant-total wood control after the local bottle identity and molecular data are resolved.

No skin application or public release is authorized by this design. Supplier restrictions, natural-composite allergens, the actual stock grades and the finished product require a separate safety assessment.
