### Key Design Decisions

| Material | Why | Dose rationale |
|----------|-----|---------------|
| Oranger Crystals (10%) | Neroli proxy — crystalline orange blossom | 400 uL → ~40 uL active — bright but not soapy |
| Iso E Super | The main character — massive transparent woody | 1500 uL (50% of total) — authentic to the style |
| Scentenal (1%) | Metallic-inky note for "dirty banknote" concept | 50 uL → ~0.5 uL active — barely perceptible, structural |
| Indole (10%) | Dead skin / animalic trace on banknotes | 30 uL → ~3 uL — threshold only |
| Cashmeran (20%) | Warm amber texture — bridge between Iso E and vetiver | 200 uL — soft spice |

### Notes vs Target

- **Key notes:** Neroli (Oranger Crystals + Nerolin + Nerol), Iso E Super, Vetiver
- **Concept:** "Vibes: Bank Vault" — clean + dirty

> **Interpretation** — Money is driven by a massive Iso E Super overdose (~50% of concentrate) with neroli-orange blossom brightness and vetiver root anchor. The "dirty banknote" effect is subtle: trace indole for skin residue and Scentenal for metallic ink. Cashmeran warms the transparent Iso E into a more textured woody-amber base. This is deliberately simple — 3 pillars (neroli, Iso E, vetiver) with supporting musk.
