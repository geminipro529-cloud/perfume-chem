### Key Design Decisions

| Material | Why | Dose rationale |
|----------|-----|---------------|
| Methyl Pamplemousse (10%) | Yuzu proxy — grapefruit-pomelo with floral lift | 100 uL → ~10 uL — bright but not sour |
| Floralozone + Verdox | Green tea accord — fresh, ozonic, slightly vegetal | Layered for tea-like transparency |
| Styrallyl Acetate | Green-gardenia note that bridges citrus to tea | 80 uL — subtle fresh-floral sparkle |
| Ambrox Super (33%) | The clean modern base — signature of fresh luxury scents | 500 uL → ~165 uL active — substantial but transparent |

### Notes vs Target

- **Key notes:** Yuzu (Methyl Pamplemousse + Citral), Green Tea Leaves (Verdox + Floralozone + Styrallyl Acetate), Ambroxan (Ambrox Super)
- **Concept:** "Grand Hotel Ginza" — luxury Tokyo hotel welcome

> **Interpretation** — This is a transparent freshie driven by a yuzu-citrus top, a green tea heart built from Floralozone-Verdox-Styrallyl Acetate, and a modern Ambroxan base. The style is similar to Le Labo Thé Noir 29 or Byredo Bal d'Afrique — translucent, modern, sophisticated. No heavy base materials — all airy diffusion and clean transparency. The green-tea accord is the differentiating element vs standard citrus-ambroxan freshies.
