### Key Design Decisions

| Material | Why | Dose rationale |
|----------|-----|---------------|
| Hexyl Acetate (10%) | Pear note from Nude's top; fruity-clean | 150 uL → ~15 uL active — transparent lift |
| Apritone (10%) | Peach nuance; lactonic softness | 100 uL → ~10 uL — traces only |
| Peonile | Clean peony mid; distinctive in Bisch's work | 250 uL — signature peony transparency |
| Galaxolide + Habanolide + Exaltolide | 3-musk chord: projection (Galaxolide), skin (Habanolide), creamy (Exaltolide) | Classic musk stacking |
