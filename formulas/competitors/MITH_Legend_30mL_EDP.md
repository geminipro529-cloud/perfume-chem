### Key Design Decisions

| Material | Why | Dose rationale |
|----------|-----|---------------|
| Hexyl Acetate (10%) + Ethyl 2-Methylbutyrate (0.1%) | Dual apple accord — green apple + ripe apple juice | Layered for realistic apple top |
| Galbanum (10%) | Elegant green note, Morillas signature | 150 uL → ~15 uL — lift, not dominate |
| Isobutyl Quinoline (10%) | Leather listed in base; IBQ is precise leather material | 150 uL → ~15 uL — structural leather trace |
| Olibanum Resinoid | Incense listed in base; neat for precision | 50 uL — sparkle, not church incense |

### Notes vs Target

- **Top:** Apple (Hexyl Acetate + Ethyl 2-Methylbutyrate), Galbanum, Lemon (Lemonile), Pepper (Black Pepper)
- **Heart:** Geranium (Geraniol), Lily-of-the-Valley (Florol), Floral notes (Hedione + Linalool)
- **Base:** Cedarwood, Incense (Olibanum), Patchouli, Benzoin, Leather (Leather FO)

> **Interpretation** — Morillas-style transparent woody aromatic. Apple is achieved via a dual ester accord (Hexyl Acetate for green apple, Ethyl 2-Methylbutyrate for juicy sweetness). The olibanum dose is set to sparkle (top-heart bridge) rather than dominate. Cashmeran at 20% adds a warm spicy-woody texture in the base without overt sweetness. Coumarin adds the sweet-tonka warmth that Morillas often uses as a signature base note.

### Perfumer Analysis

**1. Character** — Modern woody-aromatic with Morillas-style transparent structure. Juicy apple-galbanum top, geranium-muguet heart, cedar-incense-leather base.

**2. Opening (0-5min)** — Massive fruity-citrus blast. Hexyl Acetate (OAV 7399), Linalool (OAV 6916), and Bergamot FCF (OAV 5946) dominate at very strong perceptibility. Apple and citrus drive ~76% OAV. Total vapor 67.8 ppm.

**3. Heart (30min-2hr)** — Linalool remains dominant (OAV 6567) as top notes burn off. Bergamot FCF still strong (5381). Hedione HC (2086) provides jasmonic radiance. Heart emerges as floral-floral bouquet. T/H/B shifts from 76% top OAV to still top-heavy but base begins asserting.

**4. Drydown (2hr-4hr+)** — Base dominates at 63% of headspace. Iso E Super (OAV 4004) leads alongside Linalool (4361). Bergamot FCF persists surprisingly long (2551 at 4h). Leather (Isobutyl Quinoline, OAV 196) and Coumarin (OAV 156) add character. Vapor drops to 29.1 ppm (~57% of opening).

**5. Sillage & Diffusion** — Primary OAV carriers: Hexyl Acetate, Linalool, Bergamot FCF, Iso E Super, Hedione HC. Fruity-aromatic opening gives way to woody-floral drydown.

**6. Longevity** — 10% raw evaporation over 4h. Base persistence at drydown: 63%. Estimated skin life: 6-8h moderate + 2-4h skin scent.

**7. Balance** — Pyramid: T:14.9% H:26.7% B:58.4%. OAV range 0–7399 (sigma-log=1.61). Top notes dominate opening (76% OAV) then burn off to reveal woody base. Heart density (26.7% active, 8.9% OAV) could be strengthened.

**8. Flags** — Olibanum Resinoid (OAV 0.8) and Siam Benzoin (OAV 0.0) are sub-threshold but structurally acceptable (fixatives). ODT coverage: 73% derived/unverified. EU allergens: geraniol, linalool, coumarin.
