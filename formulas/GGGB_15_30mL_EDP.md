# Good Girl Gone Bad — 15 Material Core — 30 mL Eau de Parfum

**Batch:** 30.00 mL · ~18.5% EdP · 15 materials
**Profile:** By Kilian GGGB skeleton — osmanthus-peach top, three-sirens white floral heart (tuberose + jasmine + rose), woody-amber-musky base
**Maceration:** 2 weeks

---

## Procedure

| # | Material | Dilution | µL | Role |
|--:|----------|----------|----:|------|
| 1 | Iso E Super | neat | 1,300 | velvety woody diffusion |
| 2 | Hedione | neat | 1,600 | jasmine radiance |
| 3 | Galaxolide | 50% in DEP | 800 | clean projection musk (400 µL active) |
| 4 | Cedarwood oil Virginia | neat | 120 | cedar backbone |
| 5 | Geraniol | 10% in DPG | 300 | rose character |
| 6 | Rhodinol ex Citronella | neat | 100 | premium rose-geranium, complex rose depth |
| 7 | Benzyl Acetate | neat | 100 | jasmine heart, sweet floral |
| 8 | Coumarin | 20% | 80 | tonka sweetness, floral lift (16 µL active) |
| 9 | Vanillin | 10% | 200 | amber-vanillic warmth (20 µL active) |
| 10 | Ambrettolide | 10% in DPG | 100 | natural skin musk luxury (10 µL active) |
| 11 | Gamma Decalactone | neat | 200 | peach lactone — osmanthus effect |
| 12 | Methyl Anthranilate | neat | 30 | fruity grape-floral, neroli/osmanthus booster |
| 13 | p-Cresyl Methyl Ether | 10% | 200 | tuberose-narcissus — the "gone bad" (20 µL active) |
| 14 | Javanol | neat | 40 | creamy sandalwood — the missing creamy base note |
| 15 | Indole | 10% | 80 | animalic floral depth (8 µL active) |
| 16 | Bergamot FCF oil Sicilian | neat | 300 | sparkling citrus opener — the "good girl" |

**Total concentrate:** 5,550 µL (18.5%)

Top with ethanol to 30 mL. Cap, invert 50 times.
Macerate 2 weeks minimum.
