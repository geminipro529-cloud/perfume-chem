# Osmanthus Incense Cathédrale — 30mL EdP

## Character
A meditative incense-resinous composition where osmanthus absolute provides an unexpected warm-apricot heart inside a cathedral of frankincense, myrrh-like labdanum, and smoky woods. The osmanthus's natural leather-tea facet pairs with the frankincense smoke, while its apricot sweetness cuts through the resinous darkness like stained glass catching light. Think Comme des Garçons Incense series (Avignon/Zagorsk) with a floral-fruity center, or Serge Lutens Ambre Sultan softened by osmanthus.

**Family**: Incense / Resinous — frankincense-smoke, labdanum-amber, osmanthus heart

## Material Justification

- **Frankincense EO (150 µL neat)**: The incense spine — Boswellia carterii/serrata essential oil provides the lemony-smoky-pine cathedral character. Chosen over the depleted Olibanum Resinoid Absolute because Frankincense EO is brighter and more terpenic, which cuts through the heavy base better. The EO's top-note volatility pairs naturally with the bergamot opening.

- **Labdanum Resinoid (10%, 180 µL = 18 µL active)**: The warm-amber undercurrent of every great incense fragrance. At moderate dose, it provides the resinous depth without shifting to full oriental. Kept under the family-shifting ceiling (~20 µL active per 6 mL).

- **Nagarmotha Oil (60 µL)**: Cypriol profile — dry, earthy, smoky-woody. Functions as an incense amplifier and vetiver bridge. Chosen because it adds the dark, almost oud-like smokiness that defines niche incense fragrances, without needing actual oud.

- **Guaiacol (10%, 30 µL = 3 µL active)**: Smoky-phenolic accent at trace dose — adds the "burning resin" character to frankincense. At 3 µL active, it's below the threshold where it reads as BBQ smoke.

- **Cashmeran (150 µL neat)**: Textile-warmth and spicy-musky depth. At this dose in an incense context, it adds the "hot stone" / "ancient temple" sensation. The spicy-musky facets of Cashmeran reinforce both the incense and the musk drydown.

- **Benzoin Sumatra Resinoid (10%, 150 µL = 15 µL active)**: Sweet-balsamic vanilla-almond warmth — the "gold light" in the cathedral. Balances the smoky-dry Nagarmotha and Guaiacol. Well under family-shifting ceiling.

- **Ambermax (50%, 80 µL = 40 µL active)**: Warm, rounded, full-bodied amber — chosen over Ambrofix for this composition because incense needs cozy warmth, not crystalline mineral. The cozy-amber register supports the labdanum-benzoin resin bed.

- **Koavone (60 µL)**: Warm woody, slightly balsamic — supports the cedar-vetiver frame while adding warmth. Chosen over Timberol (too dry/architectural for incense) and over Norlimbanol (too rigid).

- **Musk Ketone (10%, 60 µL)**: Nitro-musk powder warmth — adds a "smoky dust" quality that reinforces the incense atmosphere. The vintage nitro character pairs naturally with frankincense's ancient-temple associations.
