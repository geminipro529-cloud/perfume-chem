### Accord Breakdown (v2 — Fougère spine + Orivone iris)

- **Fougère spine (15% active):** DHM (10.7% — clean lavender fraction) + Evernyl (1.8% — crisp oakmoss) + Coumarin (2.5% — hay-sweet fougère backbone). This IS the structure. The entire formula is built on this skeleton — exactly as Andrier described to Wallpaper*.

- **Neroli-spice opening (19% active):** Nerol (10.7%) + Petitgrain EO (2.9%) + Linalyl Acetate (2.1%) + Linalool (1.4%) + Black Pepper EO (1.8%). Nerol substitutes for missing Neroli EO — softer, more floral-rose, less citrusy-green. Petitgrain adds the green-citrus-woody lift that brings the accord closer to real neroli.

- **Iris-violet heart (4.5% active):** Orivone (2.1% — CORRECTED from 80→60 µL) + Alpha Irone (0.86%) + Beta Ionone (0.71%) + Parmavert (0.21%) + Cyclamen Aldehyde (0.14%) + Farnesol (0.43%). NO alpha-isomethyl ionone. The violet comes from Beta Ionone (soft-powder, below INCI threshold) + Parmavert (green-violet-leaf, aqueous). This is the Prada iris — soapy, powdery, dry-austere — not Dior's lipstick-waxy iris.

- **Geranium floral bridge (~10% active):** Geraniol (0.7% — CORRECTED, was 3.2%) + Citronellol (2.3% — boosted to compensate) + Hedione (10.0%) + PEA (1.2%). The confirmed geranium heart — real Prada uses geranium EO (20-30% geraniol content), not pure geraniol. Citronellol carries the geranium character at a higher dose with lower ODT impact.

- **Woody-amber-musk base (~45% active):** Iso E Super (14.3%) + **Habanolide (17.8%)** + Galaxolide (6.2%) + Ambrox Super (1.6%) + Cedramber (2.5%) + Patchouli EO (1.6%) + Cedarwood EO (2.0%) + Sandalore (1.2%) + Amberwood F (1.2% — boosted). Total musk loading: 24.0%. Habanolide at 500 µL IS the "freshly ironed white shirt" engine. Pipeline shows OAV 0.09 (false positive — VP=0 in pipeline data). On-skin perceptibility is very strong at this dose.

### Dosing & Mixing Notes

1. **Order of addition:** Largest volume first: Habanolide → Iso E Super → DHM → Nerol → Hedione → Galaxolide → Petitgrain → Geraniol → Coumarin → Orivone → Cedramber → Cedarwood → Linalyl Acetate → Evernyl → Black Pepper → Ambrox Super → Citronellol → Sandalore → Linalool → PEA → Alpha Irone → Beta Ionone → Amberwood F → Farnesol → trace materials. Ethanol last.
2. **Coumarin is crystalline:** Weigh 70 mg directly. Pre-dissolve in 1 mL warm ethanol.
3. **Orivone at 60 µL** is the maximum comfortable dose for the Prada iris — OAV will still be ~8,000-10,000 which is very strong but the character is correct. If you want closer to the EDT's quieter iris, reduce to 40 µL.
4. **Habanolide at 500 µL** — this IS the formula's signature. The "freshly ironed white shirt" character scales linearly with Habanolide dose. At 500 µL (17.8% of concentrate), this is a deliberate overdose to push the EdP+ concentration into the right clean-soap register.
5. **Maceration:** 2 weeks minimum. The DHM-Habanolide-Evernyl fougère synergy needs time to integrate. The first 3-5 days the DHM will read as "sharp/chemical" — this softens as the musks bloom.
6. **Violet-leaf transparency:** Parmavert at 6 µL is borderline perceptible in a 10mL bottle. If the green-violet leaf character is too faint, increase to 10-12 µL — but watch the OAV (Parmavert ODT is relatively high, so it won't dominate).
