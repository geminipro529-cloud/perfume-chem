## DESIGN RATIONALE

**Family:** Floral — Jasmine-Orris duet with rose brightness  
**Concept:** Jasmine leads (38% OAV), orris supports (29% OAV), rose brightens (14%), musks fix  
**Jasmine:** cis-Jasmone ketone (10,000 OAV) + Gamma Undecalactone lactonic bridge (2,667) + Sambac authentic touch + Hedione radiance cloud + HC boost (833)  
**Orris:** Alpha Irone (2,000) + Beta Ionone 1% (3,810) for woody-violet depth + Orivone buttery body + AIMI violet sparkle + Ultralia ghost-iris  
**Rose:** Rose Oxide 1% (2,000) for metallic brightness + Alpha Damascone (3,704) for rose-ketone lift  
**Musk chord (3 axes):** Ambrettolide (skin-warmth) + EB (creamy depth) + Cashmeran (textile projection)

## PROCEDURE

1. **Pre-flight.** Label mixing beaker: *Jasmin d'Orris — 30mL — YYYY-MM-DD*. Zero balance. Wipe pipette tips between material families.
2. **Dose Base** (1→6). Swirl after every 2 additions. Expect viscous cloudiness (DPG carriers) until ethanol.
3. **Dose Heart** (7→23). Swirl after each chord:
   - After #14 (jasmine chord complete) — should smell jasminic-radiant
   - After #19 (orris chord complete) — powder-iris emerges
   - After #23 (rose complete) — brightness lifts
   - **VISUAL CHECK:** Pale straw-yellow liquid. If you smell fenugreek/mothball, stop — verify no PCME/Methyl Anthranilate cross-contamination.
4. **Dose Top** (24→25) quickly — ≤45 seconds. Cap immediately. Aroma: bergamot-petitgrain over jasmine-orris heart.
5. **Add ethanol** in 3 portions (~8 mL each). Swirl 15 seconds between portions. Liquid clarifies dramatically.
6. **Transfer** to 30 mL amber atomiser. Leave ≤1 mL headspace.
7. **Macerate** 4 weeks minimum at 18-22°C dark. Evaluate at day 28. Peak at day 60-90.

## TROUBLESHOOTING

| Off-note | Likely cause | Fix |
|----------|--------------|-----|
| Medicinal/fenugreek | Indolic cross-contamination (PCME/Methyl Anthranilate on pipette) | Discard, remake with fresh tips |
| Too woody/dry (overly ionone) | Beta Ionone overdose or undiluted stock used | Verify 1% stock used, not neat |
| No jasmine presence | cis-Jasmone under-dose or Sambac oxidised | Verify #9 and #10 |
| Celery-soup | cis-Jasmone overdose | Verify 150 µL (not 500 µL) |
| Flat after 2 hours | Under-dosed fixatives or musks | Verify #1-6 amounts |
| Chalky-dry | Missing Ultralia or Heliotropal | Verify #19 and #6 |
