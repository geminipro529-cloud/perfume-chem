# Osmanthus Jour Léger — 30mL EdP

## Character
Lighter daytime osmanthus-rose-geranium-iris aromatic. Osmanthus leads at 7.4% of concentrate. Rose de Mai and Geranium EO form the rose escort. Beta Ionone + Methyl Ionone + Alpha Ionone complete the iris floor. Darkness (tobacco + nagar motha + IBQ) present but not dominant — the original formula's heaviness diluted by bright additions. Petitgrain and boosted Bergamot freshen the opening. Freesia HDI adds green breeze. Clean, modern, unisex, wearable.

**Family**: Aromatic Floral (iris-tinged)
**Materials**: 33
**Concentration**: 15.9% EdP
