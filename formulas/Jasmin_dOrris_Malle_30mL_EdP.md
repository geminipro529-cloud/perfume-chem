## HOUSE DNA

Frédéric Malle gives perfumers **unlimited budget, no brief constraints, final creative control**. The result: massive overdoses of key materials (Portrait of a Lady uses 400+ units of patchouli heart per kg of concentrate). Materials are NOT dosed to "balance" — they're dosed to **make a statement**.

**Jasmin d'Orris à la Malle:**
- **Massive Sambac overdose** (800 µL of 10% = 80 µL active) — jasmine IDENTITY, not jasmine "note"
- **Extreme Hedione** (2,000 µL) — radiance that fills an entire room
- **Brutal cis-Jasmone** (250 µL) — unapologetic green jasmine ketone
- **Clean structural base** — Iso E Super cocoon + Ambrofix crystal + Cashmeran
- **No rose** — jasmine-orris is the duet, rose would distract
- **No gourmand** — no Coumarin, no Heliotropal. Structure only.

## DESIGN STATEMENT

This is what happens when you remove budgetary constraints and let the jasmine speak at full volume. 80 µL active Sambac in 30 mL — a dose most commercial houses would cap at 20 µL for cost reasons. The Hedione at 2,000 µL creates a radiance cloud that makes the jasmine project across a room. The cis-Jasmone at 250 µL reads as "green jasmine ketone" with zero apology — it cuts through like Ropion's Portrait of a Lady cuts through with patchouli.

The orris is present but secondary (16% OAV vs jasmine 39%). This is a JASMINE perfume with orris, not a duet.

No rose. No gourmand. No filler. Just jasmine, orris, wood, crystal amber, and structural musks.

**This is what "unlimited creative freedom" smells like.**
