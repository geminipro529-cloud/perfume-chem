# L'Homme Approximation — Data for Sonnet

## Goal
Approximate YSL L'Homme (2006) as a 30mL EdP at 20% (6,000 µL concentrate + 24 mL ethanol).

## Perfumer Philosophy

**Anne Flipo** (IFF Master Perfumer):
> "I like to keep it simple. My style is abstract but always very finished."
> Favorite smells: neroli, basil, jasmine sambac, patchouli, orris.
> Technique: "touches of green and fruity notes to make the floralcy even more addictive."

**Dominique Ropion** (IFF Master Perfumer):
> "A perfume must always be explicit."
> "I create each perfume as if it were for me, which allows me to keep a balance between popular success and aesthetic quality."
> "balancing excessive doses of powerful ingredients with meticulously measured, subtler accords"

**Pierre Wargnye** (IFF, retired 2012):
> "His affinity for woods, especially patchouli, cedar, vetiver, and sandalwood."
> "clean, aromatic openings that resolved into rich woody bases, often with subtle spices or herbs like basil, clary sage, and lavender"

## Key Insight
L'Homme is a transparent woody-amber disguised as a fresh citrus. The citrus opening burns off in 15 minutes, revealing: Iso E Super + Cedarwood + Vetiver + Galaxolide.
