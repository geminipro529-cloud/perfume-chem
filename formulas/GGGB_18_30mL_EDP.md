## Key fix vs prior version

The earlier version had Hedione + Iso E Super + Ethylene Brassylate at 66% of concentrate. That's a woody-musky base with hints of osmanthus. The real GGGB is an osmanthus-tuberose floral with a supportive base. This version flips the ratio: osmanthus block (Gamma Decalactone + Beta Ionone + Linalool + Methyl Anthranilate) at 1,180 µL (21%) and tuberose block (PCME + Methyl Salicylate + Eugenol + Indole) at 190 µL are now the dominant voice. Hedione is reduced to 18% as a radiant support rather than the main material.
