**Batch:** 30.00 mL · ~21.6% EdP · 29 materials

**Profile:** DHC Thailand flanker. Neroli core over a more polished bergamot-lemon-grapefruit brightness, cooled with restrained fresh-air lift, and anchored by a lighter musky-woody base that survives Bangkok heat without becoming a woody skin scent.
