# Osmanthus Sandalwood Crème — 30mL EdP

## Character
Osmanthus apricot-tea floating on a bed of creamy sandalwood. The peach-apricot fruit opens over warm, milky-soft Ebanol and fresh Sandalore. No citrus competition, no leather-darkness, no oakmoss bitterness. Clean, modern, universally pleasant. Think osmanthus meeting Santal 33 in a By Kilian bottle.

**Family**: Woody Floral (modern niche) — sandalwood-anchored, osmanthus-centered
**Materials**: 18
**Concentration**: 16% EdP

## What Was Deliberately Excluded

| Not Used | Why |
|----------|-----|
| Oakmoss Absolute, Patchouli, Evernyl, IBQ, Suederal, Labdanum | No chypre skeleton — this is WOODY floral, not chypre |
| Damascone Beta | Osmanthus + Apritone + Gamma Decalactone already cover apricot. Adding damascone = overdetermined |
| Benzyl Salicylate | Too heavy, cosmetic. Breaks the clean sandalwood transparency |
| Cedarwood EO | Too dry-pencil for creamy sandalwood. Ebanol + Sandalore alone |
