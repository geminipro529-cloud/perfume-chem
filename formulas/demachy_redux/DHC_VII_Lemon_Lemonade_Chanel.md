# DHC VII — Lemon Lemonade (Chanel / Polge)

**Tree:** Lemon (Citrus limon) + Lemon blossom
**Character:** Bright Amalfi lemon, sugared-citrus effervescence, crystalline lily flower.
**Hedione:** 1300uL H + 550uL HC (30% HC) | **Score:** 9.0/10

## What Makes It 'Lemonade'

| Material | Role |
|----------|------|
| Ethyl Maltol 2uL (10% x 20uL) | The sugar. Dior's lemonade trick. Vanishingly low OAV, works via taste/tactile sweetness. |
| Hedione 1300 + HC 550 | Jasmine radiance carries the sweet impression. HC adds natural floral nuance. |
| Linalyl Acetate 160uL | Sweet lavender-bergamot lift. ODT 2.7 = very visible brightness. |
| Lemon blossom (Hydroxycitronellal + Nympheal) | The tree's own white flower. Muguet-lily crystalline. |
| Scentenal 0.02uL | Metallic sparkle = the effervescence. Like tonic water bubbles. |
