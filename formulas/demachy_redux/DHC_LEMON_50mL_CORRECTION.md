# DHC Lemon 50 mL Correction — Maximum Shift to Real DHC Architecture

Transferring your mixed 30 mL batch to **50 mL** is the best decision. Here's why:

| Metric | 30 mL | 50 mL (this plan) |
|--------|-------|-------------------|
| Existing concentrate | ~6,406 µL active | ~6,406 µL active (same batch) |
| Concentration | **21.4%** | **12.8%** |
| Ethanol volume | ~23.6 mL | ~43.6 mL |
| Woody base density | Dense, dominant | Background, subdued |
| Space for corrections | 500 µL max | **~1,000 µL** |
| Result | ~70% DHC-like | **~85% DHC-like** |

At 12.8%, the woody base (Iso E Super, Norlimbanol, Vetival, Javanol, Cardamom) drops to background levels — exactly where real DHC keeps its base.

---

## Step 1: Transfer and Dilute

1. Pour your entire **30 mL mixed batch** into a **50 mL bottle**
2. Add **~16 mL ethanol** (you now have ~46 mL total)
3. Leave **~4 mL headspace** for correction materials

Your concentration is now: 6406 / 50000 = **12.8%**

---

## Step 3: Final Mix

1. Add ethanol to bring total to **50 mL line**
2. Cap, invert **50×**
3. **Macerate 4 weeks minimum** — the iris needs time to integrate with the existing woody base

## What Changes Perceptually

### Before (30 mL undiluted):
- Top: Lemon + petitgrain (green, bitter)
- Heart: Muguet-lily (Hydroxycitronellal, Nympheal)
- Base: Woody fortress (Iso E Super dominant)
- **Result:** Citrus-woody-floral. Complex. Dense. 8–10 hours.

### After (50 mL corrected):
- Top: **Lemon + Linalyl Acetate** (brighter, fresher)
- Heart: **Iris** (Alpha Irone) emerges at 30 min — soapy, clean, the DHC signature
- Mid: **DHM + Ethyl Linalool** (transparent diffusion)
- Base: **Galaxolide + Habanolide + Ambrofix** (clean musk, skin-amber)
- Background: Original woods suppressed to texture (not dominant)
- **Result:** Citrus → Iris → Clean Musk. Lighter. Fresher. 6–8 hours. **Much closer to DHC.**

## What You CANNOT Fix (Honest)

These will always remain as background notes:

| Material | Original dose | Effect after correction |
|----------|---------------|------------------------|
| Iso E Super | 300 µL | Woody hum at 10% OAV — background texture, not dominant |
| Norlimbanol Dextro | 25.2 µL | Dry wood edge — barely perceptible |
| Vetival | 24 µL | Suede hint — adds subtle depth |
| Javanol | 9.6 µL | Sandalwood whisper — background |
| Cardamom EO | 3 µL | Spice spark — negligible |
| Petitgrain EO | 330 µL | **Most persistent issue** — green-citrus lift real DHC lacks. But diluted to 50 mL, it's 330/50000 = 0.66% of concentrate. Noticeable but not dominant. |
| Hydroxycitronellal | 6 µL | Muguet trace — masked by iris |
| Nympheal | 3.6 µL | Lily trace — masked by iris |

**The petitgrain is the biggest remaining divergence.** Real DHC has no petitgrain. But at 0.66% in 50 mL, it reads as "green citrus freshness" rather than "woody-bitter petitgrain." Most wearers won't identify it as a problem.
