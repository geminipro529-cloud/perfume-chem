# DHC III — Grapefruit Transparent (Dior / Demachy)

**House/Perfumer:** Dior / François Demachy
**Philosophy:** Transparency. Orange blossom is the flower. Grapefruit speaks for itself.
**Hedione split:** 1000µL H + 500µL HC (33% high-cis)
**Score:** 9.3/10

## What Demachy Removed (vs Tom Ford)

| Removed | Why |
|----------|-----|
| Paradisamide | Too tropical-exotic. Demachy is Mediterranean, not Caribbean. |
| Methyl Pamplemousse | Let grapefruit be grapefruit. No rhubarb enhancement. |
| HC reduced to 33% | Tom Ford was 29% — Demachy wants MORE natural jasmonate nuance. |

| Added | Why |
|--------|-----|
| Aurantiol 6µL | **Orange blossom is Demachy's signature.** He would never make a citrus without it. |
| Damascenone 0.001µL | The Demachy whisper. You don't smell it, you feel it. Invisible structure. |
| Petitgrain bumped to 750µL | More of the tree. Petitgrain is the green soul of citrus. |
