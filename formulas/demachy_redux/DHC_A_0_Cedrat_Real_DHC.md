# DHC A — Cedrat FCF (Real Dior Homme Cologne Architecture)

**House/Perfumer:** Chanel / Demachy
**Architecture:** Real DHC — Hedione-overdose transparency → cold iris → citrus → clean musk
**Concentration:** ~12% | **Materials:** 13
**Key insight:** DHC's transparency comes from Hedione at 25% of concentrate — it hollows out the structure, creating a deliberate gap that reads as air. No DHM, no aquatics.

## Architecture Note

The formula has a **deliberate hole in the middle.** There is no heavy floral or woody heart. The sequence is:
1. **Citrus peel** (Cedrat + Lemon + Lime) — 1–15 min
2. **Hedione halo** (the air space) — 5 min–3 hr
3. **Cold iris** (Alpha Irone) — 30 min–2 hr
4. **Clean musk** (Galaxolide + Habanolide + Ambrofix) — 1–8 hr

Iso E Super runs underneath everything as a transparent woody diffuser, not a heavy base. The "hollowness" is the feature, not a bug.
