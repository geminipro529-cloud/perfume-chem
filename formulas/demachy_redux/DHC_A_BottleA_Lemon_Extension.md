# DHC A Bottle A — Lemon Extension Additions

**Date:** 2026-05-13
**Base:** DHC A Bottle A (Realistic Lemon Tree), 50 mL
**Goal:** Extend lemon phase from ~15 min to ~1.5 hr without changing perfume identity

## Pre-preparation (24–48 hr ahead)

| Step | What | Instructions |
|:----:|------|-------------|
| A | **Lemonile 1% in DPG** | 0.1 g Lemonile + 9.9 g DPG in a vial. Label. |
| B | **Citral + MA Schiff base** | 100 µL Citral + 100 µL Methyl Anthranilate in a separate vial. Cap. Wait 24–48 hr. Optionally add a few granules of anhydrous MgSO₄. |

## After Addition

1. Cap tightly
2. Invert 50×
3. Wait minimum 72 hr maceration before evaluating
4. If lemon still fades too fast: add another 50 µL of Lemonile 1%
5. If reads too bright/sweet: add 50–100 µL Hedione to rebalance
