# DHC III — Grapefruit + White Jasmine Gardenia (The Pomelo Statement)

**House/Perfumer:** Tom Ford / Rodrigo Flores-Roux
**Architecture:** Demachy Triptych — Massive Citrus → Tree Flower → Woody-Musk

## Key Innovations

1. **Methyl Pamplemousse (3 µL active)** — The grapefruit's own skin. Rhubarb-tart bitterness at meaningful dose. Never used in the other 6 formulas.
2. **DBCA gardenia (5 µL)** — Unexpected white floral. Gardenia-rose bridges jasmine heart to the base. Only formula using this material.
3. **Aldehyde C10 (0.2 µL active)** — Champagne sparkle at the opening. Only this and Blood Orange use aldehydes.
4. **Damascenone + Alpha Damascone** — Both rose-ketones together. Cooked apple + rose-plum depth. Only Blood Orange has similar richness.
5. **Paradisamide doubled (1.2 µL)** — Tropical passionfruit-guaiava. The highest tropical dose of all 7 formulas.
6. **Vetival bumped to 45 µL** — Vetiver-grapefruit is a classic luxury pairing (Terre d'Hermès). Stronger here than in any other formula.
