# DHC Lemon 30 mL Correction — Shift Toward Real DHC Architecture

Your mixed 30 mL batch follows the **DHC Veritas VII Lemon** formula:
- ~6,406 µL active concentrate in 30 mL = **~21% concentration**
- Heavy woody base (Iso E Super, Norlimbanol, Vetival, Javanol, Cardamom)
- Muguet-lily flower accord (Hydroxycitronellal, Nympheal, Scentenal, Floralozone)
- Petitgrain 330 µL
- No iris

**You cannot remove materials from a mixed batch.** But you can shift the balance by adding the missing DHC signature materials.

## Step 1: Dilute (Recommended)

Your batch is at **21%** — much denser than real DHC (~10%). Diluting makes it fresher and reduces woody density.

**Option A — Light dilution (to ~15%):**
- Add **10 mL ethanol** to your 30 mL batch
- New total: 40 mL
- Concentration: 6406/40000 = **16%**

**Option B — Full DHC dilution (to ~12%):**
- Add **20 mL ethanol** to your 30 mL batch
- New total: 50 mL
- Concentration: 6406/50000 = **12.8%**

> **Recommendation:** Start with Option A (10 mL). You can always dilute further.

Add these materials **directly to your diluted batch**:

### Technique:
1. Pipette each material directly into your 30 mL (or 40 mL diluted) batch
2. Cap, invert **50×**
3. **Macerate 2–4 weeks minimum** — the correction needs time to integrate with the existing woody base

**Before correction:** Citrus-jasmine-woods. Dense, complex, 8–10 hour longevity.

**After correction:** Citrus-iris-musk with woody undertone. Fresher, soapier, 6–8 hour longevity. The woods don't disappear — they become a subtle background texture rather than the main story.

**Limitations (honest):**

- ✅ But: added iris + musk + DHM will dominate the perceptual profile for most wearers
