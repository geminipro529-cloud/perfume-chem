# DHC Veritas vs. Real Dior Homme Cologne (2026 Counter) — Cross-Check

## What DHC Actually Smells Like Today

**Opening:** Synthetic bergamot blast — bright, slightly candied, metallic-clean. Less "Calabrian peel" than early batches.

**Heart:** Clean, soapy **iris** (not the lipstick-violet iris of Dior Homme 2005). Stripped-down, laundry-fresh white iris. Trace neroli/orange blossom — subtle.

**Drydown:** Clean musk + ambroxan. Skin-scent territory within 3–4 hours. **No woods. No depth. No darkness.** Deliberately hollow in the base.

**Overall:** "Shower fresh" citrus-musk. Safe, mass-pleasing, office-friendly.

## Critical Divergences

### 1. Iris — COMPLETELY MISSING
- **Real DHC:** Clean soapy iris is the heart signature. Not powdery, not violet — laundry-fresh white iris.
- **Our formula:** Zero iris material. No orris, no ionone, no Alpha Irone, no Molecule Iris.
- **Impact:** Our formula reads as "citrus-jasmine-woods." DHC reads as "citrus-iris-musk." Different family entirely.

### 2. Base Architecture — OPPOSITE PHILOSOPHY
- **Real DHC:** Hollow base. Musk + ambroxan only. Skin-scent at 3–4 hours. **Subtraction aesthetic.**
- **Our formula:** Dense wood fortress. Iso E Super (24% OAV) + Norlimbanol + Vetival + Javanol + Cardamom. **Addition aesthetic.**
- **Impact:** Our formula lasts 8–10 hours. DHC dies at 4–6. Our base is thick and warm; DHC's is thin and clean.

### 3. Musk Class — WRONG MUSK FAMILY
- **Real DHC:** Clean white musk (likely Galaxolide-type or Habanolide-type polycyclic) + ambroxan.
- **Our formula:** Romandolide (clean woody-musk) + Ethylene Brassylate (creamy lactonic) + Ambrofix (amber).
- **Impact:** DHC smells like "fresh linen." Ours smells like "warm skin with wood."

### 4. Flower Accord — WRONG FAMILY
- **Real DHC:** Iris heart + trace neroli/orange blossom.
- **Our formula:** Aurantiol (orange blossom Schiff base) + Dihydrojasmone (jasmine fruity-green).
- **Impact:** DHC is floral-soapy. Ours is floral-fruity-jasmine. Orange blossom ≠ iris.

### 5. Complexity — TOO MANY MATERIALS
- **Real DHC:** Reportedly 8–12 materials. Deliberately linear.
- **Our formula:** 16 materials with 8 in the drydown alone.
- **Impact:** DHC doesn't "develop" — it stays shower-fresh. Our formula evolves through woody-amber phases.

### 6. Petitgrain — TOO PROMINENT
- **Real DHC:** No detectable petitgrain. The green note is from synthetic bergamot boosters, not natural petitgrain.
- **Our formula:** 350µL Petitgrain EO (5.7% OAV). Adds green-bitter complexity DHC doesn't have.

### 7. Hedione Load — TOO HIGH
- **Real DHC:** Uses hedione for radiance but not as the dominant headspace note.
- **Our formula:** Hedione is #1 at 28.9% OAV, beating bergamot. DHC's bergamot should dominate the opening.

## What Our Formula Actually Is

**Not Dior Homme Cologne.** It's a **citrus-woody-floral** in the style of:
- **Bergamote 22** (Le Labo) — petitgrain + bergamot + woods
- **Neroli Portofino** (Tom Ford) — massive citrus + white florals + ambroxan
- **Eau de Bergamote** (The Different Company) — neroli/bergamot with vetiver

Our formula is **better** than current DHC in terms of complexity and naturalism. But it's **not a flanker of DHC** — it's a different perfume entirely.

## Two Paths Forward

### Path A: Fix Veritas to Match Real DHC
- Replace flower accord with **iris** (Alpha Irone or Molecule Iris at trace)
- Strip drydown to **musk + ambroxan only** (remove Iso E Super, Norlimbanol, Vetival, Javanol, Cardamom)
- Add **white musk** (Galaxolide or Habanolide)
- Reduce hedione to let bergamot dominate opening
- Remove or drastically reduce petitgrain
- Target: 8–10 materials, linear, 4–6 hour longevity

### Path B: Keep Veritas as "Artisanal DHC" — Better Than the Original
- Acknowledge our formula is **not a flanker** of commercial DHC
- Position it as "what DHC would be if Demachy had our materials and no cost constraints"
- Add iris anyway (because it makes it smell better)
- Keep the woody base but lighten it
- Market as "DHC DNA rebuilt with naturalism and depth"
