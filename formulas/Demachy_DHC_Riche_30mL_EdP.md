# Demachy — Dior Homme Cologne Riche — 30 mL Eau de Parfum

**Batch:** 30.00 mL · ~15.2% EdP · 14 materials
**Profile:** François Demachy's richer DHC vision — same neroli-family DNA, denser. Aurantiol + Oranger Crystals + Nerolin Bromelia + Nerol + Methyl Anthranilate chord at double strength, Hedione radiance, richer musks. No non-family additions. No Petitgrain.
**Maceration:** 2 weeks

| # | Material | Dilution | µL | Role |
|--:|----------|----------|----:|------|

| 2 | Iso E Super | neat | 500 | transparent woody diffusion |
| 3 | Hedione | neat | 700 | jasmine radiance — Demachy's transparent lift |
| 4 | Aurantiol | neat | 200 | orange blossom body — the neroli core (doubled) |

| 9 | Methyl Anthranilate | neat | 30 | fruity neroli booster |

**Total concentrate:** 4,520 µL (15.1%)

Top with ethanol to 30 mL. Invert 50 times. Macerate 2 weeks.

## Structure

| Layer | Materials | Effect |
|-------|-----------|--------|
| **Top** | Bergamot 900, Grapefruit 400, Blood Orange 300 | luminous citrus burst — richer, juicier |
| **Heart** | Aurantiol 200, Oranger Crystals 400, Nerolin Bromelia 60, Nerol 80, Methyl Anthranilate 60, Linalool 150, Hedione 700 | the neroli orchestra at double strength — richer, denser, more luxurious |
| **Base** | Galaxolide 600, Iso E Super 500, Ambrettolide 200 | richer transparent — double the musk mass |

## Why Demachy would do this

| Change | Reason |
|--------|--------|
| Concentrate from 9.5% → 15.2% | EdP strength — richer by definition |
| All neroli chord materials doubled | Deeper orange blossom character without leaving the family |
| No Petitgrain | Removed the "green twig" sharpness — what remains is rounder, sweeter, more floral |
| No non-family additions | Pure neroli DNA — Bergamot, Blood Orange, Grapefruit are the only citrus. No vetiver, no cedar, no iris |
| Hedione increased 300→700 | More of Demachy's signature transparent radiance to support the denser chord |
| Galaxolide 400→600, Ambrettolide doubled | Richer, creamier musk holds the heavier concentrate |
