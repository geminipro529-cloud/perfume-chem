# Good Girl Gone Bad — Minimal Core — 30 mL Eau de Parfum

**Batch:** 30.00 mL · ~18.5% EdP · 8 materials
**Profile:** By Kilian GGGB skeleton — Hedione (jasmonate radiance) + p-Cresyl Methyl Ether (tuberose-narcissus) + Gamma Decalactone (peach osmanthus) + Geraniol (rose) + Indole (animalic depth) + Iso E Super (woody) + Galaxolide (musk) + Vanillin (warmth).
**Maceration:** 2 weeks

---

## Equipment

- 1 amber glass bottle 30 mL
- 1 graduated pipette 1 mL
- 1 micro-pipette (for 5–80 µL)
- Ethanol 96%

---

## Procedure

Pipette directly into bottle in this order (heaviest first):

| # | Material | Dilution | µL | Role |
|--:|----------|----------|----:|------|
| 1 | Iso E Super | neat | 1,400 | woody diffusive base |
| 2 | Hedione | neat | 1,800 | jasmine radiance — the core floral |
| 3 | Galaxolide | 50% in DEP | 1,000 | clean projection musk (500 µL active) |
| 4 | Geraniol | 10% in DPG | 500 | rose character — the "good girl" |
| 5 | Vanillin | 10% | 300 | amber-vanillic warmth (30 µL active) |
| 6 | Gamma Decalactone | neat | 300 | peach-apricot osmanthus effect |
| 7 | p-Cresyl Methyl Ether | 10% | 200 | tuberose-narcissus — the "bad girl" (20 µL active) |
| 8 | Indole | 10% | 100 | animalic floral depth (10 µL active) |

**Total concentrate:** 5,600 µL

Top with ethanol to 30 mL fill line. Cap, invert 50 times.
Label: "GGGB Minimal — 2026-05-23 — macerate 2 weeks"

---

## Accord Architecture

**Top:** Gamma Decalactone (peach) hits first — the osmanthus/apricot signature of GGGB. Vanillin (10%) at 30 µL active adds instant sweetness.

**Heart:** Hedione (1,800 µL massive) creates the transparent jasmine radiance that IS Alberto Morillas' signature. p-Cresyl Methyl Ether at 20 µL active provides the tuberose-narcissus "bad girl" character. Geraniol (500 µL) gives the rose backbone — the "good girl" floral. Indole at 10 µL active adds the animalic narcotic edge that makes the whole thing "gone bad."

**Base:** Iso E Super (1,400 µL) creates the velvety woody diffusion. Galaxolide (500 µL active) provides the clean projection musk. Together they form a transparent cocoon that lets the white florals hover above skin.
