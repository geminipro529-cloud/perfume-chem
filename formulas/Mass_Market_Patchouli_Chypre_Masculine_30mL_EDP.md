## WHAT IT SMELLS LIKE

**Opening:** Bergamot + Grapefruit + Cardamom + Black Pepper. Bright, spicy, dry — no sweetness.

**Heart:** Iso E Super woody cocoon + Hedione radiance + trace rose (PEA, Geraniol, Damascone) + warm jasmine whisper (Dihydrojasmone). The floral is structural, not dominant.

**Base:** 500 µL Patchouli + 400 µL Vetiver — dark earth and dry roots. Nagarmortha smoke + Frankincense incense. Evernyl moss spine. Coumarin + Vanillin at structural warmth, not sweet. Romandolide projects clean luxury musk.

**In one sentence:** Dry, woody, incense-patchouli chypre — mass market masculine in the Terre d'Hermès / TF Patchouli Absolu lineage.
