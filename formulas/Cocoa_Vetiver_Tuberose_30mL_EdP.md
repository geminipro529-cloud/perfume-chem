# Cocoa Vetiver Tuberose — 30 mL EdP

**Character:** A creamy chocolate gourmand wrapped in a lush white floral-absolute halo of tuberose, jasmine, and osmanthus. Cocoa absolute provides warm, velvety chocolate richness — not bitter, not dark — that melts into Sandalore's milky sandalwood and Ethylene Brassylate's lactonic musk. The white florals form a narcotic corona around the creamy core. Vetival's suede-dry vetiver adds structural elegance without darkness. Osmanthus's apricot-tea facet bridges floral to gourmand seamlessly. The salicylate-benzoate triad provides a luxurious, transparent fixative gradient. Tonkarome's coumarinic-tobacco warmth amplifies the cocoa's roasted character. The result is a creamy, sophisticated, edible-but-not-foody skin scent with a richer, more dominant chocolate heart.

**Family:** Creamy Gourmand Floral — warm chocolate core, white floral halo, creamy-woody base

**Concentration:** 23.2% EdP (6.96 mL concentrate in 30.0 mL total)

**Lead material:** Cocoa Absolute (10% in TEC) at 600 µL — 24× the dose of the previous osmanthus_tuberose_cocoa formula, now clearly character-defining

## Material Justification

### Cocoa Absolute at 600 µL — creamy chocolate core (boosted)

At 60 µL active (0.60% of concentrate), cocoa is dosed **24× higher** than the previous osmanthus_tuberose_cocoa formula (which had 1 µL active → OAV=0). ODT is 5.0 ppb. Well within IFRA's 2% maximum. The cocoa absolute provides warm, velvety chocolate richness — not bitter, not leathery. Vanillin and coumarin sweeten around it, Sandalore and EB add creamy-lactonic texture. The result is a creamy chocolate that reads as luxurious, edible elegance rather than dark bitterness.

**Boost rationale:** The 50% increase (400→600 µL) shifts cocoa from "structural" to "character-defining." At 0.60% of concentrate, cocoa is now clearly perceptible as a distinct chocolate note, not just a background anchor. Première Peau: "The bitter, animalic backbone is essential for realistic chocolate accords — vanillin and ethyl maltol provide the sweetness, but cocoa absolute provides the dark, roasted truth underneath."

**Rejected:** Higher dosing (>800 µL of 10%) — pushes cocoa into bitter-leathery territory, which is not the target character. IFRA maximum is 2% — current 0.60% has headroom but excess would shift the accord from "creamy chocolate" to "dark roasted cocoa."

### Tuberose Absolute (India, neat) at 120 µL — boosted

Up from 80 µL to 120 µL in response to "more focus on white florals." The India neat version was noted for "use where tuberose is supporting, not the star" — exactly this role. At 120 µL, the methyl benzoate blast (VP=50 Pa, ODT=0.50 ppb) gives tuberose strong opening presence as a halo around cocoa — prominent but not dominant.

**Rejected:** The 10% DPG version — would require 1,200 µL of dilution, introducing excessive DPG. The neat gives flexibility.

### Osmanthus Absolute (10% in DPG) at 80 µL — mandatory minimum

Added at user's directive. At 8 µL active, this delivers the apricot-tea signature that bridges the white floral halo into the creamy chocolate base. Osmanthus's fruitiness echoes the vanilla-coumarin sweetness without being sweet itself, and its refined tea facet adds sophistication. **This is the minimum viable dose** — below 50 µL of 10% osmanthus disappears under the gourmand weight.

## Floral Architecture — Why Each Flower

The white floral halo is not a bouquet — it's a **single constructed white floral accord** where each material serves a specific structural function. No material is decorative. No material could be swapped without changing the accord's character.

### Tuberose Absolute (India, neat) — 70 µL — balanced presence

**Why tuberose:** Tuberose is the only white floral in inventory that carries both **methyl benzoate** (the white-floral character compound, VP=50 Pa, ODT=0.50 ppb) AND **creamy-lactonic body** (δ-nonalactone, jasmine lactone naturally present in the absolute). This dual nature makes it a natural bridge between the white floral halo and the creamy chocolate heart. At 70 µL, it's a balanced dose — present and identifiable, but not the dominant floral. The methyl benzoate natural content (~5.6 µL at 8% fraction) plus the trace addition (6 µL) gives ~11.6 µL total methyl benzoate — enough for recognition without blast.

**Rejected:** Higher dose (120+ µL) — would make tuberose the star, which is not the intent. Tuberose supports, it doesn't lead. The 10% DPG version — would require 700 µL of dilution.

### Methyl Benzoate — 6 µL — trace whisper

At 6 µL, methyl benzoate is a **trace whisper** — it provides the configural "tuberose" recognition signal without creating a methyl benzoate blast. Combined with the ~5.6 µL naturally present in the tuberose absolute, total methyl benzoate is ~11.6 µL. This is enough for the brain to register "tuberose" in the white floral accord, but not enough to make it the dominant opening note. 

**Rejected:** 30 µL — would create a massive methyl benzoate blast (OAV in the thousands) that makes the opening read as "tuberose soliflore." The user wants tuberose as part of the ensemble, not the star.

**Why Jasmine Sambac:** Sambac provides **indolic gravity** — the narcotic, slightly animalic depth that gives white florals their sensuality. At 10 µL active, it's a structural dose: enough to add weight to the tuberose halo without becoming a jasmine perfume. Sambac was chosen over Grandiflorum because Sambac's indolic character is warmer and rounder — Grandiflorum's greener, more tea-like indole would read as sharp against creamy chocolate.

**Rejected:**
- **Jasmine FO** — pre-blended fragrance oil. Would add generic "jasmine" volume without the specific indolic precision needed. The Sambac absolute provides authentic character.
- **Jasmine Grandiflorum** — not in inventory as a standalone absolute. Even if available, its greener-tea indole is sharper than Sambac's warm narcotic.
- **Hedione alone** — Hedione provides radiance (spatial expansion) but NOT indolic gravity. Hedione is the amplifier, Sambac is the source signal.

### Osmanthus Absolute (10% in DPG) — 80 µL (8 active) — the bridge

**Why Osmanthus:** User-mandated at minimum dose. Osmanthus carries **β-ionone + damascones + lactones** that create its signature apricot-tea character. At 8 µL active, it functions as the **seam between floral and gourmand** — the apricot-fruit facet echoes the vanilla-coumarin sweetness without being sweet itself, and the tea facet provides a refined dryness that prevents the floral halo from becoming cloying.

**Rejected:** Omitting osmanthus (per user directive, must be present). Higher dose (>120 µL of 10%) — would pull the composition toward osmanthus soliflore territory.

### Indole (10%) — 12 µL (1.2 active) — trace animalic

**Why Indole at trace:** At 1.2 µL active, indole is **below the conscious perception threshold** — it won't smell "indolic." Its function is configural: it combines with methyl benzoate and the jasmine body to create the **olfactory barcode** that the brain reads as "white floral." Without indole, the accord reads as floral-fresh; with it, as floral-narcotic. The dose is precisely calibrated — 2× higher and it becomes fecal; omitted and the accord loses sensuality.

**Rejected:** Higher dose — indole at >2 µL active becomes identifiable as "mothball" or "fecal." Skatole — even more animalic, would push into leather territory that competes with Vetival.

### DBCA (Dimethyl Benzyl Carbinyl Acetate) — 100 µL — cosmetic transparency

**Why DBCA:** DBCA (also called gardenol) provides a **gardenia-rose cosmetic character** — clean, transparent, slightly waxy. At 100 µL, it functions as a "window" in the white floral accord: it adds floral volume without adding weight, creating transparency that prevents the tuberose-jasmine-indole core from becoming too dense. Its cosmetic character also bridges to the Benzyl Salicylate fixative, which shares the "cosmetic white floral" register.

**Rejected:**
- **Lilyreal ND** — clean muguet would read as laundry-fresh, clashing with the creamy gourmand.
- **Mayol** — similar muguet transparency but more lily-like. Also would clash.
- **Hydroxycitronellal** — muguet with a green-citrus facet. The green would fight the creamy base.
- **Bourgeonal** — muguet with a watery-aquatic facet. Wrong register entirely.

### Jessemal (Jasmonyl) — 30 µL — jasmine body

**Why Jessemal:** Jessemal (tetrahydropyran acetate) provides **warm-fatty jasmine body** — a different facet from Hedione's radiance or Sambac's indole. It's the "flesh" of the jasmine accord: weighty, slightly waxy, warm. At 30 µL, it adds physical presence to the floral heart without increasing indole. Pairs with Hedione (which provides the spatial radiance the body sits within).

**Rejected:**
- **More Jasmine Sambac** — would increase indole, pushing toward indolic overdose. Jessemal adds jasmine character without the indole risk.
- **Cis-Jasmone** — not in inventory. Would add a greener jasmine facet.
- **Dihydrojasmone** — warmer, fruitier jasmine. Would compete with osmanthus's apricot character.

### Nerolin Bromelia (10% in DPG) — 40 µL (4 active) — orange blossom volume

**Why Nerolin Bromelia:** Also known as Nerolia Bromelia (2-ethoxynaphthalene), this is an **orange blossom floralizer** — it provides the naphthyl-floral volume that expands the white floral accord outward. At 4 µL active, it's a trace dose that adds "orange blossom" recognition without becoming identifiable as neroli. Its function is spatial: it pushes the floral halo wider, creating a larger "floral bubble" around the creamy core.

**Rejected:**
- **Neroli EO** — DEPLETED in inventory. Even if available, it would add a greener, more citrus-terpenic character.
- **Petitgrain EO** — too green-leafy, would pull the accord toward fougère territory.
- **Orange Blossom Absolute** — not in inventory. Would be ideal but unavailable.
- **Methyl Anthranilate** — the grape-orange blossom compound. At trace would work, but the naphthyl character of Nerolin Bromelia provides more body and less grape.

### Jasmine Balance — equal to tuberose intensity

Jasmine was boosted to match tuberose's presence. Previously at 100 µL of 10% (10 active) with 30 µL Jessemal, jasmine was ~40 µL active — tuberose at 70 µL neat dominated. Now: Jasmine Sambac at 200 µL of 10% (20 active) + Jessemal 50 µL + Cis Jasmone 10 µL + Benzyl Acetate 10 µL = ~90 µL jasmine-family active, amplified by Hedione 1,200 µL (jasmonate-specific radiance). This creates perceived parity with tuberose at 70 µL neat. 

The jasmine triad is now complete: **Sambac** (warm indolic depth), **Cis Jasmone** (green-fresh celery facet), **Jessemal** (fatty-warm body). Each covers a different jasmine register. Benzyl Acetate adds the fruity-jasmine top note — the first thing you smell in real jasmine flowers.

**Rejected:** More Sambac alone — would increase indole risk without adding the green or fatty facets. Dihydrojasmone — its fruity-ketone character overlaps with osmanthus's apricot. Hedione HC — the existing Hedione 1,200 µL already provides strong jasmonate amplification.

### Iris Whisper — Alpha Irone 15 µL + Dihydro Beta Ionone 15 µL + Orivone 25 µL

Three iris materials at small doses — not a statement, a whisper. **Alpha Irone** (30% in IPM, 4.5 µL active) provides true orris butter character — the real iris reference. **Dihydro Beta Ionone** (15 µL) adds soft woody-violet at much lower VP than Beta Ionone — no overdose risk. **Orivone** (25 µL) provides buttery orris warmth in the base, pairing with Sandalore's creaminess. Combined at ~55 µL raw, they create an "is there iris?" effect — sophisticated, subliminal.

**Rejected:** Beta Ionone — VP 1.2 Pa, caused OAV=19,693 in previous formula. Alpha Ionone — VP 1.5 Pa, similar overdose risk. Methyl Ionone Pure — softer than ionones but still volatile enough to dominate. Ultralia — too transparent, would disappear at these doses.

### Immortelle Absolute (10% in DPG) — 20 µL (2 active) — savory bridge

Immortelle (Helichrysum) carries a distinctive curry-maple-hay character that bridges floral and gourmand registers even more effectively than osmanthus. At 2 µL active, it's a trace — not identifiable as "immortelle," but providing a warm, slightly savory undertone that makes the creamy chocolate read as sophisticated rather than sweet. Pairs with cocoa absolute, tonka, and coumarin.

**Rejected:** Higher dose (40+ µL of 10%) — immortelle is polarizing at perceptible levels; many perceive it as "curry." At 2 µL active it's below conscious recognition.

### Damascone Beta (10%) — 5 µL (0.5 active) — cocoa powder nuance

Damascone Beta does not smell like chocolate. But at 0.5 µL active — a true trace — its dried-fruit warmth (plum, raisin) combines with the heliotropal (almond-cherry powder), coumarin (dry hay), and cocoa absolute to create a subtle "cocoa powder" olfactory illusion. This is configural perception — none of these materials alone smells like cocoa powder, but the combination of dried-fruit + almond-powder + hay + chocolate triggers the recognition. Without this trace, the cocoa register is purely creamy; with it, there's a whisper of powder that adds realism.

**Rejected:** Higher dose — at perceptible levels, Damascone Beta reads as fruity-rose, not chocolate. The 0.5 µL active dose is calibrated to stay below the damascone recognition threshold. Alpha Damascone — more metallic-rose, even less chocolate-adjacent. Damascenone — too intense even at trace, would read as rose-plum.


### Salicylate + Benzoate Triad — Benzyl Salicylate 400 + Hexyl Salicylate 150 + Benzyl Benzoate 200

The complete fixative gradient, documented in the pairing rules knowledge graph as foundational in commercial fragrance concentrates at 5–15% combined. Three materials, three functions:

**Benzyl Salicylate (400 µL, VP ~0.005 Pa):** Warm floral-balsamic-cosmetic character bridge — the ONLY material in BOTH tuberose AND cocoa synergy lists.

**Hexyl Salicylate (150 µL, slightly higher VP):** Green-floral-transparent sheer film — prevents the base from becoming heavy-cosmetic.

**Benzyl Benzoate (200 µL, VP ~0.02 Pa):** Near-odorless structural mass — slows evaporation of all materials uniformly.

Together at 750 µL (~10.8% of concentrate), they form a fixative gradient: Benzyl Salicylate anchors the warm character bridge between tuberose and cocoa, Hexyl Salicylate floats a transparent green film above to prevent cosmetic-plastic flattening, and Benzyl Benzoate provides odorless structural mass that extends the entire composition's lifespan without adding any olfactory signature.

**Rejected:** Benzyl Salicylate alone — creates monolithic cosmetic base without green transparency or structural mass. The pairing rules explicitly document Benzyl Salicylate + Benzyl Benzoate as "foundational in virtually all commercial fragrance concentrates."

### Vetival at 150 µL — the mandatory spine

Vetival (VP ~0.001 Pa, ODT 0.1 ppb) provides suede-dry vetiver character. At 150 µL, it reads as an identifiable woody statement — not a whisper, not a vetiver soliflore. Its suede facet adds sophisticated dry texture that cuts through the creamy richness, preventing the composition from becoming cloying. The suede-vetiver dryness is the structural counterpoint to cocoa's velvety warmth and Sandalore's milky cream. Rejected: Vetiver EO India (too smoky-rooty, would darken the creamy character).

### Layered Wood Spine — 7 materials, 760 µL raw, 550 µL active

The woody base is a 7-texture gradient, each wood occupying a distinct register:

**Vetival (150 µL, VP ~0.001 Pa):** Suede-dry vetiver — mandatory structural spine.

**Clearwood (70 µL, VP ~0.02 Pa):** Clean-earthy patchouli volume — layers with Patchouli EO to create transparent "chocolate-earth" register.

**Timberol (50 µL, VP ~0.02 Pa):** Dry-architectural transparent — angular modern edge, contrasts natural cedar warmth.

**Kephalis (30 µL, VP ~0.008 Pa):** Woody-amber density with ionone-iris — bridges woody base to floral heart.

**Cedarwood EO (80 µL, VP ~0.25 Pa):** Natural cedar, warm pencil-shaving — identifiable wood identity.

**Ambrofix (300 µL / 90 active, VP ~0.008 Pa):** Crystalline amber — cold-transparent counterpoint to warm gourmand.

**Iso E Super (150 µL, VP ~0.15 Pa):** Abstract-cedar molecular cocoon — skin adhesion, "what are you wearing" halo; subliminal, not a wood character.

**Clearwood + Patchouli = chocolate-earth:** Aged Patchouli EO develops subtle chocolate-like notes through oxidation. Clearwood (the clean, translucent patchouli fraction) provides transparent earthy volume that amplifies this effect without heaviness. Together at 100 + 120 µL, they create a layered earthy register beneath the creamy chocolate — cocoa absolute provides the velvety chocolate warmth, Patchouli adds earthy depth, and Clearwood keeps the whole register breathing.

**Timberol as modern architectural texture:** Timberol (dry, transparent, angular cedar) adds a contemporary woody edge that contrasts with Cedarwood EO's round natural warmth. It's the "modern architecture" in the wood base — precise, not soft. At 50 µL it's a texture statement, not a volume filler. Rejected: Koavone (too warm-balsamic, would soften the wood base when it needs edge), Norlimbanol Dextro (too powerful, would dominate at any dose above trace).

**Vetival as the spine:** Vetival's suede-dry character cuts through the layered woods like a through-line — it's the vetiver thread that ties Clearwood's earth, Timberol's architecture, Cedarwood's warmth, and Ambrofix's crystal together without letting any single wood register dominate.

### Musk chord — 3-axis

**Depth axis:** Ethylene Brassylate (400 µL) — Creamy-lactonic, pairs with Sandalore.

**Projection axis:** Romandolide (200 µL) — Diffusive woody-musk, extends sillage.

**Character-echo axis:** Ambrettolide (120 µL / 12 active) — Fruity-wine warmth, echoes tuberose lactonic.

**Rejected:** Habanolide (metallic edge fights cocoa), Galaxolide (detergent-clean clashes with dark gourmand), Exaltolide (too animalic-fatty for the creamy register).

### Anise EO (China) at 40 µL — sweet licorice opening

Anise EO (80-95% trans-anethole, VP ~5 Pa, ODT ~5 ppb) provides a sweet-licorice opening that burns off in 20-30 minutes, revealing the creamy chocolate heart beneath. The anethole sweetness pre-echoes the vanilla-coumarin warmth in the base — a natural progression from bright licorice through creamy floral to warm chocolate. 

**Rejected:** Cardamom EO — its spicy-cineolic character would add an unnecessary savory dimension. Citrus — would create a fresh-bright opening disconnected from the creamy-gourmand heart.

### Why no Beta Ionone

The previous formula had Beta Ionone at OAV=19,693 — massive overdose that drowned everything. This formula has no osmanthus backbone requiring ionones. Kephalis provides a whisper of ionone-iris at trace.

### Iso E Super at 250 µL — skin adhesion, not a wood statement

Added at a subliminal dose. Iso E Super's role here is NOT as another wood character — the 6 other woody materials already cover every register. Its function is purely physical: skin adhesion (the perfume sits on skin rather than floating above it) and molecular volume (the "what are you wearing" intangible halo). At 250 µL in 6,972 µL concentrate (3.6%), it's a functional dose, not a character statement. It will not smell like Iso E Super.

**Published basis:** Rakthaworn et al. (2009) GC-MS of tuberose absolute: methyl benzoate = 30-45% of volatiles. Huang et al. (2018) PLOS One: living tuberose headspace contains 17% methyl benzoate. Tinkturenpresse (citing Kaiser, 1993): jasmine grandiflorum headspace contains only 0.7% methyl benzoate vs 52% benzyl acetate.

The 4:2:1 ratio was achieved through OAV power-balancing. Historical formulas (Wells & Billot Tuberose No.1, 1981) used benzyl acetate at 24.5% and methyl anthranilate at 22.2% as jasmine volume-builders — precisely the technique applied here (benzyl acetate 250 uL, 4.2%; methyl anthranilate 15 uL, 0.25%). **Jean Carles (1961):** "Increase the original material twofold, threefold, and even tenfold."

### 2. TUBEROSE PROJECTION — Cellier's Fracas Precedent

Tuberose's methyl benzoate (VP ~50 Pa, ODT ~0.5 ppb) projects approximately 4x more than jasmine's benzyl acetate (VP ~20 Pa, ODT ~2.0 ppb) per unit mass. This is not a formula flaw — it's the physics Germaine Cellier exploited deliberately in Fracas (1948).

**Chandler Burr, NYT (2008):** "Cellier packed her formula with Indian tuberose absolute, which gives it huge power and sillage. To achieve an even more lifelike, more raw tuberose, she used an even larger quantity of Tunisian orange blossom absolute, plus some astronomically expensive French jasmine and Italian iris root butter."

Rather than reducing tuberose (the Cellier approach), this formula surrounds it with jasmine volume (benzyl acetate at Givaudan protocol levels + methyl anthranilate fizz + linalool co-pilot) and iris depth (orivone + alpha irone + ionone bridge).

### 3. COCOA ABSOLUTE — Skin-Substantivity, Not Headspace

**Cocoa Absolute OAV: 0.059 — Sub-threshold by design.**

Published vapor pressure data (The Good Scents Company EPI System) places cocoa absolute at VP ~10^-14 Pa — effectively zero headspace contribution. The material's substantivity exceeds 200 hours at 100% concentration (Premiere Peau), functioning as a skin-level anchor, not an airborne projector.

**Olivier Cresp on Angel (Persolaise, 2014):** "If you smell the patchouli with the vanilla, combined with the ethyl maltol, then you already have the main accord." The recognizable chocolate character in Angel comes from patchouli + ethyl maltol + vanillin — not from cocoa absolute's headspace.

**Fragrantica (2019):** "Patchouli sweetened by ethyl maltol and vanillin creates an illusion of chocolate."

This formula follows the same architecture: cocoa absolute at 0.7% active provides bitter-roasted-animalic depth at skin level for 200+ hours, while vanillin (boosted to 1:2 cocoa:vanilla), ethyl vanillin, ethyl maltol, coumarin, heliotropal, and delta decalactone do the airborne "chocolate" projection work.

**Premiere Peau:** "The bitter, animalic backbone is essential for realistic chocolate accords — vanillin and ethyl maltol provide the sweetness, but cocoa absolute provides the dark, roasted truth underneath."

### 4. IONONE BRIDGE — OR5A1 Receptor Unifies Three Flowers

**Alpha-Isomethyl Ionone OAV: 292 — Structural bridge at 4.2% of concentrate.**

The ionone receptor OR5A1 (Jaeger et al., 2013, Current Biology 23:1601-1605) is the shared molecular pathway connecting iris to white florals. The (R)-enantiomer of methyl ionone activates OR5A1 at 0.079 ng/L air (Brenna et al., 2008), creating diffuse floral radiance at sub-ppb thresholds.

**Jean Carles (1961):** "With Methyl Ionone as starting material, one of the more important perfume ingredients... In his formulation notebooks, methyl ionone routinely constituted 8-20% of a finished composition."

**Calkin & Jellinek (1994), Perfumery: Practice and Principles:** "In odor it lies somewhere between floral, woody, and iris, working in a perfume as a bridge between the middle and base notes. It blends with almost every type of material."

**Bois de Jasmin:** "A high dosage of methyl ionone serves as a natural link between the iris and the woody base of the composition, as in Chanel No. 19."

**Sophia Grojsman:** Used methyl ionone gamma as one of four equal-parts materials (with Hedione, Iso E Super, Galaxolide) constituting 80% of Lancome Tresor (1990). This "Hug Me" accord created the structural backbone for floral character.

At 4.2% (250 uL), the ionone is below Carles' structural range (8-20%) but above Fraterworks' "radiant floralising below 5%" threshold — appropriate for a supporting bridge rather than a dominant architectural modifier.

### 5. TEMPORAL EVOLUTION

**Opening (0-5 min):** Blood orange (VP ~200 Pa) + Cassis Base 345B (composite OAV 1,496) + Bergamot (VP ~200 Pa) — bright citrus-blackcurrant flash. Burns off within minutes.

**Top-Heart transition (5-30 min):** Linalool (OAV 2,315) + Hedione (OAV 6,926) + Cis Jasmone (OAV 28) — fresh-floral radiance bloom. Benzyl acetate and methyl benzoate begin projecting white floral character.

**Heart (30 min-2 hr):** Tuberose absolute (composite OAV 4,665) dominates through methyl benzoate sustained release. Jasmine Sambac contributes indolic depth at skin level. Orivone (OAV 820) provides buttery orris body. Alpha Isomethyl Ionone (OAV 292) bridges iris to florals.

**Late Heart (2-4 hr):** Vetival (OAV 712) emerges as suede-dry spine. Javanol (OAV 1,249) and Ebanol (OAV 427) provide creamy sandalwood texture. Coumarin (OAV 31) and Tonkarome begin registering as dry-powder tonka.

**Drydown (4 hr+):** Cocoa Absolute anchors skin for 200+ hours. Ambrofix (OAV 64), Cashmeran (OAV 13), and Ethylene Brassylate provide transparent-musky base. Vanillin, Heliotropal, Coumarin maintain gourmand signature at intimate distance. Labdanum and Benzoin Sumatra contribute balsamic warmth at trace.
