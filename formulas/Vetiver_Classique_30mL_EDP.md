### Key Design Decisions

| Change from v3 | v3 | v4 | Why |
|----------------|----:|----:|-----|
| Bergamot FCF | 600 | 340 | Cut 43%. At 10k OAV it was a soliflore, not an opening. Classical vetivers use bergamot as a bright top, not the dominant character. |
| Linalool | — | 80 | Fresh floral-aromatic lift. Classical ingredient in Guerlain Vetiver. Bridges citrus to geranium without Hedione's modern radiance. |
| Lavender EO | — | 50 | Trace herbal-floral. Classical companion to geranium in traditional vetivers. |
| Geraniol | 170 | 350 | Doubled. The floral heart must be clearly perceptible (target OAV 50-80), not a whisper. |
| Iso E Super | 950 | 200 | Drastically cut. Classical vetivers don't use molecular cocoons. Kept at trace for modern skin adhesion only. |
| Hedione HC | 700 | — | Removed. Jasmonic radiance is modern. Classical vetivers use linalool + geraniol for floral lift. |
| PEDMC | 80 | — | Removed. Modern material, wrong era. |
| Vetiver EO | 1050 | 1600 | Boosted 52%. Must be the unmistakable star. Target OAV 30-50 in headspace. |
| Cedarwood EO | 400 | 600 | Strengthened classical woody spine. |
| Sandalore | — | 350 | Sandalwood warmth. Classical vetiver base note. Pair with cedar for full woody register. |
| Ethylene Brassylate | 300 | — | Removed. Creamy-lactonic is modern niche, not classical. |
| Zenolide | — | 100 | Fresh-citrusy musk lift. Classical vetivers use clean musk, not heavy macrocyclics. |
| Coumarin (20%) | 180 | 400 | More tonka warmth in drydown. 80 µL active. |
| Tobacco Absolute (10%) | 220 | 400 | More active tobacco warmth (40 µL active). |
| Vetival | 180 | 200 | More suede-vetiver depth. |
| Vertofix | 300 | 450 | Stronger woody-musky fixative. |
| Habanolide | 270 | 450 | More warm skin musk for projection. |
| Patchouli EO | 100 | 80 | Reduced slightly — vetiver carries the earth now. |
