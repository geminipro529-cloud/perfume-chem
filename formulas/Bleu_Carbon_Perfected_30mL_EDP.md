# Bleu Carbon Perfected — 30 mL Eau de Parfum

**Batch:** 30.00 mL · ~17.8% EdP · 35 materials
**Profile:** Pipeline-optimized luxury aromatic fougere — hedonic-maximized blend of Sauvage crystallinity, Bleu de Chanel transparency, Luna Rossa Carbon warmth, and Ocean Le Parfum depth.
**Hedonic strategy:** Javanol (3.5), Pamzest (3.5), Nerol (3.5), DBCA (3.5), Bergamot FCF Sicilian (4.0), Ethylene Brassylate (3.0), Vanillin trace (3.0).
**Maceration:** 3 weeks minimum

### Hedonic Optimization Delivered
- **Javanol (40 µL)** → OAV 5,435 (very strong). The #5 material at opening. Persists at OAV 6,709 through drydown. Hedonic 3.5 — the luxury sandalwood creaminess no mass-market blue has.
- **DBCA (20 µL)** → OAV 11.9 (moderate). Third rose dimension: Geraniol (green-rose) + Nerol (citrusy-rose) + DBCA (transparent-rose).

- **Vanillin 10% (8 µL)** → OAV 0.06 (structural). Subliminal gourmand warmth. You feel the comfort without naming the vanilla.

## Perfumer's Notes

- **Javanol (40 µL, hedonic 3.5)** — the key addition. Creamy sandalwood precision bridges the cold Ambrofix crystal and warm Cashmeran textile. The "expensive" note that designer blue fragrances lack.
- **Ethylene Brassylate (40 µL, hedonic 3.0)** — lactonic creamy body depth. The fifth musk in the chord, adding milky-soft skin texture.
- **Vanillin 10% (8 µL, hedonic 3.0)** — subliminal gourmand warmth at 0.8 µL active. Below vanilla perception, above warmth threshold.
- **DBCA (20 µL, hedonic 3.5)** — floral-rose lift with transparent radiance. Third rose dimension.
- **Coumarin reduced to 80 µL (20%)** — OAV 57.6, below the 60.0 cliff, strong fougere identity at 16 µL active.
- **Top volume reduced** — pyramid balance improved to T:30.1% H:21.8% B:48.1%.
