# Tuberose Vert Chypre — 30mL EdP

## Character
A green chypre anchored by Tuberose Absolute — galbanum's bitter-resinous green cuts through tuberose's narcotic creaminess, creating a "crushed white flower in a wet forest" effect. The chypre spine (Bergamot → Evernyl → Labdanum → Patchouli) provides the classical fresh-to-dark arc, while the tuberose-jasmine-ylang heart injects an unexpected white floral opulence into the mossy-woody chypre skeleton. Dynascone and Leafovert amplify the galbanum-green attack, Aldehyde C10 + C12 MNA create sparkle at the top, and Damascone Beta bridges the green opening to the floral heart. Think Vent Vert with a tuberose overdose — the most assertive green floral in this collection.

**Family**: Green Chypre — galbanum-green top, tuberose narcotic heart, mossy-woody-labdanum chypre base

## Material Justification

- **Tuberose Absolute (10% in DPG)**: Narcotic white floral anchor at 450 µL of 10% = 45 µL active (0.79% of concentrate). Tuberose's creamy-methyl benzoate character creates a deliberate tension with the galbanum-green chypre spine — the "crushed flower in a wet forest" concept depends on this contrast. Chosen over Tuberalia base (too clean, lacks the narcotic depth needed to stand against galbanum).

- **Galbanum Resinoid (10%) + Dynascone (10%) + Leafovert**: The green attack triad. Galbanum Resinoid (8 µL active) provides the bitter-resinous green backbone — this is the defining material of a green chypre. Dynascone (4 µL active) injects a powerful green-galbanum bomb at trace level — chosen over Galbanum EO because Dynascone's synthetic intensity cuts through the dense white floral heart. Leafovert (30 µL neat) adds sharp cut-grass freshness. Parmavert (25 µL neat) is also present for violet-leaf softness — together the 4 green materials create a multi-dimensional green register (resinous + sharp + violet-leaf). Rejected: Galbanum EO (would double up the galbanum facet; Resinoid chosen for its tenacity).

- **Aldehyde C10 (1%) + Aldehyde C12 MNA (1%)**: Aldehydic sparkle pair. C10 (0.4 µL active) provides citrus-orange lift. C12 MNA (0.4 µL active) adds metallic-waxy sparkle that pairs with tuberose's natural waxy facet. Together they create the aldehydic "fizz" that defines classical chypres. At these trace doses they read as sparkle, not a distinct aldehyde note.

- **Jasmine Sambac (10% in DPG) + Ylang Complete EO**: White floral body at 300 + 180 µL. Sambac (30 µL active) provides indolic-jasmonate depth. Ylang Complete (180 µL neat) adds banana-custard-benzyl ester creaminess. Together they provide the floral volume that transforms a green chypre into a green-floral chypre.

- **PCME (10%)**: Cresylic-narcotic facet at 25 µL of 10% = 2.5 µL active. Reinforces tuberose's natural horse-stable character — essential in a green context where tuberose must fight to be heard over the galbanum. Capped at 25 µL to stay below the OAV overdose threshold.

- **Damascone Beta (10%) + Damascenone (1%)**: Rose-fruit depth pair. Damascone Beta (5 µL active) provides plum-rose body that bridges the green top to the white floral heart. Damascenone (0.3 µL active) adds a magical dried-fruit-rose facet at trace level — the ghost of rose in the green.

- **Hydroxycitronellal**: Muguet volume at 200 µL. Adds a clean-lily-of-the-valley transparency that lifts the dense floral heart. Its schiff-base chemistry pairs with Methyl Anthranilate traces in Jasmine Sambac. Chosen over Bourgeonal (too sharp-aqueous for this green context) and Lilyreal (too cosmetic — would compete with Benzyl Salicylate).

- **Evernyl (50% in DPG)**: The chypre anchor at 30 µL of 50% = 15 µL active. Evernyl provides the bitter-lichen-mossy character that defines the chypre spine — it's the "dark forest floor" that the galbanum-green top eventually descends into. At 15 µL active it stays within IFRA safety limits while providing unmistakable chypre character. The 50% dilution in DPG allows precise dosing.

- **Labdanum Resinoid (10% in DPG)**: Amber-resin chypre base at 150 µL of 10% = 15 µL active. Labdanum provides the warm-amber undercurrent that softens the mossy Evernyl — the third pillar of the classical chypre accord (Bergamot → Evernyl → Labdanum). At 15 µL active it supports rather than dominates.

- **Patchouli EO + Vetiver EO India + Cedarwood oil Virginia**: The dark earthy-woody triad. Patchouli (130 µL) provides camphoraceous-earth backbone — essential for anchoring the green-floral heart. Vetiver EO India (100 µL) adds smoky-rooty depth — Indian ruh khus is darker and more complex than standard Vetiver EO. Cedarwood oil Virginia (120 µL) adds warm-pencil-shaving woodiness. Together they create the forest-floor base that makes a chypre a chypre.

- **Benzyl Salicylate + Hexyl Salicylate**: Fixative gradient. Benzyl Salicylate (450 µL) provides the deep cosmetic-waxy diffusion that carries the tuberose heart outward — chosen because the composition needs cosmetic weight to balance the bitter-green attack. Hexyl Salicylate (180 µL) adds a lighter, more transparent fixation that prevents the Benzyl Salicylate from becoming oppressive. Chosen over Benzyl Benzoate because this composition benefits from the salicylate character contribution.

- **Iso E Super**: Molecular skin cocoon at 500 µL. Provides abstract-cedar skin-adhesion that anchors the entire composition. Chosen over Timberol (too architectural — would make the green-woody base uncomfortably dry) and Azarbre (cedar-amber warmth that would pull toward oriental — the chypre needs moss, not warmth).

- **Ethylene Brassylate + Romandolide + Ambrettolide**: 3-axis musk chord. EB (200 µL) = creamy depth. Romandolide (150 µL) = woody-diffusive projection — the best musk for extending the galbanum-green outward. Ambrettolide (70 µL of 10% = 7 µL active) = naturalistic warmth that softens the bitter-green edge without adding sweetness.

- **Vertofix**: Woody-musky bridge at 100 µL. Glues the Iso E Super molecular layer to the patchouli-vetiver-cedar woody base. Its vetivert-acetate character reinforces the Vetiver EO India.

- **Ambrofix (30% w/v)**: Mineral-amber depth at 80 µL of 30% = 24 µL active. Provides a crystalline-transparent lift that prevents the mossy-earthy base from becoming muddy. Chosen to replace Ambrox Super 30% (DEPLETED).

- **Cashmeran (neat) + Coumarin (20%)**: Soft textile-hay base. Cashmeran (70 µL) adds soft cashmere-musky warmth. Coumarin (14 µL active) provides hay-almond sweetness and a subtle tobacco facet that reinforces the chypre's classical character. Both are within safe non-oriental ceilings.
