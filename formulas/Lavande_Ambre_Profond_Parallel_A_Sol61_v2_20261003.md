## 5. Why the composition changed

1. Fix the natural lavender block at 34% total: 23.8% Bontoux and 10.2% Aroma&More, exactly 70:30 by mass. This does not claim equivalence to the older 71.875:28.125 nominal-volume ratio.
2. Keep Ambrox as 300 mg crystals per 6 g product target. There is no crystal uL conversion and no guessed fixed TEC addition.
3. Choose Romandolide alone for the lead. Habanolide is a separate replacement/omission experiment, not automatically richer.
4. Resolve AIMI to the owned PW 3IW00300 Alpha Isomethyl Ionone. It is not the stale Givaudan label, Gamma Coeur or Alpha Irone.
5. Remove redundant Cedrat, retain the small resin/hay layer and do not add superambers, vanilla or a generic floral bouquet.

No larger total, extra musk, higher vapor-pressure spread or ingredient count is treated as richness. Iso E Super is selected for abstract woody space, not identifiable cedar; identifiable cedar/Azarbre is used only where the brief needs that register. Adding Timberol/superambers would require a separate target-linked hypothesis.
