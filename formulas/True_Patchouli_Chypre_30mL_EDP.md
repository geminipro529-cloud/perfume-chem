## WHAT IT SMELLS LIKE

**Opening:** Bergamot + Grapefruit + Cardamom. Bright, warm, inviting. No sharp herbal edge.

**Heart:** Rose (PEA + Geraniol + Damascone) and Jasmine (Hedione + Dihydrojasmone) in equal measure — a classic chypre floral heart. Clary Sage adds ambery-herbal depth without the lavender barbershop register.

**Base:** **500 µL Patchouli** leading. Clearwood and Nagarmortha wrap it in dark earth and smoke. Evernyl (oakmoss) is the structural spine — that "forest floor" that defines chypre. Olibanum + Benzoin + Coumarin = warm amber-resinous drydown. Romandolide keeps it clean and modern.

**In one sentence:** A proper patchouli chypre — citrus crown, rose-jasmine heart, mossy-amber-patchouli base. No shortcuts, no barbershop, no modern crutches.
