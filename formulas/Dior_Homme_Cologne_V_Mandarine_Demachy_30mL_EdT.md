# Dior Homme Cologne V — Mandarine — Demachy Edition — 30 mL EdT

**Batch:** 30.00 mL · ~16.7% EdT · **14 materials** (5 EOs/naturals)
**Profile:** Red Mandarin as sole citrus — the warmest, richest DHC. The grand finale.
**Premise:** The line began cool (I), went cold (II), brightened (III), went transparently green
(IV). V is the sunset: red mandarin, sweet-tangerine warmth. The only DHC you can wear to dinner.
**Maceration:** 4 weeks minimum

---

## Why Red Mandarin, at 36% of Concentrate

DHC V originally had mandarin at 1200 µL (24%). OAV analysis revealed the problem: mandarin
has ODT 2.0 ppm — twice as high as grapefruit (1.0 ppm), meaning it needs 2× more volume to
achieve the same perceived strength. At 1200 µL, the mandarin OAV was only 20,000 — the quietest
citrus in the line, completely smothered by the base (B:T ratio 3.00:1).

At 1800 µL (36% of concentrate), mandarin OAV rises to 30,000 — matching bergamot's 31,111.
This is the dose required for mandarin to carry the opening as confidently as bergamot does in
DHC I. The base is simultaneously lightened (Romandolide 500→400, Ambrofix 130→100, IES 400→350)
to bring the B:T ratio from 3.00 to 1.72 — a healthy range.

The result is the richest, most citrus-forward DHC in the line: 36% mandarin, warm-golden opening,
sweet-tangerine that fills the room before the blossom even begins to bloom.

## Key Adjustments from DHC III

| Axis | DHC III (Grapefruit) | DHC V (Mandarin) | Reason |
|------|---------------------|------------------|--------|
| **Citrus dose** | 1100 µL (22%) | **1800 µL (36%)** | Mandarin ODT 2.0 ppm — needs 2× grapefruit's volume for same perceived strength |
| **Linalyl Acetate** | 350 µL (7%) | **250 µL (5%)** | Reduced to make room for the massive mandarin dose |
| **Hedione** | 1000 µL (20%) | **800 µL (16%)** | Reduced — mandarin carries its own warmth, needs less radiance support |
| **Ethyl Linalool** | 450 µL (9%) | **350 µL (7%)** | Reduced — mandarin has better natural tenacity than grapefruit |
| **Petitgrain** | 400 µL (8%) | **360 µL (7.2%)** | Slight reduction for volume — still full blossom character |
| **Paradisamide** | 200 µL (0.4%) | **180 µL (0.36%)** | Slight reduction — mandarin's natural sweetness fills some of paradisamide's role |
| **Hedione HC** | 120 µL (2.4%) | **80 µL (1.6%)** | Reduced for volume |
| **Romandolide** | 500 µL (10%) | **400 µL (8%)** | Lightened base for OAV balance — arm's length still achieved |
| **Iso E Super** | 400 µL (8%) | **350 µL (7%)** | Lightened |
| **Ambrofix (30%)** | 130 µL | **100 µL** | Lightened — 0.6% active |
| **Ethylene Brassylate** | 250 µL (5%) | **230 µL (4.6%)** | Lightened |
| **Base subtotal** | 1380 µL | **1180 µL** | From 27.6% to 23.6% of concentrate |

## Procedure

### Layer A: Base (~1,180 µL)

| # | Material | Dilution | µL | Active µL | Role |
|--:|----------|----------|----:|----------:|------|
| 1 | Romandolide | neat | 400 | 400.0 | projection musk — arm's length, lightened for OAV |
| 2 | Iso E Super | neat | 350 | 350.0 | skin cocoon — 7% |
| 3 | Ethylene Brassylate | neat | 230 | 230.0 | lactonic depth |
| 4 | Ambrofix | 30% | 100 | 30.0 | crystalline pillar — 0.6% active |
| 5 | Ambrettolide | 10% in DPG | 50 | 5.0 | natural skin finish |
| 6 | Norlimbanol Dextro | neat | 25 | 25.0 | longevity scaffold |
| 7 | Vetival | neat | 25 | 25.0 | suede-vetiver micro-texture |

Swirl gently 5 seconds.

### Layer B: Heart (~1,770 µL)

| # | Material | Dilution | µL | Active µL | Role |
|--:|----------|----------|----:|----------:|------|
| 8 | Hedione | neat | 800 | 800.0 | radiance — 16%, mandarin carries its own warmth |
| 9 | Petitgrain EO | neat | 360 | 360.0 | blossom skeleton — 7.2% |
| 10 | Ethyl Linalool | neat | 350 | 350.0 | bridge — 7%, mandarin has good natural tenacity |
| 11 | Paradisamide | 10% | 180 | 18.0 | blossom character — 0.36% active |
| 12 | Hedione HC | neat | 80 | 80.0 | high-cis radiance — 1.6% |

Swirl gently 5 seconds.

### Layer C: Top (~2,050 µL)

| # | Material | Dilution | µL | Active µL | Role |
|--:|----------|----------|----:|----------:|------|
| 13 | Red Mandarin EO | neat | 1800 | 1800.0 | sole citrus — 36%, the massive mandarin opening. OAV 30,000, matching bergamot |
| 14 | Linalyl Acetate | neat | 250 | 250.0 | bridge — 5% |

Concentrate **~5,000 µL** + ethanol to 30 mL. Invert 50×. Label: **"DHC V — Mandarine — Demachy"**. Macerate 4 weeks.
