# Immortelle Tobacco Cuir — 30mL EdP

## Character
A dense leather-tobacco composition where Immortelle Absolute acts as the curry-honey bridge between phenolic smoke and animalic leather. The chypre skeleton (oakmoss-patchouli-labdanum) provides structural integrity while the tobacco-leather axis (Tobacco Absolute + IBQ + Guaiacol + Birch Tar) reads as dark, smoky, and urbane. Coumarin and vanillin soften the smoke without sweetening into gourmand territory. Think Lutensian leather — Serge Lutens *Cuir Mauresque* meets an immortelle-centric chypre.

**Family**: Leather with chypre bones — bergamot-top, oakmoss-patchouli base, tobacco-leather heart with immortelle modulation

## Material Justification

- **Immortelle Absolute (10%)**: The central modulator — 180 µL of 10% = 18 µL active (0.3% of concentrate). Honeyed-herbal curry character bridges the tobacco sweetness and leather animality. Synergizes with Coumarin (sweet-tobacco axis) and Labdanum (resinous-amber axis). Kept at modifier level — immortelle at full-dose would collapse the leather register into maple-syrup territory.
- **Tobacco Absolute (10%)**: 250 µL of 10% = 25 µL active. Warm, dried-tobacco-leaf character. Creates the sweet-smoky bridge between immortelle's honey and the leather base. Pairs with Coumarin for the classic tobacco-coumarin accord (tonka-tobacco synergy).
- **IBQ (10%) + Guaiacol (10%) + Birch Tar Rectified**: The leather triad. IBQ at 3.5 µL active provides the quintessential "dirty leather" animalic bite — the signature of classic leathers like Knize Ten and Bandit. Guaiacol at 4.5 µL active adds phenolic-smoke depth (the "burning wood" facet of leather). Birch Tar at 18 µL neat provides clean-charred smokiness at trace. Together they create a multi-faceted leather that reads as natural, not synthetic.
- **Suederal (10%)**: 80 µL of 10% = 8 µL active. Suede-leather without smoke — softens the IBQ/Guaiacol/Birch Tar triad, adding a refined chamois-leather texture that prevents the leather from reading as "motor oil."
- **Oakmoss Absolute (10%) + Evernyl + Patchouli + Labdanum**: Classic chypre quaternity. Oakmoss at 15 µL active provides bitter-green lichen character; Evernyl at 110 µL neat adds synthetic body and brightness; Patchouli at 150 µL neat anchors the earth register; Labdanum at 18 µL active provides resinous warmth. This is the structural frame — without it, the leather-tobacco would have no architecture.
- **Coumarin (20%) + Vanillin (10%)**: Sweet-balance axis against the smoke. Coumarin at 24 µL active is the classic tobacco partner (hay-sweet, tonka-like). Vanillin at 15 µL active rounds the edges without entering gourmand territory. Both kept moderate — the formula is a leather, not a dessert.
- **Iso E Super (750 µL)**: Molecular cocoon at high dose — the "what are you wearing" skin-adhesion effect. Creates volume and sillage projection without adding identifiable wood character. Pairs with Cashmeran as anosmia anchor.
- **Cashmeran (120 µL neat) + Cedarwood EO (200 µL)**: Textile-warmth + natural-cedar identity. Cashmeran provides the "wool sweater" warmth that softens the leather. Cedarwood EO adds identifiable wood character to anchor the abstract Iso E effect.
- **Clearwood (100 µL) + Vertofix (180 µL)**: Modern patchouli layering. Clearwood adds clean-earthy-modern patchouli character without the camphoraceous heaviness of pure Patchouli EO. Vertofix bridges the wood and musk layers — woody-musky fixative that glues the accord together.
- **Azarbre (80 µL) + Cedramber (80 µL) + Koavone (60 µL)**: Warm-wood amber layer. Azarbre provides cedar-amber warmth (warmer than Ambrox, no crystalline edge — chosen because Ambrox Super 30% is depleted). Cedramber bridges cedar and amber registers. Koavone supports the cedar accord with warm balsamic undertones.
- **Ambrofix (30% w/v)**: Clean amber radiance at 24 µL active. The Ambrox alternative — provides transparent-mineral sparkle without dominating. Chosen at moderate dose so the leather stays front and center.
- **Hedione HC (550 µL) + Hedione (300 µL)**: Radiance amplifier pair. High-cis Hedione HC provides rapid floral lift; standard Hedione sustains projection through the heart. Hedione synergizes with immortelle (a known pairing) and the tobacco-coumarin accord.
- **Bergamot FCF + Bergamot FCF Sicilian (600 µL total)**: Dual-citrus opening. Standard Bergamot FCF provides the classic chypre top; Sicilian adds richer, more complex hesperidic lift. Bergamot chosen over other citruses because it's the canonical chypre top note and pairs naturally with the oakmoss-patchouli base.
- **Black Pepper EO (40 µL) + Clary Sage EO (50 µL)**: Aromatic lift at trace. Black pepper adds dry-spice sparkle that cuts through the leather density. Clary sage provides herbal-aromatic freshness that bridges the citrus top to the tobacco heart. Chosen over Lavender EO — clary sage's drier, less floral character suits the leather register better.
- **Rhodinol ex Citronella (60 µL) + Dihydrojasmone (40 µL)**: Rose-jasmine heart trace. Rhodinol provides natural rose-geranium freshness at the heart level — prevents the tobacco-leather from becoming monochromatic. Dihydrojasmone adds a fresh-jasmine ketone warmth that bridges Hedione to the darker base.
- **Alpha Isomethyl Ionone (60 µL)**: Powder-violet heart bridge. The ionone adds a cosmetic-powder dimension that connects the coumarin-vanillin sweetness to the floral traces. Prevents the heart-to-base transition from being abrupt (tobacco straight to leather would feel unfinished).
- **Ethylene Brassylate (220 µL)**: Depth musk — creamy skin-sit. The lactonic quality softens the leather without making it "clean" (a risk with Romandolide alone). Pairs with Ambrettolide for naturalistic skin depth that doesn't read as "perfumey."
- **Romandolide (120 µL)**: Projection musk — diffusive woody-musk that extends the sillage outward. Chosen over Galaxolide to avoid laundry-detergent character in a dark leather. Chosen over Habanolide because warmth is already provided by EB + Ambrettolide + Cashmeran — Romandolide's cleaner outward push is what this formula needs.
- **Ambrettolide (10% in DPG)**: Character-echo musk — naturalistic fruity-musky warmth with wine-like undertones. Bridges immortelle's honeyed character to the skin. At 8 µL active, it whispers rather than shouts — the "quiet skin warmth" against the loud leather.
