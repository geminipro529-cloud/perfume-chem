## Purpose

V2 is a compressed current-inventory successor, not a sensory winner. This protocol asks whether the protected immortelle nucleus is recognizable, whether Benzyl Salicylate helps without cosmetic takeover, whether Ethylene Brassylate earns its sole-musk role, and whether reduced Hedione preserves air without masking the subject.

No arm may be compounded until stock labels/bases and formula-specific safety are reconciled. Skin work is later and separate from blotter approval.

## Locked target and failure vocabulary

The anonymous sample must first be described without showing the formula name, family, ingredient list, or expected notes. Advance requires an unprompted golden dry-floral/hay/pollen/immortelle object or a stable close description, followed later by a recognizable transformation into cured leaf and amber resin.

Pre-registered takeover failures are: curry seasoning, pancake/maple syrup, vanilla dessert, almond powder, tobacco perfume, root vegetable/orris, incense, patchouli, generic amberwood, sandalwood cream, laundry musk, cosmetic floral/salicylate, leather, and disconnected multiple odors.

## Stage C — sole-musk necessity

Compare exact V2 with a candidate in which 350 µL Ethylene Brassylate is replaced by 350 µL DPG.

Ethylene Brassylate survives only if it improves warm resin-to-skin continuity, comfort, and desire to re-smell in both blocks while immortelle remains recognizable and no laundry, blank softness, masking, fatigue, or generic musk appears. Parity favors the no-musk arm.

## Stage D — Hedione masking boundary

Run only if V2 passes Stage A. Compare exact V2 with a candidate in which 150 µL Hedione is replaced by 150 µL DPG.

Hedione survives only if it improves living air and the flower-to-resin transition without becoming the first object, lowering immortelle rank, or making the heart generically jasmine-radiant. If the no-Hedione arm is clearer and equally liked, remove Hedione. If more air is needed, test a separately randomized 75 µL add-back; do not escalate from OAV alone.

## Schedule and ballot

Use matched blotters at 0, 5, 30, and 120 minutes, then 6 and 24 hours. Lock each timepoint before the next. For every coded sample record:

1. up to five unprompted objects/materials in order;
2. first recognizable object;
3. strongest object;
4. immortelle/golden dry-flower rank from absent to first object, recorded only after free description;
5. one coherent odor / one main odor with integrated facets / several detached odors;
6. dry versus syrupy, pollen versus powder, living versus fossil, brittle versus yielding, and warm versus harsh on anchored scales;
7. liking and desire to re-smell as separate ratings;
8. intensity and fatigue as separate ratings;
9. all pre-registered takeover flags;
10. forced choice with ties allowed and a short reason.

At 2, 6, and 24 hours ask whether the earlier golden flower is still recognizable in transformed form before showing any desired-answer vocabulary.

## Decision rule

No single favorable sniff advances a material. A row survives only when its exact target-linked effect repeats in both independent blocks, the nucleus remains recognizable, liking or desire-to-resmell does not decline, and takeover failures do not increase. Stronger is not automatically better. Ties favor the simpler arm.

The completed formula after testing is the simplest arm that preserves unprompted immortelle identity at 5 and 30 minutes, transformed recurrence at 2 and 6 hours, and a warm hay-resin echo at 24 hours.
