## Named identity contract

**Name:** Immortelle Ambre Fossile.  
**Identity:** a golden immortelle flower caught in amber while still alive — bitter pollen and dry hay at the surface, restrained maple and cured leaf inside, then a mineral balsamic fossil that remembers the same flower.  
**Subject hierarchy:** immortelle first; amber is its state and enclosure, not a second generic subject.  
**Emotional register:** private sun-warmed abundance under a severe mineral contour; voluptuous, composed, and never syrupy.  
**Forbidden takeovers:** pancake/maple syrup, curry seasoning, vanilla gourmand, tobacco perfume, generic amberwood, sandalwood cream, laundry musk, cosmetic salicylate, incense, leather, iris, or floral bouquet.  
**Protected nucleus:** Immortelle Absolute + Coumarin + Maple Lactone + Ethyl Safranate + Tobacco Absolute + Anisaldehyde. Carrot Seed EO gives dry root/stem anatomy but is not allowed to become an orris substitute.

Complexity is carried by transformations and relationships: flower → pollen/hay → cured leaf → resin grain → warm skin echo. It is not inferred from the row count.
