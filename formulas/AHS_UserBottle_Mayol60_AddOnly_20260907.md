## Add-only proposal

For the reported 30 mL parent, the sole proposed addition is **Mayol neat: 60 microlitres (0.060 mL)**. Nominal final volume is **30.060 mL** before unmeasured mixing-volume effects. No extra Helional, Dihydromyrcenol, Hedione, Calone, musk, wood, carrier or ethanol is proposed. Every existing material amount is retained. This is an experimental candidate, not a recovered Chanel formula or evidence that the bottle is fixed. The amount is a chosen trial level, not a literature optimum.

## Target and alternative selection

Target more muguet presence in the immediate opening while preserving the AHS-adjacent citrus-to-wood identity. [Mayol manufacturer information](https://studio.dsm-firmenich.com/product/mayolr-pe-957230) supports soft fresh white-petal character and citrus/woody-methylionone connections. The role is a hypothesis for this mixture. Florol remains a soft-body alternative; Nympheal a watery-density alternative. They are not added together. Helional has already been confirmed neat and is held fixed; further DHM is excluded after the user's excessive-impact report. Cyclamen Aldehyde, Bourgeonal, Freesia HDI, Lilyreal ND and other muguet/green materials would be separate character/stock contrasts, not extra layers by default. Hydroxycitronellol is not Hydroxycitronellal. Neither a stronger citrus block nor added sweet/woody base addresses the stated test question.
