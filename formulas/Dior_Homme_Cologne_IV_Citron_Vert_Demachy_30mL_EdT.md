# Dior Homme Cologne IV — Citron Vert — Demachy Edition — 30 mL EdT

**Batch:** 30.00 mL · ~16.7% EdT · **14 materials** (5 EOs/naturals)
**Profile:** Lime as sole citrus — the greenest, most ethereal DHC. Refined with Demachy's
stated principles: more flowers (paradisamide restored), more amber sensuality (ambrofix
warmed), more roundness (hedione softened). The transparent concept remains but now breathes.
**Maceration:** 4 weeks minimum

---

## Why Lime, Why Now

Demachy initially rejected lime as "too cocktail." But that was for DHC II — where the brief
was artistic severity. For DHC IV, the brief is different: create the most transparent,
most ethereal expression of the DHC architecture. Lime is the only citrus that can do this.

OAV analysis revealed the problem: lime has ODT 2.0 ppm (same as cedrat and mandarin). At the
original 1300 µL (26%), lime OAV was only 21,667 — the quietest citrus in the line, completely
smothered by a B:T ratio of 2.84:1. The fix: lime at 1800 µL (36%), matching mandarin's dose
and achieving OAV 30,000 — equal to bergamot's 31,111. The base was simultaneously lightened
from 1380 to 1110 µL, bringing B:T from 2.84 to 1.62 — the best ratio in the line. This is
appropriate: the "transparent" DHC should have the lightest base.

Not the lime of margaritas — sharp, acidic, juvenile. The lime of a grove: green, cold,
herbal. Distilled lime EO is greener and cleaner than expressed lime. It reads as *Citrus
aurantifolia* — the plant, not the fruit. When paired with elevated petitgrain (9% — the
highest in any DHC) and reduced paradisamide, the result isn't "lime + grapefruit blossom"
but "lime grove" — green citrus leaves, green citrus fruit, green blossom. All one color,
in three textures.

This is the closest Demachy ever comes to Jean-Claude Ellena's watercolor transparency.
DHC IV is what happens when the architect who built in steel and glass decides to build
in air.

## Key Adjustments from DHC III

| Axis | DHC III (Grapefruit) | DHC IV (Lime) | Reason |
|------|---------------------|---------------|--------|
| **Citrus dose** | 1100 µL (22%) | **1800 µL (36%)** | Lime ODT 2.0 ppm — needs grapefruit's volume times (2.0/1.0)*(367/1100/...). Fixed to match bergamot's 31k OAV |
| **Petitgrain** | 400 µL (8%) | **400 µL (8%)** | Lime + petitgrain = green-on-green. Full volume for grove effect |
| **Paradisamide** | 200 µL (0.4% active) | **150 µL (0.3% active)** | Demachy: "Flowers, flowers, flowers! No perfume without flowers." Restored — the blossom must bloom even in the most ethereal entry |
| **Ambrofix (30%)** | 130 µL | **100 µL (0.6% active)** | Demachy: "Amber has a sensuous effect that is deeply appealing even if we are unaware." Slightly warmed from 0.54% — felt, not identified |
| **Hedione** | 1000 µL (20%) | **870 µL (17.4%)** | Demachy: "I like to give perfumes a feeling of roundness." Hedione softens lime's sharpness into a rounded glow |
| **Ethyl Linalool** | 450 µL (9%) | **380 µL (7.6%)** | Trimmed to make room for flower + amber + roundness |
| **Linalyl Acetate** | 350 µL (7%) | **200 µL (4%)** | Lime has minimal natural LA; structural bridge |
| **Romandolide** | 500 µL (10%) | **360 µL (7.2%)** | Trimmed — the lightest projection musk in the line |
| **Base subtotal** | 1380 µL | **1100 µL (22%)** | Lightest base — serves transparent concept |

## Procedure

### Layer A: Base (~1,100 µL)

| # | Material | Dilution | µL | Active µL | Role |
|--:|----------|----------|----:|----------:|------|
| 1 | Romandolide | neat | 360 | 360.0 | projection musk — lightest dose. Demachy balance: nothing dominates |
| 2 | Iso E Super | neat | 300 | 300.0 | skin cocoon — 6% |
| 3 | Ethylene Brassylate | neat | 240 | 240.0 | lactonic depth |
| 4 | Ambrofix | 30% | 100 | 30.0 | Demachy amber warmth — 0.6% active, the "felt but not identified" sensuality |
| 5 | Ambrettolide | 10% in DPG | 50 | 5.0 | natural skin finish |
| 6 | Norlimbanol Dextro | neat | 25 | 25.0 | longevity scaffold — unchanged, essential |
| 7 | Vetival | neat | 25 | 25.0 | suede-vetiver micro-texture |

Swirl gently 5 seconds.

### Layer B: Heart (~1,900 µL)

| # | Material | Dilution | µL | Active µL | Role |
|--:|----------|----------|----:|----------:|------|
| 8 | Hedione | neat | 870 | 870.0 | Demachy roundness — 17.4%. Softens lime's sharpness into a rounded glow |
| 9 | Petitgrain EO | neat | 400 | 400.0 | green blossom skeleton — 8%, the "grove leaves" |
| 10 | Ethyl Linalool | neat | 380 | 380.0 | citrus-blossom bridge — 7.6% |
| 11 | Paradisamide | 10% | 150 | 15.0 | Demachy flowers — 0.3% active. Restored blossom: "No perfume without flowers" |
| 12 | Hedione HC | neat | 100 | 100.0 | high-cis radiance — 2% |

Swirl gently 5 seconds.

### Layer C: Top (~2,000 µL)

| # | Material | Dilution | µL | Active µL | Role |
|--:|----------|----------|----:|----------:|------|
| 13 | Lime Distilled EO | neat | 1800 | 1800.0 | sole citrus — 36%, OAV 30,000 |
| 14 | Linalyl Acetate | neat | 200 | 200.0 | bridge — 4% |

Concentrate **~5,000 µL** + ethanol to 30 mL. Invert 50×. Label: **"DHC IV — Citron Vert — Demachy"**. Macerate 4 weeks.
