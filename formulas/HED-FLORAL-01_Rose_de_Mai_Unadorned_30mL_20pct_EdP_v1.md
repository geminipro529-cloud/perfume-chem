## Target / Ideal

Rose de Mai itself is the subject: warm living petals, soft honey, and the flower's own green-natural shadow. The ideal is not a generic luxury rose chassis. Citrus, geranium, woods, moss, musks, ionones, added damascones, aldehydes, jasmine materials, muguet materials, gourmand materials, and essential oils are outside the target.

The support system is deliberately narrow. Phenethyl Alcohol supplies quiet petal volume; Citronellol, Geraniol, and Nerol extend constituents already represented in the live Rose de Mai composite model; Hexyl Salicylate supplies the lightest stocked structural film. Benzyl Salicylate is intentionally omitted despite its return to stock because its warm, cosmetic-waxy cushion is not neutral to this flower.

## Current-Inventory Build

The subject stock is the user-confirmed `Rose de Mai Absolute (10% in DPG)`. Its 10% fraction basis is not recorded as w/w, w/v, or v/v, and its density is unresolved. Raw-volume arithmetic is exact, but authoritative active mass and active ppm w/w are therefore `UNKNOWN / HOLD` rather than fabricated.

Rose de Mai is a natural mixture. Its pipeline OAV must use the repository's constituent composite model, not a monomolecular parent ODT. That model is literature-partial and non-batch-specific; modeled OAV is screening evidence only.
