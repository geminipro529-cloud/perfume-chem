## Why this is the EDP direction

- **No benzaldehyde:** the bitter-almond axis belongs to the Extrait interpretation and is intentionally removed.
- **Lower sugar density:** Ethyl Maltol is reduced to a trace signature rather than a plush gourmand body.
- **Lighter saffron load:** Safraleine supplies warm, metallic depth while Ethyl Safranate supplies a drier lift; neither should dominate the jasmine-air structure.
- **Transparent floral volume:** Hedione is the principal diffusion space, with Jasmine Absolute used as a small natural shadow rather than a dense floral center.
- **Dry, radiant wood:** Ambrox Super, Iso E Super, and Virginia cedar create a mineral cedar scaffold without adding a heavy balsamic or oud base.
- **One musk only:** Romandolide has the distinct role of clean fabric persistence and finish. Additional musks would need pairwise nonredundancy and controlled omission evidence.
- **Evernyl is a trace modifier:** it should suggest dry moss, heated sugar, and mineral shadow without turning the perfume into a moss accord.

4. Performs consistently across both independently prepared replicates and more than one evaluator when available.
5. Passes inventory authority, exact active-dose accounting, composite-natural OAV, IFRA/allergen review, pre-mix parent comparison where applicable, and the full release gate.
