# F1 — Le Barbier de Grasse · 30 mL Finished EDP

**Family:** Classical Fougère (lavender · coumarin · oakmoss-cedar · musk)
**Bottle:** 30.00 mL Eau de Parfum
**Concentration:** 20 % concentrate in perfumer's ethanol
**Composition of bottle:** 6.00 mL concentrate (17 raw materials) + 24.00 mL ethanol

---

## What you'll need

- 1 × clean 30 mL atomizer or amber bottle (final bottle)
- 1 × clean 10 mL amber vial (concentrate build vial)
- Positive-displacement pipette (10–100 µL) for trace doses
- 1 mL glass syringe or graduated dropper for larger doses
- Perfumer's ethanol ≥ 96 %, denatured-undenatured both fine, fragrance-grade
- Optional: 1 × distilled water in dropper (for final softening)

> Build the **6 mL concentrate first** in the small vial, mature, then dilute into the 30 mL bottle. Do **not** add raw materials directly to ethanol — the base layer needs time to homogenize neat before dilution.

## Step 2 — Mature the concentrate

1. Cap the 10 mL build vial tightly. Label "F1 concentrate · DD-MM-YYYY".
2. Rest **dark, 18–22 °C, minimum 4 weeks** before dilution. Coumarin + Evernyl + macrocyclic musks integrate slowly; rushing produces a thin, "ingredient-list" reading.

---

## Step 3 — Dilute into the 30 mL bottle

> Once concentrate has matured.

| # | Component | µL | mL |
|---:|---|---:|---:|
| A | Matured concentrate (Step 1) | 6 000 | 6.00 |
| B | Perfumer's ethanol ≥ 96 % | 24 000 | 24.00 |
| | **Finished EDP** | **30 000** | **30.00** |

**Procedure:**
1. Transfer all **6.00 mL concentrate** into the 30 mL atomizer bottle.
2. Add **24.00 mL ethanol**. (Cleanest way: weigh ~19 g ethanol on a 0.01 g scale; ρ ≈ 0.79 → 24 mL.)
3. *Optional softener:* 1–2 drops distilled water. Cap, invert 10×.
4. Macerate **2 more weeks** dark before evaluation on skin.
