## Target / Ideal

Hyacinth stays foreground: green-stemmy, honeyed-floral, cool, and dimensional without becoming cut grass, aquatic detergent, generic muguet, or white-floral shampoo. The requested three-plus-floral construction is hierarchical rather than democratic:

1. PADMA-led hyacinth subject.
2. Soft muguet/lily petal from Florol and trace Mayol.
3. Quiet rosewater petal from PEA.
4. Jasmine-like air from restrained Hedione.

The ideal naturalistic hyacinth tool, Phenylacetaldehyde, is not in current inventory. PADMA is the live neat green-hyacinth acetal and is therefore the current subject anchor, not a claim of odor-identical substitution.
