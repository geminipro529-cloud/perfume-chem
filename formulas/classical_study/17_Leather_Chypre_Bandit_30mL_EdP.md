# Leather Chypre — Bandit Study — 30 mL EdP

**Historical reference:** Robert Piguet Bandit  
**Family archetype:** `chypre_leathery.bandit_reference`  
**Concentration:** 20.0% EdP · 6,000 µL concentrate in 30 mL

**Why this structure matters:** Leather chypre teaches severity: green bitterness, tarred leather, and a dry chypre base with almost no sweetness cushioning the effect.
