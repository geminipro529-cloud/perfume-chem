# Soliflore — Rose de Mai Study — 30 mL EdP

**Historical reference:** Classical French rose soliflore / Rose de Mai style  
**Family archetype:** `floral_soliflore.rose_reference`  
**Concentration:** 20.0% EdP · 6,000 µL concentrate in 30 mL  
**Historical gap:** There is no true rose absolute or Rose de Mai concrete in stock. This version teaches the classical rose shape through `PEA`, `Geraniol`, `Citronellol`, `Rhodinol ex Citronella`, damascones, and rose oxide.  
**Why this structure matters:** A classical rose soliflore is the clearest way to learn how perfumery reconstructs one flower across freshness, petal heart, and soft stem/base shadow.
