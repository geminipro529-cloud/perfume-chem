# Muguet Floral — Diorissimo Study — 30 mL EdP

**Historical reference:** Diorissimo  
**Family archetype:** `floral_muguet.diorissimo_reference`  
**Concentration:** 18.0% EdP · 5,400 µL concentrate in 30 mL  
**Historical gap:** Lyral (the definitive muguet material, 1960-2015) is IFRA-banned. True Diorissimo also used neroli and natural jasmine which are unavailable. This version rebuilds the classical muguet using `Hydroxycitronellal` (1909, the original), `Farnesol`, `Linalool`, and classical jasmine-rose supports — materials that would be recognizable to a 1956 perfumer.  
**Why this structure matters:** Muguet is one of the purest demonstrations of synthetic floral architecture: a flower that cannot be directly extracted but can be built persuasively by accord design.
