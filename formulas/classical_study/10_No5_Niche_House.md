# Niche House Aldehydic Floral — 1921 Luxury Study — 30 mL EdP

**Historical reference:** Chanel No. 5 (1921) — reimagined as a 1920s niche house creation using premium materials available to a luxury perfumer of the era.

**Philosophy:** The original No.5 was deliberately synthetic-forward — a revolutionary abstract. This version asks: what if a 1920s niche house (think De Laire, Guerlain's private collection, or an independent Parisian perfumer) had the budget and access to the finest materials AND the new aldehydes? The result: aldehydes as a bright accent over a foundation of real jasmine, natural rose materials, classical musks, amber resins, and delicate animalic traces.

**Concentration:** 20.0% EdP · 6,000 µL concentrate in 30 mL
