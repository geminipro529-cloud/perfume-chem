# Citrus Aromatic — Eau Sauvage Study (Period-Correct 1966) — 30 mL EdT

**Historical reference:** Dior Eau Sauvage (1966, Edmond Roudnitska)
**Family archetype:** `citrus_aromatic.eau_sauvage_reference`
**Concentration:** 12.0% EdT · 3,600 µL concentrate in 30 mL
**Approach:** Stripped of all post-1966 materials (Iso E Super invented 1973, Galaxolide 1975, Romandolide 1990s, Grapefruit FCF modern blender, Amberwood F modern, Black Pepper EO not in original), replaced with period-appropriate stacking: higher Bergamot/Lavender/Rosemary/Clary Sage for the classical aromatic body, higher Coumarin for the coumarinic base, higher Cedarwood/Vetiver/Patchouli for the woody-mossy anchor, and animalic Musk Ketone for the original's dirty musk register. Hedione halved to period-appropriate level (8% vs 16%).
