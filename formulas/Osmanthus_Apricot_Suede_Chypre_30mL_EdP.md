# Osmanthus Apricot-Suede Chypre — 30mL EdP

## Character
A warm vintage chypre anchored by osmanthus absolute — apricot-lactonic fruit meets suede leather over classic oakmoss-patchouli bones. The osmanthus bridges the fruity top and leathery base naturally. Think Mitsouko reimagined with an osmanthus heart.

**Family**: Chypre (classic) — bergamot top, oakmoss-patchouli base, floral-fruity heart
**Materials**: 23 (Carles-optimal 15-40 range)
**Concentration**: 16% EdP
**Literature**: Calkin & Jellinek (1994) chypre ratios; Hong et al. (2023) GC-O osmanthus decomposition
