# DHC V Mandarin -- 50 mL EdP (14%) -- House-Reviewed

**Profile:** Golden sunset. Tom Ford sensual warmth, Malle perfumer's balance.
**Reviewed by:** Tom Ford + Frederic Malle
**Concentrate:** 7818 uL raw / 7144 active  |  Ethanol: ~42 mL
