# DHC IV Lime -- 50 mL EdP (14%) -- House-Reviewed

**Profile:** Transparent ghost. Hermes Ellena water-color, Byredo skin-feel.
**Reviewed by:** Hermes + Byredo
**Concentrate:** 8423 uL raw / 6809 active  |  Ethanol: ~42 mL

**Hermes:** Scentenal -- the water. The lime should feel like a freshly cut wedge on a wet counter. Floralozone for Ellena transparency. Vetival skeleton.
**Byredo:** More Ambrofix -- Ben Gorham skin-feel. More IES cocoon -- Byredo fragrances cling to fabric.
