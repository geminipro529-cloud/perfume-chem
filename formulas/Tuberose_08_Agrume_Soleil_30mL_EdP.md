# Tuberose Agrume Soleil — 30mL EdP

## Character
A solar citrus floral where Tuberose Absolute's narcotic white floral basks under a radiant multi-citrus canopy — tuberose in Mediterranean sunshine. A four-material citrus opening (Bergamot FCF Sicilian, Lime Distilled EO, Pamzest, Lemonile) creates a complex, multi-dimensional hesperidic top that reads as "sunlight on citrus groves." The tuberose-jasmine-ylang heart is deliberately lightened — the goal is "citrus cologne with a tuberose heart," not "tuberose with citrus on top." Aurantiol and Oranger Crystals add neroli-orange blossom radiance that bridges the citrus top to the white floral heart. Ylang Extra (the Extra grade, not Complete) provides a brighter, more top-note-forward ylang that supports the solar character. Ambrofix provides ambergris-mineral lift, while Zenolide and Romandolide create a clean-citrusy projection musk chord. A touch of Vanillin and Coumarin prevents the citrus from burning off without a trace. Think Eau de Cologne Solaire with tuberose — the brightest, most radiant tuberose in this collection.

**Family**: Citrus Floral — radiant multi-citrus top, tuberose white floral heart, mineral-citrusy transparent base

## Material Justification

- **Tuberose Absolute (10% in DPG)**: Narcotic white floral anchor at 450 µL of 10% = 45 µL active (0.80% of concentrate). In this solar citrus context, tuberose's creamy-narcotic character is deliberately lightened — the goal is "citrus cologne with a tuberose heart," where tuberose provides depth rather than dominance. The tuberose sits beneath the citrus, emerging as the hesperidic top burns off.

- **Bergamot FCF Sicilian + Lime Distilled EO + Pamzest + Lemonile**: The four-material citrus canopy. Bergamot FCF Sicilian (350 µL) provides the richer, more complex bergamot — chosen over standard Bergamot FCF because the Sicilian grade's complexity supports the solar concept. Lime Distilled EO (150 µL) adds cold-tart lime freshness — chosen for its cleaner, less phototoxic profile vs. expressed lime. Pamzest (40 µL) is Synarome's powerful grapefruit zest specialty base — it provides a long-lasting grapefruit brightness that persists beyond the fleeting citrus top notes. Lemonile (50 µL neat) adds a nitrile-citrus sparkle with excellent tenacity — it bridges the citrus top into the floral heart. Together, these four create a multi-dimensional citrus that reads as "sunlight" rather than "cleaning product."

- **Aurantiol + Oranger Crystals (10% in DPG) + Nerolin Bromelia (10% in DPG)**: The neroli-orange blossom triad. Aurantiol (150 µL neat) is the schiff base of Hydroxycitronellal + Methyl Anthranilate — it provides warm, long-lasting neroli-orange blossom radiance with excellent tenacity. Oranger Crystals (10 µL active) adds neroli-grape-floral character. Nerolin Bromelia (5 µL active) contributes naphthyl-floral orange blossom volume — it floralizes the orange blossom register and bridges to the tuberose. Together they create the orange-blossom-solar bridge between citrus top and white floral heart — the key architectural innovation of this composition.

- **Ylang Ylang EO (Extra grade)**: Brighter ylang at 180 µL neat. The Extra grade was chosen over Ylang Comoros Complete because its higher top-note fraction provides more citrusy-benzyl-ester brightness that pairs with the solar citrus concept. The Complete grade's sesquiterpene base tenacity would add unwanted weight to this transparent composition.

- **Jasmine Sambac (10% in DPG)**: White floral depth at 250 µL of 10% = 25 µL active. At this restrained dose, Jasmine Sambac provides authentic jasmonate-indole character without darkening the solar brightness.

- **Lilyreal ND + Mayol + Hydroxycitronellal**: Muguet transparency triad at 150 + 120 + 180 µL. Lilyreal provides clean synthetic muguet body. Mayol adds transparent muguet-lily freshness — post-Lyral, cleaner, fresher. Hydroxycitronellal provides lily-of-the-valley volume and the schiff-base chemistry that creates Aurantiol. Together they create a sheer, transparent floral body that supports the tuberose without competing.

- **Paradisamide (10%)**: Tropical fruit sparkle at 80 µL of 10% = 8 µL active. Paradisamide's guava-passionfruit character reads as "sun-ripened fruit" in this solar context. Its 150hr tenacity means the fruit sparkle persists alongside the tuberose.

- **Pamzest**: Grapefruit zest specialty base at 40 µL neat. Pamzest provides a powerful, long-lasting grapefruit brightness — it's the material that makes the citrus top persist beyond the typical 5-minute bergamot evaporation. Chosen because standard grapefruit materials (Grapefruit FCF) evaporate too quickly for a solar citrus concept.

- **Lemonile**: Citrus nitrile sparkle at 50 µL neat. Lemonile's nitrile-citrus character has surprisingly low VP for a citrus material — it bridges the citrus top into the heart with persistence that natural citrus oils cannot achieve. Chosen over Citral (too fleeting, too sharp) and D-Limonene (too terpenic-oxidative).

- **Hexyl Salicylate + Benzyl Salicylate**: Fixative gradient at 220 + 350 µL. Hexyl Salicylate is the primary fixative in this composition — its light green-floral transparent character preserves the solar transparency. Benzyl Salicylate provides the cosmetic diffusion cushion at a reduced dose (350 vs. 450–500 µL in heavier compositions) — enough to carry the tuberose heart, not enough to add cosmetic weight.

- **Iso E Super**: Molecular skin cocoon at 450 µL. Provides abstract-cedar skin-adhesion. In this solar context, Iso E Super creates the "phantom citrus" trail — the molecular halo that extends the citrus impression beyond its natural lifespan.

- **Ebanol + Sandalore**: Sandalwood pair at 70 + 60 µL. Ebanol provides creamy-milky sandalwood at a restrained dose — enough to support the tuberose creaminess without weighing down the citrus. Sandalore adds fresh-citrus-woody lift that pairs with the solar citrus concept. Both are at lower doses than in heavier compositions.

- **Zenolide + Romandolide + Ethylene Brassylate**: 3-axis clean-citrusy musk chord. Zenolide (100 µL) = cold citrusy-fresh character-echo — the "sunlight" musk that echoes the citrus top into the drydown. Romandolide (200 µL) = woody-diffusive projection that extends the solar radiance outward. EB (150 µL) = creamy depth at a restrained dose that anchors without weighing down.

- **Ambrofix (30% w/v)**: Mineral-ambergris depth at 80 µL of 30% = 24 µL active. In this solar context, Ambrofix's crystalline-mineral character reads as "sunlight on white stone" — the transparent mineral lift that makes the composition radiant rather than heavy. Replaces Ambrox Super 30% (DEPLETED).

- **Vanillin (10%) + Coumarin (20%)**: Sheer sweet base at 50 + 40 µL. Vanillin (5 µL active) adds a whisper of creamy sweetness. Coumarin (8 µL active) provides hay-almond warmth. Both at minimal doses — the solar citrus concept requires the lightest possible base.

- **Cashmeran (neat)**: Textile warmth at 50 µL. At exactly the 50 µL/6mL non-oriental ceiling, Cashmeran adds a soft cashmere undercurrent that prevents the transparent base from reading as clinical.

- **Vertofix**: Woody-musky bridge at 80 µL. Glues the Iso E Super to the sandalwood-musk base.
