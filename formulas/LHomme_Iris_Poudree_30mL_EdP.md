## Concept

A powdery iris that radiates without shouting. Opens with the green-spice lift of black pepper + cardamom over a soft nerol-floral citrus frame, never cologne-shout. The heart is the **iris-violet-musky accord** — alpha irone provides the powdery orris butter, alpha isomethyl ionone adds the violet-softness, helional gives the watery-fresh lift that keeps it from being a lipstick iris (Dior Homme territory). The base is Andrier's signature: **iso e super + cedramber + ambrox + vertofix** for the abstract-cedar-amber-mineral depth, capped by a transparent musk chord that sits on skin (Habanolide + Ambrettolide) rather than projects outward.

This is **Woody Chypre / Powdery Iris** at 22% concentrate — deliberately intimate, deliberately under-stated, the way the original L'Homme was designed to be worn in a boardroom or on a Sunday morning.

**Family:** Woody Chypre (Powdery Iris direction)
**Concentration:** 32.3% in ethanol (EdP+)
**Luxe Differentiators:** Alpha Irone (powdery orris, the "Dior Homme à la Prada"), Nympheal (radiant muguet for the cosmetic-clean side), Helional (the watery freshness that keeps L'Homme from going lipstick), dual salicylate gradient (Hexyl on top of Benzyl for transparent-then-cushion fixation), Ambrettolide (the "quiet skin warmth" macrocyclic, more naturalistic than Habanolide alone).

## Accord Breakdown

- **Green-spice top:** Bergamot FCF Sicilian (richer bergamot, Sicilian quality) + Black Pepper EO (the L'Homme signature spice) + Cardamom EO (trace aromatic, tea-like warmth) + Nerol (neroli substitute, sweet-rose floral) + Linalool (the "freshly-ironed" facet)
- **Iris-violet heart:** Alpha Irone (powdery orris) + Alpha Isomethyl Ionone (soft violet, not lipstick) + Orivone (buttery iris) + Helional (watery-mineral lift, prevents lipstick-iris) + Nympheal (radiant muguet bridge) + Hedione (radiance amplifier) + PEA (skin-rose-floral)
- **Abstract wood-mineral base:** Iso E Super (workhorse wood, dominant base) + Cedramber (sheer cedar transparency) + Ambrox Super (crystalline mineral ambergris) + Amberwood F (invisible amber-wood warmth)
- **Musk veil:** Habanolide (depth) + Ambrettolide (naturalistic character-echo) + Macrolide (whisper dimension)
- **Transparent fixative gradient:** Hexyl Salicylate (light, green-floral, transparent) layered OVER Benzyl Salicylate (heavier cushion for diffusion) — the L'Homme signature is that it is fixed but never heavy

## Dosing & Mixing Notes

1. **Order of addition:** Add ethanol last. Dispense concentrate materials largest-volume-first (Iso E Super, Alpha Irone, Ambrox Super, Hexyl Salicylate, Bergamot FCF Sicilian), then work down to trace amounts (Cardamom EO, Macrolide).
2. **Alpha Irone at 400 µL of 10%** is the largest single dilution dispense. This is the central iris material — it MUST be accurate. Use the 100-1000 µL pipette if available, or dispense 200+200 with the 200 µL pipette.
3. **Maceration:** Allow 2 weeks minimum before evaluating. Iris-violet materials often read "sharp" or "metallic" in the first days and need time to soften. The Habanolide-Ambrettolide skin-musks also need 2-3 weeks to fully bloom.
4. **Viscous materials:** None in this formula. All materials are liquid or pre-diluted.
5. **Intimate projection note:** This formula is designed for **arm's-length sillage** — you wear it for yourself and the people within 1-2 feet. If you want more projection, increase Iso E Super and Ambrox Super by 30%. If you want even more intimacy, decrease them.
