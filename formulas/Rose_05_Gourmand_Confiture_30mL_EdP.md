# Rose Gourmand Confiture — 30mL EdP

## Character
A gourmand rose built around the jammy, honeyed character of Rose de Mai Absolute — the natural rose becomes the center of an edible, confiture-like composition. Raspberry Ketone and Paradisamide create a berry-jam accord that wraps around the rose heart, while Vanillin, Coumarin, Ethyl Vanillin, and Maple Lactone build a patisserie base. The rose core is deliberately clean (Geraniol at 60 µL, no Rhodinol) because the gourmand direction needs a clear, bright rose silhouette rather than complex green-geranium facets. Damascone Beta contributes the dark-fruity rose ketone character that naturally bridges the floral to the berry-gourmand, while Gamma Undecalactone adds a peach-skin lactonic cushion. The musk chord (Ethylene Brassylate creamy depth, Musk Ketone powdery character-echo, Macrolide gentle skin-sit) keeps the base soft and skin-close rather than aggressively sweet. Think Angel's rose facet married to the jammy rose of Parfums de Marly Delina, rendered as a transparent gourmand rather than a heavy oriental.

**Family**: Gourmand Floral (confiture-rose) — berry-citrus top, rose-jam heart, vanilla-lactonic-musk base

## Material Justification

- **Rose de Mai Absolute (10%)**: The natural rose anchor at 400 µL (40 µL active, 0.70% of concentrate). The absolute's intrinsic honeyed-jammy character makes it the perfect natural material for a gourmand rose — unlike molecular rose components, Rose de Mai already possesses the confiture-like sweetness that the gourmand concept amplifies. At 400 µL it provides enough natural complexity without overwhelming the edible accords.

- **Citronellol + Geraniol + PEA + PEDMC + Nerol**: The molecular rose accord — 5 materials but deliberately omitting Rhodinol because the geranium-green facets of Rhodinol would clash with the confiture direction. Citronellol (260) provides fresh-citrus-rose lift. Geraniol (60) at the new maximum — sweet-geranium body. PEA (350) fills honeyed-rose volume. PEDMC (160) — the key differentiator — adds the cleaner, magnolia-rose character that pairs better with gourmand sweetness than the earthy richness of Rhodinol. Nerol (80) adds sweet citrusy-rose lift that connects the rose to the Blood Orange top.

- **Damascone Beta (10%) + Damascol (10%) + Rose Oxide (10%)**: The rose modifier triad. Damascone Beta at 90 µL (9 µL active) provides dark, fruity-rose ketone — this is the critical bridge between rose and berry-jam, as damascones naturally possess a plum-apple fruitiness. Damascol at 70 µL (7 µL active) adds softer rose-plum character that extends the fruity bridge further. Rose Oxide at 60 µL (6 µL active) adds just enough metallic-green freshness to prevent the composition from becoming cloying. Chose Damascol over Damascenone because the softer, rounder character pairs better with gourmand than Damascenone's intense honeyed-woody depth.

- **Raspberry Ketone + Paradisamide (10%)**: The berry-jam accord. Raspberry Ketone (120 µL neat) provides the authentic raspberry character — the ionone-related ketone that smells of ripe red berries. Paradisamide at 80 µL of 10% (8 µL active) adds the tropical-fruity complexity — guava-passionfruit-cassis facets that make the berry accord read as "confiture" rather than "candy." Paradisamide's 150-hour tenacity means the berry impression persists into the drydown. Neither material is a muguet material — Paradisamide's tropical-fruity character is exactly what this accord needs.

- **Blood Orange Sicilian**: The juicy citrus top at 300 µL — chosen over Bergamot FCF because Blood Orange's sweet-tart, less-terpenic character provides the citrus-fruitiness that introduces the confiture theme. Bergamot FCF's classical cologne freshness would pull the opening toward chypre territory — Blood Orange keeps it in the edible-gourmand register. Linalool + Linalyl Acetate extend the citrus opening without adding bergamot character.

- **Hedione HC**: Radiance amplifier at 520 µL — lifts the rose-jam heart with jasmine radiance. Essential for preventing the gourmand base from pulling the fragrance inward. High-cis for maximum floral lift.

- **Gamma Undecalactone + Delta Decalactone**: The lactonic cushion at 40 + 30 µL. Gamma Undecalactone provides the classic peach-skin lactone character — the "aldehyde C14" peachy softness that sits beneath the berry-jam accord. Delta Decalactone adds a creamier, coconut-adjacent lactonic warmth. Together they create the lactonic "cushion" effect that makes the gourmand feel soft and skin-close rather than sharp or sugary. Both at trace doses because lactones are powerful — too much and the composition becomes fatty rather than confiture-like.

- **Vanillin (10%) + Coumarin (20%) + Ethyl Vanillin (10%) + Maple Lactone (20%)**: The gourmand base quartet. Vanillin at 200 µL of 10% (20 µL active) provides the classic vanilla sweetness — the structural gourmand anchor. Coumarin at 160 µL of 20% (32 µL active) adds the hay-like coumarinic softness with almond undertones. Ethyl Vanillin at 100 µL of 10% (10 µL active) provides the more intense, sweeter vanilla facet — roughly 3× stronger than Vanillin, so this 10 µL active reads like ~30 µL of Vanillin. Maple Lactone at 80 µL of 20% (16 µL active) contributes the maple-caramel facet that makes the confiture read as "confiture de rose" rather than "rose with vanilla." All materials within family-shifting limits — this IS a gourmand, so the ceilings don't apply.

- **Ethylene Brassylate**: Creamy depth musk at 320 µL — the highest musk dose because a gourmand needs the lactonic-milky skin-sit that only EB provides. Its creamy quality echoes the vanilla-lactonic base, creating a seamless musk-to-gourmand transition. Chosen over all other musk options because EB is the only musk that directly reinforces the creamy-lactonic gourmand register.

- **Musk Ketone (10%)**: Character-echo musk at 240 µL of 10% (24 µL active). The powdery talcum character that defines the confectionery-powder texture — in a gourmand, the powder note creates the "macaron" or "dragée" effect. No other musk can provide this: Musk Ketone's nitro-powder character is unique and irreplaceable for the confiture concept.

- **Macrolide (10%)**: Gentle skin-sit musk at 160 µL of 10% (16 µL active). The "barely-there" musk — chosen because a gourmand rose should not project aggressively through musks. Macrolide's whisper-soft character allows the rose and berry to dominate while providing just enough musky skin-adhesion. Chosen over Romandolide (too projecting — would push the gourmand outward aggressively) and Habanolide (too warm-skin — would add an animalic dimension that clashes with the confiture purity).

- **Hexyl Salicylate + Benzyl Benzoate**: Fixative pair. Hexyl Salicylate (180) as the sheer green-floral fixative — holds the Blood Orange-Raspberry top without adding cosmetic weight. Benzyl Benzoate (200) as inert mass. NO Benzyl Salicylate — its heavy cosmetic character would add a vintage-cosmetics note that fights the modern gourmand direction.

- **Iso E Super**: Molecular skin-cocoon at 400 µL — the abstract cedar halo that creates skin-level resonance. Dosed lower than in the rose chypre (450) because a gourmand needs less woody architecture — the vanilla-lactonic base IS the architecture.

- **Ambrofix (30%) + Sandalore + Vertofix**: The transparent structural trio. Ambrofix (120 µL, 36 active) adds crystalline depth — the cold mineral point that prevents vanilla sweetness from becoming cloying. Sandalore (160 µL) provides the lightest sandalwood — its citrus-woody freshness keeps the base airy. Vertofix (160) bridges the woody and musk layers. Cashmeran (100) adds textile-warmth — the "cashmere scarf" effect that softens the entire composition without adding oriental weight.
