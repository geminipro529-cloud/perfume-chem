# Tuberose Coco Exotique — 30mL EdP

## Character
A narcotic white floral anchored by Tuberose Absolute over a creamy coconut-lactonic heart, supported by tropical florals and a warm woody-sandalwood-musk base. The tuberose-jasmine-ylang axis creates a heady white floral bouquet, while the gamma/delta lactone trio delivers a coconut-cream texture that reads as "tropical exotic" rather than gourmand. Paradisamide injects a guava-passionfruit spark that brightens the floral core without pulling it into fruity-floral territory. Think Fracas meets Virgin Island Water — tuberose on a beach.

**Family**: Fruity Floral — white floral anchor, tropical fruit accent, lactonic-creamy body, woody-sandalwood-musk base

## Material Justification

- **Tuberose Absolute (10% in DPG)**: The narcotic white floral anchor at 450 µL of 10% = 45 µL active (0.8% of concentrate). Tuberose carries a natural methyl benzoate/salicylate profile that integrates seamlessly with the synthetic salicylate fixative layer. Chosen because no other material in inventory delivers the creamy-narcotic-camphoraceous white floral register that defines this composition.
- **Gamma Decalactone + Gamma Undecalactone + Delta Decalactone**: The coconut-creamy lactone triad. Gamma Decalactone (60 µL) is the primary coconut carrier — the most overtly "coconut" of the aliphatic lactones. Gamma Undecalactone (40 µL) adds peachy-creamy body that bridges the lactone axis to the tuberose heart. Delta Decalactone (30 µL) contributes a milky-buttery depth that prevents the coconut note from reading as sunscreen. Rejected: Maple Lactone (too maple-syrup gourmand), Raspberry Ketone (wrong fruit register).
- **Ylang Comoros Complete EO F3255**: The tropical floral body at 180 µL. Ylang's banana-custard-benzyl ester profile is the natural companion to tuberose — both share methyl benzoate and benzyl salicylate facets. Ylang Extra was rejected because the Complete grade provides more base-note tenacity from the sesquiterpene fraction, essential for bridging the floral heart into the woody drydown.
- **Jasmine Sambac (10% in DPG)**: Indolic white floral depth at 300 µL of 10% = 30 µL active. Sambac was chosen over Jasmine FO because it provides the authentic indolic-jasmonate character needed to give the tuberose heart narcotic gravity. Jasmine FO was rejected as too generic and sweet.
- **Champaca Flower EO**: Exotic floral accent at 80 µL. Champaca adds a spicy-green-tea floral facet distinct from both tuberose and ylang. Its indolic-camphoraceous character reinforces tuberose's natural complexity without redundancy. The dose (80 µL) is deliberately restrained — champaca can dominate at higher levels.
- **Paradisamide (10%)**: Tropical-fruity guava-passionfruit spark at 100 µL of 10% = 10 µL active. Paradisamide's 150hr tenacity means this fruit note persists deep into the drydown, creating the "exotic" character that distinguishes this from a plain white floral. At 10 µL active it's within the 0.1-1% typical range — perceptible but not candy-like.
- **Hedione HC (high cis)**: Radiance amplifier at 600 µL. High-cis jasmonate provides more floral lift than standard Hedione, expanding the tuberose-jasmine heart outward into sillage. Chosen over standard Hedione because the high-cis form has better tenacity and pairs more effectively with Jasmine Sambac's natural jasmonate content.
- **Methyl Benzoate + Benzyl Acetate**: White floral support pair. Methyl Benzoate (40 µL) reinforces tuberose's natural top note — tuberose absolute naturally contains ~3-5% methyl benzoate, so this dose amplifies rather than adds a new note. Benzyl Acetate (150 µL) provides the fresh-green-jasmine facet that bridges the floral heart to the bergamot top. Both are natural constituents of tuberose and ylang.
- **Damascone Beta (10%)**: Fruity-rose depth at 60 µL of 10% = 6 µL active. The beta isomer provides a plum-rose dimension that fills the gap between the lactones and the white florals. Chosen over Damascone Alpha (more metallic-rose, would clash with tuberose's creaminess) and Damascenone (too intense, would push the composition toward rose soliflore).
- **Phenethyl Alcohol + Citronellol**: Rose body pair at 200 + 150 µL. PEA provides the classic rose alcohol volume; Citronellol adds the rosy-citronella freshness that lifts the lactone-heavy heart. Together they create a rosy bridge between the green-citrus top and the white floral heart without competing with the tuberose anchor.
- **Bergamot FCF + Blood Orange oil Sicilian**: Citrus top pair. Bergamot (400 µL) provides the classic hesperidic-linalyl opening that pairs with Linalool and Linalyl Acetate. Blood Orange (200 µL) injects juicy-sweet warmth — its lower terpene content prevents the top from burning off too fast, and its sweetness foreshadows the lactone heart. Rejected: Grapefruit FCF (too bitter-dry for this creamy composition), Cedrat (too sharp-mineral).
- **Parmavert**: Green violet-leaf freshness at 30 µL, replacing cis-3-Hexenol (not in inventory). Parmavert's violet-leaf green is softer than Leafovert's cut-grass, pairing more elegantly with the white floral heart. At 30 µL it provides green lift without introducing a sharp galbanum edge.
- **Methyl Pamplemousse (10% in TEC)**: Grapefruit-rhubarb tartness at 80 µL of dilution. Adds a tart-fresh counterpoint to the creamy lactones, preventing the heart from becoming cloying. The rhubarb facet pairs unexpectedly well with Paradisamide's guava character.
- **Ebanol + Sandalore**: Sandalwood duo at 100 + 80 µL. Ebanol provides creamy-milky sandalwood texture that reinforces the lactone axis in the base. Sandalore adds fresh-citrus-woody lift that prevents the sandalwood from reading as heavy. Rejected: Javanol (too dry-mineral, would fight the creamy character), Bacdanol (too heavy-milky, would compete with EB).
- **Iso E Super**: Molecular skin cocoon at 500 µL. Provides the abstract-cedar skin-adhesion effect that anchors the entire composition. Chosen over Timberol (too architectural-pencil for a creamy floral), Azarbre (cedar-amber warmth that would push toward amber-oriental), and Cedarwood EO (too literal-cedar, would clash with sandalwood).
- **Vanillin (10%) + Coumarin (20%)**: Creamy-sweet base pair at 100 + 80 µL. Vanillin (10 µL active) adds a sweet-creamy undercurrent that supports the lactone axis into the drydown. Coumarin (16 µL active) provides hay-almond warmth and a subtle tobacco facet. Both are within the safe non-oriental ceilings per the family-shifting rules.
- **Ethylene Brassylate**: Depth musk at 200 µL. The lactonic-creamy quality of EB is the natural musk partner for a coconut-lactone composition — it reinforces rather than contradicts the heart character. Chosen over Habanolide because EB's overt creaminess supports the concept, whereas Habanolide's warm-skin intimacy would add a separate warm register.
- **Romandolide**: Projection musk at 150 µL. Romandolide's diffusive woody-musk character extends the sillage architecture outward, projecting the tuberose-jasmine bouquet rather than letting it sit on skin. Chosen over Galaxolide (too synthetic-clean, would fight the naturalistic floral character) and Zenolide (too cold-citrusy).
- **Ambrettolide (10% in DPG)**: Character-echo musk at 80 µL of 10% = 8 µL active. The most naturalistic macrocyclic musk — its fruity-wine warmth echoes Paradisamide's tropicality and provides "quiet skin warmth" without competing with EB's lactonic depth.
- **Cashmeran (neat)**: Textile warmth at 80 µL. Adds a soft cashmere-textile undercurrent that rounds the woody-sandalwood base and prevents the musks from reading as clinical. At 80 µL it's safely below the 50 µL/6mL oriental-shifting ceiling (~48 µL/6mL equivalent).
- **Vertofix**: Woody-musky bridge at 100 µL. The amber-cedarwood fixative character glues the Iso E Super molecular layer to the sandalwood-musk base. Its vetivert-acetate character adds a subtle dry-woody texture without introducing actual vetiver.
- **Ambrofix (30% w/v)**: Mineral-amber depth at 80 µL of 30% = 24 µL active. Provides a crystalline-transparent amber register that adds lift to the base without warmth. Chosen over Ambrox Super 30% (DEPLETED) and Ambermax (too warm-rounded for this composition). Ambrofix crystals dissolved at 30% w/v provide the same effect as Ambrox Super with slightly smoother character.
