# Allure Homme Sport Luxe — 30 mL EdP

**Luxury reinterpretation of Chanel Allure Homme Sport (Jacques Polge, 2004).**  
**Family:** Woody Aquatic · Aldehydic Fresh (neo-fougère, luxury grade)  
**Concentration:** ~20% EdP · ~5,900 µL concentrate in 30 mL  
**Character:** Blood orange-aldehydic sparkle, neroli-pepper-cardamom heart, creamy tonka-sandalwood-macrocyclic musk drydown. No laundry detergent. No generic citrus. No Galaxolide.

## What Changes from the Original

| Original AHS | Luxe Replacement | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Generic orange/mandarin oils | Blood Orange Sicilian + Red Mandarin EO + Bergamot FCF Sicilian | Richer, juicier citrus with less terpenic burn |
| Calone-heavy marine accord | Helional + Undecavertol (violet-leaf aquatic) | More sophisticated green-ozonic; no cheap watermelon |
| Galaxolide-dominated musk | 5-musk macrocyclic chord (EB + Romandolide + Ambrettolide + Exaltolide + Musk Ketone) | No laundry-detergent clean; naturalistic skin warmth |
| Synthetic neroli accord | Petitgrain EO Paraguay + Oranger Crystals + Nerolin Bromelia + Aurantiol | Real citrus-floral complexity, schiff base tenacity |
| Iso E Super-heavy woody | Cedarwood EO + Timberol + Koavone + Iso E Super (reduced) | Identifiable cedar character, not just abstract halo |
| Synthetic pepper | Black Pepper EO + Cardamom EO | Aromatic spice depth; the cardamom-rose synergy lifts the neroli |
| Generic amber | Ambrofix + Labdanum Resinoid | Smoother, more natural amber without crystalline edge |
| Synthetic vanilla | Vanillin + Siam Benzoin + Tonkarome (Firmenich) | Multi-layered: vanilla sweetness + balsamic almond + coumarinic cream |
| No galbanum (reformulated out) | Galbanum EO at 0.13% | Restores Polge's original Antaeus-era green-metallic signature |
| Generic white musk | Ambrettolide (naturalistic fruity-musk) + Exaltolide (skin-fatty intimacy) | The "quiet skin warmth" that reads as luxury, not functional |

## The Musk Chord (5-Axis Luxury)

| Axis | Material | Dose | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Depth** | Ethylene Brassylate | 350 µL | Creamy-lactonic skin anchor — what you smell on skin 4+ hours later |
| **Projection** | Romandolide | 200 µL | Diffusive clean-woody — what extends outward from skin |
| **Natural warmth** | Ambrettolide 10% | 100 µL | Fruity-musky wine-like — the "something sexy" whisper |
| **Skin intimacy** | Exaltolide 10% | 80 µL | Skin-fatty lactonic — the "your skin but better" quality |
| **Character-echo (powder)** | Musk Ketone 10% | 50 µL | Nitro-musk talcum — connects to ionone powder axis |

No Galaxolide. No Tonalide. No Habanolide. The luxury musk chord uses only macrocyclics (EB, Romandolide, Ambrettolide, Exaltolide) plus one nitro-musk (Musk Ketone) for powder character.

## Dosing Notes

- **Galbanum EO**: 8 µL. Do NOT exceed 10 µL — flips from green-metallic to vegetal-swamp. This is the margin between "Chanel luxury" and "unwearable."
- **Aldehydes C10/C11/C12**: All at 1% dilution. If using neat, divide volumes by 100. These are among the most powerful materials in the formula despite being only 40 µL each.
- **Tonkarome 20%**: Pre-diluted in TEC. Contains Firmenich's tonka base — more complex than pure coumarin. Do not substitute with neat coumarin at equal volume.
- **Ambrettolide 10%**: Already pre-diluted. 100 µL of dilution = 10 µL active. At this dose it provides the fruity-musk warmth without becoming animalic.
- **Maturation**: 4 weeks minimum sealed, cool, dark. The ionones, musks, and aldehydes require integration. Testing before 2 weeks: the aldehydes will dominate and the musk chord won't have bloomed.

## Material Rejections

- **No Calone** — watermelon-ozonic reads as cheap aquatic. Undecavertol + Helional provide the marine accord with sophistication.
- **No Galaxolide** — laundry-detergent clean. The 5-macrocyclic musk chord provides warmth without synthetic cleanliness.
- **No generic orange oil** — Blood Orange Sicilian + Red Mandarin + Bergamot FCF Sicilian = the premium citrus trio. Juicier, less terpenic, more character.
- **No synthetic black pepper** — Black Pepper EO provides terpene complexity (α-pinene, β-caryophyllene) that a single synthetic can't match.
- **No Ambrox Super** — too crystalline-mineral. Ambrofix is smoother; Labdanum provides the warm-amber body.
- **No Lilial / Lyral** — restricted muguet materials. Jasmine Sambac + Aurantiol provide the floral register without regulatory risk.
- **No DPG-heavy diluents for key materials** — Tonkarome is in TEC (less odor), coumarin at 20% is minimal DPG. The base should not be diluted by carrier solvents.
