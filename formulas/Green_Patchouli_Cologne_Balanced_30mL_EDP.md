## WHAT IT SMELLS LIKE

**Opening:** Bright bergamot-grapefruit with a black pepper sparkle — fresh, modern, inviting.

**Heart:** Lavender-geranium herbal warmth with a transparent vanilla-cream halo and a warm jasmine body beneath. The Dihydrojasmone reads not as "jasmine flower" but as warm, full-bodied texture.

**Base:** Dark patchouli-earth over Clearwood-chocolate, anchored by Vetiver's dry roots and Evernyl's mossy powder. The Ambrox-Cedramber-Galaxolide base gives clean mineral projection.

**Drydown:** Soft vanilla-touched patchouli musk. Warm, clean, familiar. No smoke, no animalics, no challenge. This is the version you can wear to the office and ask "does this smell OK?" and get yes.
