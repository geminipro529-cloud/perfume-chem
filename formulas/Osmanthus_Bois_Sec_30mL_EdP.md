# Osmanthus Bois Sec — 30mL EdP

## Character
Osmanthus apricot-tea blossom floating over a layered dry-cedar forest. Three wood tiers — warm aerial Koavone, architectural Timberol pillars, and extreme-tenacity Norlimbanol Dextro as the foundation. No creamy sandalwood, no chypre darkness. Clean, precise, modern. Think osmanthus meeting Aesop Hwyl in a Comme des Garcons bottle.

## Three-Tier Wood Layering Explained

```
Canopy (VP 0.15-0.01): Koavone + Iso E Super + Cedramber
  ─ The "forest air" — warm, balsamic cedar that reads at opening
  ─ Evaporates by heart phase, leaving...
  
Structure (VP 0.02-0.001): Timberol + Norlimbanol
  ─ The "bones" — dry architectural cedar that persists 6-8h
  ─ Timberol gives the character, Norlimbanol gives the power
  ─ Norlimbanol Dextro = transparent, you feel the wood without smelling it
  
Foundation: Romandolide + Ethylene Brassylate + Vertofix
  ─ The "floor" — musky-woody glue that holds the tiers together
```
