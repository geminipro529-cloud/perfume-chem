## Concept Lock

A glowing mandarin over a classical resin amber and a broad sandalwood body. Here, **amber means the resin structure**—labdanum, benzoin, olibanum, opoponax, Peru balsam, and myrrh. Ambrox Super and Amber Xtreme are subordinate structural supports, not the amber identity.

This formula uses **Recursive Deep Plane Perfumery**. Every macro-plane contains a miniature sequence of deep seed, body, contour, upper veil, and return thread. The completed perfume forms a loop rather than a one-way pyramid:

Deep residue → sandal body → living resin interior → amber identity → saffron-incense veil → mandarin glint → evaporation reveals the residue → the residue projects a transformed olibanum-saffron-citrus aura.

## Material Selection Rationale

- **Resin amber:** Labdanum is the leathery amber seed; Siam Benzoin supplies sweet balsamic volume; opoponax and myrrh darken and dry the interior; Peru balsam rounds the middle; olibanum provides the luminous incense return. This preserves the requested resin definition of amber.
- **Sandalwood recursion:** Ebanol supplies the dense kernel, Bacdanol the milky mass, and Azarbre the warmer cedar-amber contour. Their roles are differentiated; removing Ebanol thins the core, removing Bacdanol removes mass, and removing Azarbre disconnects sandalwood from the resin plane.
- **Modern amber supports:** Ambrox Super is retained only as a cool crystalline return against the warm resins. Amber Xtreme is a 5 µL-active pressure trace. Neither is allowed to replace the classical amber body.
- **Single musk:** Ethylene Brassylate is chosen for creamy, low-frequency diffusion that joins salicylate residue to sandalwood. A projection musk was not added because it would sharpen and clean the late amber aura.
- **Fixative gradient:** Benzyl Benzoate forms the quiet residue anchor; Benzyl Salicylate lifts the residue toward the floral-incense plane. Their functions are not interchangeable.
- **Opening:** Red Mandarin is the named luminous identity; Blood Orange adds a darker fruit body; Black Pepper supplies the shadow that prevents a soft, confectionary citrus opening.

## Recursive Compounding Procedure

1. Use one clean amber vial and add P6 through P1 in the formula-table order.
2. After every completed plane, cap and gently roll until homogeneous. The planes are compositional relationships, not literal unmixed liquid layers.
3. After the concentrate reaches 6,000 µL, mix until uniform.
4. Add approximately 22 mL ethanol 96%, mix, then add ethanol q.s. to the 30.00 mL final-volume mark.
5. Inspect clarity and color immediately, at 24 hours, 7 days, and 14 days. The saffron infusion and resinoids may produce deep color or later haze.
6. Evaluate on blotters at 0, 5, and 30 minutes; 2, 6, 24, and 48 hours. The decisive DPP question is whether the late residue produces a transformed olibanum-saffron-mandarin echo.
