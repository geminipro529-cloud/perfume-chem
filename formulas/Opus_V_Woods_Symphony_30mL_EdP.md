## CRITICAL WARNINGS

1. **Alpha Irone (30% in IPM)** — at this dose (1,110 µL), this is your most expensive material. Dose carefully. IPM is a solvent, not a fixative — be aware that the final 30 mL will have 3.7% IPM from this single addition.

2. **Genetic anosmia** — 8% of the population has reduced Beta Ionone perception; 20% has reduced Iso E Super perception. If the iris reads as "missing" to a tester, that's why.

3. **Maturation** — the formula reads "harsh" and "chemical" for the first 3 days. Do not re-dose or adjust during this window. The ionones need 14+ days to cross-adapt into "orris" percept.

4. **Storage** — keep in a dark glass bottle, away from heat. UV will oxidize limonene and linalool. BHT (0.01%) can be added if you have it, but isn't required for the first 6 months.
