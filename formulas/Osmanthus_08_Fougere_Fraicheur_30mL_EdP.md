# Osmanthus Fougère Fraîcheur — 30mL EdP

## Character
A modern aromatic fougère where osmanthus absolute replaces the traditional geranium-rose heart with an apricot-leather floral that pairs unexpectedly well with the lavender-coumarin-oakmoss skeleton. The osmanthus's tea-leather facet bridges the aromatic lavender top and the coumarinic base, while the apricot sweetness softens the typically-rigid fougère architecture. Think a more floral, less barbershop Azzaro pour Homme, or YSL Libre's fougère structure with osmanthus instead of orange blossom.

**Family**: Aromatic Fougère — lavender-coumarin-oakmoss, osmanthus replacing traditional floral, modern unisex

## Material Justification

- **Lavender EO (BONTAUX SAS, 300 µL)**: The defining fougère material — premium French lavender angustifolia with floral-herbaceous character and soft honeyed sweetness. Chosen over standard Lavender EO for its natural sweetness, which pairs with the osmanthus apricot. Spike Lavender EO (80 µL) adds the camphoraceous-aromatic edge for structure.

- **Geranium EO (80 µL)**: The traditional fougère heart material — fresh rosy-green with minty lift. At moderate dose, it provides the period-correct fougère heart character while leaving room for the osmanthus to be the primary floral. Not replaced by synthetics because the natural EO complexity is essential for fougère authenticity.

- **Juniper Berry EO (60 µL) + Rosemary EO (30 µL) + Cardamom EO (40 µL)**: The aromatic-herbal triad — juniper provides gin-like coniferous clarity, rosemary adds cineolic-camphor freshness, cardamom brings spicy-warmth. Together they create the modern aromatic complexity that distinguishes contemporary fougères from 1970s barbershop.

- **Terpinyl Acetate (60 µL)**: Pine-citrus-herbal acetate — pairs with lavender and cedar for coniferous lift. Chosen over Beta-Pinene because Terpinyl Acetate is more tenacious and less oxidation-prone, providing the pine facet throughout the heart rather than only in the top.

- **Azarbre (100 µL)**: Cedar-amber-warm, smooth, slightly musky — chosen over Ambrofix as the primary amber-wood because fougères benefit from warmth without crystalline edge. Azarbre bridges cedar and amber registers, adding the rounded warmth that modern fougères need without the clinical coldness of Ambrox.

- **Heliotropal (40 µL neat)**: Sweet-almond-powdery bridge — adds a soft-powder dimension that pairs with the coumarin base and prevents the lavender from reading as too sharp-herbal. The almond facet also echoes the osmanthus apricot.

- **Orivone (50 µL)**: Warm orris-butte base — adds a creamy-woody depth to the heart that softens the fougère's angular edges. The buttery iris facet pairs with the osmanthus leather-tea for a more polished, less aggressive fougère.

- **Musk Ketone (10%, 80 µL)**: Vintage nitro-musk powder — reinforces the classical fougère character. The powdery-sweet nitro musk pairs with coumarin's hay-like sweetness for the traditional fougère drydown.
