# Rose Chypre Classique — 30mL EdP

## Character
A classical rose chypre anchored by Rose de Mai Absolute — the natural rose heart beats against the bitter-green structure of oakmoss, patchouli, and labdanum. The rose accord is built from molecular rose components (citronellol, geraniol, PEA, rhodinol, nerol) layered around the natural absolute, while damascones and rose oxide add depth and realism. Think vintage Femme Rochas or a drier, more transparent Ungaro Diva — rose-forward chypre with bergamot sparkle and mossy leather drydown.

**Family**: Chypre (classic) — bergamot top, oakmoss-patchouli-labdanum base, rose-floral heart

## Material Justification

- **Rose de Mai Absolute (10%)**: The natural anchor at 400 µL (40 µL active, 0.68% of concentrate). Provides the living-rose character — honeyed, slightly spicy, with the natural complexity that molecular rose materials cannot replicate. Chosen over Osmanthus Abs or Tuberose Abs because rose is the core identity.

- **Citronellol + Geraniol + PEA + Rhodinol + Nerol**: The molecular rose accord. Citronellol (250, fresh-citrus-rose) and Geraniol (180, sweet-geranium-rose) form the primary rose skeleton. PEA (350) adds the honeyed-rose volume — the most neutral rose alcohol that fills without steering the character. Rhodinol ex Citronella (150) contributes the natural geranium-rose richness that bridges the molecular rose to the absolute. Nerol (100) adds the sweeter, citrusy-rose facet and freshens the heart. Together, these 5 materials create a full-spectrum rose accord that surrounds and amplifies the Rose de Mai absolute.

- **Damascone Beta (10%) + Damascenone (1%) + Rose Oxide (10%)**: The rose modifier triad. Damascone Beta at 100 µL (10 µL active) contributes the dark, fruity-rose ketone character — the plum-apple facet of rose. Damascenone at 80 µL (0.8 µL active) — the most powerful rose-impact molecule — provides the honeyed-woody depth that makes rose smell "real" rather than "geranium." Rose Oxide at 80 µL (8 µL active) adds the metallic-green geranium leaf facet. Together the three consume only ~19 µL active but dominate the rose character perceptually.

- **Hedione HC**: Radiance amplifier at 520 µL — expands the rose accord outward into the bergamot top, creating the jasmine-rose luminosity that prevents the chypre from reading as austere. High-cis chosen over standard Hedione for brighter floral lift and faster diffusion onset.

- **Bergamot FCF**: The classical chypre top at 450 µL — fresh, slightly bitter citrus that cuts through the rose density. Chosen over Bergamot FCF Sicilian (too rich — competes with rose) or Cedrat (too sharp — fights oakmoss). Petitgrain EO Paraguay (120 µL) adds the green-woody citrus lift that bridges bergamot to the mossy base. Standard Bergamot FCF at 450 µL is the reference chypre dose.

- **Linalool + Linalyl Acetate**: Bergamot co-factors. Linalool (250) provides the fresh-lavender-citrus lift that pairs with bergamot's own linalool content. Linalyl Acetate (100) extends bergamot's persistence into the heart — prevents the top from evaporating before the rose emerges.

- **Oakmoss Absolute (10%) + Patchouli EO + Evernyl**: The chypre trinity. Oakmoss at 200 µL (20 µL active) provides the bitter-green lichen character — the soul of any chypre. Patchouli EO at 180 µL anchors the earthy-camphoraceous base. Evernyl at 100 µL adds synthetic moss body — extends the oakmoss tenacity beyond what the natural absolute can deliver at IFRA-compliant doses.

- **Labdanum Resinoid (10%)**: The warm amber-resin undercurrent at 160 µL (16 µL active). Balances the bitter oakmoss dryness with balsamic sweetness — without it, the chypre reads as cold and clinical. Also provides the leather-amber bridge to the woody base.

- **Benzyl Salicylate + Hexyl Salicylate**: Fixative gradient — Benzyl Salicylate (420) as the heavy cosmetic cushion that diffuses the rose heart, Hexyl Salicylate (180) as the lighter green-floral film that holds the bergamot-petitgrain top without adding cosmetic weight. Chose Hexyl Salicylate over Benzyl Benzoate because the green-floral character of Hexyl Sal adds to the chypre's bitter-green identity rather than being odorless filler.

- **Ethylene Brassylate**: Creamy depth musk at 280 µL — sits closest to skin, its lactonic quality echoes the honeyed-rose of PEA and the absolute. Chosen over Exaltolide (which would add fatty notes that clash with the chypre's dry moss character).

- **Romandolide**: Projection musk at 220 µL — clean, diffusive, slightly woody. Extends the rose sillage outward without adding the synthetic-fruity character of Galaxolide. Chosen over Galaxolide to avoid detergent associations in a luxury chypre context.

- **Habanolide**: Skin-warmth intimate musk at 160 µL — the macrocyclic warmth that prevents the moss-patchouli base from reading as cold. Chosen over Ambrettolide (too fruity-wine for a chypre) and Zenolide (too cold, fights the warm labdanum).

- **Iso E Super**: Molecular skin-cocoon at 450 µL — adds the abstract cedar halo that resonates on skin without asserting identifiable wood character. Chosen over Azarbre (too warm for chypre architecture) and Timberol (too rigid — chypre benefits from Iso E's anosmia-prone blur effect).

- **Vertofix + Cedarwood EO**: Woody-musky bridge at 200 + 140 µL. Vertofix bridges the wood and musk layers — its amber-cedar character connects the labdanum warmth to the musks. Cedarwood EO provides natural woody identity — the pencil-shaving warmth that humanizes the abstract Iso E Super. Chose Cedarwood EO over Cedarwood Virginia for the warmer, less-smoky character that pairs better with rose.

- **Ambrofix (30%)**: Crystalline mineral amber at 80 µL (24 µL active). Adds transparent depth to the base without warmth — the cold structural counterpoint to the warm labdanum. Chosen over Ambrox Super (depleted) and Ambermax (too warm — would pull the chypre toward oriental). The Ambrofix mineral edge echoes the metallic-green facet of Rose Oxide.
