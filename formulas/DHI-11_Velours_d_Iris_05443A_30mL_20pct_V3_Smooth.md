## Why this replaces the fourteen-row control

The parent was useful as a sparse ablation control, but it made Ambrettolide
stand in for ambrette, pear, talc, skin, and the entire musk system. It also made
Tonkarome stand in for both coumarinic and vanillic warmth, and asked four iris
rows to carry the complete orris anatomy. Passing that formula through the old
gates proved only that a coarse marker skeleton was present.

V3 uses thirty rows because it has thirty independently testable jobs. Count is
still not evidence of complexity. The additional rows earn their place only by
creating a missing recognizer, a distinct depth perspective, or a reversible
handoff. If an omission causes no repeatable loss in coded evaluation, that row
must be removed.

## Target identity and evidence ceiling

The selected silhouette is the Dior Homme Intense re-edition associated with
the 2011–2014 05443/A package lineage:

- a short fine-lavender veil;
- a restrained pear-liqueur gleam bound into ambrette and talc;
- a dominant powdery, violet-mobile, root-textured iris object;
- coumarinic and vanillic warmth underneath the iris, never dessert-like;
- a delayed Virginia-cedar and vetiver counterform;
- a warm textile skin trace that preserves residual iris.

The current Dior page corroborates the long-running iris, ambrette pear-liqueur
and silk-talc, and Virginia-cedar architecture. A contemporaneous 2011 report
supports lavender, iris, ambrette, pear, cedar, and vetiver. Neither source
discloses the 05443/A concentrate formula. Velours d'Iris is therefore an
original DHI-inspired architecture study, not Dior's formula, a clone claim, or
evidence of sensory equivalence.

### Positive recognizers

1. Lavender is recognizable on application but is no longer the subject at 30
   minutes.
2. Pear appears as a small fermented-fruit light inside the musky powder, not
   as a freestanding juicy accord.
3. Iris remains the principal named object from 5 minutes through 2 hours and
   contains powder body, violet motion, cool root, and a dry late tail.
4. Warmth thickens the underside of iris without vanilla, tonka, cocoa, almond,
   or pastry being named before iris.
5. Cedar and vetiver become clearer after the heart maximum without an abrupt
   powder-to-pencil cut.
6. The late trace reads as dry wood, silk talc, iris skin, and warm textile.

### Immediate drift failures

- flat lipstick, chalk, or a single methyl-ionone wall;
- candy apple, pineapple, banana, peach, or obvious apricot fruit;
- barbershop lavender or lavender-tonka fougère;
- yellow mimosa, sunscreen salicylate, or floral soap;
- marzipan, pastry cream, vanilla frosting, chocolate, or praline;
- creamy sandalwood perfume, sharp cedar, root-smoke vetiver, or generic Iso E
  cloud;
- wine musk, detergent musk, or an anonymous thick musk blanket.

### Six noncompensatory handoffs

| Handoff | Source | Physical bridges | Target | Smoothness rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Lavender → pear | Lavender EO | Linalyl Acetate + E2MB + Linalool | Verdox pear body | lavender must decay as fruit appears; neither may vanish instantly |
| Pear → ambrette/talc | Verdox + Benzyl Acetate | Osmanthus + Ambrettolide | Mimosa + Ethylene Brassylate cushion | fruit must become texture, not sit above powder |
| Ambrette/talc → iris | Ambrettolide + Benzyl Salicylate | Mimosa + Alpha Ionone + Hedione | AIMI + Alpha Irone body | silk texture must remain while iris becomes dominant |
| Iris → warm shadow | AIMI + root triad | Orivone + Mimosa | Tonkarome + Isobutavan | warmth must thicken iris without becoming edible |
| Warm shadow → wood | Tonkarome + Isobutavan | Dihydro Beta Ionone + Vetival + Sandalore + Cashmeran | Virginia cedar + vetiver | wood must emerge through suede and textile, never as a hard cut |
| Wood → skin echo | cedar + vetiver | Iso E + Cashmeran + Ultralia | three-axis musk closure | the base must preserve an iris/talc memory and avoid laundry |

Smoothness is a separate endpoint from liking. A muddy blend does not pass, and
an apparently seamless transition created by erasing the source or target does
not pass. Each seam requires continuity **and** legibility of both endpoints.

3. **Talc cushion:** full Benzyl Salicylate + Mimosa + Ethylene Brassylate
   cushion versus partial and full omission. The full arm must be smoother and
   at least as iris-legible.
4. **Iris anatomy:** selected eight-row geometry versus a constant-active AIMI
   collapse and a late-tail omission. Each submodule needs repeatable loss.
5. **Warm seam:** Tonkarome + Isobutavan + Orivone versus Isobutavan omission
   and Orivone omission. Reject edible naming.
6. **Wood grading:** selected cedar/vetiver geometry versus ±40 µL exchange;
   Vetival and Sandalore remain fixed. Reject pencil, smoke, or creamy-wood
   takeover.
7. **Musk axes:** omit Romandolide first, then omit Ethylene Brassylate. Retain
   a support musk only if its distinct outward-trail or velvet loss is repeated.
8. **Whole formula:** current build versus documented 05443/A reference plus a
   hidden repeated control, all with the same ethanol lot, maturation,
   application mass, substrate, and room.

Evaluate opening, 5 min, 30 min, 2 h, and 4 h. Record identity, target fidelity,
