# CHIMIE Femme - Orchard / Lychee / Freesia R1
Date: 5 October 2026. Creative development formulation, not a reconstruction or commercial release.

The development lead is **pear-led crisp apple, fresh lychee, a restrained cool freesia-like heart, trace osmanthus texture, and polished pear musk/pale wood**. Its quality and consumer appeal are hypotheses to smell, not demonstrated results.

This file is the human-readable view of [the canonical JSON](CHIMIE_Femme_Orchard_Lychee_Freesia_R1_20261005.json). Detailed source and ingredient decisions are in [the chemical review](../docs/research/CHIMIE_Femme_Orchard_Lychee_Chemical_Review_20261005.md). The previous PW2 formula remains unchanged.

## What is ready, and what is not

- Ready: exact target percentages, named PW products, practical weighed working-stock charges, carrier accounting and a small first-trial procedure.
- Conditional: the actual products must be available and stocks must match the prescribed w/w strengths and carriers.
- Not done: physical mixing, smelling, skin-use clearance, stability testing, measured intensity/projection, or commercial comparison.
- No photographs, purchase receipts or lot questionnaire are needed to make a personal blotter trial. A stock strength still matters when calculating its mass.
- Orris Liquid is excluded. There is no Ambrox, superamber, vanilla, ethyl maltol, jasmine absolute or ylang in this R1.

## Locked target and mass basis

Pear is the protagonist; apple supplies bite/juice, lychee adds freshness rather than a rose bouquet. Protect fruit at 30-60 minutes, floral restraint, warm-climate wearability and a subordinate base. Reject generic tropical fruit, candy, shampoo, vegetal greens, peach cream, dominant rose and heavy woods.

Percentages below describe **named undiluted supplier products**, including whole naturals and trade grades, before deliberately added stock solvents. They are not claims that each product is 100% chemically pure, nor that all non-solvent constituents are known.

All quantities are **w/w**. The first trial contains approximately **2.000 g product equivalents in 10.000 g finished perfume (20% w/w)**. This is a development strength, not a category definition or safety clearance.

## Concentrate and mixing order

The table first makes a **3.157 g working concentrate**; the underlying undiluted-product target is 2.000 g. The percentages also define a stock-independent concentrate at any scale. Directly weighing the tiny undiluted traces requires a larger master or adequate equipment; use the stocks for this small batch.

1. Prepare/check the required stocks. Set out a capped vial, suitable balance, clean transfer tools and blotters. Work ventilated, protect eyes/skin and keep alcohol away from ignition sources.
2. Base basket: weigh Helvetolide, Iso E Super, Habanolide, Ambrettolide stock, Sandalore and Clearwood stock. Mix gently.
3. Heart basket: add Hedione, Hedione HC, Florol, PEDMCA, Verdox, and Nympheal/Peonile stocks.
4. Texture basket: add Osmanthus, Rose de Mai, Beta Ionone and Gamma Undecalactone stocks. Mix; inspect for homogeneity.
5. Fresh-fruit basket: add Ethyl Linalyl Acetate, Hexyl Acetate, Linalool, Geraniol, Nerol, citrus oils and the green/fruit/rose-oxide/damascenone stocks. Cap promptly between transfers to limit evaporation.
6. Record actual weights. If a wrong stock or substantial overshoot occurs, stop and calculate the resulting formula; do not silently call it R1.
7. Once homogeneous, add 6.843 g perfumery alcohol, mix and cap. That amount applies to the stated practical card. If using exact or changed stock amounts, recompute the remainder to the intended final mass.
8. Evaluate on blotter after roughly 48-72 hours and again after 1-2 weeks. These are handling checkpoints, not proven chemical maturation times. A development formula is not skin-use-cleared.

Basket order is a reproducible handling policy, not a claim that this order chemically makes the perfume superior. Within baskets, weigh larger quantities first. Solid/viscous-product handling follows its actual supplied form, not a guessed density.

## 30 mL without pretending g = mL

Do the 10 g first trial before making a larger bottle.

To maintain the same w/w composition, scale **every stock and the additional alcohol together**. Then fill 30 mL of the homogeneous final perfume at a recorded temperature, retaining any excess.

If finished-blend density rho has actually been measured in g/mL:

- 30 mL finished mass = 30 x rho g.
- Multiply every exact 10 g recipe charge by 3 x rho.
- Product-equivalent mass = 6 x rho g.

There is no fixed exact 30 mL gram card until rho is known. You do not need density for the initial weighed trial. Alcohol is not "24 mL" by default, and this working concentrate is not "6 mL of neat perfume."

Default: make **one 10 g R1**, not several 30 mL bottles. That is enough for first evaluation.

If you want discriminating comparisons, make small matched trials from common premixes or the full weighed recipe; do not try to remove ingredients from an already mixed bottle.

The two exact alternatives preserve total product mass and working-carrier mass; they intentionally change odorant identity. They do not claim dose equivalence of the replaced materials.

At 5 minutes, 30-60 minutes, 4 hours and next-day blotter, write one short answer each:

- What fruits do you recognize **before** reading the intended notes?
- Is the pear still clear in the heart?
- Is lychee fresh fruit or rose/geranium?
- Is the heart fruit-led, or just floral shampoo?
- Which is more enjoyable? Allow tie/cannot determine separately from intensity.

Use blind codes if comparing. For casual personal discovery one session is useful; it is not population evidence or a controlled consumer-win claim.

## Failure modes and next changes

- **Banana/solvent/shampoo opening:** test lower Hexyl Acetate or Verdox, one axis at a time; do not solve it by adding sweetness.
- **Pear missing despite attractive fruit:** consider a genuine, identity-verified Pear Ester microtrial. Public PW stock of ethyl 2,4-decadienoate was not established. Another ester is not equivalent.
- **Lychee becomes metallic rose:** reduce Rose Oxide and/or Geraniol in a newly weighed test; do not conceal it with jasmine.
- **Apricot/peach cream or leather:** compare lower/omitted Osmanthus/lactone. Do not automatically raise them for richness.
- **Fruit vanishes into generic musk:** reduce competing floral/base load in a declared new version before increasing all top esters.
- **Habanolide reads like ironed linen:** evaluate lower Habanolide versus the musk alternative.
- **Sandalwood/woody drydown dominates:** lower Sandalore/Iso E Super; this brief does not need superambers.
- **No perceptible value from traces:** remove the redundant material after a controlled comparison rather than keeping it for paperwork.

A request for less of a material cannot be executed as a negative addition to an existing bottle. Future bottle adjustments remain propose -> human confirm -> measured addition -> commit, with an explicit new composition. This document creates no bottle or inventory event.
