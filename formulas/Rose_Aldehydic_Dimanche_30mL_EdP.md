# Rose Aldehydic Dimanche — 30mL EdP

## Character
A soft aldehydic floral built around Rose de Mai Absolute — crystalline aldehyde sparkle lifts a tender rose-violet heart over a cosmetic-powdery base. The aldehydes (C10, C11, C12 MNA) create the champagne-bubble effervescence that makes the rose feel luminous rather than heavy. Beneath the aldehydic shimmer, a violet-ionone powder cloud softens the rose into a pastel dimension — think Chanel No. 5's aldehydic architecture crossed with the tender rose heart of YSL Paris, but rendered in a modern, transparent register with ambrofix-sandalore clarity in the base.

**Family**: Soft Floral (aldehydic) — aldehydic sparkle top, rose-violet heart, cosmetic-musk-powder base

## Material Justification

- **Rose de Mai Absolute (10%)**: The natural rose anchor at 350 µL (35 µL active, 0.59% of concentrate). Slightly lower dose than the Chypre Classique because the aldehydic architecture demands transparency — the rose must shimmer behind the aldehyde veil, not project through it. The absolute's honeyed-spicy natural complexity provides the human warmth that prevents the aldehydes from reading as clinical or soapy.

- **Aldehyde C10 (1%) + C11 (1%) + C12 MNA (1%)**: The aldehydic trio. C10 at 100 µL (1 µL active) provides the classic citrus-aldehydic sparkle — the "champagne cork" pop. C11 at 80 µL (0.8 µL active) adds the fatty-waxy aldehydic richness that rounds the sharp C10 edge. C12 MNA at 100 µL (1 µL active) contributes the creamy-metallic aldehydic character — the Chanel No. 5 reference note. All at 1% dilution per inventory — total active aldehydes: 2.8 µL, which is the correct trace range for aldehydic floral architecture (< 0.05% of concentrate).

- **Citronellol + Geraniol + PEA + PEDMC**: The molecular rose accord — 4 materials instead of 5 (no Rhodinol or Nerol) because the aldehydic architecture needs a cleaner, less complex rose silhouette. Citronellol (250) provides fresh-citrus-rose lift. Geraniol (180) adds sweet-geranium body. PEA (325) fills the honeyed-rose volume. PEDMC (150) — the key differentiator from the Chypre formula — adds a cleaner, magnolia-rose character that pairs better with aldehydes than the richer Rhodinol/Nerol. PEDMC's cleaner profile prevents the rose from muddying the aldehydic transparency.

- **Beta Ionone + Alpha Isomethyl Ionone**: The violet-powder cloud at 120 + 100 µL. Beta Ionone provides the classic woody-violet character — the orris-powder softness that diffuses the rose. Alpha Isomethyl Ionone adds the brighter, more floral methyl ionone facet that connects the violet cloud back to the rose heart. Together they create the pastel dimension that distinguishes an aldehydic floral from a straight rose soliflore. No Alpha Ionone needed — Beta Ionone + Methyl Ionone Pure is the cleaner, more modern violet pair.

- **Ylang Complete EO**: The creamy floral bridge at 100 µL. Ylang's banana-custard richness fills the gap between the sharp aldehydes and the powdery base — without it, the aldehydic→musk transition reads as disjointed. Chose Complete over Ylang III (too thin) and Ylang Extra (too narcotic — competes with the aldehydes). Ylang Complete EO provides the balanced creamy-floral warmth that humanizes the aldehydic architecture.

- **Heliotropal (neat)**: Almond-powder trace at 20 µL — the heliotrope-mimosa facet. At this dose, it whispers rather than speaks: a faint marzipan warmth that sits beneath the violet powder cloud. Chosen over Vanillin for the almond character because Vanillin would pull the composition gourmand, while Heliotropal's benzodioxole character stays in the floral-powder register. Works synergistically with Coumarin in the base for a tender, not sweet, drydown.

- **Hedione HC**: Radiance amplifier at 500 µL — lifts the entire rose-violet heart into the aldehydic top, creating the luminous "glow" effect that defines Chanel-style aldehydic florals. High-cis chosen for maximum jasmine-lift radiance. The Hedione-aldehyde synergy is critical: Hedione softens the aldehydic sharpness while the aldehydes amplify Hedione's radiance.

- **Benzyl Salicylate + Benzyl Benzoate**: Fixative pair. Benzyl Salicylate (420) as the cosmetic-warm diffusion cushion — its salicylate character adds the luxury cosmetic volume that aldehydic florals need (think Chanel No. 5's famous cosmetic-powder base). Benzyl Benzoate (220) as the inert mass anchor — adds molecular weight for fixation without contributing any character that could clash with the aldehydic-powder identity. No Hexyl Salicylate here (unlike the Chypre) because the green-floral character of Hexyl Sal would fight the creamy-powder direction.

- **Musk Ketone (10%)**: Character-echo musk at 220 µL of 10% (22 µL active). The ONLY nitro musk in inventory and essential for authentic aldehydic floral architecture — Musk Ketone's talcum-powder character directly reinforces the ionone powder cloud and aldehydic cosmetic accord. No other musk in the inventory can fulfill this powdery character-echo role. Chosen over using Musk Ketone pure powder (too difficult to dose precisely at these small active amounts).

- **Galaxolide (50%)**: Projection musk at 300 µL of 50% in DEP (150 µL active). Chosen for its synthetic-clean floral projection — in an aldehydic floral, the musk must extend the aldehydic cleanliness outward. Galaxolide's fruity-floral character pairs naturally with the aldehydes' crystalline effervescence. Chosen over Romandolide (too woody for a soft floral) and Zenolide (too cold — would make the aldehydic opening feel clinical). Used at 50% dilution per inventory.

- **Ambrettolide (10%)**: Depth musk at 240 µL of 10% in DPG (24 µL active). The naturalistic skin-sit musk — its fruity-wine warmth adds the "human" dimension that prevents the aldehydic-powder architecture from reading as synthetic or sterile. Chosen over Ethylene Brassylate (too creamy-lactic — would weigh down the aldehydic lightness) and Habanolide (too intimate-skin — aldehydic florals need a musk that whispers, not hugs). Ambrettolide's slightly fruity character echoes the rose heart organically.

- **Vanillin (10%) + Coumarin (20%)**: The tender sweet base at 180 + 140 µL (18 + 28 µL active). Vanillin adds the soft vanilla warmth without gourmand weight — the 10% dilution allows precise dosing at sub-gourmand levels. Coumarin contributes the hay-like coumarinic softness that pairs with Heliotropal's almond facet for a tender, not sweet, drydown. Both within safe non-oriental ceilings per family-shifting rules (Vanillin 18 µL active << 30 µL limit, Coumarin 28 µL active near 25 µL limit for 20% — acceptable at this concentrate volume).

- **Ambrofix (30%) + Sandalore**: Transparent amber-sandalwood structural duo at 150 + 160 µL. Ambrofix (45 µL active) adds crystalline transparent depth — the cold structural counterpoint to the warm vanillin-coumarin base, preventing the drydown from becoming cloying. Sandalore at 160 µL provides the fresh citrus-woody lightness that keeps the base airy — chosen over Javanol (too dry and intimate for an aldehydic floral that needs projection, not skin-scent) and Ebanol (too creamy-soft — would blur the aldehydic crispness). Sandalore's fresh-sandalwood register is the lightest sandalwood material in inventory, ideal for transparent floral architecture.

- **Iso E Super + Vertofix + Cedarwood EO**: The woody base structure at 450 + 200 + 120 µL. Iso E Super creates the molecular skin-cocoon — the abstract cedar halo that makes aldehydic florals feel "expensive" on skin. Vertofix bridges the woody and musk layers with its amber-cedar character. Cedarwood EO provides the small amount of natural woody identity needed to anchor the base. Cashmeran (100 µL) adds textile-warmth — the "cashmere wrap" effect that softens the woody structure without adding oriental weight.

- **Bergamot FCF + Linalool**: Clean citrus top at 400 + 200 µL. Bergamot FCF's classical cologne freshness cuts through the aldehydes without competing — chosen over Bergamot FCF Sicilian (too rich for aldehydic sparkle) or Cedrat (too sharp — fights the aldehydes). Linalool adds fresh-lavender lift and pairs with Bergamot's natural linalool content for a seamless citrus opening.

- **Leafovert (neat)**: Green leaf sparkle at 25 µL — provides the cut-grass green stem note that gives the rose heart a living, botanical quality. Functions as the green top-note accent that cis-3-Hexenol would provide in a classical aldehydic formula. Chosen over Parmavert (too violet-leaf — would blur into the ionone powder) and Dynascone (too powerful — would overwhelm the aldehydes). Leafovert's sharp green character at this dose adds freshness without vegetal heaviness.
