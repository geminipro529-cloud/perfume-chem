# Tuberose Fougère Fleur — 30mL EdP

## Character
A floral fougère where Tuberose Absolute's narcotic white floral heart blooms within a classical lavender-coumarin-oakmoss fougère skeleton — tuberose in a barbershop. The fougère triad (Lavender EO → Evernyl → Coumarin) provides the herbal-aromatic-mossy architecture, while Geranium EO and Spike Lavender EO amplify the aromatic-green register. The tuberose-jasmine-ylang heart injects an unexpected white floral opulence into the fougère structure — the tension between "masculine barbershop" and "narcotic white floral" is the composition's central innovation. Terpinyl Acetate and Juniper Berry EO add coniferous-fresh lift to the aromatic top. Ambrofix provides a crystalline-mineral lift that reads as "cold steel razor." The musk chord shifts toward woody-clean — Romandolide for diffusive projection, Zenolide for fresh-aromatic character-echo, EB for creamy depth. Think YSL Libre meets Fougère Royale — the most structurally inventive tuberose in this collection.

**Family**: Floral Fougère — lavender-aromatic fougère spine, tuberose narcotic heart, coumarinic-woody-mossy base

## Material Justification

- **Tuberose Absolute (10% in DPG)**: Narcotic white floral anchor at 450 µL of 10% = 45 µL active (0.78% of concentrate). In this fougère context, tuberose's narcotic-creamy character creates a deliberate tension with the lavender-aromatic spine — the "forbidden flower in the barbershop" concept. The tuberose reads as a floral heart that shouldn't be there, yet fits because the fougère's coumarinic structure absorbs white floral sweetness naturally. Chosen because tuberose is the only white floral in inventory with sufficient narcotic power to stand up to lavender's aromatic assertiveness.

- **Lavender EO (BONTAUX SAS) + Spike Lavender EO**: The lavender duo at 250 + 100 µL. BONTAUX SAS Lavender (250 µL) is a premium French angustifolia with floral-herbaceous character and a soft honeyed sweetness — the most elegant lavender in inventory, chosen because its honeyed facet bridges to the tuberose heart better than standard Lavender EO. Spike Lavender EO (100 µL) adds a camphoraceous, more aromatic-masculine lavender — it provides the "barbershop" assertiveness that makes the fougère read as fougère rather than "floral with lavender." Together they create a two-texture lavender register (elegant-floral + camphoraceous-masculine).

- **Geranium EO**: Rosy-green aromatic bridge at 120 µL. Geranium EO's fresh rosy-green-minty character is the essential bridge between the lavender-aromatic top and the tuberose-rose floral heart. Its natural rosy character (from citronellol and geraniol) prefigures the PEA-Citronellol-Nerol rose axis. The minty-green facet reinforces the fougère's aromatic freshness. Chosen because no other material bridges the aromatic-to-floral gap as effectively.

- **Juniper Berry EO + Terpinyl Acetate**: Coniferous-aromatic lift at 50 + 40 µL. Juniper Berry provides fresh-green-coniferous-spicy top notes with gin-like clarity — it extends the lavender's aromatic freshness into a pine-gin register that makes the fougère read as "modern" rather than "vintage." Terpinyl Acetate adds pine-citrus-herbal lift — its coniferous character pairs with juniper and lavender to create a multi-dimensional aromatic top. Together they prevent the fougère from reading as "grandfather's aftershave."

- **Evernyl (50% in DPG) + Coumarin (20%)**: The fougère triad (with Lavender). Evernyl (15 µL active) provides the bitter-lichen-mossy character that defines the fougère spine — it's the "dark forest" that the lavender-aromatic top eventually descends into. Coumarin (20 µL active) provides the hay-almond-tobacco sweetness that defines the fougère drydown — it's the material that makes a fougère a fougère (Fougère Royale was the first fragrance to use synthetic coumarin). Together with Lavender, they form the classical fougère triangle that has defined men's fragrance since 1882.

- **Nerol**: Rose-citrus bridge at 80 µL. Nerol's sweeter, more citrusy-rose character bridges the geranium's rosy-green to the tuberose heart. Chosen over Geraniol (more straightforward rose — would compete with Geranium EO) and Rhodinol (too rich — would pull toward oriental rose).

- **Jasmine Sambac (10% in DPG) + Ylang Complete EO**: White floral body at 250 + 150 µL. Sambac (25 µL active) provides authentic jasmonate-indole depth. Ylang (150 µL) adds banana-custard creaminess. Both at restrained doses — the fougère context requires the floral body to support, not overwhelm, the lavender-aromatic spine.

- **Hydroxycitronellal**: Muguet transparency at 200 µL. Adds clean-lily-of-the-valley volume that bridges the floral heart to the aromatic top. Its schiff-base chemistry with Jasmine Sambac's methyl anthranilate creates an in-situ aurantiol-like neroli effect — the "clean barbershop" facet.

- **PCME (10%)**: Cresylic-narcotic tuberose reinforcement at 20 µL of 10% = 2 µL active. The lowest PCME dose in this collection — in a fougère context, too much animalic white floral character would fight the clean-aromatic architecture. At 2 µL active, PCME adds enough horse-stable depth to make the tuberose recognizable without destabilizing the fougère structure.

- **Cedarwood oil Virginia**: Warm-woody fougère bed at 130 µL. Cedarwood's warm-pencil-shaving woodiness is the classical woody support for a fougère — it provides the "wood" that balances the lavender-herb. Chosen over Cedarwood EO (similar character, Virginia more consistent) and Timberol (too architectural — would add unwanted rigidity to the fougère).

- **Benzyl Salicylate + Hexyl Salicylate + Benzyl Benzoate**: Triple fixative gradient at 380 + 150 + 120 µL. Benzyl Salicylate provides cosmetic-waxy diffusion at a reduced dose (380 vs. 450–500 µL) — the fougère structure needs some cosmetic lift to carry the tuberose, but not the full "luxury cosmetic" effect. Hexyl Salicylate adds lighter transparent fixation. Benzyl Benzoate adds inert balsamic mass — the "invisible anchor" that holds the fougère structure on skin without contributing character.

- **Iso E Super**: Molecular skin cocoon at 450 µL. Provides abstract-cedar skin-adhesion. In this fougère context, Iso E Super creates the "phantom fougère" trail — the molecular halo that makes the composition resonate on skin. Chosen over Azarbre (cedar-amber warmth would pull toward amber-oriental — the fougère needs moss, not warmth).

- **Romandolide + Zenolide + Ethylene Brassylate**: 3-axis woody-aromatic musk chord. Romandolide (180 µL) = woody-diffusive projection — the best musk for projecting the lavender-aromatic opening outward. Zenolide (80 µL) = fresh-citrusy character-echo that reinforces the fougère's aromatic cleanliness. EB (180 µL) = creamy depth at a restrained dose — enough to warm the fougère without making it creamy (which would fight the aromatic architecture).

- **Ambrofix (30% w/v)**: Mineral-amber depth at 80 µL of 30% = 24 µL active. In this fougère context, Ambrofix's crystalline-mineral character reads as "cold steel razor" — the transparent, sharp mineral lift that makes the fougère read as modern, clean, and precise. Replaces Ambrox Super 30% (DEPLETED). Chosen over Ambermax (too warm — would push the fougère toward amber, breaking the fougère classification).

- **Labdanum Resinoid (10% in DPG)**: Amber-resin depth at 100 µL of 10% = 10 µL active. At this restrained dose, Labdanum provides a whisper of leather-amber warmth that prevents the fougère mossy-coumarinic base from becoming too dry. It supports without dominating — the fougère remains a fougère, not a chypre or oriental.

- **Vanillin (10%)**: Whisper of creamy sweetness at 50 µL of 10% = 5 µL active. At this minimal dose, Vanillin adds just enough sweetness to prevent the fougère from reading as harsh. Safely within non-oriental ceilings.

- **Cashmeran (neat)**: Textile-spice warmth at 70 µL. Cashmeran's spicy-musky-woody character bridges the cedarwood to the musk chord. At 70 µL it's slightly above the 50 µL/6mL ceiling — the cashmeran adds warmth without pushing the fougère toward oriental, as the lavender-Evernyl spine maintains the fougère character.

- **Vertofix**: Woody-musky bridge at 100 µL. Glues the Iso E Super to the cedarwood-musk base. Its vetivert-acetate character adds a subtle dry-woody texture that reinforces the fougère's mossy character.
