## What you'll need

- 1 × clean 30 mL atomizer or amber bottle (final bottle)
- 1 × clean 10 mL amber vial (concentrate build vial)
- Positive-displacement pipette (10–100 µL) for trace doses
- 1 mL glass syringe or graduated dropper for larger doses
- Perfumer's ethanol ≥ 96 %, denatured-undenatured both fine, fragrance-grade

> Build the **6 mL concentrate first** in the small vial, mature, then dilute into the 30 mL bottle. Do **not** add raw materials directly to ethanol — the base layer needs time to homogenize neat before dilution.

## Step 2 — Mature the concentrate

1. Cap the 10 mL build vial tightly. Label "F2 concentrate · DD-MM-YYYY".
2. Rest **dark, 18–22 °C, minimum 4 weeks** before dilution. Evernyl + Coumarin + macrocyclic musks integrate slowly; rushing produces a thin, "ingredient-list" reading.

---

## Step 3 — Dilute into the 30 mL bottle

> Once concentrate has matured.

**Procedure:**
1. Transfer all **6.00 mL (6 000 µL) matured concentrate** into the 30 mL atomizer bottle.
2. Add **24.00 mL (24 000 µL) perfumer's ethanol ≥ 96 %**. (Cleanest way: weigh ~19 g ethanol on a 0.01 g scale; ρ ≈ 0.79 → 24 mL.)
3. Cap, invert 10×.
4. Macerate **2 more weeks** dark before evaluation on skin.

The finished EDP totals **30 000 µL (30.00 mL)** at **20 % concentrate**.

### Perfumer Analysis

**1. Character** — Classical Fougère in the 1882–1960 tradition: lavender, coumarin, oakmoss (Evernyl), rose-geranium (Geraniol), and citrus (Bergamot + D-Limonene). No Hedione (no jasmonate radiance), no Galaxolide (no laundry-musk projection). The architecture is the pre-1965 fougere: projection comes from the high-VP top notes (D-Limonene 190 Pa, Lavender 22 Pa, Bergamot 40 Pa), not from synthetic musks. The heart is the lavender-geraniol-coumarin triangle with aromatic herbs (rosemary, clary sage) and linalool as a natural top-heart bridge.

**2. Opening (0–5 min)** — D-Limonene (11 851, massive) leads — pure sparkling citrus. Bergamot FCF (7 387) provides classical hesperidic lift. Linalool (3 371) and Linalyl Acetate (928) bridge citrus to lavender. Clary Sage (418) adds wine-like aromatic sparkle. Total vapor: 209 ppm. The opening reads as pre-1960s barbershop: citrus + aromatic herbs, no synthetic radiance boosters.

**3. Heart (30 min – 2 hr)** — Lavender EO (10 046 OAV at opening, sustained through heart) and Geraniol (149 OAV) form the lavender-geranium backbone. Coumarin rises to ~250 OAV in the heart — the hay-tobacco signature of classical fougeres. Rosemary (738) and Clary Sage (418-485) provide the aromatic herb layer. No Hedione radiance — the heart is direct and unmediated: lavender, geranium, herbs, coumarin.

**4. Drydown (2–4 hr+)** — Lavender persists at OAV ~6 000 at 4 h. Iso E Super (3 184) provides the woody molecular halo. Coumarin (243 at opening, rising to ~300) delivers the hay-tobacco drydown. Ethylene Brassylate (OAV 3) + Habanolide (OAV 3) provide creamy-warm skin musk depth — intimate rather than projecting. The drydown is lavender-hay-woody with a soft macrocyclic musk skin effect.

**5. Sillage & Diffusion** — Projection comes exclusively from high-VP materials: D-Limonene (190 Pa), Lavender (22 Pa), Bergamot (40 Pa), Linalool (20 Pa). No synthetic musk projection. The sillage signature is a citrus-aromatic top cloud over a lavender-geranium body — classical fougere sillage, not the laundry-musk trail of 1980s fougeres.

**6. Longevity** — ~16% raw evaporation over 4 h. Low-VP base: Iso E Super (0.15 Pa), Vetiver (0.04 Pa), Habanolide (0.02 Pa), Ethylene Brassylate (0.01 Pa). Expected skin life: 6–8 h. The Iso E Super + Vetiver + Benzyl Salicylate + Ethylene Brassylate base mat provides substantial fixative mass.

**7. Balance** — Pyramid: T ~24 / H ~33 / B ~43 (fit 0.86). The top is slightly over-weighted from multi-citrus layering. OAV range: <1 (Vertofix Coeur) to 11 851 (D-Limonene). The sigma-log contrast is moderate — lavender and D-Limonene dominate, but geraniol and coumarin register clearly in the heart.

**8. Flags** — Sub-threshold: Vertofix Coeur (OAV < 1, structural bridge — acceptable). IFRA edge: Evernyl at 0.1% finished (pipeline flags perturbation risk). Oxidation: 22% active mass (D-Limonene, lavender, bergamot — standard for citrus-aromatic fougeres). Data quality: 12 materials with derived/unverified ODTs (~68% OAV share) — primarily whole EOs.
