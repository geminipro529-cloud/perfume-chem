## Practical priorities

- Start with the 5 g trial of the one you want most. Use its actual full table, not a generic 6 g concentrate + 24 g alcohol shortcut.
- Reuse matching owned mass-fraction stocks; do not reprepare them just for paperwork. Resolve only stock facts needed for the actual arithmetic.
- Compare after a practical initial 7–14 day settling period, at 5 min, 30–60 min, 4 h and next-day blotter.
- Valaya retains an unsolved exact Akigalawood gap; its small PW proxy is optional and testable, not an equivalent.
- Empirical reference fidelity, liking, stability and Category 4 conformity remain unestablished.
