# PRE-DILUTION: β-Ionone 1% in Ethanol
## Required for Both Formulas Below

> **⚠️ Inventory Check:** Read [`inventory.txt`](/inventory.txt) before use. Verify β-Ionone neat stock available.

**Reason:** β-Ionone neat has ODT = 0.007 ppb — one of the lowest thresholds in perfumery. Dosing it neat at 50-80 µL produces ~238,000 OAV (floods everything). At 1% dilution, 50-80 µL = 0.5-0.8 µL active = ~1,400-3,800 OAV (graceful woody-violet depth).

### MAKE:

| Step | Material | Amount |
|------|----------|--------|
| 1 | β-Ionone (neat) | 100 µL |
| 2 | Ethanol 96% | 9,900 µL |
| 3 | **Shake well, label "β-Ionone 1% in EtOH"** | |

**This makes 10 mL of 1% stock.** More than enough for both formulas (130 µL needed total).

**Storage:** Amber vial, dark cupboard. Stable. IFRA allows up to 15% of concentrate — our 0.009–0.014% dosing is 1,100× below safety limits. Pre-dilution serves OAV control, not regulatory compliance.
