# Niche House Ambroxan-Iris — Dior Homme Intense 2025 Study — 30 mL EdP

**Historical reference:** Dior Homme Intense 2025 (Francis Kurkdjian reformulation). The 2025 DHI strips back the heavy iris-cocoa of the 2011 Demachy version and builds forward on a massive ambroxan skeleton — cleaner, brighter, more transparent. This niche house version uses Ambrofix (premium ambergris) generously, with real iris-orris and jasmine depth.

**2025 vs 2011:** Iris reduced ~40%. Ambroxan added as the backbone. Less cocoa-vanilla, more radiant-clean.

**Concentration:** 20.0% EdP · 6,000 µL concentrate in 30 mL
