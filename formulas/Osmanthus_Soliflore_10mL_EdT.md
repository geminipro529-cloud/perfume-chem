# Osmanthus Soliflore Study — 10 mL EdT

**Historical reference:** Osmanthus fragrans (桂花) — classical Chinese flower accord, used in perfumery since the 1920s (Guerlain Mitsouko, Jean Patou).

**Why this structure matters:** Osmanthus teaches how a flower's identity spans fragrance families — it's simultaneously fruity, floral, leathery, and tea-like. Building it requires precise balance across violet-ionones, peach-lactones, and a trace of suede leather.

**Concentration:** 15% EdT · 1,500 µL concentrate in 10 mL
