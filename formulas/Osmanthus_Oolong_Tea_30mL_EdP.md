# Osmanthus Oolong Tea — 30mL EdP

## Character
A transparent tea composition built around osmanthus absolute — the apricot-leather-tea floral leads, with hedione radiance and a whisper of bergamot framing. No competing citrus, no woody-aromatic blast. One note, floating in watercolor transparency.

**Family**: Transparent Floral (Ellena-style) — osmanthus-centered, tea-like, modern
**Materials**: 17 (Ellena-optimal 12-18 range)
**Literature**: Ellena (2011) Journal d'un Parfumeur; Hong et al. (2023) GC-O of Osmanthus fragrans; Jia et al. (2025) osmanthus flavor imitation
