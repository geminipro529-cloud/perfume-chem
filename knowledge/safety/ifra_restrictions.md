# IFRA Restrictions: The Legal Limits (50th Amendment, 2023)

**Allergen limits, banned materials, and how to stay compliant.**

---

## Core Principle
IFRA (International Fragrance Association) sets maximum usage levels for allergenic and toxic ingredients. Violate these = potential recalls, lawsuits, or bans in certain markets (EU is strictest).

**Source:** IFRA Standards Library, 50th Amendment (2023)

### BANNED OR SEVERELY RESTRICTED

| Ingredient | Status | Reason | Replacement |
|------------|--------|--------|-------------|
| **Musk Ketone** | Effectively banned (0.0004% max) | Neurotoxic, persistent | Galaxolide, Musk T, Habanolide |
| **Musk Xylene** | Banned in EU | Toxic | Galaxolide |
| **Natural Musk (Deer)** | Banned (CITES) | Animal cruelty | Synthetic musks |
| **Civet (Animal)** | Banned (CITES) | Animal cruelty | Synthetic civettone |
| **Castoreum (Natural)** | Restricted | Animal sourcing | Synthetic bases |
| **Nitromusks (most)** | Banned or <0.001% | Toxicity, bioaccumulation | Polycyclic musks |

**Rule:** Don't use animal-derived musks or nitrotic musks. Use polycyclics (Galaxolide, Habanolide).

## PART 4: CALCULATING COMPLIANCE

### The Formula
```
[Ingredient % in concentrate] × [Concentrate % in final product] = [Ingredient % in finished product]
```

### Example: Coumarin in EdP

**Your Formulation:**
- Coumarin in concentrate: 5%
- Concentrate in EdP: 15%

**Calculation:**
```
5% × 0.15 = 0.75% in finished product

IFRA limit for Coumarin (Category 4): 0.2%

Result: NON-COMPLIANT (0.75% > 0.2%)
```

**Fix:** Reduce coumarin to 1.33% in concentrate:
```
1.33% × 0.15 = 0.2% in finished product (COMPLIANT)
```

## PART 5: COMMON VIOLATIONS (What Beginners Get Wrong)

### Violation 1: Oakmoss Overdose
**Typical Error:** "I want a classic chypre, so I'll use 5% oakmoss in my concentrate."

**Calculation:**
```
5% × 0.15 (EdP) = 0.75% in finished product

IFRA limit: 0.1%

Result: 7.5x over limit
```

**Fix:** Use 0.67% max in concentrate, or use Evernyl (synthetic oakmoss substitute, no limit).

---

### Violation 2: Coumarin in Fougère
**Typical Error:** "Fougère needs lots of coumarin for that tonka note, 10%!"

**Fix:** Max 1.33% in concentrate. Use Tonka extract (natural coumarin at compliant levels) or Tonkalide (synthetic).

---

### Violation 3: Forgetting Naturals Have Allergens
**Example:** "I'm using 20% lavender oil, it's natural so it's safe."

**Problem:** Lavender oil contains:
- Linalool (~30-40%)
- Linalyl acetate (~30-40%)
- Limonene (~1-2%)

**Math:**
```
20% lavender × 35% linalool = 7% linalool in concentrate
7% × 0.15 = 1.05% linalool in finished product

Must declare: Linalool (because >0.001%)
```

**Rule:** Natural extracts COUNT. Calculate the allergen content in each natural oil.

## PART 6: REFORMULATION FOR COMPLIANCE

### Strategy 1: Reduce the Offending Ingredient
**If Coumarin is too high, reduce it to compliant level.**

---

### Strategy 2: Use a Synthetic Alternative
**Replace Oakmoss with Evernyl (no limit).**

---

### Strategy 3: Change Product Category
**If Cat 4 (perfume) is too restrictive, formulate for Cat 9 (household) or Cat 12 (air freshener).**

**Example:**
- Coumarin limit in Cat 4 (perfume): 0.2%
- Coumarin limit in Cat 9 (household): 0.5%
- Coumarin limit in Cat 12 (air freshener): 1.5%

**Use:** If making room spray, you have more headroom.

---

### Strategy 4: Dilute the Concentrate
**Lower the concentrate % in the final product.**

**Example:**
- EdP (15% concentrate): Coumarin max 1.33% in concentrate
- EdT (8% concentrate): Coumarin max 2.5% in concentrate

**Trade-off:** Weaker scent, but more compliance headroom.

## PART 7: ALLERGEN DECLARATION (EU Cosmetics Regulation)

### The Rule
**If >0.001% in leave-on product OR >0.01% in rinse-off, you MUST declare it on the label.**

### Example Label (EdP with Allergens)
```
Ingredients: Alcohol Denat., Parfum (Fragrance), Aqua (Water), Linalool, Limonene, Citronellol, Geraniol, Coumarin, Eugenol.

(Allergens listed separately after "Parfum")
```

## PART 8: BATCH TESTING

### If You're Selling or Gifting
**You should GC-MS test your finished product to confirm allergen levels.**

**Why:** Natural extracts vary. Lavender from one batch may have 35% linalool, another 45%.

**Protocol:**
1. Send sample to testing lab
2. Request allergen quantification (26 EU allergens)
3. Verify compliance
4. Adjust formula if needed

**Cost:** ~$100-300 per sample (depending on lab).

## PART 10: ADVANCED COMPLIANCE TRICKS

### Trick 1: The "Dual Formula" Approach
**Make TWO versions:**
1. **Master Formula:** Full strength, may exceed IFRA (for personal use)
2. **Commercial Formula:** IFRA-compliant (for sale/gifting)

**Use:** Experiment freely, then scale back for compliance.

---

### Trick 2: The "Natural Extract Audit"
**Track allergens in every natural oil you use.**

**Example: Rose Absolute (Typical Composition)**
- Phenylethyl Alcohol: 45% → Must declare
- Citronellol: 18% → Must declare
- Geraniol: 10% → Must declare
- Nerol: 5%
- Eugenol: 0.5% → Must declare if >0.001% in final
- Others: 21.5%

**If using 10% Rose Absolute in concentrate:**
```
10% × 45% PEA = 4.5% PEA in concentrate
4.5% × 0.15 (EdP) = 0.675% in finished product → MUST DECLARE
```

---

### Trick 3: The "Concentrate Dilution Strategy"
**If you're over limit in 15% EdP, make a 10% EdT instead.**

**Math:**
```
Coumarin in concentrate: 3%
EdP (15%): 3% × 0.15 = 0.45% (OVER limit of 0.2%)
EdT (10%): 3% × 0.10 = 0.30% (STILL over, but closer)
EdC (5%): 3% × 0.05 = 0.15% (COMPLIANT)
```

**Trade-off:** Weaker scent, but compliant.

## PART 11: RED FLAGS (Ingredients That Always Cause Problems)

### The "Trouble 10"
1. **Oakmoss** - Always over limit unless tiny %
2. **Coumarin** - Fougères and orientals easily exceed
3. **Isoeugenol** - VERY low limit (0.02%)
4. **Lyral** - BANNED (don't use)
5. **Lilial** - BANNED (don't use)
6. **Musk Ketone** - Effectively banned (0.0004%)
7. **Citral** - Easy to exceed in citrus blends (0.6% limit)
8. **Furocoumarins (Bergapten)** - Phototoxic (use FCF oils)
9. **Cinnamaldehyde/Cinnamyl Alcohol** - 0.2% limit each
10. **Methyl Eugenol** - 0.001% limit (VERY low)

**Rule:** If using any of the Trouble 10, calculate compliance FIRST.

## THE GOLDEN RULES

1. **Calculate compliance BEFORE finalizing** the formula.
2. **Oakmoss, Coumarin, Isoeugenol** = instant red flags (low limits).
3. **Declare all allergens >0.001%** on EU labels.
4. **Lyral and Lilial are BANNED** — don't use.
5. **Natural extracts contain allergens** — calculate their contribution.
6. **When in doubt, use synthetics** (usually fewer restrictions).
7. **Test batches with GC-MS** if selling (natural extracts vary).
