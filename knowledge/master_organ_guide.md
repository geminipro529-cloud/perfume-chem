# The Master Organ: A Functional Guide to Your Inventory

This guide categorizes your selected materials by their **structural function** in a perfume. Use this to build any genre.

## 1. The Foundation (Base Notes & Solvents)
*These materials provide volume, longevity, and the "canvas" for your scent.*

| Material | Function | Vibe | Dosage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Iso E Super** | The Velvet Filter | Woody, cozy, blurring | 10-50% |
| **Galaxolide 50%** | The Volume | Clean laundry, thick air | 10-30% |
| **Habanolide** | The Iron | Hot steam, metallic musk | 5-20% |
| **Ethylene Brassylate** | The Polish | Sweet, floral musk | 5-20% |
| **Ambroxan 10%** | The Radiance | Mineral, amber, skin | 1-10% |
| **Patchouli Light** | The Earth | Clean dirt, woody | 1-5% |
| **Vertofix Coeur** | The Leather Wood | Dry wood, sturdy | 5-15% |

## 2. The Heart (The Character)
*These define the "genre" of the perfume.*

| Material | Function | Vibe | Dosage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hedione** | The Blender | Transparent jasmine, air | 10-30% |
| **Alpha Isomethyl Ionone** | The Powder | Violet, lipstick, cosmetic | 1-10% |
| **Dihydromyrcenol** | The Cologne | Lime, soap, metallic | 1-10% |
| **Linalool** | The Fresh Floral | Lavender, bergamot core | 5-15% |
| **Phenyl Ethyl Acetate** | The Jam | Honey, rose, fruity | 1-5% |
| **Benzyl Salicylate** | The Sunscreen | Balsamic, floral, UV | 5-15% |

## 3. The Top (The Sparkle)
*These are the first 15 minutes. They sell the bottle.*

| Material | Function | Vibe | Dosage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Aldehyde C-10** | The Peel | Orange skin, waxy | 0.1-1% |
| **Aldehyde C-12 MNA** | The Shimmer | Incense, hot iron | 0.1-0.5% |
| **Linalyl Acetate** | The Crispness | Earl Grey tea, bergamot | 5-15% |
| **Triplal** | The Snap | Green grass, zest | 0.1-1% |
| **Helional** | The Silver | Watery, metallic, airy | 1-5% |

## 4. The Modifiers (The "Secret Sauce")
*These are the high-impact traces that make it smell "Niche."*

| Material | Function | Vibe | Dosage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Alpha Irone** | The Luxury | Cold, metallic iris | 0.1-0.5% |
| **Beta Damascone** | The Glow | Roasted fruit, rose | 0.1-0.5% |
| **Undecavertol** | The Anti-Sugar | Violet leaf, watery | 0.1-1% |
| **Isobutyl Quinoline** | The Grit | Green leather, roots | 0.1-1% |
| **Birch Tar** | The Smoke | Campfire, leather | 0.1-1% |
| **Ethyl Maltol** | The Addiction | Cotton candy, sugar | 0.1-1% |
| **Cashmeran** | The Texture | Wet concrete, pine | 0.5-2% |

## 5. Quick Accord Recipes

### The "Modern Cologne" (Aventus-ish)
*   **Base:** Iso E Super (40) + Ambroxan (10) + Patchouli (5)
*   **Heart:** Hedione (20) + Dihydromyrcenol (10)
*   **Top:** Triplal (1) + Beta Damascone (0.5) + Birch Tar (0.5)

### The "Luxury Soap" (Prada-ish)
*   **Base:** Habanolide (30) + Galaxolide (20)
*   **Heart:** Alpha Isomethyl Ionone (10) + Helional (5) + Neroli (5)
*   **Top:** Alpha Irone (0.5) + Linalyl Acetate (10)

### The "Gourmand" (Baccarat-ish)
*   **Base:** Veramoss (5) + Ambroxan (20)
*   **Heart:** Hedione (30)
*   **Modifier:** Ethyl Maltol (1) + Saffron/Safranal (Trace)
