# Accord Building Blocks (Starter Recipes)

Practical, modifiable recipes. Percentages assume concentrate; dilute to desired strength.

## 1) Rose Accord (Basic)
| Ingredient | % | Role |
|------------|---|------|
| Phenethyl Alcohol | 50 | Body |
| Geraniol | 20 | Rosy freshness |
| Citronellol | 15 | Lift |
| Rose Oxide | 2 | Green metallic edge |
| Eugenol | 1 | Spice depth |
| Damascenone | 0.1 | Diffusive sweetness |
| Ethanol | balance | Solvent |
