# Emotional Mapping & Mood Association

Translating abstract feelings into chemical reality.

## The Mood Matrix (Jellinek's Map)
A classic 2-axis model for mapping perfume effects.
- **Axis 1:** Ergenic (Active/Stimulating) <--> Trophotropic (Calming/Relaxing)
- **Axis 2:** Exalting (Spiritual/Intellectual) <--> Narcotic (Physical/Sensual)

### Quadrants:
1.  **Stimulating & Physical (Fresh/Green):**
    - *Mood:* Sporty, energetic, awake.
    - *Notes:* Citrus (Limonene), Grass (Cis-3-Hexenol), Herbs (Rosemary, Mint), Marine (Calone).
2.  **Stimulating & Spiritual (Floral/Aldehydic):**
    - *Mood:* Happy, elegant, bright.
    - *Notes:* Aldehydes, Lily of the Valley (Hydroxycitronellal), Rose, Fruity esters.
3.  **Calming & Spiritual (Woody/Oriental):**
    - *Mood:* Meditative, comforting, safe.
    - *Notes:* Sandalwood, Incense (Olibanum), Cedar, Iris (Ionones).
4.  **Calming & Physical (Narcotic/Musk):**
    - *Mood:* Sensual, seductive, heavy.
    - *Notes:* Tuberose, Jasmine (Indole), Musk, Vanilla, Amber.

## Seasonality & Temperature
- **Summer (Heat):**
  - *Requirement:* High volatility, cooling effect.
  - *Chemistry:* High vapor pressure molecules (Citrus, Mint). Avoid heavy lactones (curdle in heat).
  - *Accord:* Eau de Cologne, Marine.
- **Winter (Cold):**
  - *Requirement:* Low volatility, warming effect, enveloping.
  - *Chemistry:* Heavy molecules (Vanillin, Patchouli, Musk) that rely on body heat to project.
  - *Accord:* Gourmand, Amber, Leather.

## Cultural Associations (The "Proustian" Trigger)
- **Cleanliness:**
  - *West:* White Musk (Galaxolide), Citrus, Lavender (Laundry detergent association).
  - *Japan:* Hinoki wood, Yuzu, unscented/soapy.
  - *Middle East:* Rose, Oud, Saffron (Cleanliness is spiritual purity, not sterility).
- **Luxury:**
  - *Old World:* Oakmoss, Iris, Animalics (Complexity = Wealth).
  - *New World:* Minimalist, transparent, "Molecule" scents (Health/Hygiene = Wealth).

## Functional Fragrance Mapping
- **Focus/Work:** Rosemary (1,8-Cineole), Lemon. Increases alertness.
- **Sleep:** Lavender (Linalool), Chamomile, Sandalwood. Reduces heart rate.
- **Intimacy:** Musk, Vanilla, Skin scents. Mimics pheromones (conceptually, if not biologically).
