## Emotion → Ingredient Mapping

### WARM (Comfort, Intimacy, Heat)
- **Cozy/Safe:** Vanilla, Benzoin, Tonka Bean, Sandalwood, Cashmeran
- **Spicy/Hot:** Cinnamon, Clove, Pink Pepper, Cardamom
- **Animalic/Skin:** Civet (synthetic), Castoreum (synthetic), Musks (Musk Ketone, Ambrettolide)
- **Resinous/Deep:** Labdanum, Opoponax, Myrrh, Peru Balsam

### COOL (Distance, Freshness, Ice)
- **Clean/Laundry:** Aldehydes (C-10, C-11, C-12), Galaxolide, Dihydromyrcenol
- **Icy/Sharp:** Menthol, Eucalyptol, Mint, Birch Leaf
- **Metallic/Modern:** Rose Oxide, Geranium, Habanolide
- **Mineral/Stone:** Ambroxan, Evernyl (Oakmoss), Geosmin

### LIGHT (Air, Space, Lift)
- **Ethereal/Radiant:** Hedione, Iso E Super, Ambroxan
- **Citrus/Sunny:** Bergamot, Lemon, Grapefruit, Neroli
- **Green/Natural:** Galbanum, Violet Leaf, Cis-3-Hexenol (Cut Grass)
- **Ozonic/Sky:** Calone, Precyclemone B, Helional

### HEAVY (Earth, Roots, Darkness)
- **Grounded/Rooty:** Vetiver, Patchouli, Orris Root
- **Dark/Smoky:** Birch Tar, Cade, Guaiac Wood, Oud
- **Rich/Indulgent:** Chocolate, Coffee, Tobacco, Tuberose
- **Leather/Saddle:** Suede bases, Isobutyl Quinoline

## Usage in Chat

**User:** "I want something that feels cozy but not heavy."
**AI:** "Cozy-not-heavy = warm heart with a light base.
    → Benzyl benzoate (warmth), Ethyl maltol (soft sweet),
    → Cashmeran (fuzzy but airy), skip the Patchouli."

**User:** "I need a scent that feels like cold stone."
**AI:** "Cold stone = Cool + Heavy/Grounded.
    → Geosmin (earth), Ambroxan (mineral), Iris (dusty/cool),
    → A touch of Frankincense (cold smoke)."
