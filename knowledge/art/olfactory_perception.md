# Olfactory Perception & Psychophysics

How the brain processes scent, and why the same molecule smells different to different people.

## The Weber-Fechner Law in Perfumery
**Principle:** The intensity of a sensation is proportional to the logarithm of the stimulus intensity.
- **Application:** To double the perceived strength of a note, you must increase its concentration by ~10x.
- **Implication:** Adding 0.1% vs 0.2% of a powerful material (like Aldehyde C-12 MNA) makes a huge difference. Adding 10% vs 11% of Hedione makes almost no difference.

## Specific Anosmia (Smell Blindness)
**Definition:** The inability to smell specific molecules due to genetic variation in olfactory receptors.
- **Common Culprits:**
  - **Musks (Galaxolide, Habanolide):** ~30-50% of the population is anosmic to specific musks.
  - **Ambergris (Ambroxan):** Some perceive it as odorless or faintly sweaty.
  - **Sandalwood (Javanol):** High variability in sensitivity.
  - **Iso E Super:** Many people cannot smell it directly but perceive its effect (velvety sensation).
- **Formulation Strategy:** Never rely on a single musk. Use a "Musk Cocktail" (e.g., Galaxolide + Ethylene Brassylate + Ambrettolide) to ensure everyone smells *something*.

## Olfactory Fatigue (Adaptation)
**Mechanism:** Receptors stop firing after continuous exposure to prevent sensory overload.
- **Fast Fatigue:** Ionones (Violet), Octine (Green/Metallic). The nose "cuts out" after seconds.
- **Slow Fatigue:** Musks, Resins.
- **The "Disappearing Act":** A wearer of *Violetta di Parma* stops smelling it after 5 minutes, while others are overwhelmed.
- **Counter-Strategy:** Use "pulsing" scents (varying volatility) or contrasting notes to reset the nose.

## Cross-Modal Perception (Synesthesia)
**Scent & Color:**
- **Citrus:** Yellow/Orange/Green.
- **Amber/Vanilla:** Brown/Gold.
- **Aldehydes:** White/Silver/Blue.
- **Patchouli/Oud:** Black/Dark Brown.
**Scent & Texture:**
- **Aldehydes:** Sharp, metallic, cold.
- **Musks:** Soft, fuzzy, cotton-like.
- **Iso E Super:** Velvety, smooth.
- **Norlimbanol:** Dry, scratchy, desiccant.

## The Proust Effect (Memory)
**Mechanism:** The olfactory bulb projects directly to the amygdala (emotion) and hippocampus (memory), bypassing the thalamus (conscious processing).
- **Result:** Scent evokes visceral, emotional memories faster and deeper than sight or sound.
- **Application:** "Nostalgia accords" (Baby powder, Crayons, Sunscreen, Old books) trigger powerful emotional responses.

## Cultural Variation
- **Wintergreen (Methyl Salicylate):**
  - *USA:* Candy, root beer, positive.
  - *UK/Europe:* Antiseptic, medicine, bathroom cleaner, negative.
- **Lavender:**
  - *Global:* Clean, spa.
  - *Latin America:* Baby products (very popular for infants).
- **Lemon:**
  - *Global:* Fresh.
  - *Some contexts:* Dish soap, furniture polish (cheap association).
