# The Olfactory Bridge

Connecting physical properties to perception.

## Structure-Odor Relationships (SOR)
- **Macrocyclic Musks:** Large rings (>15 carbons). Smell soft, powdery, skin-like.
- **Nitro Musks:** (Banned/Restricted). Smell sweet, powdery, explosive.
- **Aldehydes (Aliphatic):**
  - *C-8 to C-10:* Citrus peel, sharp, waxy.
  - *C-11 to C-12:* Soapy, metallic, "hot iron."
- **Esters:** Generally fruity (Apple, Pear, Banana). High volatility.
- **Lactones:** Creamy, coconut, peach, milky.

## Vibrational Theory vs. Shape Theory
- **Shape Theory (Lock & Key):** The dominant theory. Molecules fit into receptors based on geometry.
- **Vibrational Theory (Turin):** The nose detects electron tunneling frequencies (molecular vibrations).
  - *Relevance:* Explains why isotopes (deuterated musk) smell different despite identical shape.

## Functional Group Impact
- **-OH (Alcohol):** Softens, rounds, adds floral/green notes.
- **-CHO (Aldehyde):** Adds power, lift, "sparkle."
- **-COOH (Acid):** Sour, fatty, rancid (usually bad, but useful in traces for cheese/fruit accords).
- **-SH (Thiol):** Sulfurous, tropical fruit (in traces), rotting egg (in bulk).

## Molecular Weight & Perception
- **< 150 g/mol:** Top notes. Sharp, piercing, fleeting.
- **150 - 220 g/mol:** Heart notes. Floral, spicy, fruit.
- **> 220 g/mol:** Base notes. Musk, wood, amber. Low volatility, high tenacity.
- **The "Cut-off":** Molecules > 300 g/mol are generally odorless (too heavy to fly).
