# Tom Ford White Suede - Estimated Chemical Composition

Based on GCMS analysis patterns for this fragrance profile (Musky Floral Leather), the following aroma chemicals are likely present:

## 1. Musk Complex (The Core)
*   **Ethylene Brassylate**: Large macrocyclic musk, provides the "white" clean laundry feel.
*   **Galaxolide (HHCB)**: Clean, powerful polycyclic musk.
*   **Ambrettolide**: Adds a botanical, slightly fruity muskiness (mimicking Musk Mallow).
*   **Helvetolide**: Modern pear-like musk for top-note diffusion.

## 2. Suede & Leather Accord
*   **Suederal (IFF)**: A specialized base providing the soft, velvety suede effect.
*   **Safraleine (Givaudan)**: Saffron and leather notes with a spicy edge.
*   **Isobutyl Quinoline (IBQ)**: Trace amounts for the "tougher" leather undertone.

## 3. Floral Heart
*   **Hydroxycitronellal**: Classic Lily of the Valley (Muguet) note, essential for the "white" floral texture.
*   **Linalool**: Fresh, floral, woody (from Thyme/Tea facets).
*   **Phenethyl Alcohol**: Soft rose petal scent.

## 4. Base & Fixatives
*   **Ambroxan**: Provides the ambergris-like saltiness and skin-like warmth.
*   **Iso E Super**: For "velvet" texture and diffusion.
*   **Sandalore / Ebanol**: Synthetic sandalwood for creamy volume.
