# Extremely Powerful Trace Materials - Dosage Guide
**Date Created:** 2025-12-17
**Purpose:** Prevent overdosing powerful aroma chemicals that can ruin a formula

---

## Core Principle
**Some molecules are so powerful that 0.01% can dominate a blend. Less is MUCH more.**

Detection thresholds for these materials are incredibly low (parts per billion in some cases), meaning tiny amounts create massive impact.

---

## TIER 1: NUCLEAR-LEVEL POWER (Use at <0.001%)

### β-Damascenone
- **Detection Threshold:** 0.000009 ppm (9 parts per TRILLION!)
- **Smell:** Fruity, honey, roasted apple, rose undertone
- **Common Dilution:** 1:100 or 1:1000 in carrier
- **Safe Dosage:** 0.0001-0.001% in final formula
- **What Happens if Overdosed:** Dominates everything, sickly-sweet fruity mess
- **Found In:** Rose absolute (trace), high-end florals
- **Usage Note:** Even 1 drop of pure material in 100mL can overpower

---

### Skatole (3-Methylindole)
- **Detection Threshold:** 0.0002 ppm
- **Smell:** Fecal/animalic at any noticeable concentration
- **Common Dilution:** 1:10,000 in ethanol
- **Safe Dosage:** 0.00001-0.0001% in final formula
- **What Happens if Overdosed:** Literal fecal smell, ruins formula
- **Found In:** Jasmine (0.0001% naturally), civet (banned natural source)
- **Usage Note:** Only use in EXTREME dilution for animalic depth

---

### Indole
- **Detection Threshold:** 0.00014 ppm (0.14 parts per BILLION)
- **Smell:** Fecal at high concentration, floral-animalic at trace
- **Common Dilution:** 0.1-1% in ethanol
- **Safe Dosage:** 0.0001-0.001% in final formula
- **What Happens if Overdosed:** Overpowering fecal-mothball smell
- **Found In:** Jasmine (0.001-0.01%), orange blossom
- **Usage Note:** THE most common mistake - people use 10-100x too much

**Indole Dilution Protocol:**
```
Stock Solution: 0.1% Indole in ethanol
Usage: 1-5 drops per 30mL formula
Final concentration: ~0.0003-0.0015%
```

---

### Civetone (Synthetic Civet)
- **Detection Threshold:** ~0.01 ppm
- **Smell:** Animalic, fecal, musky
- **Common Dilution:** 1% in carrier oil
- **Safe Dosage:** 0.0001-0.001% in final formula
- **What Happens if Overdosed:** Overpowering animal smell, unwearable
- **Usage:** Only in orientals, chypres, vintage-style perfumes

## TIER 2: EXTREMELY POWERFUL (Use at 0.01-0.1%)

### E2MB (Ethyl 2-Methylbutyrate)
- **Detection Threshold:** Very low (exact value not documented)
- **Smell:** Berry-fruity, strawberry, apple, green
- **Common Dilution:** 1:100 to 1:1000 in DPG
- **Safe Dosage:** 0.01-0.1% in final formula
- **What Happens if Overdosed:** Sickly-sweet berry candy, artificial
- **Usage Note:** If you have 1:1000 dilution, use 1-3 drops max in 30mL

**E2MB Dosage by Dilution:**
| Your Dilution | Amount for 30mL | Final Concentration |
|---------------|-----------------|---------------------|
| 1:100 (1%) | 3-6 drops (0.15-0.3 mL) | 0.05-0.1% |
| 1:1000 (0.1%) | 1-3 drops (0.05-0.15 mL) | 0.017-0.05% |
| Neat | **DO NOT USE** - must dilute first | N/A |

---

### Undecavertol
- **Detection Threshold:** Low (powerful green character)
- **Smell:** Violet leaf, green-floral, fruity (kiwi, fig, pear), watery, seedy
- **Common Dilution:** Usually sold neat
- **Industry Standard Dosage:** 0.5% average, range traces-5%
- **IFRA:** No restriction (Category 4)
- **What Happens if Overdosed:** Can dominate with green-metallic character, but surprisingly forgiving
- **Nickname:** "The Anti-Sugar" (counteracts sweet notes)
- **Historical Examples:**
  - Givenchy Extravagance d'Amarige: ~0.1%
  - Lancaster Sunwater: ~0.4%
  - DKNY Be Delicious: **8-10%** (extreme overdose by Maurice Roucel)
- **Usage Note:** Requires careful dosage but can be pushed to high % for apple/green bombs

**Undecavertol Dosage Guide (Updated with Web Research):**
| Goal | Amount in 30mL | Final % | Reference |
|------|----------------|---------|-----------|
| Subtle background | 0.03-0.05 mL | 0.1-0.17% | Extravagance d'Amarige level |
| Moderate presence | 0.1-0.15 mL | 0.33-0.5% | Lancaster Sunwater level |
| Strong green/violet | 0.3-0.6 mL | 1-2% | Typical modern usage |
| Apple bomb | 1.5-3 mL | 5-10% | DKNY Be Delicious level (extreme!) |

### Pyrazines (2-Methoxypyrazine, Isobutyl-methoxypyrazine)
- **Detection Threshold:** 0.001-0.01 ppm
- **Smell:** Earthy, green, bell pepper, vegetable
- **Common Dilution:** 0.1-1% in ethanol
- **Safe Dosage:** 0.0001-0.01% in final formula
- **What Happens if Overdosed:** Overpowering vegetable/earthy smell, unpleasant
- **Found In:** Vetiver, galbanum (trace amounts)
- **Usage:** Adds "rootedness" to woody blends

---

### Methyl Anthranilate
- **Detection Threshold:** 0.04 ppm
- **Smell:** Grape, sweet, slightly medicinal
- **Common Dilution:** 1-10% in carrier
- **Safe Dosage:** 0.01-0.1% in final formula
- **What Happens if Overdosed:** Cloying grape soda smell
- **Found In:** Neroli, jasmine (trace)
- **Usage:** Adds grape undertone to florals

---

### Rose Oxide (cis and trans)
- **Detection Threshold:** 0.5 ppm
- **Smell:** Green-metallic rose, "thorn"
- **Common Dilution:** 1-10% in carrier
- **Safe Dosage:** 0.01-0.1% in final formula
- **What Happens if Overdosed:** Harsh metallic edge, unpleasant
- **Usage:** Adds "naturalness" to synthetic rose

---

### Furaneol (Strawberry Furanone)
- **Detection Threshold:** Very low
- **Smell:** Caramellic strawberry, cotton candy
- **Common Dilution:** 1% in DPG
- **Safe Dosage:** 0.001-0.01% in final formula
- **What Happens if Overdosed:** Sickly caramel-strawberry, artificial
- **Usage:** Adds depth to strawberry/berry accords

## TIER 3: VERY POWERFUL (Use at 0.1-1%)

### Eugenol
- **Smell:** Clove, spicy, warm
- **Safe Dosage:** 0.05-0.5% in final formula
- **What Happens if Overdosed:** Overwhelming clove, medicinal
- **IFRA:** Restricted in leave-on products (check limits)
- **Usage:** Adds spicy warmth to florals, orientals

---

### Safranal (Saffron component)
- **Smell:** Saffron, metallic, slightly medicinal
- **Safe Dosage:** 0.01-0.1% in final formula
- **What Happens if Overdosed:** Harsh metallic-medicinal smell
- **Usage:** Creates saffron note in modern niche perfumes

---

### Aldehyde C-12 MNA (Methylnonyl Acetaldehyde)
- **Smell:** Waxy, citrus peel, aldehydic "sparkle"
- **Safe Dosage:** 0.01-0.5% for subtle effect
- **What Happens if Overdosed:** Harsh waxy-soapy smell, too "Chanel No. 5"
- **Usage:** Adds aldehydic lift to florals

---

### Calone (Watermelon Ketone)
- **Smell:** Marine, watermelon, ozonic
- **Safe Dosage:** 0.1-2% (can go higher but risky)
- **What Happens if Overdosed:** Overwhelming melon, artificial aquatic
- **Usage:** Marine/aquatic accords

---

### Heliotropin (Piperonal)
- **Smell:** Cherry, vanilla, almond, heliotrope
- **Safe Dosage:** 0.5-3% in final formula
- **What Happens if Overdosed:** Sickly cherry-vanilla, cloying
- **IFRA:** Restricted (check limits)
- **Usage:** Adds cherry-vanilla to gourmands

---

## TIER 4: POWERFUL (Use at 1-5%)

### Hedione
- **Smell:** Jasmine, radiant, transparent
- **Safe Dosage:** 1-25% (very forgiving, but powerful amplifier)
- **What Happens if Overdosed:** At >25%, Hedione itself becomes obvious
- **Special Property:** Amplifies other notes without dominating (up to ~20%)
- **Usage:** Universal radiator/amplifier

---

### Cashmeran
- **Smell:** Musky, woody, soft, wet concrete
- **Safe Dosage:** 0.5-5% in final formula
- **What Happens if Overdosed:** Dominates with heavy musky-woody character
- **Usage:** Adds "texture" and hydrophobic caging

---

### Iso E Super
- **Smell:** Woody, cedar, velvety
- **Safe Dosage:** 5-40% (very forgiving)
- **What Happens if Overdosed:** At >40%, becomes obvious "woody veil"
- **Special Note:** ~10% of population can't smell it well (anosmia)
- **Usage:** Molecular cage, skin-scent creator

## DILUTION PROTOCOLS

### For Ultra-Powerful Materials (Indole, Skatole, β-Damascenone)

**Stock Solution Method:**
1. Weigh 0.1g of material
2. Dissolve in 99.9g ethanol
3. Result: 0.1% stock solution
4. Use 1-10 drops per 30mL formula

**Alternative - Sequential Dilution:**
1. Make 1% solution (1g in 99g ethanol)
2. Take 10mL of 1% solution
3. Dilute with 90mL ethanol → 0.1% solution
4. Take 1mL of 0.1% solution
5. Dilute with 9mL ethanol → 0.01% solution
6. Use this for trace additions

---

### For Very Powerful Materials (E2MB, Pyrazines, Eugenol)

**Direct Dilution:**
1. Weigh material
2. Dilute 1:100 in DPG or ethanol (1g + 99g carrier)
3. Use by drops in formula

**Example - E2MB 1:100 dilution:**
- 1 drop of 1:100 solution in 30mL = ~0.017% final
- 5 drops of 1:100 solution in 30mL = ~0.083% final

## TROUBLESHOOTING OVERDOSES

### "I added too much Indole and it smells fecal"
**Solution:**
- Add 2-3x more of your base oils (Iso E Super, Ambroxan)
- Add 20% more Hedione (masks indole)
- Let macerate 1 week (indole fades slightly)
- If still bad: dilute entire formula 1:2 with ethanol

---

### "E2MB is overwhelming, everything smells like berry candy"
**Solution:**
- Add citrus top notes (Bergamot, Lemon) to balance
- Add green notes (Undecavertol, Galbanum) to dry it out
- Increase woody base by 20-30%
- Consider starting over if >0.2% E2MB

---

### "Undecavertol killed all my sweetness"
**Solution:**
- Add more Vanillin or Ethyl Maltol (2x amount)
- Add Benzoin or Tonka to restore warmth
- Add 10% more Hedione to lift everything
- Undecavertol fades over time - wait 1 week

---

### "My aldehydes smell too harsh/waxy"
**Solution:**
- Add Linalool or Linalyl Acetate (smooths aldehydes)
- Add Hedione (softens aldehydic harshness)
- Reduce aldehyde concentration by 50%
- Let macerate - aldehydes mellow over time

## THE GOLDEN RULES

1. **When in doubt, use 1/10th of what you think you need** - you can always add more
2. **Dilute first, experiment later** - pure trace materials are dangerous
3. **One drop can ruin 100mL** - respect detection thresholds
4. **Maceration reveals power** - what seems weak on Day 1 may dominate on Day 7
5. **Test on skin, not paper** - some powerful materials bloom differently on skin
6. **Keep dilutions labeled** - forgetting dilution ratio = formula disaster
7. **β-Damascenone is stupidly powerful** - treat like radioactive material

---

## SAFE EXPERIMENTATION PROTOCOL

### Testing Unknown Powerful Materials

1. **Make 0.1% stock solution** (1g in 999g ethanol)
2. **Make 10mL test base:**
   - Iso E Super: 3mL
   - Hedione: 2mL
   - Ethanol: 5mL
3. **Add 1 drop of 0.1% stock** to test base
4. **Wait 24 hours, smell**
5. **If barely noticeable, add 1 more drop**
6. **Repeat until detectable**
7. **Calculate final %**

This prevents ruining large batches.

## STORAGE NOTES

**Powerful materials degrade faster:**
- **Indole:** Oxidizes → store in amber glass, cool, dark
- **Aldehydes:** Oxidize and polymerize → add BHT antioxidant
- **Pyrazines:** Evaporate easily → seal tightly
- **E2MB:** Relatively stable in dilution

**Diluted stocks last 6-12 months if stored properly.**
