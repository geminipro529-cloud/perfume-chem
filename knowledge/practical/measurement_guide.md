# Practical Measurement Guide (Drops / Grams / mL)

## Typical Conversions
- 1 mL ≈ 20 drops (varies by dropper and viscosity)
- Ethanol density ≈ 0.79 g/mL
- Most aroma chemicals are close to 0.85–1.05 g/mL; check density if precision matters.

## Quick Tables (for a 10 g batch, assuming 20 drops/mL)
| % | grams | drops |
|---|-------|-------|
| 1% | 0.10 g | ~2 drops |
| 2% | 0.20 g | ~4 drops |
| 5% | 0.50 g | ~10 drops |
| 10% | 1.00 g | ~20 drops |
| 15% | 1.50 g | ~30 drops |
| 20% | 2.00 g | ~40 drops |
| 30% | 3.00 g | ~60 drops |

For a 100 mL EdP (15–18% concentrate), perfume oil is ~15–18 mL; ethanol ~82–85 mL.

## Recommended Workflow
1) Weigh when possible (grams) for repeatability.
2) Use drops for fast prototyping (<10 g batches).
3) Record densities for any ingredient you use often.

## Notes
- High-viscosity materials (resins, some musks) may give fewer drops per mL; warm gently if needed, don’t overheat.
- Citrus oils oxidize; make small batches or add antioxidant (e.g., 0.05–0.1% BHT).
