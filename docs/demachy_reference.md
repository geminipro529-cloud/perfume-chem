# François Demachy — Perfumer Reference

## Vital
- Born 1949, Grasse, France. Father: pharmacist.
- Trained: Roure Bertrand Dupont (Givaudan), Charabot School (Grasse).
- Student worker in Grasse factories — every position from handling to composition.
- Director of R&D, Chanel (taught by Henri Robert).
- Dior Perfumer-Creator 2006–2021. Retired. TFF Lifetime Achievement 2022.
- Documentary: *NOSE* (2021) — follows him sourcing materials globally.

## Philosophy — Direct Quotes

> *"A perfume is a liquid in a nice bottle but if the liquid stays on the shelf, it's dead. The way to make it alive is to wear it."*

> *"I asked to be responsible for the purchasing of the raw materials. If you know the origin and quality of your ingredients, almost 50% of the job is already done."*

> *"I don't have a genuine process of inspiration — I don't have a recipe."*

> *"The main problem I have is to know when to stop."*

> *"I am an artisan... it's art craft, not art. I can't compare my work to Mozart."*

## His Teacher's Rule (Jean Cavallier)

> *"In a mixture of items no one product should stand out. A balance is required."*

## Aesthetic Preferences

- **"I like to give perfumes a feeling of roundness."**
- **"Patchouli and amber, in particular."** — favorite materials.
- **"Flowers, flowers, flowers! I'm from Grasse. No perfume without flowers."**
- **"Amber has a sensuous effect that is deeply appealing even if we are unaware of it."**

## Method

1. **Raw materials first**: Controls Dior's entire supply chain. Calabrian bergamot, Grasse flowers, Indonesian patchouli, Madagascar ylang-ylang, Scottish ambergris.
2. **Sensorial, not archival**: Doesn't reference old formulas. Creates from sensory memory.
3. **Remove to reveal**: "Remove all the earthy notes and let the flowers shine."
4. **Multiple directions**: Starts every project with many directions, not one.
5. **Difficulty stopping**: Knows when to begin, struggles knowing when it's finished.
6. **Unconscious appeal**: Uses amber/musk materials as felt-presence, not identified-notes.

## Key Demachy Dior Fragrances
- Dior Homme Cologne (2007, 2013) — radical minimalism: bergamot, grapefruit blossom, white musk
- Sauvage (2015) — bergamot, ambrox, pepper: "appeals to the unconscious"
- Eau Sauvage Parfum (2012) — myrrh, vetiver
- Miss Dior reformulations
- Fahrenheit 32, Fahrenheit Absolute
- La Collection Privée (Maison Christian Dior)

## Rules for Demachy-Style Formulation
1. One citrus per formula — solitary, massive dose (22-36% of concentrate)
2. Grapefruit blossom = DHC signature: petitgrain + paradisamide
3. Base must not dominate the citrus opening (B:T < 2.0 preferred)
4. Amber materials as felt-scaffold, not identified-notes (Ambrofix/Norlimbanol at sub-note doses)
5. Vetival as micro-texture whisper (OAV 1000-2500) — rewards the curious
6. Every material earns its place — no decorative traces
7. Maximum impact per material, minimum material count (~14)
8. OAV analysis required — material must be perceptible but balanced
9. Roundness over sharpness — hedione provides radiance-softness
10. Flowers must be present even in "citrus" fragrance — DHC has grapefruit blossom
