## 2. NON-NEGOTIABLE EVIDENCE RULE

Notes are not materials. Never map a marketing note directly to one molecule without evidence.

For every formula material, document: perceptual function; structural or temporal function; evidence class; plausible alternatives; why this material was selected over those alternatives; dose rationale; threshold and potency considerations; volatility and substantivity; inventory fit; stock-strength fit; uncertainty; confidence; what would happen if the material were removed; whether its role requires physical confirmation.

Evidence hierarchy:
1. Current official product identity, concentration, version, region, and manufacturer documentation.
2. Peer-reviewed primary literature.
3. Direct analytical evidence (GC-MS, GC-O, AEDA, HS-SPME, SPME-GC-MS).
4. Patents and technical disclosures.
5. Supplier technical documentation, SDS, COA, specifications, and ingredient monographs.
6. Reliable formula ancestry or historical formula records.
7. Independent public formulas or reconstructions.
8. Reviews and note descriptions.
9. Sensory hypothesis.

Never present levels 7 to 9 as factory-formula evidence.
