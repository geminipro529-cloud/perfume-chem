# Aventus Cassis Rewrite Design

**Date:** 2026-07-06
**Formula target:** `formulas/Cassis_Iris_Smoke_30mL_EdP.md`
**Goal:** Rewrite the formula so it clearly reminds the wearer of Aventus while avoiding green, mossy, or vintage chypre character.

---

## Problem

The current formula is intentionally differentiated from Aventus. It centers cassis, iris, smoke, moss, and dark chypre structure. That architecture conflicts with the new brief, which is to make the fragrance read as Aventus-adjacent rather than as an artistic cassis-iris chypre.

The rewrite must shift the formula away from:

- iris as the signature heart
- oakmoss-labdanum dominance
- green petitgrain/juniper/chypre lift
- obvious vintage mossy darkness

The rewrite must move toward:

- polished fruity top with a pineapple-adjacent effect
- noticeable cassis as a supporting identity note
- soft modern smoke
- balanced cedar-musk-ambergris-style drydown
- enough earthy darkness to feel masculine, but not enough to read as a 1950s green chypre

---

## Brief

### Primary olfactive target

Create a modern fruity-smoky woody masculine that reminds someone of Aventus on first spray and through the first hour, while adding a noticeable cassis thread.

### Explicit user decisions

- Fruit direction: pineapple-adjacent, less literal and more polished
- Smoke level: soft smoke, mostly fruity-woody
- Drydown: balanced cedar, musk, and a little earthy darkness
- Cassis level: noticeable, clearly blackcurrant, but still secondary to the overall Aventus memory

### Non-goals

- Do not make a vintage green chypre
- Do not make iris the signature
- Do not build around oakmoss-labdanum weight
- Do not let green herbal materials dominate the top
- Do not turn cassis into the main character ahead of the Aventus memory

---

## Chosen approach

### Recommended direction: memory-match

Use a recognizable Aventus-style structure:

1. bright citrus opening
2. pineapple-adjacent fruity illusion
3. smooth smoky woody heart
4. cedar-patchouli-musky drydown

Then add cassis as a dark berry shadow inside the fruit accord rather than replacing the fruit accord outright.

### Rejected approaches

#### Cleaner luxury

This would emphasize transparent platform materials and reduce the fruit signature. It would be elegant but less immediately recognizable as Aventus-adjacent.

#### Smoky woods-first

This would emphasize smoke, patchouli, and woods earlier. It would risk losing the fruity identity too quickly and reading as a generic smoky woody masculine.

---

## Architecture

### Family

Modern fruity-woody smoky masculine.

### Opening

The opening should read as bright citrus and polished fruit rather than sharp green. It should suggest the memory of pineapple without forcing a literal pineapple reconstruction.

The top should be built from:

- bergamot brightness
- orange and/or grapefruit support
- pineapple-adjacent effect from specialty fruity materials
- noticeable cassis as a dark accent

It should avoid:

- heavy petitgrain
- obvious aromatic green lift
- aldehydic champagne sharpness as a dominant effect

### Heart

The heart should bridge fruit into smoke and wood without becoming floral or powdery. It should use soft radiance rather than a floral bouquet or iris body.

The heart should be built from:

- Hedione for lift and diffusion
- a restrained smoke bridge
- a light supporting floral-radiant layer only if needed to avoid hollowness

It should avoid:

- iris materials
- powdery violet/orris signatures
- obvious muguet or floral-chypre construction

### Smoke

Smoke should be present but smooth. It should recall Aventus-style smoky woods rather than tarry leather, church incense, or phenolic ash.

The smoke should be led by:

- Nagarmortha Oil as the primary smoke texture

Optional support can come from:

- very controlled birch or phenolic accents

It should avoid:

- dominating cade character
- loud guaiacol bite
- dirty leather emphasis

### Drydown

The drydown should be balanced, modern, and masculine with some earthy darkness. It should not feel moss-led or vintage.

The drydown should be built from:

- cedar structure
- patchouli darkness
- Ambrofix for ambergris-like mineral persistence
- a modern musk chord for projection and skin presence

It should avoid:

- obvious oakmoss-labdanum gravity
- thick resinous chypre warmth
- powdery iris residue

## Material strategy

### Materials to keep and repurpose

- `Bergamot FCF`
- `Cassis Base 345B`
- `Nagarmortha Oil`
- `Hedione`
- `Iso E Super`
- `Ambrofix`
- `Cedarwood oil Virginia`
- `Patchouli EO`
- `Romandolide`

These materials align with the desired fruity-woody smoky direction and can anchor the rewrite without keeping the old formula's identity.

### Materials to add or emphasize

- `Allyl Amyl Glycolate`
- `Hexyl Acetate (10%)`
- `Orange Peel EO` and/or `Grapefruit FCF`
- `Habanolide` or `Ethylene Brassylate`, depending whether the drydown needs cleaner diffusion or rounder depth

These materials support a polished pineapple-adjacent fruit accord and a modern masculine base.

### Materials to reduce strongly

- `Oakmoss Absolute`
- `Labdanum Resinoid`
- `Petitgrain EO Paraguay`
- `Juniper Berry EO`
- `Black Pepper EO`

These materials are major contributors to the current green, chypre, or overly textured direction.

### Materials to remove

- `Alpha Irone`
- `Ultralia`
- `Orivone`
- `Beta Ionone`
- `Geosmin`
- `Freesia HDI`
- `Osmanthus Absolute`

These materials push the formula toward iris, powder, earth, or artistic complexity that conflicts with the new brief.

### Smoke support note

If smoke needs support, the first candidate should be a very restrained dark woody-smoky accent rather than an overtly tarry or green material. The smoke must stay smooth and subordinate to the fruit-to-wood transition.

---

## Candidate role map

### Fruit accord

- `Bergamot FCF`: classical bright citrus entry
- `Orange Peel EO` and/or `Grapefruit FCF`: warm citrus body and brightness
- `Allyl Amyl Glycolate`: pineapple-adjacent juicy lift
- `Hexyl Acetate (10%)`: smooth fruit skin effect and soft juiciness
- `Cassis Base 345B`: noticeable blackcurrant shadow
- optional trace `Methyl Pamplemousse (10%)`: tart support if the top needs more sparkle

### Heart bridge

- `Hedione`: radiance amplifier and volume expander
- optional restrained floral support only if the formula needs connection through the middle

### Smoke and wood

- `Nagarmortha Oil`: primary smoky woody bridge
- `Cedarwood oil Virginia`: dry pencil-shaving cedar identity
- `Patchouli EO`: earthy masculine darkness
- `Iso E Super`: molecular woody space and diffusion
- `Ambrofix`: clean ambergris-like persistence

### Musk chord

- `Romandolide`: projection axis
- `Habanolide`: clean warmth axis if the base needs more skin warmth
- `Ethylene Brassylate`: depth axis if the base needs more roundness than Habanolide provides

---

## Performance shape

### 0-5 minutes

Bright bergamot-citrus opening with polished fruity lift and clear cassis memory. The fruit should feel expensive and modern rather than candy-like or green.

### 5-30 minutes

Fruit transitions into smooth smoky woods. Nagarmortha should appear clearly but softly. The composition should still feel recognizably linked to its fruity top.

### 30 minutes-4 hours

Cedar, patchouli, Ambrofix, and musks dominate, with some residual fruit stain and light smoke. The fragrance should feel masculine and modern, not mossy or vintage.

---

## Constraints

- Must obey inventory availability and current dilutions
- Must use ppm, ODT, and OAV logic in any later evaluation
- Must not create new pipeline scripts
- Must optimize for the Aventus-adjacent brief, not for arbitrary score chasing
- Must keep green/chypre/vintage cues below the threshold where they dominate perception

---

## Testing and validation plan

After the rewrite is drafted:

1. run the formula through the existing release gate pipeline
2. run the required pipeline analysis formatter
3. inspect the headspace OAV ranking before gate summaries
4. confirm the top is fruit-led rather than green-led
5. confirm the heart is smoke-wood transition rather than chypre heart
6. confirm the drydown is cedar-musk-patchouli with limited moss/resin pull
7. adjust any sub-threshold character materials that fail their functional role

---

## Open implementation decisions

- exact citrus balance between `Orange Peel EO` and `Grapefruit FCF`
- whether `Methyl Pamplemousse (10%)` is needed or redundant
- whether the musk chord should lean cleaner (`Habanolide`) or rounder (`Ethylene Brassylate`)
- how much smoke support is needed beyond `Nagarmortha Oil`

These are implementation-level decisions and should be resolved during formula construction and pipeline review rather than changing the approved design direction.
