# Volume 14 — Comparison and Benchmarking

**Status:** non-scalar comparison doctrine; comparative sensory superiority is `NOT TESTED`

## Construct

Comparison is a structured account of similarities, differences,
incompatibilities, and missing evidence between two target-locked depth
architectures. It is not a race toward one total. Perfumes can be deeper in
different senses: one may contain unusually resolved internal anatomy, another
may achieve stronger temporal transformation, another may create spatial
enclosure, and another may generate a particularly rewarding tension-and-relief
path. Combining these dimensions into one number destroys the information
needed to improve the formula and invites arbitrary weights.

A valid benchmark begins by deciding what the reference is allowed to mean.
It can be a formula architecture, a construction principle, a physical sample,
a blinded sensory control, a market positioning reference, or a named ideal.
These are not interchangeable. A formula document can support statements about
rows, stocks, declared functions, ratios, and modeled outputs. It cannot by
itself prove how the perfume smells. A physical sample can support sensory
comparison only through a defined protocol, and its identity, storage, batch,
concentration, carrier, application, and age must be recorded.

The user-named DHP 2025 and Opus V references are therefore bounded
architectural comparators in this doctrine. Their useful role is to ask whether
a new perfume has equally serious object resolution, base or support anatomy,
texture, topology, temporal movement, enclosure, and hedonic organization
applied to its own target. They are not universal ingredient templates and do
not authorize the claim that a floral, wood, or hybrid smells similar, equally
deep, more luxurious, more hedonic, or better performing. If exact formulas or
physical controls are absent, those outcomes remain `NOT TESTED`.

## Mechanisms

The comparison mechanism has seven stages. First, lock each target independently:
name, emotional tone, family hierarchy, realism or abstraction, forbidden
drift, ideal ledger, current-inventory ledger, and claim ceiling. Second, map
the required depth dimensions for each target. Third, list target-linked
mechanisms within each dimension. Fourth, record the controlled probe and
evidence state for each mechanism. Fifth, identify shared mechanism kinds,
unique mechanisms, and target incompatibilities. Sixth, compare coverage and
failure modes without converting absence into inferiority. Seventh, abstain
where the evidence cannot support a comparison.

Shared mechanism kinds do not imply shared smell. A magnolia and a wooden
perfume may both use a surface/interior contrast, recurrent recognizers, a
shadow layer, and a late enclosure mechanism while expressing wholly different
objects. Conversely, two floral formulas may use different mechanisms to
achieve comparable target functions. Mechanism equivalence is functional and
evidence-bounded, not molecular, olfactory, or aesthetic identity.

Unique mechanisms are valuable only in relation to the target. A thirty-part
wooden base may contain grain, fibre, body, polish, resin, root, mineral seam,
smoke, humidity, air, skin contact, recurrence, and multiple handoffs. Its
construction burden is justified if controlled changes show that these
functions are distinct and necessary. Transplanting thirty wooden rows into a
flower does not create floral depth. The flower must instead resolve the
specific anatomy, interfaces, texture, time, and pleasure its name requires.

Target incompatibilities protect against false deficits. Botanical realism may
require recognizer fidelity and living moisture that an aldehydic abstraction
explicitly rejects. A skin musk may seek low contrast and intimate continuity,
where an incense chamber seeks source/plume/residue separation. A saturated
amber may intentionally minimize negative space. These differences must be
reported as incompatible goals, not scored as failures.

## Material translation

Material-level comparison starts only after functional alignment. For every
mechanism, compare ideal candidates, current-inventory bindings, stock
strengths, active dose, role overlap, natural-composite treatment, physical
data provenance, and missing-chemical impact. A material present in both
formulas may perform different functions; a function present in both formulas
may be implemented by different materials. Neither condition authorizes a
sensory equivalence claim.

Formula length can be reported as construction metadata alongside active
concentration, number of modules, number of controlled functions, and number
of tested mechanisms. It cannot enter a total depth score. Duplicate rows,
carrier rows, alternate stock strengths, or multiple chemicals contributing
one undifferentiated chord must not be counted as independent perceptual depth.
Likewise, a low row count is not automatically elegant or sufficient.

OAV, vapor pressure, activity coefficients, modeled headspace, and temporal
simulation may reveal implausible doses, cliffs, stock errors, dominance risks,
or candidate windows for testing. They do not establish similarity,
performance, texture, hedonic quality, or depth. A Lemonile-like stock error or
neat-versus-diluted mismatch can change calculated activity by orders of
magnitude and must trigger a stock-rebase audit before any comparison proceeds.

## Interactions

Comparison depends on every preceding volume. Construct boundaries determine
which statements are computational, physical, sensory, or hedonic. Identity
prevents a reference from replacing the candidate's name. Information theory
distinguishes useful target information from redundant material count. Anatomy
and topology supply the dimension-wise comparison units. Contrast and texture
expose whether density is articulated or merely saturated. Temporal and
spatial volumes prevent longevity and projection from standing in for
architecture. Hedonic comparison separates valence, re-smelling, fascination,
comfort, tension, relief, and fatigue. Nonlinear behavior warns that matching
parts does not imply matching mixtures. Physical chemistry provides plausibility
gates without granting perception.

Cross-family comparisons require more target incompatibilities and fewer
direct equivalence claims than within-family comparisons. Within-family work
still requires independent target locks: two roses can differ in species,
temperature, age, moisture, petal texture, stem prominence, realism, emotional
tone, and desired shadow. A benchmark may impose a floor on experimental rigor
or architectural resolution, but not a requirement to reproduce the
reference's materials or family-specific anatomy.

Benchmarking also interacts with learning. Each controlled result should update
only the mechanism and target conditions actually tested. A successful floral
shadow ratio does not become a universal shadow rule. A wood-interface result
may suggest a cross-family hypothesis, but transfer requires a new target-linked
trial. This prevents an impressive reference from becoming an unexamined
house chassis.

## Failure modes

The principal failure is scalar collapse: averaging dimensions into an overall
depth score, ranking candidates, or naming a winner despite target
incompatibilities and missing evidence. The second is reference capture, where
the candidate abandons its identity to imitate a prestigious benchmark. The
third is count worship, including the assumption that matching a thirty-row
base requires thirty rows regardless of functions. The fourth is physical-
sensory promotion, where OAV, headspace, volatility, or formula metadata are
described as observed performance or pleasure.

Other failures include treating persistence as temporal quality, projection as
spatial architecture, pleasantness as hedonic complexity, darkness as shadow,
saturation as depth, classification accuracy as object resolution, and density
as texture. Benchmark leakage occurs when assessors know candidate identity,
price, author, ingredient count, or intended prestige. Order confounding occurs
when every assessor smells the reference first. A duplicated canonical cell,
stock-strength conflict, missing sample, or inconsistent total must trigger
reconciliation or hold; it must not be averaged away.

A final failure is forced completeness. When one design has no valid probe for
a nonlinear or spatial hypothesis, the comparator must abstain. It must not
convert `NOT TESTED` to zero, infer absence from a single sample, or reward the
other design merely for having more paperwork.

## Observables

The comparison record contains, per dimension: each target definition;
mechanism identifiers and kinds; participant bindings; probe identifiers;
evidence states; shared mechanisms; mechanisms unique to A; mechanisms unique
to B; explicit incompatibilities; unresolved stock or data conflicts; and the
required abstentions. It may also report construction row counts as descriptive
metadata, never as a verdict.

Formula evidence can establish deterministic equality or difference in source
hashes, rows, concentrations, modules, and declared roles. Model evidence can
establish the output of a specified calculation. Physical analytical evidence
can establish observations from an admitted method and sample. Sensory
evidence can establish protocol-bound assessor responses. Hedonic evidence can
establish blinded choices, ratings, or ties for the tested population and
conditions. Each statement must carry the narrowest applicable evidence state.

The final display should be a dimension table or ledger, not a leaderboard.
Useful conclusions include: shared causal grammar, different material
realization, stronger probe coverage in one dimension, unresolved evidence in
another, incompatible targets, and candidate-specific failure risks. “Better”
is admissible only for an explicitly isolated endpoint and valid controlled
data, never as an unqualified perfume-wide conclusion.

## Controlled tests

Begin with a document-only comparator test. Supply two complete architecture
profiles and verify that the output contains dimension-wise shared and unique
mechanisms, target incompatibilities, probe coverage, and abstentions. It must
contain no winner, rank, total, weighted sum, or superiority field. Submit a
count-only argument and confirm fail-closed rejection rather than a low score.

For physical comparison, use exact reference controls and constant-total,
blinded arms. Same-family candidates should be compared first on named-object
identity, defining anatomy, and forbidden drift; cross-family candidates should
be compared on shared mechanism kinds and rigor rather than note similarity.
Evaluate fixed temporal windows, with order balancing, adaptation control,
washout, and separate score sheets. A five-window record is a minimum temporal
shape, not automatic evidence of good temporal architecture.

Test DHP- or Opus-V-like architectural ambition through isolated endpoints:
support anatomy resolution, surface/interior texture, bridge legibility,
shadow benefit, transition quality, enclosure, identity retention, desire to
re-smell, and anti-collapse under omission. Do not ask one assessor for a
single “same depth” judgment and treat it as mechanistic evidence. Include
ties, record assessor heterogeneity, and separate preference from depth,
complexity, realism, similarity, and performance.

Include adversarial fixtures. A high-row formula with redundant materials must
not outrank a low-row coherent design. A long-lasting flat formula must not pass
temporal comparison. A high-projecting formula must not pass spatial comparison
without spatial data. A dense low-negative-space target must not be penalized
merely for saturation. A family-migrating hybrid must not fail solely because
its dominant family changes. A stock-strength conflict, incomplete pair total,
unblinded hedonic response, or absent reference sample must produce hold or
abstention.

## Claim boundary

The comparator describes architectures and evidence coverage. It cannot prove
that either perfume smells deeper, more complex, more luxurious, more realistic,
more similar to DHP 2025 or Opus V, more pleasant, longer-lasting, safer, or
release-ready. A complete document comparison remains a computational design
result. Physical and sensory outcomes remain `NOT TESTED` until exact samples,
protocols, and admissible results exist. Even then, conclusions stay endpoint-
specific and population-bounded. There is no universal winner and no single
score.
