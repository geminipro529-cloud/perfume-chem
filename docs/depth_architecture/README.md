# Universal Perfume Depth Architecture

**Status:** canonical computational design doctrine  
**Physical sensory depth, liking, realism, similarity, performance, safety, stability, and release:** `NOT TESTED` unless a formula-specific evidence packet states otherwise

This directory is Perfume-Chem's detailed reference for reasoning about depth
in any perfume family. It exists because the word *depth* is often used to
collapse several different phenomena: a long formula, a dark base, persistence,
many detectable materials, rich texture, movement through time, emotional
reward, or a coherent but internally differentiated odor object. Those
phenomena may cooperate, but they are not interchangeable and cannot be
recovered from one number.

## Normative precedence

The volumes below have normative precedence over abbreviated depth summaries,
formula commentary, benchmark prose, and family-specific shorthand. A family
document may impose stricter target-specific gates, but it may not redefine
construction count as perceptual depth, turn a model into sensory evidence, or
replace dimension-wise comparison with a single score. `AGENTS.md`, live
inventory authority, formula-specific physical evidence, and explicit user
instructions retain their higher repository authority.

The doctrine is deliberately distributed. No single volume is the entire
theory. A reader evaluating a perfume must begin with construct boundaries,
lock the named object, describe its anatomy and relations, then examine
contrast, texture, time, space, hedonics, nonlinear interactions, physical
constraints, family translation, comparison, and validation. Skipping a volume
is permitted only when the target explicitly makes that construct inapplicable;
the omission must be recorded rather than silently scored as zero.

## How to use the doctrine

The minimum admissible workflow is target-first. Name the perfume, emotional
tone, family or hybrid hierarchy, realism or abstraction target, forbidden
drift, TARGET/IDEAL ledger, CURRENT-INVENTORY ledger, and claim ceiling. Then
declare the dimensions that the target actually requires. For each required
dimension, state a causal mechanism, participating materials or modules, the
expected target-linked change, the likely failure mode, and a controlled probe
capable of falsifying the mechanism. Dosing begins only after this architecture
closes.

The doctrine does not impose a formula-length aesthetic. A sixty-row wood
system can be justified when it resolves grain, body, temperature, polish,
root, mineral seam, resin, air, recurrence, persistence, interfaces, and
controlled microtexture. The same sixty rows are padding when they duplicate
one generic woody-amber chassis. A twelve-row perfume can be deep when its
relations and transformations are exact; it can also be underresolved when the
named target explicitly requires more independent anatomy and interfaces than
the design represents. Count diagnoses construction burden and possible
compression or padding; it does not answer the perceptual question.

All computational states remain experiment-design states. `DESIGN_READY` means
that the hypotheses and controls are closed enough to test. It does not mean
the perfume smells deep, performs well, resembles a reference, is liked, is
safe, or is ready to release. Those outcomes remain `NOT TESTED` until the
corresponding physical evidence is bound to the exact formula and protocol.

## Formula-bound execution gate

For an existing formula, `adapt_floral_formula_depth_profile` binds the exact
parser-visible CURRENT-INVENTORY rows to one exact pipeline payload. The gate
rejects material-set mismatches, total-dose mismatches, fewer than five temporal
windows, incomplete universal-dimension coverage, or any direct raw transfer
below 10 uL without a practical prepared dilution. It preserves each row's
role ID and declared function and binds formula-definition, inventory,
scientific-input, pipeline-source, and analysis-input hashes. Unknown OAV
materials remain explicit holds; modeled OAV and heuristic temporal output do
not become sensory or performance evidence.

`build_bounded_reference_depth_profile` and `compare_depth_profiles` may then
compare a formula with a named local reference dimension by dimension. A
cross-family match of mechanism vocabulary is reported as shared structure
under different target anatomy, never as equivalence or superiority. The V9
complexity registry hash-binds this program as
`FUTURE_CANDIDATE_NOT_VALIDATED`; it is durable candidate governance, not
runtime admission.
