## Finish procedure retained from the source workbook

1. After step 39, weigh the separate `300 mg` Ambrox operation at step 40.
2. Mix at room temperature. Do not convert the solid to microlitres, apply
   uncontrolled heat, filter it out, or alter another fragrance dose.
3. Continue with B4 and B6 only after the Ambrox operation has been incorporated.
4. After all 63 operations, let the mixture return to room temperature and
   measure the actual compound volume.
5. If completely dissolved and below `6.000 mL`, add TEC incrementally to the
   measured `6.000 mL` compound endpoint. Do not assume `400 µL`.
6. If the compound is already at the endpoint, add no TEC. If above the endpoint
   or if solids persist, stop and record the condition; do not remove material.
7. Transfer quantitatively, using part of the final alcohol for rinses. Add
   alcohol to an actually measured final volume of `30.000 mL` when suitable
   volumetric equipment is available. With bottle markings or summed pipette
   transfers alone, label the result `NOMINAL_30_ML`, not exact measured volume.
8. Record immediate clarity, clarity after `24–48 h`, and clarity after 7 days.
