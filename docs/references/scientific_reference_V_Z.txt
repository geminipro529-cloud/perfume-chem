  1. VANILLIN (10%)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  Your stock: 10% solution (dissolved from crystalline solid)

  CHEMICAL IDENTITY
  ─────────────────
  IUPAC:          4-Hydroxy-3-methoxybenzaldehyde
  Synonyms:       Vanillin, Vanilla aldehyde, Vanilin, Lioxin
  CAS:            121-33-5
  Formula:        C₈H₈O₃
  MW:             152.15 g/mol
  BP:             285°C (sublimes before boiling)
  VP:             ~0.02 Pa (crystalline solid at RT)
  CLP:            1.21
  ODT:            ~0.02 µg/L (low — quite potent for its ubiquity)

  OPK — STRUCTURE-ACTIVITY
  ────────────────────────
  SAR Class:      Hydroxymethoxybenzaldehydes — phenolic aldehyde class
  Olfactophore:   Ohloff: Vanillin's three functional groups create a 
                  TRIDENTATE binding motif:
                  • Aldehyde (-CHO) → sweet/sharp receptor activation
                  • Phenolic OH (para) → hydrogen bond donor → warmth
                  • Methoxy (-OCH₃) (meta) → modulator → round, creamy facet
                  
                  Remove any one group = different smell entirely:
                  • Remove -OCH₃ → p-hydroxybenzaldehyde (weak, less sweet)
                  • Remove -OH → veratraldehyde (harsh, less vanilla)
                  • Remove -CHO → guaiacol (smoke, NOT vanilla)
  Mechanism:      Vanillin is the MOST PRODUCED flavor/fragrance chemical in 
                  the world (~18,000 tons/year). It occurs naturally in cured 
                  Vanilla planifolia pods (1.5-2.5%) where it forms via 
                  enzymatic hydrolysis of glucovanillin during the curing 
                  process.
                  
                  Kraft: Vanillin activates the VR1 (TRPV1) receptor at 
                  low concentrations, which may explain why vanilla is 
                  perceived as "warm." This cross-modal sensation (smell 
                  → warmth) is pharmacologically mediated.
  Key SAR Rule:   4-OH + 3-OCH₃ + 1-CHO on benzene ring = vanilla. All three 
                  mandatory. Position-specific.

  ARCTANDER — ODOR PROFILE
  ────────────────────────
  Character:      Sweet, warm, creamy, balsamic, vanilla, with a faint chocolate-
                  phenolic undertone. "The smell of home, comfort, warmth — 
                  the most universally liked odor in human psychology studies."
  Tenacity:       Heart → base; crystalline nature gives excellent persistence. 
                  On blotter: 24-48h. On skin: variable (metabolized).
  Behavior:       At 10% solution: extremely workable. Universal sweetener. 
                  Even 0.5% of your 10% solution in a formula (= 0.05% neat) 
                  adds perceptible warmth. Use with restraint in fresh or 
                  green compositions.

  CARLES — SYSTEMATIC POSITION
  ────────────────────────────
  Position:       HEART → BASE (sweet-warm anchor)
  Carles Method:  A fundamental Carles "note" — vanillin is one of the base 
                  pillars (alongside coumarin, musk, frankincense, sandalwood).
  Pairing Rule:   Vanillin 10% + labdanum = amber (THE classic amber formula).
                  Vanillin 10% + coumarin (Tonka FO) = soft oriental base.
                  Vanillin 10% + ISO E Super = modern woody-sweet.
                  Vanillin 10% + Ethyl Maltol = CAREFUL (double sweetness).
                  Vanillin 10% + patchouli = chypre-sweet foundation.

  ROUDNITSKA — AESTHETIC ROLE
  ──────────────────────────
  Function:       "Universal warmth" — the single most effective way to make 
                  any composition warmer, sweeter, more inviting.
  Craft Note:     Used by EVERY perfumer in EVERY formula type. Even "fresh" 
                  fragrances contain traces of vanillin for perceived warmth 
                  and roundness.

  JELLINEK — PSYCHOLOGICAL MAP
  ────────────────────────────
  Axis Position:  NARCOTIC, erogenic
  Effect:         Comforting, addictive, warm, nurturing, maternal, sensual.
  Map Quadrant:   Lower-right (deeply narcotic, warm, addictive)

  PRACTICAL USAGE
  ───────────────
  Your form:      10% solution (dissolved)
  Typical %:      1-5% of concentrate (of your 10% solution = 0.1-0.5% neat)
  Max safe %:     5% (neat)
  Best with:      Labdanum (amber), coumarin/tonka (oriental), musks (soften), 
                  ISO E Super (modernize), Ethylene Brassylate (sweet musk)
  Avoid:          Stacking with Ethyl Maltol AND Maple Lactone (triple sweetness 
                  = cloying); overdose in fresh/green compositions
  Handle as:      At 10%, moderate potency. Versatile workhorse. The most 
                  universally used perfumery ingredient after ethanol.


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  2. VERDOX
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  Your stock: Neat

  CHEMICAL IDENTITY
  ─────────────────
  IUPAC:          2-tert-Butylcyclohexyl acetate (mixture of cis/trans isomers)
  Synonyms:       Verdox (Privi/various), Agrumex, Vertenex, 
                  Woody-green acetate
  CAS:            88-41-5
  Formula:        C₁₂H₂₂O₂
  MW:             198.30 g/mol
  BP:             ~230°C
  VP:             ~3 Pa
  CLP:            3.80
  ODT:            ~2 µg/L (moderate — volume player)

  OPK — STRUCTURE-ACTIVITY
  ────────────────────────
  SAR Class:      Cyclohexyl esters — tert-alkyl substituted cyclohexanyl acetate
  Olfactophore:   The tert-butyl group on the cyclohexane ring provides steric 
                  bulk → woody character. The acetate ester provides the 
                  fruity-green lift. cis/trans ratio affects the 
                  green-to-woody balance.
  Mechanism:      Kraft: tert-Butylcyclohexanol and its acetate belong to a 
                  family of "green-woody-fruity" odorants. The tert-butyl 
                  group prevents easy metabolic clearance, contributing to 
                  longevity on skin.
                  
                  Ohloff: Cyclohexyl acetates with branched alkyl substituents 
                  generate odors straddling the green-woody-fruity triangle. 
                  The trans-isomer is more "green-woody"; the cis-isomer 
                  more "fruity-fresh."
  Key SAR Rule:   tert-Butyl-cyclohexyl-OAc = green-woody-fruity bridger. 
                  Branching = woody; ester = fresh.

  ARCTANDER — ODOR PROFILE
  ────────────────────────
  Character:      Fresh, green, slightly woody, fruity (pear/apple), clean, 
                  with an outdoorsy quality. "A green apple in a forest."
  Tenacity:       Top → heart; reasonable persistence for an ester (4-6h). 
                  The tert-butyl group extends longevity vs simpler acetates.
  Behavior:       Excellent fresh modifier. Adds "green freshness" without 
                  the harsh grassiness of cis-3-hexenol or the overwhelming 
                  power of galbanum.

  CARLES — SYSTEMATIC POSITION
  ────────────────────────────
  Position:       TOP → HEART (green-fresh-woody connector)
  Carles Method:  The "gentle green" — provides green character without 
                  aggression.
  Pairing Rule:   Verdox + cis-3-hexenol = amplified green (careful!).
                  Verdox + linalool = fresh-green floral bridge.
                  Verdox + bergamot = green-citrus opening.
                  Verdox + ISO E Super = green-woody haze.
                  Verdox + Dihydromyrcenol = reinforced masculine freshness.

  ROUDNITSKA — AESTHETIC ROLE
  ──────────────────────────
  Function:       "Polite green" — provides green freshness for compositions 
                  that need nature but not wilderness.
  Craft Note:     A very versatile modifier. Can go into almost any 
                  composition type to add freshness without hijacking 
                  the direction.

  JELLINEK — PSYCHOLOGICAL MAP
  ────────────────────────────
  Axis Position:  STIMULATING, slightly exalting
  Effect:         Fresh, clean, energizing, outdoorsy, "morning walk."
  Map Quadrant:   Upper-left (fresh stimulation)

  PRACTICAL USAGE
  ───────────────
  Your form:      Neat
  Typical %:      3-10% of concentrate
  Max safe %:     15%
  Best with:      Linalool, bergamot, cis-3-hexenol, ISO E Super, 
                  Dihydromyrcenol
  Avoid:          Overuse creates a generic "freshener" quality
  Handle as:      Standard fresh green material. Good volume. Forgiving. 
                  Nice alternative to harsher green materials.


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  3. VERTOFIX COEUR
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  Your stock: Neat

  CHEMICAL IDENTITY
  ─────────────────
  IUPAC:          Methyl cedryl ether (specifically methyl ether of 
                  cedr-8-en-13-ol, also written as methyl cedryl ketone 
                  in some sources — Vertofix Coeur is the pure high-
                  quality fraction)
  Synonyms:       Vertofix Coeur (IFF), Cedramber (Firmenich), 
                  Methyl cedryl ether, Amber cedarwood note
  CAS:            19870-74-7 (cedramber) / 67874-81-1 (Vertofix Coeur grade)
  Formula:        C₁₆H₂₈O
  MW:             236.39 g/mol
  BP:             ~295°C
  VP:             ~0.01 Pa
  CLP:            4.80
  ODT:            ~0.03 µg/L

  OPK — STRUCTURE-ACTIVITY
  ────────────────────────
  SAR Class:      Cedryl ethers — tricyclic sesquiterpenoid ether (cedrene 
                  skeleton)
  Olfactophore:   The cedrene tricyclic skeleton (cedrane = three fused rings 
                  including a gem-dimethylcyclobutane) provides the woody 
                  foundation. The methyl ether modification converts dry 
                  cedarwood into warm, ambery cedarwood.
  Mechanism:      Ohloff: The cedrene skeleton is found naturally in Virginia 
                  cedarwood oil (Juniperus virginiana). Etherification of the 
                  hydroxyl group changes the receptor interaction from 
                  "dry pencil" (cedrol) to "warm amber-wood" (Vertofix) — a 
                  major shift from one simple chemical transformation.
                  
                  Kraft: Vertofix Coeur is the PUREST grade of this chemistry, 
                  containing predominantly the olfactorily preferred isomer. 
                  The "Coeur" designation = heart-cut of the distillation, 
                  removing less desirable fractions.
  Key SAR Rule:   Cedrane-skeleton + methyl ether = warm amber-wood. 
                  Dry cedar → warm cedar is accomplished by one -OCH₃.

  ARCTANDER — ODOR PROFILE
  ────────────────────────
  Character:      Warm, ambery-cedarwood, soft, balsamic, slightly creamy, 
                  precious. "If cedarwood and amber had a child." Much warmer 
                  and rounder than raw cedarwood EO.
  Tenacity:       Deep base; extremely persistent (48h+ on blotter). One of 
                  the most tenacious woody materials available. A true 
                  "last-to-leave" base note.
  Behavior:       Powerful fixative. Even small quantities anchor woody bases 
                  and provide the "warm wood" impression that lasts all day.

  CARLES — SYSTEMATIC POSITION
  ────────────────────────────
  Position:       DEEP BASE (amber-wood anchor)
  Carles Method:  The "timber anchor" — the last woody note the wearer 
                  smells at the end of the day.
  Pairing Rule:   Vertofix + ISO E Super = modern woody base (redundant but 
                  complementary — Vertofix warmer, ISO E more transparent).
                  Vertofix + cedarwood EO = amplified cedar (natural + 
                  synthetic = full spectrum).
                  Vertofix + labdanum = deep amber-wood.
                  Vertofix + patchouli = earthy-wood monolith.
                  Vertofix + musks = smooth woody-musk base.

  ROUDNITSKA — AESTHETIC ROLE
  ──────────────────────────
  Function:       "Warm timber foundation" — the base that makes everything 
                  above it feel grounded and substantial.
  Craft Note:     Essential for any woody composition that needs all-day 
                  persistence. Creates the "I can still smell it at midnight" 
                  effect.

  JELLINEK — PSYCHOLOGICAL MAP
  ────────────────────────────
  Axis Position:  Slightly NARCOTIC, slightly erogenic
  Effect:         Grounding, warm, protective, "wooden embrace," stable.
  Map Quadrant:   Center-right (warm, gently narcotic)

  PRACTICAL USAGE
  ───────────────
  Your form:      Neat (viscous liquid)
  Typical %:      3-12% of concentrate
  Max safe %:     20%
  Best with:      ISO E Super, cedarwood EO, patchouli, labdanum, musks
  Avoid:          Overuse = heavy, monotonous woody base
  Handle as:      Premium base fixative. Very tenacious. Your strongest 
                  "warm wood" anchor.


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  4. VETIVAL (VETIVERYL ACETATE)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  Your stock: Neat

  CHEMICAL IDENTITY
  ─────────────────
  IUPAC:          Mixture of acetylated vetiver sesquiterpene alcohols — 
                  primarily (1aS,3aR,5aR,6R)-6-isopropenyl-3a-methyl-
                  1a,2,3,3a,4,5,5a,6-octahydrocyclopropa[f]inden-3-yl acetate 
                  (khusimyl acetate)
  Synonyms:       Vetival, Vetiveryl acetate (IFF), Vetiverol acetate, 
                  Acetylated vetiver
  CAS:            62563-80-8
  Formula:        C₁₇H₂₆O₂ (khusimyl acetate, representative)
  MW:             262.39 g/mol
  BP:             ~280°C
  VP:             ~0.03 Pa
  CLP:            4.50
  ODT:            ~0.1 µg/L

  OPK — STRUCTURE-ACTIVITY
  ────────────────────────
  SAR Class:      Vetiver sesquiterpenoid esters — acetylated vetiver alcohols
  Olfactophore:   The vetiver skeleton (khusane, zizaane, and related 
                  polycyclic sesquiterpenes) provides the earthy-woody 
                  foundation. Acetylation of the hydroxyl smooths and 
                  sweetens the raw, dark vetiver character.
  Mechanism:      Ohloff: Natural vetiver EO contains 150+ sesquiterpenoids. 
                  The most important olfactory components are khusimol 
                  (earthy), vetiselinenol (woody), and β-vetivone (smoky). 
                  Vetival (acetylated fraction) removes the harsh, smoky 
                  aspects while preserving the refined, "clean vetiver" 
                  facets.
                  
                  Kraft: Ester formation converts the -OH to -OAc, which 
                  changes the hydrogen bonding pattern → less dark/smoky, 
                  more bright/woody. Same principle as terpineol → terpinyl 
                  acetate (softer, brighter).
  Key SAR Rule:   Vetiver sesquiterpenoid + acetate = civilized vetiver. 
                  Acetylation removes darkness, adds brightness.

  ARCTANDER — ODOR PROFILE
  ────────────────────────
  Character:      Clean, refined vetiver-woody, slightly sweet, grapefruity, 
                  earthy but WITHOUT the dark, smoky, harsh facets of raw 
                  vetiver EO. "Vetiver in a tailored suit."
  Tenacity:       Heart → deep base; excellent persistence. Slightly less 
                  tenacious than raw vetiver EO but still 24h+ on blotter.
  Behavior:       The "wearable vetiver." When raw vetiver EO is too dark 
                  or polarizing, Vetival provides the same rootsy character 
                  in a more refined, accessible form.

  CARLES — SYSTEMATIC POSITION
  ────────────────────────────
  Position:       HEART → BASE (refined woody-earthy)
  Carles Method:  The "polished earth" — clean vetiver for modern compositions.
  Pairing Rule:   Vetival + Vetiver EO = full-spectrum vetiver (clean top + 
                  dark base).
                  Vetival + ISO E Super = transparent woody earth.
                  Vetival + grapefruit FCF = the "modern vetiver" accord 
                  (Vetiver Tonka zone).
                  Vetival + cedarwood = amplified woody depth.

  ROUDNITSKA — AESTHETIC ROLE
  ──────────────────────────
  Function:       "Civilized earth" — provides vetiver's noble character without 
                  its occasional harshness. The refined brother of Vetiver EO.
  Craft Note:     Use Vetival for the body of your vetiver accord, then add 
                  touches of raw Vetiver EO for darkness and authenticity.

  JELLINEK — PSYCHOLOGICAL MAP
  ────────────────────────────
  Axis Position:  Slightly NARCOTIC, slightly stimulating
  Effect:         Grounded, composed, sophisticated, "confident calm."
  Map Quadrant:   Center (balanced between stimulating and narcotic)

  PRACTICAL USAGE
  ───────────────
  Your form:      Neat
  Typical %:      3-10% of concentrate
  Max safe %:     15%
  Best with:      Vetiver EO (duo), ISO E Super, grapefruit, cedarwood, 
                  bergamot
  Avoid:          Using WITHOUT any vetiver EO = loses authenticity
  Handle as:      Premium refined vetiver. Use as vetiver "body," add raw 
                  vetiver EO for "soul."


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  5. VETIVER ESSENTIAL OIL
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  Your stock: Neat (natural EO)

  CHEMICAL IDENTITY
  ─────────────────
  IUPAC:          NATURAL MIXTURE — Chrysopogon zizanioides root steam 
                  distillate. 150+ components. Major: khusimol (10-20%), 
                  isovalencenol, vetiselinenol, β-vetivone, α-vetivone, 
                  khusimone, vetiselinene
  Synonyms:       Vetiver oil, Vetivert, Khus oil (Indian)
  CAS:            8016-96-4
  Formula:        C₁₅H₂₄O (khusimol, representative)
  MW:             220.35 g/mol (khusimol)
  BP:             ~285°C (khusimol; distillation requires vacuum)
  VP:             ~0.01 Pa (complex mixture)
  CLP:            4.50 (khusimol)
  ODT:            ~0.01 µg/L (β-vetivone, the most potent component)

  OPK — STRUCTURE-ACTIVITY
  ────────────────────────
  SAR Class:      Polycyclic sesquiterpenoids — the most chemically complex 
                  natural EO in routine perfumery use (150+ identified 
                  compounds)
  Olfactophore:   Multiple active compounds contribute simultaneously:
                  • Khusimol → earthy, rooty (cyclopropane ring system)
                  • β-Vetivone → smoky, dark (α,β-unsaturated ketone)
                  • Vetiselinenol → woody (spiro bicyclic alcohol)
                  The combined impression = "earth itself."
  Mechanism:      Ohloff: Vetiver oil is unique in that it improves with AGE. 
                  Unlike most EOs that deteriorate via oxidation, vetiver 
                  undergoes slow rearrangement reactions that create new, 
                  more complex odorants. Old vetiver oil (5-10 years) is 
                  prized.
                  
                  Kraft: The origin DRAMATICALLY affects character:
                  • Haiti/Réunion: smoky, dark, dry, grapefruity
                  • Java: cleaner, lighter, less complex
                  • India (Khus): heavy, rooty, sweet
                  
                  No two vetiver oils smell the same — origin, distillation 
                  method, and age all matter enormously.
  Key SAR Rule:   Polycyclic sesquiterpene complexity = irreplaceable vetiver 
                  character. No single synthetic replicates this.

  ARCTANDER — ODOR PROFILE
  ────────────────────────
  Character:      Dark, earthy, smoky, woody, rooty, slightly grapefruity 
                  (depending on origin), warm, deep, "ancient earth." With 
                  age: richer, more amber, less harsh.
  Tenacity:       Deep base; one of the MOST TENACIOUS naturals in existence. 
                  72-96h on blotter. Weeks on fabric. Gets better with time.
  Behavior:       Powerful, polarizing, transformative. Even small amounts 
                  impose character. Vetiver is sometimes called "the oil of 
                  tranquility" in aromatherapy — its deep, grounding quality 
                  is pharmacologically demonstrated (anxiolytic effects in 
                  animal studies).

  CARLES — SYSTEMATIC POSITION
  ────────────────────────────
  Position:       HEART → DEEP BASE (the darkest natural base)
  Carles Method:  The "dark earth pillar" — alongside patchouli, one of 
                  perfumery's two great earthy foundations.
  Pairing Rule:   Vetiver + patchouli = the earth dyad (masculine).
                  Vetiver + grapefruit = the "modern vetiver" accord.
                  Vetiver + ISO E Super = transparent earthy-wood.
                  Vetiver + Hedione = earthy radiance (Terre d'Hermès DNA).
                  Vetiver + rose = the "vetiver-rose" niche accord.

  ROUDNITSKA — AESTHETIC ROLE
  ──────────────────────────
  Function:       "The soul of earth" — provides depth, gravitas, darkness, 
                  and connection to nature that no synthetic can replicate.
  Craft Note:     Use Vetival for the "clean" body and Vetiver EO for the 
                  "dark soul." Together they create full-spectrum vetiver.

  JELLINEK — PSYCHOLOGICAL MAP
  ────────────────────────────
  Axis Position:  NARCOTIC, slightly erogenic
  Effect:         Grounding, calming, serious, "deep meditation," masculine, 
                  protective.
  Map Quadrant:   Lower-right (deeply narcotic, grounding)

  PRACTICAL USAGE
  ───────────────
  Your form:      Neat (natural EO — often dark brown/viscous)
  Typical %:      2-8% of concentrate
  Max safe %:     15%
  Best with:      Vetival (duo), patchouli (earth), grapefruit (modern), 
                  ISO E Super (transparent), Hedione (radiant)
  Avoid:          Overdose = earthy/smoky dominance overwhelms everything else
  Handle as:      Premium natural base. Use with Vetival for full spectrum.

                  Also described as: ethylenedioxydiundecanoic acid cyclic

  OPK — STRUCTURE-ACTIVITY
  ────────────────────────
  SAR Class:      Macrocyclic musks — dilactone (diester) subclass.
                  Same broad family as Ethylene Brassylate, Exaltolide, 
                  Habanolide.
  Olfactophore:   Kraft: Macrocyclic musks require a ring of 14-18 atoms 
                  (including the heteroatoms) with at least one polar group 
                  (lactone, ester, ketone) embedded. The ring must be large 
                  enough to adopt conformations that present the polar group 
                  at the periphery for receptor binding while the hydrocarbon 
                  chain fills the hydrophobic pocket.
                  
                  Zenolide's DILACTONE structure (two ester groups in the ring) 
                  means it has TWO polar anchoring points vs one for 
                  Exaltolide or Habanolide. This gives it a CLEANER, less 
                  animalic character — more "fresh laundry" than "warm skin."
  Mechanism:      Ohloff: The macrocyclic musk class was discovered by Ružička 
                  (Nobel Prize 1939) who identified muscone (macrocyclic ketone) 
                  in natural musk. Dilactone macrocycles like Zenolide are a 
                  later development — replacing one ester bond with two makes 
                  the molecule more polar, more water-soluble, and more 
                  biodegradable than monoester macrocycles.
                  
                  Kraft: Zenolide's dual polarity creates a "cleaner" musk 
                  impression because the two ester groups provide more 
                  hydrogen bonding sites, mimicking the receptor interaction 
                  pattern of clean/fresh notes rather than warm/animalic ones.
                  
                  ENVIRONMENTAL NOTE: Macrocyclic dilactones are among the 
                  most environmentally friendly musk types — excellent 
                  biodegradability, low bioaccumulation, no endocrine 
                  disruption concerns.
  Key SAR Rule:   Macrocyclic dilactone (2 ester groups in ring) = clean, 
                  transparent musk. More esters = cleaner impression.
