  Your stock: 10% solution

  CHEMICAL IDENTITY
  ─────────────────
  IUPAC:          11-Oxahexadecanolide (11-Oxa-16-hexadecanolide) or similar — 
                  PerfumersWorld "Macrolide" is likely a specific macrocyclic 
                  lactone similar to Habanolide or Exaltolide.
  Synonyms:       Macrolide, likely referencing Cervolide or Zenolide-type
  CAS:            Depends on exact structure — likely 6707-60-4 or related
  Formula:        C₁₅H₂₈O₃ (if oxalactone type)
  MW:             ~256 g/mol
  BP:             >300°C
  VP:             <0.005 Pa
  CLP:            ~4.5
  ODT:            ~0.05 µg/L

  OPK — STRUCTURE-ACTIVITY
  ────────────────────────
  SAR Class:      Macrocyclic musks — generic macrolide category
  Olfactophore:   Large ring (14-17 atoms) containing at least one ester (lactone) 
                  linkage. The "macrolide" designation identifies it as a ring 
                  lactone musk following Ohloff's ring-size rules.
  Mechanism:      Same ring-size principle as EB, Exaltolide, Habanolide: 
                  14-17 membered rings with ester groups = musky. The specific 
                  character depends on ring size, saturation, and number of 
                  heteroatoms. This 10% form suggests it may be high-melting or 
                  viscous at room temperature.
  Key SAR Rule:   Macrocyclic lactone = musk. Compare with your other 
                  macrocyclics to understand this specific variant.

  ARCTANDER — ODOR PROFILE
  ────────────────────────
  Character:      Musky, clean, slightly powdery-warm, soft
  Tenacity:       Deep base
  Behavior:       Another voice in your macrocyclic musk chorus. Evaluate

                  specific character contribution.

  CARLES — SYSTEMATIC POSITION
  ────────────────────────────
  Position:       BASE — Fond (musky)
  Pairing Rule:   Layer with your other macrocyclics for musk complexity.

  ROUDNITSKA / JELLINEK
  ─────────────────────
  Role:           Additional musk depth. Evaluate character vs. your other musks.
  Jellinek:       CENTER → slightly NARCOTIC (warm, musky)

  PRACTICAL USAGE
  ───────────────
  Your form:      10% solution
  Typical %:      2-8% of concentrate (neat equivalent)
  Max safe %:     15%
  Best with:      Other musks (EB, Habanolide, Galaxolide), woods, florals
  Handle as:      Part of your musk palette. Do a side-by-side test strip 
                  comparison with EB, Exaltolide, and Habanolide.

  2. MAPLE LACTONE (20%)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  Your stock: 20% solution

  CHEMICAL IDENTITY
  ─────────────────
  IUPAC:          5-Ethyl-3-hydroxy-4-methyl-2(5H)-furanone
  Synonyms:       Maple Lactone, FEMA 3168, Sotolon (close structural analog)
  CAS:            698-10-2 (Sotolon: 28664-35-9)
  Formula:        C₇H₁₀O₃
  MW:             142.15 g/mol
  BP:             ~187°C
  VP:             ~2 Pa
  CLP:            0.15
  ODT:            0.00001 µg/L air (ULTRA-POTENT — among the very lowest ODTs 
                  in perfumery. Sotolon/Maple Lactone-type compounds are 
                  detectable at parts per TRILLION)

  OPK — STRUCTURE-ACTIVITY
  ────────────────────────
  SAR Class:      Hydroxyfuranones — caramellic/sugar family
  Olfactophore:   The 3-hydroxy-2(5H)-furanone ring is a cyclic α,β-unsaturated 
                  hydroxylactone. The hydroxyl adjacent to the carbonyl creates 
                  the same intense hydrogen-bonding/chelating system as ethyl 
                  maltol but in a 5-membered ring instead of 6-membered.
  Mechanism:      Kraft: Sotolon/Maple Lactone is responsible for the smell of:
                  - Maple syrup
                  - Aged rum and sherry
                  - Lovage herb
                  - Fenugreek seeds
                  - Burnt sugar
                  
                  Its ODT is so low (~10 pg/L air) that it's one of the most 
                  potent flavor/fragrance chemicals known. Like ethyl maltol, 
                  it's a "sweetness bomb" that requires EXTREME micro-dosing.
                  
                  At very, very low concentrations: maple/caramel/curry.
                  At slightly higher: burnt sugar, overwhelming.
  Key SAR Rule:   Hydroxy-furanone = extreme sweetness/caramel. Among most 
                  potent odorants known. ODT in parts per trillion.

  ARCTANDER — ODOR PROFILE
  ────────────────────────
  Character:      Sweet, maple syrup, caramellic, burnt sugar, fenugreek, 
                  nutty, warm, "pancake house"
  Tenacity:       Heart → base; persistent despite low MW
  Behavior:       ULTRA-POTENT sweetener. Even more concentrated than ethyl maltol. 
                  At your 20% dilution, a single drop in a 30mL formula may still 
                  be too much. Creates instant "maple/caramel" impression.

  CARLES — SYSTEMATIC POSITION
  ────────────────────────────
  Position:       HEART → BASE — Modifier (gourmand/sweet)
  Pairing Rule:   Maple lactone at TRACE + vanillin = "crème brûlée."
                  Maple lactone + tobacco/leather = "rum-aged leather."
                  ABSOLUTE MICRO-DOSE REQUIRED.

  ROUDNITSKA — AESTHETIC ROLE
  ──────────────────────────
  Function:       "Sucre brûlé" (burnt sugar). A modern gourmand tool.
  Craft Note:     DANGER. More potent than ethyl maltol. Start with 0.01% of 
                  concentrate and work upward in increments of 0.005%.

  JELLINEK — PSYCHOLOGICAL MAP
  ────────────────────────────
  Axis Position:  NARCOTIC, warm, erogenic
  Effect:         Comforting, edible, cozy, childlike, gourmand.
  Map Quadrant:   Lower-right (deeply narcotic, sweet)

  PRACTICAL USAGE
  ───────────────
  Your form:      20% solution
  Typical %:      0.01-0.1% of concentrate (neat equivalent)
  Max safe %:     0.5% neat
  Best with:      Vanillin, ethyl maltol, tonka, rum notes, tobacco
  Avoid:          Fresh/citrus (overpowers), any overdose
  Handle as:      EXTREME DANGER MATERIAL (olfactively). More potent than ethyl 
                  maltol. Even your 20% solution needs single-drop dosing in 
                  30mL batches. Evaluate after each addition. Cannot be undone.

  3. METHYL IONONE (Gamma)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  Your stock: neat

  CHEMICAL IDENTITY
  ─────────────────
  IUPAC:          (E)-1-(2,6,6-Trimethyl-2-cyclohexen-1-yl)-1-penten-3-one
                  (gamma-methyl ionone / Methyl Ionone G, typically sold as 
                  isomer mixture)
  Synonyms:       Methyl Ionone, Iralia, Orris Synthetic, Ionone Methyle
  CAS:            1335-46-2 (mixture); 127-42-4 (alpha); 127-43-5 (gamma)
  Formula:        C₁₄H₂₂O
  MW:             206.33 g/mol
  BP:             ~260°C
  VP:             ~0.2 Pa (25°C)
  CLP:            4.31
  ODT:            0.003 µg/L air (very potent)

  OPK — STRUCTURE-ACTIVITY
  ────────────────────────
  SAR Class:      Ionones — methylated variants (iris/orris family)
  Olfactophore:   Same ionone cyclohexenyl ring as beta ionone, BUT with an 
                  additional METHYL group on the sidechain, extending the keto 
                  system from pentenone to hexenone. This extra carbon shifts the 
                  character from "violet" to "powdery-woody-iris."
  Mechanism:      Kraft's methyl ionone SAR: Adding a methyl to the ionone 
                  sidechain does three things:
                  1. INCREASES MW and lipophilicity → better persistence
                  2. SHIFTS odor from "violet" to "powdery iris"
                  3. ADDS woody-orris character that ionones lack
                  
                  Methyl ionones are the "orris substitute" of perfumery — they 
                  give the powdery, elegant, cosmetic iris quality without using 
                  prohibitively expensive natural orris butter/alpha-irone.
                  
                  Ohloff: The methylation fills in the "woody-powdery" dimension 
                  that pure ionones miss. Alpha-irone (real orris) has its own 
                  methylation pattern that methyl ionone approximates.
  Key SAR Rule:   Ionone + methyl on sidechain = violet → iris/orris. 
                  More powdery, more persistent, more "cosmetic."

  ARCTANDER — ODOR PROFILE
  ────────────────────────
  Character:      Powdery, warm, woody-violet, iris-like, "face powder," 
                  cosmetic, soft, elegant
  Tenacity:       Heart → base; better persistence than ionones
  Behavior:       The "powdery iris" material. Found in virtually every prestige 
                  fragrance at 2-8%. Creates the "expensive cosmetic" quality 
                  associated with iris/orris without the cost.

  CARLES — SYSTEMATIC POSITION
  ────────────────────────────
  Position:       HEART — Note de coeur (iris/violet/powdery family)
  Carles Method:  The heart of the iris family. More structural than ionones.
  Pairing Rule:   Methyl ionone + allyl ionone = iris duo.
                  Methyl ionone + ISO E Super = "cashmere wood."
                  Methyl ionone + musks = prestige skin scent.

  ROUDNITSKA — AESTHETIC ROLE
  ──────────────────────────
  Function:       "Poudre d'iris" (iris powder). The elegant, cosmetic powderiness 
                  that defines luxury in perfumery.
  Craft Note:     Methyl ionone is the "luxury signal" material. Its powdery 
                  quality registers subconsciously as "expensive." Use at 3-8%.

  JELLINEK — PSYCHOLOGICAL MAP
  ────────────────────────────
  Axis Position:  CENTER → slightly NARCOTIC, cold (elegant)
  Effect:         Sophisticated, elegant, powdery, cosmetic, refined.
  Map Quadrant:   Center-lower (cold, elegant narcosis)

  PRACTICAL USAGE
  ───────────────
  Your form:      Neat
  Typical %:      2-8% of concentrate
  Max safe %:     15%
  Best with:      Allyl ionone, ISO E Super, hedione, musks, sandalwood
  Avoid:          Nothing
  Handle as:      Your "iris/orris proxy." Use at 3-8% for instant luxury 
                  powderiness. Combine with allyl ionone for complete iris.

  Olfactophore:   Methyl Pamplemousse likely contains or mimics the p-menthane-

  Handle as:      Your "grapefruit reality enhancer." Use 2-5 drops of the 10%

  6. MUSK KETONE (10%)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  Your stock: 10% solution

  CHEMICAL IDENTITY
  ─────────────────
  IUPAC:          4-tert-Butyl-2,6-dimethyl-3,5-dinitroacetophenone
  Synonyms:       Musk Ketone, MK, Ketone Musk
  CAS:            81-14-1
  Formula:        C₁₄H₁₈N₂O₅
  MW:             294.30 g/mol
  BP:             ~350°C (decomposes)
  VP:             <0.001 Pa
  CLP:            3.88
  ODT:            0.5 µg/L air (moderate)

  OPK — STRUCTURE-ACTIVITY
  ────────────────────────
  SAR Class:      Nitro musks — aromatic nitro series
  Olfactophore:   A trisubstituted benzene with TWO nitro groups (-NO₂) plus a 
                  tert-butyl group, methyl groups, and an acetyl group. The 
                  nitro groups are the key — they create a specific electronic 
                  distribution across the ring that mimics the receptor fit of 
                  macrocyclic musks despite being structurally completely different.
  Mechanism:      Ohloff: Nitro musks were the FIRST synthetic musks (Musk 
                  Baur, 1888). They represent a completely ACCIDENTAL discovery 
                  — the musky smell of nitrated aromatics has NO structural 
                  resemblance to natural muscone. Yet they activate the same 
                  receptors.
                  
                  Kraft: The nitro groups create a specific quadrupolar charge 
                  distribution and molecular flatness that happens to match the 
                  macrocyclic musk receptor profile. This "molecular mimicry" 
                  between flat nitro aromatics and large flexible macrocycles 
                  is one of the great puzzles of olfactory chemistry.
                  
                  REGULATORY: Nitro musks are RESTRICTED by many regulations 
                  and are being phased out. Musk Ketone has better regulatory 
                  status than Musk Xylene or Musk Ambrette (both phased out).
  Key SAR Rule:   Polynitro aromatic with specific substitution = musk by 
                  accident. Molecular mimicry of macrocyclic shape at receptor.

  ARCTANDER — ODOR PROFILE
  ────────────────────────
  Character:      Sweet, powdery, "classic" musk, warm, slightly animalic, 
                  softer and sweeter than polycyclic musks, "old-fashioned 
                  perfume" quality, cosmetic
  Tenacity:       Heart → base; moderate persistence
  Behavior:       Musk Ketone has a unique "sweet powdery" character that 
                  polycyclic musks (Galaxolide) and macrocyclics (EB, Habanolide) 
                  don't fully replicate. Many classic 20th-century fragrances were 
                  built on MK — replacing it changes the character. Some 
                  reformulated classics lost their identity when MK was removed.

  CARLES — SYSTEMATIC POSITION
  ────────────────────────────
  Position:       HEART → BASE (sweet musk family)
  Carles Method:  The "sweet" musk vs. Galaxolide's "clean" musk.
  Pairing Rule:   MK + Galaxolide = sweet-clean musk blend.
                  MK + florals = "classic perfume" effect.
                  MK + aldehyde C11/C12 = Chanel No. 5 territory.

  ROUDNITSKA — AESTHETIC ROLE
  ──────────────────────────
  Function:       "Musc doux" (sweet musk). The warmth and softness in classic 
                  feminine perfumery.
  Craft Note:     MK adds a "vintage" quality to formulas that modern musks 
                  can't replicate. Use for retro/classic aesthetics.

  JELLINEK — PSYCHOLOGICAL MAP
  ────────────────────────────
  Axis Position:  NARCOTIC, warm, slightly erogenic
  Effect:         Comforting, warm, nostalgic, feminine (classic), soft.
  Map Quadrant:   Lower-center (warm narcotic, sweet)

  PRACTICAL USAGE
  ───────────────
  Your form:      10% solution
  Typical %:      2-5% of concentrate (neat equivalent)
  Max safe %:     Check current IFRA limits (increasingly restricted)
  Best with:      Galaxolide, florals, aldehydes, vanillin
  Avoid:          Check regulations for intended product category
