  1. ALDEHYDE C10 (Decanal)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  Your stock: 1% dilution

  CHEMICAL IDENTITY
  ─────────────────
  IUPAC:          Decanal
  Synonyms:       Aldehyde C-10, Capric aldehyde, n-Decanal
  CAS:            112-31-2
  Formula:        C₁₀H₂₀O
  MW:             156.27 g/mol
  BP:             208°C
  VP:             2.0 Pa (25°C)
  CLP:            3.76
  ODT:            0.0004 µg/L air (extremely potent)

  OPK — STRUCTURE-ACTIVITY
  ────────────────────────
  SAR Class:      Linear aliphatic aldehydes (C8-C12 series)
  Olfactophore:   -CHO terminal aldehyde function on a C10 straight chain
  Mechanism:      The aldehyde carbonyl forms a Schiff base with olfactory receptor
                  proteins. Chain length determines character: C8=orange, C9=rose,
                  C10=waxy-orange peel, C11=clean-waxy, C12=metallic-soapy.
                  Kraft notes the "odd-even effect" — odd-carbon aldehydes are 
                  more floral, even-carbon more fatty/waxy.
  Key SAR Rule:   Every additional CH₂ in the chain shifts character from fruity→
                  waxy→soapy→metallic. Branching (see C12 MNA) softens the waxy note.

  ARCTANDER — ODOR PROFILE
  ────────────────────────
  Character:      Powerful, penetrating, sweet-waxy, orange peel, slightly fatty
  Tenacity:       Top note, moderate persistence (fades to waxy skin note)
  Behavior:       Extremely strong raw — must be diluted. In trace amounts gives
                  sparkle and "lift" to citrus and floral compositions. Overdosed
                  it becomes rancid-fatty and overwhelms everything.
  Natural occ.:   Orange peel oil, lemongrass, citronella, coriander seed

  CARLES — SYSTEMATIC POSITION
  ────────────────────────────
  Position:       TOP NOTE — Effets (effects/sparkle modifiers)
  Carles Method:  Used as a "modifier" in his top-note trials. Carles paired 
                  aldehydes with florals in his systematic grid — C10 with 
                  rose/jasmine/orange blossom to create the "aldehydic floral" 
                  archetype (Chanel No. 5 approach).
  Pairing Rule:   Always trial aldehyde modifiers at 0.1-1% before raising dose.

  ROUDNITSKA — AESTHETIC ROLE
  ──────────────────────────
  Function:       "Transparence" — Roudnitska used C10 to give compositions an
                  airy, lifted, slightly abstract quality. He described aldehydes
                  as materials that "open the window" in a composition.
  Craft Note:     Roudnitska warned that aldehydes used at >2% create an
                  "aldehydic wall" that blocks perception of the heart.
  Key Use:        Diorissimo uses a precise balance of C10/C11 for sparkle.

  JELLINEK — PSYCHOLOGICAL MAP
  ────────────────────────────
  Axis Position:  STIMULATING ← → (slightly erogenic at low doses)
  Effect:         Clean, bright, attention-getting. Creates a sense of freshness
                  and "newness." At higher levels becomes narcotic/fatty.
  Map Quadrant:   Upper-left (fresh-stimulating)

  PRACTICAL USAGE
  ───────────────
  Your form:      1% dilution — already safe to pipette
  Typical %:      0.5-3% of concentrate (at 1% dilution = 0.005-0.03% active)
  Max safe %:     5% of concentrate (at 1% dilution)
  Best with:      Bergamot, orange, rose, jasmine, musks
  Avoid:          Overdosing — goes rancid-fatty immediately
  Handle as:      Top note sparkle tool, not a volume contributor

  2. ALDEHYDE C11 (Undecanal)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  Your stock: 1% dilution

  CHEMICAL IDENTITY
  ─────────────────
  IUPAC:          Undecanal
  Synonyms:       Aldehyde C-11 undecylic, n-Undecylaldehyde, Hendecanal
  CAS:            112-44-7
  Formula:        C₁₁H₂₂O
  MW:             170.29 g/mol
  BP:             223°C
  VP:             0.67 Pa (25°C)
  CLP:            4.29
  ODT:            0.0008 µg/L air (extremely potent)

  OPK — STRUCTURE-ACTIVITY
  ────────────────────────
  SAR Class:      Linear aliphatic aldehydes (C8-C12 series)
  Olfactophore:   -CHO terminal aldehyde on C11 straight chain
  Mechanism:      One carbon longer than C10 — the odd-carbon member. Ohloff 
                  demonstrates that odd-carbon aldehydes in the C9-C11 range are
                  perceived as more "floral-clean" vs the even-carbon "waxy-fatty"
                  character. C11 sits at the transition from waxy citrus to soapy.
  Key SAR Rule:   The odd-carbon effect gives C11 a cleaner, more laundry-like,
                  less fatty character than C10 or C12.

  ARCTANDER — ODOR PROFILE
  ────────────────────────
  Character:      Clean, fresh, waxy, soapy-floral, slightly citrus, "laundry"
  Tenacity:       Top note → clean waxy drydown
  Behavior:       Cleaner and less fatty than C10. Gives a "freshly ironed linen"
                  quality. Important in aldehydic florals and fresh masculines.
  Natural occ.:   Traces in coriander, citrus oils

  CARLES — SYSTEMATIC POSITION
  ────────────────────────────
  Position:       TOP NOTE — Effets
  Carles Method:  Paired with muguet/lily-of-the-valley accords in Carles' grid.
                  C11 + hydroxycitronellal = classic clean muguet sparkle.
  Pairing Rule:   Slightly more forgiving than C10 — can be used at marginally 
                  higher levels without going fatty.

  ROUDNITSKA — AESTHETIC ROLE
  ──────────────────────────
  Function:       "Propreté" (cleanliness). Roudnitska chose C11 specifically for 
                  its clean linen quality. Used in Diorissimo alongside C10 for 
                  the fresh sparkle over the muguet heart.
  Craft Note:     "The clean aldehyde" — Roudnitska's favorite of the series for
                  fresh-floral work.

  JELLINEK — PSYCHOLOGICAL MAP
  ────────────────────────────
  Axis Position:  STIMULATING, clean
  Effect:         Creates an impression of hygiene, freshness, and order.
  Map Quadrant:   Upper-left (fresh-stimulating), slightly more centered than C10

  PRACTICAL USAGE
  ───────────────
  Your form:      1% dilution
  Typical %:      0.5-3% (at 1% dilution = 0.005-0.03% active)
  Max safe %:     5% (at 1% dilution)
  Best with:      Hydroxycitronellal, musks, citrus, floral bouquets
  Avoid:          Pairing with heavy vanillin (creates dissonance)
  Handle as:      Clean sparkle modifier, top note lift

  3. ALDEHYDE C12 MNA (2-Methylundecanal)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  Your stock: 1% dilution

  CHEMICAL IDENTITY
  ─────────────────
  IUPAC:          2-Methylundecanal
  Synonyms:       Aldehyde C-12 MNA, Methylnonyl acetaldehyde, MNA
  CAS:            110-41-8
  Formula:        C₁₂H₂₄O
  MW:             184.32 g/mol
  BP:             238°C
  VP:             0.22 Pa (25°C)
  CLP:            4.82
  ODT:            0.001 µg/L air (very potent)

  OPK — STRUCTURE-ACTIVITY
  ────────────────────────
  SAR Class:      Branched aliphatic aldehydes
  Olfactophore:   -CHO with α-methyl branch on C11 backbone
  Mechanism:      Kraft's key insight: branching at the α-carbon fundamentally 
                  changes the odor. The methyl group at C2 shields the aldehyde 
                  carbonyl from rapid oxidation and shifts the odor from 
                  waxy-fatty (straight C12/lauraldehyde) to metallic-amber-clean.
                  This is the "Chanel No. 5 aldehyde" — the branching creates a 
                  persistent metallic floral note that straight aldehydes lack.
  Key SAR Rule:   α-Branching = metallic/amber shift + increased persistence.
                  This is one of the most important SAR lessons in aldehydes.

  ARCTANDER — ODOR PROFILE
  ────────────────────────
  Character:      Powerful, metallic, warm, amber-like, slightly floral, soapy
  Tenacity:       Top → long heart; much more persistent than straight-chain C12
  Behavior:       Less fatty than straight aldehydes. Has a metallic "ring" that is
                  both cutting and warm. The workhorse aldehyde for Chanel-type
                  compositions. More forgiving than C10/C11 in higher doses.
  Natural occ.:   Not significant in nature — this is primarily synthetic

  CARLES — SYSTEMATIC POSITION
  ────────────────────────────
  Position:       TOP NOTE → bridges into HEART (unusually persistent for top note)
  Carles Method:  Carles' "aldehydic" trial row — C12 MNA is the anchor aldehyde
                  because it has the most body and persistence of the series.
                  In his method: trial C12 MNA with every base combination.
  Pairing Rule:   Works with nearly anything. The universal aldehyde.

  ROUDNITSKA — AESTHETIC ROLE
  ──────────────────────────
  Function:       "Architecture" — Roudnitska saw C12 MNA as a structural material,
                  not just an effect. It provides metallic scaffolding that lifts
                  and separates other notes. Creates "volume" in a composition.
  Craft Note:     Can be used at 2-3x the dose of C10/C11 without going rancid.

  JELLINEK — PSYCHOLOGICAL MAP
  ────────────────────────────
  Axis Position:  STIMULATING → NARCOTIC transition
  Effect:         Creates an impression of luxury, warmth, metallic shimmer.
                  Associated with classic haute couture.
  Map Quadrant:   Upper-center (stimulating to narcotic, warm)

  PRACTICAL USAGE
  ───────────────
  Your form:      1% dilution
  Typical %:      1-5% (at 1% dilution = 0.01-0.05% active)
  Max safe %:     8% (at 1% dilution)
  Best with:      Rose, jasmine, musks, powdery accords, amber
  Avoid:          Pure aquatic compositions (metallic note clashes with marine)
  Handle as:      The most versatile aldehyde. Your go-to for aldehydic sparkle.

  4. ALLYL IONONE (Alpha-Isomethyl Ionone)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  Your stock: neat

  CHEMICAL IDENTITY
  ─────────────────
  IUPAC:          3-Methyl-4-(2,6,6-trimethyl-2-cyclohexen-1-yl)-3-buten-2-one
  Synonyms:       α-Isomethyl ionone, Allyl ionone, Allyl α-ionone
  CAS:            79-69-6
  Formula:        C₁₄H₂₂O
  MW:             206.33 g/mol
  BP:             262°C (est.)
  VP:             0.12 Pa (25°C, est.)
  CLP:            4.12
  ODT:            0.003 µg/L air

  OPK — STRUCTURE-ACTIVITY
  ────────────────────────
  SAR Class:      Ionones — cyclic terpenoid ketones (violet/iris family)
  Olfactophore:   Cyclohexenone ring with gem-dimethyl + unsaturated ketone sidechain.
                  The allyl (3-methyl) substitution on the sidechain differentiates 
                  this from alpha/beta ionone.
  Mechanism:      Ohloff's ionone SAR: The six-membered ring with gem-dimethyl is 
                  essential for the violet/iris character. The position and geometry 
                  of the double bond determines whether the note is more "violet" 
                  (beta-ionone) or "woody-warm" (alpha-ionone). Allyl ionone adds 
                  a methyl branch that creates a WARMER, more WOODY-POWDERY character 
                  than either parent ionone. Kraft classifies it as the "dry iris/woody"
                  member of the ionone family.
  Key SAR Rule:   Allyl substitution = increased woodiness, decreased fruitiness.
                  Makes it a bridge between ionones (floral) and woody materials.

  ARCTANDER — ODOR PROFILE
  ────────────────────────
  Character:      Warm, woody-violet, powdery, slightly fruity-dry, iris-like
  Tenacity:       Heart → base; excellent persistence (much longer than α/β ionone)
  Behavior:       A workhorse iris material. Less "violet" than beta ionone, more 
                  "woody-powdery." Very good diffusion. Blends seamlessly with 
                  everything — the "universal ionone."
  Natural occ.:   Not found in nature — fully synthetic

  CARLES — SYSTEMATIC POSITION
  ────────────────────────────
  Position:       HEART — Fond (body/foundation)
  Carles Method:  Core material in Carles' violet/iris trials. Used as the bulk 
                  ionone when building powdery hearts because it's more stable and 
                  persistent than alpha or beta ionone alone.
  Pairing Rule:   Pair with alpha irone for iris depth, with musks for powdery skin.

  ROUDNITSKA — AESTHETIC ROLE
  ──────────────────────────
  Function:       "Volume filler" — Roudnitska used allyl ionone to create body and 
                  powdery volume in the heart. Not a characterizing note, but an 
                  essential space-filler that gives compositions depth.
  Craft Note:     Can be used at high percentages (5-15%) without distortion.

  JELLINEK — PSYCHOLOGICAL MAP
  ────────────────────────────
  Axis Position:  Slightly NARCOTIC, warm
  Effect:         Comforting, powdery, intimate. Creates a sense of softness.
  Map Quadrant:   Center-right (warm, slightly narcotic)

  PRACTICAL USAGE
  ───────────────
  Your form:      Neat
  Typical %:      3-10% of concentrate
  Max safe %:     15% (very safe material)
  Best with:      Other ionones, iris materials, musks, coumarin, hedione
  Avoid:          Nothing specific — blends well with everything
  Handle as:      Your bulk iris/powdery material. High-volume, safe, reliable.

  5. ALPHA IONONE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  Your stock: neat

  CHEMICAL IDENTITY
  ─────────────────
  IUPAC:          (E)-4-(2,6,6-Trimethyl-2-cyclohexen-1-yl)-3-buten-2-one
  Synonyms:       α-Ionone, Alpha ionone
  CAS:            127-41-3
  Formula:        C₁₃H₂₀O
  MW:             192.30 g/mol
  BP:             228°C
  VP:             1.35 Pa (25°C)
  CLP:            3.58
  ODT:            0.0004 µg/L air (extremely potent — one of the most odorous)

  OPK — STRUCTURE-ACTIVITY
  ────────────────────────
  SAR Class:      Ionones — cyclic terpenoid ketones
  Olfactophore:   Cyclohexenone ring with gem-dimethyl. The ALPHA isomer has the 
                  C=C double bond in the 2,3-position of the ring (between C2 and C3).
  Mechanism:      Ohloff's foundational ionone work: α-ionone has the double bond 
                  at a different position than β-ionone, resulting in a MORE FRUITY, 
                  LESS VIOLET character. The ring double bond position determines 
                  receptor binding geometry. Alpha ionone binds differently to the 
                  OR5A1 receptor than beta ionone (Kraft 2004), producing a warm 
                  woody-fruity note rather than the "pure violet" of beta.
  Key SAR Rule:   α-ionone = warm, fruity, woody-violet, less "true violet" than β.
                  The isomeric difference is one of the most important SAR lessons.

  ARCTANDER — ODOR PROFILE
  ────────────────────────
  Character:      Warm, woody, fruity-violet, slightly cedar-like, less floral than β
  Tenacity:       Top-heart; moderate persistence, good diffusion
  Behavior:       More accessible and "warmer" than beta ionone. Less powerfully 
                  violet but more blendable. Gives warm woody-fruity tonality.
                  Important in men's fragrances for warmth without sweetness.
  Natural occ.:   Formed by degradation of carotenoids; found in osmanthus,
                  boronia, violet leaf absolute, carrots

  CARLES — SYSTEMATIC POSITION
  ────────────────────────────
  Position:       HEART — Modifier
  Carles Method:  Used in Carles' fruity-floral trials. Alpha ionone adds warmth 
                  to a rose or jasmine heart without making it powdery (that's 
                  beta ionone's job). In his grid: α-ionone with bergamot = warm 
                  fruity top; α-ionone with sandalwood = warm woody base.
  Pairing Rule:   Use for warmth. Beta ionone for violet. Both together for full iris.

  ROUDNITSKA — AESTHETIC ROLE
  ──────────────────────────
  Function:       "Chaleur" (warmth). Roudnitska valued alpha ionone as a warm-fruity 
                  connector — it bridges citrus top and floral heart without adding 
                  the overt "powdery" character of beta ionone.
  Craft Note:     "Where beta ionone is the violet, alpha ionone is the warmth 
                  around the violet."

  JELLINEK — PSYCHOLOGICAL MAP
  ────────────────────────────
  Axis Position:  Center — balances stimulating and narcotic
  Effect:         Warm, comforting but not drowsy. A "neutral warm."
  Map Quadrant:   Center (balanced warmth)

  PRACTICAL USAGE
  ───────────────
  Your form:      Neat
  Typical %:      2-8% of concentrate
  Max safe %:     12%
  Best with:      Beta ionone, bergamot, rose, jasmine, woods
  Avoid:          Nothing specific — very safe blender
  Handle as:      Warm fruity-woody ionone. Use for warmth, not for powdery iris.


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  6. ALPHA IRONE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  Your stock: 10% dilution

  CHEMICAL IDENTITY
  ─────────────────
  IUPAC:          (6R)-6-Methyl-2-(2,6,6-trimethyl-2-cyclohexen-1-yl)-
                  2-hepten-4-one (mixture of isomers)
  Synonyms:       α-Irone, cis-α-Irone, Alpha isomethyl ionone (historic, imprecise)
  CAS:            79-69-6 (tech. grade); 28555-77-5 (cis-α-irone)
  Formula:        C₁₄H₂₂O
  MW:             206.33 g/mol
  BP:             268°C (est.)
  VP:             0.07 Pa (25°C, est.)
  CLP:            4.50
  ODT:            0.0001 µg/L air (among the most odorous substances known)

  OPK — STRUCTURE-ACTIVITY
  ────────────────────────
  SAR Class:      Irones — methylated ionone homologues (the "orris" family)
  Olfactophore:   Cyclohexenone ring with gem-dimethyl AND an additional methyl 
                  group at C6. This extra methyl is the critical structural 
                  difference between ionones and irones.
  Mechanism:      Ohloff's landmark finding: the single extra methyl at C6 transforms 
                  ionone's "violet" into irone's "orris butter." This is one of the 
                  most celebrated SAR demonstrations in perfumery chemistry. The C6 
                  methyl creates an asymmetric center, making chirality important:
                  - cis-α-Irone: the most valuable isomer, buttery-powdery-violet
                  - trans-α-Irone: less fine, more woody
                  The absolute configuration at C6 is (R) in the natural isomer.
                  Kraft refined the mechanism: the C6 methyl forces the sidechain 
                  into a specific conformation that matches the "iris receptor" 
                  binding pocket with extreme affinity → ODT of 0.1 ng/L.
  Key SAR Rule:   ONE methyl group = violet (ionone) → orris butter (irone).
                  This is the most cost-efficient SAR lesson: the most expensive 
                  natural raw material (orris butter, €5000/kg) differs from 
                  cheap ionone by exactly one CH₃ group.

  ARCTANDER — ODOR PROFILE
  ────────────────────────
  Character:      Rich, buttery, powdery-violet, suede-like, warm, precious
  Tenacity:       Heart → deep base; exceptional longevity
  Behavior:       The reference material for "true iris." Nothing else fully

  8. BETA IONONE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  Your stock: neat

  CHEMICAL IDENTITY
  ─────────────────
  IUPAC:          (E)-4-(2,6,6-Trimethyl-1-cyclohexen-1-yl)-3-buten-2-one
  Synonyms:       β-Ionone, Beta ionone
  CAS:            14901-07-6
  Formula:        C₁₃H₂₀O
  MW:             192.30 g/mol
  BP:             237°C
  VP:             0.85 Pa (25°C)
  CLP:            3.84
  ODT:            0.000007 µg/L air (7 picograms — one of the lowest ODTs known!)

  OPK — STRUCTURE-ACTIVITY
  ────────────────────────
  SAR Class:      Ionones — cyclic terpenoid ketones (the violet-definitive member)
  Olfactophore:   Cyclohexenone ring with gem-dimethyl group. The BETA isomer has 
                  the C=C double bond in the 1,2-position of the ring — shifted 
                  one position from alpha ionone's 2,3-position.
  Mechanism:      Ohloff's ionone masterclass: β-ionone is THE violet odorant. The 
                  specific position of the endocyclic double bond matches the OR5A1 
                  olfactory receptor with extraordinary precision — this is why its 
                  ODT is among the lowest known for any organic compound (~7 pg/L).
                  
                  Kraft's receptor model: The 1,2-double bond creates a planar 
                  region on the ring that fits into a narrow hydrophobic pocket 
                  of the receptor. The α-ionone double bond position (2,3) doesn't 
                  achieve this fit → 50× higher ODT, different odor.
                  
                  GENETIC NOTE: ~30% of humans have a mutation in OR5A1 that makes 
                  them "β-ionone blind" — they cannot smell it at all. This is one 
                  of the most common specific anosmias.
  Key SAR Rule:   β-ionone = TRUE VIOLET. α-ionone = warm-fruity-woody. 
                  One double bond position = completely different smell and 
                  receptor binding. ODT difference: 50× between isomers.

  ARCTANDER — ODOR PROFILE
  ────────────────────────
  Character:      Violet, woody-floral, dry, slightly fruity, sweet, powdery
  Tenacity:       Heart note — moderate persistence; the odor "blooms" over time 
                  as more molecules reach threshold. Ionones are famous for 
                  "olfactory fatigue" — you stop smelling them, step away, 
                  and then they hit you again.
  Behavior:       The purest violet note available. More "violet" and less "warm" 
                  than alpha ionone. At neat concentration it can cause rapid 
                  olfactory fatigue (you go nose-blind). The phenomenon is called 
                  "ionone adaptation" — the most dramatic adaptation in perfumery.
  Natural occ.:   Dominant odorant of violet flowers, osmanthus, boronia; formed 
                  by carotenoid degradation in many fruits and vegetables

  CARLES — SYSTEMATIC POSITION
  ────────────────────────────
  Position:       HEART — Characterizing (violet/iris family)
  Carles Method:  The "violet" characterizing note. Carles placed beta ionone as 
                  the defining material for the violet family. In his grid: 
                  β-ionone in the heart with every possible base combination.
  Pairing Rule:   β-ionone for violet character. α-ionone for warmth. 
                  Allyl ionone for bulk. Alpha irone for luxury.
                  The ionone quartet covers all of iris/violet.

  ROUDNITSKA — AESTHETIC ROLE
  ──────────────────────────
  Function:       "Couleur violette" (violet color). Roudnitska described ionones 
                  in visual terms — beta ionone "paints violet" on the composition.
                  He used it specifically when he wanted the listener to perceive 
                  something as "violet-colored."
  Craft Note:     Olfactory fatigue warning: "The perfumer who works with ionones 
                  all day is blind to them by afternoon." Make ionone decisions 
                  in the morning, on a fresh nose.

  JELLINEK — PSYCHOLOGICAL MAP
  ────────────────────────────
  Axis Position:  Center → slightly NARCOTIC
  Effect:         Dreamy, nostalgic, romantic. Strong association with old-fashioned 
                  elegance and grandmother's perfume (Parma violets).
  Map Quadrant:   Center to lower-center (balanced to slightly narcotic)

  PRACTICAL USAGE
  ───────────────
  Your form:      Neat
  Typical %:      1-5% of concentrate
  Max safe %:     10%
  Best with:      Other ionones, iris materials, rose, coumarin, musks
  Avoid:          Overdosing → olfactory fatigue makes you think it's gone (it's not)
  Handle as:      The violet paintbrush. Small amounts for violet character. 
                  If you can't smell it anymore, you've adapted — walk away 10 min.
                  ALWAYS evaluate beta ionone compositions on separate smelling 
                  strips, not from the bottle.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  Your stock: 20% dilution

  CHEMICAL IDENTITY
  ─────────────────
  IUPAC:          6,7-Dihydro-1,1,2,3,3-pentamethyl-4(5H)-indanone
  Synonyms:       Cashmeran (IFF), Cashmere Ketone
  CAS:            33704-61-9
  Formula:        C₁₄H₂₂O
  MW:             206.33 g/mol
  BP:             ~280°C
  VP:             ~0.05 Pa
  CLP:            4.23
  ODT:            0.08 µg/L air (moderate — needs volume to project)

  OPK — STRUCTURE-ACTIVITY
  ────────────────────────
  SAR Class:      Polycyclic musks / woody-ambery — indanone family
  Olfactophore:   The pentamethyl-indanone scaffold — a fused bicyclic ketone 
                  system with extreme methyl substitution. The ketone carbonyl 
                  provides warmth; the methyls provide lipophilicity and diffusion.
  Mechanism:      Kraft's Cashmeran analysis: Cashmeran defies simple classification. 
                  It is simultaneously woody, musky, spicy, and warm — an "olfactory 
                  chimera." The indanone ring provides a warm-sweet character similar 
                  to musks, while the extreme methylation creates a "cashmere" 
                  softness that no other molecule achieves.
                  
                  Ohloff places it at the intersection of the "musk," "woody," and 
                  "amber" families — one of the few molecules that bridges all three.
                  
                  Key Kraft observation: Cashmeran has a "chameleon" effect — it 
                  takes on the character of whatever surrounds it. In a woody context 
                  it smells woody. In a musky context it smells musky. It adapts.
  Key SAR Rule:   Indanone + pentamethyl = "cashmere." A unique olfactory space 
                  between musk, amber, and wood.

  ARCTANDER — ODOR PROFILE
  ────────────────────────
  Character:      Warm, musky-woody, spicy, "cashmere," slightly fruity, smooth
  Tenacity:       Base note — excellent persistence, moderate diffusion
  Behavior:       The "finishing touch" material. Cashmeran makes everything 
                  it touches feel soft, warm, and expensive. It doesn't draw 
                  attention to itself — it makes OTHER materials sound better.
                  Widely used in niche perfumery as a "luxury signal."

  CARLES — SYSTEMATIC POSITION
  ────────────────────────────
  Position:       BASE — Fond/Modifier (versatile base enrichment)
  Carles Method:  Sits between the woody and musky slots. Use in both.
  Pairing Rule:   Cashmeran + musks = warm cashmere musk.
                  Cashmeran + iris = soft powdery luxury.
                  Cashmeran + ambrox = modern warmth.

  ROUDNITSKA — AESTHETIC ROLE
  ──────────────────────────
  Function:       "Cachemire" (cashmere). The material that makes a composition 
                  feel like a cashmere sweater feels on skin. Roudnitska would 
                  have recognized this as a "noble support" material.
  Craft Note:     Never the star. Always the supporting actor that wins the award.

  JELLINEK — PSYCHOLOGICAL MAP
  ────────────────────────────
  Axis Position:  NARCOTIC, warm, comforting
  Effect:         Cozy, luxurious, intimate. Creates a "wrapped in warmth" 
                  sensation.
  Map Quadrant:   Lower-center (warm, narcotic, comforting)

  PRACTICAL USAGE
  ───────────────
  Your form:      20% dilution
  Typical %:      3-8% of concentrate (= 0.6-1.6% active)
  Max safe %:     12% (= 2.4% active)
  Best with:      Musks, iris, ambrox, woods, pepper, coumarin
  Avoid:          Nothing — universal enhancer
  Handle as:      Your "cashmere blanket." Add to almost everything at 2-5%.

  6. CEDRAMBER
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  Your stock: neat

  CHEMICAL IDENTITY
  ─────────────────
  IUPAC:          8-Methoxy-2,6,6,8-tetramethyloctahydro-2H-chromen 
                  (or related acetalized cedrene derivative — several variants exist)
  Synonyms:       Cedramber (Firmenich), sometimes confused with Cedamber
  CAS:            67874-81-1 (one common variant)
  Formula:        C₁₃H₂₄O₂ (est.)
  MW:             ~212.33 g/mol
  BP:             ~270°C
  VP:             ~0.03 Pa
  CLP:            ~4.1
  ODT:            ~0.02 µg/L air

  OPK — STRUCTURE-ACTIVITY
  ────────────────────────
  SAR Class:      Woody odorants — cedrene/chroman family
  Olfactophore:   The chroman (benzopyran) skeleton with gem-dimethyl substitution 
                  provides the woody character. The methoxy group adds warmth.
  Mechanism:      Kraft's woody SAR: Cedramber belongs to the "warm cedar" 
                  subclass — molecules that provide the heartwood character of 
                  cedar (warm, rich, slightly sweet) rather than the "pencil 
                  shaving" character (dry, sharp) of cedrene oxides.
                  
                  The gem-dimethyl pattern on the chroman ring is essential — 
                  removing either methyl destroys the woody character (Ohloff's 
                  "dimethyl rule" for woody molecules).
  Key SAR Rule:   gem-Dimethyl + chroman oxygen = warm woody-amber.
                  Compare: Iso E Super (no oxygen on ring) = drier, more abstract.

  ARCTANDER — ODOR PROFILE
  ────────────────────────
  Character:      Rich, warm cedar, slightly ambery, smooth, persistent, 
                  more "luxury cedar" than pencil-shaving cedar
  Tenacity:       Base note — very good persistence
  Behavior:       Your premium cedar material. Warmer and richer than cedarwood EO.
                  Blends beautifully with Iso E Super to create a multifaceted 
                  woody base. Essential for masculine and unisex compositions.

  CARLES — SYSTEMATIC POSITION
  ────────────────────────────
  Position:       BASE — Fond (woody foundation, cedar family)
  Carles Method:  Cedar slot in base grid. Cedramber is the synthetic upgrade 
                  over natural cedarwood for persistence and consistency.
  Pairing Rule:   Cedramber + Iso E Super = modern woody base.
                  Cedramber + vetiver + Vertofix = complete wood section.

  ROUDNITSKA — AESTHETIC ROLE
  ──────────────────────────
  Function:       "Bois précieux" (precious wood). The cedar that smells like 
                  a cedar chest in a luxury home, not a construction site.
  Craft Note:     Use alongside cedarwood EO: EO for natural texture, 
                  Cedramber for warmth and persistence.

  JELLINEK — PSYCHOLOGICAL MAP
  ────────────────────────────
  Axis Position:  Center-right (warm, slightly narcotic)
  Effect:         Masculine, grounding, reliable, warm.
  Map Quadrant:   Center-right (warm, woody confidence)

  PRACTICAL USAGE
  ───────────────
  Your form:      Neat
  Typical %:      3-10% of concentrate
  Max safe %:     15%
  Best with:      Iso E Super, cedarwood EO, vetiver, musks, ambrox
  Avoid:          Stacking excessively with Cedamber (partially redundant)
  Handle as:      Premium warm cedar. Your go-to cedar base material.

  7. CEDARWOOD EO
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  Your stock: neat

  CHEMICAL IDENTITY
  ─────────────────
  IUPAC:          Juniperus virginiana or Cedrus atlantica EO (specify type).
                  Virginia cedar: α-Cedrene (25-35%), Thujopsene, Cedrol (25-35%)
                  Atlas cedar: β-Himachalene, α-Himachalene, Atlantone
  Synonyms:       Cedarwood Oil, Cedar Texas, Cedar Virginia, Cedar Atlas
  CAS:            8000-27-9 (Virginia); 8023-85-6 (Atlas)
  Formula:        Cedrol C₁₅H₂₆O MW 222.37 (dominant in Virginia)
  MW:             Sesquiterpene-rich (200-230 range)
  BP:             N/A (complex mixture, ~250-275°C)
  VP:             Low (sesquiterpene-heavy = less volatile than citrus)
  CLP:            ~4.5 (cedrol)
  ODT:            ~0.02 µg/L air (cedrol)

  OPK — STRUCTURE-ACTIVITY
  ────────────────────────
  SAR Class:      Woody naturals — sesquiterpene/sesquiterpene alcohol complex
  Olfactophore:   Cedrol: A tricyclic sesquiterpene alcohol. The -OH group on the 
                  polycyclic framework provides the "pencil shaving" cedar character.
                  α-Cedrene: The unsaturated hydrocarbon without -OH provides a 
                  drier, more camphoraceous woody note.
  Mechanism:      Ohloff's cedar analysis: The "cedar" smell is created by the 
                  combination of cedrol (creamy-woody) + cedrene (dry-woody) + 
                  thujopsene (slightly earthy). The ratio between these determines 
                  whether the oil smells more "pencil" (high cedrol) or more 
                  "forest" (high cedrene).
                  
                  Virginia vs Atlas: Virginia = dryer, pencil-shaving. 
                  Atlas = creamier, more amber-woody (himachalene-rich).
  Key SAR Rule:   Tricyclic sesquiterpene alcohol = cedar character.
                  Virginia = classic dry cedar. Atlas = creamy cedar.

  ARCTANDER — ODOR PROFILE
  ────────────────────────
  Character:      Dry, woody, pencil-shaving, slightly camphoraceous, warm
  Tenacity:       Heart → base; good persistence
  Behavior:       The "honest wood." No abstraction, no transparency — pure 
                  recognizable cedar. Provides the "natural wood" foundation 
                  that synthetics like Iso E Super can't fully replace. 
                  Every woody composition benefits from some natural cedarwood 
                  for authenticity.

  CARLES — SYSTEMATIC POSITION
  ────────────────────────────
  Position:       BASE — Fond (natural woody foundation)
  Carles Method:  The natural wood reference in Carles' base grid.
  Pairing Rule:   Cedarwood EO (natural texture) + Cedramber (warmth) + 
                  Iso E Super (abstract aura) = complete cedar trilogy.

  ROUDNITSKA — AESTHETIC ROLE
  ──────────────────────────
  Function:       "Authenticité" (authenticity). Roudnitska valued natural 
                  materials for the micro-complexity they bring — the hundreds 
                  of trace compounds that make a natural oil smell "alive."
  Craft Note:     Use cedarwood EO at 2-5% even in synthetic-heavy formulas 
                  to add "soul" and natural texture.

  JELLINEK — PSYCHOLOGICAL MAP
  ────────────────────────────
  Axis Position:  Center (balanced, grounding)
  Effect:         Reliable, honest, masculine, calming, contemplative.
  Map Quadrant:   Center (balanced warm-cool, balanced stim-narcotic)

  PRACTICAL USAGE
  ───────────────
  Your form:      Neat
  Typical %:      2-8% of concentrate
  Max safe %:     15%
  Best with:      Cedramber, Iso E Super, vetiver, bergamot, musks
  Avoid:          Nothing — universal woody natural
  Handle as:      Natural cedar texture. Use alongside synthetics for authenticity.

  8. cis-3-HEXENOL
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  Your stock: neat

  CHEMICAL IDENTITY
  ─────────────────
  IUPAC:          (Z)-Hex-3-en-1-ol
  Synonyms:       cis-3-Hexen-1-ol, Leaf Alcohol, Green Alcohol
  CAS:            928-96-1
  Formula:        C₆H₁₂O
  MW:             100.16 g/mol
  BP:             156°C
  VP:             ~100 Pa (25°C — VERY volatile!)
  CLP:            1.61 (polar — hydrophilic)
  ODT:            0.07 µg/L air (moderate)

  OPK — STRUCTURE-ACTIVITY
  ────────────────────────
  SAR Class:      Green leaf volatiles (GLVs) — C6 aldehydes and alcohols
  Olfactophore:   The cis-3-double bond + terminal primary alcohol. Ohloff's 
                  "green note rule": Green character requires a C6 chain with 
                  an unsaturation at C3 and an oxygen function at C1 or C6.
  Mechanism:      This is the actual molecule released when you tear a leaf. 
                  In plants, it's formed by the lipoxygenase pathway: linolenic 
                  acid → 13-HPOT → cis-3-hexenal → cis-3-hexenol (via alcohol 
                  dehydrogenase). Ohloff: "The smell of green is literally the 
                  smell of plant tissue destruction."
                  
                  Kraft's geometry note: The CIS double bond is essential. 
                  Trans-3-hexenol smells completely different (more fatty, less 
                  green). This is one of the cleanest examples of geometric 
                  isomerism affecting odor.
  Key SAR Rule:   CIS-3 double bond on C6 alcohol = "fresh green leaf."
                  TRANS = fatty. Only ONE double bond position/geometry works.

  ARCTANDER — ODOR PROFILE
  ────────────────────────
  Character:      Intensely green, fresh-cut grass, leafy, raw, slightly 
                  harsh-unripe, watery-green
  Tenacity:       Top note — flashes off very quickly (minutes, not hours)
  Behavior:       The most "natural green" smell available. Creates an instant 
                  impression of being outdoors. Extremely volatile — the first 
                  thing you smell and the first thing gone. Must be supported 
                  by longer-lasting greens (Verdox, Galbanum) for persistence.
  Natural occ.:   Every green plant — the universal "damage signal" molecule

  CARLES — SYSTEMATIC POSITION
  ────────────────────────────
  Position:       TOP — Effets (green effect, fleeting)
  Carles Method:  Green modifier position. Use as a "flash green" in the first 
                  seconds of a composition.
  Pairing Rule:   cis-3-Hexenol (flash) + Verdox (sustained) + galbanum (deep) 
                  = complete green gradient from seconds to hours.

  ROUDNITSKA — AESTHETIC ROLE
  ──────────────────────────
  Function:       "Verdure instantanée" (instant greenery). Roudnitska used 
                  natural materials rich in C6 alcohols (galbanum, violet leaf) 
                  for this effect. cis-3-Hexenol is the isolated pure molecule.
  Craft Note:     Evaluate on strips only — too volatile to smell from bottle.

  JELLINEK — PSYCHOLOGICAL MAP
  ────────────────────────────
  Axis Position:  STIMULATING, cold, anti-erogenic
  Effect:         Sharp, alert, outdoors, "alive." Creates a sense of immediacy.
  Map Quadrant:   Upper-left (cold-fresh-stimulating)

  PRACTICAL USAGE
  ───────────────
  Your form:      Neat — very volatile, handle quickly
  Typical %:      0.5-2% of concentrate
  Max safe %:     4% (harsh/grassy if overdosed)
  Best with:      Verdox, galbanum, bergamot, hedione, violet leaf notes
  Avoid:          Heavy orientals (green + sweet = discord)
  Handle as:      Flash green. Use sparingly. Combine with longer-lasting greens.

  9. CITRAL
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  Your stock: neat

  CHEMICAL IDENTITY
  ─────────────────
  IUPAC:          3,7-Dimethyl-2,6-octadienal (mixture of E and Z isomers)
                  E-isomer = Geranial (citral A), Z-isomer = Neral (citral B)
  Synonyms:       Citral, Geranial + Neral, Lemonal
  CAS:            5392-40-5
  Formula:        C₁₀H₁₆O
  MW:             152.23 g/mol
  BP:             229°C
  VP:             ~5 Pa (25°C — quite volatile)
  CLP:            3.17
  ODT:            0.003 µg/L air (potent)

  OPK — STRUCTURE-ACTIVITY
  ────────────────────────
  SAR Class:      Monoterpenoid aldehydes — citral/citronellal group
  Olfactophore:   The α,β-unsaturated aldehyde (CH=CH-CHO) is the olfactophore.
                  The conjugated system between the double bond and the aldehyde 
                  creates the intense lemon character.
  Mechanism:      Ohloff's citrus SAR: Citral is the "pure lemon" molecule — it 
                  defines what "lemon" smells like. The E-isomer (geranial) is 
                  more potent and "sharper," the Z-isomer (neral) is softer. 
                  Commercial citral is ~60:40 E:Z.
                  
                  Kraft warns: Citral is CHEMICALLY REACTIVE. The α,β-unsaturated 
                  aldehyde undergoes:
                  1. Schiff base formation with amines
                  2. Aldol condensation in base conditions
                  3. Oxidation to geranic acid (unpleasant)
                  4. Cannizzaro reaction with strong bases
                  This means citral CHANGES over time in formulas. Plan for this.
  Key SAR Rule:   α,β-Unsaturated aldehyde = intense citrus + chemical reactivity.
                  Citral is the most reactive material in your collection.

  ARCTANDER — ODOR PROFILE
  ────────────────────────
  Character:      Intense, pure lemon, sharp, fresh, slightly herbaceous, bright
  Tenacity:       Top note — relatively short-lived (2-3 hours on strip)
  Behavior:       The purest lemon note available. More "lemon" than actual lemon 
                  EO (which contains many other terpenes). Excellent for sharp 
                  citrus effects but limited by its reactivity. Will form Schiff 
                  bases with methyl anthranilate (creating a different smell), and 
                  can sensitize skin (IFRA restricted at high levels).
  Natural occ.:   Lemongrass (70-85%), lemon verbena, lemon myrtle, citrus peels

  CARLES — SYSTEMATIC POSITION
  ────────────────────────────
  Position:       TOP — Note de tête (citrus/lemon)
  Carles Method:  Lemon position in citrus row. Pure citral is sharper and more 
                  defined than bergamot's complex citrus.
  Pairing Rule:   Citral + D-limonene = natural lemon reconstruction.
                  Citral + linalool = lemon-floral bridge.

  ROUDNITSKA — AESTHETIC ROLE
  ──────────────────────────
  Function:       "Fraîcheur citronnée" (lemony freshness). Direct, honest, 
                  uncomplicated lemon. Use when you want clear citrus without 
                  bergamot's complexity.
  Craft Note:     Reactivity is the main concern. Test stability at 1 week.

  JELLINEK — PSYCHOLOGICAL MAP
  ────────────────────────────
  Axis Position:  STIMULATING, cold, anti-erogenic
  Effect:         Energizing, clean, hygienic, sharp, clear-minded.
  Map Quadrant:   Upper-left (cold, stimulating)

  PRACTICAL USAGE
  ───────────────
  Your form:      Neat — REACTIVE, monitor formulas for changes over time
  Typical %:      1-5% of concentrate
  Max safe %:     5% (IFRA skin sensitization concern at higher levels)
  Best with:      D-Limonene, linalool, bergamot, verbena accords
  Avoid:          Formulas with methyl anthranilate or strong amines (Schiff 
                  base formation). Long aging (degrades).
  Handle as:      Pure lemon. Use carefully due to reactivity. Test stability.

  10. CITRONELLAL
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  Your stock: neat

  CHEMICAL IDENTITY
  ─────────────────
  IUPAC:          3,7-Dimethyloct-6-enal
  Synonyms:       Citronellal, Rhodinal (natural, from citronella)
  CAS:            106-23-0
  Formula:        C₁₀H₁₈O
  MW:             154.25 g/mol
  BP:             207°C
  VP:             ~8 Pa (25°C)
  CLP:            3.30
  ODT:            0.005 µg/L air (potent)

  OPK — STRUCTURE-ACTIVITY
  ────────────────────────
  SAR Class:      Monoterpenoid aldehydes — non-conjugated type
  Olfactophore:   Terminal aldehyde on a branched monoterpenoid chain. Unlike 
                  citral, citronellal's aldehyde is NOT conjugated with a double 
                  bond → much less reactive and less "sharp."
  Mechanism:      Ohloff's comparison: Citral = sharp lemon (conjugated aldehyde). 
                  Citronellal = soft, rosy-citrus, almost floral (non-conjugated 
                  aldehyde). The saturation at C2-C3 (no double bond there, unlike 
                  citral) completely changes the smell from "lemon" to "rosy-fresh."
                  
                  Kraft notes: Citronellal is a key intermediate in cyclization 
                  to isopulegol (menthol precursor). It also readily forms 
                  acetals in acidic solutions, providing slow-release effects.
  Key SAR Rule:   Conjugated C=C-CHO = lemon (citral). 
                  Saturated C-C-CHO = rosy-fresh (citronellal). 
                  One double bond makes all the difference.

  ARCTANDER — ODOR PROFILE
  ────────────────────────
  Character:      Fresh, rosy-green, citrus, slightly floral, soft, watery
  Tenacity:       Top note — short to moderate persistence
  Behavior:       Gentler and more floral than citral. Has a noticeable rosy-green 
                  facet that citral lacks. Used in rose accords and fresh-floral 
                  compositions for a natural, dewy quality. Less reactive than 
                  citral so better for longer-aged formulas.
  Natural occ.:   Citronella oil (25-40%), eucalyptus citriodora, rose otto (trace)

  CARLES — SYSTEMATIC POSITION
  ────────────────────────────
  Position:       TOP — Note de tête/Modifier (citrus-floral bridge)
  Carles Method:  Between citrus and floral rows. Citronellal bridges the two.
  Pairing Rule:   Citronellal + geraniol = rosy-citrus accord.
                  Citronellal + linalool = dewy freshness.

  ROUDNITSKA — AESTHETIC ROLE
  ──────────────────────────
  Function:       "Rosée" (dew). The "morning dew on a rose" quality. Softer 
                  and more feminine than citral's sharp lemon.
  Craft Note:     Less reactive than citral — better chemistry behavior.

  JELLINEK — PSYCHOLOGICAL MAP
  ────────────────────────────
  Axis Position:  STIMULATING, fresh, slightly anti-erogenic
  Effect:         Fresh, clean, slightly feminine, dewy, natural.
  Map Quadrant:   Upper-left (stimulating, fresh)

  PRACTICAL USAGE
  ───────────────
  Your form:      Neat
  Typical %:      1-5% of concentrate
  Max safe %:     8%
  Best with:      Geraniol, rose, linalool, hydroxycitronellal, bergamot
  Avoid:          Strong acids (cyclization to isopulegol can occur)
  Handle as:      Rosy-green-citrus bridge. Gentler alternative to citral for 
                  fresh effects with a floral lean.

  11. COUMARIN
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  Your stock: 20% dilution

  CHEMICAL IDENTITY
  ─────────────────
  IUPAC:          2H-Chromen-2-one (Benzopyran-2-one)
  Synonyms:       Coumarin, Tonka lactone, Cumarin
  CAS:            91-64-5
  Formula:        C₉H₆O₂
  MW:             146.14 g/mol
  BP:             301°C (sublimes from ~70°C)
  VP:             ~0.02 Pa (low — but SUBLIMES, so effective VP is higher)
  CLP:            1.39 (relatively polar)
  ODT:            0.002 µg/L air (potent)

  OPK — STRUCTURE-ACTIVITY
  ────────────────────────
  SAR Class:      Lactones — benzopyranone family (aromatic lactone)
  Olfactophore:   The α,β-unsaturated lactone fused to the benzene ring. The 
                  entire chromanone system IS the olfactophore — both the aromatic 
                  ring and the lactone carbonyl are required.
  Mechanism:      Ohloff's coumarin analysis: This is one of the most historically 
                  important molecules in perfumery. Coumarin was the FIRST synthetic 
                  material used in a perfume (Fougère Royale, Houbigant, 1882). 
                  Its powdery-tonka-hay character defined the fougère family and 
                  remains one of the most used materials in perfumery 140+ years later.
                  
                  Kraft's mechanism: The planar lactone bicyclic system fits into 
                  a "powdery" receptor pocket. Coumarin is the reference odorant 
                  for the "powdery" descriptor — when perfumers say "powdery," 
                  they mean "coumarin-like."
                  
                  NOTE: Coumarin is crystalline (MP 68-70°C) — your 20% dilution 
                  prevents crystallization in formulas.
  Key SAR Rule:   Benzene + fused α,β-unsaturated lactone = powdery/tonka.
                  Coumarin defines the "powdery" family. It IS the reference.

  ARCTANDER — ODOR PROFILE
  ────────────────────────
  Character:      Warm, powdery, hay-like, tonka bean, slightly sweet, slightly 
                  herbaceous (fresh-mown hay), vanillic undertone
  Tenacity:       Heart → base; excellent persistence (sublimes slowly)
  Behavior:       The backbone of fougère perfumery (lavender + coumarin + oakmoss).
                  Also essential in oriental, powdery, and ambery families. Creates 
                  the "powdery warmth" that millions of men's fragrances depend on.
                  Blends with almost everything.
  Natural occ.:   Tonka bean (1-3%), sweet woodruff, lavender (traces), hay, cassia

  CARLES — SYSTEMATIC POSITION
  ────────────────────────────
  Position:       HEART → BASE — Fond (powdery foundation)
  Carles Method:  ESSENTIAL in fougère, oriental, and ambery families. Carles' 
                  fougère formula always starts with: lavender + coumarin + 
                  oakmoss/patchouli.
  Pairing Rule:   Coumarin + lavender = fougère base.
                  Coumarin + vanillin = sweet powdery oriental.
                  Coumarin + iris = powdery luxury.

  ROUDNITSKA — AESTHETIC ROLE
  ──────────────────────────
  Function:       "Poudre" (powder). THE powder material. Roudnitska recognized 
                  coumarin as one of the indispensable materials — no collection 
                  without it, no powder without it.
  Craft Note:     Coumarin creates the "barbershop" impression in men's fragrances.
                  For women's, it creates the "face powder" impression.

  JELLINEK — PSYCHOLOGICAL MAP
  ────────────────────────────
  Axis Position:  NARCOTIC, warm, slightly erogenic
  Effect:         Comforting, nostalgic, "powdered warmth." Strong cultural 
                  association with classic men's grooming and women's powder rooms.
  Map Quadrant:   Lower-center (narcotic, warm, comforting)

  PRACTICAL USAGE
  ───────────────
  Your form:      20% dilution (crystalline solid dissolved)
  Typical %:      3-10% of concentrate (= 0.6-2% active)
  Max safe %:     15% (= 3% active — IFRA limit is around 2.3% in fine fragrance)
  Best with:      Lavender, iris, vanillin, musks, bergamot, tonka FO
  Avoid:          Nothing — blends with everything
  Handle as:      THE powder note. Your most important single heart-base bridge. 
                  Use it like salt in cooking — a little in everything.

  Your stock: neat

  CHEMICAL IDENTITY
  ─────────────────
  IUPAC:          4-(2,6,6-Trimethyl-1-cyclohexen-1-yl)-2-butanone
  Synonyms:       Dihydro-β-ionone, Dihydro beta ionone, DH-β-ionone
  CAS:            17283-81-7
  Formula:        C₁₃H₂₂O
  MW:             194.31 g/mol
  BP:             ~242°C
  VP:             ~0.8 Pa (25°C)
  CLP:            3.98
  ODT:            0.02 µg/L air (potent but less than β-ionone)

  OPK — STRUCTURE-ACTIVITY
  ────────────────────────
  SAR Class:      Ionones — saturated sidechain variant
  Olfactophore:   Same cyclohexenyl ring as beta ionone BUT the sidechain C=C 
                  double bond is reduced (saturated). The endocyclic double bond 
                  in the ring is retained.
  Mechanism:      Ohloff's ionone reduction series: When you hydrogenate the 
                  exocyclic sidechain double bond of β-ionone, the smell shifts 
                  from "violet" to "woody-amber." The violet character requires 
                  the conjugated enone system (ring C=C conjugated with C=O 
                  through the sidechain C=C). Breaking the sidechain conjugation 
                  by reduction destroys the violet note and reveals a dry, 
                  woody-amber character underneath.
                  
                  Kraft: Dihydro-β-ionone is the "woody skeleton" of the ionone 
                  family. It shows what happens when you remove the "violet 
                  feature" (conjugation) and leave only the "terpenoid warmth."
  Key SAR Rule:   Full conjugation (β-ionone) = violet. Broken conjugation 
                  (dihydro-β-ionone) = woody-amber. Conjugation is the switch.

  ARCTANDER — ODOR PROFILE
  ────────────────────────
  Character:      Dry, woody, slightly amber, warm, cedar-like, less floral 
                  than beta ionone, slightly fruity
  Tenacity:       Heart → base; better persistence than β-ionone (less volatile)
  Behavior:       The "woody ionone." Provides persistence and woody depth to iris 
                  accords without adding more violet. Bridges the ionone family 
                  into the woody family. Excellent for extending iris heart notes 
                  into the base.

  CARLES — SYSTEMATIC POSITION
  ────────────────────────────
  Position:       HEART → BASE — Bridge (ionone-to-wood connector)
  Carles Method:  Between the ionone heart and the woody base. 
  Pairing Rule:   Use β-ionone (violet) + DH-β-ionone (woody persistence) 
                  together: β-ionone blooms in the heart, DH-β-ionone persists 
                  into the base. This creates "violet that lasts."

  ROUDNITSKA — AESTHETIC ROLE
  ──────────────────────────
  Function:       "Persistance violette" (violet persistence). The bridge that 
                  carries the violet impression from heart into base.
  Craft Note:     Without DH-β-ionone (or allyl ionone), β-ionone disappears 
                  too quickly. Use them together for structural violet.

  JELLINEK — PSYCHOLOGICAL MAP
  ────────────────────────────
  Axis Position:  Center → NARCOTIC (warm, woody)
  Effect:         Grounding, warm, slightly melancholic.
  Map Quadrant:   Center-right (warm, grounding)

  PRACTICAL USAGE
  ───────────────
  Your form:      Neat
  Typical %:      2-6% of concentrate
  Max safe %:     10%
  Best with:      Beta ionone, allyl ionone, methyl ionone, cedarwood, ISO E Super
  Avoid:          Nothing — excellent blender
  Handle as:      The "woody backbone" of your iris/violet accords. Use for 
                  persistence and warmth in the ionone family.


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  4. DIHYDROMYRCENOL
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  Your stock: neat

  CHEMICAL IDENTITY
  ─────────────────
  IUPAC:          2,6-Dimethyl-7-octen-2-ol
  Synonyms:       Dihydromyrcenol, DHM, the "Cool Water molecule"
  CAS:            18479-58-8
  Formula:        C₁₀H₂₀O
  MW:             156.27 g/mol
  BP:             202°C
  VP:             ~10 Pa (25°C — volatile)
  CLP:            3.03
  ODT:            0.01 µg/L air

  OPK — STRUCTURE-ACTIVITY
  ────────────────────────
  SAR Class:      Terpene alcohols — acyclic monoterpenol (linalool derivative)
  Olfactophore:   Tertiary alcohol on a branched dimethylheptene chain. The 
                  gem-dimethyl carbinol (C(CH₃)₂-OH) is the key feature — it 
                  provides the "fresh-soapy-citrus" character.
  Mechanism:      Kraft's "fresh masculine" SAR: DHM is structurally related to 
                  linalool (both are monoterpenols) but the SATURATED portion of 
                  the chain and the TERTIARY alcohol give it a distinctly different 
                  character — less floral, more "soap/clean/metallic."
                  
                  Ohloff notes: DHM is the most-used material in men's perfumery 
                  by weight. It defines the "fresh masculine" concept since 
                  Davidoff Cool Water (1988), which used it at ~15-20% of 
                  concentrate. Nearly every "fresh blue" fragrance since contains 
                  DHM at high percentages.
  Key SAR Rule:   Tertiary monoterpenol + saturated chain = fresh-soapy-metallic.
                  Compare: linalool (secondary, unsaturated) = floral-sweet.

  ARCTANDER — ODOR PROFILE
  ────────────────────────
  Character:      Fresh, clean, soapy, citrus-lime-like, metallic, slightly 
                  floral, "laundry detergent," cool
  Tenacity:       Top → heart; moderate persistence, huge diffusion
  Behavior:       The "fresh masculine" workhorse. Creates an immediate impression 
                  of cleanliness and freshness. At high doses (>10%) it becomes 
                  the dominant character and creates the "blue fragrance" genre.
                  At low doses (2-5%) it adds fresh sparkle without domination.
  Natural occ.:   Not significant in nature — industrial synthesis from myrcene

  CARLES — SYSTEMATIC POSITION
  ────────────────────────────
  Position:       TOP — Note de tête (fresh/aromatic family)
  Carles Method:  Anchor of the "fresh" top note row. Modern update to Carles' 
                  citrus-only top note thinking — DHM adds a soapy-metallic 
                  freshness that citrus alone doesn't provide.
  Pairing Rule:   DHM + bergamot = modern fresh opening.
                  DHM + hedione + musks = "fresh blue" trinity.

  ROUDNITSKA — AESTHETIC ROLE
  ──────────────────────────
  Function:       "Propreté masculine" (masculine cleanliness). Roudnitska's Eau 
                  Sauvage used hedione for freshness; DHM is the next generation 
                  of that concept — even cleaner, even fresher.
  Craft Note:     DHM is the #1 material for instant commercial appeal in men's 
                  fragrances. But overuse creates "sameness" — every blue fragrance.

  JELLINEK — PSYCHOLOGICAL MAP
  ────────────────────────────
  Axis Position:  STIMULATING, cold, anti-erogenic
  Effect:         Clean, fresh, youthful, sporty, approachable.
  Map Quadrant:   Upper-left (maximum freshness/cleanliness)

  PRACTICAL USAGE
  ───────────────
  Your form:      Neat
  Typical %:      3-15% of concentrate (high user)
  Max safe %:     20% (used up to 25% in functional products)
  Best with:      Bergamot, hedione, musks, lavender, calone
  Avoid:          Heavy orientals (fresh-clean clashes with dark-sweet)
  Handle as:      Your "fresh clean" engine. Use at 3-5% for sparkle, 
                  10-15% for a full "blue fresh" character.


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  5. DIPROPYLENE GLYCOL (DPG)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  Your stock: neat

  CHEMICAL IDENTITY
  ─────────────────
  IUPAC:          1-(2-Methoxypropoxy)propan-2-ol (mixture of isomers)
  Synonyms:       DPG, Dipropylene Glycol
  CAS:            25265-71-8
  Formula:        C₆H₁₄O₃
  MW:             134.17 g/mol
  BP:             232°C
  VP:             <0.01 Pa
  CLP:            -0.67 (very hydrophilic)
  ODT:            Essentially odorless (>1000 µg/L)

  OPK — STRUCTURE-ACTIVITY
  ────────────────────────
  SAR Class:      Functional — glycol solvent/diluent
  Olfactophore:   None — odorless
  Mechanism:      DPG is the standard diluent for perfumery. Its hydroxyl groups 
                  provide excellent solubility for both polar and nonpolar 
                  aromatics. It does not evaporate significantly, so it acts as 
                  a non-volatile carrier that holds materials in solution on skin.
  Key SAR Rule:   Odorless + miscible + non-volatile = ideal diluent.

  ARCTANDER — ODOR PROFILE
  ────────────────────────
  Character:      Odorless, slightly sweet-viscous feel
  Behavior:       The industry standard for diluting powerful materials.

  CARLES / ROUDNITSKA / JELLINEK
  ──────────────────────────────
  Position:       FUNCTIONAL — outside the creative grid entirely
  Role:           Solvent, carrier, evaluator

  PRACTICAL USAGE
  ───────────────
  Your form:      Neat
  Use:            Diluent for strong materials, carrier for evaluations, and 
                  as a base for pre-diluted solutions.
  Handle as:      Your default diluent. Use to prepare working dilutions of 
                  overly potent materials (e.g., making a 10% solution of 
                  something you received neat but is too strong to dose).


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  6. D-LIMONENE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  Your stock: neat

  CHEMICAL IDENTITY
  ─────────────────
  IUPAC:          (R)-4-Isopropenyl-1-methylcyclohexene
  Synonyms:       D-Limonene, (R)-(+)-Limonene, Orange Terpene
  CAS:            5989-27-5
  Formula:        C₁₀H₁₆
  MW:             136.24 g/mol
  BP:             176°C
  VP:             ~190 Pa (25°C — VERY volatile)
  CLP:            4.57
  ODT:            0.01 µg/L air

  OPK — STRUCTURE-ACTIVITY
  ────────────────────────
  SAR Class:      Cyclic monoterpene hydrocarbons
  Olfactophore:   The cyclohexene ring with isopropenyl group. The (R)-enantiomer 
                  is the "orange" form; the (S)-enantiomer smells of lemon/turpentine.
  Mechanism:      Ohloff's terpenoid masterwork: D-Limonene is the most abundant 
                  monoterpene in nature (>90% of orange peel oil). The chiral 
                  center at C4 determines the odor completely:
                  - (R)-(+)-Limonene = sweet orange peel
                  - (S)-(−)-Limonene = harsh turpentine/lemon
                  This is one of the most dramatic examples of CHIRALITY affecting 
                  odor. A single mirror-image configuration change completely 
                  transforms the perception.
                  
                  Kraft notes: D-Limonene is also used industrially as a green 
                  solvent and degreaser. Its high VP makes it a fleeting top note — 
                  but those first 30 seconds of "fresh orange" are essential for 
                  creating positive first impressions.
  Key SAR Rule:   (R) = orange. (S) = turpentine. Chirality determines everything.

  ARCTANDER — ODOR PROFILE
  ────────────────────────
  Character:      Fresh, sweet, orange peel, citrusy, slightly terpenic
  Tenacity:       Very fleeting top note — gone in 15-30 minutes on strip
  Behavior:       Creates the immediate "orange" impression. Very short-lived but 
                  very impactful. The first thing anyone smells. Must be supported 
                  by longer-lasting citrus materials (bergamot, citral, linalool) 
                  for the citrus impression to persist.
  Natural occ.:   Orange peel (>90%), grapefruit, lemon, virtually all citrus

  CARLES — SYSTEMATIC POSITION
  ────────────────────────────
  Position:       TOP — Note de tête (fleeting citrus)
  Carles Method:  The most volatile citrus material. Use as the "opening flash" 
                  that draws attention in the first seconds.
  Pairing Rule:   D-Limonene (flash 0-5 min) + bergamot (5-30 min) + linalool 
                  (30-120 min) = cascading citrus top note that evolves over time.

  ROUDNITSKA — AESTHETIC ROLE
  ──────────────────────────
  Function:       "Éclat initial" (initial burst). The very first impression. 
                  Roudnitska valued the opening moment — D-Limonene is the material 
                  that creates the "spritz effect" — the moment of application 
                  that people associate with fragrance.
  Craft Note:     Oxidizes readily — keep bottle sealed and cool. Oxidized 
                  limonene is a strong skin sensitizer.

  JELLINEK — PSYCHOLOGICAL MAP
  ────────────────────────────
  Axis Position:  STIMULATING, fresh, anti-erogenic
  Effect:         Happy, energetic, clean, sunny, optimistic.
  Map Quadrant:   Upper-left (stimulating, fresh, cheerful)

  PRACTICAL USAGE
  ───────────────
  Your form:      Neat — store cool and sealed (oxidation risk)
  Typical %:      2-8% of concentrate
  Max safe %:     15% (watch oxidation-related sensitization)
  Best with:      Bergamot, citral, grapefruit, linalool, hedione
  Avoid:          Old/oxidized limonene — SKIN SENSITIZER when oxidized
  Handle as:      Opening flash. Use for the first 30 seconds of a perfume. 
                  Replace if the material smells harsh/turpentine-like (oxidized).
