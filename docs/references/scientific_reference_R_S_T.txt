━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  1. RASPBERRY KETONE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  Your stock: Neat (crystalline solid — dissolve before use)

  CHEMICAL IDENTITY
  ─────────────────
  IUPAC:          4-(4-Hydroxyphenyl)butan-2-one
  Synonyms:       Rheosmin, Frambinone, Oxyphenylon, 
                  p-Hydroxybenzyl acetone, Raspberry ketone
  CAS:            5471-51-2
  Formula:        C₁₀H₁₂O₂
  MW:             164.20 g/mol
  BP:             292°C (high for its molecular weight)
  VP:             ~0.03 Pa
  CLP:            1.45
  ODT:            ~0.01 µg/L (VERY POTENT — impact material)

  OPK — STRUCTURE-ACTIVITY
  ────────────────────────
  SAR Class:      Hydroxyphenyl ketones — para-substituted phenol + methyl 
                  ketone connected by ethylene bridge
  Olfactophore:   Ohloff: The "raspberry olfactophore" requires BOTH the 
                  phenolic OH (hydrogen bond donor) AND the carbonyl (hydrogen 
                  bond acceptor), separated by exactly 4 atoms. Remove the 
                  phenol OH → anise/cumin character. Remove the ketone → 
                  tyrosol (weak). The combination = raspberry.
  Mechanism:      This is the ACTUAL chemical responsible for raspberry smell 
                  in nature (found at 1-4 mg/kg in fresh raspberries). It's 
                  one of the rare cases where a single chemical IS the 
                  characteristic smell of a fruit.
                  
                  Kraft: The para-OH group is critical — moving it to the meta 
                  or ortho position destroys the raspberry character. The 
                  4-atom spacer between OH and C=O creates a bidentate binding 
                  motif that's highly specific.
  Key SAR Rule:   p-HOC₆H₄CH₂CH₂COCH₃ = raspberry. Para-OH mandatory. 
                  4-atom spacer mandatory. No shortcuts.

  ARCTANDER — ODOR PROFILE
  ────────────────────────
  Character:      Sweet-fruity, raspberry, with warm jammy undertones, slightly 
                  floral (violet-like from the phenol ring). Clean, bright fruit 
                  that's distinctly "berry" rather than citrus or tropical.
  Tenacity:       Heart → base; surprisingly persistent for a fruity material 
                  due to its high boiling point and low vapor pressure.
  Behavior:       Crystalline solid — dissolve in DPG or ethanol before use. 
                  Even dissolved, it needs time to bloom. In a formula, it 
                  appears in the heart/dry-down rather than the top.

  CARLES — SYSTEMATIC POSITION
  ────────────────────────────
  Position:       HEART → BASE (unusually persistent fruit)
  Carles Method:  The "true fruit" — not a top note sparkler but a deep 
                  fruit that lives in the heart.
  Pairing Rule:   Raspberry ketone + Peonile = full-spectrum berry-rose.
                  Raspberry ketone + vanillin = raspberry crème.
                  Raspberry ketone + ISO E Super = modern fruity-wood.
                  Raspberry ketone + violet (Methyl Ionone) = berry-violet.

  ROUDNITSKA — AESTHETIC ROLE
  ──────────────────────────
  Function:       "True berry" — the only material in your inventory that IS the 
                  actual natural raspberry odorant. Brings truthfulness and 
                  recognizability to berry accords.
  Craft Note:     Must be dissolved first. Do not add crystals directly to a 
                  formula — they'll sit undissolved. Pre-dissolve to 10-20% 
                  in DPG or ethanol for working solution.

  JELLINEK — PSYCHOLOGICAL MAP
  ────────────────────────────
  Axis Position:  EROGENIC, slightly narcotic
  Effect:         Sweet, inviting, youthful, playful, sexy-sweet, addictive.
  Map Quadrant:   Lower-right (erogenic sweetness)

  PRACTICAL USAGE
  ───────────────
  Your form:      Neat (CRYSTALLINE SOLID — must dissolve first!)
  Typical %:      0.5-3% of concentrate (after dissolving)
  Max safe %:     5% (neat equivalent)
  Best with:      Peonile (fruit amplifier), vanillin (crème), Methyl Ionone 
                  (violet-berry), labdanum (warm berry)
  Avoid:          Adding as undissolved crystals; stacking with Ethyl Maltol 
                  (becomes cloying)
  Handle as:      Dissolve 10-20% in DPG or ethanol for a working solution. 
                  Then dose from that solution. Potent — LOW doses effective.


━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  2. ROMANDOLIDE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  Your stock: Neat

  CHEMICAL IDENTITY
  ─────────────────
  IUPAC:          Methyl 2-{[3-(1-methylethyl)bicyclo[2.2.1]hept-5-en-2-yl]
                  carbonyloxy}propanoate (Firmenich designation)
                  More common: methyl (1R)-2-oxo-2-[(2-norbornyl)oxy]propanoate
  Synonyms:       Romandolide (Firmenich), "fruity musk," 
                  "modern replacement for Musk Ketone."
  CAS:            236391-76-7
  Formula:        C₁₁H₁₈O₄
  MW:             214.26 g/mol
  BP:             ~275°C
  VP:             ~0.05 Pa
  CLP:            2.20
  ODT:            ~0.5 µg/L

  OPK — STRUCTURE-ACTIVITY
  ────────────────────────
  SAR Class:      Linear/alicyclic musk — norbornyl ester type. NOT a macrocyclic 
                  or polycyclic musk. Romandolide is a structurally unusual musk 
                  that doesn't fit neatly into traditional categories.
  Olfactophore:   Kraft: Romandolide's musk character comes from its ester bonds 
                  + norbornyl skeleton creating a molecular "footprint" that 
                  mimics the macrocyclic musk receptor binding. The two ester 
                  groups provide the clean, fresh facet; the norbornyl ring 
                  provides volume/body.
  Mechanism:      Romandolide was discovered by Firmenich as part of their 
                  "new musk" program seeking non-nitro, non-polycyclic musks. 
                  It binds musk receptors via a different mechanism than 
                  macrocyclics (Habanolide) or polycyclics (Galaxolide) — 
                  it's a third pathway to musk perception.
                  
                  Key property: Romandolide is MORE BIODEGRADABLE than 
                  polycyclic musks (Galaxolide, Tonalide) — better 
                  environmental profile.
  Key SAR Rule:   Norbornyl + di-ester = clean fruity musk via novel receptor 
                  binding pathway.

  ARCTANDER — ODOR PROFILE
  ────────────────────────
  Character:      Clean, fresh, fruity-sweet, musky, with a distinct pear-like 
                  fruit facet. Less animalic than traditional musks; less 
                  powdery than nitro musks. "Laundry-clean with a fruit garnish."
  Tenacity:       Base; excellent persistence comparable to macrocyclic musks. 
                  Very good on fabric.
  Behavior:       Blends seamlessly with other musks. Never dominant. Adds a 
                  unique fruity dimension that no other musk in your inventory 
                  provides.

  CARLES — SYSTEMATIC POSITION
  ────────────────────────────
  Position:       BASE (musk foundation + fruity facet)
  Carles Method:  The "fruity musk" — adds fruit without adding a separate 
                  fruity ingredient. Clever economy.
  Pairing Rule:   Romandolide + Galaxolide = clean-fruity base (modern fabric 
                  softener accord).
                  Romandolide + Habanolide = fruity + animalic = complex musk.
                  Romandolide + Ethylene Brassylate = enormous fruity musk 
                  volume.

  ROUDNITSKA — AESTHETIC ROLE
  ──────────────────────────
  Function:       "Musk with a secret" — adds an unexpected fruity wink to the 
                  musk palette, preventing musk bases from becoming monotonous.
  Craft Note:     Use alongside your other musks (Galaxolide, Habanolide, 
                  Ethylene Brassylate, Exaltolide) to create a multi-dimensional 
                  musk base.

  JELLINEK — PSYCHOLOGICAL MAP
  ────────────────────────────
  Axis Position:  Slightly EROGENIC, slightly stimulating
  Effect:         Fresh, clean, inviting, youthful, approachable.
  Map Quadrant:   Center-low (gently sensual, fresh)

  PRACTICAL USAGE
  ───────────────
  Your form:      Neat
  Typical %:      3-10% of concentrate
  Max safe %:     15%
  Best with:      Other musks (Galaxolide, Habanolide, EB), ISO E Super, 
                  Hedione, fresh florals
  Avoid:          Isolation — Romandolide works best in ENSEMBLE with other musks
  Handle as:      Standard musk building block. Friendly. Adds fruity dimension.

  3. ROSE OXIDE (10%)
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  Your stock: 10% solution

  CHEMICAL IDENTITY
  ─────────────────
  IUPAC:          4-Methyl-2-(2-methylprop-1-en-1-yl)tetrahydro-2H-pyran
  Synonyms:       Rose oxide, Rosenoxid, cis- and trans-Rose oxide, 
                  Tetrahydro-4-methyl-2-(2-methylpropenyl)-2H-pyran
  CAS:            16409-43-1 (cis-isomer, the more potent one)
  Formula:        C₁₀H₁₈O
  MW:             154.25 g/mol
  BP:             185°C
  VP:             ~25 Pa (VOLATILE!)
  CLP:            3.20
  ODT:            ~0.0005 µg/L (ULTRA-POTENT — among the most potent 
                  odorants in all of nature)

  OPK — STRUCTURE-ACTIVITY
  ────────────────────────
  SAR Class:      Oxygen heterocycles — tetrahydropyran with exocyclic 
                  isobutenyl group
  Olfactophore:   Ohloff (foundational work): The pyran oxygen + methyl groups 
                  at specific positions = rose olfactophore. The cis-isomer 
                  (2S,4R) is ~100× more potent than the trans-isomer.
  Mechanism:      Rose oxide occurs naturally in Bulgarian rose oil (~1-3%) 
                  despite contributing a DISPROPORTIONATE amount of the rose 
                  character due to its incredibly low ODT.
                  
                  Kraft: Rose oxide activates olfactory receptors at lower 
                  concentrations than almost any other rose material in 
                  existence. The cis-isomer's geometry positions the pyran O, 
                  the methyl at C-4, and the isobutenyl group in a spatial 
                  arrangement that fits the rose receptor with extraordinary 
                  precision.
                  
                  Ohloff: Rose oxide was the molecule that helped establish 
                  the concept of "character-impact compounds" — materials 
                  present at trace levels that define the entire character 
                  of a natural.
  Key SAR Rule:   Tetrahydropyran + isobutenyl + cis-geometry = SUPREME rose 
                  potency. ODT 0.0005 = this is your second or third most 
                  potent material (after Norlimbanol and Maple Lactone).

  ARCTANDER — ODOR PROFILE
  ────────────────────────
  Character:      Green-floral, rose-geranium, metallic-fresh, with an 
                  unmistakable "bright, shearing" rose character. Not a 
                  soft pillowy rose (that's PEA) — this is a sharp, 
                  crystalline, modern rose.
  Tenacity:       Top → early heart. VOLATILE (VP ~25 Pa), so it flashes 
                  brightly in the opening and fades within a few hours. 
                  But its extreme potency means even small residual amounts 
                  remain perceptible.
  Behavior:       At 10% dilution: very workable. The sharp, almost metallic-green 
                  rose character instantly modernizes any rose accord. 
                  Signature ingredient of "lychee rose" accords (combined with 
                  citronellol, it creates the lychee illusion).

  CARLES — SYSTEMATIC POSITION
  ────────────────────────────
  Position:       TOP → EARLY HEART (bright rose flash)
  Carles Method:  The "modern rose opener" — gives roses their contemporary, 
                  sharp, dewy quality.
  Pairing Rule:   Rose oxide 10% + geraniol + PEA = modern rose (dewy + sweet).
                  Rose oxide + citronellal = lychee-rose illusion (classic trick).
                  Rose oxide + Dihydromyrcenol = fresh masculine rose.
                  Rose oxide + Hedione = transparent crystalline rose.

  ROUDNITSKA — AESTHETIC ROLE
  ──────────────────────────
  Function:       "Rose electricity" — the spark that makes roses contemporary 
                  rather than old-fashioned. Without rose oxide, most modern 
                  rose compositions would smell dated.
  Craft Note:     At 10%, dose carefully — 1-3% of your 10% solution in the 
                  concentrate (= 0.1-0.3% neat). More than that becomes 
                  metallic and geranium-like.

  JELLINEK — PSYCHOLOGICAL MAP
  ────────────────────────────
  Axis Position:  STIMULATING, EXALTING
  Effect:         Bright, sharp, modern, energizing, dewy, "morning rose garden."
  Map Quadrant:   Upper-left (exalting freshness with stimulating energy)

  PRACTICAL USAGE
  ───────────────
  Your form:      10% solution (ESSENTIAL dilution — neat would overdose easily)
  Typical %:      1-5% of concentrate (of your 10% solution = 0.1-0.5% neat)
  Max safe %:     1% (neat)
  Best with:      Geraniol, PEA, citronellal (lychee trick), Hedione, 
                  Dihydromyrcenol
  Avoid:          Overdose → harsh metallic geranium; high % overwhelms 
                  delicate florals
  Handle as:      ULTRA-POTENT even at 10%. ODT 0.0005 µg/L = among the 
                  most potent odorants in your entire inventory. Think of 
                  your 10% solution as "already strong."

  1. SANDALWOOD FRAGRANCE OIL
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
  Your stock: Pre-blended fragrance oil

  CHEMICAL IDENTITY
  ─────────────────
  IUPAC:          PROPRIETARY BLEND — likely contains: Ebanol, Bacdanol or 
                  Javanol, polysantol, Sandalore, β-santalol synthetic analogs, 
                  POSSIBLY traces of natural sandalwood distillate, fixative 
                  musks, cedarwood fractions
  Synonyms:       Sandalwood FO, Sandalwood base, Mysore sandalwood accord
  CAS:            Proprietary mixture
  Formula:        Mixture
  MW:             Mixture (~200-260 g/mol for key sandalwood chemicals)
  BP:             N/A (blend)
  VP:             Mixture (generally low — designed for base persistence)
  CLP:            Mixture
  ODT:            ~0.01 µg/L (for dominant sandaloid components)

  OPK — STRUCTURE-ACTIVITY
  ────────────────────────
  SAR Class:      Synthetic sandalwood reconstruction (multi-component blend)
  Olfactophore:   The "sandalwood" olfactophore (Ohloff landmark work): requires 
                  a primary or secondary alcohol on a flexible chain, attached 
                  to a compact 5-or-6 membered ring bearing 2-3 methyl groups. 
                  The spatial arrangement must place the OH ~5-6Å from the 
                  ring center. All major sandalwood synthetics (Ebanol, 
                  Bacdanol, Javanol, Polysantol) satisfy this pharmacophore.
  Mechanism:      Natural Mysore sandalwood (Santalum album) contains 
                  α-santalol (50-60%) and β-santalol (20-25%) as the key 
                  odorants. α-Santalol is the "creamy, milky" note; β-santalol 
                  is "woody, balsamic." A good FO reconstructs this balance.
                  
                  You already have Ebanol (neat), which covers the creamy side. 
                  This FO adds the complexity and balance you can't get from 
                  a single chemical.
  Key SAR Rule:   Sandalwood triad: compact ring + flexible chain + terminal 
                  alcohol. FO pre-balances these relationships.

  ARCTANDER — ODOR PROFILE
  ────────────────────────
  Character:      Creamy, milky, warm, balsamic-woody, soft, precious, with 
                  subtle animal-skin undertones. "Liquid gold."
  Tenacity:       Heart → deep base; sandalwood notes are among the most 
                  persistent in perfumery.
  Behavior:       Drop-in "instant sandalwood" — designed to need no 
                  modification. Provides full sandalwood spectrum.

  CARLES — SYSTEMATIC POSITION
  ────────────────────────────
  Position:       HEART → DEEP BASE (warm precious wood)
  Carles Method:  The "precious wood pillar." Core of oriental und woody bases.
  Pairing Rule:   Sandalwood FO + ISO E Super = modern wood duo.
                  Sandalwood FO + rose (PEA + geraniol) = the classic "rose 
                  de mai on sandalwood" — perfumery's most famous dyad.
                  Sandalwood FO + vanillin = warm oriental base.
                  Sandalwood FO + patchouli = deep earthy wood.

  ROUDNITSKA — AESTHETIC ROLE
  ──────────────────────────
  Function:       "Warmth incarnate" — the material that makes compositions 
                  feel skin-like, intimate, precious.
  Craft Note:     As a FO, treat it as a convenience base. Use alongside your 
                  Ebanol for more specific sandalwood control.

  JELLINEK — PSYCHOLOGICAL MAP
  ────────────────────────────
  Axis Position:  NARCOTIC, erogenic
  Effect:         Intimate, spiritual, warm, meditative, sensual, enveloping.
  Map Quadrant:   Lower-right (deeply narcotic, sensual)

  PRACTICAL USAGE
  ───────────────
  Your form:      Pre-blended FO
  Typical %:      3-12% of concentrate
  Max safe %:     20% (FO is pre-balanced)
  Best with:      ISO E Super (modern), rose (classic), vanillin (oriental), 
                  Ebanol (boost)
  Avoid:          Overcombination with BOTH this FO and neat Ebanol at high 
                  doses = overly creamy
  Handle as:      Convenience base. Flexible dosing. Complements Ebanol.

  Your stock: 1% solution (CRITICAL — raw Scentenal would be unusable)

  CHEMICAL IDENTITY
  ─────────────────
  IUPAC:          (2E)-2-Pentylcyclopent-2-en-1-one (putative — Scentenal is 
                  a Givaudan captive or near-captive; exact structure may vary)
  Synonyms:       Scentenal (Givaudan), powerful green-metallic aldehyde-like
  CAS:            NOT publicly listed (captive)
  Formula:        Approximately C₁₀H₁₆O (estimated)
  MW:             ~152 g/mol (estimated)
  BP:             ~220°C (estimated)
  VP:             ~2 Pa (estimated)
  CLP:            ~2.8 (estimated)
  ODT:            ~0.0001 µg/L (EXTREMELY POTENT — one of the most powerful 
                  green-metallic odorants; in the same potency class as 
                  Maple Lactone and Norlimbanol)

  OPK — STRUCTURE-ACTIVITY
  ────────────────────────
  SAR Class:      Cyclopentenone — α,β-unsaturated cyclopentanone subclass
  Olfactophore:   The cyclopentenone ring with conjugated enone provides a 
                  powerful, sharp, metallic-green olfactory signal. The long 
                  alkyl chain (pentyl) adds waxy-aldehydic character.
  Mechanism:      Cyclopentenones are among the most universally potent odorant 
                  classes. The combination of the enone's electrophilic 
                  character with the specific ring strain of cyclopentanone 
                  creates an odorant that activates multiple receptor subtypes 
                  simultaneously — hence the "complex, multi-faceted" perception.
                  
                  The 1% dilution is NOT optional. Raw Scentenal at even 
                  0.1% in a formula would obliterate everything else.
  Key SAR Rule:   Cyclopentenone + alkyl chain = green-metallic power. 
                  Conjugated enone = extreme receptor affinity.

  Character:      (At 1%): Green, metallic, aldehydic, waxy, slightly

  Tenacity:       Heart; moderate persistence but EXTREME projection. Even at 
                  1%, tiny doses produce enormous sillage.
  Behavior:       At 1%, it's a precision tool. Add to provide "transparent 
                  green radiance" — a quality that lifts other notes without 
                  being identifiable itself. At correct dose, nobody will 
                  detect "Scentenal" — they'll perceive everything around it 
                  as more alive and fresh.

  CARLES — SYSTEMATIC POSITION

  Position:       HEART (green-fresh radiance modifier)
  Carles Method:  Not a note-builder but a MODIFIER — changes the texture of 
                  everything around it.
  Pairing Rule:   Scentenal 1% + Hedione = double radiance (powerful combo).
                  Scentenal 1% + florals = transparent, dewy, "morning" quality.
                  Scentenal 1% + aldehydes = amplified Chanel-style sparkle.

  ──────────────────────────

  OPK — STRUCTURE-ACTIVITY
  ────────────────────────
  SAR Class:      FUNCTIONAL INGREDIENT — triester plasticizer/solvent, NOT 
                  an odorant
  Olfactophore:   None. Triethyl citrate has negligible vapor pressure relative 
                  to its molecular weight and no meaningful olfactory receptor 
                  binding.
  Mechanism:      Ohloff/Kraft: TEC is NOT a perfumery odorant — it's a 
                  FUNCTIONAL SOLVENT and FIXATIVE.
                  
                  PRIMARY FUNCTION: Deodorant additive / anti-perspirant 
                  fragrance carrier. TEC is hydrolyzed by skin bacteria 
                  to citric acid, which lowers skin pH, inhibiting 
                  odor-producing bacteria. This is why TEC is common in 
                  deodorant formulations.
                  
                  SECONDARY FUNCTION: Non-phthalate plasticizer. Can replace 
                  DEP in formulations where phthalate-free claims are needed. 
                  Excellent solvent for crystalline materials (coumarin, 
                  vanillin, raspberry ketone).
                  
                  TERTIARY FUNCTION: IPM alternative for alcohol-free 
                  formulations.
  Key SAR Rule:   Citrate triester = virtually odorless, deodorizing, 
                  non-phthalate carrier.

  Tenacity:       N/A (not an odorant)
  Behavior:       Functions as a carrier, solvent, and fixative. Does not

  Craft Note:     TEC is your phthalate-free alternative to DEP. If you're
