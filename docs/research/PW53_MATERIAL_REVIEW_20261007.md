# Research review of all 53 received PerfumersWorld stocks

Roles, odor character, evidence limits, handling and relevance to Chimie L’Homme

Reviewed **7 October 2026**. All 53 purchase lines from order 261004-055451ce1; 52 catalogue identities because Buccoxime was bought at two strengths.

## Main findings

The collection expands the palette in several genuinely different directions: clean musks; dry woods and ambergris; citrus-to-floral materials; green/fruit accents; creamy lactones; natural extracts; and sweet/spicy character materials.

For the original L’Homme, the most useful first question remains the balance and character of its musk system. Habanolide and Romandolide are distinct comparison options. This review does not demonstrate that Galaxolide caused the block or that replacing it will preserve the original smell.

Florol, Magnolan and ethyl linalyl acetate offer different ways to connect fresh top notes to the heart. Their documented descriptions support separate hypotheses, not a combined addition or a claim that they physically separate odors.

Ambrocenide is an odor-active woody-amber booster, including in the opening. The manufacturer's 10% DPG sheet cannot supply quantitative properties or dosing for your 10% TEC bottle.

Many new materials would be excellent for other briefs but introduce a new recognizer into L’Homme: peach/coconut, apple/pineapple/cassis, tea/tobacco, smoky leather, saffron, maple or vanilla.

## Scope and evidence

- Received masses are the purchase quantities confirmed by the user, not current remaining inventory or active chemical masses.
- Nominal 10% and 85% preparations retain an unspecified percentage basis. 'As supplied' records whole-product loading, not a purity assay.
- Odor character and common roles are sourced primarily from the supplier's own descriptions and manufacturer technical literature. The proposed role in L’Homme is the reviewer's untested formulation hypothesis.
- Strip persistence, stability in a product base, diffusion descriptions, vapor pressure, and wearer-observed longevity are different endpoints. No 6-hour skin-performance guarantee is inferred.
- No complete lot-specific composition, safety assessment, IFRA compliance calculation, numerical OAV model or dosing card was produced. Missing or contradictory quantitative data remain unresolved.
- Research collection is complete for the 53-item scope; scientific and regulatory evidence depth varies by material. No runtime physics, odor-threshold values, inventory authorities or formulas were changed by this research.

Document existence does not establish completeness. 19 of the screened SDS pages explicitly state that classification data are unavailable. ## Identity and document findings

### Both Buccoxime purchases are the same odorant in different preparations

Keep the undiluted crystals and 10% DPG stock separate in dosing records. They do not supply two complementary musk or fruit roles. See stock cards 21, 41.

### Three misleading historical trade names

C-14 and C-18 'aldehydes' are lactones. The supplier's Maple Lactone CAS identifies cyclotene, a hydroxyketone. The latter also has an unresolved physical-form discrepancy. See stock cards 10, 11, 48.

### Fructone B is not ordinary Fructone

The supplier's 6290-17-1 identity matches IFF Fraistone. IFF Fructone is 6413-10-1. Formula aliases must preserve the difference. See stock cards 46.

### Natural preparations are whole products

Ambrette absolute is not ambrettolide; vanilla absolute is not vanillin; orris butter is not an irone assay; lavandin absolute is not lavandin essential oil. See stock cards 1, 26, 49, 51.

### Terpeneless lemon is not an FCF declaration

The retrieved lemon document lacks usable composition/classification data. Its name alone does not resolve furocoumarins or phototoxicity. See stock cards 37.

### Supplier document presence is not proof of suitability

Several retrieved SDS pages contain no classification data, and some pages have contradictory physical or hazard entries. Generic conformity tables were not promoted into material-specific safe-use limits. See stock cards 8, 18, 19, 36, 37, 48.

### Published restrictions and grade specifications matter

Coumarin, rose ketones, phenylacetaldehyde and Safranal have relevant published IFRA restrictions. Rectified birch tar must be distinguished from prohibited crude pyrolysate and requires the applicable PAH specification. See stock cards 8, 19, 20, 36, 52.

## Relevance to the original Chimie L’Homme

These are separate comparison questions, not a combined reformulation or a compounding card. Preserve the original sporty citrus/pink-pepper identity and its restrained heart as the sensory reference.

| Materials | Question to test | Main limitation |
|---|---|---|
| Habanolide or Romandolide | Does changing one defined musk role improve citrus/pink-pepper clarity while preserving the original drydown? | Compare alternatives separately; no equal-strength replacement factor was established. |
| Zenolide or Helvetolide | Would a softer fruit-tinted musk texture help? | Red-fruit or pear/ambrette facets can change the original smell. |
| Florol, Magnolan, or ethyl linalyl acetate | Is there a missing transition between the fresh opening and the restrained heart? | Each supplies its own floral/citrus character; adding all three confounds the question. |
| Ambrocenide | What happens when a distinct woody-amber booster is introduced? | An addition to an old aliquot tests an addition, not replacement of its existing superamber. |
| Ambrofix, Vertofix, Kephalis or cedryl acetate | Does the base need a different texture rather than more power? | These have separate ambergris, cedar and tobacco/amber roles; they are not interchangeable fixatives. |

## Evidence and handling notes

### What the sensory literature supports

Zak and colleagues measured nonlinear, antagonistic responses to odor mixtures in olfactory sensory neurons in freely breathing mice. This supports caution about predicting a mixture by adding individual material descriptions. It does not identify a culprit in L’Homme or demonstrate a human hedonic benefit from any replacement. [Zak et al., 2020: antagonistic odor interactions in freely breathing mice](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-020-17124-5).

### IFRA status checked on 7 October 2026

The public documentation located includes the 51st Amendment. IFRA reported that the 52nd consultation closed in June 2026, with formal notification expected towards late November 2026, and published an end-of-consultation update in August. Forthcoming changes are not treated as already-notified limits. [IFRA 51st Amendment index](https://ifrafragrance.org/docs/default-source/51st-amendment/ifra-51st-amendment---index-of-ifra-standards.pdf?sfvrsn=6511ee03_2), [IFRA 52nd Amendment consultation closure](https://ifrafragrance.org/latest-updates/ifra-news/ifra-52nd-amendment-consultation-closed), [IFRA August 2026 end-of-consultation announcement](https://ifrafragrance.org/latest-updates/ifra-news/ifra-publishes-end-of-consultation-letter-for-the-52nd-amendment-to-the-ifra-standards).

### Manufacturer and purchased grade

The manufacturer sources describe their own grades. Agreement of a trade name/CAS supports identity-level interpretation, but does not prove the rebottled supplier's producer, assay or lot composition. The Ambrocenide DPG-to-TEC and Ambrette extract comparisons are explicitly limited. [Symrise Ambrocenide 10 DPG technical sheet](https://www.symrise.com/fileadmin/symrise/Marketing/Scent_and_care/Aroma_molecules/Ingredient_finder/SYM_PC_Datenblaetter/SYM_PC-Ambrocenide_10_DPG.pdf), [Givaudan Ambrette Seed Absolute](https://www.givaudan.com/fragrance-beauty/fragrance-ingredients-business/natural-ingredients/ambrette-seed-absolute).

### Handling and storage

Retain original labels and carriers, close containers promptly, and follow the exact product SDS for storage and handling. Use mass for solids and confirm any future dilution is homogeneous. No universal refrigeration temperature, shelf-life, dissolution procedure or safe skin-evaluation concentration is inferred for the entire collection.
