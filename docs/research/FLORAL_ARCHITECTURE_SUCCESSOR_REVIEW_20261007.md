## Fruit-recognizer defect and repair

The initial two regression tests failed on the current source before the repair:
named lychee intent was absent from the fruit query, and Juniper Berry EO could
qualify as a fruit-role candidate from its name and a category-proxy profile.

The repaired path:

1. Retains requested fruit names, including the lexical lychee/litchi relation,
   as design intent rather than inventing a stock alias.
2. Requires a material's own annotated/reviewed fruit descriptors for generic
   fruit-role eligibility. Names, categories, comments, carrier strings and
   synergy partners cannot alone supply that eligibility. Freshness/sweetness
   or the chemical class "ester" cannot manufacture a fruit annotation.
3. Keeps explicit material anchors independent: requesting Juniper Berry EO
   still permits that exact inventory material for its requested role.
4. Applies token boundaries and the same fruit-alias vocabulary to exclusions:
   "without apple" no longer deletes pineapple, and excluded lychee cannot
   reappear through the spelling litchi.
5. Reports `NAMED_FRUIT_RECOGNITION_UNVERIFIED` as a nonblocking limitation and
   `STRUCTURAL_ROLE_COVERAGE_ONLY_NOT_SENSORY_ACCURACY` as the alignment basis.

This is not complete named-fruit realism. The ordinary ranking vocabulary still
contains contextual information after eligibility, and all descriptor matching
and stock allocation remain heuristic. A fruit-qualified material is not thereby
a measured lychee, pear or apple reconstruction. Non-authoritative design work
remains available without demanding photographs or new laboratory evidence.

## Provenance and diagnostic hardening

- The active v2 loader requires an externally selected v2 schema; a payload
  cannot bypass predecessor checks by calling itself v1.
- The successor must match the fixed predecessor path, exact raw-byte hash and
  ordered inherited mapping prefix. Caller-selected predecessor paths are rejected.
- Full executable role data is serialized. The diagnostic recomputes its hash
  rather than trusting a claimed digest or counting different labels as diversity.
- Source-bound branches require all four exact false authority fields; missing,
  extra or numeric-false fields cannot become a successful receipt.
- Source closure checks the selected manifest, subtype-card hash, complete
  required dependency list, source hashes and literal-true review admission.
- The control and comparison arms both undergo authority, network, role-signature
  and applicable source-closure checks. Neither can borrow the other's pass.
- Both adapter versions participate in durable-job reference fingerprints. All
  changed runtime modules already participate in implementation fingerprints.
