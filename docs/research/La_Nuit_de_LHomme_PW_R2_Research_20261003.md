# La Nuit de L'Homme PW-R2 — research, purchase options and audit

Date: 3 October 2026. Read-only research plus additive design artifacts; no inventory, runtime, stock or original-formula edits.

## Decision boundary

Reconstruct the **original EDT architecture** with PW ingredients, not EDP/Le Parfum/Bleu Électrique. The proprietary formula and its proportions remain unknown. Public marketing provides recognizers; chemistry/manufacturer data provide candidate functions. Neither provides a blind similarity percentage.

Official YSL references:
- [US EDT page](https://www.yslbeautyus.com/fragrance/mens-fragrances/la-nuit-de-lhomme/la-nuit-de-lhomme-eau-de-toilette-spray/426YSL.html): cardamom/cedar/coumarin; additional iris/tonka framing and a limited presence declaration.

- Different descriptions are not merged into an alleged commercial formula. Reformulation, market and reference-bottle uncertainty remain explicit.

## Purchase prioritization — reuse owned bottles first

**Target realization / high-value comparison:** Grosso as a different aromatic grade; undiluted Exaltolide if choosing the low-carrier master.

**Conditional preparation convenience:** undiluted Galaxolide and vanillin. These supply a known mass basis/low carrier load, not new odor functions. Skip buying duplicates if existing stocks can be used accurately and the changed carrier matrix is acceptable.

**Optional detail:** cis-3 hexenyl salicylate, rose ketones and light patchouli. Do not buy them before the lead-versus-control result suggests the detail block earns its place.

**Do not buy for this retry:** Ambrocenide/Amber Xtreme/Norlimbanol/Sylvamber/Timbersilk to indiscriminately intensify woods; heavy vanilla/ethyl maltol/benzoin to fake tonka richness; a fourth musk; pear/apple esters; leather/smoke bases; Lilial. These do not address the locked EDT architecture. This is a campaign-specific rejection, not a judgment that the materials are generally inferior.

## Safety-screening arithmetic, not clearance

At a 15% w/w product-equivalent loading:

- Added pure coumarin target: 1.2% × 15% = **0.18%** of finished product by mass.
- Added alpha damascone: 0.025% × 15% = **0.00375%**.
- Added damascenone: 0.005% × 15% = **0.00075%**.
- Their added rose-ketone subtotal is **0.00450%**, not a full total accounting for natural constituents or any other restricted-group member.
- Whole-oil constituents, grade-dependent constituents, certificates and current applicable restrictions remain to be assessed. No numeric limit is used here as a substitute for that review.
- A 30 mL w/v build has a different denominator from a 30 g w/w build; final finished mass is required for mass-based compliance calculations.
- No skin application or commercial release has been performed or authorized by this artifact.

## Persistence and versioning

The original formulas, July research report, inventory and governing documents are hash-bound in the JSON. No history is rewritten. PW-R2 is a proposed new design, not a claimed physical bottle successor. No sensory posterior was updated because no new observations exist.

A future PW-R3 must identify the tested recipe, actual stocks and measured changes. For an existing bottle, use only a separately confirmed nonnegative delta; the replacement/removal candidates in this campaign are not instructions to subtract liquid from a bottle.

## Fresh verification boundary

Run focused Decimal accounting, source preservation, exact SKU matching, candidate total checks, authority flags and whitespace checks. No new pipeline script, model training, legacy headspace/OAV run, full project verifier or safety release is needed to verify these additive design documents. The local verification artifact records exactly which checks ran.

The research and formulation **design** can be complete while similarity, stability, safety and physical compounding remain explicitly not established.
