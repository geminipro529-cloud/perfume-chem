# CHIMIE Femme PW2 — target-first pear/apple development campaign

Date: 2 October 2026.

## Executive conclusion

Develop **Rich Orchard** first, but retain the simpler control. The ideal lead is an original pear-led/apple-crisp perfume with a layered ester/peel construction, a small osmanthus/petal seam, a pear-associated musk bridge, and subordinate pale woods. The PW-only lead is a separate buildable purchasing hypothesis because genuine **ethyl (E,Z)-2,4-decadienoate** was not found in the public PW catalog/search checked.

There is no inventory restriction in this campaign. This follows the user's latest instruction: treat materials as procurements, not as owned/unavailable bottles. No inventory gap questionnaire, lot/photo demand, or historical stock assumption is used.

The design is not a clone of Pera Granita or English Pear & Freesia, and is not a tested consumer winner. Its value is a precise architecture, a costed supplier route, four genuinely different challengers, a simpler control, and practical comparisons that can remove complexity that does not earn its place.

## A. Locked sensory strategy

Mode: **creative target-first, new formula, development proposal**. Claim: a literature-informed, supplier-bounded pear/apple hypothesis that can be tested, not demonstrated superiority.

Protected recognizers:

- Pear first; apple gives bite, peel and juiciness.
- A fruit identity that should remain recognizable through the early heart.
- Fine texture without a larger floral bouquet.
- Soft fruit-to-musk/wood continuity without a heavy amber/gourmand tail.
- Immediate mainstream readability, with detail revealed at close smelling.

Negative space: tropical fruit soup, pear-drop candy, shampoo, obvious rose/jasmine/ylang, leafy vegetables, peach/coconut cream, dry masculine-leaning wood dominance, giant amberwoods, large Ambrox/Cashmeran, vanilla/ethyl maltol, syrupy sweetness.

Richness is assessed separately from intensity, sweetness, darkness and ingredient count. A heavier or stronger trial does not win automatically.

### Lessons from the references

[Pera Granita EDT](https://www.guerlain.com/us/en-us/p/aqua-allegoria-pera-granita---eau-de-toilette-P014403.html) supplies a useful public architecture: fresh citrus and pear linked to an osmanthus/apricot seam and a musk/wood foundation. [English Pear & Freesia Cologne](https://www.jomalone.com/product/25946/12553/colognes/english-pear-freesia-cologne) supplies clarity, transparent flower support and restrained patchouli.

These are official marketed descriptions, not proprietary formula disclosures, analytical profiles, exact material quantities, or evidence that CHIMIE smells similar. Reference concentration, edition and potential reformulation differences remain explicit.

## B. Gap analysis — procurement functions, not owned inventory

The current-inventory build requested earlier is intentionally replaced by a **from-scratch supplier build** under the latest instruction.

The important gaps to solve are:

1. Literal pear specificity: genuine (E,Z) Pear Ester, if sourced.
2. A peel/juice/flesh distinction, not a single “apple base.”
3. Ripe apple volume without pineapple/banana drift.

### Research findings that changed the design

- [Bartlett pear aroma-active research](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27935705/) supports testing characteristic pear esters and separate fresh ester facets. Fruit/brandy findings do not set perfume percentages.
- [Elstar/Cox Orange character-impact research](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12108219/) shows cultivar-dependent ester/aldehyde profiles. There is no universal one-chemical apple solution.
- [The 2025 apple sensomics study](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/abs/10.1021/acs.jafc.4c12028) supports recombination/omission as an experimental method. It does not justify transferring the fruit's whole chemical list into a perfume.
- [The osmanthus release study](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/chemistry/articles/10.3389/fchem.2024.1381835/full) used **1,2-propanediol/ethanol**, not DPG. It supports a bounded warning that nonvolatile matrix components can alter release and odor; its coefficients are not used for this EDP.
- [The Helvetolide manufacturer sheet](https://www.firmenich.com/sites/default/files/uploads/files/ingredients/marketing-sheet/perfumery/HELVETOLIDE_947650.pdf) supports pear/ambrette facets and 2–11% fine-fragrance formulation guidance. This is not a safety maximum or proof of recognizable pear at four hours.
- [Bedoukian's true Pear Ester specification](https://bedoukian.com/wp-content/uploads/FL-433-spec-sheet.pdf) supports exact (2E,4Z) identity and characteristic pear. Its flavor ppm are not perfume dosage instructions.

No literature-derived OAV is used as perceived contribution, intensity, pleasantness or beauty. No gas concentrations, response curves or personal liking labels are applicable to these proposed whole perfumes.

### Important catalog corrections

- **Fructone B — 3FG00196 — CAS 6290-17-1** is not standard Fructone, CAS 6413-10-1. PW describes a warmer/cooked/fermented fruit direction. It becomes a small optional trial, not the pear/apple cornerstone.
- **Manzanate — 4FG15438 — CAS 39255-32-8** is not ethyl 2-methylbutyrate, CAS 7452-79-1. Keep the rows distinct.
- **Phenyl Ethyl Dimethyl Carbinyl Acetate — 3LR00160** is not its corresponding alcohol, ordinary phenethyl acetate, or dimethyl benzyl carbinyl butyrate.
- **Liffarome and Leafovert** have matching supplier CAS/alias listings. Do not buy both as separate complementary effects. Exact grade equivalence is not granted solely by CAS; Leafovert is a cheaper possible replacement after product/grade confirmation.
- Habanolide and Ambrettolide are macrocyclic musks; Helvetolide and Romandolide are alicyclic ester musks. “Four macrocyclic musks” would be wrong.
- Supplier strip-life numbers are not whole-perfume drydown measurements. Zero/TBA density/physics entries remain unknown.
- “FCF” does not clear the full formula's phototoxicity/allergen/safety assessment by itself.

### Genuine Pear Ester sourcing gap

Searches covered Pear Ester, ethyl decadienoate, ethyl 2,4-decadienoate, ethyl trans-2/cis-4-decadienoate and CAS **3025-30-7**, plus the full cached listing. No matching public PW SKU was found. Ask PW for an exact-product quote/special order. Do not substitute a **decadienal**, a different geometric-isomer product, Fructone B, Manzanate or Helvetolide under the same identity.

The ideal starts at **0.05%** of this material, with proposed 0.01/0.05/0.15% range tests. The PW-only trial omits it and closes 0.05% mass with ELA. That is an intentionally disclosed **non-equivalent trial**, not an ideal formulation secretly altered by purchasing convenience.

## F. Candidate families, chassis and Pareto view

There is no accepted parent scent or measured CHIMIE target to “prove” a chassis against. Parent is **null**. The control is a new comparator, not already accepted ground truth.

The proposed chassis protects pear/apple recognition, a transparent heart, and a subordinate musk/wood tail. Mobile sockets are the green micro-detail, ripeness modifiers, natural texture and musk balance. Removing a socket is a test, not an assumed equivalence.

| Commercial Clear — 28 products | 15% hexyl acetate, 18% ELA, reduced flesh/natural detail, crisp apple support | Fast fruit readability and accessibility | More estery, flatter, or functional/shampoo-like |

## I. 30 mL EDP plan and concentrate form

PW-only version:

1. Prepare a homogeneous **5.000 g undiluted-material-equivalent working compound** using the table's weighed stock amounts. Actual charge is **14.225 g**, including **9.225 g** ethanol carrier.
2. Add the same perfumery-grade alcohol, mix, rest/equilibrate and **q.s. to measured 30.00 mL** at a stable recorded temperature.
3. Record finished mass if reporting w/w concentration. Do not prescribe “add 24 mL” or treat 5 g as 5 mL.

This is **16.6667% w/v product-equivalent loading**, not a claimed 20% w/w or v/v. It is not exactly the same loading convention as the 18% w/w small trials. If exact strength replication matters, determine the final blend density from a small measured aliquot and scale by mass; no supplier/default density is invented.

Concentrate is also given independently as 1000 mass parts. At a 10 g-equivalent bench scale, the carrier-containing working compound weighs **28.450 g**. It is not a neat 10 g perfume oil; volatile dilution carrier cannot be magically removed. For storage and scale-up, a less diluted manufacturing stock scheme can be versioned after trace-weighing and solubility capability are known.

No complete micro-litre table is forced from zero/TBA supplier densities. Weighing avoids the conversion. Where a verified stock density is available, use **µL = 1000 × mass_g / density_g_mL**; never use an undisclosed 1 g/mL default.

## J. Mixing order

Proposed bench order, not a claim it has been carried out:

1. Label/cap vessels and verify all intended new working stocks are homogeneous.
2. Combine musk and pale wood products.
3. Add Hedione grades and restrained transparent heart materials.
4. Add weighed natural-texture stocks and body/flesh modifiers.
5. Add ELA and citrus oils.
6. Add volatile juice esters and green/aldehyde stocks last; cap promptly.
7. Mix thoroughly, let equilibrate, inspect clarity, and only then take trial aliquots or finish dilution.

Work ventilated, protect eyes/skin, and avoid heating/open flames around alcohol. Follow material SDS handling. Stop for clouding, precipitation or suspected stock error rather than dosing an inhomogeneous stock.

## K. Working-stock preparation

These are **new preparations**, not assumptions about existing bottles.

- **10% w/w:** 0.200 g undiluted product + 1.800 g same ethanol = 2.000 g.
- **1% w/w:** 0.200 g homogeneous 10% stock + 1.800 g ethanol = 2.000 g.
- **0.1% w/w:** 0.200 g homogeneous 1% stock + 1.800 g ethanol = 2.000 g.
- To avoid a large powerful-material intermediate, make 0.200 g of 10% stock from 0.020 g material + 0.180 g ethanol, then dilute serially.

Use a balance with 1 mg readability or better; record actual masses. Readability is not calibrated accuracy. Label name, w/w fraction, carrier and preparation date. No photograph/invoice/lot questionnaire is needed for this creative stage.

If an absolute does not make a homogeneous ethanol stock, do not use it anyway. Make a separately documented carrier-compatible version, recalculate carrier totals and matrix-match the comparison. Do not silently import a seller's “10%” as w/w when the basis is unspecified.

### Richness rubric — no invented numerical ratings

Assess separately: fruit dimensionality; apple and pear realism; peel; flesh/juice; fruit persistence; natural texture; floral restraint; transition quality; musk and woody integration; smoothness; sweetness; shampoo/body-spray risk; luxury impression; immediate liking; and mainstream readability.

Initial expectations are hypotheses: Rich Orchard should offer more facet detail; Commercial Clear should be easier to read; Luxury Polished should be smoother but may obscure fruit; Control may be preferred. Persistence, luxury and liking are **unmeasured** in every case.

## M. Expected failure modes and safety boundary

- **Green/vegetal:** reduce/remove the leaf alcohol, Liffarome or excess green ester, one block at a time.
- **Shampoo:** inspect Verdox, muguet/watery support, and musk/green balance; do not simply add another fruit base.
- **Peach/apricot:** omit Apritone and/or tiny lactone before blaming pear dosage; then check osmanthus.
- **Tropical/candy:** reduce the specific high-impact ester/modifier causing drift, rather than darkening the base.
- **Floral takeover:** inspect HC/muguet support and natural texture; do not add more woods as a generic antidote.
- **Fruit disappears:** distinguish volatile depletion from sensory masking. More fixative does not guarantee retained pear identity.
- **Metallic clean musk:** compare the paired Habanolide/Ambrettolide version.
- **Too soft/blurred:** reduce the masking socket only after omission evidence.
- **Complex only on paper:** use the control and discard unhelpful rows.

For a commercial candidate, evaluate the **complete finished formula** under Category 4 using current supplier SDS/CoA/IFRA information, including combined rose-ketone restrictions, allergens/oxidation in citrus and flower naturals, trace materials, exact trade products, and any proprietary base.

The [IFRA rose-ketone standard](https://ifrafragrance.org/standards/IFRA_STD_077.pdf) applies a combined finished-product limit, not individual aesthetic usage suggestions. Added damascenone alone contributes **0.0009%** in the 18% w/w trials; this isolated calculation is **not full-formula clearance**.
