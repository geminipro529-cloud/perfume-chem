## 2. Best combination families

### A. Cassis harvest

Best shape:
- `Blackcurrant FTEC + Dewberry FTEC + Raspberry Ketone + Paradisamide + Beta Ionone`

Why it works:
- `Blackcurrant FTEC` gives the sulfurous cassis core.
- `Dewberry FTEC` softens the edge and adds dark berry-green body.
- `Raspberry Ketone` keeps the berry readable instead of purely catty.
- `Paradisamide` carries the berry memory into drydown.
- `Beta Ionone` bridges berry into violet/iris territory and reinforces cassis identity.

Best shape:
- `Blackcurrant FTEC + Grapefruit FCF + Bergamot FCF + Galbanum/Leafovert + Evernyl + Paradisamide`

## 3. Best bridge materials

These are the materials that help berry feel like part of a perfume, not just a fruit note.

- `Beta Ionone` — best berry-to-violet bridge
- `Dihydro Beta Ionone` — warmer, rounder berry bridge
- `Apritone` — stone-fruit bridge into florals, chypres, woods, and saffron
- `Ethyl 2-Methylbutyrate` — pear/apple/juicy tart bridge
- `Cyclamen Aldehyde` — pear-skin / watery floral bridge
- `Helional` — cool pear/water bridge
- `Methyl Pamplemousse` — tart grapefruit-juice bridge
- `Grapefruit FCF` and `Cedrat FCF` — dry citrus frame for berry-chypre work
- `Leafovert` and `Parmavert` — leafy green bridges into cassis leaf and violet leaf
- `Aurantiol` — fruit to blossom bridge when fruit is being pushed into floral territory

## 4. Avoid / mismatch notes

- `Peonile` can clash with cassis sulfur and was explicitly removed from one berry build.
- Too much citrus makes berry read like cologne or juice instead of cassis.
- Too much green makes berry read leafy and bitter instead of fruity.
- Too much `Raspberry Ketone` can make the result candy-like or too clean.
- Too much `Paradisamide` can drag berry toward tropical-guava territory.
- `Undecavertol` is useful for aquatic-fruit, but not the best fit for dark cassis work.
- `Allyl Amyl Glycolate` is more tropical-green/pineapple than true berry.

## Practical ranking

Best for dark berry luxury:
- `Blackcurrant FTEC + Dewberry FTEC + Raspberry Ketone + Paradisamide + Beta Ionone`

Best for wearable mass-appeal berry:
- `Apritone + berry quartet + soft musks`

Best for niche green cassis:
- `Blackcurrant FTEC + Leafovert / Galbanum + Evernyl + Paradisamide`

Best for creamy berry:
- `lactones + Ethyl 2-Methylbutyrate + Paradisamide`

Worst fit for your current blackcurrant-chypre experiments:
- `pear-heavy or watery-fruity patterns`
- `too much green without fruit`
- `too much tropical-green sparkle`
