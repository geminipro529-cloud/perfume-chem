**What this unlocks:** Full damask rose, rose-oud, rose-amber, any rose-centered composition with depth beyond PEA/Citronellol linearity.

### 1C. Ylang / Exotic Tropical Floral
**Currently impossible.** You have zero exotic-floral materials.

| Material | Character | Role in accord |

### 1D. Honey Accord
**Currently impossible.** No phenylacetic acid or honey-character molecules.

### 1F. Animalic Depth (beyond IBQ)

| Material | Synergy with | Effect |
|----------|-------------|--------|

### 2D. Musk Portfolio Depth

| Material | Synergy with | Effect |
|----------|-------------|--------|
| **Romandolide** ⚠️ Priority Zero | Habanolide, Galaxolide, Ambrettolide | Each macrocyclic has a character: Habanolide=clean, Galaxolide=sweet-laundry, Ambrettolide=skin. | **Muscone (synthetic)** | Ambrettolide (10%) | Muscone is the actual deer-musk molecule (synthetic). Combined with Ambrettolide, creates the only truly "natural-reading" musk blend possible with synthetics. The animalic-warm facet your musk palette lacks |

### 2E. Woody-Amber Molecular Depth

### 2F. Orange Blossom Completion

|----------|-------------|--------|
| **Methyl Anthranilate** | Neroli EO, Linalool, Linalyl Acetate | Neroli EO alone = citrus-floral-green. Adding Methyl Anthranilate = orange blossom absolute effect. This molecule is what separates neroli from orange blossom in a perfumer's palette |

## 4. Everything Else — Luxury & Niche Materials

### 4A. Natural Absolutes — The Raw Materials That Define Niche

These are what separate a $25 designer fragrance from a $350 niche one. Even small amounts of naturals in a primarily synthetic formula add complexity, depth, and that "living" quality synthetics approximate but never fully replicate.

| Material | Character | Price tier | What it adds to your palette |
|----------|-----------|-----------|------|
| **Jasmine Grandiflorum Absolute** | Rich, narcotic, indolic-honeyed jasmine | $$$ (~$80-120/10mL) | Your Jasmine FO and Benzyl Acetate approximate jasmine. The absolute adds 400+ molecular components that create depth, warmth, and the narcotic "living flower" quality. Even 50-100 µL in a formula transforms it |
| **Rose Absolute (Centifolia or Damascena)** | Deep, honeyed, jammy rose | $$$ (~$60-100/10mL) | Completes the rose palette far beyond Citronellol/Geraniol/PEA. The honeyed-waxy depth of natural rose is not reproducible synthetically |
| **Ylang Ylang EO Extra** | Exotic, creamy, jasmine-banana | $$ (~$20-40/10mL) | The exotic-floral anchor material. Used in Chanel No. 5, Joy, and every serious oriental floral. Your inventory has zero exotic florals |
| **Osmanthus Absolute** | Leather-apricot-tea, dried-fruit floral | $$$$ (~$150-200/10mL) | Unique material with no synthetic substitute. Creates osmanthus accords and deepens tea/leather compositions |
| **Tuberose Absolute** | Heavy, narcotic, buttery white floral | $$$$ (~$200-300/10mL) | The ultimate narcotic floral — used sparingly at 20-50 µL, it adds a depth of white-floral character that Methyl Benzoate alone can't achieve |
