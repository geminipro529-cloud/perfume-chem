# Formulation knowledge integration — 5 October 2026

## Outcome and boundary

Reviewed iris/violet and broader formulation research now reaches Formula
Studio and goal-directed analysis as local, inspectable design guidance. It
does not become a measured sensory model, a stock declaration, an optimizer
beauty objective or action authority. No formula, inventory, bottle or
scientific-admission ledger was changed by this integration.

There are two intentionally different objects:

| Object | Contents | Runtime status |
|---|---|---|
| `data/formulation_knowledge/literature_v1.json` | 31 source records, 36 bounded claims, 11 architecture profiles, 11 exact material-role records | Facts, manufacturer descriptions and labeled design hypotheses |
| `data/formulation_knowledge/prior_research_corpus_v1.json` | 128 prior local records with source-byte hashes, titles and deterministic text-derived topic tags | Searchable references only; not automatically reviewed/admitted claims |

The prior corpus is a frozen pre-integration census of `knowledge/`,
`docs/research/`, `data/source_manifests/` and the existing
`future_modules/literature_references.py`. It includes available CHIMIE Femme,
lavender, measured-intensity, hedonic, commercial-reference and recent
fragrance-reconstruction research. It does not claim to recover missing
ChatGPT threads or to freshly adjudicate every paper cited in those files.
This new integration report is not part of that earlier snapshot.

## Structural review and repairs

1. The former `iris_violet` umbrella could make violet leaf a powder-flower
   requirement. Specific profiles now distinguish root/mineral iris, butter/wax
   iris, cosmetic iris, transparent iris, woody iris, violet petals, violet
   powder and violet leaf. The umbrella remains a compatibility vocabulary
   fallback for genuinely unspecific requests.
2. `rooty` previously required vetiver even in an iris brief. Iris context now
   suppresses that inference; an explicit vetiver request still survives.
   Mineral iris alone does not force an earthy/camphoraceous accent.
3. A single-family material limit prevented iris recognizer and root texture
   from working together. The solver now allows distinct reviewed iris slots,
   not arbitrary family duplication. Alternate stocks of one identity still
   cannot fill several rows. Multiple iris profiles share one recognizer role.
4. Supplier grades and stock preparations remain separate. AIMI is not an
   alias for Gamma Coeur, and generic "Methyl Ionone" does not bind a grade.
   Natural orris, assay percentages, commercial bases and prepared irone
   solutions do not inherit one another's identity or calibration.
5. The historical OR5AN1 ionone ceiling and family-based incompatibility
   explanation are withdrawn from governing guidance and quarantined in the
   new pack. The broad historical tables are explicitly non-authoritative.
6. Copilot's mandatory liquid-ppm/OAV objective, assumed density and prohibition
   on grams contradicted current accounting and endpoint rules. Those clauses
   now preserve mass/volume separation and compatible-phase diagnostics.

Architecture shares and existing component-profile weights are still design
heuristics. Their presence in a solver does not make them literature-fitted
coefficients. There are no newly admitted dose, threshold, release, receptor,
intensity, pleasantness or personal-liking calibrations in this pack.

## Source review

Fresh primary/manufacturer checks were used for iris stereoisomers,
beta-ionone sensitivity, violet emissions, specific ionone/Orivone product
descriptions, the orris assay distinction, Helvetolide's pear facet, the
Wakayama correction, apple/pear aroma context, mixture challengers and
monomolecular response variability. Other records are explicitly tagged as
prior local reviews rather than freshly reverified publications.

Key evidence and bounded interpretations:

- [Brenna, Fuganti and Serra, irone stereoisomers](https://comptes-rendus.academie-sciences.fr/chimie/articles/en/10.1016/S1631-0748(03)00087-0/): retain stereochemical/grade distinctions; do not equate all iris products.
- [Jaeger et al., beta-ionone sensitivity](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23910657/): OR5A1-related variation, not a universal formulation ceiling or a fabricated receptor map.
- [Keene et al., Viola odorata emissions](https://journals.ashs.org/downloadpdf/view/journals/hortsci/59/7/article-p974.pdf): botanical temporal/cultivar context, not perfume proportions.
- [IFF Orivone](https://www.iff.com/scent/ingredients-compendium/orivone/): earthy/camphoraceous orris description, not universal buttery-iris equivalence.
- [IFF Gamma Coeur](https://www.iff.com/scent/ingredients-compendium/meth-ionone-gamma-coeur/), [IFF Alpha Extra](https://www.iff.com/scent/ingredients-compendium/meth-ionone-alpha-extra/) and [Givaudan Dihydro Ionone Beta](https://www.givaudan.com/fragrance-beauty/fragrance-ingredients-business/fragrance-molecules/dihydro-ionone-beta): grade-specific vocabulary, not substitutions decided by CAS alone.
- [Biolandes orris butter product sheet](https://www.biolandes.com/wp-content/uploads/fiche-technique-E2515.pdf): extract irone assay is not working-stock dilution. Only metadata and an original bounded summary are retained.
- [Wakayama correction](https://pubs.acs.org/doi/10.1021/acs.iecr.0c05822): derived threshold and forward curve remain separate, with explicit gas units. Existing capability machinery remains the calibration authority.
- [Pellegrino intensity preprint](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12363845/): mixture rules remain challengers rather than universal perfume rules.
- [Mixture-character preprint](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13371102/): a simple baseline is worth testing in the studied small-mixture domain; neither its empirical data nor a model is commercially admitted here. Its CC-BY-NC scope is recorded.
- [Apple cultivar aroma](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12108219/) and [pear/brandy aroma](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27935705/): layered fruit and context distinctions, not food-to-EDP dose transfer.
- [Bierling et al.](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-025-04644-2): population/individual uncertainty, not finished-perfume liking predictions.

Publication access is not a dataset or commercial-model license. Standard
scope identifiers are metadata only; no standards text is reproduced. External
source pages are linked and review-dated, not falsely represented as locally
stored, content-hashed HTML/PDF evidence. Exact local source records are
hash-bound and source drift withholds their dependent guidance.

## Runtime wiring

```text
Reviewed pack + reference-only corpus
  ├─ semantic brief: specific profiles, protected request and separate knowledge hashes
  ├─ material capability index: exact-grade vocabulary and claim/role references
  ├─ solver: separate recognizer/texture slots with unchanged stock arithmetic
  ├─ critic: target-linked clue and uncalibrated-hypothesis limitation
  ├─ goal analysis: bounded source clues, after observations/applicable endpoints
  └─ Formula Studio: optional research details and explicit-click source links
```

The original request remains user evidence; literature is not relabeled as user
intent. Avoid/preserve constraints and ineligible-action gates remain in control.
Curated, generic, withheld and evolving-bottle design paths return a knowledge
receipt. Specific iris/violet requests use the generic role solver rather than
silently reverting to an unrelated curated capsule.

Durable FORMULA_DESIGN and FORMULA_ANALYSIS jobs include the new implementation,
pack and corpus in their fingerprints. Current bytes of indexed research and
local claim sources are also bound; changing an original file cannot reuse an
old result merely because its corpus record still contains yesterday's hash.
Reference paths are server-owned, root-scoped and read-only, never execution
targets. Traversal and unregistered paths fail closed.

## User experience

Examples: "rooty mineral iris, no vanilla", "transparent iris with neroli",
"violet petals, not leaf", or "make the pear flesh richer without more flowers".
The user receives a target-linked clue and a design/comparison hypothesis.
Research details expand only when wanted. No photos, invoices, new questionnaires,
web searches or physical experiments are introduced into normal analysis.
