## Primary-source review before implementation

- [IFF neroli oil](https://www.iff.com/scent/lmr-compendium/neroli-oil-tunisia/)
  and [orange flower absolute](https://www.iff.com/scent/lmr-compendium/orange-flower-absolute-tunisia/):
  blossom hydrodistillate and solvent extract are different products. Fresh-green
  versus warmer honeyed framing is a comparison hypothesis, not equal-dose substitution.
- [IFF tuberose](https://www.iff.com/scent/lmr-compendium/tuberose-abs-india-traceable/):
  the described absolute has green, spicy and solar facets. This does not identify
  the user's lot or prove a transparent support will increase projection.
- [Champaca primary study](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12864-017-3846-8):
  sampled fully opened flowers, headspace GC-MS and biosynthesis work support
  separate terpenic and ester-associated hypotheses, not perfume proportions.
- [Lilium study abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/31645964/): cultivar
  diversity prevents one universal lily identity; abstract access is not full
  reconstruction or quantitative-data admission.
- [Florol](https://studio.dsm-firmenich.com/product/florolr-pe-966458),
  [violet leaf absolute](https://www.iff.com/scent/lmr-compendium/violet-leaf-absolute-egypt/),
  [Orivone](https://www.iff.com/scent/ingredients-compendium/orivone/) and
  [broom absolute](https://www.iff.com/scent/lmr-compendium/broom-absolute-italy/):
  manufacturer descriptions support distinct soft-floral, leaf, root and
  honey/hay/fruit textures. No grades, physical properties or stock aliases are
  inferred from those categories. Existing source receipts remain unchanged.

The review uses selected sections and prior locally hashed source reviews, not
a claim to have reread every full text or completed a registry-wide retraction
search. No source data, numerical doses or publisher tables are imported.
Fresh retrieval failures remain retrieval failures, not evidence of absence.

## Acceptance boundary

Software success means evidence-bound, distinguishable, reproducible role plans
and truthful gaps. Different stock selections do not prove better scent, correct
named-fruit recognition, longevity, liking or safety. Exact CHIMIE L'HOMME
campaign identity remains withheld until its accepted brief/formula is available.
