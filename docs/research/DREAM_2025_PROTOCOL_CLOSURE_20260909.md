# DREAM 2025 protocol closure for Gin Vetiver EDP

Date: 2026-09-09. Scope: admission to this version's numerical formula selection, not a general rejection of the dataset.

## Resolved preparation fact

The official challenge preparation discussion explicitly answers that prepared component solutions are combined in equal liquid volumes:

> When preparing the mixtures, each individual molecule is combined in equal volumes.

Source: https://www.synapse.org/Synapse:syn64743570/discussion/threadId=12185

The parent engineer retrieved and read this primary discussion. The question describes the component dilution numbers as weight fractions in their respective solvents. The response does not correct that interpretation, but it does not explicitly certify those units either. Consequently, component-solution weight fraction is an operational interpretation with medium confidence, while equal-volume combination is directly confirmed.

For equal stock volumes, active mass is proportional to component stock mass fraction times stock-solution density. Unknown stock densities prevent exact reconstruction of final active mass fractions. No complete delivery/headspace protocol was recovered in this bounded review. These are remaining data limitations, not claims that the experiments were defective.

## Final admission for the current perfume

The local concentration-variant audit identifies eight same-solvent-assignment identity groups with 30 aggregate observations. Equal component-stock volumes are now known; this corrects the earlier unresolved preparation-ratio question. It does not establish a validated concentration-to-perception model for an 18-stock natural/synthetic ethanol EDP, or measured endpoints for its richness, layering and target identity.

Disposition: **EXCLUDED from numerical candidate selection in this version**. Retain the downloaded data for research and possible concentration-awareness diagnostics. Do not treat a dataset-domain mismatch as evidence that a particular perfume dose is bad. Do not turn an unvalidated numerical prediction into a preferred formula.

No new physical mixing prerequisite is introduced. The bounded run may end with **NO_SUPPORTED_CHANGE**; that is not proof of optimality or completion of a validated full-perfume hedonic model.
