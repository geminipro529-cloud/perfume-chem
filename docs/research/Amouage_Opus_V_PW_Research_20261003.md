# Opus V PW R1 — Research, design decisions and work-order record

Date: 3 October 2026. Root Codex reasoning, local source review and primary/official web research. No delegated provider was used, no raw prompts sent to another LLM, no physical experiment or purchase executed.

## Executive decision and exact claim

Select **Root-Rum Lead (L1)** for the first small evaluation, not as a demonstrated closest clone. Its rationale is simultaneous coverage of the marketed iris, rhum, quiet flowers, dry wood and secondary oud/civet axes. The prior local sheets are comparators, not authenticated Amouage formulas. Brand notes constrain an architecture; they do not reveal raw materials or proportions.

Reference: [Amouage current Opus V Woods Symphony EDP](https://amouage.com/en-ca/products/opus-v-woods-symphony). The [23 November 2023 brand FAQ](https://intercom.help/amouage/en/articles/6281737-the-library-collection-faqs) credits Jacques Cavallier and describes the relaunch as packaging/name changes. However, its 20% oil/2+4 week ageing information differs from the current 24%/3+3 week page. This is preserved as a dated documentary conflict, not used to assert all original and current batches are identical.

Our default is 24% **w/w named-source-product equivalents**, independent of the unspecified original concentration basis. Whole-product weights and added stock carriers are exact design arithmetic; molecular purity, natural authenticity, delivered gas, consumer liking and sensory match are not thereby established.

## Primary literature and what it changes

1. [Brenna, Fuganti and Serra (2003), irone stereoisomers](https://comptes-rendus.academie-sciences.fr/chimie/articles/10.1016/S1631-0748(03)00087-0/), DOI 10.1016/S1631-0748(03)00087-0. Publisher full text read. Irone isomers/enantiomers and natural iris grades have differing odor properties. **Design consequence:** retain the supplied grade identity; use natural-sounding root products, a separate alpha-irone accent and ionone body for distinct roles, not as molecular equivalents. This paper supplies no Opus V percentages and no assay for our products.

2. [Pino et al. (2012), aged rum odor-active compounds](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29243633/), DOI 10.1016/j.foodchem.2011.11.133. Primary abstract reviewed; publisher full text inaccessible. The reported rum system combines fermentation esters, norisoprenoid, phenolic and oak-derived accents. **Design consequence:** a rum facet need not be a maple/benzoin accord. Its thresholds were evaluated in 35% v/v ethanol, so we do not transfer them into perfume gas-phase OAVs.

3. [Franitza, Granvogl and Schieberle (2016), rum production and recombination](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/27788585/), DOI 10.1021/acs.jafc.6b04046. Primary abstract reviewed; publisher full text inaccessible. The study links changes in ethyl esters and wood-derived odorants with rum processing and reports aroma recombination. **Design consequence:** small ester/dried-fruit/cask contrasts are testable; this is not evidence that our selected perfume quantities reconstruct beverage rum or Opus V.

4. [Pripdeevech, Khummueng and Park (2011), Thai agarwood GC/MS and GC-O](https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/10412905.2011.9700468), DOI 10.1080/10412905.2011.9700468. Publisher indexed abstract reviewed, not the full article. Different Aquilaria oils have distinct chemical and odor-active profiles. **Design consequence:** cypriol, an artificial oud base and a named agarwood oil are not interchangeable identities. No study oil is bound to the present PW lot.

No downloaded dataset, trained model, external analytical formula, measured receptor map or sensory superiority score was admitted. No copyrighted standards text is reproduced. Correction/retraction status was not exhaustively audited; these sources guide an exploratory design, not empirical model/dataset admission.

## Supplier findings that materially changed the design

- [Orris Butter 8IA00344](https://www.perfumersworld.com/view.php?pro_id=8IA00344) is listed in stock at $21.50/g and marketed as a root/butter material, but its [generated documentation](https://www.perfumersworld.com/document-list.php?pro_id=8IA00344&tab=coa) describes a proprietary compound. We use the exact named product at product-mass level, **not certified natural butter of a known irone percentage**.
- [Orris Liquid 8IQ24653](https://www.perfumersworld.com/view.php?pro_id=8IQ24653) supplies a different waxy/floral/cosmetic profile. Its name, generic CAS and supplier text do not establish an exact natural species, lot assay or molecular equivalence to irone.
- [Caribbean Rum Extract 8FY24655](https://www.perfumersworld.com/view.php?pro_id=8FY24655) is listed at $8.80/g. Its [generated documentation](https://www.perfumersworld.com/document-list.php?pro_id=8FY24655&tab=coa) says proprietary, alcohol-free compound. Its raisin/molasses/tropical/cask description is a **supplier claim**, not an authenticated single natural extract. Keep its quantity low and include an omission.
- [Civet Art 3UU07513](https://www.perfumersworld.com/view.php?pro_id=3UU07513) is an in-stock synthetic proprietary compound ($4/g), not pure civetone. A bounded base exception is justified by the explicit civet reference axis and the absence of a verified purchasable pure-civetone listing in the targeted catalog/site search. PW history/training mentions of civetone are not a product availability record.
- [Agarwood Oil Siam 7WU09890](https://www.perfumersworld.com/view.php?pro_id=7WU09890), $25/g, is claimed natural by its supplier. This is not independent authentication or same-lot matching to Amouage.
- [Irotyl 3IA06874](https://www.perfumersworld.com/view.php?pro_id=3IA06874) is **ethyl 2-ethylhexanoate, CAS 2983-37-1**, an ester—not another irone. Its role is an early fatty/fruity/root bridge. Do not label it a durable irone calibration.
- Orivone was considered; the current page shows an anomalous price unit “/0.” It is not assigned a valid $/g purchase price. Its current owned row does not by itself make it the best substitute for natural root texture.
- Genuine oak/whisky lactone and sotolon were not found as verified purchasable listings in the targeted current PW search. They are not invented SKUs or declared absent from every possible special-order channel. Maple Lactone is not a chemically equivalent replacement and is currently not owned.

The search used the complete cached catalog for discovery and fresh pages for selected materials; it was **not a fresh examination of every catalog product**. Supplier descriptor percentages, strip life and impact indexes are not compound composition, measured perfume intensity, or skin longevity.

## Why this is different from the old local Opus V sheet

### Alternatives considered rather than defaulting to workhorses

- **Iris:** AIMI is the body, alpha irone the sharper identity accent, and dihydro-beta-ionone the dry wood connection. Beta Ionone, Allyl Ionone and Ultralia are omitted from the lead to avoid extra violet/fruit/floral haze; they are not declared universally worse. Irotyl is deliberately retained only for an early fatty/fruity role, not credited with persistent root realism. Orris Butter and Liquid are distinct product-texture hypotheses, subject to the supplier-composition caveats above.
- **Rhum:** trace esters/cask accents have source-supported functions in rum, but their perfume quantities are authored. Maple Lactone, ethyl maltol, heavy benzoin/myrrh, ethyl vanillin and actual drinking rum are omitted because they would bias toward sweetness/resin, inappropriate water, or unknown beverage constituents. Whisky lactone/sotolon are sourcing gaps, not silently substituted by Maple Lactone. The named PW rum compound is a bounded product-level exception with an omission control, not evidence of natural authenticity.
- **Flowers:** ordinary Hedione, rather than automatically combining Hedione and HC, supplies restrained space behind low rose/jasmine. No added muguet, ylang, tuberose or neroli bouquet is required by the locked Opus brief. No claim that HC is intrinsically inferior is made.
- **Woods:** Iso E supplies abstract space; Vertofix supplies denser grain; the small natural/acetylated-root materials supply actual texture rather than making Iso E the only wood. Javanol/Ebanol/Bacdanol are omitted to avoid a sandalwood-led redesign. Large Amber Xtreme/Norlimbanol/Ambermax loads are not a remedy for thin iris. Kephalis and Ambrox remain modest wood/mineral hypotheses, not generic longevity guarantees.
- **Musks:** Habanolide's cleaner lifted edge and Ambrettolide's warmer skin/fruity bridge are separately hypothesized; neither is asserted necessary before the equal-total single-musk tests. Helvetolide would introduce a pear association, Ethylene Brassylate more creamy bulk, and Galaxolide more laundry-clean mass; these directions are not required here. Romandolide/Exaltolide remain possible later alternatives, not compulsory extra rows. Depleted Tonalide, Macrolide and Musk Ketone are omitted.
- **Animality:** Civet Art is a bounded exception for the explicitly named civet axis. IBQ/Suederal describe leather, castoreum another animalic register, and skatole a potent fecal accent; none is a same-dose identity replacement. No pure-civetone product was verified for purchase, so no imaginary PW SKU is used.

The June sheet explicitly repeats AIMI, uses Alpha Irone “30% IPM” rather than the current 10% w/w DEP stock, and treats a 33% Ambrox volume dose as an exact active volume. It uses maple/benzoin/myrrh as its rhum construction, IBQ as civet, and asserts box-allergen and perfumer-signature matches without an authenticated complete formula. The present design:

- Aggregates one AIMI physical identity instead of obtaining extra “depth” from duplicate rows.
- Separates each product target from working-stock carriers and weighs solids.
- Uses dry fermentation/cask hypotheses rather than a sugar/resin rum shortcut.
- Distinguishes civet from leather and oud from cypriol.
- Avoids filling every disclosed allergen with a separate row; ingredient presence cannot reveal its original source or dose.
- Removes automatic citrus, muguet, four-musk, vanilla/tonka and amberwood expansion.
- Preserves the old files rather than relabeling them validated or overwriting history.

The newer 95-row chemclass sheet is also not an automatic richness or accuracy standard. A paper-rich roster, a generic OAV model or a “Cavallier signature” name cannot replace smell and exact reference evidence.

## Work-order deliverables

### 1. Locked brief/reference and claim

Dry floral-woody iris/rhum, contrasting floral-waxy and buttery-root facets, faint rose/jasmine, secondary oud/civet. Protect that contrast. No demonstrated clone, measured similarity or consumer preference claim. The accepted physical reference bottle/batch remains unbound.

### 2. Evidence and identity matrix

The canonical JSON has all **36** exact PW names/SKUs, chemical/product classes, roles, failure modes, stock fractions/carriers and source links. Natural/proprietary internal constituents remain an unknown remainder, not invented molecules. Product labels do not become receptor maps or curve aliases. Source quantity authority is FORMULATOR_SELECTED_NOT_RECONSTRUCTED.

### 3. Active/product target with uncertainty

The target totals 100.00 product parts; chemically pure odorant total is not asserted. Exact compounds use their supplied trade grade, and mixtures retain whole-product identity. The 36 selected quantities are first-test hypotheses, not point estimates of a hidden commercial formula. Sensitive ranges for later separate tests: named rum product 0/0.6/1.2%, civet product 0/0.05/0.15/0.30%, and butter product 1/2.5/4%. These are design ranges, not confidence intervals or validated maximums.

### 5. Chassis/module analysis

Core: AIMI/irone/ionone/root products plus the restrained dry-wood frame and quiet flower bridge. Mobile: rum, civet quantity, minor root/wood grain and exact musk balance. Core plus the authored modules reproduce the **authored 100-part target arithmetically only**; they do not prove reproduction of authentic Opus V. No measured recognizer floor or empirical compensation relationship is available.

## Safety and commercial scope

[IFRA's official framework](https://ifrafragrance.org/initiatives-positions/safe-use-fragrance-science/ifra-standards) does not make a whole perfume compliant from a few low-dose ingredients. Three PW product-document spot checks were made. Direct AIMI is 4.44% and direct Benzyl Salicylate 1.44% in the unrounded 24% trial, below the fine-fragrance values shown on those pages. Damascenone is 0.0024%, but its generated certificate calls the restricted row beta damascone; do not silently ingest that name into policy or treat the page as an exact scientific authority.

Those spot checks are **not** a full screen. Aggregate constituents from rose, jasmine, woods, rum, civet and root mixtures; obtain applicable supplier documents for any skin/commercial use. No IFRA compliance, regulatory approval, skin safety, sale readiness or measured sensory claim is issued. All action-authority flags remain false.
