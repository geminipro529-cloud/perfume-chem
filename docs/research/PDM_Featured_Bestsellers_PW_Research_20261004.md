## Research findings that changed formulation decisions

1. **Layton is not just apple/vanilla/amber.** Current official architecture includes cardamom, lavender, violet, geranium, guaiacwood and praline. Its declaration also gives presence-level support for vanillin, coumarin and cool aromatic constituents. The R1 retains apple and aromatic contrast; a tiny menthol/praline experiment has an omission control. Ingredient declarations do not identify proprietary grades or amounts.
2. **Althaïr needs air around vanilla.** Current official architecture includes mandarin, elemi, orange blossom, cinnamon and cardamom, with guaiac and praline. The draft therefore avoids a brown caramel/amber base and treats pyrazine and ethyl maltol as removable trace hypotheses. A Bourbon-vanilla marketing note does not compel an expensive vanilla absolute.
3. **Delina's fruit and rose are coupled, not separate blocks.** Two primary lychee studies support multiple floral/fruit odorants but give different cultivar/extraction-dependent prominence. We therefore test rose oxide, terpene alcohols, small fruit esters and rose ketones together rather than claiming “one molecule is lychee.” Food GC/O and food sensory effects do not supply perfume dose/intensity/liking calibration. PW mixed Rose Oxide is not automatically the exact stereochemical material reported in research. [Ong and Acree](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/10563950/), [Sweetheart lychee study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29567218/).
4. **Delina has a specific tart axis.** [Firmenich's Rhubofix sheet](https://www.firmenich.com/sites/default/files/uploads/files/ingredients/marketing-sheet/perfumery/RHUBOFIX_978880.pdf?v1.2.86=) supports its rhubarb/green/woody function. Its recommended typical usage is not an IFRA safety limit and not proof of this blend's recognizability. Nutmeg was added at trace level after checking the current official heart, rather than copying older note pyramids.
5. **Exclusif is not Delina plus more sugar.** Current official notes specify pear/lychee, rose/incense, vanilla/musk/moss/tonka. Its ingredient declaration supports the presence of Alpha-Isomethyl Ionone and coumarin. The draft uses a separate creamy pear/rose chassis, AIMI, restrained tonka/moss and a tiny incense accent; it does not automatically install a large oud accord from older community descriptions. AIMI is the verified PW product in current inventory, not depleted Gamma Coeur.
6. **Valaya has a real captive gap.** [Givaudan](https://www.givaudan.com/fragrance-beauty/technologies-and-captives/unique-ingredients) describes Akigalawood as a distinct ingredient; [its biotechnology page](https://www.givaudan.com/fragrance-beauty/biotechnology) describes its production context. No exact PW listing was verified by local-catalog and targeted live searches. Patchouli Light + Kephalis + Black Pepper is only a test proxy for woody/spicy functions, not a chemical or sensory equivalent. Its complete omission is a mandatory control.
7. **Apple evidence supports an accord experiment, not a cloned dose.** The [2017 apple reconstitution study](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/nskkk/64/1/64_34/_article) motivates testing ester combinations. Layton's apple hypothesis combines persistent apple-associated body, peel and juiciness; shampoo/tropical drift is a reject condition.
8. **Airy floral materials are targeted support.** [Nympheal's manufacturer description](https://www.givaudan.com/fragrance-beauty/fragrance-ingredients-business/fragrance-molecules/nympheal) supports a transparent watery-floral role. It does not establish a universal mixture ratio or measured receptor/synergy matrix.

Reference formulations have unknown proprietary chemicals, exact grades, and quantities. No analytical peak or GC-O event was invented. Undisclosed original components remain an open roster, not zero mass. Source correction/retraction review was limited to the primary records inspected; it was not an exhaustive systematic review.

## Chemical-family/function graph and chassis

The full per-row graph is stored in canonical JSON: product → family → target role → module → time window → identity/risk note → inventory evidence. Interactions are marked **perfumery hypotheses**, never a measured receptor map.

- **Layton:** fruit esters/acetals + Verdox → apple flesh/peel → apple/lavender recognizer; lavender/terpene alcohols/ionone → aromatic-violet heart → vanilla/guaiac bridge. Romandolide and Ethylene Brassylate are intended fruit-skin versus rounding roles.

## Information-rich experiment plan

Start one fragrance at a time to keep this enjoyable.

1. Make its specified working master; take the **5 g** trial aliquot. Retain the master, rather than immediately committing a full 30 mL bottle.
2. Smell R1 against an authentic exact-edition reference on identical blotters, using the same recorded nominal application. Weighed deposition improves comparison if equipment permits. Quick smelling may stay exploratory; no skin protocol is silently authorized.
3. Evaluate at about 5 min, 30–60 min, 4 h and next-day blotter. Record one recognizer, one strongest mismatch, and preference/tie/cannot determine.
4. Prepare the control and the most relevant alternative only after the first mismatch points to a question. Each variant needs its own correctly recalculated stock/dilution table, not a negative removal from the current bottle.
5. For a more controlled comparison, code samples, balance presentation order and repeat on at least three sessions with fixed dose and substrate. Keep not-perceived, missing timepoints and uncertainty as observations.
6. For musks, use the existing balance alternatives as the first redundancy test. If needed, omit one musk while reallocating product mass to a declared chassis product; note that this changes the target. More musks are not intrinsically richer.
7. Stop a direction if it worsens the core recognizer, creates the listed failure modes, crystallizes/separates, or cannot be dosed reproducibly. Repeated ties justify retaining the simpler or cheaper design, not inventing superiority.

Optional analytical expansion: exact-reference GC-MS/GC-O, same-scenario headspace and recombination/omission work can later resolve unidentified materials or masking. Context-compatible OAV may prioritize investigation only; it cannot decide deletion or liking.
