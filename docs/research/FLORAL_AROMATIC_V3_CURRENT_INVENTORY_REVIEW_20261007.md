## What was repaired

1. The received-stock update made the soft-lavender test produce three distinct
   formulas. Its old expectation of two formulas and one duplicate was stale.
   The live test now checks diversity without forcing an obsolete stock choice.
   A separate controlled assignment fixture creates a real duplicate through
   the normal allocator and critic, so suppression remains directly tested.
2. Arithmetic and internal consistency alone had accepted forged stock IDs,
   identities, fractions, carriers and role/solver receipts. Every attempt now
   supplies role/stock assignments and execution-input bindings. The offline
   auditor verifies eligibility and replays allocation and critique against
   the current stock capability index.
3. Suppressed duplicate proofs require their real critic and physical rows.
   A fabricated allocation-failure prefix is not a valid reason. Claimed
   coverage failures are replayed through the solver, including the adversarial
   case that clears all assignments and lists genuine required roles.
4. Received-stock receipt files now participate in the diagnostic snapshot.
   One frozen index is reused only inside that run. No cross-request scientific
   cache or replay overhead was introduced into ordinary Formula Studio calls.
5. The quick verifier exposed an unrelated stale live-inventory test count.
   Its reviewed snapshot changed from 253 to 290 non-solvent material rows.
   The test now binds the immutable received-stock receipt and inventory hash.
   It expects 221 modeled-OAV rows and 69 unknown rows, with FAIL_CLOSED_GAPS
   retained. New ownership is explicitly not treated as threshold calibration.

No stock-selection policy, quantity template, threshold, release model or
empirical sensory model was changed in this acceptance pass. No formula,
inventory receipt, compounding hold or physical bottle was modified by it.
