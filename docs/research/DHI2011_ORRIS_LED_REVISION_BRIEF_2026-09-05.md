# DHI2011-inspired Orris-led revision: selected direction

Status: design decision and confirmed stock specification only. This is not a
new compounding formula or a release receipt.

The user is still planning the fragrance. Their remembered evaluation of the
raw materials favors Orris Liquid for its rich orris-root smell and rejects
Irotyl for smell and texture. This is material-selection evidence, not a
measurement of the unmade fragrance or proof of DHI2011 similarity.

## Selected architecture

- Orris Liquid is the proposed lead for root richness and texture.
- Irotyl is excluded from the proposed next candidate. Its physical inventory
  ownership is unchanged; existing formulas containing it remain historical.
- Methyl ionone remains a candidate for the surrounding cosmetic powder body.
- Alpha Irone is a separately adjustable iris accent, to be reassessed against
  the new Orris Liquid contribution.
- Orivone is a possible dry contour, with no automatic increase.
- Carrot Seed EO is an optional botanical accent, retained only if its
  controlled comparison supports the desired texture.
- Retain the DHI-inspired lavender/pear/ambrette opening and cedar/tonka/musk
  frame as the target while testing root definition and smooth transitions.
