# AHS full muguet completion — research, decision and verification plan

Date: 2026-09-07. Scope: add-only bench design for the existing AHS bottle, not a standalone muguet perfume or an accepted Chanel reconstruction. Parent is sole editor and scientific acceptor; native reviewer findings were checked against the primary pages below. No external model provider is used.

## Confirmed starting point

User confirms the whole-bottle column: Lilyreal ND 360 µL, Hydroxycitronellal 120 µL and PEA 60 µL after Florol 240 µL and Nympheal 60 µL. The older card said Hydroxycitronellol; the user's chemical correction supersedes that label in a new successor only. The aldehyde and diol remain different identities. Mayol has not been confirmed added. Nominal parent volume is 30.840 mL; component-sum parent is 30.740 mL. Remaining volume and exact mass are unknown.

## Design decision

The user rejected a two-material softening patch and requested a complete muguet addition. Select a five-component completion module: Florol 180 µL, PEDMC 120 µL, PEA 120 µL, Hedione 120 µL, Farnesol 60 µL per nominal parent. Total 600 µL. This ratio is a parent-designed, untested starting point, not a literature optimum, OAV-normalized optimum, chemical neutralizer or safety limit.

The complete combined architecture retains Hydroxycitronellal/Lilyreal ND for existing lily character, Nympheal/Helional for existing watery definition, and adds soft muguet body (Florol), green-rosy lily petal connection (PEDMC), soft blending (PEA), transparent floral space (Hedione), and later floral continuity (Farnesol). Existing benzyl salicylate, woods and musks remain the foundation. No added material is declared nonredundant without omission evidence.

Farnesol is selected instead of the initially considered extra hexyl salicylate: its direct muguet role is more closely linked to this request; hexyl salicylate adds a different herbaceous/fruity floral direction. No extra benzyl salicylate is needed by default because 250 µL is already present. DBCA adds fruity/herbaceous character; Mayol has manufacturer-described aldehydic undertones. Neither is selected. No added HCA, Lilyreal ND, Nympheal, Helional, DHM, Lilial, Bourgeonal, Calone, cyclamen/green aldehydes or sweeteners.

## Primary evidence and limitations

- [Florol manufacturer](https://studio.dsm-firmenich.com/product/florolr-pe-966458): soft muguet building block. This supports selection, not cancellation. Its current page gives VP 0.00712 Pa at 20 C; the [older manufacturer sheet](https://www.firmenich.com/sites/default/files/uploads/files/ingredients/marketing-sheet/perfumery/FLOROL_966458.pdf) gives 0.95 Pa at 20 C. The conflict is unresolved; do not tune either value to obtain a pass.
- [Carbinol Muguet manufacturer](https://studio.dsm-firmenich.com/product/carbinol-muguet-pe-920100), CAS 103-05-9: soft rosy-muguet floral body. [Owned-name supplier page](https://www.perfumersworld.com/view.php?pro_id=3MR02826) supports a mild green-lily role and links with linalool/linalyl acetate. PEDMC is not the similarly named acetate or DBCA. The material may crystallize near room temperature; raw-volume dosing is conditional on clear homogeneous liquid. Its generic [supplier COA](https://www.perfumersworld.com/ifra/COA/3MR02826.pdf) calls the product a proprietary compound while the catalogue gives a chemical identity. No lot purity is inferred from either page.
- [PEA supplier](https://www.perfumersworld.com/view.php?pro_id=3RM00358): rose/floral blending use, including muguet. Rose/green/phenolic drift remains a rejection criterion.
- [Hedione manufacturer](https://studio.dsm-firmenich.com/product/hedioner-pe-964898): transparent white-floral enhancement, not necessarily stronger intensity. Existing Hedione makes an omission comparison necessary. Web VP 0.00071 Pa at 20 C differs from legacy runtime 0.09466 Pa labelled 25 C; not adjudicated by this formula.
- [Farnesol supplier](https://www.perfumersworld.com/view.php?pro_id=3LW00187): delicate muguet/green floral that develops slowly; not instant punch. Oily texture and change on prolonged air exposure must be checked.
- [Hexyl salicylate manufacturer](https://www.givaudan.com/fragrance-beauty/fragrance-ingredients-business/fragrance-molecules/hexyl-salicylate): herbaceous/fruity floral modifier; considered and excluded from the first module.
- [Mayol manufacturer](https://studio.dsm-firmenich.com/product/mayolr-pe-957230): aldehydic odor undertones despite being an alcohol. Not selected for this complaint.
- [BASF HCA technical information](https://download.basf.com/p1/EN_StaticDocuments_5941/en/Technical_Information_Hydroxycitronellal): 0.005472 hPa = 0.5472 Pa at 20 C, versus legacy runtime 0.005 Pa labelled 25 C. Do not carry the old diol OAV into this bottle.
