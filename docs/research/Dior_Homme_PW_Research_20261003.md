## Executive decision

Produce separate Intense, Parfum 2025 and historical Parfum 2014-style recipes. A common iris vocabulary does not make them one perfume at different strength. Leads are architecture-informed candidates, not published original Dior compositions or sensory winners.

The three full recipes and exact supplier/stock ledger are new files. All earlier formulas and inventory remain untouched. The brief is comparison/reconstruction-oriented rather than an unrelated niche enhancement.

### Primary iris research

[Brenna, Fuganti and Serra, 2003](https://comptes-rendus.academie-sciences.fr/chimie/articles/10.1016/S1631-0748(03)00087-0/), DOI 10.1016/S1631-0748(03)00087-0, studies the ten irone isomers. Odor depends on stereochemistry, and isolated-sample results differed from earlier GC-sniff evidence. Therefore a commercial alpha-irone name cannot supply a complete isomer distribution, an enantiopure threshold or a whole-perfume intensity calibration. Alpha Ionone, Alpha Isomethyl Ionone and Alpha Irone are separate products, not synonyms.

This paper guides the identity boundary, **not the selected percentages**. No correction/retraction banner was seen on the publisher page during this read; no comprehensive Crossmark audit is claimed. Citation/paraphrase only: no restricted dataset or full article is copied. A previously remembered 2025 irone article could not be opened here and is not promoted as freshly verified support.

Only a stock strength, identity or conversion actually used needs resolution. Photographs, invoices and lot paperwork are not prerequisites for these personal design hypotheses. No inventory file, receipt, reservation or physical command is generated.

## 7. Minimal information-rich experiment

Choose only the requested edition initially. Use one lead plus one conservative or simpler control; a third matched-total musk alternative is optional. Avoid making a full 30 mL bottle before a 5 g weighed trial has exposed the dominant failure.

- **Intense:** lead vs lower-root balance asks whether the root accents become cold/lipstick-heavy. Lead vs simpler control asks whether carrot, seed nuance and trace damascone earn their cost/complexity.
- **Parfum 2025:** lead vs lower-Ambrox/Hedione redistribution asks whether warm wood obscures dual iris. This is a balance experiment, not a clean isolated Ambrox deletion.
- **Classic Parfum:** lead vs the conservative lower-leather balance asks whether suede supports or overwhelms iris. The compensating Hedione/AIMI changes are explicit; no single-component causal conclusion is claimed.

For Intense's 14% musk register, optional two-musk alternatives are Helvetolide/Ambrettolide/EB = 8.4/5.6/0, 8.4/0/5.6, and 0/7/7, compared with the full 6/4/4. For current Parfum compare Ambrettolide/EB 4/6, 10/0 and 0/10. For classic compare Ambrettolide/Exaltolide 4/5, 9/0 and 0/9. These are matched-total alternatives, **not measured synergistic pairs**. If a single musk wins, retain it.

Weigh from prepared masters so the smallest stock transfers are practical. When different working-stock additions change carrier, matrix-match known added DPG to the highest arm and deduct the balancing carrier from its alcohol; keep product-equivalent loading fixed. This only matches the known added carrier, not undisclosed intrinsic product composition. Recompute each variant; do not apply a base master card after changing percentages.

Compare on the same blotter and consistent deposit, preferably coded. Timepoints: approximately 5 minutes, 30–60 minutes, 4 hours and next day. Keep the same maturation period for compared candidates. Use the correct commercial edition when a reference is available. “Cannot tell,” “not perceived” and uncertainty are valid observations. A personal unblinded reaction is exploratory, not a consumer-superiority or population liking result.

Stop or revise if iris is lost, the initial impression is generic lavender/blue freshness, pear becomes candy, a root nuance becomes vegetable/cumin, amber turns harsh, musk flattens the heart, classic leather turns bitter/medicinal, or the desired base becomes vanilla dessert. Do not cure these automatically with more ingredients. One coded pair and a short reaction are enough for the next personal design clue; a formal multi-session study is only needed for a stronger claim.

No experiment or skin use was performed. After observations, create R2 with explicit changes and reasons; retain R1 and the tested outcomes. No posterior update is currently justified.

## 8. Physical proposal and safety boundary

The written bench order is a PROPOSE-only plan. Human confirmation, measurement, actual bottle commit and inventory movement were not performed. These new-batch alternatives are not subtraction instructions for an existing bottle. The existing lifecycle would have to receive actual confirmed quantities for a real action.

All benchmark percentages are w/w product-equivalent, not pure aromatic assay and not commercial Dior concentration. The recipes provide both calculated and milligram-rounded quantities. Thirty grams is not thirty millilitres; exact w/w is preserved by preparing a homogeneous weighed batch, then measuring the desired fill volume. The optional measured-final-volume alternative is explicitly w/v, never silently relabeled w/w.

Category 4 assessment must use actual products, final loading and aggregate restricted constituents. [IFRA's official guidance](https://ifrafragrance.org/initiatives-positions/safe-use-fragrance-science/ifra-standards) requires constituent-aware assessment of natural complex substances. No complete final-formula limit review, safety clearance or certificate was generated.

Priorities for later screening: Coumarin and natural coumarin contributions; rose ketones including alpha damascone and natural sources; oxidizable/allergen-bearing citrus/lavender; Orris/ambrette/rose/hay/resinoid identity; and Suederal/IBQ/Safraleine in classic leather. FCF does not establish whole-formula safety. A proprietary mixture's blank generic restriction table is unknown, not automatically unrestricted. Laboratory handling follows actual supplier SDS; no unsupported numerical dermal limit is installed.
