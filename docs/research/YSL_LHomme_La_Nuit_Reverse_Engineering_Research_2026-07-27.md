# YSL L'Homme and La Nuit de L'Homme — Evidence and Reverse-Search Report

**Date:** 2026-07-28  
**Scope:** public evidence for modular structural chassis and inventory-buildable reconstruction studies  
**Evidence ceiling:** no authenticated commercial concentrate formula, chromatogram, calibration table, or bottle-specific quantitative assay was found. The accompanying formulas are research hypotheses, not exact proprietary recipes or sensory-equivalence claims.

## Executive Finding

The strongest defensible model is a shared modern masculine woody-musky platform with different recognition modules:

- **L'Homme:** transparent woods + clean musks + fresh floral volume + violet/green tonka-vetiver support, with ginger, white-pepper-like spice, cardamom, a basil-shaped herbal accent, and a watery trace acting as a small signature module.
- **La Nuit de L'Homme:** a darker Iso E/amberwood/vertiver/cashmeran platform with coumarin, lavender, cedar, and vetiver, recognized principally by a cool cardamom module.

The public evidence does not support reconstructing either fragrance from the advertised note pyramid alone. A public commercial GC-MS reconstruction page gives a much larger woody/musk scaffold than the marketing notes imply, but withholds percentages and does not publish its analytical method. It is useful for **material presence and rough rank order only**.

## Official Product Evidence

YSL describes a fresh woody progression of bergamot and white pepper; ginger, violet leaf, and basil; then vetiver, tonka, and cedar. Its displayed ingredient list declares limonene, coumarin, linalool, geraniol, alpha-isomethyl ionone, citral, isoeugenol, citronellol, and cinnamal. The page also mentions lavender and bergamot sourcing.

### La Nuit de L'Homme EDT

YSL describes a spicy bergamot top, cedar and lavender heart, and vetiver base. Elsewhere on the same page it names tonka. The displayed ingredient list declares linalool, limonene, coumarin, alpha-isomethyl ionone, geraniol, citral, citronellol, and isoeugenol.

Source: [YSL La Nuit de L'Homme Eau de Toilette](https://www.yslbeauty.com/int/fragrance/fragrance-for-him/la-nuit-de-l-homme/la-nuit-de-l-homme-eau-de-toilette/426YSL.html)

## Allergen-Label Reverse Reading

EU ingredient lists are descending by weight above 1%; ingredients below 1% may appear in any order. Perfume materials may be collapsed under `PARFUM`, while specifically regulated fragrance allergens are declared separately. Therefore allergen order cannot be converted into a hidden perfume formula.

Source: [Regulation (EC) No 1223/2009, Article 19](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32009R1223)

| Declared name | L'Homme | La Nuit | Useful structural inference | Limitation |
|---|:---:|:---:|---|---|
| Limonene | Yes | Yes | Citrus and terpene-rich naturals are materially present | Cannot distinguish bergamot, lemon, cardamom, ginger, or added limonene |
| Linalool | Yes | Yes | Supports a bergamot/lavender/linalool axis | May be natural carry-through or added material |
| Coumarin | Yes | Yes | Supports tonka/fougère warmth | Does not reveal coumarin versus tonka-base proportions |
| Alpha-isomethyl ionone | Yes | Yes | Supports violet/ionone body | Does not identify the complete violet accord |
| Geraniol | Yes | Yes | Supports a small rosy-floral bridge | May arise from several naturals |
| Citral | Yes | Yes | Supports citrus/ginger freshness | Cannot identify a source oil |
| Citronellol | Yes | Yes | Supports trace floral-natural carry-through | Not proof of rose or geranium oil |
| Isoeugenol | Yes | Yes | Supports a trace spicy-floral facet | Not proof of clove |
| Cinnamal | Yes | Not displayed | Consistent with L'Homme's broader spice signature | Label threshold does not reveal dose |

Commission Regulation (EU) 2023/1545 adds 56 fragrance allergens to individual-label requirements above 0.001% in leave-on products, with transition periods. Future labels may therefore be longer without a corresponding formula change.

Source: [Commission Regulation (EU) 2023/1545](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2023/1545/oj/eng)

### Lilial warning

The international L'Homme page still displays `BUTYLPHENYL METHYLPROPIONAL` (BMHCA/Lilial). Regulation (EU) 2021/1902 moved non-exempt CMR substances into the prohibited list, and public EU market-surveillance notices have reported withdrawal of labelled L'Homme stock containing BMHCA. The displayed web list may therefore be stale or region-specific. None of the new formulas uses Lilial; Florol supplies only a modern muguet function and is not claimed as an odor-identical replacement.

Sources: [Regulation (EU) 2021/1902](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32021R1902), [Cyprus public-health withdrawal notice](https://www.moh.gov.cy/moh/phs/phs.nsf/All/4A20AA81B6BB470AC2258BE2004459EC/$file/%CE%91%CE%A0%CE%9F%CE%A3%CE%A5%CE%A1%CE%A3%CE%97%20%CE%9A%CE%91%CE%9B%CE%9B%CE%A5%CE%9D%CE%A4%CE%99%CE%9A%CE%A9%CE%9D%2027.11.2024.pdf)

## Public GC-MS Reconstruction Findings

These pages claim GC-MS decoding but publish only ingredient identities in apparent descending order. Percentages, chromatograms, standards, bottle code, and validation data are locked or absent. The lists are treated as secondary candidate rankings.

### L'Homme

The first materials are Iso E Super, Ethylene Brassylate, Galaxolide, Habanolide, Hedione, Vertofix Coeur, Ethyl Linalool, and Dihydromyrcenol. Lemon, cedar, ionone, sandalwood, coumarin, and musks follow. Fresh ginger, black pepper, cardamom, nutmeg, and basil appear much later.

That ordering supports a **large woody-musky scaffold with a small signature spice module**, not a ginger-dominant concentrate by mass.

Source: [Dupe Hacking public YSL L'Homme material list](https://dupehacking.com/fragrances/ysl-lhomme)

### La Nuit de L'Homme

The first materials are Iso E Super, Galaxolide, bergamot FCF, Amberwood F, Vertofix Coeur, Ethyl Linalool, Ambroxan, Dihydromyrcenol, Hedione, Cedramber, Cashmeran, Exaltolide, and Coumarin. Cardamom, cedar, lavandin, vetiver materials, and sandalwood appear later; Muscenone Delta and Edenolide are trace-ranked.

This supports a **dark woody-amber platform with a perceptually decisive but not necessarily mass-dominant cardamom module**.

Source: [Dupe Hacking public La Nuit material list](https://dupehacking.com/fragrances/la-nuit-de-lhomme)

## Substitution Chemistry and Non-Equivalence

| Amendment | Evidence-supported relationship | What must not be claimed |
|---|---|---|
| Habanolide -> Galaxolide 50% | dsm-firmenich describes Habanolide as an elegant warm, slightly woody macrocyclic musk. IFF describes Galaxolide as a clean, powerful, persistent iso-chroman musk whose quality approaches macrocyclic musks. Galaxolide can carry clean musk diffusion, but it is a different molecule and musk class. | Odor identity, equal potency, or preservation of Habanolide's complete macrocyclic character |
| Muscenone Delta -> Exaltolide 10% | Muscenone Delta is described as powerful, powdery, orris-like and slightly animalic, with an enhancer effect. Exaltolide is described as elegant, natural-musky, powdery/woody, subtly fruity, and useful for depth and roundness. | One-for-one sensory equivalence or preservation of Muscenone's animalic/orris enhancer facet |
| Edenolide -> Zenolide | IFF describes Edenolide as creamy, warm, powdery and fruity-green with top-to-base linearity. Zenolide is a soft, delicate red-fruit musk with medium heart and lower base impact. | That Zenolide reproduces Edenolide's creamy green-apple texture or temporal profile |
| Ambroxan -> Ambrofix 30% | Ambrofix is an ambroxide-family material described by Givaudan as highly powerful, substantive, dry woody-ambery and ambergris-like. | Vertofix Coeur -> inventory `Vertofix` | IFF identifies Vertofix Coeur as a specific warm woody, cedar/leather, musky material. The inventory label does not identify supplier, CAS, or Coeur grade. | Automatic proof that the local bottle is the exact IFF Coeur grade |

| Lavandin Grosso -> true lavender EO | Both are linalool/linalyl-acetate systems, but lavandin hybrid oils and L. angustifolia differ in camphor/cineole balance and batch profile. | Chemotype or sensory identity |

## Natural-Material Chemistry

- Cardamom oil is an oxygenated-terpene system centered on 1,8-cineole and alpha-terpinyl acetate, with linalool, linalyl acetate, and alpha-terpineol also contributing. That chemistry explains why cardamom can bridge bergamot freshness, lavender, and warm woods rather than behave like a simple “hot spice.” Source: [PubMed 37836202](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37836202/).
- Ginger oils commonly center on zingiberene, alpha-curcumene, and sesquiphellandrene, with batch-dependent monoterpenes. That supports ginger as a warm citrus-woody texture, not a substitute for cardamom. Sources: [PubMed 24020099](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24020099/), [PubMed 32872604](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/32872604/).
- Provence lavender GC-MS data show a linalool/linalyl-acetate chemotype, reported around 40.2% and 44% respectively in one commercial oil. That supports a lavender + linalool + linalyl acetate bridge in La Nuit. Source: [PubMed 33922508](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33922508/).

These studies characterize raw materials, not either finished fragrance.

## Patent-Family Reverse Search

### Strongest vehicle/stability lead: EP1994921A1 family

L'Oréal's EP1994921A1 family has a 2007-05-21 priority date. Its examples use 15% unnamed `perfume concentrate x`, 65% ethanol, water to 100%, and combinations that include Ethylhexyl Methoxycinnamate, Butyl Methoxydibenzoylmethane, and Tris(tetramethylhydroxypiperidinol) citrate. Those stabilizers overlap the displayed YSL ingredient lists.

This is a plausible patent family for the **finished alcoholic perfume vehicle and light/heat stabilization system**. It does not disclose the odorant concentrate `x`, does not name either product, and cannot recover their aromatic formulas.

Sources: [EP1994921A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/EP1994921A1/en), [FR2916347A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/FR2916347A1/en)

### Exact-name hit that is not a formula patent: EP3862027A1

EP3862027A1 lists La Nuit de L'Homme among commercial fragrances that can be measured in a portable diffusion device. Its claims concern the measurement device and method, not the fragrance composition.

Source: [EP3862027A1](https://patents.google.com/patent/EP3862027A1/en)

### Formula codes are not patent IDs

Community reports associate codes such as `5YB04-1`, `8YB02-2`, and `C167049/1` with packaging or formula revisions. No reliable mapping from those strings to a patent publication was found. They should be treated as internal manufacturing/formula-version identifiers, not patent numbers.

## Design Consequences

1. Keep both structural chassis dominated by woods, musks, floral-air materials, and low-volatility anchors.
2. Make L'Homme's ginger/pepper/cardamom/herbal module removable as a single 100 uL slot. Literal Basil EO remains excluded from the validated study because its batch chemotype is unverified and the repository has no defensible composite OAV profile for it.
3. Make La Nuit's cardamom module removable as a single 50 uL slot.
4. Use only available inventory materials; record unavailable musks as explicit functional substitutions.
5. Preserve Vetiver EO (India) in all four studies. It was already present in the live inventory, so no duplicate inventory line is needed.
6. Treat all active ppm values as volume-basis screening proxies. Exact ppm w/w is withheld where stock density is unavailable.
7. Require pipeline OAV/ODT, IFRA/allergen, and bench evaluation before skin use or release.
