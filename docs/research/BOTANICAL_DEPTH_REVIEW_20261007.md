## What was added

### Flowers: nine more precise targets

- White hyacinth headspace, with cultivar and voucher uncertainty retained.
- Tilia species and flowering-stage variation, bound to its 2023 correction.
- Boronia megastigma absolute, distinguished from heart and leaf products.
- Spartium junceum broom absolute, not an alias for Cytisus scoparius.
- Nelumbo lotus, kept separate from Nymphaea water lilies.
- Nymphaea prolifera floral benzenoids and organ/time dependence.
- Water-lily cultivar Paul Stetson, without inventing a species identification.
- Phalaenopsis scent under light and development conditions.
- Cymbidium Sunny Bell floral-organ differences.

### Orchard fruit and green detail: four targets

- Lychee cultivar realism, including floral, green and sulfur off-note limits.
- Fresh fig fruit pulp and peel.
- Fig leaf, explicitly not fig latex or a ripe-fruit profile.
- Cydonia oblonga quince, not Pseudocydonia sinensis.

### Mosses and balsams: six exact product scopes

- Biolandes oakmoss absolute F0420.
- Biolandes treemoss absolute F0080.
- IFF Siam benzoin, reusing the existing bound source.
- IFF Sumatra benzoin, distinguished from Siam.
- IFF Peru Balsam Oil MD, distinguished from crude balsam and resinoid.
- Named Tolu reconstructions, distinguished from natural Tolu extracts.

Each card separates source findings from an untested construction hypothesis,
functional roles, negative space, identity limits and one controlled-comparison
question. None supplies automatic material selections, doses, stock substitutions,
gas concentrations, consumer liking labels or action authority.

## Research findings that changed the implementation

The Tilia publication has a substantive correction, including an incomplete
table and updated results. The new card requires both the paper and correction.
A test removes the correction review and verifies that the card is withheld.
[Original paper](https://www.mdpi.com/2311-7524/8/8/719);
[correction](https://www.mdpi.com/2311-7524/9/2/286).

Nelumbo, Nymphaea prolifera and a named ornamental water-lily cultivar cannot
stand in for one generic measured lotus profile. Organ preparation, sampling
temperature and floral stage are retained as limitations, not interpreted as
ambient perfume release. [Nelumbo study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12182725/);
[Nymphaea prolifera study](https://academic.oup.com/hr/article/10/12/uhad237/7420481);
[Paul Stetson study](https://www.mdpi.com/2223-7747/15/3/384).

Phalaenopsis source label/photoperiod inconsistencies and Cymbidium prose/table
count differences remain visible. No disputed numeric table was imported.
[Phalaenopsis study](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s40529-017-0204-8);
[Cymbidium study](https://www.mdpi.com/2223-7747/8/8/251).

Lychee food-aroma results motivate a comparison question, not a sulfur dosing
rule. Fig fruit and leaf observations do not establish a fig-latex reconstruction.
[Lychee cultivar study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17266328/);
[fig organ study](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0308814610005212).

The two reviewed Tolu products are reconstructions. A natural-category label or
the word alcoholic in a product name does not establish a natural extract or a
known supplied carrier. [dsm-firmenich product](https://studio.dsm-firmenich.com/product/tolu-res-type-nat-pe-220951);
[Robertet product](https://matieres-premieres.robertet.com/tolu-reco-type-resinoid-alcoholic).

An internally inconsistent hyacinth numeric study, Cytisus ecology as evidence
for Spartium odor, Chinese-quince substitution, and unsupported fig-latex or
natural-Tolu assertions were not admitted.
