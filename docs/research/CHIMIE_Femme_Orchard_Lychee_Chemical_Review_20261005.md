# CHIMIE Femme - ingredient-level formulation research
5 October 2026. Root Codex formulation judgment; no LLM worker, purchase, evidence admission or physical execution.

## Executive decision

Select **Rich Orchard / Fresh Lychee / Cool Freesia** as the first development lead. Use fruit juice, skin and body as different functions; a very small lychee module; trace osmanthus/petal texture; and a three-role musk foundation. The full formula is [here](../../formulas/CHIMIE_Femme_Orchard_Lychee_Freesia_R1_20261005.md).

It is selected for fit to the user's locked brief, not on an invented sensory/beauty score. There is no measured Pareto winner. Actual pear recognition, richness, liking, diffusion and fruit persistence still require smelling.

This review concerns all **31 materials selected for this perfume**, plus the serious alternatives below. It is not a claim to have experimentally characterized all PW catalogue chemicals. The reusable catalogue contains 1,329 SKU rows as of 30 September; its exact bytes are pinned in the formula. Selected products and manufacturer descriptions were checked, but not every SKU page, supplier certificate or proprietary composition.

The requested exact DeepSeek route was not exposed as a usable native model/verified bridge in this session, so work continued locally. No alternative paid worker/provider was silently substituted.

## 1. Locked claim, evidence and design boundary

Mode: creative new formulation, with a proposed post-purchase/label-matched bench build. Not a reconstruction, a stock rebase, a physical bottle delta or a released perfume.

Claim: an original, pear-led apple/lychee fruity-floral design with practical weighed doses. Never "accurate clone," "consumer winner," "best perfume," "measured sillage" or "IFRA compliant."

Mandatory recognizers: pear/juicy orchard, crisp apple, subordinate fresh lychee. Protected anchors: Hexyl Acetate fruit juice; controlled Verdox/Manzanate/apple bite; fresh fruit-to-heart body; Helvetolide pear-musk connection; transparent rather than dominant florals.

Negative space: no gourmand sweetness, fruit soup, rose/jasmine/ylang takeover, obvious peach cream, vegetal leaf, masculine amberwood dominance or shampoo.

References: Pera Granita's pear/citrus/osmanthus bridge, English Pear & Freesia's readable pear/transparent floral/wood structure, Delina's lychee freshness without adopting its rose heart, and Dylan Purple's pear/freesia/wood-musk trajectory. Public marketed notes are not their formulas or a verified description of every bottle/reformulation.

No analytical chromatogram, unknown-peak roster, GC-O event list, authenticated reference sample, blind panel, measured release trajectory or safety snapshot is available for this R1. Those states are unavailable, not empty "zero findings."

## 2. What the primary literature actually changes

### Pear and apple

[Zierer et al. 2016](https://portal.fis.tum.de/en/publications/aroma-active-compounds-in-bartlett-pears-and-their-changes-during/) identified relevant Bartlett-pear odorants, including ethyl decadienoate and Hexyl Acetate. This supports layered fruit construction and a genuine Pear Ester test, not transferring food OAVs or fruit concentrations into perfume doses.

[Young et al. 1996](https://scijournals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/%28SICI%291097-0010%28199607%2971%3A3%3C329%3A%3AAID-JSFA588%3E3.0.CO%3B2-8) supports multi-component apple aroma. Important named apple esters are not interchangeable: 2-methylbutyl acetate, ethyl 2-methylbutyrate and Manzanate have different identities. R1 does not claim the latter two reproduce the former.

Therefore allocate different materials to juice, skin, ripe body and transition. Do not assume one generic "apple" product supplies every facet.

### Lychee: a real interaction worth testing

[Wang, Frank and Steinhaus 2024](https://portal.fis.leibniz-lsb.tum.de/en/publications/molecular-background-of-the-lychee-aroma-of-vitis-vinifera-l-musc-4/) used reconstitution/omission experiments to identify a lychee note from a particular rose-oxide stereoisomer together with Geraniol in Muscaris grapes.

**Inference for this perfume:** a small Rose Oxide/Geraniol module is worth testing, with Linalool/Nerol controlling freshness. The grape matrix, odorant delivery and exact stereochemistry differ from our alcohol perfume. The paper does not validate our proportions or imply that generic PW Rose Oxide is the same stereoisomer.

Damascenone is a separate trace ripe-fruit modifier, not a mandatory "lychee molecule." Sulfurous/cooked and fermented notes found in food-aroma studies are not added merely because they are analytically authentic.

### Freesia and restrained osmanthus

[Gao et al. 2018](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6093421/) found Linalool prominent in the studied Freesia cultivars, with significant profile differences. This justifies considering a cool Linalool-containing scaffold; it does not show that our muguet materials are present in a flower or reveal Jo Malone's composition.

[Cai et al. 2014](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4097373/) distinguished osmanthus cultivar profiles with GC-O/GC-MS. Beta Ionone and floral terpenes can matter, and abundance alone did not establish odor importance. Fresh flower headspace is not the exact composition of the supplied absolute.

**Formulation inference:** use low Osmanthus Absolute with tiny Beta Ionone/lactone texture, then verify that it adds fruit detail instead of apricot leather or violet powder. Rose de Mai is deliberately a trace, not an automatic luxury claim.

### Manufacturer descriptions are useful but bounded

Helvetolide's manufacturer-described pear/ambrette facet makes it a target-linked fruit/musk bridge. Habanolide supplies a different clean macrocyclic musk facet; Ambrettolide is a softer fruity/floral finish challenger. Their combined benefit is unproved until omissions/alternatives are smelled.

Standard Hedione and HC are separate grade hypotheses despite sharing a broad chemical identity. Their official descriptions support comparison of transparency versus a stronger floral boost, not receptor-affinity calculations. Do not copy inconsistent web/PDF vapor-pressure fields into headspace modeling.

## 4. Family-to-function graph and chassis

The functional graph is:

- Green aliphatic esters/carbonate -> skin/cut-fruit -> juice/body esters -> orchard module -> opening/early heart.
- Fruit/citrus terpene derivatives -> fresh pear-associated floral body -> restrained floral scaffold -> early heart.
- Terpene alcohols + Rose Oxide -> candidate lychee interaction -> cool petal connection -> top/heart.
- Whole Osmanthus/Rose products + trace ionone/lactone -> natural petal/flesh texture -> orchard/floral seam -> heart.
- Fruity ester musk + two macrocyclic musk roles -> fruit-memory/soft finish -> pale wood -> late heart/drydown.

Only the rose-oxide/geraniol interaction has a directly relevant reconstitution result, and that result is in grapes, not R1. All other cross-block integration edges here are **uncalibrated formulation hypotheses**. Family resemblance is not a measured receptor map or proof of synergy.

Chassis: orchard ester/fruit body + transparent freesia-like space + pear-associated musk. Mobile sockets: amount/type of sharp peel, lychee strength, natural texture, second/third musk and pale wood. Changes to the sockets must not rewrite the primary pear/apple identity.

No accepted sensory parent exists, so "core + parent module reproduces accepted target" cannot be proven. The older PW2 pear/apple campaign is a historical design comparator. The historical 30,000-search/35-guardrail/DNA score is neither reproduced nor relevant enough to import as a universal gate.

## 8. Hard gates and experiment decisions

**Passable now by local checks:** target sums, identity uniqueness at product-row level, Decimal stock/carrier conservation, no hidden density conversion, no prohibited held material, practical mass increments, equal product/carrier accounting of two swaps.

**Cannot pass on paper:** pear/apple recognition, lychee-versus-rose outcome, fruit retention at 45 minutes, absence of shampoo/tropical/peach/wood dominance, perceived musk nonredundancy, consumer liking, measured projection or stability.

Evaluate one small R1 first. The exact Manzanate/Hexyl Acetate and Ambrettolide/Habanolide alternatives preserve product and stock-carrier totals. The 15-material control preserves nominal total product load through explicit Hedione compensation and matches DPG/DEP; it is a whole-architecture comparator, not a causal omission or ingredient-count experiment.

For true one-component omissions, match stock solvent, retain other doses and report the lower fragrance-product load. Do not add Hedione and call it an isolated omission.

Stop conditions: nonhomogeneity; wrong stock basis; substantial weighing error; the uncued scent is chiefly peach/tropical/rose/shampoo; or the extra texture is less enjoyable than the simpler comparator. Ask for a new target version, not a patch to the old history.

## 9. Posterior and claim release

Current posterior is qualitative and unupdated by observations: no R1 has been smelled. Data from the previous Good Girl Gone Bad skin comparisons do not calibrate this unrelated orchard perfume.

After testing, keep actual recipe/weights and notes; create R2 with an explicit immediate parent and intentional active-product differences. Useful results may be "keep R1," "simpler is better," "lychee module failed," or "no discrimination."

Physical additions are human-measured actions, not a formula file pretending a transfer occurred. There is no bottle event, reservation or inventory movement from this work. Compounding, safety, release and evidence-admission authority flags remain false.

Current IFRA material information must come from the [official library](https://ifrafragrance.org/standards-library) and exact supplier documents. The safety review here identifies required checks; it is not a completed Category 4 clearance. Natural composition/allergens, oxidation, aggregation rules, solvent and package compatibility need appropriate review before skin-use/commercial claims.
