# Amber, musk and citrus: bounded foundation tranche

This is an advisory role/identity review, not a dosing guide, a calibrated performance model or complete coverage of these families. No formula or inventory changed.

## Findings and implementation choices

### Musk roles are product-specific

The current manufacturer page describes Helvetolide through fruit/pear and ambrette facets. Habanolide is described as a warm, slightly woody macrocyclic musk. These descriptions motivate different bridge/support hypotheses; they do not show that combining them improves a particular perfume. Keep an unchanged comparison and test role-specific omissions instead of maximizing musk quantity. [Helvetolide](https://studio.dsm-firmenich.com/product/helvetolider-pe-947650), [Habanolide](https://studio.dsm-firmenich.com/product/habanolider-pe-947303).

The runtime retrieves a distinct product-role clue and keeps exact material labels separate. Supplier adjectives such as powerful, elegant or substantive are not imported as measured intensity, human liking or longevity values. Manufacturer application tables are not working-stock receipts or formula prescriptions.

### Amber is not one universal performance block

IFF describes Amber Xtreme as an amber-woody ingredient. Its marketing describes broad performance; that is not independent evidence of better projection, identity preservation or preference for a finished perfume. Keep woody-amber support distinct from ambergris/Ambrox character and resinous/vanillic architecture. This is a design taxonomy, not a receptor, potency or chemical-equivalence map. [IFF Amber Xtreme](https://www.iff.com/scent/ingredients-compendium/amber-xtreme-2/).

The new profile asks which amber character the brief requires. It cannot recommend an automatic superamber addition or turn a high OAV into an aesthetic finding. The existing exact Ambrox Super description remains a separate source-bound product clue.

### Citrus process and grade remain part of identity

IFF distinguishes mechanical peel expression from steam distillation. That process vocabulary supports retaining botanical identity and processing grade separately. It does not measure the differences between every commercial expressed, distilled, FCF or terpeneless product. [IFF extraction-method overview](https://www.iff.com/media/stories/how-perfume-made/).

The runtime therefore asks for exact grade only when a particular comparison, conversion or higher-authority claim depends on it. Generic citrus exploration remains possible. No grade receives an invented composition, threshold, activity coefficient or safety status. Current product-specific certificates remain necessary for relevant safety claims, not for every playful design question.

## Source review and rights

Three source records were added; the already existing Helvetolide source was freshly reread without changing its historical metadata object. All four reviews record `SELECTED_PRIMARY_SECTIONS`, `REVIEWED_ADVISORY`, metadata/original-summary rights only, no empirical-data admission and no numerical calibration. The manufacturer correction/retraction field is explicitly not applicable to this descriptive-source class; this is not a scientific-paper retraction census.

The other 65 source wrappers remain `LEGACY_ADVISORY`. They retain their prior summaries but are not retrospectively certified as fresh full-text, rights or retraction reviews. Changing a source metadata object without updating its independently reviewed binding removes its dependent claims and profiles. A missing review manifest withholds literature retrieval rather than manufacturing a clean empty result.
