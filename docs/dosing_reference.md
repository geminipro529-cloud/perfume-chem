# Dosing Reference — All Inventory Materials
## µL Ranges for 10 mL · 30 mL · 50 mL · 100 mL Batches
**Updated:** 2026-04-13

All values in **µL of your stock solution** (dilution already factored in — volumes are what you pipette from your bottle).

> **1,000 µL = 1 mL.** At 100 mL batch size some saturated values reach several mL — that's correct; workhorse materials like Hedione or Iso E Super CAN be 5–10% of a formula.

> **Solid materials** (Myristic Acid Powder) are listed in **grams (g)** — weigh on a milligram scale. Melt into warmed ethanol or carrier before mixing.

---

## How to Read These Tables

| Level | Meaning | When to use |
|-------|---------|-------------|
| **T (Threshold)** | First detectable trace. Subconscious. | Subliminal effects, animalic undertow, "something's there but I can't name it" |
| **L (Low)** | Audible but supporting. Doesn't lead. | Background texture, secondary accents, quiet structure |
| **N (Normal)** | Standard functional dose. Named role. | Typical formula placement — the material does its job |
| **H (High)** | Prominent, featured. Pushes the composition. | When this material IS the accord or a starring note |
| **S (Saturated)** | Maximum before distortion or masking. | Soliflore / single-note domination. Beyond this = off-notes or waste |

## Potency Tiers — Quick Reference

### Tier 1 — ppb Threshold (1–5 µL can change everything at 10 mL)
Skatole (1%), Damascenone (1%), Ethyl 2-Methylbutyrate, Birch Tar, cis-3-Hexenol, Cinnamaldehyde, Guaiacol (10%), Leafovert, Norlimbanol Dextro, IBQ (10%), Ultralia

### Tier 2 — Low Threshold (5–20 µL range at 10 mL)
Cis Jasmone, Methyl Anthranilate, Gamma Decalactone, Gamma Undecalactone, Rose Oxide (1%), Dynascone (10%), Allyl Amyl Glycolate, Melonal, Raspberry Ketone, Ethyl Vanillin, Anisaldehyde, Eugenol, Isoeugenol, Ethyl Safranate, Beta Ionone, Cyclamen Aldehyde, Indole (10%), PCME (10%), Damascone Beta (10%)

### Tier 3 — Normal Threshold (10–30 µL at 10 mL)
Most neat florals, citrus EOs, woods, musks, accord bases. Standard dosing applies.

### Tier 4 — Heavy-Dose Safe (200–1,000 µL at 10 mL without distortion)
Iso E Super, Hedione, Hedione HC, Benzyl Salicylate, Galaxolide, Ethylene Brassylate, Habanolide, Benzyl Benzoate, Ambrox Super (30%), I-IRIS FTEC, Orris FTEC, PEA

---

*All ranges are guidelines. When in doubt, start at Low and work up — you can always add, never subtract.*
