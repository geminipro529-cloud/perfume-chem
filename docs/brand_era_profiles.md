# Brand and Era Adaptation

The reconstruction and chassis algorithms are universal. Their priors are not.

A brand profile changes:

- which evidence is most informative;
- which transitions deserve protection;
- how large a socket may be;
- what analytical methods are emphasized;
- and which compression errors are most dangerous.

It never replaces product-specific evidence.

## YSL

Emphasize:

- parent/flanker comparison;
- aromatic-spice integration;
- fresh-to-woody transitions;
- violet/green bridges;
- and designer musk/wood architecture.

Do not fully remove signature spices from the fixed core unless core-alone testing proves recognition survives.

## Prada

Emphasize:

- iris and powder;
- clean soap/muguet transmission;
- musk texture;
- neroli and geranium;
- controlled patchouli;
- pepper;
- and amber/cedar structure.

A Prada-style chassis often needs the iris/soap/musk object protected as a whole. Treating “iris” as one ionone and “soap” as Florol alone is destructive compression.

## Dior

Emphasize:

- exact edition and concentration;
- sibling differential analysis;
- citrus and aromatic openings;
- ambroxide and wood ladders;
- and signature block dose response.

Do not mix Sauvage EDT, EDP, Parfum, and Elixir evidence, or different Dior Homme eras, without explicit version separation.

## Chanel

Emphasize:

- production era;
- aldehyde series;
- layered floral heart;
- ionone/orris architecture;
- sandalwood and moss systems;
- musk texture;
- and long transitions.

Aldehydes should be treated as a series with different contours and timing, not one generic “aldehyde.”

## Amouage

Emphasize:

- resins;
- incense;
- spices;
- smoke;
- balsams;
- high-boiling woods;
- oud-like effects;
- naturals;
- and the nonvolatile fraction.

Use coupled sockets when necessary:

1. volatile spice socket;
2. heart resin/floral socket;
3. drydown smoke/wood socket.

Evaluation may need 24–48 hours. One small top-note socket is usually inadequate.

## Guerlain

Emphasize:

- historical era;
- house bases;
- naturals;
- ionone/powder systems;
- vanilla and balsams;
- moss;
- patchouli;
- and reformulation-sensitive materials.

## Hermès

Emphasize:

- negative space;
- precise citrus quality;
- vegetal/mineral transitions;
- restrained woods;
- and low-dose balance.

Do not compensate for a missing transition by adding more mass.

## Middle Eastern oils and attars

Change the matrix model:

- oil carrier;
- lower immediate volatility;
- stronger deposition;
- heavier naturals and resins;
- and longer evaluation.

Do not reuse an ethanol headspace model without recalibration.
