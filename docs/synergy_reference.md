  # Synergy Reference — Chemical Interactions & Dosing Triggers
## Mapped to Current Inventory · All Values in µL
**Updated:** 2026-04-13

Each synergy describes a **chemical interaction** where materials produce an effect greater than the sum of their parts. Dosing is shown at 4 intensity levels across 4 batch sizes.

| Intensity | Effect |
|-----------|--------|
| **Whisper** | Barely detectable — subconscious. The synergy is there but unnamed. |
| **Audible** | Clearly present, supporting the composition. Not dominant. |
| **Statement** | Featured effect. This IS the accord. |
| **Dominant** | Composition revolves around this synergy. |

> **How to use:** Find the synergy you want, pick your batch size column, and dose each material at the listed µL. Materials are listed in mixing order (top of list → first into the beaker).

## 1. RADIANCE AMPLIFICATION
**Effect:** Hedione/HC creates a molecular radiance field around floral materials, multiplying diffusion and perceived volume by 3–5×. The jasmonates act as "diffusion lenses" that extend sillage radius.

**Core materials:** Hedione HC (preferred) or Hedione + any floral partner

|----------|----------|---------|---------|-----------|----------|
| | | 10 / 30 / 50 / 100 | 10 / 30 / 50 / 100 | 10 / 30 / 50 / 100 | 10 / 30 / 50 / 100 |
| Hedione HC | neat | 50/150/250/500 | 150/450/750/1500 | 300/900/1500/3000 | 500/1500/2500/5000 |
| *+ Floral partner* | — | *N-level dose* | *N-level dose* | *H-level dose* | *H-level dose* |

**Best floral partners (pick 1–2):**
- **Hydroxycitronellal** — muguet radiance, cosmetic-clean aura
- **Phenethyl Alcohol** — rose radiance, honeyed shimmer
- **Aurantiol** — orange-blossom radiance, Schiff base amplification
- **DBCA** — gardenia radiance, transparent white-floral

**Key ratio:** Hedione HC should be 2–5× the volume of its floral partner. Below 2× ratio the radiance effect doesn't fully engage.

## 2. SALICYLATE CUSHION (Cosmetic Diffusion Bed)
**Effect:** Salicylates create a "cushion" layer that lifts the composition off skin, adds cosmetic volume, and extends longevity. Two-salicylate gradient = transparent top → deep base diffusion.

| Benzyl Salicylate | neat | 40/120/200/400 | 100/300/500/1000 | 200/600/1000/2000 | 400/1200/2000/4000 |

**Gradient rule:** Hexyl Sal floats on top (lighter, green-transparent), Benzyl Sal anchors below (heavier, waxy-cosmetic). A 1:1.5 ratio (Hexyl:Benzyl) creates the cleanest cushion. For sheer skin-scents, use Hexyl Sal alone. For cosmetic white florals, favor Benzyl Sal.

## 26. SOLID FIXATIVE / SKIN-ADHESION BASE (Myristic Acid)
**Effect:** Myristic Acid Powder is a C14:0 fatty acid (mp ~54°C) that serves two functions: (1) in alcohol-based perfume, trace amounts anchor volatile top notes via hydrogen-bond networks between the fatty acid's carboxyl group and polar aroma molecules — extends citrus and green note longevity by 30–60 min; (2) in solid perfume, provides waxy structure alongside carrier oils and beeswax.

**All values in grams (g)** — weigh on a milligram scale. Melt into warmed ethanol or IPM before incorporating into alcohol-based formulas.

| Material | Form | Whisper | Audible | Statement | Dominant |
|----------|------|---------|---------|-----------|----------|

**Synergy partners (alcohol-based):**
- **Citrus anchoring:** Myristic Acid at Audible + Benzyl Benzoate at Normal dose — hydrogen bonding from the fatty acid plus molecular weight from BB create a fixative gradient that extends citrus longevity without adding scent character.
- **Green note hold:** Myristic Acid at Whisper + Hexyl Salicylate at Low dose — lightweight invisible fixation for volatile green materials (cis-3-Hexenol, Leafovert, Dynascone).
- **Full-spectrum fixative bed:** Myristic Acid at Audible + Benzyl Salicylate at Normal + Iso E Super at Normal — three distinct fixation mechanisms (hydrogen bonding + salicylate cushion + molecular cocoon).
