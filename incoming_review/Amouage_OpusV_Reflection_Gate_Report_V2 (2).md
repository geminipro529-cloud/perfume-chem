## Principal corrections

### Opus V

The earlier inventory build used 14 parts of a 30% Ambrofix stock, delivering only 4.2 active-equivalent parts against a 40-part neat target. V2 uses 133.333 raw-equivalent parts of the 30% stock before product-volume normalization. The corrected formula also restores the target Galaxolide, Habanolide and Benzyl Benzoate balance and keeps separate orris, oud, civet and rum systems.

### Reflection Man clean V2

- rosemary, red pepper berries and bitter orange leaves;
- neroli, orris, jasmine and ylang-ylang;
- vetiver, patchouli, sandalwood and cedarwood.

It reduces the previous formula's iris, Javanol, sweet-amber and heavy aromatic pressure.
