# Designer 10 + Niche 10 — Deep Research and Literature Report V2

## Scope

This report supports twenty new fragrance reconstructions beyond the existing atlas. The selected fragrances are widely discussed, influential and structurally diverse. Exact production formulas remain proprietary.

## Research method

- Official brand product pages and current note architecture.
- Current finished-product ingredient/allergen disclosures.
- Community consensus and disagreement through Fragrantica and Reddit search sources.
- Secondary GC-MS/formula-count sources when public.
- Patent-style functional reconstruction logic for missing odorant classes.
- Peer-reviewed GC-O, AEDA, recombination and natural-material chemistry.
- Inventory-only formula mapping.

## Methodological cautions

GC-MS abundance is not equal to sensory importance. GC-O/AEDA and omission/recombination studies show that trace odorants can dominate, while mixtures can create emergent characters through synergy and masking. Therefore strict OAV is not fabricated where matrix-compatible thresholds are absent.

## Patent and allergen-disclosure logic

Finished-product disclosures were used as family constraints rather than as percentages. Examples include Alpha-Isomethyl Ionone supporting violet/orris families, Coumarin supporting tonka/fougère structures, Eugenol/Cinnamal supporting clove-cinnamon spice, Patchouli and Cedar oils supporting literal woods, and Benzyl Salicylate/Benzyl Benzoate supporting floral binding and fixative structure.

## Final-gate policy

A formula reaches `SOL_PRO_PASS_BENCH` only when mass balance, complexity, signature registration and phase-direction gates pass. Unknown supplier concentrations cause `SOL_PRO_PASS_VERIFY_STOCK`, not an invented active percentage.
