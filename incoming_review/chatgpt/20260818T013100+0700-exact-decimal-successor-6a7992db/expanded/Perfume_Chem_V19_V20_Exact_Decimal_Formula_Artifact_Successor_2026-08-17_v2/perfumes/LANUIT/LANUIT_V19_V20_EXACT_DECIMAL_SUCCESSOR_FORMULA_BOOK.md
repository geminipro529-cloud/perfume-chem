## Artistic architecture preserved

**Art title:** Cardamom Velvet  
**Primary motif:** Cardamom warmth glowing inside a dark cedar frame.  
**Secondary motif:** A cool lavender-floral shadow with a low iris whisper over tonka-coumarin skin.  
**Tension:** Restraint versus abandon at close range.  
**Release:** The bright cardamom is slowly absorbed by cedar and tonka until it becomes warm skin rather than a spice note.  
**Negative space:** Hedione, muguet and restrained transparent wood preserve intimacy without making the perfume weak or opaque.  
**Recurrence:** Cardamom returns as a warm pulse at two and eight hours, no longer bright but embedded in coumarin and musk.  
**Texture progression:** electric cardamom → cool aromatic velvet → iris haze → dark cedar → warm skin  
**Spatial identity:** Highly legible within conversational distance, deliberately restrained in the far field.  
**Late residue:** Cedar, tonka-coumarin, vetiver, resin, suede and soft macrocyclic musk.  
**Controlled imperfection:** A faint smoky animalic shadow under the polished warmth creates nocturnal depth without fecal or bandage character.
