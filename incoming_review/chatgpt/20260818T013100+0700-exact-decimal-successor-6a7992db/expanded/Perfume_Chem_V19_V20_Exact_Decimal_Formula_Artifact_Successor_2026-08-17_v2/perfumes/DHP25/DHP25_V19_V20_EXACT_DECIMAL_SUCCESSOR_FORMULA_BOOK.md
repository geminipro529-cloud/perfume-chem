## Artistic architecture preserved

**Art title:** Petal Under Pressure  
**Primary motif:** A pale iris petal suspended above a black-violet rhizome.  
**Secondary motif:** Cut hay, vetiver and patchouli form a dry field compressed by a polished amberwood grip.  
**Tension:** Powdery caress versus ambery pressure.  
**Release:** The base does not simply crush the petal; it gradually turns petal into wax, wax into root, and root into polished dark wood.  
**Negative space:** Hedione, muguet air, magnolia and floral transmission leave a lit chamber above the dense base.  
**Recurrence:** The petal disappears under patchouli at two hours, then reappears as a violet wax contour around the eight-hour woods.  
**Texture progression:** cold petal air → wax bloom → dark rhizome → cut hay → polished mineral wood  
**Spatial identity:** The far field is firm and woody; the near field retains floral wax and root detail.  
**Late residue:** Iris-root wax, vetiver, patchouli, hay and three-way amberwood pressure with a suede edge.  
**Controlled imperfection:** A trace of root earth, roasted grain and saffron-suede gives the immaculate structure a pulse.
