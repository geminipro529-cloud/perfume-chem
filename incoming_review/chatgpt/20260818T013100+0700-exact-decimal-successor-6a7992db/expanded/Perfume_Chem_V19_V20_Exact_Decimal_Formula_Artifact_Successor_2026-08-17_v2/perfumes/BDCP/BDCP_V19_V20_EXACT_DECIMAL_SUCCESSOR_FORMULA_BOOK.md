## Artistic architecture preserved

**Art title:** Blue Cedar Continuum  
**Primary motif:** Bitter aromatic freshness that travels through full cedar instead of disappearing above it.  
**Secondary motif:** New Caledonian-style sandalwood cream, incense air and dark musky wood.  
**Tension:** Invigorating clarity versus dense creamy authority.  
**Release:** Freshness is translated into aromatic wood, then into creamy sandalwood, so the perfume feels continuous rather than top-heavy.  
**Negative space:** Incense air, floral transmission and dark shadow create depth between the cedar and sandalwood masses.  
**Recurrence:** Grapefruit bitterness reappears as a dry mineral edge around the late sandalwood.  
**Texture progression:** bitter blue light → aromatic air → cedar grain → sandalwood cream → dark polished aura  
**Spatial identity:** Clear bitter freshness projects first; the far field becomes polished wood while the near field retains incense and cream.  
**Late residue:** Cedar, sandalwood, incense, mineral amber, musk, moss and a restrained suede-oud shadow.  
**Controlled imperfection:** A trace of smoke, moss and root darkness prevents the smooth woods from becoming a showroom surface.
