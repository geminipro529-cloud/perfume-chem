## Artistic architecture preserved

**Art title:** Mandarin Air / Cedar Skin  
**Primary motif:** A juicy mandarin arc thrown through open air onto clean cedar.  
**Secondary motif:** Almond-tonka warmth and a radiant white-musk skin trail.  
**Tension:** Outdoor kinetic freshness versus bare-skin sensuality.  
**Release:** The air collapses inward into cedar, then cedar softens into tonka and skin musk.  
**Negative space:** Marine materials stay below the point of blue-fragrance identity; floral-air materials carve space between citrus and cedar.  
**Recurrence:** Mandarin returns in the drydown as a warm peel echo inside tonka and white musk.  
**Texture progression:** sparkling peel → wind on skin → clean cedar → almond cream → luminous musk  
**Spatial identity:** Bright kinetic opening in the far field, with softer cedar-tonka detail close to skin.  
**Late residue:** Dry cedar, almond-tonka, sandalwood fibers and a clean metallic-soft musk blend.  
**Controlled imperfection:** A green-bitter petitgrain seam keeps the sporty cleanliness from becoming shower-gel polish.
