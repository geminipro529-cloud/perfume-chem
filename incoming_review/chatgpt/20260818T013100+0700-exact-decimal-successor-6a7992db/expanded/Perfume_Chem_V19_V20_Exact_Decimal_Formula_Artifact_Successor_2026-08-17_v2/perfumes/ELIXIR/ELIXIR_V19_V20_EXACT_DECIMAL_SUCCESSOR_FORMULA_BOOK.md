## Artistic architecture preserved

**Art title:** Red Moon Licorice  
**Primary motif:** A persistent grapefruit spear surrounded by hot cinnamon/nutmeg and cool cardamom.  
**Secondary motif:** A floral lavender crown sinking into sweet licorice velvet and dark polished woods.  
**Tension:** Explosive spice-freshness versus velvety dark smoothness.  
**Release:** The sharp spear is wrapped first in lavender, then in licorice, until only a dark red trace remains in the woods.  
**Negative space:** Floral transmission and distributed woods stop the formula from becoming a compressed spice-amber wall.  
**Recurrence:** Grapefruit bitterness returns late as a dry mineral edge inside licorice, vetiver and amberwood.  
**Texture progression:** bitter spear → hot/cool spice corona → lavender velvet → licorice lacquer → dark wood  
**Spatial identity:** The opening radiates in all directions; the late structure is denser, lower and more controlled.  
**Late residue:** Licorice, patchouli, vetiver, incense, dark oud-suede and distributed amberwood pressure.  
**Controlled imperfection:** A faint phenolic, bitter and smoky edge keeps the licorice from becoming candy.
