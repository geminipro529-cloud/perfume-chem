## Artistic architecture preserved

**Art title:** Cypress Pulse  
**Primary motif:** A mandarin spark hitting a green cypress spine.  
**Secondary motif:** A dense almond-tonka core inside a persistent white-musk field.  
**Tension:** Cold green propulsion versus enveloping musky warmth.  
**Release:** The coniferous pulse slows and folds inward, becoming a warm musk-tonka heartbeat.  
**Negative space:** Floral-air and restrained ozonic materials keep the cypress from turning into a pine-cleaner wall.  
**Recurrence:** Cypress returns in the late drydown as a dry green filament through tonka and musk.  
**Texture progression:** mandarin flash → cold needles → aromatic fabric → almond warmth → musky pulse  
**Spatial identity:** Strong cool projection, then a dense warm aura that hugs closer than the EDT.  
**Late residue:** Cypress-dry woods, tonka, soft sandalwood, labdanum and radiant skin musk.  
**Controlled imperfection:** A peppery resinous needle edge prevents the dense warmth from becoming smooth to the point of anonymity.
