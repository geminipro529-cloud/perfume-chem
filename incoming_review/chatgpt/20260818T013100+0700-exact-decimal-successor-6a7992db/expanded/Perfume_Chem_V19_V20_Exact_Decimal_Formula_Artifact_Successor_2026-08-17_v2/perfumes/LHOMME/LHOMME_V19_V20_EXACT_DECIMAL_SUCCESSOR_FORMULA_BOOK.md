## Artistic architecture preserved

**Art title:** Urban Ginger Metal  
**Primary motif:** White-pepper light and ginger movement flashing across metallic violet leaf.  
**Secondary motif:** A basil-flower kink and polished vetiver-tonka-cedar root.  
**Tension:** Sharp kinetic freshness versus smooth urban sensuality.  
**Release:** The metallic green heart is polished by floral air and descends into a clean, warm woody root.  
**Negative space:** Hedione and muguet materials create breathing room around ginger and violet leaf, preventing toothpaste/cucumber collision.  
**Recurrence:** The ginger disappears after an hour, then returns as a dry warm shimmer over vetiver and cedar.  
**Texture progression:** pepper light → ginger snap → metallic leaf → floral polish → warm woody skin  
**Spatial identity:** High-definition kinetic near field with a clean polished woody aura at distance.  
**Late residue:** Vetiver, cedar, smooth sandalwood, tonka, incense and clean macrocyclic musk.  
**Controlled imperfection:** An anisic basil kink and a trace metallic leaf bitterness keep the polish from feeling corporate and generic.
