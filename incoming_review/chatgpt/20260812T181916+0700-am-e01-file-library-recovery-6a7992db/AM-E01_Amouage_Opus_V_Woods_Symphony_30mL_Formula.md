1. Prepare and label every required dilution and premix; verify heterogeneous stocks such as Evernyl before sampling.
2. Combine resins, leather materials, heavy woods and musks first.
3. Add iris/floral-body materials, then aquatic/green connectors.
4. Add citrus, spices and aldehydic trace stocks last.
5. Add the listed carrier balance, mix to homogeneity, then add ethanol 95–96% q.s. to exactly 30.0 mL.
6. Mature coded pilots under identical conditions. Do not skin-test before current safety review.
