# YSL La Nuit V2 — Inventory-Fit Spice-Open Chassis

**Status:** Working build hypothesis derived from the uploaded `YSL_La_Nuit_chassis_partition.csv`. The source partition totals 4,500 µL and is treated here as a design target, not an authenticated YSL formula.

## Purpose

The uploaded partition used a broad 4,200 µL core plus a 300 µL parent module. That parent module was not a pure spice socket. This revision extracts the complete named spice/aromatic signature into one replaceable 88 µL socket.

```text
4,500 µL total concentrate
= 4,412 µL fixed La Nuit-shaped chassis
+    88 µL replaceable spice socket
```

The fixed chassis contains no Cardamom, Pink Pepper, Litsea Cubeba, Methyl Geranate, or Citronellal.

## D. Custom spice socket

Replace all 88 µL with one custom spice accord.

Recommended first-pass constraints:

- exact raw total: 88 µL
- target odorant-active load: roughly 50–75 µL neat-equivalent, because the parent module contains 75 µL of neat aromatic materials
- keep a cool top component, a heart recognizer, and one bridge into Cashmeran/coumarin/woods
- prepare strong materials at 1% or 10% when direct dosing would be too coarse
- do not add the custom module on top of the parent module or DPG control

## Mixing sequence

1. Build the heavy wood, amber, vetiver, patchouli, musk, Sandalore, coumarin, and vanillin block.
2. Add violet/orris, Hedione, Florol, Bourgeonal, rosy traces, and salicylate-green materials.
3. Add linalool, linalyl acetate, Dihydromyrcenol, bergamots, aldehydic traces, and technical material.
4. Add the 88 µL parent, neutral, or custom module.
5. Mature and evaluate coded samples at 0, 5, and 30 minutes, then 2, 4, and 8 hours.
