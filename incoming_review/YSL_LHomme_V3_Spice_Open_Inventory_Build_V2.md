# YSL L’Homme V3 — Spice-Open Inventory Build V2

**Status:** Working research build. This is not an authenticated YSL formula, a passed release, a safety authorization, or a claim of sensory equivalence.

**Purpose:** Preserve the broad L’Homme woody–musk–violet–muguet–green chassis while leaving the original spice identity out, so a new spice direction can be tested cleanly.

**Provisional build-row hash:** `902b882e8f6822eeeb2e2843f7c1de8cf500f06d2820299160f8f7dcffb27b4b`

## Architecture

```text
4,500 µL total concentrate
= 4,200 µL fixed spice-neutral core
+   212 µL non-spice interface/support bridge
+    88 µL replaceable custom-spice socket
```

For a neutral control, fill the 88 µL socket with DPG. For a new spice flanker, replace that 88 µL of DPG one-for-one with the new spice accord.

The 212 µL support bridge is retained across versions because it supplies citrus, green, violet, and muguet continuity. It is not the spice payload.

## Spice-neutral decision

The fixed chassis contains none of the following original spice materials:

- Fresh Ginger Oil
- Black Pepper Oil
- Cardamom Oil
- Nutmeg Oil
- Coriander Seed Oil
- Basil Oil, ct. Methyl Chavicol
- Elemi Oil

For stricter neutrality, this version also removes **Caryophyllene Acetate** and **Cinnamon Bark Oil 1%** from the fixed core. Their combined 18 µL is replaced with DPG, so they cannot quietly bias every future spice version.

## Galaxolide concentration correction

This build assumes **Galaxolide 80%**:

| Actual Galaxolide stock | Galaxolide raw µL | Fixed-core DPG µL |
|---|---:|---:|
| 50% | 280 | 148 |
| **80% assumed here** | **175** | **253** |
| 100% | 140 | 288 |

Use only one row. The Galaxolide plus DPG adjustment preserves the 4,200 µL fixed-core total and the 140 µL Galaxolide active target.

## Mixing order

1. Preblend Romandolide, Zenolide, Galaxolide, Ambrettolide, Exaltolide, Iso E Super, Vertofix, Cedramber, Amberwood F, cedar, Sandalore, Ambrofix, vetiver, Norlimbanol, Cashmeran, coumarin, and the other base traces.
2. Add the ionone/violet materials, muguet materials, salicylate/green reconstruction, Hedione, and floral bridges.
3. Add citrus, aromatic, fruity, aldehydic, watery, and mint traces.
4. Add the 212 µL support bridge.
5. Add either the 88 µL DPG control or one 88 µL custom spice module.
6. Add the remaining DPG last, mix gently, and record the actual measured additions.
7. Mature at least 14 days before judging the architecture.
8. Compare coded blotters at 0, 5, and 30 minutes, then 2, 4, and 8 hours.

## Stop-and-check items before mixing

- Verify the actual Galaxolide concentration and use the correct Galaxolide/DPG row.
- Verify whether Leafovert is neat or diluted. Do not treat an unknown stock as neat.
- Verify that Ambrettolide and Exaltolide are the stated 10% stocks.
- Confirm the exact identity and current safety documentation of the Lilial Replacer.
- Recalculate active/carrier/solvent accounting from the bottle labels.
- Run the project’s current safety and formula-artifact checks before any skin-use batch.

## Interpretation

This is an inventory-mapped **build**, not a rewrite of the V3 reconstruction target. The target should retain the missing identities and evidence history; this build records the substitutions and the open spice socket separately.
