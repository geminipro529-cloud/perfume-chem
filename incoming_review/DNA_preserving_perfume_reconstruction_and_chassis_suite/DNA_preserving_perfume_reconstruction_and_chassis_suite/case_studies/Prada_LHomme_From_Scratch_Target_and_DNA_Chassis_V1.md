# Prada L'Homme From-Scratch Target and DNA-Preserving Chassis V1

**Authority:** Tier 1-2 documentary and functional hypothesis. No ranked identity roster or calibrated chromatogram was used. The 54 rows are a testable center formula, not a recovered commercial recipe.

**Official sources:**

- https://www.prada-beauty.com/fragrance/lhomme-prada/lhomme-prada-eau-de-toilette/MPL01352.html
- https://www.prada.com/ww/en/p/lhomme-prada-edt-150-ml/2A1251_2HC0_F0Z99_P_ML150

The official materials describe a fougere-aromatic fragrance with black pepper, geranium, patchouli, and a broader architecture involving neroli, violet, iris, amber, cedar, and patchouli. The ingredient label supports linalool, limonene, coumarin, citronellol, geraniol, citral, farnesol, and technical UV/stability materials, but does not disclose the fragrance concentrate formula.

## Method changes from the YSL cases

1. Official notes were translated into functions, not literal percentages.
2. Label evidence constrained chemical families but was not expanded automatically into independent additions.
3. A functional graph was built around iris-amber, soap-muguet, neroli, pepper-geranium-patchouli, clean musk, and cedar.
4. Candidate materials were selected as an explicit ensemble center.
5. Unknown captive functions remain an important unresolved possibility.
6. The formula must be improved through blind recombination and omission studies.
