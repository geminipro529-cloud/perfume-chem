## Why the prior chassis was over-compressed

The original 24-row chassis removed ginger, pepper, cardamom, basil, and watery accents into a neutral slot; collapsed Habanolide into Galaxolide, Muscenone Delta into Exaltolide, and several ionone/green functions into broad replacements; and omitted many transition materials. That was appropriate only as an inventory-constrained scaffold, not as a high-detail parent reconstruction.

4500 = 4200\;\text{µL fixed core} + 300\;\text{µL parent module}
