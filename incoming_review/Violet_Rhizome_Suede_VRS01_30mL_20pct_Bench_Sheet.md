# Violet Rhizome Suede VRS-01B

## 30 mL Eau de Parfum Bench Sheet

**Final concentration:** 20% v/v

```text
6,000 µL fragrance concentrate
+ 24.0 mL ethanol 96%
= nominal 30.0 mL Eau de Parfum
```

**Status:** proposed laboratory formula. Raw-volume arithmetic is verified; sensory performance and category-specific safety clearance are not yet established.

## Required preparation

Prepare Carrot Seed EO at 10% in DPG before mixing:

```text
50 µL Carrot Seed EO
+ 450 µL DPG
= 500 µL Carrot Seed EO 10%
```

The formula uses **30 µL** of that 10% working dilution.

> Orris Liquid is dosed as supplied. Its internal active concentration is unknown, so do not reinterpret its 300 µL raw dose as 300 µL pure irones.

## Mixing sequence

1. Add Iso E Super, Benzyl Salicylate, all musks, Ambrofix, Sandalore, Polysantol, Cedarwood Virginia, Vetival, Cashmeran, Suederal, Olibanum, and DPG.
2. Add Orris Liquid, Alpha Isomethyl Ionone, Orivone, Ultralia, Alpha Irone 30%, Dihydro Beta Ionone, Alpha Ionone, and Carrot Seed 10%.
3. Add Hedione, Phenethyl Alcohol, Osmanthus 10%, and Violet Leaf Absolute 10%.
4. Add Cedrat, Grapefruit, Petitgrain, Linalyl Acetate, Linalool, Dihydromyrcenol, Neroli 10%, Leafovert, Galbanum, and Aldehyde C12 MNA 1%.
5. Mix thoroughly and rest the concentrate for 48 hours.
6. Add 24.0 mL ethanol 96%, mix, and mature at least 14 days away from heat and light.

## Evaluation checkpoints

Evaluate on blotter at 0, 5, and 30 minutes, then 2, 6, and 24 hours.

Watch especially for:

- cold metallic violet leaf versus cucumber-like excess
- realistic rhizome texture versus cosmetic powder
- restrained osmanthus-suede shadow versus peach or leather drift
- dry cedar-amber persistence without smothering the violet

## Formula boundary

This is the recommended VRS-01B center level, containing **120 µL Violet Leaf Absolute 10%** in the 6,000 µL concentrate, equivalent to 12 µL pure absolute by dilution arithmetic.
