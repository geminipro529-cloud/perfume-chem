## Compounding logic

Build in the workbook’s perfumery sequence: **Base → Heart → Opening → Trace**. Within each group, additions are listed **highest volume to lowest volume**. This keeps the structural backbone stable, adds the transformation layer next, keeps the most volatile opening materials later, and leaves the smallest ratio-sensitive handoff materials for final precision.

Before batching, verify every exact bottle / working-stock identity. For **1–20 µL** additions, the workbook requires a verified **0.5–10 µL pipette**. If a required working stock is not homogeneous or not prepared at the stated concentration, **HOLD** rather than guessing.

## 30 mL finishing procedure

1. Label the concentrate vessel and record the batch ID, date, room temperature, and exact stock references.
2. Add **Group 1 — Base / Afterimage** from highest to lowest.
3. Add **Group 2 — Heart / Transformation** from highest to lowest.
4. Add **Group 3 — Opening / Aromatic** from highest to lowest.
5. Add **Group 4 — Trace / Handoff** from highest to lowest.
6. Mix until visually homogeneous. **Do not heat** unless a separate material-preparation SOP explicitly allows it.
7. Cap the concentrate and let it rest for **at least 24 hours**.
8. Add **25,500 µL ethanol** to the **4,500 µL concentrate** (15% finished strength), or bring to **30.00 mL final volume**.
9. Mix thoroughly and cap immediately.
10. Keep storage temperature/light matched to the authenticated reference and other comparison samples.
