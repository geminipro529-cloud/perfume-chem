## Why V1 was over-compressed

- Habanolide was collapsed into Galaxolide, erasing radiant macrocyclic musk lift.
- Muscenone Delta was collapsed into Exaltolide, erasing powdery-cosmetic enhancement.
- Isoraldeine 95 and Methyl Ionone Gamma Coeur were collapsed into one alpha-isomethyl-ionone stock, flattening the violet-leaf/orris body.
- Verdox, Helional, Bourgeonal, cis-3-hexenyl salicylate, and cis-3-hexenol were absent or compressed, removing the apple-green-water transition.
- Cedramber, Amberwood F, Bacdanol, and Norlimbanol were absent, reducing the wood base to a broad block instead of a multi-speed ladder.
- The ginger/pepper/cardamom/nutmeg/basil system was quarantined in a module rather than integrated into the fragrance.
- Small esters, aldehydes, rose ketones, herbs, mints, and watery traces were removed, eliminating the hand-offs that create perceived complexity.

## Structural blocks

### 1. Radiant musk and woody scaffold
Iso E Super, Ethylene Brassylate, Galaxolide, Habanolide, Vertofix Coeur, Cedramber, Amberwood F, cedarwood, Sandalore, Bacdanol, Ambroxan, Norlimbanol, Cashmeran, vetiver, Muscenone Delta, and Exaltolide form a layered structure rather than one generic woody-musky slab.

### 2. Apple-green-water hinge
Verdox, Helional, Bourgeonal, cis-3-hexenyl salicylate, cis-3-hexenol, Adoxal, Liffarome, Calone, pear ester, Allyl Amyl Glycolate, and Allyl Cyclohexyl Propionate create the elusive transition between citrus, violet leaf, and clean woods.

### 3. Split violet/orris system
Isoraldeine 95, Methyl Ionone Gamma Coeur, Dihydro Alpha Ionone, Dihydro Beta Ionone, Alpha Damascone, and Damascenone provide multiple violet, orris, leafy, woody, tea, and fruit angles. They are not treated as one interchangeable ionone line.

### 4. Integrated spice signature
Ginger is the lead. Black pepper supplies dry sparkle; coriander and elemi link spice to citrus; cardamom cools the heart; nutmeg and a trace of cinnamon extend warmth; basil gives the distinctive herbal-green kink. The spices are part of the formula, not a removable afterthought.

### 5. Muguet and floral transmission
Lilial Replacer, Florol, Bourgeonal, Helional, Hedione, Phenethyl Alcohol, geraniol, geranium, and the acetate esters create transparent floral continuity without converting L’Homme into a floral bouquet.

## Mixing order

1. Prepare every 10% and 1% trace stock accurately before starting. Do not attempt sub-microlitre neat dosing.
2. Dissolve Coumarin, Vanillin, Heliotropin, Evernyl, and BHT stocks into the DPG/carrier portion.
3. Add musks and the full wood ladder. Mix until uniform.
4. Add ionones, muguet materials, green materials, Hedione, and floral esters.
5. Add citrus, ginger, pepper, cardamom, nutmeg, coriander, basil, elemi, aldehyde, mint, and Calone stocks last.
6. Rest the concentrate for 48–72 hours, then dilute 4.5 mL concentrate into 25.5 mL ethanol 96%.
7. Mature for at least 14 days. Evaluate against the reference at 0, 5, and 30 minutes, then 2, 4, and 8 hours.
