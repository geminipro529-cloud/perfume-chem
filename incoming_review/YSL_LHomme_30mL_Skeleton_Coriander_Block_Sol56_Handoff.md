### Execution differences

- Cinnamyl Alcohol 10% was not added because the owned bottle was neat.
- Isoeugenol 10% was later skipped.
- Anisaldehyde 10% was added at 1 µL.
- Olibanum was accidentally dosed as **2 µL of 50% resinoid**, rather than 2 µL of a 10% dilution.
- Therefore, the executed Olibanum active-equivalent is approximately **1.0 µL**, compared with **0.2 µL** in the recommendation.
