## Target identity and architecture

**Target:** dry black pepper and restrained neroli into fresh green-rosy geranium, clean floral linen and a distributed subdued iris/violet object over patchouli, cedar, mineral amber and mixed textile musks.

**Do not allow:** geranium/citronellol dominance; heavy rooty orris; lipstick/candy iris; laundry fog; detergent/marine drift; generic woody-amber wall.

**Current official Prada cross-check:** Prada currently foregrounds black pepper, geranium and patchouli, while also describing iris and amber as characteristic parts of the fragrance architecture.
