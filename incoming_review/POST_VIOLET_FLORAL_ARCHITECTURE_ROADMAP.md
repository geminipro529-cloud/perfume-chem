# Post–Violet Floral Architecture Roadmap

## Preserved idea
After the current Violet–Orris program reaches its physical-selection boundary, build a reusable **Common Floral Architecture Spine** and then develop architecture programs for:

1. Osmanthus
2. Immortelle
3. Tuberose
4. Jasmine
5. Rose
6. Muguet
7. Iris Flower
8. Orris Root
9. Lavender

## Governing principle
Use full architecture studies at the **floral-family level**, not for every named flower. Original perfume work may use the architecture as a blueprint. Commercial perfume reconstruction may use it only as a diagnostic grammar labelled:

`GENERAL FLORAL ARCHITECTURE / NOT TARGET-SPECIFIC FORMULA EVIDENCE`

The architecture library may suggest what functions and omission tests to examine. It must never be treated as proof of a commercial perfume's materials or formula.

## Common Floral Architecture Spine
Every family maps the same twelve functions:

1. Primary recognizer
2. Living petal mechanism
3. Floral volume and diffusion
4. Leaf, stem, or calyx
5. Dew, humidity, or dry air
6. Wax, pollen, powder, or flesh
7. Fruit, nectar, honey, or spice coloration
8. Indolic, herbal, animalic, or root depth
9. Floral-to-wood bridge
10. Musk and textile behavior
11. Temporal handoff
12. Late floral residue
