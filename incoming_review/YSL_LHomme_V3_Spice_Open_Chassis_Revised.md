# YSL L’Homme V3 — Spice-Open DNA Chassis

**Status:** Working research hypothesis; not an authenticated YSL formula, not a passed release, and not a safety authorization.

## Design decision

This revision keeps the full L’Homme-shaped structural core while moving the seven named spice/resin materials completely out of the fixed chassis:

- Fresh Ginger Oil
- Black Pepper Oil
- Cardamom Oil
- Nutmeg Oil
- Coriander Seed Oil
- Basil Oil, ct. Methyl Chavicol
- Elemi Oil

The architecture is:

```text
4,500 µL total concentrate
= 4,200 µL spice-open fixed core
+   212 µL parent support submodule
+    88 µL original L’Homme spice payload
```

For a new spice flanker, retain the 4,200 µL fixed core and normally retain the 212 µL support submodule, then replace only the 88 µL original spice payload with a new 88 µL spice payload. A more radical 300 µL module replacement is possible, but it should be treated as a new experiment rather than an exact parent reproduction.

## Inventory substitutions applied

| Missing target material | Revised inventory-mapped replacement | Raw-volume accounting | Classification |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Ethyl Linalool, 180 µL | Linalool 120 µL + additional Linalyl Acetate 45 µL + additional Dihydromyrcenol 15 µL | 180 µL | Functional replacement, not identity-equivalent |
| Isoraldeine 95, 100 µL | Alpha Isomethyl Ionone 100 µL | 100 µL | Grade-level functional replacement, not authenticated equivalence |
| Cis-3-Hexenyl Salicylate, 50 µL | Hexyl Salicylate 35 µL + Leafovert 10 µL + additional Cis-3-Hexenol 1% 5 µL | 50 µL | Green-salicylate reconstruction, not identity-equivalent |

The combined totals therefore become **Linalyl Acetate 135 µL**, **Dihydromyrcenol 175 µL**, and **Cis-3-Hexenol 1% in DPG 13 µL**.

> **Critical stock assumption:** Leafovert and Hexyl Salicylate are written as neat stocks. If either bottle is diluted, stop and recalculate the raw dose before mixing. If Hexyl Salicylate is unavailable, Benzyl Salicylate may replace its 35 µL raw allocation, but the result will be heavier, softer, and less leafy.

Assuming the stated stock strengths and assuming Leafovert and Hexyl Salicylate are neat, the revised active-proxy sum is approximately **3,727.06 µL**. The raw-volume partition is exact; sensory equivalence is not established.

For a nominal 30 mL, 15% v/v EDT study, the traditional volumetric finishing step would be 4.5 mL concentrate plus 25.5 mL ethanol. Treat that as a formulation convention rather than a gravimetric proof of final volume, and rerun the project’s quantity and safety checks before making a skin-use batch.

## E. How to create a new spice version

1. Keep the 4,200 µL fixed core unchanged.
2. Keep the 212 µL support submodule unchanged for the first experiment.
3. Replace the 88 µL original spice payload with a new spice payload totaling exactly 88 µL.
4. Do not alter the accepted target record; record the new 88 µL payload as a separate build/module version.
5. Compare the new module against the parent module using coded blotters at the same dose and time points.

## Expected differences from the unmodified V3 target

- The Ethyl Linalool replacement should be brighter and somewhat less tenacious or polished.
- Alpha Isomethyl Ionone should preserve the violet-orris field, but it does not authenticate the missing Isoraldeine 95 grade.
- The Hexyl Salicylate–Leafovert–Cis-3-Hexenol accord should preserve leafy-green salicylate function, but it may be sharper and less smoothly substantive than Cis-3-Hexenyl Salicylate.
- Moving the complete seven-material spice cluster into the module makes the fixed core genuinely reusable for alternate spice directions.

## Before building

- Verify the exact Leafovert stock concentration.
- Verify whether Hexyl Salicylate is actually present; otherwise apply the documented Benzyl Salicylate fallback.
- Confirm the identity and safety documentation for the Lilial Replacer.
- Recalculate active/carrier/solvent accounting from the actual stock labels.
- Run the project’s current safety and formula-artifact checks.
