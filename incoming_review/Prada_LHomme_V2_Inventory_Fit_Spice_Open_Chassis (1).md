# Prada L’Homme V2 — Inventory-Fit Spice-Open Chassis

**Status:** Working build hypothesis derived from the uploaded Prada L’Homme target. It is not an authenticated Prada formula, a passed sensory equivalence result, or a skin-use authorization.

## Purpose

This version preserves the clean iris, soap/muguet, neroli, musk, patchouli, cedar, and amber structure while removing the original spice/aromatic payload from the fixed chassis.

```text
4,500 µL total concentrate
= 4,412 µL fixed Prada DNA chassis
+    88 µL replaceable spice socket
```

The source target is explicitly a 54-row documentary and functional hypothesis, not a recovered commercial recipe. This document therefore preserves its architecture while keeping substitutions in the build layer.

## Inventory assumptions

This build uses the ingredient limits already stated in the conversation:

- no Ethylene Brassylate
- no Habanolide
- no Muscenone Delta
- no Methyl Ionone Gamma Coeur
- no Isoraldeine 95
- no Bacdanol
- no Cis-3-Hexenyl Salicylate

## D. Custom spice socket

Replace the entire 88 µL parent module or DPG control with a custom spice accord totaling **exactly 88 µL**.

Do not add a custom module on top of the parent module.

Recommended first-pass constraints:

- raw module total: exactly 88 µL
- target odorant-active spice load: roughly 25–45 µL neat-equivalent
- use working dilutions for very powerful aldehydes, pyrazines, phenols, and cinnamon materials
- keep at least two roles: a top sparkle and a heart-to-base bridge
- record every material, stock strength, and measured dose as a new module version

## Mixing sequence

1. Mix woods, amber materials, patchouli, salicylates, and musks.
2. Add iris/violet materials and the soap/muguet/neroli system.
3. Add citrus, green, rosy, sweet, and technical traces.
4. Add the fixed-chassis DPG.
5. Add either the 88 µL parent module, 88 µL DPG control, or one 88 µL custom spice module.
6. Mature and compare coded blotters at 0, 5, and 30 minutes, then 2, 4, and 8 hours.
