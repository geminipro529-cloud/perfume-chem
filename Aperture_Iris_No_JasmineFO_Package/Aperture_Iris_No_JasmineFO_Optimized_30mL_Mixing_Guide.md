## Intent

This is the locked `Aperture Iris` premiere build after the no-`Jasmine FO` pass, the wood parse, the musk parse, and the inventory-wide candidate scan.

The brief stays the same:
- crystal iris first
- niche-luxury finish
- mass-readable structure
- jewelry-like surface polish
- no literal floral-oil shortcut

The final direction is not a new heart. It is a cleaner frame around the same iris center:
- `Red Mandarin EO` and `Pink Pepper Base` for faceted top sparkle
- `Hedione + PEA + Florol + Neroli EO + Cis Jasmone + trace Indole` for an implied jasmine-white-floral lock
- `Iso E Super + Clearwood + Cedramber + Bacdanol + Ambrox Super + Cashmeran` for mineral satin wood
- `Habanolide + Zenolide + Exaltolide + Musk Ketone` for a transparent macrocyclic musk finish

- after step `25`: the full perfume should smell complete before dilution
- after step `26`: more open and airy, but temporarily less dense until it rests

## Why This Version Won

### 1. The iris still sits in the center

`Opus V Iris Crystal` stays at `2.40 mL`. The shell is there to show it in better light, not to compete with it.

### 2. The floral bridge is built, not pasted on

The no-FO jasmine lock is:
- `Hedione` — trimmed from 1190µL to 1020µL (diminishing returns past ~15% of concentrate)
- `Phenethyl Alcohol`
- `Florol` — dewy muguet, replaces subliminal Hydroxycitronellal (was OAV 0.07)
- `Neroli EO` — boosted from 15µL to 40µL for stronger naturality
- `Cis Jasmone` — boosted from 10µL to 25µL, closer to perceptible threshold
- `Indole (10%)`

That gives floral polish, lift, and naturality without turning the perfume into a literal jasmine perfume.

### 3. The woods are bright and satin, not dark and forceful

After the wood parse, the retained frame is:
- `Iso E Super`
- `Clearwood`
- `Cedramber`
- `Bacdanol`
- `Ambrox Super`
- `Cashmeran (20%)` — frosty-musky-woody bridge between woods and musks

This reads more like mineral fabric and polished wood grain than amber lumber.

### 4. The musks are perceptible and luxury-coded

The prior musk stack had three subliminal materials (Romandolide OAV 0.30, Ethylene Brassylate OAV 0.40, Galaxolide OAV 0.09) consuming 290µL with no perceptual contribution. The rebuild:
- `Habanolide` — main clean macrocyclic halo (OAV 4.3)
- `Zenolide` — transparent clean macrocyclic, crystalline character
- `Exaltolide (10%)` — powdery-sweet depth, musk undercurrent
- `Musk Ketone (10%)` — warm powdery nitro musk (ODT 0.005 ppb, perceptible at trace)

### 5. The top now has premiere sparkle

The inventory scan kept pushing the gains into small surface edits rather than deeper structural changes. The winning adds were:
- `Red Mandarin EO`
- `Pink Pepper Base`

Those give a brighter, faceted, more launch-worthy top without pulling the perfume out of the iris family.

## Why This Order Works

- the wood-musk base goes in first so the dense materials are fully blended before the iris arrives
- the floral bridge goes in before the iris so the heart support is already open and smooth
- the iris module is added after that so you can smell the core identity clearly
- the top materials and metallic traces go in near the end so they stay cleaner and easier to judge
- ethanol goes in last so you are not chasing a diluted smell while building the concentrate

---

## Adjustment Knobs

If you want it more crystal:
- raise `Hedione` by `40-60 uL`
- lower `Ethylene Brassylate` by the same amount

If you want it more mineral-woody:
- raise `Ambrox Super` by `20-30 uL`
- lower `Hexyl Salicylate` by the same amount

If you want it softer and more skin-like:
- raise `Romandolide` by `20-30 uL`
- lower `Cedramber` by the same amount

If you want less sparkle:
- reduce `Pink Pepper Base` from `25 uL` to `10-15 uL`
- move that volume back into `Bergamot FCF`
