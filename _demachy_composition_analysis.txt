## Compositional Clusters

### Cluster 1: Clean Florals (Muguet + Citrus)
- IV Lime + Muguet — cool, green, transparent
- VII Lemon + Muguet-Lily — bright, crystalline, precise
Both use Hydroxycitronellal + ozonic materials. Lightest of the 7.

### Cluster 2: Jasmine-Heavy (Jasmine + Citrus)
- I Bergamot + Orange Blossom — classic, warm jasmine heart
- III Grapefruit + Jasmine — tropical jasmine, bold
- V Mandarin + Jasmine-Orange Blossom — opulent, layered jasmine
Three different jasmine interpretations. Bergamot is reference, Grapefruit is bold, Mandarin is opulent.

### Cluster 3: Oriental-Dark (Unusual Flower + Citrus)
- II Cedrat + Costus-Suede — animalic, strange, Le Labo
- VI Blood Orange + Rose — rich, damascene, Guerlain-esque
Both use unexpected flower pairings. Cedrat is the anti-DHC. Blood Orange is the oriental DHC.
