Bottom Line

The non-mass-market answer is much clearer than the earlier market-shaped one: the strongest attack products in the accepted set are not the broadest or darkest formulas. They are the ones with the best balance of family clarity, prestige cues, Thai climate fit, and repeat-wear comfort.

  • Best against MITH: Cobalt Cedar Air, Bleu Carbon Final, DHC II Cedrat, Porcelaine, Iris-Lavender Lipstick
  • Best against Vibeslab: Porcelaine, Iris-Lavender Lipstick, Iris Reverie Lactee v8, Bleu Luxe Mineral Jasmine, DHC II Cedrat
  • Best families to lead: blue masculine, clean cosmetic floral, powdery iris prestige, premium citrus, wearable green chypre
