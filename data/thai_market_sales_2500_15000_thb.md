## 3. CLIMATE-ADJUSTED THAI PREFERENCES

| Factor | Prefer | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Heat + humidity | Lighter orientals, elevated Hedione/Iso E, aquatic/fresh | Heavy vanilla overdose (>3% vanillin), syrupy ambers |
| AC environments | Cozy ambers, gourmand softness work indoors | Cloying patchouli-heavy, spice-forward |
| Tropical lifestyle | Citrus-green freshness, coconut creaminess | Heavy oakmoss, leather extremes |
| Work culture | Clean musk, powder, transparent woods | Loud animalic, strong barnyard, skank |
| Cultural markers | Jasmine (temple), sandalwood (wat incense), coconut-mango (food culture) | Boozy, smoky-aggressive, saffron overdose |

## 8. HEDONIC FLOOR — UNIVERSAL PLEASANTNESS

All formulas in this collection target hedonic composite score ≥ 0.65 (conventionally pleasant).
Materials with negative hedonic valence used only in trace doses, balanced by pleasant materials.

**High-hedonic materials (≥ 0.80):** Vanillin, Ethyl Vanillin, Linalool, Linalyl Acetate, PEA, Heliotropin, Coumarin, Maple Lactone, Gamma Decalactone, Raspberry Ketone, Hedione
**Medium-hedonic (0.60–0.79):** Ionones, Geraniol, Citronellol, Benzyl Acetate, Benzyl Salicylate, Sandalore, Iso E Super, Ambrox, Galaxolide, Cashmeran
**Low-hedonic (< 0.60 — use as trace accent only):** Indole, Birch Tar, Guaiacol, IBQ, Skatole, Geosmin
