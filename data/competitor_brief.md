## STRATEGIC POSITION

- **MITH:** Low-moderate price + moderate uniqueness (broad appeal, volume-driven)
- **Vibeslab:** High price + high uniqueness (differentiation, B2B-backed)
- **Collision zone:** 2,000-3,000 THB band only
- **Blue ocean:** 1,500-2,500 THB — both brands absent

## VRIO ADVANTAGES

- **MITH:** Thai-cultural IP (sustained), perfumer roster (sustained but fragile), mall booths (sustained)
- **Vibeslab:** 12-year B2B network (sustained, irreplicable), novelty concepts (sustained), curation discipline (sustained)

## TOP 3 RECOMMENDATIONS

### MITH
1. **B2B scent branding division** — hotel packages at 50k/150k/300k THB
2. **Prune to 25 hero SKUs** + 5 "Bangkok Stories" concept scents
3. **Discovery set** — 8x2ml at 399 THB

### Vibeslab
1. **Fix 100ml supply** — ~2M THB/month latent demand
2. **Launch 30ml at 1,900 THB** — enter the blue ocean gap
3. **Body care line** — Money, Skins, Ginza at 590-1,200 THB

## KEY INSIGHT

MITH and Vibeslab rarely compete for the same customer. MITH is a volume-driven cultural scent house for accessible mass-premium. Vibeslab is a concept-driven niche house with a 12-year B2B moat. Their primary collision point is the 2,000-3,000 THB band where MITH's 100ml (2,859 THB) meets Vibeslab's 50ml (2,900 THB). The real whitespace is **1,500-2,500 THB**, where neither brand operates.
