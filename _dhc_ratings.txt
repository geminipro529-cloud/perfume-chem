SEPHORA SHELF POSITIONING

I BERGAMOT -- Hermes counter
  "What if Eau de Citron Noir dropped the incense and just gave you
   Calabrian bergamot in a transparent woody frame? The most versatile.
   The one a 40-year-old lawyer wears to the office and the opera."

II CEDRAT -- Le Labo discovery set
  "Cedrat 29. Cold mineral citrus. No flowers. No sweetness. No apology.
   5% of people will love this. The other 95% won't understand it.
   That's the point. The art piece."

III GRAPEFRUIT -- Jo Malone display table
  "Pink grapefruit, frozen gin, white cotton. Spritz it and the room
   brightens. The most gift-able. The one your girlfriend steals.
   Sells itself."

IV LIME -- Byredo shelf
  "Caribbean lime so transparent it's almost a ghost. Vetiver earth
   anchors the ethereal top. The cool-girl fragrance. Smells like a
   white linen dress in a beach town you can't afford to live in."

V MANDARIN -- Tom Ford private blend
  "Sicilian mandarin + jasmine + benzoin warmth. The sunset citrus.
   The most expensive-smelling. The one you wear to dinner when you
   want to be remembered for taste, not for trying."


WHICH ONE SELLS?

  Sephora volume ranking:   Grapefruit > Bergamot > Mandarin > Lime > Cedrat

  Perfumer's ranking:       Cedrat > Lime > Bergamot > Mandarin > Grapefruit

  They invert perfectly. What sells fastest (Grapefruit) is what Demachy
  respects least. What sells slowest (Cedrat) is what he'd frame.
