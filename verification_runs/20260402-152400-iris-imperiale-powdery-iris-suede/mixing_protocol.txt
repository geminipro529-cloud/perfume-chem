═══ MIXING PROTOCOL: Iris Impériale — Powdery Iris / Suede ═══
Materials: 16 | Total: 100.0%

─── WARNINGS ───
⚠ CRYSTALLINE SOLIDS: Hedione, Habanolide, Iso E Super, Benzyl Salicylate, Vanillin, Coumarin — dissolve in warm solvent before adding.

─── PHASE 0.5: DISSOLUTION ───
  1. Dissolve Hedione in warm ethanol or DPG (40-50°C) until fully clear before adding to formula.
  2. Dissolve Habanolide in warm ethanol or DPG (40-50°C) until fully clear before adding to formula.
  3. Dissolve Iso E Super in warm ethanol or DPG (40-50°C) until fully clear before adding to formula.
  4. Dissolve Benzyl Salicylate in warm ethanol or DPG (40-50°C) until fully clear before adding to formula.
  5. Dissolve Vanillin in warm ethanol or DPG (40-50°C) until fully clear before adding to formula.
  6. Dissolve Coumarin in warm ethanol or DPG (40-50°C) until fully clear before adding to formula.

─── PHASE 1: BASE LAYER (foundation) ───
  Step 7. Add Ambrox Super (8.0%)
           Base note — add early for structural foundation.
  Step 8. Add Orivone (3.2%)
           Base note — add early for structural foundation.
  Step 9. Add Suederal (4.0%)
           Base note — add early for structural foundation.
  → Stir gently for 2-3 minutes. Let rest 5 minutes.

─── PHASE 2: HEART LAYER (character) ───
  Step 10. Add Alpha Irone (18.0%)
           Heart note — forms the core character.
  Step 11. Add Methyl Ionone (4.8%)
           Heart note — forms the core character.
  Step 12. Add Javanol (6.0%)
           Heart note — forms the core character.
  → Stir gently for 2-3 minutes. Let rest 5 minutes.

─── PHASE 3: MUSKS (smoothing) ───
  Step 13. Add Habanolide (4.0%)
           Musk — add after structure is set; wraps and smooths.
  Step 14. Add Cashmeran (8.0%)
           Musk — add after structure is set; wraps and smooths.
  → Stir gently for 2-3 minutes. Let rest 5 minutes.

─── PHASE 4: TOP NOTES (sparkle) ───
  Step 15. Add Aldehyde C11 (2.0%)
           Top note — most volatile, add last to preserve freshness.
  Step 16. Add Bergamot FCF Sicilian (4.8%)
           Top note — most volatile, add last to preserve freshness.
  Step 17. Add Cardamom FTEC (3.2%)
           Top note — most volatile, add last to preserve freshness.
  → Stir gently for 2-3 minutes. Let rest 5 minutes.

─── MACERATION ───
  Seal tightly and store in a cool, dark place.
  Estimated maturation: 23 days minimum.
  Check at day 7 for initial impressions.
  Best results typically at day 23 – 37.
