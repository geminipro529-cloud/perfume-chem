The exact target is **the current L’Oréal-era L’Homme Prada Eau de Toilette as presently sold through Prada Beauty**. Pre-2021 Puig-era liquid is outside the target, except as historical corroboration. The licence transition on January 1, 2021 is a provenance boundary, not proof of reformulation.

1. Official current Prada product page: current product identity, EDT concentration, Daniela Andrier attribution, black pepper, geranium and patchouli, plus iris, amber, neroli and powdery softness.

4. Repeated current reviews embedded on the official page: clean, soapy, powdery, smooth iris and restrained projection.
5. Public claimed reconstruction list: hypothesis generator only because no public chromatograms, calibration, sample provenance or percentages are supplied.
6. Inventory Master v3: sole stock-strength and implementation ledger.
