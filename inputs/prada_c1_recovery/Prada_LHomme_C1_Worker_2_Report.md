# Worker 2 — Perceptual and Chemistry-Skeptic Audit

**Execution mode:** Four reviewer lanes were run sequentially by GPT-5.6 Pro in one ChatGPT session. No DeepLuna, native Codex subagent, or true parallel worker was spawned. The lanes were procedurally separated by role and evidence packet, but not runtime-isolated.  
**Lane role:** Time-resolved target and note-to-molecule skeptic. This lane did not authorize a formula.

## Time-resolved target

### First 30 seconds
Crisp, sharpened and comparatively prominent for Prada. Black pepper and citrus/aromatic sparkle are supported. Metallic/aldehydic linen is plausible but not molecularly identified. Iris is texture, not heavy root.

### 5 minutes
The sharp top settles quickly. Neroli, geranium, soap and clean textile facets become clearer. Powder emerges without lipstick-heavy or gourmand weight.

### 30 minutes
Clean soap, powdery iris and fresh woody musk are central. Sweetness remains low. Rooty orris remains restrained.

### 2 hours
Iris/powder, musk, patchouli and dry wood balance. Amber is warm without becoming a dominant Ambroxan signature.

### 8 hours
Musky, patchouli-woody and softly ambered residue with moderate-to-low projection.

### 24-hour blotter
Dry musk, cedar/patchouli and faint powdery iris memory. No physical claim exists yet.

## Note-to-molecule audit

- Black pepper → Black Pepper EO: functional reconstruction, not disclosed identity.
- Geranium → Geranium EO/citronellol/trace Geraniol 10%: function supported; exact source unknown.
- Neroli → Neroli EO 10%/petitgrain/linalool/citral: function supported; carrier distortion remains.
- Iris → Beta Ionone/Alpha Irone: plausible hypothesis only; half-dose test mandatory.
- Linen → Habanolide/Romandolide: unproven identities; separate omissions mandatory.
- Metallic lift → Adoxal/C11/C10: trace falsifiable hypothesis; group omission mandatory.
- Soap → Nympheal/Florol/citronellol/musks: multiple mechanisms, no unique mapping.
- Amber → Ambrofix/woods/labdanum: functional reconstruction only.

## C1 architecture risks

- Iso E Super + Hedione + Linalyl Acetate + Linalool = 485 raw parts, risking a generic transparent shampoo/wood chassis.
- Habanolide + Romandolide = 120 raw parts, risking laundry glare or blanket-like musky volume.
- Beta Ionone 28 + Alpha Irone 30% 8 is substantial enough to require a half-dose iris variant.
- Neroli EO 10% at 60 raw parts supplies 6 product-equivalent parts and 54 carrier parts.
- Nine low-confidence trace modifiers total 14 raw parts and require group omission.
- Adoxal carries a manufacturer-described fresh-linen/detergent association and must be omitted in a controlled variant.

## Lane conclusion

The time-resolved target is sufficiently locked for a pilot. C1 remains a functional reconstruction, not a disclosed formula. Pilot work must include MU-C1, MU-C2, IR-C1, SC-C1, TR-C1, ML-C1, NE-C1 and GE-C1. No empirical similarity pass is justified.
