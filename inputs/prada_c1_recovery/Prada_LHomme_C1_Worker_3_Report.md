## Physical program

### Authenticated reference
Record authorized seller, receipt, market, SKU/EAN, carton and bottle batch codes, full ingredient panel, photographs, first opening and storage.

### Direct liquid analysis
Use GC-MS and GC-FID, orthogonal non-polar and polar columns, authentic standards, retention indices, calibration curves, internal standards, blanks, carryover checks, replicates and multiple confirmation ions/full-scan evidence.

### Temporal headspace
Run at 5 minutes, 30 minutes, 2 hours, 8 hours and 24 hours. Lock fiber chemistry, extraction time, temperature and matrix. Uncalibrated SPME peak area is not airborne concentration.

### GC-O, AEDA, recombination and omission
Use GC-O to locate odor-active regions, AEDA to rank dilution persistence, calibrated recombination to test sufficiency and omissions/control additions to test causality. Do not combine Adoxal, C12 MNA, Helional and Floralozone in one test.

### Sensory
Use a defined descriptive profile, coded equal-load blotters, repeated timepoints and delayed unblinding. Triangle tests are late-stage and require carryover controls.
